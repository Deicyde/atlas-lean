# ATLAS

A map of the root ATLAS v2 libraries. The standalone developments under
`Atlas/` remain outside the roadmap and its progress percentages until their
`MAPPED` coverage rows are decomposed, and the archived ATLAS v1 release is not
part of the wiki. Each subject collects the articles of the ATLAS v2 libraries:
every documented public MathlibExt, CSLibExt and WantedExt declaration is an
article whose statement is its docstring. All pages are generated by
`scripts/generate_wiki.py`; edit the generator, not the pages.

- [Roadmap](roadmap/README.md): subjects, libraries, topics, and statements,
  with the dependencies between statements.
- [Coverage](coverage/README.md): which parts of the repository the roadmap
  covers, and which it does not yet.
