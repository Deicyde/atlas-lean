# Quaternion algebras with isotropic norm form split

**Quaternion algebras with isotropic norm form split.** If the norm form of a quaternion algebra over a field of characteristic
not two is isotropic, then the algebra splits: it is isomorphic to the
`2 × 2` matrix algebra.
