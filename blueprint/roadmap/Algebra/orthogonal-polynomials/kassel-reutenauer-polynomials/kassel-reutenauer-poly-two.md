# The Kassel–Reutenauer polynomial $\bar P_2$

**The Kassel–Reutenauer polynomial $\bar P_2$.** The Kassel–Reutenauer polynomial
$\bar P_2(X) \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$ equals $X + 1$.
