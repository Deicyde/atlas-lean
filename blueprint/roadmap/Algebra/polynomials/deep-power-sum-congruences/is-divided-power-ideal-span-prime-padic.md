# $(p)$ is a divided-power ideal of $\mathbb{Z}_p$

**$(p)$ is a divided-power ideal of $\mathbb{Z}_p$.** For every prime $p$, the ideal generated
by $p$ in the ring $\mathbb{Z}_p$ of $p$-adic integers is a divided-power ideal.
