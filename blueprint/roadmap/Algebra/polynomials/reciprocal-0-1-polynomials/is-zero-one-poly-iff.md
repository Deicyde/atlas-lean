# Characterization of 0,1-polynomials

**Characterization of 0,1-polynomials.** A polynomial $f \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ is a
$0,1$-polynomial if and only if, for every $n \ge 0$, the coefficient of $x^n$ in $f$ is
$0$ or $1$.
