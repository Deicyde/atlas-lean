# Corollary (Lecture 11, Section: Review: Hilbert's Nullstelensatz)

The maximal ideals in $\mathbb{C}[x_1, \cdots, x_n]/(P_1, \cdots, P_m)$ are in bijection with the common zeroes of $P_1$, \ldots, $P_m$.
