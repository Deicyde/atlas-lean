# Corollary (Corollary \ref{primitive})

If $P, Q \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ are such that $P$ divides $Q$ in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ and $P$ is primitive, then $P$ divides $Q$ in $\mathbb{Z}[x]$.
