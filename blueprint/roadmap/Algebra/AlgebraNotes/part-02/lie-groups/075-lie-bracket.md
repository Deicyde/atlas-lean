# Definition (Lie Bracket)

Let the Lie bracket of $A, B \in \operatorname{Mat}_{n \times n}(\mathbb{R})$ be $[A, B] \coloneqq AB - BA \in \operatorname{Mat}_{n \times n}(\mathbb{R})$.
