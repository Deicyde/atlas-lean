# Definition (Section: Lie Groups, Subsection: Lie Bracket)

A **Lie algebra** (over $\mathbb{R}$) is a vector space $V$ with a Lie bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]: V \times V \to V$ satisfying $[A, B] = -[B, A]$ and the Jacobi identity.
