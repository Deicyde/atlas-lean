# Theorem (Main Theorem)

Let $G$ be a finite group. Then: (a) The characters of irreducible representations form a basis in the space of class functions on $G$. (b) This basis is orthonormal with respect to the Hermitian form on the space of class functions given by $\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} f_1(g)\overline{f_2(g)}.$ (c) If $d_1$, \ldots, $d_m$ are the dimensions of the irreducible representations of $G$, then $d_1^2 + d_2^2 + \cdots + d_n^2 = |G|,$ and each $d_i$ divides $|G|$.
