# Definition (Sylow $p$-subgroup)

Let $G$ be a group such that $|G| = n = p^e m$ such that $\gcd(p, m) = 1.$ Then a subgroup $H \leq G$ such that $|H| = p^e$ is called a Sylow $p$-subgroup.
