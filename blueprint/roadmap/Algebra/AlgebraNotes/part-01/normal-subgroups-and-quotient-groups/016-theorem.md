# Theorem (Section: Normal Subgroups and Quotient Groups, Quotient Groups subsection)

The following two statements are true about the quotient group: (1) The composition law, as defined in the definition of the quotient group, does define a group structure on $G/N$ (all the group axioms hold). (2) There exists a surjective homomorphism $\pi: G \rightarrow G/N$ defined by $x \mapsto [xN]$ such that $\ker(\pi) = N.$
