# Definition (Section 3, Subsection 3.2 (Homomorphisms))

Given groups $G$ and $G'$, a homomorphism between them is a map $f: G \to G'$ such that: (1) For all $a, b \in G,$ $f(ab) = f(a)f(b)$. (2) The identity element is mapped to the identity: $f(e_G) = e_{G'}.$ (3) Inverses are preserved under the mapping: $f(a)^{-1}=f(a^{-1})$ for all $a \in G.$
