# Is Divided Power Ideal maximal Ideal iff

The maximal ideal of a discrete valuation ring `O` with `p O = 𝔪 ^ e`, `e ≥ 1`, is a
divided-power ideal if and only if the ramification degree `e` is at most `p - 1`.
