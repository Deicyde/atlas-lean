# P Equiv Class

The `p`-equivalence class `C_λ` of the multiset of parts `s`: the partitions `μ` of `|s|`
with `μ ∼_p s`, where `∼_p` (`p`-equivalence) is the equivalence relation generated by
`p`-splitting.
