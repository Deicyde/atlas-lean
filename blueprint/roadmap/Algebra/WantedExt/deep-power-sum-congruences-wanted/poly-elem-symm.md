# Poly Elem Symm

The paper's `e_n(P)` for a monic polynomial `P` of degree `d`: `1` for `n = 0`, the
`X ^ (d - n)`-coefficient times `(-1) ^ n` for `1 ≤ n ≤ d`, and `0` for `n > d`.
