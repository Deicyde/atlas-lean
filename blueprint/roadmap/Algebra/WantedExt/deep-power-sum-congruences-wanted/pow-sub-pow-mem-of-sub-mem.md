# Pow sub pow mem of sub mem

In a `ℤ_(p)`-algebra with divided-power ideal `𝔞`, `x ≡ y mod 𝔞` implies
`x ^ (p ^ m) ≡ y ^ (p ^ m) mod p ^ m 𝔞` for all `m` and `xⁿ ≡ yⁿ mod n𝔞` for all `n`.
Torsion-freeness is not needed.
