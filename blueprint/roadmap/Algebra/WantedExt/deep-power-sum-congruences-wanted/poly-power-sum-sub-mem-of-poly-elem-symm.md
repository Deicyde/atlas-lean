# Poly Power Sum sub mem of poly Elem Symm sub mem

Let `A` be a torsion-free `ℤ_(p)`-algebra, `𝔞` a divided-power ideal, `P, Q` monic and
`N ≥ 1`. If `e_n(P) ≡ e_n(Q) mod 𝔞` for `1 ≤ n ≤ N`, then `p_N(P) ≡ p_N(Q) mod N𝔞`.
