# Is Divided Power Ideal

An ideal `I` of a commutative ring is a *divided-power ideal* when `a ^ k ∈ k! • I` for every
`a ∈ I` and every `k ≥ 1`; in a torsion-free `ℤ_(p)`-algebra this says `a ^ k / k! ∈ I`.
