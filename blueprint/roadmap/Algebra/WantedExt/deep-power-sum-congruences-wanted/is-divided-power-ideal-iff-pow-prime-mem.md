# Is Divided Power Ideal iff pow prime mem

In a `ℤ_(p)`-algebra, an ideal is a divided-power ideal if and only if `a ^ p ∈ p 𝔞` for every
`a ∈ 𝔞`. The paper also assumes `A` torsion-free; the statement does not need it.
