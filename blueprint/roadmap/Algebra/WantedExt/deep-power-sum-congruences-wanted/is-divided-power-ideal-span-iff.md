# Is Divided Power Ideal span iff

In a `ℤ_(p)`-algebra, the ideal generated by `S` is a divided-power ideal if and only if
`a ^ p ∈ p 𝔞` for every generator `a ∈ S`. Torsion-freeness is not needed.
