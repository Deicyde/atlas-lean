# Chebyshev CPartial Sum

The polynomial `F_n(X) = 1 + ∑_{k=1}^n T̄_k(X)`, where `T̄_k` is the monic
Chebyshev polynomial (`Polynomial.Chebyshev.C`); equivalently `F_0 = 1` and
`F_n = F_{n-1} + T̄_n`.
