# Kassel Reutenauer Poly sq sub dvd

For coprime positive `m, n`, `P̄_{mn}(X)² - P̄_m(X)² P̄_n(X)²` is divisible
by `X (X + 1) (X² - 4)`, and moreover by `X (X + 1) (X² - 4) (X - 1)` when
`(m, n) ≡ (0,1), (1,1)` or `(1,2) (mod 3)`.
