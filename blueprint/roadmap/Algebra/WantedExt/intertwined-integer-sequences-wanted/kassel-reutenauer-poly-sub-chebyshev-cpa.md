# Kassel Reutenauer Poly sub chebyshev CPartial Sum coeff

Approximation theorem: for `n ≥ 2`, `P̄_n(X) - F_{n-1}(X)` has degree
`< n/2 - 1`, i.e. every nonzero coefficient sits in a degree `k` with
`2k + 2 < n`.
