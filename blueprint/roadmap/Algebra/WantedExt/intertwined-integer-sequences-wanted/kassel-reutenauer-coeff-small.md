# Small values of the coefficients $a_{n,i}$

**Small values of the coefficients $a_{n,i}$.** The Kassel–Reutenauer coefficients satisfy
$a_{1,0} = 1$, $a_{2,0} = a_{2,1} = 1$, $a_{3,0} = 0$ and $a_{3,1} = a_{3,2} = 1$.
