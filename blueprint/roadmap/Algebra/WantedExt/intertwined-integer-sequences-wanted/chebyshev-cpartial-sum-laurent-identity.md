# Chebyshev CPartial Sum laurent identity

For `r ≥ 0`, `q^{r+1} + q^{-r-1} - q^r - q^{-r} = q⁻¹ (q - 1)² F_r(q + q⁻¹)`
in `ℤ[T;T⁻¹]` (`q = T 1`).
