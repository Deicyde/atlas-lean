# Non-cyclotomic polynomial

**Non-cyclotomic polynomial.** A polynomial $f \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ is *non-cyclotomic* if
$f \ne \Phi_n$ for every positive integer $n$, where $\Phi_n$ denotes the $n$-th cyclotomic
polynomial.
