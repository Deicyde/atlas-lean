# Characterization of reciprocal polynomials

**Characterization of reciprocal polynomials.** A polynomial $f \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ of
degree $d$ (with $d = 0$ for the zero polynomial) is reciprocal if and only if its reversal
$x^d f(1/x)$ equals $f$.
