# Perron irreducible

Perron's irreducibility criterion: a monic `f = x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + ⋯ + a_0 ∈ ℤ[x]`
of degree `n ≥ 1` with `a_0 ≠ 0` and `|a_{n-1}| > 1 + |a_{n-2}| + ⋯ + |a_0|` is irreducible.
