# Arith Prog Poly floor lam pow lt

For `m ≥ 2`, positive integers `a`, `d` with `2a > (m + 1)d`, and `λ` the positive solution
of equation (3): `⌊a λ^p⌋ < a_{p+1} = a + pd` for every `1 ≤ p ≤ m - 1`.
