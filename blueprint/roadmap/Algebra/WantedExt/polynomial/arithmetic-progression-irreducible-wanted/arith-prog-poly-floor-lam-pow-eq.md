# Arith Prog Poly floor lam pow eq

For `m ≥ 2`, positive integers `a`, `d`, `1 ≤ p ≤ m - 1` with `2a > p(m - p)d²`, and `λ` the
positive solution of equation (3): `⌊a λ^p⌋ = a_{p+1} - 1 = a + pd - 1`.
