# Arith Prog Poly floor lam pow eq of eight

For `m ≥ 2` and positive integers `a`, `d` with `8a > m²d²`, and `λ` the positive solution
of equation (3): `⌊a λ^p⌋ = a + pd - 1` for every `1 ≤ p ≤ m - 1`.
