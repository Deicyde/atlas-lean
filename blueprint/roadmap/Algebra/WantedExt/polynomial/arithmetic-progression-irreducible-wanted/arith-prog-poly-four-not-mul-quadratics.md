# Arith Prog Poly four not mul quadratics

For coprime positive integers `a ≥ d`, the quartic
`A_4(x) = a x^4 + (a + d) x^3 + (a + 2d) x^2 + (a + 3d) x + (a + 4d)` is not a product of two
quadratic polynomials in `ℤ[x]`.
