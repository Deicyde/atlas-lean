# Are Poly Farey Neighbors iff

Theorem 2: two fractions `u / v`, `h / k` of `𝓕ₙ` are neighbours if and only if
`deg (u k - h v) = 0`. Stated over any field (the paper, quoting [6], works over
`𝔽_{p^r}`).
