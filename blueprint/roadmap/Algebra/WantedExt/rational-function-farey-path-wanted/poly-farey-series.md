# Poly Farey Series

The Farey series `𝓕ₙ` of order `n` over `F`: pairs `(u, v)` of polynomials,
read as the fraction `u / v`, with `deg u < deg v ≤ n`, `u` and `v` coprime and
`v` monic (so each fraction has exactly one such pair).
