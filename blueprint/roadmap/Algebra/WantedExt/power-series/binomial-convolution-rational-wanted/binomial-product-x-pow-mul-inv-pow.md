# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4.** `xʲ / (1 - αx)^{j+1} ⊙ xᵏ / (1 - βx)^{k+1} =
binom(j + k, j) x^{j+k} / (1 - (α + β)x)^{j+k+1}`.
