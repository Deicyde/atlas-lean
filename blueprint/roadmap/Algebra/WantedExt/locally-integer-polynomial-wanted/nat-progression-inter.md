# Lemma 8

**Lemma 8.** For `d₁, d₂ > 0`, the progressions `a₁ + d₁ℤ_{≥0}` and `a₂ + d₂ℤ_{≥0}` in `ℕ`
intersect iff `gcd(d₁, d₂) ∣ a₁ - a₂`, and a nonempty intersection is `a₃ + lcm(d₁, d₂)ℤ_{≥0}`.
