# Theorem 9

**Theorem 9.** Let `U = ⋃_α U_α ⊆ ℤ` be such that for all `α₁, α₂` and distinct
`a₁ ∈ U_{α₁}`, `a₂ ∈ U_{α₂}`, the set `U_{α₁} ∩ U_{α₂}` contains infinitely many terms of
`a₁ + (a₂ - a₁)ℤ`. Then `f` is LIP on `U` iff it is LIP on every `U_α`.
