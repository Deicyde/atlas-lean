# Corollary 6

**Corollary 6.** Let `U₁ = a₁ + d₁ℤ_{≥0}` and `U₂ = a₂ + d₂ℤ_{≥0}` (`d₁, d₂ > 0`) be
intersecting progressions in `ℕ`. If `f` is LIP on `U₁` and on `U₂`, it is LIP on `U₁ ∪ U₂`.
