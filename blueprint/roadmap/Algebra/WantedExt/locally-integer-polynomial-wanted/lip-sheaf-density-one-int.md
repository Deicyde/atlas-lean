# Corollary 4

**Corollary 4** (density one topology on `ℤ`). On `ℤ`, whose nonempty open sets are the sets
`U` with `|{n ∈ U : |n| ≤ x}| / (2x + 1) → 1`, every locally LIP function on a nonempty open
`U` is LIP on `U`.
