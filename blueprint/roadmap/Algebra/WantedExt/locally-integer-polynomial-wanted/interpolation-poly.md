# Interpolation Poly

The interpolation polynomial `f_X ∈ ℚ[x]` of `f : ℤ → ℤ` on a finite set `X ⊆ ℤ`: the
unique rational polynomial of degree `< |X|` agreeing with `f` on `X`.
