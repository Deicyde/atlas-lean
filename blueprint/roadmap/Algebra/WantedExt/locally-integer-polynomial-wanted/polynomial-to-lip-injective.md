# Lemma 1

**Lemma 1** (polynomial part). For infinite `U ⊆ ℤ`, `LIP(U)` contains `ℤ[x]`: evaluation
`ℤ[x] → LIP(U)` is injective. The subring part of Lemma 1 is the construction of `lipSubring`.
