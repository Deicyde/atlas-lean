# Lemma 6

**Lemma 6** (Exchange Formula). For a finite `X ⊆ ℤ` and `a, b ∉ X`,
`f_{X ⊔ {a}} = f_{X ⊔ {b}} + c (a - b) ∏_{z ∈ X} (x - z)`, where `c` is the coefficient of
`x^{|X|+1}` in `f_{X ⊔ {a,b}}`.
