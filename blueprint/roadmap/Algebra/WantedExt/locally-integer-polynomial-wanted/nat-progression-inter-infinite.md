# Lemma 9

**Lemma 9.** If `a ∈ a₁ + d₁ℤ_{≥0}` and `b ∈ a₂ + d₂ℤ_{≥0}` (`d₁, d₂ > 0`), the two
progressions intersect, and `a ≠ b`, then their intersection meets the progression
`min(a,b) + |b - a| ℤ_{≥0}` generated by `a` and `b` in infinitely many points.
