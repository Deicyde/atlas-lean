# Cyclic Holomorph

The holomorph `G = A ⋊ A^×` of `A = ℤ/nℤ`; the element `(a, u)` is
`⟨Multiplicative.ofAdd a, u⟩`, with product `(a, u)(b, v) = (a + u b, u v)`.
