# Sum neg one pow choose mul pow

For complex `x, y, k` and positive integers `n, m`,
`∑_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i C(n, i) (kx + iy)^m` equals `(-1)^n y^n n!` when `n = m` and `0` when
`n > m`.
