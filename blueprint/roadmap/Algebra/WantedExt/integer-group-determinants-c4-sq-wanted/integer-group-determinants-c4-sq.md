# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1.** `S(C₄²) = {16m + 1, 2^15 p (2m + 1), 2^16 m | m ∈ ℤ, p ∈ P} ∪ A`, and
`A ⊊ {16m - 7 | m ∈ ℤ}`.
