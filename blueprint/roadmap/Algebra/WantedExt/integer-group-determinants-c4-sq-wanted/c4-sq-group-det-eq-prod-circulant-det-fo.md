# Corollary 2

**Corollary 2.** For complex variables `y`, the `C₄²` group determinant factors as
`∏_{k=0}^{3} D₄(∑_s i^{ks} y_{4s}, ∑_s i^{ks} y_{1+4s}, ∑_s i^{ks} y_{2+4s}, ∑_s i^{ks} y_{3+4s})`.
