# Lemma 10

**Lemma 10.** If `b₀ + b₂ ≢ b₁ + b₃ (mod 2)`, then
`D₄(b) D₄(c) ≡ 1 - 4(b₀b₂ + b₁b₃ + c₀c₂ + c₁c₃) (mod 16)`.
