# Lemma 16

**Lemma 16.** Powers of `2` dividing `β β̄ γ γ̄` according to the parities of
`b₀ + b₂` and `b₁ + b₃`, with
`d = {(d₀+d₂)(d₅+d₇) + (d₄+d₆)(d₁+d₃)}{(d₀-d₂)(d₁-d₃) + (d₄-d₆)(d₅-d₇)}` in the odd case.
