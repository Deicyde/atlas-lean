# Lemma 14

**Lemma 14.** `S(C₄²) ∩ 2ℤ ⊆ 2^15 ℤ` and
`S(C₄²) ∩ 2^15 ℤ_odd ⊆ {2^15 p (2m + 1) | p ∈ P, m ∈ ℤ}`.
