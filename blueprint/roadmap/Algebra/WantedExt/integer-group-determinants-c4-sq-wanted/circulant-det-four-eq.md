# Corollary 1

**Corollary 1.** `D₄(x₀, x₁, x₂, x₃) = {(x₀ + x₂)² - (x₁ + x₃)²}{(x₀ - x₂)² + (x₁ - x₃)²}`
over any commutative ring. The paper's intermediate factorization through `D₂` is
omitted.
