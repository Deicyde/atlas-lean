# Lemma 9

**Lemma 9.** If `b₀ + b₂ ≢ b₁ + b₃ (mod 2)`, the residues of
`(b₀b₂ + b₁b₃, c₀c₂ + c₁c₃) (mod 4)` determine the shape of `D₄(b) D₄(c)`.
