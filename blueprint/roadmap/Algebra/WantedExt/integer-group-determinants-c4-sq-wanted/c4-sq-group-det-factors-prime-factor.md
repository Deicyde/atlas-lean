# Lemma 19

**Lemma 19.** If `b₀ + b₂ ≡ b₁ + b₃ ≡ 1 (mod 2)`, `D₄(b) D₄(c) ∈ 2^8 ℤ_odd` and
`β β̄ γ γ̄ ∈ 2^7 ℤ_odd`, then `D₄(b) D₄(c) β β̄ γ γ̄ = 2^15 p (2m + 1)` with `p ∈ P`.
