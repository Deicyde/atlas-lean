# Lemma 11

**Lemma 11.** If `b₀ + b₂ ≢ b₁ + b₃ (mod 2)` and `β β̄ ≡ -3 (mod 8)`, then
`D₄(b) D₄(c) = (8k - 3)(8l - 3)` with `8l - 3 ∈ P`, where `k ≢ l` or `k ≡ l (mod 2)`
according as `b₀b₂ + b₁b₃ + c₀c₂ + c₁c₃ ≡ 0` or `2 (mod 4)`.
