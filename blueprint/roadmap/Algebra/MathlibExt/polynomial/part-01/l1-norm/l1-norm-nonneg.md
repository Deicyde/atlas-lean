# Nonnegativity of the L1 norm

**Nonnegativity of the L1 norm.** Let $K$ be a semiring and $v$ a real-valued absolute value
on $K$. For every polynomial $p \in K[X]$, the L1 norm of $p$ with respect to $v$ satisfies
$\|p\|_v \ge 0$.
