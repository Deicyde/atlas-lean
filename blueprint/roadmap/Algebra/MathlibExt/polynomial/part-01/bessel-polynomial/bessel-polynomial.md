# Bessel polynomial

**Bessel polynomial.** For a natural number $n$, the $n$-th classical Bessel polynomial
$y_n$ is the polynomial with rational coefficients
$y_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{(n+k)!}{2^k \, k! \, (n-k)!} \, x^k$.
