# Inversion on basis elements

**Inversion on basis elements.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $G$ a commutative group.
For every $g \in G$ and $r \in R$, the inversion of the group ring $R[G]$ sends $r\, g$ to
$r\, g^{-1}$.
