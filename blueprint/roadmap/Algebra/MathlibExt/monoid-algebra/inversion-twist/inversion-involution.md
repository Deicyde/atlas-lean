# Group-ring inversion is an involution

**Group-ring inversion is an involution.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $G$ a commutative
group and $\iota$ the inversion of the group ring $R[G]$. For every $x \in R[G]$,
$\iota(\iota(x)) = x$.
