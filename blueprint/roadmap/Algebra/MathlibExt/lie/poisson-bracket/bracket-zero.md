# Bracket with zero

**Bracket with zero.** Let $A$ be a commutative $\mathbb{C}$-algebra with a Poisson bracket
$\lbrace \cdot, \cdot \rbrace$. For every $a \in A$, $\lbrace a, 0 \rbrace = 0$.
