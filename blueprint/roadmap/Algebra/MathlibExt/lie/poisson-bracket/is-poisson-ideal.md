# Poisson ideal

**Poisson ideal.** Let $A$ be a commutative $\mathbb{C}$-algebra with a Poisson bracket
$\lbrace \cdot, \cdot \rbrace$. An ideal $P$ of $A$ is a Poisson ideal when
$\lbrace a, p \rbrace \in P$ for all $a \in A$ and all $p \in P$.
