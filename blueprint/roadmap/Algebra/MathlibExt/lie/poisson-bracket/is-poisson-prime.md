# Poisson prime ideal

**Poisson prime ideal.** Let $A$ be a commutative $\mathbb{C}$-algebra with a Poisson bracket.
An ideal $P$ of $A$ is a Poisson prime when it is both a prime ideal and a Poisson ideal.
