# Jacobson's commutativity theorem

**Jacobson's commutativity theorem.** Let $R$ be a ring. If for every $x \in R$ there is a
natural number $n > 1$ with $x^n = x$, then $R$ is commutative, that is, $xy = yx$ for all
$x, y \in R$.
