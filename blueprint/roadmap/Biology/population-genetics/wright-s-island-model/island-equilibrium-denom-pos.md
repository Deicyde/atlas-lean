# Positivity of the island-model equilibrium denominator

**Positivity of the island-model equilibrium denominator.** The denominator `M - (M - 1) (1 - m) ^ 2` of the equilibrium is positive (at least `1`) for
`M ≥ 1` and `0 ≤ m ≤ 1`.
