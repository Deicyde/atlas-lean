# Island-model contraction factor lies in (0, 1)

**Island-model contraction factor lies in [0, 1).** For `M ≥ 1` and `0 ≤ m ≤ 1`, the per-generation contraction factor
`(1 - m) ^ 2 (1 - 1 / M)` lies in `[0, 1)`.
