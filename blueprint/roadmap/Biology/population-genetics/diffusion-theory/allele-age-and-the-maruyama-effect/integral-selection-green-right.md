# Sojourn density from frequency one vanishes

**Sojourn density from frequency one vanishes.** Boundary value at `1`: a fixed allele spends no time in `(0, 1)`, so
`∫₀¹ Ψ(1, x) g(x) dx = 0` for every `g`.
