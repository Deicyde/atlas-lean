# Stronger selection, smaller `Ψ₂`

**Stronger selection, smaller `Ψ₂`.** For `0 ≤ σ < τ` and `0 < x < z < 1`,
`Ψ₂` at `τ` is strictly below `Ψ₂` at `σ`.
