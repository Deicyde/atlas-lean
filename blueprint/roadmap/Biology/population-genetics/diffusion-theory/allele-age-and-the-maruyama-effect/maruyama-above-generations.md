# Paper units

**Paper units.** The paper's
`Ψ₂(z) = (e^{-γ(1-z)} - 1)(e^{γ(1-z)} - 1)(1 - e^{γx}) / (s z (1 - z)(e^{-γ(1-x)} - 1)(1 - e^{γ}))`,
`γ = 4Ns`, equals `2N · maruyamaAbove (2Ns) x z`.
