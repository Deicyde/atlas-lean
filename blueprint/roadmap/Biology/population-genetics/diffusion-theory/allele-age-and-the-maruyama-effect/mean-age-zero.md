# Kimura and Ohta (1973)

**Kimura and Ohta (1973).** The mean age of a neutral allele at frequency `x ∈ (0, 1)` is
`-2 x log x / (1 - x)` in units of `2N` generations (`-4N x ln x / (1 - x)` generations).
