# Sojourn density from frequency zero vanishes

**Sojourn density from frequency zero vanishes.** Boundary value at `0`: an absent allele spends no time in `(0, 1)`, so
`∫₀¹ Ψ(0, x) g(x) dx = 0` for every `g`.
