# Slope constant of Kimura's fixation probability

**Slope constant of Kimura's fixation probability.** The constant `K σ` such that `(kimuraFixation σ)' x = K σ · e^{-2σx}`:
`1` if `σ = 0`, and `2σ / (1 - e^{-2σ})` otherwise.
