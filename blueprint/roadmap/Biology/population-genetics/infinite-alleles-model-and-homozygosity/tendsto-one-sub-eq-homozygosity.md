# Limit $\theta/(1+\theta)$ of the equilibrium heterozygosity

**Limit $\theta/(1+\theta)$ of the equilibrium heterozygosity.** The equilibrium heterozygosity `H* = 1 - F*` tends to `θ/(1 + θ)` as `N → ∞` with
`M = 2N` and `u = θ/(4N)`.
