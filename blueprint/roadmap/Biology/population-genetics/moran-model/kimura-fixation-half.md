# Closed form of Kimura's fixation probability

**Closed form of Kimura's fixation probability.** Kimura's diffusion fixation probability at scaled selection `σ / 2` is
`(1 - e^{-σ x}) / (1 - e^{-σ})` (for `σ ≠ 0`).
