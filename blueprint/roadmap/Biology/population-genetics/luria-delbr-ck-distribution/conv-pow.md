# Convolution power of a sequence on the naturals

**Convolution power of a sequence on the naturals.** The `k`-fold convolution power of a sequence on `ℕ`: `p^{*0} = δ₀` and
`p^{*(k+1)}(n) = ∑_{i ≤ n} p(i) p^{*k}(n - i)`.
