# Total merger rate of a Λ-coalescent

**Total merger rate of a Λ-coalescent.** The **total merger rate** `λ_b = ∑_{k=2}^b (b.choose k) λ_{b,k}` while there are `b`
blocks (Pitman 1999; Berestycki 2009, §3.1).
