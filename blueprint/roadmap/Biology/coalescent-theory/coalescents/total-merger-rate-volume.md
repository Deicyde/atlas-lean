# Total merger rate of the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent

**Total merger rate of the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent.** For the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent the total merger rate is `λ_b = b - 1` (`b ≥ 1`).
