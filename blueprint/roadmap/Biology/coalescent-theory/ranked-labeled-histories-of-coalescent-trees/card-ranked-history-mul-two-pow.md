# Number of ranked labeled histories

**Number of ranked labeled histories**: `#histories * 2^{n-1} = n! (n - 1)!`, i.e. there are
`n! (n - 1)! / 2^{n-1}` ranked labeled histories of `n` labeled genes (Edwards 1970).
