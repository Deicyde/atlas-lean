# Tajima's numerator has mean zero

**Tajima's numerator has mean zero** (Tajima 1989): under the hypotheses of
`integral_meanPairDiff` and Watterson's (`E[S | m] = (θ/2) L`),
`E[π - S / a₁] = 0` for a sample of size `n ≥ 2`.
