# Weighted sum of reciprocals of $\binom{k}{2}$ is twice a harmonic number

**Weighted sum of reciprocals of $\binom{k}{2}$ is twice a harmonic number.** `∑_{k=2}^n k (k.choose 2)⁻¹ = 2 H_{n-1}`.
