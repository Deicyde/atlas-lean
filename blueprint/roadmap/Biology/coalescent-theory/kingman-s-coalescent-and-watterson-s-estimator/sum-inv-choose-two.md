# Sum of reciprocals of binomial coefficients $\binom{k}{2}$

**Sum of reciprocals of binomial coefficients $\binom{k}{2}$.** `∑_{k=2}^n (k.choose 2)⁻¹ = 2 (1 - 1/n)` for `n ≥ 1` (telescoping `2/(k(k-1))`).
