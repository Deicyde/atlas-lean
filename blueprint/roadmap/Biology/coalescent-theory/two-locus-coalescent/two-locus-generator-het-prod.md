# Hill–Robertson / Ohta–Kimura, third row

**Hill–Robertson / Ohta–Kimura, third row.** On the simplex,
`L Π = D z - (2 + 8ν) Π + ν (p (1 - p) + q (1 - q))`; for `ν = 0` the three rows close.
