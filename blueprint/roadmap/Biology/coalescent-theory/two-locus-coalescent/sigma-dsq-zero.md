# Ohta–Kimura formula for $\sigma_d^2$ without mutation

**Ohta–Kimura formula for $\sigma_d^2$ without mutation.** The low-mutation value `σ_d² = (10 + ρ) / (22 + 13ρ + ρ²)` (Ohta & Kimura 1971).
