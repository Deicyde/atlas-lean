# Two-locus covariance is at most one

**Two-locus covariance is at most one.** The covariance is at most `Var T_A = 1` for `ρ ≥ 0` (outside this domain the formula can
exceed `1`; e.g. `twoLocusCov (-1) = 17 / 6`).
