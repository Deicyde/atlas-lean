# Ohta–Kimura `σ_d²`

**Ohta–Kimura `σ_d²`.** For a stationary law with `ρ, ν ≥ 0` and `E Π > 0`,
`E D² / E[p (1 - p) q (1 - q)] = sigmaDSq ρ ν`.
