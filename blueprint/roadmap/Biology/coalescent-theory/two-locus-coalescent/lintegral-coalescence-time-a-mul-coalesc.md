# Product moment of the two coalescence times, path form

**Product moment of the two coalescence times, path form** (Hudson 1983; Kaplan & Hudson
1985): from two linked sequences, `E (T_A T_B) = 1 + (ρ + 18) / (ρ² + 13ρ + 18)`.
