# Low-mutation limit of $\sigma_d^2$

**Low-mutation limit of $\sigma_d^2$.** `σ_d² → (10 + ρ) / (22 + 13ρ + ρ²)` as the mutation rate tends to `0`, for `ρ ≥ 0`.
