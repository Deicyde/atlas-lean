# Coordinate projections form a skyline model

The coordinate projections of the product of the exponential laws `Exp(C(k, 2) / θ k)`,
`2 ≤ k ≤ n` (see [Canonical probability space for the skyline model](skyline-pi-measure.md)),
form a skyline model whenever the `θ k` are positive; in particular, a skyline model with these
parameters exists (see [Coalescent intervals under the classic skyline model](skyline-times.md)).
