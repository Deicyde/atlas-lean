# Three-anomaly boundary is maximal at zero

**Three-anomaly boundary is maximal at zero.** `b` is maximal at `0`: `b(x) ≤ log (7/6)` for `x ≥ 0`, so for `y ≥ log (7/6)` at most one
gene tree is anomalous.
