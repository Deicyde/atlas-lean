# Balanced four-species tree

**Balanced four-species tree.** The symmetric (balanced) species tree `((0, 1), (2, 3))` with internal branch lengths `y₁`
(above the ancestor of `0, 1`) and `y₂` (above the ancestor of `2, 3`).
