# Positivity of the Griffiths–Tavaré kernel on the diagonal

**Positivity of the Griffiths–Tavaré kernel on the diagonal.** The kernel entry at `k = n - i + 1` is `(n - i + 1) / C(n-1, i-1) > 0`.
