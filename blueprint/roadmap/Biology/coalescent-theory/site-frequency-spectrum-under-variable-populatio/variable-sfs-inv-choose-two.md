# Variable-size spectrum reduces to Fu's $\theta/i$ at constant size

**Variable-size spectrum reduces to Fu's $\theta/i$ at constant size.** With the constant-size means `E T_k = 1 / C(k,2)`, the variable-size SFS is Fu's
`θ / i`.
