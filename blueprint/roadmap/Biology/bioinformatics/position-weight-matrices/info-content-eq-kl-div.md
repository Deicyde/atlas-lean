# Information content is a Kullback–Leibler divergence

**Information content is a Kullback–Leibler divergence**: for laws `P ≪ Q`, the information
content of `P` against `Q` is `KL(P ‖ Q) / log 2`, Mathlib's `klDiv` in bits.
