# Minimal speed of Fisher–KPP traveling fronts

**Minimal speed of Fisher–KPP traveling fronts.** A front of speed `c` needs `c² ≥ 4 r D`; for `c ≥ 0` this is `c ≥ 2 √(r D)`.
