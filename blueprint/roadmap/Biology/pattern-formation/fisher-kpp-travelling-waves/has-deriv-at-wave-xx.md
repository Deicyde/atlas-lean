# Traveling wave solves the Fisher–KPP equation

**Traveling wave solves the Fisher–KPP equation.** Second space derivative of the wave; with `hD : D ≠ 0` it gives the PDE
`-c U' = D U'' + r U (1 - U)`, i.e. `∂u/∂t = D ∂²u/∂x² + r u (1 - u)`.
