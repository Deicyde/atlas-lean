# SLDP first-moment identity

**SLDP first-moment identity** (Reshef et al. 2018, Methods, Equation 5): if the true effects
have mean `μ v`, then `𝔼 α̂ = μ R̂ v`, a multiple of the signed LD profile `R̂ v` of `v`.
