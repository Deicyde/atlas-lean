# Residual with respect to an orthonormal family of axes

**Residual with respect to an orthonormal family of axes.** Regressing on an orthonormal family of axes one axis at a time gives the joint residual:
`y - ∑ₖ ⟪aₖ, y⟫ aₖ`.
