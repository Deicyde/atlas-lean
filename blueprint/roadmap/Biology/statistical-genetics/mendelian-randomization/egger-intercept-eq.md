# MR-Egger intercept under pleiotropy

**MR-Egger intercept under pleiotropy.** The MR-Egger intercept under pleiotropy: it is the weighted mean pleiotropic effect
`ᾱ_v` minus `Cov_v(γ, α) / Var_v(γ) · γ̄_v`.
