# Cochran–Armitage trend statistic

**Cochran–Armitage trend statistic.** The **Cochran–Armitage trend statistic** with scores `0, 1, 2` (Armitage 1955):
`N U² / (R S (N ∑ i² n_i - (∑ i n_i)²))`.
