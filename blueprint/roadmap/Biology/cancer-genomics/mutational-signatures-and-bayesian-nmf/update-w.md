# ARD multiplicative update of W

**ARD multiplicative update of W.** The `ℓ₁`-ARD multiplicative update of `W` for `β = 1` (Tan–Févotte, Algorithm 2):
`w_fk ← w_fk (∑_n v_fn h_kn / (W H)_fn) / (∑_n h_kn + φ / λ_k)`.
