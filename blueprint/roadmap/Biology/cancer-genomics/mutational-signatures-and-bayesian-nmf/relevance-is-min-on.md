# The relevance update is the exact minimizer

**The relevance update is the exact minimizer** (Tan–Févotte, §IV-C): for `A > 0` and
`c > 0`, `λ ↦ A / λ + c log λ` attains its minimum over `λ > 0` at `λ = A / c`.
