# The `W` update is monotone

**The `W` update is monotone**: it does not increase
`D_1(V | W H) / φ + ∑ w_fk / λ_k`, for `φ > 0`, `λ > 0`, `V ≥ 0`, `H ≥ 0` with no zero column,
and `W > 0` entrywise (the transpose of `objectiveH_updateH_le`).
