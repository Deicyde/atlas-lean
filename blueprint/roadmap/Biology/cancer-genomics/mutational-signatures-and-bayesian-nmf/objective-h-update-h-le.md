# The `H` update is monotone

**The `H` update is monotone** (Tan–Févotte 2013, §IV-B, `β = 1`): it does not increase
`D_1(V | W H) / φ + ∑ h_kn / λ_k`, for `φ > 0`, `λ > 0`, `V ≥ 0`, `W ≥ 0` with no zero row, and
`H > 0` entrywise.
