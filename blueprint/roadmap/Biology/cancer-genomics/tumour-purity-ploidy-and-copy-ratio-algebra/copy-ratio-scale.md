# Ploidy-scaling non-identifiability, copy ratios

**Ploidy-scaling non-identifiability, copy ratios**: scaling every copy number and the
ploidy by `m > 0` and the purity to `α / (m - (m - 1) α)` leaves every copy ratio of Eq. 1
unchanged.
