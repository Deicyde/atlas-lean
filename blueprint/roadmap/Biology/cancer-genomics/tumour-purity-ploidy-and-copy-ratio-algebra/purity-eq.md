# Purity from copy-ratio slope and intercept

**Purity from copy-ratio slope and intercept.** Inverse of Eq. 1, purity: `α = 2 b / (2 b + δ)` for the slope `b` and intercept `δ`, when
`0 < α ≤ 1` and `0 < τ`.
