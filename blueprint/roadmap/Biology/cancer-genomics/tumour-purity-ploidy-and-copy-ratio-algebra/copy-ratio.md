# Relative copy ratio

**Relative copy ratio.** Relative copy ratio `R = (α q + 2 (1 - α)) / (α τ + 2 (1 - α))` of a locus with copy number
`q`, purity `α` and cancer-cell ploidy `τ` (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 1).
