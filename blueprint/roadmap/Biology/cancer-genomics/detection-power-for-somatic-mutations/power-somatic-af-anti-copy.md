# Power decreases with local copy number

**Power decreases with local copy number** at the ABSOLUTE allelic fraction, for a clonal
mutation of multiplicity `0 ≤ s ≤ q ≤ q'` (Carter et al. 2012, Eqs. 2, 9, 10).
