# Power increases with purity

**Power increases with purity** at the ABSOLUTE allelic fraction `α s / (α q + 2 (1 - α))`
of a clonal mutation of multiplicity `0 ≤ s ≤ q` (Carter et al. 2012, Eqs. 2, 9, 10).
