# The continuous CCF MLE

**The continuous CCF MLE**: for `0 < α ≤ 1`, `q ≥ 1`, `a ≤ n` and `0 < n`, the likelihood
`c ↦ Binom(a | n, f(c))` on `[0, 1]` is maximized at `ĉ = min 1 (a / (n κ))`.
