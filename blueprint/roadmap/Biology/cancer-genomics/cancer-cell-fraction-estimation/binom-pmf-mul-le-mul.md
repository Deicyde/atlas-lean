# The binomial family is TP2

**The binomial family is TP2** (monotone likelihood ratio in the success probability):
for `a ≤ a'` and `0 ≤ p ≤ p' ≤ 1`,
`Binom(a | n, p') Binom(a' | n, p) ≤ Binom(a' | n, p') Binom(a | n, p)`.
