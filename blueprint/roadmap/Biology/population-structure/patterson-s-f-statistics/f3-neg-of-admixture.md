# Nondegenerate pure admixture gives negative f3

**Nondegenerate pure admixture gives negative f3.** Three-population test, converse direction: a nondegenerate pure admixture
(`0 < α < 1`, `f2 A B > 0`) yields a negative `f3 C A B`.
