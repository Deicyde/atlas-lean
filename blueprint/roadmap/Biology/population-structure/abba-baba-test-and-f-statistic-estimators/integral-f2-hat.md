# Unbiased `f2`

**Unbiased `f2`** (Patterson et al. 2012, Appendix): with independent binomial samples
`X_A ~ Bin(m_A, p_A)`, `X_B ~ Bin(m_B, p_B)`, `m_A, m_B ≥ 2`,
`E[f2Hat] = (p_A - p_B) ^ 2`, the population `f2` at the site.
