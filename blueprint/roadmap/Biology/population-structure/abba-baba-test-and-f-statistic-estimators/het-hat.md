# Unbiased heterozygosity estimator

**Unbiased heterozygosity estimator.** Unbiased estimator of `p (1 - p)` from `x` derived copies among `m` sampled copies:
`x (m - x) / (m (m - 1))` (meaningful for `m ≥ 2`).
