# Unbiased `f3`

**Unbiased `f3`** (Patterson et al. 2012, Appendix): with pairwise independent binomial
samples, `m_C ≥ 2` and `m_A, m_B ≥ 1`, `E[f3Hat] = (p_C - p_A) (p_C - p_B)`.
