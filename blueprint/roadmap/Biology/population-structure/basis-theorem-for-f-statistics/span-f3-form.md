# Patterson's f3 basis spans the f-statistic space

**Patterson's f3 basis spans the f-statistic space.** Patterson's `f3` basis with base population `o`: the forms `f3Form o i j` with
`i ≤ j`, `i ≠ o`, `j ≠ o` span `V_F`.
