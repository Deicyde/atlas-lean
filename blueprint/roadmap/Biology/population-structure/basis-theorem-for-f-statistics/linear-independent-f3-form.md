# Linear independence of Patterson's f3 basis

**Linear independence of Patterson's f3 basis.** Patterson's `f3` basis with base population `o`: the forms `f3Form o i j` with
`i ≤ j`, `i ≠ o`, `j ≠ o` are linearly independent.
