# Least-squares qpGraph score S2

**Least-squares qpGraph score S2.** The least-squares qpGraph score `S2(g) = -∑ᵢ (gᵢ - fᵢ)² / (Qᵢᵢ + λ)` (Patterson et al.
2012, eq. 7). A term with `Qᵢᵢ + λ = 0` is `0` by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
