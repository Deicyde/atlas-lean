# The FRAPPE EM step

**The FRAPPE EM step** `θ ↦ (Q', F')`, with `Q' = mStepQ` and `F' = mStepF`; it
lies in the strictly positive parameter set when `J` is nonempty and every SNP is
polymorphic.
