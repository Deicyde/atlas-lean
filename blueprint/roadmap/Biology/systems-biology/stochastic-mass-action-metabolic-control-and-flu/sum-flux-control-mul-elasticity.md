# Flux connectivity theorem

**Flux connectivity theorem** (Kacser & Burns 1973): when all steady-state fluxes are
nonzero, `∑_i C^{J_j}_{e_i} ε^{v_i}_{s_m} = 0` for every flux `J_j` and metabolite `s_m`.
