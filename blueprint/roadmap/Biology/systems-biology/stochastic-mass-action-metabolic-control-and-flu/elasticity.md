# Elasticity of a reaction rate

**Elasticity of a reaction rate.** The elasticity `ε^{v_i}_{s_m} = (s_m / v_i) ∂v_i/∂s_m`, meaningful for a nonzero rate
`v_i` and a rate law differentiable at `s`.
