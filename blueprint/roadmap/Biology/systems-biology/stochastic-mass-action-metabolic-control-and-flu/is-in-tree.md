# Spanning in-tree rooted at a state

**Spanning in-tree rooted at a state.** `t : ι → ι` is a spanning in-tree rooted at `r` (each `i ≠ r` points to its parent
`t i`): `t r = r` and iterating `t` from any state reaches `r`.
