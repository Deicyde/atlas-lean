# Fast transient solves the boundary-layer equation

**Fast transient solves the boundary-layer equation.** The fast transient solves the boundary-layer equation `c' = k₁ (e₀ - c) s̄ - (kb + k₂) c`
for `k₁ > 0` and `K_M + s̄ ≠ 0`.
