# Quasi-steady-state manifold

**Quasi-steady-state manifold.** With total enzyme `e₀` (so `e = e₀ - c`), `k₁ > 0` and
`K_M + s ≠ 0`, the complex is stationary iff `c = e₀ s / (K_M + s)`.
