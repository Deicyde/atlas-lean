# Delayed rise

**Delayed rise.** After `T_on` the FFL target relaxes from `0` towards `β_z/α_z`:
`Z(t) = (β_z/α_z)(1 - e^{-α_z (t - T_on)})` (for `α_z > 0`, `K_xz < 1`).
