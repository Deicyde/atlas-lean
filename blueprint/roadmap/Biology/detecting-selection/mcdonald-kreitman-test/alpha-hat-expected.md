# `α̂` at the expected counts is the proportion of adaptive substitutions

**`α̂` at the expected counts is the proportion of adaptive substitutions**
(Smith & Eyre-Walker 2002): `1 - (E D_s E P_n) / (E D_n E P_s) = A / E D_n`.
