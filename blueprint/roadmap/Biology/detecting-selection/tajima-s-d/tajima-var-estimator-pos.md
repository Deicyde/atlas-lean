# Positivity of the denominator of Tajima's D

**Positivity of the denominator of Tajima's D.** The denominator of Tajima's `D` is well defined: `e₁ S + e₂ S (S - 1) > 0` for `n ≥ 4` and
`S ≥ 1`.
