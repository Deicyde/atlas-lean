# Positivity of Tajima's $c_2$ for at least four genes

**Positivity of Tajima's $c_2$ for at least four genes.** `c₂ = b₂ - (n + 2) / (a₁ n) + a₂ / a₁² > 0` for `n ≥ 4`.
