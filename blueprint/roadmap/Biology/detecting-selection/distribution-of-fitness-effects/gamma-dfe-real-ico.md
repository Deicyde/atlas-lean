# Fraction of mutations in a strength interval under the gamma DFE

**Fraction of mutations in a strength interval under the gamma DFE.** The fraction of new mutations whose strength `-σ` lies in `(c₁, c₂]`, `0 ≤ c₁ ≤ c₂`, is
`(Γ(a, r c₁) - Γ(a, r c₂)) / Γ(a)`.
