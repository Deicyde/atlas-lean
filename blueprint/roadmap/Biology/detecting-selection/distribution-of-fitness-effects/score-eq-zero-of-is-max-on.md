# Score equations

**Score equations.** If `ψ` maximizes the Poisson log-likelihood over a parameter set `S`
that is a neighborhood of `ψ`, and the means are positive and differentiable at `ψ`, then
`∑ (k_i / E_i(ψ) - 1) · DE_i(ψ) = 0`.
