# Rescaling a gamma DFE divides its rate

**Rescaling a gamma DFE divides its rate.** Rescaling a gamma DFE by `c > 0` (for example from `s` to `σ = 2Ns`, `c = 2N`) keeps the
shape and divides the rate by `c`.
