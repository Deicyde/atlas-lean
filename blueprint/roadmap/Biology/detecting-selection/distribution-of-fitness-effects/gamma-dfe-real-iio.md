# Fraction of strongly deleterious mutations

**Fraction of strongly deleterious mutations.** Under the gamma DFE the fraction of new
mutations with `σ < -c`, `c ≥ 0`, is the gamma tail `Γ(a, r c) / Γ(a)`.
