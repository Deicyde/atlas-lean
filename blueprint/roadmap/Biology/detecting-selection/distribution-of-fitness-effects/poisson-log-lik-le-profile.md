# Profile maximum in `θ`

**Profile maximum in `θ`.** With means `E_i = θ f_i` linear in the mutation parameter,
positive `f_i` and at least one count, every `θ > 0` has likelihood at most that of
`θ̂ = ∑ k_i / ∑ f_i`.
