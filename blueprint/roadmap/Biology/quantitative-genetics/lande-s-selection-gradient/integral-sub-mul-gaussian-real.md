# Stein's lemma

**Stein's lemma** for the normal law `N(μ, v)`, `v > 0`: if `g` is differentiable with
derivative `g'` and `g`, `(z - μ) g z` and `g'` are integrable, then
`𝔼[(z - μ) g z] = v 𝔼[g' z]` (Stein 1981, Lemma 1).

This is a special case of the general [Stein's lemma](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/stein-s-lemma-and-james-stein-estimation/integrable-and-integral-sub-mul-gaussian.md), from which it is now proved: the hypotheses $v > 0$ and the integrability of $g(z)$ and $(z - \mu) g(z)$ are not needed, the last being a conclusion of the general statement.
