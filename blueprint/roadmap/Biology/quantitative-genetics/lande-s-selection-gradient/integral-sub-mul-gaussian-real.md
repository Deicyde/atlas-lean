# Stein's lemma

**Stein's lemma** for the normal law `N(μ, v)`, `v > 0`: if `g` is differentiable with
derivative `g'` and `g`, `(z - μ) g z` and `g'` are integrable, then
`𝔼[(z - μ) g z] = v 𝔼[g' z]` (Stein 1981, Lemma 1).
