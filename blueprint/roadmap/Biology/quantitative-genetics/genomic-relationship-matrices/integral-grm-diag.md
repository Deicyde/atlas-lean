# The GCTA diagonal estimator is unbiased for `1 + F_i`

**The GCTA diagonal estimator is unbiased for `1 + F_i`** (Yang et al. 2011):
`𝔼[1 + (x_i² - (1 + 2p) x_i + 2p²) / (2p(1 - p))] = 1 + F_i`.
