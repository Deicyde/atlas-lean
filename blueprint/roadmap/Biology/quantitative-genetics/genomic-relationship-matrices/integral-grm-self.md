# Diagonal GRM entry is unbiased for $1 + F_i$

**Diagonal GRM entry is unbiased for $1 + F_i$.** The diagonal GRM entry is unbiased for `1 + F_i`: `𝔼[A_ii] = 1 + F_i`.
