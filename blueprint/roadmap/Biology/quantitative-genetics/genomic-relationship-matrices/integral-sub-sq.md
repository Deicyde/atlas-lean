# The KING moment

**The KING moment** (Manichaikul et al. 2010), general form:
`𝔼[(x_i - x_j)²] = 2p(1 - p)(1 + F_i) + 2p(1 - p)(1 + F_j) - 8p(1 - p) φ_ij`.
