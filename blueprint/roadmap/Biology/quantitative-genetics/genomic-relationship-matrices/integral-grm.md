# The GRM is unbiased for twice the kinship

**The GRM is unbiased for twice the kinship** (Yang et al. 2010):
`𝔼[A_ij] = 2 φ_ij`, for every pair `i, j` (including `i = j`, where `2 φ_ii = 1 + F_i`).
