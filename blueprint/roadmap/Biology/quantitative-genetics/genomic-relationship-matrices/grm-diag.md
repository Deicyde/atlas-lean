# GCTA diagonal relationship estimator

**GCTA diagonal relationship estimator.** The diagonal estimator of GCTA (Yang et al. 2011) at one locus,
`A_ii = 1 + (x_i² - (1 + 2p) x_i + 2p²) / (2p(1 - p))`.
