# The mean of the marginal estimates

**The mean of the marginal estimates** (Zhu and Stephens 2017): for fixed effects `β` and
noise with mean zero, `𝔼[X_jᵀ y / N] = (R β)_j` with `R = Xᵀ X / N` (`ldCorr`).
