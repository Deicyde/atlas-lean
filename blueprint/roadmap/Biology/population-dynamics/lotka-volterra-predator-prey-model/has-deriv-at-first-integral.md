# Lotka–Volterra first integral

**Lotka–Volterra first integral.** If `x' = α x - β x y` and `y' = δ x y - γ y` at time `t`
and `x t, y t > 0`, then `t ↦ V(x t, y t)` has derivative `0` at `t`.
