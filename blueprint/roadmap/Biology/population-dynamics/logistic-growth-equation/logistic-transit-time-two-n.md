# Logistic transit time from $1/(2N)$ to $1 - 1/(2N)$

**Logistic transit time from $1/(2N)$ to $1 - 1/(2N)$.** The transit time from `1 / (2N)` to `1 - 1 / (2N)` is `(2 / r) log (2N - 1)`, for
`N ≥ 1` and `r ≠ 0`.
