# Positivity of the logistic solution's denominator

**Positivity of the logistic solution's denominator.** In the biological domain (`K > 0`, `x₀ > 0`, `r t ≥ 0`, or `x₀ ≤ K`) the denominator of the
logistic solution is positive.
