# Compatible splits

**Compatible splits.** The splits `A | Aᶜ` and `B | Bᶜ` are **compatible**: one of `A ∩ B`, `A ∩ Bᶜ`, `Aᶜ ∩ B`,
`Aᶜ ∩ Bᶜ` is empty (Semple & Steel 2003, Section 3.1).
