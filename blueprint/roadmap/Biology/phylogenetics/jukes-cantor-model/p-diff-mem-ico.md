# Jukes–Cantor difference probability lies in (0, 3/4)

**Jukes–Cantor difference probability lies in [0, 3/4).** For `0 ≤ μ * t` the probability that the ends of a branch differ lies in `[0, 3/4)`, the
domain of `jcDistance`.
