# HKY85 rate matrix

**HKY85 rate matrix.** The **HKY85 rate matrix** (Hasegawa–Kishino–Yano 1985) with base frequencies `π` and
transition/transversion ratio `κ`: rate `κ π j` for a transition to `j`, `π j` for a
transversion.
