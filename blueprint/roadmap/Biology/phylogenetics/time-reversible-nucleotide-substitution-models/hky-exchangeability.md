# HKY85 exchangeabilities

**HKY85 exchangeabilities.** The HKY85 exchangeabilities: `κ` for a transition (indices of equal parity: `A ↔ G`,
`C ↔ T` in the order `A, C, G, T`), `1` for a transversion. The diagonal (also `κ`) is not
used by `gtrRate`.
