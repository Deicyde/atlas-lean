# Q-criterion of two singleton clusters

**Q-criterion of two singleton clusters.** The Q-criterion of two singleton clusters at the start of neighbor joining:
`Q({x}, {y}) = (|X| - 2) d x y - ∑_z d x z - ∑_z d y z`.
