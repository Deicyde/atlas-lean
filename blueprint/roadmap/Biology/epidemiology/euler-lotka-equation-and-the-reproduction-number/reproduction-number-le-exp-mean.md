# Jensen bound

**Jensen bound**: if the generation interval has finite mean `T̄ = ∫ a g(da)` and finite
`M_g(-r)`, then `R ≤ e^{r T̄}` (any real `r`); Wallinga and Lipsitch (2007, §3) note that the
fixed interval gives this upper bound.
