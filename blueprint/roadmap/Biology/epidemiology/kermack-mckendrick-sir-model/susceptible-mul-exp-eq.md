# Conserved quantity behind the final-size relation

**Conserved quantity behind the final-size relation.** For `γ ≠ 0`, the quantity
`S(t) e^{R₀ R(t) / N}` (`R₀ = β / γ`) is constant along a solution on `[0, ∞)`.
