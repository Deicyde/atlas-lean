# Inverse-temperature map of the Potts model at $q = 2$ is the Ising map

At `q = 2` the Potts inverse temperature `-((q - 1)/q) log (1 - p)` of an FK edge parameter `p`
(see [Potts inverse temperature of an FK edge parameter](p-to-beta.md)) is the Ising inverse
temperature `-(1/2) log (1 - p)` (see
[Ising inverse temperature of an FK edge parameter](../the-ising-model-as-the-two-state-potts-model/beta-of-p.md)).
