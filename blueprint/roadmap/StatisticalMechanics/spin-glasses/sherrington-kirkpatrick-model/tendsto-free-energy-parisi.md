# The Parisi formula for the SK model

For $\beta > 0$ and every field $h$,
$$\lim_{N \to \infty} F_N = \mathcal{P}(\xi, h) = \inf_{k, m, q}\mathcal{P}_k(m, q), \qquad
\xi(x) = \beta^2x^2/2$$
(Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*, Ann. of Math. 163 (2006), Theorem 1.1, which covers every
Gaussian Hamiltonian whose covariance satisfies its (1.3) for a function $\xi$ with (1.5); the
SK model is the case above). The infimum is encoded as a greatest lower bound, so the statement
also asserts that the values of the functional are bounded below. Stated, not yet proved.
