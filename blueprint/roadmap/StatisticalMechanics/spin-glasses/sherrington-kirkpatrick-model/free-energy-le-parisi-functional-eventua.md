# Guerra's replica-symmetry-breaking bound for the SK model

For $\beta > 0$, every field $h$ and all admissible parameters $k, m, q$,
$\limsup_{N\to\infty} F_N \le \mathcal{P}_k(m, q)$; the Lean statement says that for every
$\varepsilon > 0$, eventually $F_N \le \mathcal{P}_k(m, q) + \varepsilon$. Talagrand, *The Parisi
formula*, Ann. of Math. 163 (2006), attributes the bound "left-hand side of (1.14) $\le$
right-hand side" to F. Guerra, *Broken replica symmetry bounds in the mean field spin glass
model*, Comm. Math. Phys. 233 (2003), 1–12, who proved it with an interpolation scheme. Stated, not yet proved.
