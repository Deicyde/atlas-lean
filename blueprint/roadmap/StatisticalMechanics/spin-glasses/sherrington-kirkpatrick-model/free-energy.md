# Free energy of the SK model

$$F_N = \frac1N\,\mathbb{E}\log\sum_{\sigma \in \{-1,1\}^N}\exp\Big(H_N(\sigma) + h\sum_{i \le N}\sigma_i\Big),$$
the expectation over independent standard Gaussian couplings (Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*,
Ann. of Math. 163 (2006), left-hand side of (1.14) before the limit). Following Talagrand's
(1.14), no further minus sign is put in front of the Hamiltonian in the exponent (the one in
(1.1) is part of $H_N$), and $h$ is the external field.
