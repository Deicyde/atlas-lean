# One step of the Parisi recursion

Given a function $f$, a variance $v$ and a parameter $m$,
$$y \mapsto \frac1m\log\mathbb{E}\exp\big(m f(y + z)\big), \quad z \sim N(0, v),$$
and $y \mapsto \mathbb{E} f(y + z)$ when $m = 0$ (Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*, Ann. of Math.
163 (2006), (1.10), written on functions of the partial sum $h + \sum_{p < \ell} z_p$).
