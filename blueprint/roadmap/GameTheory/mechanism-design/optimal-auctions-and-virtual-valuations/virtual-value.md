# Virtual valuation

For the density `f` of values on `[a, b]`, the virtual valuation
`φ(t) = t - (1 - F t) / f t` with `F t = ∫_a^t f` (Myerson 1981, (5.1) with zero revision
effects).

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, (5.1), here with zero revision effects `eᵢ = 0`.
