# VCG truthfulness, pointwise

If the pivot terms do not depend on the agents' own
reports and `f v` has at least the welfare (at the true profile `v`) of the alternative chosen
after agent `i` reports `w`, then agent `i` does not gain by reporting `w` instead of `v i`.

## Informally uses

- [Welfare decomposition](welfare-eq.md) and [the others' welfare ignores the own valuation](others-welfare-update.md): the utility of agent `i` is the social welfare minus a term it does not influence.
