# Individual rationality

If `f v` maximizes the welfare at `v` and all valuations are
nonnegative, then with Clarke pivot payments every agent reporting truthfully gets a nonnegative
truthfulUtility (Nisan 2007, Lemma 9.20, second part).

## Sources

- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, Lemma 9.20.
