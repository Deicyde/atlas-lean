# Dominant-strategy incentive compatibility

The direct mechanism `f`, which maps each
profile of reported types to an outcome, is dominant-strategy incentive compatible
(strategy-proof): truthful reporting is optimal for every agent whatever the others report
(Nisan 2007, Definition 9.15, with payments absorbed into the outcome).

## Sources

- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, Definition 9.15.
