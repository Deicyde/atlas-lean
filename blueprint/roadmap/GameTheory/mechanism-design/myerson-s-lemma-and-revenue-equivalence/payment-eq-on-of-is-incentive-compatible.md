# Revenue equivalence (pointwise)

Two single-parameter mechanisms with the same
allocation rule `Q` that are incentive compatible on `[a, b]` and charge the same payment at the
lowest value `a` charge the same payment at every value in `[a, b]` (the one-bidder form of
Myerson 1981, Corollary;
Nisan 2007, Theorem 9.46).

## Informally uses

- [Payment formula](payment-formula.md): the payment depends only on the allocation and on the payment of the lowest value.

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, Corollary (the revenue-equivalence theorem), stated there for the seller's expected utility with several bidders.
- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, Theorem 9.46.
