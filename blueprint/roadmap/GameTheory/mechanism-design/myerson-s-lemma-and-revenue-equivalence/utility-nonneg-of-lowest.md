# Individual rationality from the lowest type

If `(Q, P)` is incentive compatible on
`[a, b]`, `Q ≥ 0` on `[a, b]` and the lowest value gets `U a ≥ 0`, then every value gets a
nonnegative truthfulUtility (Myerson 1981, proof of Lemma 2: (3.4) follows from (4.3) and (4.4)).

## Informally uses

- [Envelope formula](envelope-formula.md): the utility exceeds that of the lowest value by the integral of `Q ≥ 0`.

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, proof of Lemma 2.
