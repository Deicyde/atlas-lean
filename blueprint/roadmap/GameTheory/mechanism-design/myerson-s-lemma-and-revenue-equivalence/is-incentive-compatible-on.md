# Incentive compatibility on an interval

The single-parameter mechanism `(Q, P)` is
incentive compatible on `[a, b]`: for all values `s, t ∈ [a, b]`, an agent of value `t` does
not gain by reporting `s`, that is `t * Q s - P s ≤ t * Q t - P t` (Myerson 1981, (3.5) without
revision effects; Hartline and Karlin 2007, Definition 13.5).

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, (3.5).
- J. D. Hartline and A. R. Karlin, "Profit maximization in mechanism design", Chapter 13 of the same volume, Definition 13.5.
