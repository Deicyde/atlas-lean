# Payment formula

In an incentive-compatible single-parameter mechanism on `[a, b]`,
the payment is determined by the allocation rule and the utility of the lowest value:
`P t = t * Q t - ∫_a^t Q - U a` (Myerson 1981, (4.8) with `U a = 0`; Hartline and Karlin 2007,
Theorem 13.6 (ii), where `a = 0` and `P 0 = 0`).

## Informally uses

- [Envelope formula](envelope-formula.md): solved for the payment.

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, (4.8).
- J. D. Hartline and A. R. Karlin, "Profit maximization in mechanism design", Chapter 13 of the same volume, Theorem 13.6 (ii).
