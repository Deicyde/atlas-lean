# Points of the standard simplex are probability vectors

The weights of a point of Mathlib's standard simplex `Convexity.StdSimplex ℝ Q` are nonnegative and
sum to $1$. Mean field equilibria are stated for probability vectors $m : Q \to \mathbb{R}$; this
lemma converts the mixed strategies used in [Nash's theorem](../nash-s-theorem/exists-is-mixed-nash.md)
into that form.
