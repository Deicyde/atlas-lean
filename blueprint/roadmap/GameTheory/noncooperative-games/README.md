# Noncooperative games

Noncooperative game theory studies strategic games in which each player chooses a strategy to optimise its own payoff or cost: Nash equilibria and the minimax theorem, potential and congestion games, routing games with Wardrop equilibria, the price of anarchy and of stability, and learning dynamics such as no-regret play and coarse correlated equilibria.
