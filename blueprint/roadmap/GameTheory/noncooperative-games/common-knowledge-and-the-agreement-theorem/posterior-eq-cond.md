# Posteriors are conditional probabilities

On a finite set of states, the posterior $\mu(A \cap P(\omega))/\mu(P(\omega))$ of the event $A$ at
$\omega$ equals the conditional probability $\mu[A \mid P(\omega)]$ of Mathlib (as a real number).
This bridges the definition used here with Mathlib's conditional probability measure
$\mu(P)^{-1}\,\mu(P \cap \cdot)$; both are $0$ on a null cell.
