# Correlated-equilibrium sums of a product distribution

For the product distribution $\psi$ of mixed strategies $\sigma$,
$$\sum_{s :\, s_i = j} \psi(s)\bigl(c_i(s) - c_i(k, s_{-i})\bigr) = \sigma_i(j)\,\bigl(\bar c_i(j) - \bar c_i(k)\bigr),$$
where $\bar c_i$ are the expected costs of pure strategies against the others' mixed strategies.
So a product distribution is a correlated equilibrium exactly when every strategy that $\sigma_i$
plays with positive probability is a best response.
