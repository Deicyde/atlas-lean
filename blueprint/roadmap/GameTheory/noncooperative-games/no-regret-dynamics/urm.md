# Unconditional regret matching

Given the average regret vector $x$, unconditional regret matching plays each action $k$ with
probability $x_k^+ / \sum_l x_l^+$, proportional to the positive part of its regret, and
uniformly when no regret is positive (where Hart leaves the choice arbitrary).

## Sources

- S. Hart and A. Mas-Colell, "A simple adaptive procedure leading to correlated equilibrium", Econometrica 68(5) (2000), 1127–1150, §3, Theorem B.
- S. Hart, "Adaptive heuristics", Econometrica 73(5) (2005), 1401–1430, §10.1.
