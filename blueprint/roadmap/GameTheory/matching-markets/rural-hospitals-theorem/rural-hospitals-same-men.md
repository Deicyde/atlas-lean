# Rural hospitals theorem, marriage case: every stable matching matches the same men

In a finite marriage market with singles, a man has a partner at one stable matching if and only if he has a partner at every stable matching. With the men as Roth's students and every quota equal to one, this is the part of Roth's Lemma 1 saying that the set of students who are assigned positions is the same at every stable outcome.
