# Remaining single ranked last

For a strict preference `T` over partners, `withSingleLast T` is the strict preference over partners and the option of remaining single that agrees with `T` on partners and ranks remaining single below every partner. It describes an agent for whom every partner is acceptable, as in the complete preference lists of the stable marriage problem.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "On the allocation of residents to rural hospitals: a general property of two-sided matching markets", *Econometrica* 54(2) (1986), 425–427, p. 426 and footnote 9: a student is acceptable to a hospital when the hospital prefers the student to leaving a position unmatched; ranking remaining single last makes every partner acceptable.
