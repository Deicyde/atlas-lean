# Bridge to the stable marriage model

Let `mp` and `wp` be complete strict preferences, as in the stable marriage problem, and rank remaining single last for every agent. A perfect matching `μ : M ≃ W` is stable as a matching with singles, through `μ.toPEquiv`, if and only if it is stable in the sense of the stable marriage problem (no blocking pair). Individual rationality holds automatically, since every agent is matched to an acceptable partner.
