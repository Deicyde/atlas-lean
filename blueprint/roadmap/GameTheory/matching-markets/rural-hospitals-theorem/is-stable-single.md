# Stable matching with singles

A matching with singles `μ` is stable for preferences `mp`, `wp` when

- no man prefers remaining single to his assignment, and no woman prefers remaining single to hers (individual rationality), and
- no man `m` and woman `w` both prefer each other to their assignments: `m` prefers `w` to `μ(m)` and `w` prefers `m` to `μ(w)`.

A pair already matched to each other never satisfies the second condition, since nobody strictly prefers a partner to the same partner, so the condition "not matched to each other" in the source is automatic.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "On the allocation of residents to rural hospitals: a general property of two-sided matching markets", *Econometrica* 54(2) (1986), 425–427, p. 426 (the definition of an unstable outcome, with every quota equal to one).
