# Rural hospitals theorem, marriage case: every stable matching matches the same women

In a finite marriage market with singles, a woman has a partner at one stable matching if and only if she has a partner at every stable matching. With the women in the role of Roth's hospitals and all quotas equal to one, this is Roth's Theorem: a hospital that fills fewer than its one position at a stable outcome, that is, a woman single at a stable matching, has the same (empty) assignment at every stable outcome.

The proof applies the men's statement to the market with the two sides exchanged.
