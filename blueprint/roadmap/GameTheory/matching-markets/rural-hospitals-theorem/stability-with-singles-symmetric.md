# Stability does not depend on which side is called the men

A partial bijection `μ : M ≃. W` is stable for the men's preferences `mp` and the women's preferences `wp` if and only if its inverse `μ.symm : W ≃. M` is stable for the market in which the women propose the first coordinate, with preferences `wp` and `mp`. The definition of stability is symmetric in the two sides.
