# Opposition of interests: no woman prefers her men's-join partner to her second partner

Let `μ` and `ν` be stable. No woman prefers her partner in the men's join to her partner in `ν`. With the companion statement for `μ`, every woman receives the partner she likes less among her two partners.

Proof: if `w` prefers her join partner `m` to her `ν`-partner, then `ν(m) ≠ w`, so the join gave `m` his `μ`-partner `w`, which he prefers to `ν(m)`; then `(m, w)` blocks `ν`.
