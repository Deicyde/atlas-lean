# Men's join of two stable matchings

For stable perfect matchings `μ` and `ν` between finite sets of men and women, the men's join is the perfect matching that gives every man the partner he prefers among `μ(m)` and `ν(m)`. It is a perfect matching because the map is injective and the two sides have the same size.

## Informally uses

- [The men's join of two stable matchings is injective](men-join-fun-injective.md): no two men prefer the same woman, which uses the stability of `μ` and `ν`.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "On the allocation of residents to rural hospitals: a general property of two-sided matching markets", *Econometrica* 54(2) (1986), 425–427, Lemma 2 (marriage case: every quota equals one and the choice set `Ch(x(h) ∪ y(h))` is the preferred of two partners).
