# Opposition of interests: no woman prefers her men's-join partner to her first partner

Let `μ` and `ν` be stable. No woman prefers her partner in the men's join to her partner in `μ`. Together with the companion statement for `ν`, every woman receives in the men's join the partner she likes less among her two partners: what the men gain together, the women lose together.

Proof: if `w` prefers her join partner `m` to her `μ`-partner, then `μ(m) ≠ w`, so the join gave `m` his `ν`-partner `w` because he prefers it to `μ(m)`; then `(m, w)` blocks `μ`.
