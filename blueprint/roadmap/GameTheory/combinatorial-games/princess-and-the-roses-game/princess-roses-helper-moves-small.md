# Small P&R Helper positions

**Small P&R Helper positions.** In the P&R Helper game, the position $(0, 0, 0)$ has no
options, and the options of $(1, 1, 1)$ are exactly $(0, 0, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$.
