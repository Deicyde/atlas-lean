# The game Euclid

**The game Euclid.** Euclid as an impartial game: its positions are the pairs $(a, b)$ of
natural numbers, its moves from $(a, b)$ are the Euclid moves, the rank of $(a, b)$ is $a + b$,
and its starting position is $(1, 1)$.
