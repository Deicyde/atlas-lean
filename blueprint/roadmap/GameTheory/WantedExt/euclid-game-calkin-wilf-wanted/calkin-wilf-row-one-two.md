# The first rows of the Calkin–Wilf tree

**The first rows of the Calkin–Wilf tree.** As lists of numerator–denominator pairs, row $1$
of the Calkin–Wilf tree is $(1, 2), (2, 1)$ and row $2$ is $(1, 3), (3, 2), (2, 3), (3, 1)$, in
this order.
