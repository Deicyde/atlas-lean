# Princess Roses Helper Move List

The seven moves of P&R Helper on `[x, y, z]`, as the amounts removed from `(x, y, z)`:
`(-1, -1, -1)`, `(-1, 0, 0)`, `(-1, -2, -1)`, `(-1, -1, -2)`, `(0, -1, 0)`, `(0, -1, -1)` and
`(0, 0, -1)`.
