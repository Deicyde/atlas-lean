# Princess Roses Helper equiv nim Heap iff of mem D

On region `D`, the Sprague–Grundy value of a P&R Helper position `[x, y, z]` is
`0, 1, …, 7` on the parity classes `[E, E, E], [E, E, O], [E, O, O], [E, O, E], [O, E, O],
[O, E, E], [O, O, E], [O, O, O]`.
