# Simplified Princess Roses Moves

Options of a simplified P&R position `[a, b, c, d]`: a move of
`simplifiedPrincessRosesMoveList` is legal when it leaves all piles nonnegative and in
non-increasing order `a ≥ b ≥ c ≥ d`.
