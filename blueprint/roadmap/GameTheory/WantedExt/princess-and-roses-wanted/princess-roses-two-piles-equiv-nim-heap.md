# Princess Roses two piles equiv nim Heap

Two-pile P&R: for `a ≥ b`, the Sprague–Grundy value of `(a, b)` is `0, 1, 2, 3` on the
parity classes `(E, E), (O, E), (O, O), (E, O)`.
