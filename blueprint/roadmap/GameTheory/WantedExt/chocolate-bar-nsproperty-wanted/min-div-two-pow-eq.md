# Min div two pow eq

If `h` has the NS property, `k ≥ 1` and `⌊z' / 2^k⌋ = ⌊z / 2^k⌋`, then
`⌊min (h z') y / 2^(k-1)⌋ = ⌊min (h z) y / 2^(k-1)⌋` for every `y`.
