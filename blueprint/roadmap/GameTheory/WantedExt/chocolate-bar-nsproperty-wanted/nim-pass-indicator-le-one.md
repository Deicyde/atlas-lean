# The Nim pass indicator is at most one

**The Nim pass indicator is at most one.** For every $s \in \mathbb{N}$ and all
$m, x \in \mathbb{N}^s$, the pass indicator of $m$ at $x$ is at most $1$; that is, it takes values
in $\{0, 1\}$.
