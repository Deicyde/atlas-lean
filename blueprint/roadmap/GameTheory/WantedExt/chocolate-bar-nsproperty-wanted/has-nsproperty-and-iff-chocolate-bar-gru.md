# Has NSProperty and iff chocolate Bar Grundy eq xor

For monotone `g` and `h`, both have the NS property iff the chocolate bar with depth
`CB(g, h, x, y, z)` has Grundy number `x ⊕ y ⊕ z` at every bar position `x ≤ g z`, `y ≤ h z`.
