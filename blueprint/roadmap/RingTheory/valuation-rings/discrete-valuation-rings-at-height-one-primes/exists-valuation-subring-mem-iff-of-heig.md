# Valuation subring at a height-one prime of a normal domain

**Valuation subring at a height-one prime of a normal domain.** At a height-one prime of a Noetherian integrally closed domain, there
is a principal ideal valuation subring of the fraction field whose
membership is the usual numerator-denominator criterion.
