# Valuation subrings of finite extensions of dimension-one domains are DVRs

**Valuation subrings of finite extensions of dimension-one domains are DVRs.** A valuation subring of a finite extension of the fraction field of a
Noetherian domain of Krull dimension at most one, containing the base ring
and different from the whole field, is a discrete valuation ring.
