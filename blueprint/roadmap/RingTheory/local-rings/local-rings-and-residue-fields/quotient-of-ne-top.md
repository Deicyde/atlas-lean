# Quotient of a local ring by a proper ideal is local

**Quotient of a local ring by a proper ideal is local.** A quotient of a local ring by a proper ideal is local. Mathlib uses this
fact inline (via `IsLocalRing.of_surjective'`), but does not package it as a
standalone lemma.
