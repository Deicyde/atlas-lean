# Initial cluster of the ambient field

The cluster `(X_i)_{i ∈ n}` of free generators of
`ℚ(X_i : i ∈ n) = FractionRing (MvPolynomial n ℤ)`, the ambient field of a labeled seed
(Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*,
arXiv:1608.05735v5, Definition 3.1.1, with no frozen variables).
