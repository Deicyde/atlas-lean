# The maximal ideal of a DVR maps into the ideal of an Eisenstein root

**The maximal ideal of a DVR maps into the ideal of an Eisenstein root.** Let $A$ be a
discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $f \in A[X]$ be a polynomial of
positive degree that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$, and let $\alpha$ be the image of
$X$ in $A[X]/(f)$. Then the ideal of $A[X]/(f)$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak m$ is
contained in the principal ideal $(\alpha)$.
