# Adjoining a root of an Eisenstein polynomial to a DVR gives a DVR

**Adjoining a root of an Eisenstein polynomial to a DVR gives a DVR.** Let $A$ be a discrete
valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $f \in A[X]$ be a monic polynomial of
positive degree that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$. Then $A[X]/(f)$ is a discrete
valuation ring.
