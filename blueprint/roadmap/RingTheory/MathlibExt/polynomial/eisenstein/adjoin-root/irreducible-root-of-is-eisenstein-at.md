# The adjoined root of an Eisenstein polynomial over a DVR is irreducible

**The adjoined root of an Eisenstein polynomial over a DVR is irreducible.** Let $A$ be a
discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $f \in A[X]$ be a monic polynomial
of positive degree that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$, and let $\alpha$ be the image
of $X$ in $A[X]/(f)$. Then $\alpha$ is an irreducible element of $A[X]/(f)$.
