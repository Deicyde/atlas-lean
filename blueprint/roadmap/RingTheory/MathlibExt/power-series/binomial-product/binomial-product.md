# Binomial Product

The binomial (Hurwitz) product of formal power series:
`(∑ aₙ xⁿ) ⊙ (∑ bₙ xⁿ) = ∑ cₙ xⁿ` with `cₙ = ∑_{k=0}^n binom(n, k) a_k b_{n-k}`.
