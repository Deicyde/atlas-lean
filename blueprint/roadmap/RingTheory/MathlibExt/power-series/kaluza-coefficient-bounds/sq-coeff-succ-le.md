# Log-convexity of the coefficients

**Log-convexity of the coefficients.** If a real formal power series $f = \sum_n a_n X^n$
has positive log-convex coefficients, then $a_{n+1}^2 \le a_n a_{n+2}$ for every natural
number $n$.
