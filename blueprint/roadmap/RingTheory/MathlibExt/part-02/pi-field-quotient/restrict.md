# Coordinate restriction

**Coordinate restriction.** Let $K$ be a field and $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of fields
that are $K$-algebras indexed by a type $\iota$. For a finite subset $S \subseteq \iota$, the
coordinate restriction is the $K$-algebra homomorphism
$\prod_{i \in \iota} F_i \to \prod_{i \in S} F_i$ sending $(x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ to
$(x_i)_{i \in S}$.
