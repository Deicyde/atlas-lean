# Coordinates of a restriction

**Coordinates of a restriction.** Let $K$ be a field, $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of
fields that are $K$-algebras indexed by a type $\iota$, and $S \subseteq \iota$ a finite subset.
For $x \in \prod_{i \in \iota} F_i$ and $s \in S$, the $s$-th coordinate of the coordinate
restriction of $x$ to $S$ is $x_s$.
