# One is regular

**One is regular.** Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $K$ a nontrivial commutative ring.
For every ring homomorphism $\mathrm{ev} : A \to K$, the element $1$ of the total ring of fractions
of $A$ is regular at $\mathrm{ev}$.
