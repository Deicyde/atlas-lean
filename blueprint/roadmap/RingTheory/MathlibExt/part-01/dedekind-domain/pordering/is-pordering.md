# Bhargava p-ordering

**Bhargava p-ordering.** Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain, $\mathfrak p$ a nonzero prime ideal
of $R$, $S \subseteq R$ and $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a sequence in $R$. Write $v_n(x)$ for the stage-$n$
prefix-product valuation of $x \in R$ with respect to $\mathfrak p$ and $(a_n)$, the multiplicity
of $\mathfrak p$ in the ideal generated by $\prod_{k < n} (x - a_k)$. Then $(a_n)$ is a
$\mathfrak p$-ordering of $S$ when $S$ is infinite, $a_n \in S$ for every $n$, and
$v_n(a_n) \le v_n(x)$ for every $n$ and every $x \in S$.
