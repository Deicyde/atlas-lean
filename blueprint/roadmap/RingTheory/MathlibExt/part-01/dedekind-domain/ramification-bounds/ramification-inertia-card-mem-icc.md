# Ramification inertia card mem Icc

The three bounds of Corollary 5.36 packaged together: for `𝔮` above `𝔭`,
`e_𝔮`, `f_𝔮`, and the number `g_𝔭` of primes above `𝔭` all lie in `[1, n]`
with `n = Module.finrank R S`.
