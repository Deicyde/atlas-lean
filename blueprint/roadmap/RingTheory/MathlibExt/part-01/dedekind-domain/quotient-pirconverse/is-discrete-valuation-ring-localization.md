# Is Discrete Valuation Ring localization at Prime of forall is Principal Ideal Ring quotient

If every quotient of `A` by a nonzero ideal is a principal ideal ring,
then the localization of `A` at any nonzero prime is a discrete valuation
ring: the image of the prime in `A ⧸ P ^ 2` is principal, generated by the
image of some `π ∈ P`; lifting gives `P ≤ (π) + P ^ 2`, hence the maximal
ideal of the localization is contained in `(π) + 𝔪 ^ 2`, and Nakayama's lemma
gives `𝔪 = (π)`.
