# Valuation surjective of extends

Every integer-valued valuation on `L` extending `v_𝔭` up to
equivalence is equivalent to `v_𝔮` for a prime `𝔮` of `B` over `𝔭`.
The prime is the strict unit-ball ideal `{b ∈ B | w b < 1}`.
