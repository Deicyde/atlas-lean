# The discriminant ideal is the norm of the different

**The discriminant ideal is the norm of the different.** Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind
domains, with $B$ an $A$-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an $A$-module. Then the
discriminant ideal $\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}$ is the ideal of $A$ generated by the norms
$N_{B/A}(x)$ of the elements $x$ of the different ideal $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ of $B$ over $A$;
this holds by definition.
