# Norm base Change

Norm commutes with base change: the image of `N_{B/A} b` under
`A → A'` is the norm of `1 ⊗ b` in `A' ⊗[A] B`. This is the norm clause of
ATLAS `NumberTheoryI`, Lemma 4.49.
