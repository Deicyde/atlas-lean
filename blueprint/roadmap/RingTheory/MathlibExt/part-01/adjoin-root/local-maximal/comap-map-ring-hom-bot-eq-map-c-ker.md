# Kernel of reducing polynomial coefficients modulo an ideal

**Kernel of reducing polynomial coefficients modulo an ideal.** Let $A$ be a commutative
ring and $I$ an ideal of $A$. The kernel of the ring homomorphism $A[X] \to (A/I)[X]$ that
reduces every coefficient modulo $I$ is the ideal of $A[X]$ generated by the constant
polynomials $a$ with $a \in I$.
