# Dvr pth root ramified of prime spans maximal ideal

Absolutely unramified base case of cyclotomic ramification:
if the rational prime `p` generates the maximal ideal of the complete DVR
`A`, the residue characteristic is `p`, the base has no nontrivial `p`-th
root of unity, and `0 < m` with `p ∣ m`, the stated cyclotomic extension
has ramification index different from `1`. This is not the general
ramified implication or full equivalence of Corollary 10.18.
