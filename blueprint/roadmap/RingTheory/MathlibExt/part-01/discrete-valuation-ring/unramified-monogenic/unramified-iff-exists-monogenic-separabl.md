# Unramified iff exists monogenic separable reduced minpoly

Corollary 10.15 of ATLAS `NumberTheoryI`: for a finite torsion-free
extension `B / A` of discrete valuation rings with `A` complete,
unramifiedness (separable residue-field extension, same degree) is
equivalent to `B` being generated by one element whose minimal polynomial
reduces to a separable polynomial over the residue field of `A`.
