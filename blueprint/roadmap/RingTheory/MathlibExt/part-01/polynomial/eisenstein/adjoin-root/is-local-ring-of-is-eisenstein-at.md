# An Eisenstein extension of a DVR is local

**An Eisenstein extension of a DVR is local.** Let $A$ be a discrete valuation ring with
maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $f \in A[X]$ be a monic polynomial of positive degree that is
Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$. Then $A[X]/(f)$ is a local ring.
