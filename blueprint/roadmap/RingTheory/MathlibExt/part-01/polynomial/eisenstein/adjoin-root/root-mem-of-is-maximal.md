# Every maximal ideal of an Eisenstein extension of a DVR contains the root

**Every maximal ideal of an Eisenstein extension of a DVR contains the root.** Let $A$ be a
discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $f \in A[X]$ be a monic polynomial
that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$, and let $\alpha$ be the image of $X$ in
$A[X]/(f)$. Then $\alpha$ lies in every maximal ideal of $A[X]/(f)$.
