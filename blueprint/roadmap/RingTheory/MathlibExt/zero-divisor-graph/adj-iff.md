# Adjacency in the zero-divisor graph

**Adjacency in the zero-divisor graph.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Two vertices $v$
and $w$ of the zero-divisor graph of $R$ are adjacent if and only if $v \neq w$ and $vw = 0$.
