# Residue Field Maximal Ideal Pow Quotient Equiv Pow

Under `map 𝔪A ≤ 𝔪B ^ n`, the quotient by `map 𝔪A ⊔ 𝔪B ^ n` is identified
with `B ⧸ 𝔪B ^ n`, by `Ideal.quotEquivOfEq` applied to
`maximalIdealPowQuotientIdeal_eq`.
