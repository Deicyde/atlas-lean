# A nonzero submodule spans the field

**A nonzero submodule spans the field.** Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $K$ a field that is
an $A$-algebra. Then every nonzero $A$-submodule $M$ of $K$ spans $K$ as a $K$-vector space.
