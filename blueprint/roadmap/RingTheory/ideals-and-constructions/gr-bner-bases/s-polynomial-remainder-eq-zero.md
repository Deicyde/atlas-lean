# S-polynomials of a Gröbner basis reduce to zero

If `G` is a Gröbner basis of `I` and
`g, h ∈ G`, then every remainder of `S(g, h)` on division by `G` is zero: the easy direction of
Buchberger's criterion, since `S(g, h) ∈ I`.

- D. Cox, J. Little, D. O'Shea, *Ideals, Varieties, and Algorithms*, Ch. 2 §6, Theorem 6.
