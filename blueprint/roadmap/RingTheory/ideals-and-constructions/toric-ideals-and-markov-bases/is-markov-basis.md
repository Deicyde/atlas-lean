# Markov basis

Moves `f_i : X → ℤ` form a Markov basis for the statistic `T` when (a) each
`f_i` lies in the lattice of moves, `∑_x f_i(x) T(x) = 0`, and (b) any two tables `g, g'` with
the same sufficient statistic are joined by a sequence of steps `± f_i` through tables of
nonnegative counts (Diaconis–Sturmfels 1998, Definition (1.5)).
