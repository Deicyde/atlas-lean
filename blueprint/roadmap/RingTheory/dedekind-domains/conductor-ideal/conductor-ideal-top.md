# Conductor ideal of the whole algebra

**Conductor ideal of the whole algebra.** Let $R$ be a commutative semiring and $S$ a
commutative $R$-algebra. The conductor ideal of $S$, viewed as an $R$-subalgebra of itself, is
the unit ideal $S$.
