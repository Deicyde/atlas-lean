# Reed-Solomon encoder

**Reed-Solomon encoder.** For a field $F$ and points $\alpha_0, \dots, \alpha_{n-1} \in F$,
the encoding of a polynomial $p \in F[X]$ is the word $(p(\alpha_0), \dots, p(\alpha_{n-1}))$
in $F^n$.
