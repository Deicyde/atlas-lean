# Message bits of a word

**Message bits of a word.** The message of a word $y = (y_1, \dots, y_7) \in \mathbb{F}_2^7$ is
$(y_3, y_5, y_6, y_7) \in \mathbb{F}_2^4$, its bits at the positions that are not powers of two.
