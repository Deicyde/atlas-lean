# Conditional entropy of the output given the auxiliary variable

**Conditional entropy of the output given the auxiliary variable.** For a probability measure
$\nu$ on $(X \times Y) \times S$, $H(Y \mid S)$ is the conditional Shannon entropy under $\nu$, in
bits and with values in $[0, \infty]$, of the coordinate $((x, y), s) \mapsto y$ given the
sigma-algebra generated by the coordinate $((x, y), s) \mapsto s$.
