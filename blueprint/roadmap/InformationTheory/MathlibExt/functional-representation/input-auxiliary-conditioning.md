# Sigma-algebra of the input and auxiliary coordinates

**Sigma-algebra of the input and auxiliary coordinates.** For measurable spaces $X$, $Y$ and
$S$, this is the sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$ generated by the map
$((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, s)$ into $X \times S$ with its product sigma-algebra, regarded as a
sub-sigma-algebra of the product sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$.
