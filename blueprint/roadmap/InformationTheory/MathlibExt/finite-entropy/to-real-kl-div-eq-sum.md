# Kullback–Leibler divergence on a finite space

**Kullback–Leibler divergence on a finite space.** For finite measures of equal mass with
`μ ≪ ν`, Mathlib's `klDiv μ ν` is `∑ μ{a} log (μ{a} / ν{a})`.
