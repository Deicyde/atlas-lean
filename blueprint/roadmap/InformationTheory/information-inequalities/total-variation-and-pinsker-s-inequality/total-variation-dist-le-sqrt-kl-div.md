# Pinsker's inequality, square-root form

For probability measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ with $D(\mu \,\|\, \nu) < \infty$,

$$
\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) \le \sqrt{\frac{D(\mu \,\|\, \nu)}{2}}
$$

with the natural logarithm. The finiteness hypothesis only excludes the case where the right side
is infinite.

## Sources

- Y. Polyanskiy and Y. Wu, Information Theory: From Coding to Learning (Cambridge University Press, 2025), Theorem 7.10 (the form `√(½ D(P‖Q)) ≥ TV(P, Q)` in its proof).
