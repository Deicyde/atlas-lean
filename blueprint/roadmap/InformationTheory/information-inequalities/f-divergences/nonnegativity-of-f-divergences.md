# Nonnegativity of f-divergences

For $f$ convex on $[0,\infty)$ with $f(1) = 0$, and probability vectors $P \ge 0$, $Q > 0$ on a
nonempty finite alphabet, $D_f(P\|Q) \ge 0$. The equality case of the source (for $f$ strictly
convex at $1$, $D_f = 0$ only when $P = Q$) and the joint convexity of $D_f$ are not formalized.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár and P. C. Shields, *Information Theory and Statistics: A Tutorial*, Foundations and Trends in Communications and Information Theory 1(4) (2004), 417–528, Lemma 4.1 (first assertion).
