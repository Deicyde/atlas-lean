# Donsker–Varadhan inequality, integral form

Let `μ` and `ν` be probability measures with
`μ ≪ ν` and integrable log-likelihood ratio `llr μ ν` under `μ`, and let `f` be `μ`-integrable
with `exp ∘ f` `ν`-integrable. Then
`∫ f dμ - log ∫ exp f dν ≤ ∫ log (dμ/dν) dμ`.

## Sources

- Varadhan, *Large Deviations*, NYU lecture notes, 2010, Section 3, Lemma 1.2 (the inequality half of the variational formula).
