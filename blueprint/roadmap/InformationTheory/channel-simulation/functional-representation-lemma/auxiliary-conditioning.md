# Sigma-algebra of the auxiliary coordinate

**Sigma-algebra of the auxiliary coordinate.** For measurable spaces $X$, $Y$ and $S$, this is
the sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$ generated by the projection $((x, y), s) \mapsto s$
onto $S$, regarded as a sub-sigma-algebra of the product sigma-algebra on
$(X \times Y) \times S$.
