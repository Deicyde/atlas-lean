# Input law

**Input law.** The input law of a finite joint law $p$ on
$\{0, \dots, n_X - 1\} \times \{0, \dots, n_Y - 1\}$ is its first marginal: the law on
$\{0, \dots, n_X - 1\}$ that is the image of $p$ under $(x, y) \mapsto x$.
