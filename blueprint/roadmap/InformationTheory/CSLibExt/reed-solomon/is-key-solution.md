# Is Key Solution

`(Q, E)` solves the **Berlekamp-Welch key equations** for the received word `y`: `E` is monic
of degree `e`, `Q` has degree less than `e + k`, and `Q(αᵢ) = yᵢ E(αᵢ)` at every point.
