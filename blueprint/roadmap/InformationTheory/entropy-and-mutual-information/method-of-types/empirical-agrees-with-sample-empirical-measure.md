# Empirical distribution of a type agrees with the empirical measure

For a sample $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a finite alphabet with measurable singletons and $n \ge 1$, the
[empirical distribution](empirical.md) of the type of $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ gives each letter $a$
the mass that the repository's empirical measure `sampleEmpiricalMeasure x n` gives $\{a\}$. This
bridge connects the finite, counting form of Sanov's theorem on this topic to the measure-theoretic
form used by the general theorem.
