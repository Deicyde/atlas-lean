# Lee–Vempala bound on the KLS constant

**Lee–Vempala bound on the KLS constant.** There are a real $C > 0$ and an integer $N \ge 3$
such that for every $n \ge N$ the dimensional KLS constant $\psi_n \in [0, \infty]$ satisfies
$\psi_n \le C n^{1/4}$.
