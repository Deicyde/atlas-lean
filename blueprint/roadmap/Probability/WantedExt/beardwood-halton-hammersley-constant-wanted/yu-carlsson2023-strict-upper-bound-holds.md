# Yu–Carlsson upper bound on the BHH constant

**Yu–Carlsson upper bound on the BHH constant.** The real-valued Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley
constant $\beta_2$ of the unit square satisfies $\beta_2 < 0.90304$.
