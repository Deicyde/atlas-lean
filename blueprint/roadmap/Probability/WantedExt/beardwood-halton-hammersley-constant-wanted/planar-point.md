# Points of the Euclidean plane

**Points of the Euclidean plane.** A planar point is a point of the Euclidean plane
$\mathbb{R}^2$: a pair $(x, y)$ of real numbers, with the Euclidean norm
$\lVert (x, y) \rVert = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.
