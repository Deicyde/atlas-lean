# The ensemble mean stays in a subspace

If every member $v^{(j)}$ lies in a linear subspace $S$, so does $\overline v = \frac1J \sum_j v^{(j)}$.

## Sources

- M. A. Iglesias, K. J. H. Law and A. M. Stuart, *Ensemble Kalman methods for inverse problems*, Inverse Problems 29 (2013) 045001, arXiv:1209.2736, proof of Theorem 2.1.
