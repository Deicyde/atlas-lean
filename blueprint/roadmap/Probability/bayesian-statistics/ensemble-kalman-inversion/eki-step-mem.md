# One EKI step preserves every subspace containing the ensemble

If every particle lies in a linear subspace $S$, then so does every particle after an
[EKI step](eki-step.md), for any forward map $G$, any $\Gamma$ and any data: the step adds
$C^{up}(u)\, (C^{pp}(u) + \Gamma)^{-1} (y^{(j)} - G(u^{(j)}))$, which lies in $S$.

## Sources

- M. A. Iglesias, K. J. H. Law and A. M. Stuart, *Ensemble Kalman methods for inverse problems*, Inverse Problems 29 (2013) 045001, arXiv:1209.2736, the inductive step in the proof of Theorem 2.1.
