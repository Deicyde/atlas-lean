# EKI output covariance

The empirical covariance of the forward-mapped ensemble, as a linear map on the data space:

$$
C^{pp}(u)\, d = \frac1J \sum_{k=1}^J \big\langle G(u^{(k)}) - \overline G, d \big\rangle \, (G(u^{(k)}) - \overline G) .
$$

## Sources

- C. Schillings and A. M. Stuart, *Analysis of the ensemble Kalman filter for inverse problems*, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 55 (2017), arXiv:1602.02020, equation (5).
- M. A. Iglesias, K. J. H. Law and A. M. Stuart, *Ensemble Kalman methods for inverse problems*, Inverse Problems 29 (2013) 045001, arXiv:1209.2736, equation (21).
