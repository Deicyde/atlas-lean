# The posterior is a probability measure

If $\exp(-\Phi)$ is $\mu_0$-integrable and $Z = \int \exp(-\Phi) \, d\mu_0 > 0$, the posterior
$Z^{-1} \exp(-\Phi) \, \mu_0$ has total mass one.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), Theorem 4.3 (the conclusion that $\mu^y$ is well defined once $0 < Z(y) < \infty$).
