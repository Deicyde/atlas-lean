# Empty-column factor of the finite feature-model closed form

The expression `(N! / ∏_{j=1}^{N} (j + α/K))^K`, the first factor of Griffiths and Ghahramani (2011), Equation 13. In their derivation, `N! / ∏_{j=1}^{N} (j + α/K)` is the probability of an empty column of the `K`-feature beta–Bernoulli model with the factor `α/K` moved onto the non-empty columns, raised to the power `K`. Recorded here as a definition.
