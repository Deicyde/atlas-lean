# Falling factorial over a power tends to one

For every fixed `r`, `K! / ((K - r)! K^r) = K (K - 1) ⋯ (K - r + 1) / K^r → 1` as `K → ∞`. In the infinite limits of finite mixture and feature models (Griffiths and Ghahramani 2011, Equations 5 and 14) this is the factor that counts the ways of assigning the `r` occupied classes or features to distinct labels among `K`.
