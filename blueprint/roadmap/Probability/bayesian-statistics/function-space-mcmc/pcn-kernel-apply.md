# Evaluation of the pCN kernel

The pCN kernel at $u$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $\xi \mapsto \sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u + \beta \xi$, that is, the
law of the proposal $v$ when $\xi \sim \mu_0$.
