# The Metropolis–Hastings kernel is a Markov kernel

For a Markov proposal kernel $Q$ and a measurable potential $\Phi$, $P(u, \cdot)$ is a probability measure
for every $u$: its total mass is $\int a \, dQ(u, \cdot) + \int (1 - a) \, dQ(u, \cdot) = 1$ because $a \le 1$.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), equation (5.5) and the remark after it.
