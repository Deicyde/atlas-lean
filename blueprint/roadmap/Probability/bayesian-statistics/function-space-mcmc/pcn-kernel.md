# The pCN proposal kernel

The pCN proposal as a Markov kernel: $Q(u, \cdot)$ is the law of $\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u + \beta \xi$ with
$\xi \sim \mu_0$. For $\mu_0 = N(0, C)$ this is $N(\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u, \beta^2 C)$.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), Example 5.3 and Algorithm 5.10.
