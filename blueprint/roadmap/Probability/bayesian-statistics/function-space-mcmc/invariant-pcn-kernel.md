# The pCN proposal preserves its Gaussian prior

If $u \sim N(0, C)$ and $\xi \sim N(0, C)$ are independent, then $\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u + \beta \xi \sim N(0, C)$
for every $-1 \le \beta \le 1$, on any separable Banach space. This follows from reversibility, since a
reversible Markov kernel leaves its measure invariant.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), Example 5.3 and Lemma 5.2.
