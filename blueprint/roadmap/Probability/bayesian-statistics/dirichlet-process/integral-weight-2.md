# Mean of a Dirichlet-process weight

**Mean of a Dirichlet-process weight** (Sethuraman 1994): `E[πₖ] = αᵏ / (1 + α)^(k+1)`
(`α^{k-1} / (1 + α)^k` for Sethuraman's `π_k`, indexed from `1`).
