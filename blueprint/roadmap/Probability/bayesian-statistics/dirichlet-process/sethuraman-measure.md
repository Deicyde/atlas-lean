# Sethuraman's canonical probability space

**Sethuraman's canonical probability space.** The product probability space `(ℕ → ℝ) × (ℕ → X)` carrying i.i.d. `Beta(1, α)` proportions
and, independently, i.i.d. `H` atoms.
