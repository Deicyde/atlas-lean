# Cochran's heterogeneity statistic Q

**Cochran's heterogeneity statistic Q.** Cochran's heterogeneity statistic `Q = ∑ᵢ vᵢ⁻¹ (Xᵢ - ∑ⱼ wⱼ Xⱼ)²` of the estimates `X` with
variances `v`, where `w = inverseVarianceWeights v`.
