# Cochran (1954)

**Cochran (1954).** For `k ≥ 2` independent Gaussian estimates `Xᵢ ~ N(θ, vᵢ)` with
positive known variances, Cochran's `Q` has the chi-square law with `k - 1` degrees of
freedom.
