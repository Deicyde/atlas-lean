# Benjamini–Hochberg step-up index

**Benjamini–Hochberg step-up index.** The Benjamini–Hochberg step-up index: the largest `k ∈ {0, …, m}` such that at least `k`
p-values are at most `q k / m` (`k = 0` always qualifies).
