# Probability of false alarm under a geometric prior

`PFA(τ) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} πₙ Pₙ(τ < n)` with `πₙ = ρ (1 - ρ)^{n-1}` and `Pₙ = changePointLaw P₀ P₁ n`
(Veeravalli–Banerjee, equations (8) and (11)).

## Sources

- V. V. Veeravalli and T. Banerjee, *Quickest change detection*, arXiv:1210.5552 (survey chapter; cited from the revised arXiv version dated 27 November 2024), §III, equations (8) and (11).
