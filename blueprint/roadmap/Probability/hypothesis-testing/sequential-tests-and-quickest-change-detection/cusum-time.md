# CUSUM alarm time

For a threshold `c`, `N_c = inf {n ≥ 1 : W_n ≥ c}`
(Veeravalli–Banerjee, equation (43)); `⊤` if no alarm is raised.

## Sources

- V. V. Veeravalli and T. Banerjee, *Quickest change detection*, arXiv:1210.5552 (a survey chapter; I read the revised arXiv version dated 27 November 2024), §IV, equations (43) and (48).
- T. L. Lai, *Sequential analysis: some classical problems and new challenges*, Statistica Sinica 11 (2001) 303–408, §3, equation (3.1).
