# Average detection delay under a geometric prior

`ADD(τ) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} πₙ Eₙ[(τ - n)⁺]` with `πₙ = ρ (1 - ρ)^{n-1}` (Veeravalli–Banerjee,
equations (7) and (11)); the delay is computed in `ℕ∞` (truncated subtraction, `⊤ - n = ⊤`).

## Sources

- V. V. Veeravalli and T. Banerjee, *Quickest change detection*, arXiv:1210.5552 (survey chapter; cited from the revised arXiv version dated 27 November 2024), §III, equations (7) and (11).
