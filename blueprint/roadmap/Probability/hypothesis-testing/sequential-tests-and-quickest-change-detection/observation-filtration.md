# Filtration of the first `n` observations

On the coordinate space `ℕ → X`, the
`σ`-algebra at time `n` is generated by the coordinates `0, …, n - 1`, that is by the
observations `X₁, …, Xₙ`; at time `0` it is trivial.

The observation $X_{i+1}$ is the coordinate $i$ of $\omega \in X^{\mathbb N}$; a stopping time for this filtration that equals $n$ has used exactly $X_1, \dots, X_n$.
