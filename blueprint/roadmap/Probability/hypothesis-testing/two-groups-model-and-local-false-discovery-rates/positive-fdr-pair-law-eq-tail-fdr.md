# Storey's theorem in the two-groups model

**Storey's theorem in the two-groups model.** Storey (2002), Theorem 1, in the two-groups model: for `m ≥ 1` i.i.d. tests rejecting on
`Γ`, `pFDR(Γ) = π₀ P₀(Γ) / P(Γ) = Fdr(Γ)`.
