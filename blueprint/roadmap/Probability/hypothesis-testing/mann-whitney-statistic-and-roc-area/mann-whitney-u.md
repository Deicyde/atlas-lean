# Mann–Whitney U statistic

**Mann–Whitney U statistic.** Mann–Whitney count `U = ∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ (1[y j < x i] + ½ 1[y j = x i])` of (positive, negative)
pairs ranked correctly, with ties counted one half.
