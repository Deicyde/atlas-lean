# Kendall's geometric tail ratio

**Kendall's geometric tail ratio.** Kendall's ratio `β t` of the geometric tail of the law of `X t`: `b (E - 1) / (b E - d)`
with `E = exp ((b - d) t)` if `b ≠ d`, and `b t / (1 + b t)` if `b = d`.
