# Mean total progeny equals $1/(1-m)$

**Mean total progeny equals $1/(1-m)$.** The mean total progeny is `(1 - m)⁻¹` in `[0, ∞]`: it is `1 / (1 - m)` for `m < 1` and `∞`
for `m ≥ 1` (`m` the mean offspring number).
