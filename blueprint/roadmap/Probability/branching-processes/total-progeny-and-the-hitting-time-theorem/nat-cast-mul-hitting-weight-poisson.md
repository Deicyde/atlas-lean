# Borel–Tanner law

**Borel–Tanner law.** For Poisson(`r`) offspring and `k ≤ n` ancestors,
`n · P(T = n) = k e^{-rn} (rn)^{n-k} / (n-k)!` (Tanner 1961; Haight–Breuer 1960).
