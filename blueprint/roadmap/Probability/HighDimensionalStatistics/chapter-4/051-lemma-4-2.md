# Lemma 4.2

Let $A$ be a $d \times T$ random matrix such that $A \sim \mathsf{subG}_{d \times T}(\sigma^2)$. Then $\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}} \leq 4\sigma\sqrt{\log(12)(d \vee T)} + 2\sigma\sqrt{2 \log(1/\delta)}$ with probability $1 - \delta$.
