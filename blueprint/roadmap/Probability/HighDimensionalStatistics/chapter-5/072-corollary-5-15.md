# Corollary 5.15

Recall that $\mathcal{B}_0(k) \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ denotes the set of all $k$-sparse vectors of $\mathbb{R}^d$. The minimax rate of estimation over $\mathcal{B}_0(k)$ in the Gaussian sequence model is $\phi(\mathcal{B}_0(k)) = \frac{\sigma^2 k}{n} \log(ed/k)$. Moreover, it is attained by the constrained least squares estimator $\hat{\theta}_{\mathcal{B}_0(k)}^{\mathrm{LS}}$.
