# Lemma 5.8 (Pinsker's inequality)

Let $\mathbb{P}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ be two probability measures such that $\mathbb{P} \ll \mathbb{Q}$. Then $$\mathsf{TV}(\mathbb{P}, \mathbb{Q}) \leq \sqrt{\mathsf{KL}(\mathbb{P}, \mathbb{Q})}.$$
