# The Snell envelope stopped at the first meeting time is a martingale

**The Snell envelope stopped at the first meeting time is a martingale** (Peskir–Shiryaev,
Theorem 1.2, part (1.1.14) with `n = 0`). For a strongly adapted, integrable reward on a finite
measure space, `(S (k ∧ τ*))_k` is a martingale with respect to `𝒢`.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, Theorem 1.2 (finite horizon), part (1.1.14) and its proof (1.1.19)–(1.1.20).
