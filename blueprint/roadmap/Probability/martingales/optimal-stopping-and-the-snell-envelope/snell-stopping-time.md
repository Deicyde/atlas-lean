# First time the Snell envelope meets the reward

The stopping time
`τ* = min {k ∈ [0, N] : S k = G k}` (Peskir–Shiryaev, equation (1.1.8) with `n = 0`); the set is
nonempty because `S N = G N`.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, equation (1.1.8) with $n = 0$.
