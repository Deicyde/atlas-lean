# Weighted quadratic variation of Brownian motion

**Weighted quadratic variation of Brownian motion.** Under the hypotheses of
`integral_weightedQVError_sq_le`, `∑_{k < n} φ(B_{t_k}) ((ΔB_k)² - Δt) → 0` in `L²(P)`.
