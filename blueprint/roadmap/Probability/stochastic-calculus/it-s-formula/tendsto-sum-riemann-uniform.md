# Left-point Riemann sums of a continuous function

**Left-point Riemann sums of a continuous function** on the uniform partition of `[0, t]`
converge to the integral: `∑_{k < n} g (t k / n) (t / n) → ∫_0^t g` (`t ≥ 0`).
