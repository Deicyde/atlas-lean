# Kolmogorov's condition for pre-Brownian motion

A pre-Brownian motion `B` with
measurable coordinates satisfies `E |B s - B t|⁴ ≤ 3 |s - t|²` for all times `s, t`, that is,
it is a Kolmogorov process with exponents `p = 4`, `q = 2` and constant `3` (Le Gall, DEA notes
1994, Lemme 2.3, applied as in Le Gall, *Brownian Motion, Martingales, and Stochastic Calculus*,
Chapter 2).

## Informally uses

- [Fourth moment of a centered Gaussian](integral-pow-four-gaussian-real.md): the increment
  `B s - B t` is centered Gaussian with variance `|s - t|`, so its fourth moment is `3 |s - t|²`.
