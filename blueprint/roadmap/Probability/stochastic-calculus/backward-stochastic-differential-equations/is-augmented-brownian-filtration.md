# Augmented Brownian filtration

`F t` is generated by `B_s`, `s ≤ t`, together with the
`P`-null measurable sets. This is the filtration of the BSDE theory (S. Peng, Proc. ICM 2010,
Vol. I, §1.1, where the null sets are left implicit); the solution of a BSDE is adapted to it
because of the Brownian martingale representation theorem.
