# Constant solutions and constant preservation of g-expectations

If the generator
vanishes at `z = 0` along the constant `c` (`g(t, ω, c, 0) = 0`), then `(Y, Z) = (c, 0)` solves
the BSDE with terminal value `c`. With uniqueness of solutions, this is the property
`E^g[c] = c` of Peng's g-expectation, defined for generators with `g(·, y, 0) ≡ 0`
(S. Peng, Proc. ICM 2010, Vol. I, §1.6, equation (1.19) and the sentence after it).

## Background

- [Sublinear expectation](../../foundations/sublinear-expectations-and-model-uncertainty/is-sublinear-expectation.md):
  a g-expectation is a monotone, constant preserving nonlinear expectation; its uncertain models
  are all absolutely continuous with respect to the Wiener measure, a limitation that Peng's
  G-expectation removes (Proc. ICM 2010, §2, first paragraph).
