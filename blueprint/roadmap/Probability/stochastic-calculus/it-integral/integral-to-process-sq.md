# Time integral of a squared elementary integrand

**Time integral of a squared elementary integrand.** The time integral of the squared elementary integrand, path by path:
`∫_0^t H_u² du = ∑ i, ξ_i² (t_{i+1} ∧ t - t_i ∧ t)`.
