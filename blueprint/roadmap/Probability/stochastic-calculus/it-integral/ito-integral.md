# Itô integral of an elementary integrand

**Itô integral of an elementary integrand.** The **Itô integral** of an elementary integrand against a process `B`:
`t ↦ ∑ i, coeff i (B (time (i + 1) ∧ t) - B (time i ∧ t))`.
