# Truncation

**Truncation.** For a jointly measurable `H` with `E ∫_0^t H_u² du < ∞`, the truncations of
`H` at levels `M → ∞` converge to `H` in `L²(P ⊗ du)` on `(0, t] × Ω`
(Karatzas–Shreve 1991, proof of Lemma 3.2.4(c)).
