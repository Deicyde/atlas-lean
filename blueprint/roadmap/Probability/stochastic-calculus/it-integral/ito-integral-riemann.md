# Itô integral of a Riemann step approximation

**Itô integral of a Riemann step approximation.** The elementary Itô integral of `riemann F g t n` at time `t` is the Itô–Riemann sum
`∑_{k < n} g (t k / n) (B (t (k + 1) / n) - B (t k / n))`.
