# Elementary integrands

**Elementary integrands.** The Itô integral of `ξ 1_{(a, b]}` is
`ξ (B (b ∧ t) - B (a ∧ t))` (Karatzas–Shreve 1991, Section 3.2.A); this holds for every
process `B` and every `ω`, by definition.
