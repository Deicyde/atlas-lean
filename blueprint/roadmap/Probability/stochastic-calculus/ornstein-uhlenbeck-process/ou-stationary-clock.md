# Doob's clock for the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

**Doob's clock for the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process.** Doob's clock `t ↦ e^{2θt}/(2θ)` for the stationary process (positive for `θ > 0`).
