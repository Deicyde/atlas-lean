# Stationary start

**Stationary start.** Driven by a Brownian motion `B` and started from `X₀ ~ N(0, σ²/(2θ))`
independent of `B`, `ouPathSolution` is a stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process
(Karatzas–Shreve 1991, Section 5.6).
