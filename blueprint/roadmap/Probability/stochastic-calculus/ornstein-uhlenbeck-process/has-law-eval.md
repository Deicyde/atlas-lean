# One-time law of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process from zero

**One-time law of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process from zero.** The one-time law of the OU process started at `0`:
`X t ~ N(0, (σ² / (2θ)) (1 - e^{-2θt}))`.
