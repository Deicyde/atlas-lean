# The Gibbs density has zero flux

**The Gibbs density has zero flux** under the gradient drift `-∇Ψ`: for differentiable `Ψ`,
`D ≠ 0` and any constant `c`, `J = -∇Ψ · c e^{-Ψ/D} - D ∇(c e^{-Ψ/D}) = 0`.
