# Positivity of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold

**Positivity of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold.** `p̃_c(d) > 0` for `d ≥ 1`: at `p = 1/(4d + 1)`, `φ_p({0}) ≤ 2dp < 1`.
