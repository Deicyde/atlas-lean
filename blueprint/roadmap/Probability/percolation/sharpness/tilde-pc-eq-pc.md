# Equality of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold and $p_c$

**Equality of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold and $p_c$.** `p̃_c = p_c`, assuming exponential decay on `tildePcSet`, `θ(1) ≠ 0`, and `θ(q) > 0` for
`p̃_c < q < 1`.
