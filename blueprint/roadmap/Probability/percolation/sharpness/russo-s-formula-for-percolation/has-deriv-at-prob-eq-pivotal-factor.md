# Russo's formula

**Russo's formula** in pivotal-factor form: for increasing `A` and `0 < p < 1`,
`d/dp ℙ_p(A) = (p(1 - p))⁻¹ ∑_e 𝔼_p[1_A (ω_e - p)]`.
