# Mean-field lower bound

**Mean-field lower bound** (Duminil-Copin–Tassion 2016): for
`p̃_c < q < 1` (with `p̃_c > 0`), `θ(q) ≥ (q - p̃_c) / (q(1 - p̃_c))`.
