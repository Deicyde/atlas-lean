# Right-continuity of θ at p_c

For Bernoulli bond percolation on `ℤ^d`, the map
`p ↦ θ_d(p)` is right-continuous at `p_c(d)`. Source: H. Duminil-Copin, *Introduction to
Bernoulli percolation*, lecture notes (2018), Proposition 3.2.

## Background

- [Continuity of θ away from p_c](theta-continuous-on-ne-pc.md): the companion statement; left-continuity at `p_c` is the open question whether `θ(p_c) = 0`, settled on `ℤ²` by [θ(1/2) = 0](../critical-probability-of-the-square-lattice/theta-two-half-eq-zero.md).
