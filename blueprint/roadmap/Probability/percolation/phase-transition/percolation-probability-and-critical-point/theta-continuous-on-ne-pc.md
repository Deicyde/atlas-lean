# Continuity of θ away from p_c

For Bernoulli bond percolation on `ℤ^d`, the map
`p ↦ θ_d(p)` is continuous on `[0, 1] \ {p_c(d)}`. Source: H. Duminil-Copin, *Introduction to
Bernoulli percolation*, lecture notes (2018), Proposition 3.2; the proof there uses the
uniqueness of the infinite cluster (Theorem 3.1) for continuity from the left above `p_c`.

## Informally uses

- [Burton–Keane theorem](../uniqueness-of-the-infinite-cluster/burton-keane-bernoulli.md): the source's proof of continuity from the left above `p_c` uses the uniqueness of the infinite cluster.
