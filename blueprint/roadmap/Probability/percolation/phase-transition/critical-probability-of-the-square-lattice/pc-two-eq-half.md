# Kesten's theorem

**Kesten's theorem** (Kesten 1980): the critical probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on
the square lattice `ℤ²` equals `1/2`. Together with `theta_two_half_eq_zero` it gives the second
assertion of the source theorem, that there is no infinite cluster at `p_c`. Source:
H. Duminil-Copin, *Introduction to Bernoulli percolation*, lecture notes (2018), Theorem 4.3,
proved there from Corollary 4.2 and exponential decay below `p_c` (Theorem 3.3); H. Kesten,
Comm. Math. Phys. 74 (1980).

## Informally uses

- [No infinite cluster at p = 1/2 on ℤ²](theta-two-half-eq-zero.md): gives `p_c(2) ≥ 1/2` (Corollary 4.2 of the lecture notes).
- [Exponential decay below p_c](../../sharpness/sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-bernoulli/cross-prob-exp-decay-of-lt-pc.md): for `p < p_c` the probability of a left–right open crossing of `R_n = [0, n] × [0, n - 1]` tends to `0`, while it equals `1/2` at `p = 1/2` by self-duality (equation (4.1) of the lecture notes); hence `p_c ≤ 1/2`.

## Background

- [Russo–Seymour–Welsh theory](../../crossings/russo-seymour-welsh-theory/README.md): the crossing estimates that describe percolation at `p_c = 1/2`.
