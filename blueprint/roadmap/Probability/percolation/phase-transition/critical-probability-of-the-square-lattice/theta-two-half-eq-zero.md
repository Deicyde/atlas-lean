# No infinite cluster at `p = 1/2` on `ℤ²`

**No infinite cluster at `p = 1/2` on `ℤ²`** (Harris 1960): for Bernoulli bond percolation on
the square lattice, `θ_2(1/2) = ℙ_{1/2}(0 is in an infinite cluster) = 0`. In particular
`p_c(2) ≥ 1/2`. Source: H. Duminil-Copin, *Introduction to Bernoulli percolation*, lecture notes
(2018), Corollary 4.2, proved there from the self-duality identity `ℙ_{1/2}(H_n) = 1/2` (4.1) and
Proposition 4.1, which uses the uniqueness of the infinite cluster; T. E. Harris, Proc. Cambridge
Philos. Soc. 56 (1960).

## Informally uses

- [Burton–Keane theorem](../uniqueness-of-the-infinite-cluster/burton-keane-bernoulli.md): uniqueness of the infinite cluster (Theorem 3.1 of the lecture notes), used in the proof of Proposition 4.1 there: if `θ(p) > 0`, the crossing probability of `R_n` tends to `1`.
- [Harris inequality](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/harris-inequality/harris-inequality.md): through the square-root trick (Exercise 11 of the lecture notes), used in the proof of Proposition 4.1.
