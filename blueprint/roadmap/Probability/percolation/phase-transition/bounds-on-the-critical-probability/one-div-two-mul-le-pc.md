# Path-counting lower bound on the critical probability

For bond percolation on `ℤ^d`,
`d ≥ 1`, `p_c(d) ≥ 1/(2d)`: a self-avoiding path of length `n` from the origin is open with
probability `p^n` and there are at most `(2d)^n` of them, so `θ(p) ≤ (2dp)^n → 0` for
`p < 1/(2d)` (H. Duminil-Copin, *Introduction to Bernoulli percolation*, lecture notes, IHÉS,
version of 7 October 2018, proof of Theorem 1.1).

## Background

- [Positivity of the critical probability](pc-pos.md): the weaker statement `0 < p_c(d)`, proved with `p = 1/(4d)`.
- [Non-triviality of the percolation critical probability](pc-nontrivial-of-two-le.md): the matching upper bound `p_c(d) < 1` for `d ≥ 2`.

## Sources
