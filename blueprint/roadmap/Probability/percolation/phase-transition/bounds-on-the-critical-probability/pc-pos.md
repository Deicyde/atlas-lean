# Positivity of the critical probability (Hammersley's bound)

**Positivity of the critical probability (Hammersley's bound).** `p_c(d) > 0` for bond percolation on `ℤ^d`, `d ≥ 1` (Hammersley 1957, by counting paths:
`p_c(d) ≥ 1/(2d)`).
