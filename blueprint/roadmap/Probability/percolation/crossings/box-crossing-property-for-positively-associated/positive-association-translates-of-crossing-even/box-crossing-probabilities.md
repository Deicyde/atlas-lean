# Set of long-direction box-crossing probabilities

**Set of long-direction box-crossing probabilities.** The set of crossing probabilities, for `n ≥ n₀` and all translates, of the long direction of
`⌊ρn⌋ × n` and `n × ⌊ρn⌋` rectangles.
