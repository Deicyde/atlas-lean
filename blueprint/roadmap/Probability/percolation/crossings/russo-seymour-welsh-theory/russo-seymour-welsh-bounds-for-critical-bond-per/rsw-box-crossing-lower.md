# Russo–Seymour–Welsh theorem

**Russo–Seymour–Welsh theorem** (lower bound) for critical bond percolation on `ℤ²`: for
every `α > 0` there is `c > 0` with `ℙ_{1/2}(open left–right crossing of [0, ⌊αn⌋] × [0, n]) ≥ c`
for every `n`.
