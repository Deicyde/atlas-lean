# Critical bond percolation measure on the square lattice

**Critical bond percolation measure on the square lattice.** The critical bond percolation measure `ℙ_{1/2}` on `{0, 1}^{Sym2 ℤ²}`: every coordinate is
open independently with probability `1/2`.
