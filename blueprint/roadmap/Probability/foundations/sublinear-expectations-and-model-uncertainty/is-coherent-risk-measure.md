# Coherent risk measure

**Coherent risk measure** (Peng, arXiv:1002.4546v1, Chapter I, Proposition 5.5, after
Artzner, Delbaen, Eber and Heath, 1999). A functional `ρ` on the space `H` of risk positions
(containing the constants) is a coherent risk measure if it is antitone, satisfies
`ρ(1) = -ρ(-1) = -1`, and is subadditive and positively homogeneous on `H`.
