# Upper tail of the Poisson-binomial law

**Upper tail of the Poisson-binomial law.** Upper tail `P(S ≥ k) = ∑_{k ≤ j ≤ m} P(S = j)` of the Poisson-binomial law (the mass
function vanishes above `m`).
