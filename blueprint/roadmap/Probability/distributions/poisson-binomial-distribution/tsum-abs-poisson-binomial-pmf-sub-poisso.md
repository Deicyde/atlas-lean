# Le Cam's inequality

**Le Cam's inequality** (Le Cam 1960): the series `∑_j |P(S = j) - e^{-λ} λ^j / j!|` is
summable and its sum is at most `2 ∑ (p i)²`, where `λ = ∑ p i`.
