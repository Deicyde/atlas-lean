# Mean of $X(1 - X)$ under a beta law

**Mean of $X(1 - X)$ under a beta law.** For `α, β > 0`, `E[X (1 - X)] = α β / ((α + β) (α + β + 1))` (half the expected
heterozygosity of a `Beta(α, β)` allele frequency).
