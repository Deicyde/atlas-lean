# Beta-function identity for densities

**Beta-function identity for densities**: multiplying the `Beta(α, β)` density by
`x ^ k (1 - x) ^ m` gives `B(α + k, β + m) / B(α, β)` times the `Beta(α + k, β + m)` density.
