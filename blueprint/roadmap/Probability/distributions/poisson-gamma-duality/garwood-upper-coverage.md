# Exact coverage of Garwood's bound

**Exact coverage of Garwood's bound**: for every mean `r ≥ 0` and `0 < α < 1`,
`P_r(r ≤ u_α(K)) ≥ 1 - α` when `K ~ Poisson(r)`.
