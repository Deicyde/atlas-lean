# Negative binomial masses sum to one

**Negative binomial masses sum to one.** The negative binomial masses sum to one for `r > 0` and `p ∈ (0, 1]` (the binomial series
`∑ₖ r^{(k)} xᵏ / k! = (1 - x)^{-r}` at `x = 1 - p`).
