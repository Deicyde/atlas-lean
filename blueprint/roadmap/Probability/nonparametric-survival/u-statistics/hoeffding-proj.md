# Hoeffding projection of a kernel

**Hoeffding projection of a kernel.** Hoeffding's `c`-th projection of a kernel `h` of degree `m` under `μ`:
`h_c(y) = E h(y₁, …, y_c, X_{c+1}, …, X_m)` with `X_{c+1}, …, X_m` i.i.d. `μ`.
