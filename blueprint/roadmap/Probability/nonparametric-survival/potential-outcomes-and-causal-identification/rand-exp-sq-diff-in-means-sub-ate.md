# Neyman (1923)

**Neyman (1923)**: the variance of the difference in means under complete randomization is
`S₁²/n₁ + S₀²/n₀ - S_τ²/N`, where `S_τ²` is the population variance of the unit-level effects
`τᵢ = y₁ᵢ - y₀ᵢ` and `n₀ = N - n₁`.
