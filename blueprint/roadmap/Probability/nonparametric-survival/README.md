# Nonparametric statistics and survival analysis

Distribution-free and semiparametric methods: empirical distribution functions (Glivenko–Cantelli), U-statistics, the Kaplan–Meier estimator, Cox partial likelihood and the log-rank test, and design-based causal inference with potential outcomes. Park and Pham's theorem on when the supremum of a positive empirical process over a finite family of nonnegative functions is large compared with its expectation is under [suprema of selector and empirical processes](../high-dimensional-probability/suprema-of-selector-and-empirical-processes/README.md) in High-dimensional and geometric probability.
