# Survival function of the Kaplan–Meier measure

**Survival function of the Kaplan–Meier measure.** The survival function of the Kaplan–Meier measure is the Kaplan–Meier estimate on
`(-∞, τ)`: `P(T > t) = Ŝ(t)` for `t < τ`.
