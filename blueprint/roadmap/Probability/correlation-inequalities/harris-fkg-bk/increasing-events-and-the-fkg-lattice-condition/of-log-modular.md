# Log-modular weights satisfy the FKG lattice condition

**Log-modular weights satisfy the FKG lattice condition.** A log-modular weight, `π ω * π ω' = π (ω ⊔ ω') * π (ω ⊓ ω')`, satisfies the FKG lattice
condition.
