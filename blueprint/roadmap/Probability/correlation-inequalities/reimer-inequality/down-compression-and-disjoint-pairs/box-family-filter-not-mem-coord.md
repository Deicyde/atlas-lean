# Members of a disjoint-union family avoiding a coordinate

**Members of a disjoint-union family avoiding a coordinate.** The members of `𝒜 ○ ℬ` avoiding `i` are exactly the disjoint unions of members of `𝒜` and
`ℬ` avoiding `i`.
