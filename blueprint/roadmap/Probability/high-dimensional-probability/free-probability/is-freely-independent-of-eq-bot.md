# Scalars are free from everything

In a noncommutative probability space ($\varphi(1) = 1$), a family of unital subalgebras in which
every member except one is the algebra of scalars $\mathbb{C}1$ is
[freely independent](is-freely-independent.md). A centred scalar $c1$ has $c = \varphi(c1) = 0$, and
in an alternating word of length at least two one of the first two letters is such a scalar, so the
word is $0$. In particular any element is free from $0$, which shows that the hypotheses of
[additivity of free cumulants](free-cumulant-add-of-are-free.md) can be met.
