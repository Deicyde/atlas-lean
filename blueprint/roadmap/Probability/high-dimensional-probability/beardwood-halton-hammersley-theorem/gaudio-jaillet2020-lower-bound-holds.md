# Gaudio–Jaillet lower bound for the BHH constant

**Gaudio–Jaillet lower bound for the BHH constant.** The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley
constant $\beta_2$ of the unit square satisfies $0.6277 \le \beta_2$.
