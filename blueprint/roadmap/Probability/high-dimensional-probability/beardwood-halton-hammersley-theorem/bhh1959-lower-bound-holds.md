# Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley lower bound

**Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley lower bound.** The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley constant $\beta_2$
for uniform points in the unit square satisfies $\beta_2 \ge 5/8 = 0.625$.
