# Cyclic successor

**Cyclic successor.** For a natural number $n$ and an index $i$ with $0 \le i < n$, the cyclic
successor of $i$ is $(i + 1) \bmod n$; in particular the successor of $n - 1$ is $0$. For $n = 0$
there are no indices.
