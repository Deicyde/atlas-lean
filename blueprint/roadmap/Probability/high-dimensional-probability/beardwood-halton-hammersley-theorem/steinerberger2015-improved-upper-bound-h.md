# Steinerberger's upper bound for the BHH constant

**Steinerberger's upper bound for the BHH constant.** The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley constant
$\beta_2$ satisfies $\beta_2 \le 0.92117 - \frac{9}{16} \cdot 10^{-6}$.
