# Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley upper bound

**Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley upper bound.** The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley constant $\beta_2$
for uniform points in the unit square satisfies $\beta_2 \le 0.92117$.
