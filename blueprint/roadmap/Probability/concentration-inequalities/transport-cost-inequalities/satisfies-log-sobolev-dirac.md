# Dirac masses satisfy the logarithmic Sobolev inequality

For every $\alpha$, the Dirac mass $\delta_{x_0}$ satisfies the
[logarithmic Sobolev inequality](satisfies-log-sobolev.md) with constant $\alpha$: a density with
$\int f \, d\delta_{x_0} = 1$ has $f(x_0) = 1$, so $f \delta_{x_0} = \delta_{x_0}$ and the entropy
vanishes. This is a non-vacuity check for the definition.
