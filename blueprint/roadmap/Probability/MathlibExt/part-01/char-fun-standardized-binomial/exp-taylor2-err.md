# Second-order Taylor remainder bound for `exp`

**Second-order Taylor remainder bound for `exp`.**

For `‖z‖ ≤ 1`, the remainder after the degree-2 Taylor polynomial of `exp` satisfies
`‖e^z − 1 − z − z²/2‖ ≤ 2 ‖z‖³`. Used in the proof of the De Moivre–Laplace CLT.
