# Standardized binomial distribution

**Standardized binomial distribution**.

The law of `(Sₙ − np) / √(np(1−p))` where `Sₙ ~ Binomial(n, p)`. This is the
standardized sum appearing in the De Moivre–Laplace limit theorem.
