# Cond Indep

The variables in `A` and in `B` are **conditionally independent given** those in `S` under
the weight `p`: `p(x_A, x_B, x_S) p(x_S) = p(x_A, x_S) p(x_B, x_S)` for every configuration,
where `p(x_U)` is `marginal p U x`.
