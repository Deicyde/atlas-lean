# The Dirichlet integral

**The Dirichlet integral**: `∫ ∏ xᵢ^(αᵢ-1) (1 - ∑ x)^(αₙ-1) dx = ∏ Γ(αᵢ) / Γ(∑ α)` over the
corner simplex, for positive parameters.
