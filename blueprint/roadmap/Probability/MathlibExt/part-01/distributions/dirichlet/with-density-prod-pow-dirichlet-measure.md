# Dirichlet–multinomial conjugacy

**Dirichlet–multinomial conjugacy**, density form: weighting `Dir(α)` by the likelihood
`∏ θᵢ^kᵢ` of counts `k` gives `B(α + k) / B(α)` times `Dir(α + k)`.
