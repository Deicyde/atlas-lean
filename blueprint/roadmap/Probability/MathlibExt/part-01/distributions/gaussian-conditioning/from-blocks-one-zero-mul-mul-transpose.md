# From Blocks one zero mul mul transpose

The joint covariance `[[S, S Hᵀ], [H S, H S Hᵀ + R]]` of `(x, H x + v)` is
`[[1, 0], [H, 1]] diag(S, R) [[1, 0], [H, 1]]ᵀ`.
