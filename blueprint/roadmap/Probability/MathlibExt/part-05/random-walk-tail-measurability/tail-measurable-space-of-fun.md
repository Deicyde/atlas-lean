# Tail Measurable Space Of Fun

The tail σ-algebra of a sequence of random variables `X : ℕ → Ω → β`,
defined as `𝓣 = ⋂ₙ σ(X n, X (n+1), …)`. This is the standard tail σ-algebra
appearing in Kolmogorov's 0-1 law.
