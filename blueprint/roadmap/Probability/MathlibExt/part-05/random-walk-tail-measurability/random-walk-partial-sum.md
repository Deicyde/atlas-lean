# Partial sums of a random walk

**Partial sums of a random walk.** For a sequence of functions
$X_i : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th partial sum is
$S_n(\omega) = \sum_{i < n} X_i(\omega)$, the position at time $n$ of the random walk on
$\mathbb{R}$ with increments $X_i$.
