# Tail Measurable Space From

Given a sequence of σ-algebras `𝓐 i` on `Ω`, `tailMeasurableSpaceFrom 𝓐 n`
is the σ-algebra generated by `𝓐 i` for all `i ≥ n`, i.e. `σ(𝓐 n, 𝓐 (n+1), …)`.
