# Tail Measurable Space

The **tail σ-algebra** of a sequence of σ-algebras `𝓐 i`, defined as
`𝓣 = ⋂ₙ σ(𝓐 n, 𝓐 (n+1), …)`. It contains the information observable by looking
only at events arbitrarily far into the future.
