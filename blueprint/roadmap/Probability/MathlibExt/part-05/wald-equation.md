# Wald's equation

**Wald's equation.** Let `Xᵢ` be identically distributed integrable real
random variables and `T` a stopping time with `E T < ∞`, with each `Xₖ`
independent of the filtration at time `k`. Then `E[S_T] = E[X₁] · E[T]`,
where `S_T = ∑_{i < T} Xᵢ`.
