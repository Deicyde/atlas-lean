# Level sets of a stopping time are measurable

**Level sets of a stopping time are measurable.** Let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a
filtration on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time
for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the set $\{\omega : T(\omega) = n\}$ is
measurable in the σ-algebra of $\Omega$.
