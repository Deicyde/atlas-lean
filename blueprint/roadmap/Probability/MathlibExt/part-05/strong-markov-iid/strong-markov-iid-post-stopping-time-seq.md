# Strong Markov property for i.i.d. sequences: the post-stopping-time sequence is i.i.d

**Strong Markov property for i.i.d. sequences: the post-stopping-time sequence is i.i.d.**
Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let
$(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a filtration on $\Omega$, and let
$X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable space $S$ that are
mutually independent and identically distributed under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the
σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then the
post-stopping-time sequence $Y_k(\omega) = X_{T(\omega)+k+1}(\omega)$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, is
again i.i.d. with the law of $X_0$: each $Y_k$ has the same distribution under $\mu$ as $X_0$,
and $(Y_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ is mutually independent under $\mu$.
