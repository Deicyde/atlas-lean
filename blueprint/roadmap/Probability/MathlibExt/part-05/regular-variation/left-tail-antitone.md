# Monotonicity of the left tail

**Monotonicity of the left tail.** For every finite measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the left tail
of $\mu$ is antitone in the threshold: if $a \le b$, then
$\mu((-\infty, -b)) \le \mu((-\infty, -a))$.
