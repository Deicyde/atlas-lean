# Strong Markov property (independence from the past)

**Strong Markov property (independence from the past).**

For the stated i.i.d. and natural-filtration hypotheses, the stopped
σ-algebra `ℱ_T` is independent of the σ-algebra generated by
`postStoppingTimeSeq X T`.
