# Norm exp of Real mul I sub one sub le

Second-order Taylor estimate for the complex exponential along the
imaginary axis, in the range where it is needed: if `‖x‖ ≤ 1` then
`‖e^{ix} - 1 - ix‖ ≤ x²`.
