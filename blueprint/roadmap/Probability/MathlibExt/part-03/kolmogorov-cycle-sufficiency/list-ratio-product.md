# List Ratio Product

The product of ratios `P(xᵢ, xᵢ₊₁) / P(xᵢ₊₁, xᵢ)` along a path.
This is the quantity that defines the "cycle weight" used to construct a
reversible measure from Kolmogorov's cycle condition.
