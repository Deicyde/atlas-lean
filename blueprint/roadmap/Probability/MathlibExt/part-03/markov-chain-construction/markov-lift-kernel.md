# Markov Lift Kernel

Lift the Markov kernel `κ : S → S` to a kernel on tuples `(x₀, …, xₙ)` by reading off the
last coordinate `xₙ` and applying `κ`. This is the form required by the Ionescu–Tulcea trajectory
construction.
