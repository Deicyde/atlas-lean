# Discrete torus

**Discrete torus.** For natural numbers $d$ and $n$, the vertices of the discrete torus
$\mathbb{Z}_n^d$ are the functions from $\{0, \dots, d-1\}$ to $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, that is, the
$d$-tuples of residues modulo $n$.
