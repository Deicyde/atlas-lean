# Dropping the first term preserves the limsup

**Dropping the first term preserves the limsup.** For every sequence
$(u_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of extended reals,
$\limsup_{n \to \infty} u_{n+1} = \limsup_{n \to \infty} u_n$.
