# A real sequence tending to $+\infty$ has liminf $+\infty$

**A real sequence tending to $+\infty$ has liminf $+\infty$.** If a real sequence $(S_n)$
tends to $+\infty$, then its limit inferior in the extended reals $[-\infty, +\infty]$ is
$+\infty$.
