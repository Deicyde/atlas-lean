# Shift of a real sequence

**Shift of a real sequence.** The one-step shift sends a real sequence
$\omega = (\omega_0, \omega_1, \omega_2, \dots)$ to the sequence $(\omega_1, \omega_2, \dots)$
whose $i$-th term is $\omega_{i+1}$.
