# Markov property

**Markov property.** Let $S$ be a nonempty standard Borel space, $\mu$ a finite measure on
$S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$ the path measure on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the
Markov chain with initial measure $\mu$ and transition kernel $\kappa$. Let
$Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and measurable for the product $\sigma$-algebra,
let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\theta_n$ be the shift $(\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}$, and let
$\mathcal{F}_n$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the coordinates $\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n$.
For $x \in S$, let $E_x[Y]$ be the integral of $Y$ against the path measure of the chain started
at $x$. Then for $P_\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$E_{P_\mu}[Y \circ \theta_n \mid \mathcal{F}_n](\omega) = E_{\omega_n}[Y].$$

In other words, conditioning on the trajectory up to time $n$ depends only on the state at
time $n$.
