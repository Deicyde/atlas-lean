# Markov property, set-integral form

**Markov property, set-integral form.** Let $S$ be a nonempty standard Borel space, $\mu$ a
finite measure on $S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$ the path measure on
$S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the Markov chain with initial measure $\mu$ and transition kernel $\kappa$.
Let $Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and measurable for the product
$\sigma$-algebra, let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\theta_n$ be the shift
$(\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}$, and let $\mathcal{F}_n$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by
the coordinates $\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n$. For $x \in S$, let $E_x[Y]$ be the integral of $Y$
against the path measure of the chain started at $x$. Then for every set $A \in \mathcal{F}_n$,

$$\int_A Y(\theta_n \omega) \, dP_\mu(\omega) = \int_A E_{\omega_n}[Y] \, dP_\mu(\omega).$$
