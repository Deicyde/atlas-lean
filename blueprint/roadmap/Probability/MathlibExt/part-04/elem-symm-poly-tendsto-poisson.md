# Analytic core of Poisson convergence

**Analytic core of Poisson convergence** (corrected form; see the file
docstring for the correction). If the rows `p n : Fin (k n) → ℝ` are
nonnegative, have row maxima tending to `0` and row sums tending to `λ`,
then the Poisson-binomial mass at `j` tends to the Poisson mass
`λ ^ j e ^ (-λ) / j !`.
