# Partial sums of indicators

**Partial sums of indicators.** For sets $A_0, A_1, \dots$ in $\Omega$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
$S_n = \sum_{i < n} 1_{A_i}$ is the sum of the indicators of the first $n$ sets, viewed as a
real-valued function on $\Omega$.
