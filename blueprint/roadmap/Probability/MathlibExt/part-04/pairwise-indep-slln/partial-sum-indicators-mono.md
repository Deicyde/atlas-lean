# Partial sums of indicators are nondecreasing

**Partial sums of indicators are nondecreasing.** For sets $A_0, A_1, \dots$ and every
$\omega \in \Omega$, the map $n \mapsto S_n(\omega)$, where $S_n = \sum_{i < n} 1_{A_i}$, is
nondecreasing.
