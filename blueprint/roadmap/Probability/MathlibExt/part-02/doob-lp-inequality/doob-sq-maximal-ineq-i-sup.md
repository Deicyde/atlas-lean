# Doob's L² maximal inequality, infinite horizon

**Doob's L² maximal inequality, infinite horizon.** If the martingale starts
at `0` a.e., then `E[sup_n |f n|²] ≤ 4 E[A_∞]`, where `A_∞ = sup_n A_n`.
