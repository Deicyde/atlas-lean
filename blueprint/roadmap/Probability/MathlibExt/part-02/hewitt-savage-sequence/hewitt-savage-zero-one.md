# Hewitt–Savage 0–1 law

**Hewitt–Savage 0–1 law.** If `X_1, X_2, …` is an i.i.d. sequence
(independent and identically distributed) and `E` is an exchangeable event
(i.e. `E ∈ ℰ`, the σ-field of permutable events) with respect to `X`, then
`μ E ∈ {0, 1}`.
