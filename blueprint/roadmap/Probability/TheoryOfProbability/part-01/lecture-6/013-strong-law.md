# Theorem (strong law)

If $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ are i.i.d. real-valued random variables with expectation $m$ and $A_n \coloneqq n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ are the empirical means then $\lim_{n \to \infty} A_n = m$ almost surely.
