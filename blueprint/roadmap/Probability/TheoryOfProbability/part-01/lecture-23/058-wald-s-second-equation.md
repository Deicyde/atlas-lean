# Theorem (Wald's second equation)

Let $X_i$ be i.i.d. with $E[X_i] = 0$ and $E[X_i^2] = \sigma^2 < \infty$. If $N$ is a stopping time with $E[N] < \infty$ then $E[S_N^2] = \sigma^2 E[N]$.
