# Claim (Lecture 26 / Lecture 27, Martingale observations)

If $X_n$ is a martingale w.r.t. $\mathcal{F}_n$ and $\varphi$ is convex with $E|\varphi(X_n)| < \infty$ then $\varphi(X_n)$ is a submartingale.
