# Theorem (Central Limit Theorem)

Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ be i.i.d. with $E[X_i] = \mu$, $\operatorname{Var}(X_i) = \sigma^2 \in (0, \infty)$. If $S_n = X_1 + \cdots + X_n$ then $(S_n - n\mu) / (\sigma \sqrt{n})$ converges in law to a standard normal.
