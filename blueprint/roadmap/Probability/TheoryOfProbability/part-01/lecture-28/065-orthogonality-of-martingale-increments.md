# Theorem (Orthogonality of martingale increments)

Let $X_n$ be a martingale with $E[X_n^2] < \infty$ for all $n$. If $m \leq n$ and $Y \in \mathcal{F}_m$ with $E[Y^2] < \infty$, then $E[(X_n - X_m) Y] = 0$.
