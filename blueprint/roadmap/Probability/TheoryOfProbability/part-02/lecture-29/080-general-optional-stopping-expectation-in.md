# Theorem (General optional stopping, expectation inequality)

Let $X_n$ be a uniformly integrable submartingale. For any stopping time $N \leq \infty$, we have $E[X_0] \leq E[X_N] \leq E[X_\infty]$ where $X_\infty = \lim X_n$.
