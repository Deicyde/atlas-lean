# Theorem (General form of optional stopping)

If $L \leq M$ are stopping times and $Y_{M \wedge n}$ is a uniformly integrable submartingale, then $E[Y_L] \leq E[Y_M]$ and $Y_L \leq E(Y_M \mid \mathcal{F}_L)$.
