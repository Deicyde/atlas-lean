# Markov property

If $s \geq 0$ and $Y$ is bounded and $\mathcal{C}$-measurable, then for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$, we have $E_x(Y \circ \theta_s \mid \mathcal{F}_s^+) = E_{B_s} Y$, where the RHS is the function $\phi(x) = E_x Y$ evaluated at $x = B_s$.
