# Beurling–Deny: a closed form is Markovian iff its resolvent is Markovian

Let `E` be a
closed symmetric form on `L²(X; m)`, `m` σ-finite. For `α > 0` and `f ∈ L²`, the resolvent
`G_α f` is the element `u ∈ D[E]` with `E(u, v) + α (u, v) = (f, v)` for all `v ∈ D[E]`
(FOT, equation (1.3.7)). Then the unit contraction operates on `E` if and only if every `α G_α`
is Markovian: `0 ≤ f ≤ 1` a.e. implies `0 ≤ α G_α f ≤ 1` a.e. (Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda,
*Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd ed., 2011, Theorem 1.4.1, (b) ⇔ (d)). FOT
prove (b) ⇒ (e) under their topological assumption (1.1.7) and note (footnote 10) that it holds
without it, referring to Ma–Röckner (1992) and Bouleau–Hirsch (1991); the statement here has no
topological assumption.
