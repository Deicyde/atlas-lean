# Fukushima's theorem: regular Dirichlet forms have Hunt processes

Let `X` be a locally
compact separable metric space and `m` a positive Radon measure on `X` with full support. Every
regular Dirichlet form on `L²(X; m)` is the Dirichlet form of an `m`-symmetric Hunt process on
`X` (Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd ed.,
2011, Theorem 7.2.1; the process is unique up to equivalence by their Theorem 4.2.8). Separable
metrizable is expressed as metrizable and second countable; "Radon" as finite on compact sets,
which for Borel measures on such spaces implies regularity.
