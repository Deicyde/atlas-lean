# Existence of an associated `m`-symmetric Hunt process (opaque)

The proposition that
there is an `m`-symmetric Hunt process `M` on `X` properly associated with `E`: the transition
function `p_t` of `M` satisfies that `p_t f` is an `E`-quasi-continuous `m`-version of `T_t f`
for every bounded Borel `f ∈ L²(X; m)`, where `(T_t)` is the semigroup of `E` (FOT, Theorem 4.2.3
and Theorem 7.2.1; M. Fukushima, Kodaira prize lecture, Tokyo, 2023, Theorem 3.1). A Hunt
process is a strong Markov process with right-continuous paths having left limits, quasi-left-
continuous on its lifetime. Mathlib has no Hunt processes, so this is a `def_wanted`.
