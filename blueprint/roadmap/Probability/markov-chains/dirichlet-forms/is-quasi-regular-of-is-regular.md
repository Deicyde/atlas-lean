# Regular forms are quasi-regular

Let `X` be a locally compact separable metric space and
`m` a positive Radon measure on `X`. A regular Dirichlet form on `L²(X; m)` is quasi-regular
(M. Röckner and B. Schmuland, *Quasi-regular Dirichlet forms: examples and counterexamples*,
Canad. J. Math. 47 (1995), Proposition 0.9, stated there for regular semi-Dirichlet forms; cf.
Ma–Röckner 1992, Chapter IV, Example 4a). The converse fails even on compact spaces
(Röckner–Schmuland, Remark 0.10, on a non-metrizable compact space).
