# Existence of an associated right process (opaque)

The proposition that there is a
normal, right-continuous, strong Markov process `M` on `X` (an `m`-tight special standard
process) associated with `E`: its transition function `p_t` satisfies that `p_t f` is an
`m`-version of `T_t f` for bounded Borel `f ∈ L²(X; m)`, `(T_t)` being the semigroup of `E`
(M. Röckner and B. Schmuland, *Canad. J. Math.* 47 (1995), Theorem 0.1; Ma–Röckner 1992,
Chapters IV and V). Mathlib has no right processes, so this is a `def_wanted`.
