# Mean hitting time, first-step equation

**Mean hitting time, first-step equation** (compare Norris 1997, §3.3): from `i ∉ A`
with `q i = -Q i i ≠ 0`, `E_i H_A = 1 / q i + ∑ⱼ Π i j E_j H_A` in `ℝ≥0∞`.
