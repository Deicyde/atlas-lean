# Product moment of two hitting times, first-step equation

**Product moment of two hitting times, first-step equation**: from `i ∉ A ∪ B` with
`q i ≠ 0`, `E_i (H_A H_B) = (E_i H_A + E_i H_B) / q i + ∑ⱼ Π i j E_j (H_A H_B)` in `ℝ≥0∞`.
