# Minimality of the mean hitting time

**Minimality of the mean hitting time** (compare Norris 1997, §3.3): if every state off
`A` has a positive exit rate, every `m : ι → ℝ≥0∞` with `1 / q i + ∑ⱼ Π i j m j ≤ m i` off `A`
dominates `E_i H_A`.
