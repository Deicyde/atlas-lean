# A Poincaré inequality bounds the spectral gap

Under the hypotheses of [the eigenvector-orthogonal version](one-le-mul-one-sub-of-poincare-of-mean-z.md), every real eigenvalue $\mu \ne 1$ of $P$ satisfies $1 \le B(1-\mu)$: its eigenvectors are orthogonal to the constants. A. Sinclair, *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* 1 (1992), proves Theorems 5 and 5′ this way, with $B = \bar\rho(\Gamma)$ for canonical paths and $B = \bar\rho(f)$ for flows.

## Background

- [Sinclair's Poincaré hypothesis is a lower bound on the spectral gap](../dirichlet-forms/inv-le-spectral-gap-iff-poincare.md):
  the hypothesis of this theorem is `1/B ≤ λ` for the spectral gap `λ` defined variationally from the
  Dirichlet form (Diaconis and Saloff-Coste 1996, equation (1.4)).
