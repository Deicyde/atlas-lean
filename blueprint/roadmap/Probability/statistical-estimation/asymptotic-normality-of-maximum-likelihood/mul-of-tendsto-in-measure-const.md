# Slutsky's theorem (product form)

If the real random variables `X n` converge in
distribution to `Z` and `Y n` (on the same space as the `X n`) converge in probability to the
constant `c`, then `X n * Y n` converges in distribution to `c * Z`.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Lemma 2.8 (Slutsky).
