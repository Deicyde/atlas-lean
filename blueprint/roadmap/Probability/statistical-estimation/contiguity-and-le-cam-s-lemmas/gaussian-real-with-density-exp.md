# Exponential tilting of a centered Gaussian

**Exponential tilting of a centered Gaussian.** The law `N(0, v)` reweighted by the density
`exp(h x - h² v / 2)` is `N(h v, v)`. For `v = 0` both sides are the Dirac mass at `0`.
