# Statistical estimation

Point estimation theory: bias, risk and mean squared error, likelihood and maximum likelihood (consistency and asymptotic normality), Fisher information and Cramér–Rao, sufficiency, factorization (Fisher–Neyman, Halmos–Savage) and Rao–Blackwell, Stein's lemma and James–Stein shrinkage, and contiguity and Le Cam's lemmas. The local asymptotic normality of the log-likelihood ratio is under [local power of the likelihood-ratio test](../hypothesis-testing/local-power-of-the-likelihood-ratio-test/README.md) in Hypothesis testing. Penalized least squares (ridge regression, and the lasso with its soft-thresholding solution for an orthonormal design) is under [least squares, ridge and lasso regression](../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/supervised-learning/least-squares-ridge-and-lasso-regression/README.md) in Machine learning.
