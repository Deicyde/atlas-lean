# James–Stein

**James–Stein** (1961): for `p ≥ 3` and `X ~ N(θ, I_p)`, `‖X‖⁻²` is integrable and the risk
of the James–Stein estimator is `p - (p - 2)² E[‖X‖⁻²]`.
