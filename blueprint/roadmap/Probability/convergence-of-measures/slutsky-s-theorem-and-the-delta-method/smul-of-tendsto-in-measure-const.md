# Slutsky's theorem, scalar-multiple form

Let random vectors $X_n$ in a second-countable real normed space, indexed along a countably generated
filter such as $n \to \infty$, converge in distribution to $Z$, and let real random variables $Y_n$ on
the same probability space as the $X_n$ converge in probability to a constant $c$. Then $Y_n X_n$
converges in distribution to $cZ$.
