# Factorial in terms of the Stirling sequence

**Factorial in terms of the Stirling sequence.** Rewriting of `m!` in terms of Mathlib's `Stirling.stirlingSeq`:
`m! = stirlingSeq(m) · √(2m) · (m/e)^m`.
