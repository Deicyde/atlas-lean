# Gaussian approximation to the symmetric binomial point mass

**Gaussian approximation to the symmetric binomial point mass.** The Gaussian (local CLT) approximation to `binomProbHalf n k`,
`√(2/(π n)) · exp(-(k - n/2)² / (n/2))`.
