# Limit theorems under sublinear expectations

Peng's law of large numbers and central limit theorem for sequences that are i.i.d. under a sublinear expectation (each term identically distributed with, and independent from, the previous ones). The sample mean converges in law to a maximal distribution, which covers the whole interval of means `[μ_, μ̄]`, and the normalised sum of a mean-certain sequence converges in law to the G-normal distribution `N({0} × [σ²_, σ̄²])`, characterised by the G-heat equation `∂ₜu - G(∂ₓ²u) = 0`. When the variance is certain (`σ_ = σ̄`) the classical central limit theorem is recovered.
