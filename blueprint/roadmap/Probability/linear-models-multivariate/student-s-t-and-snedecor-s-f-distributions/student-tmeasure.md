# Student's t distribution

Student's t distribution with $k$ degrees of freedom (Casella and Berger, Definition 5.3.4) has the
density
$$\frac{\Gamma\big(\frac{k + 1}{2}\big)}{\sqrt{k\pi}\;\Gamma\big(\frac{k}{2}\big)} \left(1 + \frac{t^2}{k}\right)^{-(k + 1)/2}.$$
For $k \ge 1$ it is a probability distribution, and it is the law of $Z/\sqrt{V/k}$ for independent
$Z \sim N(0, 1)$ and $V \sim \chi^2_k$; for $k = 0$ the measure is defined to be zero.
