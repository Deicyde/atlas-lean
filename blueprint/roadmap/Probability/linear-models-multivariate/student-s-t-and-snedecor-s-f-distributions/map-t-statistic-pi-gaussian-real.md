# One-sample `t` statistic

**One-sample `t` statistic**: for an i.i.d. sample `X₁, …, Xₙ ~ N(μ, v)` with `n ≥ 2` and
`v > 0`, `√n (X̄ - μ) / S` with `S² = ∑ (Xᵢ - X̄)² / (n - 1)` has the Student `t` law with
`n - 1` degrees of freedom.
