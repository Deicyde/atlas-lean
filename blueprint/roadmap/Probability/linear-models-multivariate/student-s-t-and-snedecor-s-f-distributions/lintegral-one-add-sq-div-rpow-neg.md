# Normalizing integral of Student's t density

The Student `t` normalizing integral:
`∫ (1 + t²/c)^{-s} dt = √(c π) Γ(s - 1/2) / Γ(s)` for `c > 0`, `s > 1/2`.

## Sources

- G. Casella and R. L. Berger, *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed., Duxbury, 2002, Definition 5.3.4: the case $c = k$, $s = (k + 1)/2$ is the normalizing constant of Student's $t$ density.
