# Gaussian integral as a lower Lebesgue integral

For $b > 0$, $\int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{-b x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi / b}$, written as a lower Lebesgue
integral of the nonnegative integrand.

## Sources
