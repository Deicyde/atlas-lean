# Independence of orthogonal linear images of a standard Gaussian vector

For a standard Gaussian vector `Z` in `ℝ^ι` and rectangular matrices `A`, `B` with
`A Bᵀ = 0`, the linear images `A Z` and `B Z` are independent.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
