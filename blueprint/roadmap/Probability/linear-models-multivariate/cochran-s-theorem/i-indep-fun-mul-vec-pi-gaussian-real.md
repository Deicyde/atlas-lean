# Mutual independence of orthogonal linear images of a Gaussian vector

For a standard Gaussian vector `Z` in `ℝ^ι` and a family of rectangular matrices `A k` with
`A k (A l)ᵀ = 0` for `k ≠ l`, the linear images `A k Z` are mutually independent.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
