# Independence of Gaussian images under complementary symmetric matrices

For a standard Gaussian vector `Z` in `ℝ^ι` and square matrices `H`, `M` with `M` symmetric and
`H M = 0` (for instance complementary orthogonal projections), `H Z` and `M Z` are
independent.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
