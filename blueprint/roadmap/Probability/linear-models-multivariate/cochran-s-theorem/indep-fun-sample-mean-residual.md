# Independence of the Gaussian sample mean and residuals

**Independence of the Gaussian sample mean and residuals.** For an i.i.d. sample `X₁, …, Xₙ ~ N(μ, v)`, the sample mean is independent of the residual
vector `(Xᵢ - X̄)ᵢ`.
