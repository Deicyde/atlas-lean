# Independence of a Gaussian linear form and a projection quadratic form

For a standard Gaussian vector `Z` in `ℝ^ι`, a symmetric idempotent `M` and a rectangular `A`
with `A M = 0`, the linear form `A Z` is independent of the quadratic form `Zᵀ M Z`.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
