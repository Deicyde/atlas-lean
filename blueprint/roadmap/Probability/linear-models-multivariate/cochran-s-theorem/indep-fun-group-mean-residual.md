# Gaussian one-way layout

**Gaussian one-way layout**: for i.i.d. `N(μ, v)` observations `xᵢ` labelled by groups
`grp i`, the vector of group means is independent of the vector of within-group residuals
`xᵢ - x̄_{grp i}`.
