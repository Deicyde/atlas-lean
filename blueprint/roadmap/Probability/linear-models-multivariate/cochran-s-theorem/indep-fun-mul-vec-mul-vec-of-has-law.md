# Independence of orthogonal linear images of a Gaussian random vector

If `Z` has independent standard normal coordinates under `μ` and `A Bᵀ = 0`, then `A Z` and
`B Z` are independent under `μ`.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
