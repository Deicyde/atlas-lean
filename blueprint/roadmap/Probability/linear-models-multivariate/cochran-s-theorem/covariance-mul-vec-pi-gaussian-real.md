# Covariance of linear images of a standard Gaussian vector

Let $Z$ be a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, with independent $N(0, 1)$ coordinates, and
let $A$ and $B$ be real matrices with columns indexed by the finite set $\iota$. The covariance of the
coordinates $(AZ)_a$ and $(BZ)_b$ is the entry $(AB^{\mathsf{T}})_{ab}$.
