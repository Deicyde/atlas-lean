# Covariance of linear images of a standard Gaussian vector

For a standard Gaussian vector `Z` in `ℝ^ι`, the covariance of the coordinates `(A Z)_a` and
`(B Z)_b` of two linear images is the entry `(A Bᵀ)_{ab}`.

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms), the reference of the module `CochranIndependence`.
