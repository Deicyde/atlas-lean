# Aitken's theorem

**Aitken's theorem**: the GLS estimator has minimal covariance among linear unbiased
estimators: for every `A` with `A X = 1`, `A V Aᵀ - B V Bᵀ` is positive semidefinite, where
`B = glsMatrix X V`.
