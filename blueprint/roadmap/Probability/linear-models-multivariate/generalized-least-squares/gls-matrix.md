# Generalized least-squares matrix

**Generalized least-squares matrix.** The generalized least-squares matrix `(Xᵀ V⁻¹ X)⁻¹ Xᵀ V⁻¹`; `glsMatrix X V *ᵥ y` is the GLS
estimate of `b` in `y = X b + e` with `Cov e = V`.
