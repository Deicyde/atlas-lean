# The conditional covariance is the inverse of a precision block

**The conditional covariance is the inverse of a precision block**: for positive definite `S`
on `ι ⊕ κ`, `S₁₁ - S₁₂ S₂₂⁻¹ S₂₁ = ((S⁻¹)₁₁)⁻¹`.
