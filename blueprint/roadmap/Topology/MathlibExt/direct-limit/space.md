# Space

The direct limit of a directed system of topological spaces with
continuous transition maps, as a quotient of the disjoint union `Σ i, X i`
by the relation generated by the transition maps, with the quotient
topology. The `DirectedSystem` laws and the continuity hypothesis
`_hf_cont` are part of the data: they are what makes the quotient a direct
limit of topological spaces rather than an arbitrary quotient.
