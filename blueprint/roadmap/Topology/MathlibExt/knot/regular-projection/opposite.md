# Opposite dart

**Opposite dart.** For a dart diagram $D$ and a dart $d = (v, i)$ of $D$, the opposite dart
is obtained by rotating $d$ twice counterclockwise around its crossing $v$; it is the dart
$(v, i + 2)$, with labels taken modulo $4$.
