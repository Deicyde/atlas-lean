# Rotation of a dart

**Rotation of a dart.** For a dart diagram $D$, the rotation of a dart $(v, i)$ of $D$, with
$v$ a crossing and $i \in \mathbb{Z}/4$, is the dart $(v, i + 1)$: one step counterclockwise
around the crossing $v$.
