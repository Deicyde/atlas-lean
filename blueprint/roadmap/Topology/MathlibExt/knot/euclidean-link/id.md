# Identity ambient isotopy

**Identity ambient isotopy.** The identity ambient isotopy of $\mathbb{R}^3$ has forward
and inverse families $H_t$ and $G_t$ equal to the identity map of $\mathbb{R}^3$ for every
time $t$.
