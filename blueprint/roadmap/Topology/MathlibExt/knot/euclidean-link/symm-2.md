# Inverse circle reparametrization

**Inverse circle reparametrization.** The inverse of a circle reparametrization with lift
$f$ and inverse lift $f^{-1}$ is the circle reparametrization whose lift is $f^{-1}$ and whose
inverse lift is $f$.
