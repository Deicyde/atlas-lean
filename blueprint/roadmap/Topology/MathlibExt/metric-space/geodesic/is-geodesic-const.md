# Constant curves are geodesics

**Constant curves are geodesics.** Let $X$ be a pseudo-extended metric space. For every point
$p \in X$, the constant curve $\mathbb{R} \to X$, $t \mapsto p$, is a geodesic.

Its speed is $0$.
