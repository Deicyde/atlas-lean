# Brouwer's theorem for the simplex, fixed-point form

A continuous self-map of the
standard simplex has a point `x` with `Function.IsFixedPt f x`; a restatement of
`exists_fixedPoint` in Mathlib's fixed-point vocabulary.

## Informally uses

- [Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for the standard simplex](exists-fixed-point.md): the same statement, written with `f x = x`.
