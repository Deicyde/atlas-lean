# Speed of a knot

**Speed of a knot.** For a knot $K$ with parametrization $\gamma$ and a real number $t$,
the speed of $K$ at $t$ is the Euclidean norm $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert$ of the derivative of
$\gamma$ at $t$.
