# The p-dimensional cube

**The p-dimensional cube.** For $p \in \mathbb{N}$, the cube $I^p = [0,1]^p$ is the set of
points of $\mathbb{R}^p$ all of whose coordinates lie in $[0,1]$, with the subspace topology.
