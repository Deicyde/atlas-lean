# Polyhedral subdivisions

**Polyhedral subdivisions.** A polyhedral subdivision of a set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^k$ is a
geometric simplicial complex in $\mathbb{R}^k$ with finitely many faces whose underlying space
equals $P$.
