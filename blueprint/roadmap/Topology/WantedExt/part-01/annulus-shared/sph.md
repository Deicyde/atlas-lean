# Unit sphere in Euclidean space

**Unit sphere in Euclidean space.** For a natural number $n$, the unit sphere
$S^{n-1} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is the set of points of $\mathbb{R}^n$ at Euclidean distance $1$
from the origin.
