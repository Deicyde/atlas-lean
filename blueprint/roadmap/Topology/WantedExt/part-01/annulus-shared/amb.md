# Ambient Euclidean space

**Ambient Euclidean space.** For a natural number $n$, the ambient space is the real
Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ of dimension $n$, with coordinates indexed by $0, \dots, n-1$ and
the Euclidean norm.
