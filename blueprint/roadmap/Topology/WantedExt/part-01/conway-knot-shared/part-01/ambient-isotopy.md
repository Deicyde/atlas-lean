# Ambient Isotopy

A smooth ambient isotopy of `ℝ³`: a one-parameter family `H t : ℝ³ → ℝ³`
of diffeomorphisms, jointly smooth in `(t, x)`, starting at the identity.
The inverse family `Hinv` is also jointly smooth.
