# Apply Crossing

Apply one braid generator to the traversal state. The crossing
occupies the `z`-slab `[zBot, zTop]` (with `zBot < zTop`). The braid
generator is `w = ±i`; we cross strands at positions `i` and `i + 1`,
with `i = w.natAbs`.
