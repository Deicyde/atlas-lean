# Coprime arithmetic progressions are dense in the comaximal space

**Coprime arithmetic progressions are dense in the comaximal space.** Equip the positive
integers with the comaximal topology, the topology generated by the sets
$\{m : \gcd(n, m) = 1\}$ for positive integers $n$. If $a$ and $b$ are coprime positive integers,
then the arithmetic progression $\{ak + b : k \in \mathbb{N}\} = \{b, a + b, 2a + b, \dots\}$,
with $k$ ranging over the natural numbers including $0$, is dense in this topology.
