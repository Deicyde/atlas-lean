# Macías's coprime topology

**Macías's coprime topology.** For a positive integer $n$ let $\Omega_n$ be the set of positive
integers coprime to $n$. Macías's coprime topology $\tau$ is the topology on the positive integers
generated by the sets $\Omega_n$, $n \ge 1$, that is, the coarsest topology in which every
$\Omega_n$ is open.
