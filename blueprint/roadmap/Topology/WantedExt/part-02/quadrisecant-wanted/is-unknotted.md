# Unknotted knot

**Unknotted knot.** A knot $K$ is unknotted when there is a knot $J$ whose parametrization is
the standard circle $t \mapsto (\cos t, \sin t, 0)$ such that $K$ is ambient-isotopic to $J$ as
an oriented knot.
