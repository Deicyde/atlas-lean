# Primes in $(X, 9X/8)$ for $53 \le X < 9820997$

**Primes in $(X, 9X/8)$ for $53 \le X < 9820997$.** For every real number $X$ with
$53 \le X < 9820997$ there is a prime $p$ with $X < p < \frac{9}{8}X$.
