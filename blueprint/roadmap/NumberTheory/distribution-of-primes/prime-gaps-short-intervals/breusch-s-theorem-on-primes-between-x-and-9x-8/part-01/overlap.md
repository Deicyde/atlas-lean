# Every large real lies in a window $(5600n/3, 1890n)$

**Every large real lies in a window $[5600n/3, 1890n]$.** For every real number
$X \ge 5600 \cdot 4762 / 3$ there is a natural number $n \ge 4762$ with
$5600n/3 \le X \le 1890n$.
