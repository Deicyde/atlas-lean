# Tiny-range prime-counting bound

**Tiny-range prime-counting bound.** Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $7 \le n < 38$
and $k \ge 100$, and let $\pi(n)$ be the number of primes $p \le n$. Then
$n! \cdot (k + n - 1)^{\pi(n)} < k^{n}$.
