# Square root bound on a linear range

**Square root bound on a linear range.** Let $n$ and $N$ be natural numbers with
$900 \le n$ and $N \le 4n$. Then $\lfloor \sqrt{N} \rfloor \le \lfloor n/15 \rfloor$.
