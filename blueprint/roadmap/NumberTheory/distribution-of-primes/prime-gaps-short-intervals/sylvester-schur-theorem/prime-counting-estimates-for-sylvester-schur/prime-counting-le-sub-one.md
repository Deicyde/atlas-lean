# At most $r - 1$ primes up to $r$

**At most $r - 1$ primes up to $r$.** For every natural number $r \ge 2$, the number
$\pi(r)$ of primes $p \le r$ satisfies $\pi(r) \le r - 1$, since all such primes lie in
$\{2, \dots, r\}$.
