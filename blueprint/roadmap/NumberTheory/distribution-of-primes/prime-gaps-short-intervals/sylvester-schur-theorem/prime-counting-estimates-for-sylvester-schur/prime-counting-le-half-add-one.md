# At most $\lfloor r/2 \rfloor + 1$ primes up to $r$

**At most $\lfloor r/2 \rfloor + 1$ primes up to $r$.** For every natural number $r$, the
number $\pi(r)$ of primes $p \le r$ satisfies $\pi(r) \le \lfloor r/2 \rfloor + 1$.
