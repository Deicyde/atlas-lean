# The $m$-th prime, counted from one

**The $m$-th prime, counted from one.** For $m \ge 1$, $p_m$ is the $m$-th prime, so that
$p_1 = 2$, $p_2 = 3$, $p_3 = 5$; at $m = 0$ the value is also $2$.
