# Subpower bound

**Subpower bound.** For functions $f, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $f$ is bounded by $g$ up
to subpower losses if for every real $\delta > 0$ we have $f(x) = O(x^{\delta} g(x))$ as
$x \to \infty$.
