# The odometer partial sum vanishes at zero

**The odometer partial sum vanishes at zero.** For every $q \in \mathbb{C}$ and every dyadic
integer $\omega$, the interpolated odometer partial sum satisfies $S_{q,\omega}(0) = 0$.
