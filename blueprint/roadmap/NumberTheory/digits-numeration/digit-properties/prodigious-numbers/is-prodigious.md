# Prodigious numbers

**Prodigious numbers.** For natural numbers $b$ and $n$, the number $n$ is $b$-prodigious when
$b \ge 2$, $n > 0$, and $n$ is divisible by the product of the nonzero digits of $n$ in base $b$.
