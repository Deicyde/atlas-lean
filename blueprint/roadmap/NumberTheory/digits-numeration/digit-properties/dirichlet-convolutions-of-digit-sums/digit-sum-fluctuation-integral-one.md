# The fluctuation integral vanishes at $1$

**The fluctuation integral vanishes at $1$.** For every natural number $q$, let $F$ be the
Trollope–Delange fluctuation in base $q$ and $G(x) = \int_1^x F(\log u / \log q) \, du / u$.
Then $G(1) = 0$.
