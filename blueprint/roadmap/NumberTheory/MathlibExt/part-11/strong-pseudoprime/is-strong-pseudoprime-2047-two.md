# Is Strong Pseudoprime 2047 two

`2047 = 23 * 89` is a strong pseudoprime to base `2` (`2046 = 2 * 1023` and
`2 ^ 1023 = (2 ^ 11) ^ 93 = 1` in `ZMod 2047`).
