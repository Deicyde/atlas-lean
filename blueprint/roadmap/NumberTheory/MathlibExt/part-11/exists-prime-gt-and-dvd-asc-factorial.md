# Sylvester–Schur theorem

**Sylvester–Schur theorem.** Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $0 < k < n$. Then some
prime $p > k$ divides the product $n(n+1)\cdots(n+k-1)$ of the $k$ consecutive integers starting
at $n$.
