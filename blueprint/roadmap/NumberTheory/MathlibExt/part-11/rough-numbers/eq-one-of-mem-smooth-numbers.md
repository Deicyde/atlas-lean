# Only $1$ is rough and smooth at the same bound

**Only $1$ is rough and smooth at the same bound.** Let $n$ and $k$ be natural
numbers. If $n$ is $k$-rough and $n$ is positive with every prime factor less than
$k + 1$, then $n = 1$.
