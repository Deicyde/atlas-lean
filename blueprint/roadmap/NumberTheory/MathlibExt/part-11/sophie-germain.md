# Sophie Germain's theorem

**Sophie Germain's theorem.** Let $p$ be an odd prime such that $2p + 1$ is also prime. If
$x$, $y$, $z$ are pairwise coprime positive integers with $x^p + y^p = z^p$, then $p$ divides
$xyz$.
