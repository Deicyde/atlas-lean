# Gaussian Int split prime sq add sq

A prime `p ≡ 1 (mod 4)` splits in `ℤ[i]` in the explicit form
`a + bi`, `a - bi` with `a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 = p`, both factors prime.
This is the `a, b` form of `GaussianInt.gaussianInt_split_prime`.
