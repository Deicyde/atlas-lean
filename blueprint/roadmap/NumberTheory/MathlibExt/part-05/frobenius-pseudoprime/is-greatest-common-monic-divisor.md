# Is Greatest Common Monic Divisor

Greatest common monic divisor (pseudo1.tex lines 617-624).
`IsGreatestCommonMonicDivisor h g1 g2` holds when `h`, `g1` and `g2` are monic
polynomials over a commutative ring and the ideal generated by `g1` and `g2`
equals the principal ideal generated by `h`. Existence is not automatic.
