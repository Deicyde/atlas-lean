# Cauchy's lemma

**Cauchy's lemma.** Let $a$ and $b$ be odd natural numbers with $b^2 < 4a$ and
$3a < b^2 + 2b + 4$. Then there are natural numbers $s, t, u, v$ with
$a = s^2 + t^2 + u^2 + v^2$ and $b = s + t + u + v$.
