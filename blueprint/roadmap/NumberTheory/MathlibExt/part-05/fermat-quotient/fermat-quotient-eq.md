# Formula for the Fermat quotient

**Formula for the Fermat quotient.** For a prime $p$ and an integer $a$ coprime to $p$, the
Fermat quotient satisfies $q_p(a) = (a^{p-1} - 1)/p$, where $/$ is integer division rounding
down.
