# The enumeration of the Gaussian units is complete

**The enumeration of the Gaussian units is complete.** Let $u_0 = 1$, $u_1 = -1$, $u_2 = i$
and $u_3 = -i$. Every unit $u$ of $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ equals $u_j$ for some $j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$.
