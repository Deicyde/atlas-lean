# Generalized Fuss–Catalan numbers at $n = 1$

**Generalized Fuss–Catalan numbers at $n = 1$.** For all natural numbers $m$ and $k$, the
generalized Fuss–Catalan number $F_m(1, k)$ equals $k$.
