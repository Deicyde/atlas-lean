# Bertrand's postulate for consecutive primes

**Bertrand's postulate for consecutive primes.** Let $p_1 < p_2 < p_3 < \cdots$ be the prime
numbers in increasing order. For every $k \ge 1$, $p_{k+1} < 2 p_k$.
