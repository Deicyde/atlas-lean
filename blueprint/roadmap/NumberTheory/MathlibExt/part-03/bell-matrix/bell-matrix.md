# Bell matrix

**Bell matrix.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A Bell matrix over $R$ is given by a formal
power series $g \in R[[X]]$ whose constant coefficient is a unit of $R$. The corresponding
Riordan-array pair is $(g, Xg)$.
