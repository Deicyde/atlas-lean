# Periodicity of Ramanujan sums

**Periodicity of Ramanujan sums.** For every positive integer $n$ and all integers $t$ and $k$,
the Ramanujan sum $c_n$ of modulus $n$ satisfies $c_n(t + kn) = c_n(t)$.
