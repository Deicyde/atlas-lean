# Prime gap

**Prime gap.** Let $p_0 = 2$, $p_1 = 3$, $p_2 = 5, \dots$ be the primes in increasing order,
indexed from $0$. For a natural number $n$, the prime gap at $n$ is $p_{n+1} - p_n$.
