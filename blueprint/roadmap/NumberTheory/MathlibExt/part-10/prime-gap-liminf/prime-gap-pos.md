# Prime gaps are positive

**Prime gaps are positive.** Let $p_0 < p_1 < p_2 < \cdots$ be the primes in increasing
order and let $g_n = p_{n+1} - p_n$ be the $n$-th prime gap. Then $g_n > 0$ for every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, including $n = 0$.
