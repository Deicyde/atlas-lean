# Quadratic Gauss sum modulo $5$

**Quadratic Gauss sum modulo $5$.** For an integer $k$, the quadratic Gauss sum $g(k; 5)$ is
the complex number $g(k; 5) = \sum_{n=0}^{4} e^{2\pi i k n^2 / 5}$.
