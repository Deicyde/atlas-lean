# Newman's kernel

**Newman's kernel.** For real $u$, Newman's kernel is the sum of the series

$$\Phi(u) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (2\pi^2 n^4 e^{9u} - 3\pi n^2 e^{5u}) e^{-\pi n^2 e^{4u}},$$

which converges absolutely for every real $u$.
