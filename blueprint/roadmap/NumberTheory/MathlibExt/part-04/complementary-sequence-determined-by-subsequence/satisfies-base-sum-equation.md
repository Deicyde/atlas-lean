# The subsequence-sum equation $a_n = b_{2n} + b_{4n}$

**The subsequence-sum equation $a_n = b_{2n} + b_{4n}$.** Sequences
$a, b : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ satisfy this equation when $b_0 = 1$ and
$a_n = b_{2n} + b_{4n}$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
