# Maximum overlap

**Maximum overlap.** For finite sets $A, B \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$, the maximum overlap of $A$
and $B$ is the natural number obtained as the supremum over all integers $k$ of
$\#\{(a, b) \in A \times B : a - b = k\}$.
