# Quadratic Reciprocity Three Odd

Reciprocity bridge for `D % 4 = 3` at odd `p`: the Jacobi symbol
needs the `χ₄ p` factor. For `0 < D` the reciprocity sign equals `χ₄ p`;
for `D < 0` the `(-1)` factor supplies it.
