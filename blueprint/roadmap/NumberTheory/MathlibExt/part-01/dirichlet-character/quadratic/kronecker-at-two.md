# Kronecker symbol at two

**Kronecker symbol at two.** For an integer $D$, the Kronecker symbol of $D$ at $2$ is $0$
if $D$ is even, $1$ if $D \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 8$, and $-1$ if $D \equiv \pm 3 \pmod 8$.
