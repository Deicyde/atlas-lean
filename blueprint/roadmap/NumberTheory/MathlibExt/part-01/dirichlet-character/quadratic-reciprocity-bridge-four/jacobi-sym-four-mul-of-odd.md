# Jacobi symbol of $4m$ at an odd denominator

**Jacobi symbol of $4m$ at an odd denominator.** For every integer $m$ and every odd natural
number $p$, the Jacobi symbol $\left(\frac{4m}{p}\right)$ equals $\left(\frac{m}{p}\right)$.
