# Homogenized Cyclotomic

The homogenized cyclotomic value `Φ_n(a, b) = ∑ᵢ cᵢ aⁱ b^(φ(n) - i)`, where `cᵢ` are the
integer coefficients of the `n`-th cyclotomic polynomial; equal to `b ^ φ(n) Φ_n(a / b)` when
`b ≠ 0`.
