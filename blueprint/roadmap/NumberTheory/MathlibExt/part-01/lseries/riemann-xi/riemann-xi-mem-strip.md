# Zeros of the xi function lie in the critical strip

**Zeros of the xi function lie in the critical strip.** Let $\xi$ be the Riemann xi
function. If $s$ is a complex number with $\xi(s) = 0$, then $0 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < 1$.
