# p-adic expansion

**p-adic expansion.** Let $p$ be a prime and $a \in \mathbb{Z}_p$. The $p$-adic expansion of
$a$ is the sequence whose $n$-th term is the $n$-th $p$-adic digit of $a$, viewed as an element of
$\{0, 1, \dots, p - 1\}$.
