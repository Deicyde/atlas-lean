# Solvability of $ax^2 + by^2 = 1$

**Solvability of $ax^2 + by^2 = 1$.** For a field $F$ and nonzero elements $a, b \in F$, the
equation $ax^2 + by^2 = 1$ is solvable over $F$ if there exist $x, y \in F$ with
$ax^2 + by^2 = 1$.
