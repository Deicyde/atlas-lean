# Hilbert symbol of a field

**Hilbert symbol of a field.** For a field $F$ and nonzero elements $a, b \in F$, the Hilbert
symbol $(a, b)_F$ is the integer $1$ if there exist $x, y \in F$ with $ax^2 + by^2 = 1$, and $-1$
otherwise.
