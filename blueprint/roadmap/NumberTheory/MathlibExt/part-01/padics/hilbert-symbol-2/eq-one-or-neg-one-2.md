# The $p$-adic Hilbert symbol is $\pm 1$

**The $p$-adic Hilbert symbol is $\pm 1$.** For a prime $p$ and all nonzero
$a, b \in \mathbb{Q}_p$, the $p$-adic Hilbert symbol $(a, b)_p$ equals $1$ or $-1$.
