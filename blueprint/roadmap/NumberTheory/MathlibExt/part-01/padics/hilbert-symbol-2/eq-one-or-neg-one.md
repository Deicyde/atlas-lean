# The Hilbert symbol of a field is $\pm 1$

**The Hilbert symbol of a field is $\pm 1$.** For every field $F$ and all nonzero
$a, b \in F$, the Hilbert symbol $(a, b)_F$ of $a$ and $b$ over $F$ equals $1$ or $-1$.
