# $p$-adic digits determine a $p$-adic integer

**$p$-adic digits determine a $p$-adic integer.** Let $p$ be a prime. If two $p$-adic
integers $a, a' \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ have the same $n$-th $p$-adic digit for every $n \ge 0$, then
$a = a'$.
