# Sign rule for the real Hilbert symbol

**Sign rule for the real Hilbert symbol.** For nonzero real numbers $a$ and $b$, the real
Hilbert symbol of $a$ and $b$ equals $-1$ if and only if $a < 0$ and $b < 0$.
