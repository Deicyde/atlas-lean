# Real Hilbert symbol

**Real Hilbert symbol.** For nonzero real numbers $a$ and $b$, the real Hilbert symbol of $a$
and $b$ is the integer $1$ if there are real numbers $x, y$ with $a x^2 + b y^2 = 1$, and the
integer $-1$ otherwise.
