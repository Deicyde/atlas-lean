# Real Hilbert symbol equal to $-1$

**Real Hilbert symbol equal to $-1$.** For nonzero real numbers $a$ and $b$, the real Hilbert
symbol of $a$ and $b$ equals $-1$ if and only if there are no real numbers $x, y$ with
$a x^2 + b y^2 = 1$.
