# The complex-exponent divisor sum at one

**The complex-exponent divisor sum at one.** For every complex number $s$, the divisor-power
sum $\sigma_s(n) = \sum_{d \mid n} d^s$ with complex exponent $s$ satisfies $\sigma_s(1) = 1$.
