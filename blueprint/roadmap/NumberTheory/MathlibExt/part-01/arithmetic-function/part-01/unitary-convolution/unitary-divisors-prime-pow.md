# Unitary divisors of a prime power

**Unitary divisors of a prime power.** For every prime $p$ and every natural number $m$, the
unitary divisors of $p^m$, that is, the divisors $d$ of $p^m$ with $\gcd(d, p^m/d) = 1$, are
exactly $1$ and $p^m$.
