# Jordan's totient at prime powers

**Jordan's totient at prime powers.** For every natural number $k$, every prime $p$ and every
integer $e \ge 1$, the Jordan totient function satisfies $J_k(p^e) = p^{k(e-1)}(p^k - 1)$.
