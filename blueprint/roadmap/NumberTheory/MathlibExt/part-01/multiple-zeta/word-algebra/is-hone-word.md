# Words of $\mathfrak{H}^1$

**Words of $\mathfrak{H}^1$.** A word $w$ in the two letters $x$ and $y$ is an
$\mathfrak{H}^1$-word when $w$ is empty or $w = uy$ for some word $u$, that is, when $w$ is
empty or ends in $y$.
