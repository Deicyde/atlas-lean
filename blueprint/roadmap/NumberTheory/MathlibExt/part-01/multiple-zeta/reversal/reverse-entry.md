# Entries of the reversed index

**Entries of the reversed index.** Let $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{d-1})$ be an index of depth $d$;
its reverse also has depth $d$. For every $i < d$, the $i$-th entry of the reverse of $k$ is
$k_{d-1-i}$.
