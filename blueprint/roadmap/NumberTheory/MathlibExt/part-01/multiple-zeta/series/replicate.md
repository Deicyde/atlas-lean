# Repeated index

**Repeated index.** For a natural number $k$ and a positive natural number $e$, the index
$\{e\}^k = (e, \dots, e)$ consisting of $k$ entries all equal to $e$, so of weight $ke$.

For $e = 2$ its multiple-zeta-star value is $\zeta^\star(\{2\}^k)$.
