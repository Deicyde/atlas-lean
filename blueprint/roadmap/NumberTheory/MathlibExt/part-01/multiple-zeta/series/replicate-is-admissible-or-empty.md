# Replicate is Admissible Or Empty

`{e}^k` with `e ≥ 2` is admissible (or empty when `k = 0`), so its multiple-zeta and
multiple-zeta-star values are defined. Source: Iovleff, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A114,
Section 1 (`ζ*({2}^k)`).
