# Entry of a multiple-zeta index

**Entry of a multiple-zeta index.** For a multiple-zeta index $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{d-1})$ of
depth $d$ and $0 \le i < d$, the entry of $k$ at $i$ is the positive natural number $k_i$.
