# Weakly decreasing tuples

**Weakly decreasing tuples.** For a natural number $r$, a weakly decreasing $r$-tuple is a
tuple $(m_0, \dots, m_{r-1})$ of positive integers with $m_0 \ge m_1 \ge \dots \ge m_{r-1}$.
