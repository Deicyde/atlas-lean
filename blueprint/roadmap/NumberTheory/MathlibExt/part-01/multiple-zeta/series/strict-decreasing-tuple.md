# Strictly decreasing tuples

**Strictly decreasing tuples.** For a natural number $r$, a strictly decreasing $r$-tuple is a
tuple $(m_0, \dots, m_{r-1})$ of positive integers with $m_0 > m_1 > \dots > m_{r-1}$.
