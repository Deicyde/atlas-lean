# Abs discr gt exp term

ATLAS `NumberTheoryI` Corollary 14.26 (second inequality): the
absolute discriminant of a number field of degree `n` exceeds
`(π e ^ 2 / 4) ^ n / (e ^ 2 * n)`, so it grows at least exponentially
in the degree.
