# The recurrence of a Newton sequence

**The recurrence of a Newton sequence.** Let $R$ be a commutative semiring and let
$a, c \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$ be such that $a$ is a Newton sequence generated by $c$. Then for
every natural number $n$ we have $a_{n+1} = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_{n-j} c_{j+1} + (n+1) c_{n+1}$.
