# Second term of a normal number sequence

**Second term of a normal number sequence.** For all integers $a_1$ and $a_2$, the normal
second-order number sequence $(u_n)_{n \ge 0}$ with coefficients $a_1, a_2$ has $u_2 = a_1$.
