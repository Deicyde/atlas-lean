# Lucas numbers

**Lucas numbers.** The Lucas numbers are the natural numbers $L_n$ defined by $L_0 = 2$,
$L_1 = 1$ and $L_{n+2} = L_{n+1} + L_n$ for every natural number $n$. They begin
$2, 1, 3, 4, 7, \dots$.
