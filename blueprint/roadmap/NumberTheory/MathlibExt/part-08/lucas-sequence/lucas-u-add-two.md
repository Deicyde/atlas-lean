# Recurrence for the Lucas sequence $U(P, Q)$

**Recurrence for the Lucas sequence $U(P, Q)$.** For Lucas sequence parameters $(P, Q)$ and
every natural number $n$, the Lucas sequence $U = U(P, Q)$ satisfies
$U_{n+2} = P U_{n+1} - Q U_n$.
