# Special primes of a Lucas pair

**Special primes of a Lucas pair.** For integers $P$ and $Q$, a natural number $p$ is a special
prime for the Lucas parameters $(P, Q)$ if $p$ is prime and $p$ divides $\gcd(P, Q)$.
