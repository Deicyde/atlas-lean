# Nicol numbers

**Nicol numbers.** A Nicol number is a positive integer $n$ that divides
$\varphi(n) + \sigma(n)$, where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function and $\sigma(n)$ is the sum
of the positive divisors of $n$.
