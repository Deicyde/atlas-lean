# Wildly ramified prime

**Wildly ramified prime.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $S$ a commutative $R$-algebra and
$q$ a prime ideal of $S$. Then $q$ is *wildly ramified* over $R$ if it is not tamely ramified
over $R$.
