# Integer solutions of a sum of squares

**Integer solutions of a sum of squares.** For natural numbers $k$ and $n$, the solution set
is the set of all integer vectors $x = (x_1, \dots, x_k) \in \mathbb{Z}^k$ with
$x_1^2 + \cdots + x_k^2 = n$.
