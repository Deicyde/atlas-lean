# Series of arctangents of 2/k² (Entry 7, Example 4)

**Series of arctangents of 2/k² (Entry 7, Example 4).** The series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \arctan \frac{2}{(k + 1)^2}$$

converges absolutely and its sum is $3\pi/4$.
