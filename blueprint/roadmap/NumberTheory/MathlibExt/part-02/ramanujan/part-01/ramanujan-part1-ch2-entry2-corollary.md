# Ramanujan's series $\sum_{k \ge 1} 1/((3k)^3 - 3k) = (\log 3 - 1)/2$

**Ramanujan's series $\sum_{k \ge 1} 1/((3k)^3 - 3k) = (\log 3 - 1)/2$.** The series

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(3k)^3 - 3k}$$

converges, and its sum is $\frac{\log 3 - 1}{2}$.
