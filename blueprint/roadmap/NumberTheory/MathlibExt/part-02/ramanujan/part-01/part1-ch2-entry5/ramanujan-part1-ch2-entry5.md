# Ramanujan's sum identity for $1/((6k)^3 - 6k)$

**Ramanujan's sum identity for $1/((6k)^3 - 6k)$.** For every positive integer $n$,

$$
1 + 2 \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{(6k)^3 - 6k} =
\frac{2}{3} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{n+k} + \sum_{k=0}^{2n} \frac{1}{2n+2k+1}.
$$
