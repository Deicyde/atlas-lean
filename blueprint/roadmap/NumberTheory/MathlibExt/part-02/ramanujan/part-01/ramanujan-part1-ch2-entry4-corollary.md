# Ramanujan's series for $\frac{3}{2}\log 2 - 1$

**Ramanujan's series for $\frac{3}{2}\log 2 - 1$.** The numbers $\frac{2}{(4k)^3 - 4k}$ for
$k = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ are summable with sum $\frac{3}{2}\log 2 - 1$, where $\log$ is the natural
logarithm.
