# Ramanujan's finite arctangent sum (Entry 7)

**Ramanujan's finite arctangent sum (Entry 7).** For every real number $n > 0$ and every
natural number $r$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{r-1} \arctan \frac{2}{(n + 2k + 1)^2} = \arctan \frac{2r}{n^2 + 2nr + 1}.$$
