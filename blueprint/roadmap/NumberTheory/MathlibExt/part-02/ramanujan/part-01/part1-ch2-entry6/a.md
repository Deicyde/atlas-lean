# The tripling sequence of Ramanujan's Entry 6

**The tripling sequence of Ramanujan's Entry 6.** For natural numbers $n$ and $k$, let
$A(n, k) = 3^k n + (3^k - 1)/2$, a natural number. It is the sequence with $A(n, 0) = n$ and
$A(n, k + 1) = 3A(n, k) + 1$.
