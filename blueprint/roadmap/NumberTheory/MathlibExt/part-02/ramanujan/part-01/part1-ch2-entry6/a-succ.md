# Recurrence of the tripling sequence

**Recurrence of the tripling sequence.** For all natural numbers $n$ and $k$, the tripling
sequence $A(n, k)$ of Entry 6 satisfies $A(n, k + 1) = 3A(n, k) + 1$.
