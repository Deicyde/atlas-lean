# Recurrence for Fibonacci p-numbers

**Recurrence for Fibonacci p-numbers.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $r$ with $p < r$,
the Fibonacci $p$-numbers satisfy $F_p(r) = F_p(r-1) + F_p(r-p-1)$.
