# Prime gaps near $n!$, general form

**Prime gaps near $n!$, general form.** Let $n$, $p$ and $r$ be natural numbers such that
every prime $q > n$ satisfies $q \ge r$. If $p$ is a prime with $n! + 1 < p < n! + r^2$, then
$p - n!$ is prime.
