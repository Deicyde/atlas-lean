# Prime-counting bound $\pi(m) \le m/3$ for natural $m \ge 36$

**Prime-counting bound $\pi(m) \le m/3$ for natural $m \ge 36$.** Let $\pi(m)$ denote the
number of primes $p \le m$. For every natural number $m \ge 36$, $3\pi(m) \le m$.
