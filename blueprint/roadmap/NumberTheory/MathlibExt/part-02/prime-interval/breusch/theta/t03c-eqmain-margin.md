# Numerical margin of the main term

**Numerical margin of the main term.** With $\log$ the natural logarithm,
$48\,(10 \log 10 - 9 \log 9) -
\left(\frac{18900}{279} + \frac{18900}{657} + \frac{18900}{963}\right) \log \frac{34}{10} > 13$.
