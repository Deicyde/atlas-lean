# Threshold inequality for the multinomial bound

**Threshold inequality for the multinomial bound.** For every natural number $t \ge 230$,
$10^{5t-5} \cdot 432^{t} \cdot (2t+1) \le 2 \cdot 34^{5t-5}$.
