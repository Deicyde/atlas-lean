# Positivity of the multinomial coefficient $M(t)$

**Positivity of the multinomial coefficient $M(t)$.** Let $M(t)$ be the natural-number
quotient of $(6t)!$ by $(3t)!\,(2t)!\,t!$. For every natural number $t$, $M(t) > 0$.
