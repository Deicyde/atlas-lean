# Remainder bound for common multiples

**Remainder bound for common multiples.** Let $a$, $b$, $d$ be natural numbers with $b > 0$,
$d > 0$, $d \mid b$ and $d \mid a$. Then the remainder of $a$ modulo $b$ is at most $b - d$.
