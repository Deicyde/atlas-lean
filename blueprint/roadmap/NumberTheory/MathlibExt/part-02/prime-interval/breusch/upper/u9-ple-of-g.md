# A level bound turns into a bound on the prime

**A level bound turns into a bound on the prime.** Let $n \ge 1$ be a natural number, $p$ a
prime and $j \ge 1$ a real number. If $j / 18900 \le n / p$, then $p \le 18900\,n / j$.
