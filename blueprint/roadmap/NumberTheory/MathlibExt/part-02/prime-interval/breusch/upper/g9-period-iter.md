# Periodicity of $G_9$ in multiples of $18900$

**Periodicity of $G_9$ in multiples of $18900$.** For all natural numbers $o$ and $r$, the
grid $G$-function for $k = 9$ satisfies $G_9(18900\,o + r) = G_9(r)$.
