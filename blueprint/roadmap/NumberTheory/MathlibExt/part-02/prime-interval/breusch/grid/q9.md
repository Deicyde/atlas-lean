# The modulus $Q_9 = 210$

**The modulus $Q_9 = 210$.** For the $k = 9$ case of Breusch's argument, the truncation
modulus is the natural number $Q_9 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 = 210$.
