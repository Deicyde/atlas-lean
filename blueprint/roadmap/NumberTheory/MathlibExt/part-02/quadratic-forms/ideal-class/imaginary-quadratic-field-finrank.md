# $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$ is a quadratic extension

**$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$ is a quadratic extension.** For every positive integer $D$, the
subfield $\mathbb{Q}(i\sqrt{D})$ of $\mathbb{C}$ has degree $2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
