# Dense Range embedding

The image of `ℚ` is dense in each completed field `ℚ_v`.

Uses `Rat.denseRange_cast (𝕜 := ℝ)` at infinity and
`Padic.denseRange_ratCast p` under the local `Fact` at finite primes.
