# Elements of norm one in $\mathbb{Z}(\sqrt{-2})$

**Elements of norm one in $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$.** Exactly two elements $a + b\sqrt{-2}$ of
$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$, with $a, b$ integers, have norm $a^2 + 2b^2 = 1$.

These are the units $\pm 1$.
