# Wolstenholme's theorem

**Wolstenholme's theorem.** If $p \ge 5$ is prime, then $\sum_{k=1}^{p-1} k^{-1} = 0$ in
$\mathbb{Z}/p^2\mathbb{Z}$, where $k^{-1}$ is the inverse of $k$ modulo $p^2$.
