# Trinomial transform of the zero sequence

**Trinomial transform of the zero sequence.** For every semiring $R$ and every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$th term of the trinomial transform of the zero sequence
$\mathbb{N} \to R$ is $0$.
