# Card mul of coprime

With the units of `ℤ√d` (`d < 0`, unique factorization) enumerated by `Fin k`, norm
fibers multiply over coprime nonzero norms: `#N(u) * #N(v) = k * #N(u * v)`. Each fiber of
the multiplication map consists of the `k` unit translates.
