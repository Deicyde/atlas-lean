# Dvd of coprime norms

Divisibility descends through coprime-norm cofactors: if
`β' ∣ β₀' * γ₀` with `Nβ' ∣ u` and `Nγ₀ = v` for coprime nonzero
`u v`, then `β' ∣ β₀'`.
