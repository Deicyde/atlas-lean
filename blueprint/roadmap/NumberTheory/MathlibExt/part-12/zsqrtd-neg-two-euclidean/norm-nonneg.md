# The norm on $\mathbb{Z}(\sqrt{-2})$ is nonnegative

**The norm on $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ is nonnegative.** For every $x = a + b\sqrt{-2}$ in
$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$, the norm $N(x) = a^2 + 2b^2$ satisfies $N(x) \ge 0$.
