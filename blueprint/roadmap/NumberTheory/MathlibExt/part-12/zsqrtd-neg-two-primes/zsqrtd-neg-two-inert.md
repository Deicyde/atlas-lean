# Primes congruent to 5 or 7 mod 8 are inert in ℤ(√−2)

**Primes congruent to 5 or 7 mod 8 are inert in ℤ[√−2].** If $p$ is a prime number with
$p \equiv 5 \pmod 8$ or $p \equiv 7 \pmod 8$, then $p$ is a prime element of
$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$.
