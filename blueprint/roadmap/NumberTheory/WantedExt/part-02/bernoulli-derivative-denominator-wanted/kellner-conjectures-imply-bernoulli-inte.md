# Kellner conjectures imply bernoulli integral derivative conjectures

Conjecture 2 (i) implies `\overline{𝒮} = 𝒮 = {1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 12, 28, 30, 36, 60}`
(Conjecture 1 (ii)), and Conjecture 2 (ii) implies that `\overline{𝒮}` is finite
(Conjecture 1 (i)).
