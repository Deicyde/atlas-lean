# Kellner Large Part Asymptotic Conjecture and finite integral derivative

Conjecture 2 (ii) holds (Bordellès, Luca, Moree, and Shparlinski, with `κ = 2`), so the set
`\overline{𝒮}` of `n ≥ 1` with `B_n'(x) ∈ ℤ[x]` is finite.
