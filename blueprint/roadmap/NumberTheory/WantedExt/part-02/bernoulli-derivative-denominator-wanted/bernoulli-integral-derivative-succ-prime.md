# Bernoulli integral derivative succ prime and large

If `B_n'(x) ∈ ℤ[x]` then `n + 1` is prime, and any such `n` outside
`𝒮 = {1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 12, 28, 30, 36, 60}` exceeds `10^7`.
