# Bernoulli Integral Derivative Indices finite and mono

Every `\overline{𝒮}_k` (`k ≥ 1`) is finite, and `\overline{𝒮}_k ⊆ \overline{𝒮}_{k+1}`.
The paper writes `⊂`; its proof gives inclusion, which is what is formalized.
