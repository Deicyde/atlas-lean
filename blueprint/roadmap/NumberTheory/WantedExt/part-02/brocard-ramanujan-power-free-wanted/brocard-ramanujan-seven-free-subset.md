# Brocard–Ramanujan solutions with $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ seven-free

**Brocard–Ramanujan solutions with $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ seven-free.** If natural numbers $n$
and $x$ satisfy $n! + 1 = x^2$ and $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ is $7$-free, then $(n, x)$ is one of
$(4, 5)$, $(5, 11)$ and $(7, 71)$.
