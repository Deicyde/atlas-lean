# Brocard–Ramanujan solutions with $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ a prime power

**Brocard–Ramanujan solutions with $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ a prime power.** If natural numbers
$n$ and $x$ satisfy $n! + 1 = x^2$ and $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ is a prime power, then
$(n, x) = (4, 5)$.
