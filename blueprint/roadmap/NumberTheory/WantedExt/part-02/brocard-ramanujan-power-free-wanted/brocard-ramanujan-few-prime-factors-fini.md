# Brocard Ramanujan few Prime Factors finite

For every `l ≥ 2`, the Brocard-Ramanujan equation `n! + 1 = x ^ 2` has only
finitely many solutions `(n, x)` with `x - 1` or `x + 1` having fewer than `l`
distinct prime divisors.
