# Le rpow mul pow of dvd factorial

Lemma 2: if a positive integer `x` divides `n!` (`n ≥ 1`), then for every positive
integer `a`, `x ≤ n^(n/a) (a ω(x) + 1)^(n ω(x))`, where `ω(x)` is the number of distinct
prime divisors of `x`.
