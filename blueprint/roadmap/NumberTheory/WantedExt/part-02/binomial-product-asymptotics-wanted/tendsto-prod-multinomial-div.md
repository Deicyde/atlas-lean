# Tendsto prod multinomial div

For `r ≥ 2`, `b_1, …, b_r ≥ 1` and `a = b_1 + ⋯ + b_r`,
`∏_{n=1}^m (an; b_1 n, …, b_r n) ∼ C_{a,b} × P_a(m)/(P_{b_1}(m) ⋯ P_{b_r}(m))`, with
`C_{a,b} = c_{a,b} (2π)^{(1-r)/4}` and `c_{a,b} = F_a/(F_{b_1} ⋯ F_{b_r}) ∈ (0, 1)`.
