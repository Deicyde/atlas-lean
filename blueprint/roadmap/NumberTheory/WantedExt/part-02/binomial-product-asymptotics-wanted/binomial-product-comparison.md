# Binomial Product Comparison

The comparison function `P_{a,b}(x) = (a^a/(b^b c^c))^{x(x+1)/2}
x^{(1/a - a/(bc))/12 - 1/4} / ((2π/e)(bc/a) x)^{x/2}` with `c = a - b`.
