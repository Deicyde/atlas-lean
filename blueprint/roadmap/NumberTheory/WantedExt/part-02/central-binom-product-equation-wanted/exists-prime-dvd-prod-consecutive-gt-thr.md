# Exists prime dvd prod consecutive gt three halves iff

Lemma 5 (Hanson): for `0 < m < n`, the product `n (n+1) ⋯ (n+m-1)` has a prime divisor
`p > 3m/2` if and only if `(n, m)` is none of the exceptions `3·4`, `8·9`,
`6·7·8·9·10`.
