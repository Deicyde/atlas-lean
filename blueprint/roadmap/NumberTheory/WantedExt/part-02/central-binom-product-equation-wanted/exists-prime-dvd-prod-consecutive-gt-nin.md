# Exists prime dvd prod consecutive gt nine fifths

Lemma 9 (Shanta-Shorey): if `n > m > 2` and `n + m ≥ 150`, then
`n (n+1) ⋯ (n+m-1)` has a prime divisor `p > 1.8 m`.
