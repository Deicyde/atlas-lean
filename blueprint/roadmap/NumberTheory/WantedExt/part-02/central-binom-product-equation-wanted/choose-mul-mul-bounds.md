# Choose mul mul bounds

Lemma 6 (Stănică): for positive integers `m > r ≥ 1` and `n ≥ 1`, with
`B = m^(mn+1/2) / ((m-r)^((m-r)n+1/2) r^(rn+1/2))`,
`e^(-1/(8n)) n^(-1/2) B / √(2π) < C(mn, rn) < n^(-1/2) B / √(2π)`.
