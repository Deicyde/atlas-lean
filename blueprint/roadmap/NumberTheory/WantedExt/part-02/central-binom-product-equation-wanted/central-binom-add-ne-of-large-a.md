# Central Binom add ne of large a

Lemma 12: for positive `b`, `x ≥ 2`, `a + b ≥ 150` and `a ≥ (171/121) x`,
`C(2(a+b), a+b) ≠ C(2a, a) C(x+2b, b)`.
