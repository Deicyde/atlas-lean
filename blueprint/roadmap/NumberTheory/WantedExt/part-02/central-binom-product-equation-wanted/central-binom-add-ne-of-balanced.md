# Central Binom add ne of balanced

Lemma 11: for positive `a`, `b`, `x ≥ 2` with `k = a + b ≥ 75`, `a ≤ 0.9 k` and
`b ≤ 0.8 k`, `C(2k, k) ≠ C(2a, a) C(x+2b, b)`.
