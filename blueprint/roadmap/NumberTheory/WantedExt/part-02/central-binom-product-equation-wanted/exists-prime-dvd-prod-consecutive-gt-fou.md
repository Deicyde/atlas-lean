# Exists prime dvd prod consecutive gt four point four two

Lemma 10 (Nair-Shorey): if `n > 4m`, `m > 3` and `n + m ≥ 150`, then
`n (n+1) ⋯ (n+m-1)` has a prime divisor `p > 4.42 m`.
