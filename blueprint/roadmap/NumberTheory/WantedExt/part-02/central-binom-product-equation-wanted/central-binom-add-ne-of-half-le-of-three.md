# Central Binom add ne of half le of three le

Lemma 7: for positive `x`, `a`, `b` with `a ≥ (a+b)/2` and `b ≥ 3`,
`C(2(a+b), a+b) ≠ C(2a, a) C(x+2b, b)`.
