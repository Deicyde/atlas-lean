# Central binomial products with $a \ge b \ge 3$

**Central binomial products with $a \ge b \ge 3$.** Let $x$, $a$, $b$ be natural numbers with
$x \ge 1$ and $3 \le b \le a$. Then $\binom{2(a+b)}{a+b} \ne \binom{2a}{a}\binom{x+2b}{b}$.
