# Tverberg Sum periodic

Lemma 3: for `ℓ ≥ 2`, each `f_j` has period `m` in every `a_i` and in `k`. The paper's
standing hypothesis `ℓ ≥ 2` is needed: at `ℓ = 1`, `f_1({a}, m, k) = ⌊(k+a)/m⌋ - ⌊k/m⌋` shifts by
`q` when `a` does by `qm`.
