# Incl Excl Like Sum round Down extrema

Theorem 3: for `n ≥ 2`, `g_4` (nearest integer, ties down: `⌈x - 1/2⌉`) has minimum
`-2^{n-2}`, attained at `x_k = 1/2`, and maximum `2^{n-2}`, attained at `x_k = 1/2 + 1/n²`.
