# Incl Excl Like Sum ceil extrema

Theorem 1: for `n ≥ 2`, `g_2` (ceiling) has maximum `2^{n-2}`, attained at `x_k = 1/2`, and
minimum `-2^{n-2} + 1`, attained at `x_k = 1/2 + 1/n²`.
