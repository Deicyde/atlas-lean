# Incl Excl Like Sum

The inclusion-exclusion-like sum
`g^(F)(x_1, …, x_n) = Σ_{∅ ≠ T ⊆ [1, n]} (-1)^{|T| - 1} F(Σ_{i ∈ T} x_i)` (Definition 2 for
`F = ⌊·⌋`, the general `g^(F)` of Section 1 and Definition 3 for the other `F`).
