# Tverberg Sum

Tverberg's sum
`f^(F)({a_1, …, a_ℓ}, m, k) = Σ_{T ⊆ [1, ℓ]} (-1)^{ℓ - |T|} F((k + Σ_{i ∈ T} a_i) / m)`
(Definition 1 for `F = ⌊·⌋`, Section 1 and Definition 3 for the other `F`).
