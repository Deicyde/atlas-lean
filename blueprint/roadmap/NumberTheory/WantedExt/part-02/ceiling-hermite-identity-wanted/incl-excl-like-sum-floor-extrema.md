# Incl Excl Like Sum floor extrema

Lemma 5 (Onphaeng and Pongsriiam): for `n ≥ 2`, `g_1` has minimum `-2^{n-2}`, attained at
`x_k = 1/2`, and maximum `2^{n-2} - 1`, attained at `x_k = 1/2 - 1/n²`.
