# Gao unit constant of even

Theorem 6.3: for even `n`, `E_{U(n)}(n) = n + Ω(n)`, where `E_A(n)` is the least positive `k`
such that every length-`k` sequence in `ℤ_n` has an `A`-weighted zero-sum subsequence of
length `n`.
