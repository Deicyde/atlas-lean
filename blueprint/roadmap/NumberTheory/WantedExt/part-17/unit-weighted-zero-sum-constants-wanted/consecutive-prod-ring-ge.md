# Consecutive prod ring ge

Theorem 2.3: for an `R`-module `M`, an `R'`-module `N`, `A ⊆ R` and `B ⊆ R'`, with `M × N`
a module over `R × R'` (componentwise action), `C_{A × B}(M × N) ≥ C_A(M) C_B(N)`.
