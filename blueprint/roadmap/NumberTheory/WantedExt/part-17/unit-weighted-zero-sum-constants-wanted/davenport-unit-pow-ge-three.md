# Davenport unit pow ge three

Corollary 7.8: for `k ≥ 2` and a prime `p ≡ 1 (mod k)`, `D_{U(p)^k}(p) ≥ 3`: some sequence of
length `2` in `ℤ_p` has no `U(p)^k`-weighted zero-sum subsequence.
