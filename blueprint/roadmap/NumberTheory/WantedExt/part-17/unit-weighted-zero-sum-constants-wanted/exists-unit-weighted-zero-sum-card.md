# Exists unit Weighted Zero Sum card

Theorem 5.5: for `n ≥ 1` and even `m ≥ 2^{Ω(n)}`, every sequence of length `m + Ω(n)` in
`ℤ_n` has a `U(n)`-weighted zero-sum subsequence of length `m`.
