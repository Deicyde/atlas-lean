# Davenport unit pow le

Theorem 7.5: for an odd prime `k` and a prime `p` with `p ≡ 1 (mod k)` and
`p ≢ 1 (mod k²)`, `D_{U(p)^k}(p) ≤ k`: every sequence of length `k` in `ℤ_p` has a
`U(p)^k`-weighted zero-sum subsequence (`U(p)^k = {u^k : u ∈ U(p)}`).
