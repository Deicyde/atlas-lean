# Sum hamming Weight range eq takagi dc DNodes

Corollary 31: for `n = 2^k + r` with `0 ≤ r < 2^k` and `x = r/2^k`,
(1) `S₁(n) = (n k + 2^k (2x - τ(x)))/2`, (2) `S₁(n) = (n k + 2r - 2^k τ(r/2^k))/2`, and
(3) `S₁(n) = (n k + 2r - δ(n))/2`.
