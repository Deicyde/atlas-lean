# Tree DNodes eq dc DNodes of is Divide Conquer Tree

Theorem 9 (Coronado et al.), tree form: every divide-and-conquer tree with `n` leaves
has `δ(n)` `D`-nodes, where `δ` is the recurrence `dcDNodes`.
