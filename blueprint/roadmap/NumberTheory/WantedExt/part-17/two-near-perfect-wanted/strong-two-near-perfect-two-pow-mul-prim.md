# Strong two near perfect two pow mul prime form

Lemma 8: if `n = 2^k p` (`p` an odd prime) is strongly `2`-near perfect, then
`p = (2^(k+1) - 2^a - 1) / (1 + 2^(k-a))` for some `a ≤ k`.
