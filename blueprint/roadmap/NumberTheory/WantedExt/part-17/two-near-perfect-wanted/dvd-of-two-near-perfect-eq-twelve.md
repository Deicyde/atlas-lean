# Dvd of two near perfect eq twelve

Lemma 6: if `k, b > 0`, `p` is odd and
`(2^(k+1) - 1)(p^2 + p + 1) = 2^(k+1) p^2 + 2^b p^2 + 1` (Equation (12)), then
`p ∣ 2^k - 1`.
