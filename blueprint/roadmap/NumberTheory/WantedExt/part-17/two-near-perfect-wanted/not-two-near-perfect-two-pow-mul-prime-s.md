# Not two near perfect two pow mul prime sq case two

Proposition 2 (Case 2: omitted divisors `2^i`, `i ≤ k`, and `p^m`, `m ∈ {1, 2}`): no
`n = 2^k p^2` (`p` an odd prime) is `2`-near perfect with such omitted divisors.
