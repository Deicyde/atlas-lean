# Dvd of two near perfect eq six

Lemma 3: if `b, k ≥ 0`, `p` is odd and
`(2^(k+1) - 1)(p^2 + p + 1) = 2^(k+1) p^2 + 2^b p + 1`, then `p ∣ 2^k - 1` and
`p + 1 ∣ 2^b - 2`.
