# Omitted divisors sum two pow mul prime sq

Lemma 5: if `n = 2^k p^2` (`p` an odd prime) is `2`-near perfect with omitted divisors
`d₁, d₂`, then `d₁ + d₂ = -p^2 + (2^(k+1) - 1) p + (2^(k+1) - 1)` and `d₁, d₂` have
opposite parity.
