# Terai ten fifteen five eq one one two

For every integer `m > 1`, the only solution of
`(10m² + 1)^x + (15m² - 1)^y = (5m)^z` in positive integers is `(x, y, z) = (1, 1, 2)`.
Natural-number subtraction in `15 m² - 1` is exact since `m > 1`.
