# Card totient sigma gt div log five is Big O

Except for `O(x log₅ x / log₄ x)` positive integers `n ≤ x`, `φ(σ(n)) ≤ n / log₅ n`.
The finitely many small `n` for which `log₅ n` is not a positive real are absorbed by the
`O`-term.
