# Card lift padic Val eq one

For a prime `p`, `f ∈ ℤ[x, y]` and a point `(r, s)` of `C_f(𝔽_p)` with `f_x(r, s)` or
`f_y(r, s)` non-zero modulo `p`, exactly `p(p - 1)` of the lifts `(r + kp, s + lp)` have
`ν_p(f) = 1`.
