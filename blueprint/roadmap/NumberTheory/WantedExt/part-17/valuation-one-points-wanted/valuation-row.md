# Valuation Row

The row `(f_x(r, s), f_y(r, s), f(r, s)/p)` modulo `p` of the paper's matrix `B_k(P)`
(eq. (2)) at a point `(r, s)`; for points of `C_f(𝔽_p)` the division is exact.
