# Points of $C_f(\mathbb{F}_p)$

**Points of $C_f(\mathbb{F}_p)$.** For $f \in \mathbb{Z}[x, y]$ and a natural number $p$,
$C_f(\mathbb{F}_p)$ is the set of pairs $(r, s)$ of natural numbers with $r, s < p$ such that $p$
divides $f(r, s)$.
