# Card lift padic Val gt one le one

For a prime `p`, `g ∈ ℤ[x]` and `0 ≤ r < p` that is not a common root of `g` and `g'`
modulo `p`, at most one `k ∈ {0, …, p - 1}` has `ν_p(g(r + kp)) > 1`.
