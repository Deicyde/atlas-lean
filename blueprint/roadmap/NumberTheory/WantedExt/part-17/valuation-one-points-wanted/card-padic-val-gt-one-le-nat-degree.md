# Card padic Val gt one le nat Degree

For a non-zero `g ∈ ℤ[x]` of degree `d` with non-zero discriminant, for all sufficiently large primes `p`
at most `d` residues `0 ≤ x < p²` have `ν_p(g(x)) > 1`.
