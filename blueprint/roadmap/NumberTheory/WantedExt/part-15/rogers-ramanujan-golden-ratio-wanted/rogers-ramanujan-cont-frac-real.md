# Rogers Ramanujan Cont Frac Real

The Rogers-Ramanujan continued fraction for real `0 ≤ x < 1`, through its product formula
`R(x) = x^{1/5} (x; x⁵)_∞ (x⁴; x⁵)_∞ / ((x²; x⁵)_∞ (x³; x⁵)_∞)` with the positive real fifth
root.
