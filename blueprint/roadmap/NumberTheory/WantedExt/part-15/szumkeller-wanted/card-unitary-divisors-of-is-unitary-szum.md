# Card unitary Divisors of is Unitary SZumkeller

For a unitary s-Zumkeller number whose least unitary divisor exceeding `1`
is `2`, `3` or `4`, the number of unitary divisors exceeds `2⁶`, `2²` or `2²`
respectively.
