# Upper bound for $H(x) \log H(x)$

**Upper bound for $H(x) \log H(x)$.** With $H$ the real extension of the harmonic numbers and
$\log$ the natural logarithm, every real $x \ge 4$ satisfies

$$H(x) \log H(x) < \frac{x^2}{x + 6/\pi^2}.$$
