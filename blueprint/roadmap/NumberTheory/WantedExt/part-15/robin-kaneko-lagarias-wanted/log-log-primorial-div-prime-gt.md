# Log log primorial div prime gt

For `k ≥ 99` and `1 ≤ j ≤ k + 1`, with `m = p_{k+1}# / p_j`,
`log log m > log((k+1)(log(k+1) + log log(k+1) - 1) log 2.51 - log p_j)`.
