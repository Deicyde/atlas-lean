# Least kaneko lagarias counterexample superabundant

If the Kaneko–Lagarias inequality `σ(n) < exp(H_n) log H_n` (asserted for `n > 60`) fails,
its least counterexample `n₀ > 60` is superabundant: `σ(m)/m < σ(n₀)/n₀` for all `1 ≤ m < n₀`.
