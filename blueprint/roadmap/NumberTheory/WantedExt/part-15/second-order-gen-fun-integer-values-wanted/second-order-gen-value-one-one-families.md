# Second Order Gen Value one one families

For `(a, b) = (1, 1)` there are no families beyond those of Theorem 2: every rational `x`
with `A(x) ∈ ℤ` is a ratio of consecutive terms of `A^{(0,1)}` or of `A^{(-1,1)}`.
