# Is In Second Order Family

`x` belongs to the family generated by `x₀ = r/s` (lowest terms, `s > 0`): `x` is a ratio
`A^{(r,s)}_n / A^{(r,s)}_{n+1}` with `A^{(r,s)}_{n+1} ≠ 0`.
