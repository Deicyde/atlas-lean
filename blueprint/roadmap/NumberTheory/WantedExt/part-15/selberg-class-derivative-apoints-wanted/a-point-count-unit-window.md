# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4.** `N_k(a; T + 1, F) - N_k(a; T, F) = O(log T)` as `T → ∞`. Stated for every
strip `E₂ < Re s < E₁` (the paper uses the `E₁`, `E₂` of Lemmas 1 and 3) and under `a ≠ 0` or
`a(n) ≠ 0` for some `n ≥ 2`.
