# Choose three mul mod Eq

Helou and Terjanian, `p = 3`: for `0 < m < n`,
`C(3n, 3m) ≡ C(n, m) (mod 3^{2 + max(ν_3(m), ν_3(n-m)) + ν_3(C(n,m))})`.
