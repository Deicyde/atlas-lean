# Choose two mul mod Eq

Helou and Terjanian, `p = 2`: for `0 < m < n`,
`C(2n, 2m) ≡ C(n, m) (mod 2^{1 + max(ν_2(m), ν_2(n-m)) + ν_2(C(n,m))})`.
