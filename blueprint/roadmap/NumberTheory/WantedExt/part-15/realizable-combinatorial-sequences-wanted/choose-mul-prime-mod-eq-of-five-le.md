# Choose mul prime mod Eq of five le

Helou and Terjanian, prime `p ≥ 5`: for `0 < m < n`,
`C(np, mp) ≡ C(n, m) (mod p^{3 + max(ν_p(m), ν_p(n-m)) + ν_p(C(n,m))})`.
