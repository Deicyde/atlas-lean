# Moebius conv nonneg of geometric growth

If `b(n) ≥ 0` and `b(n+1) ≥ C b(n)` for all `n ≥ 1`, with a constant `C ≥ 1.221`, then
`(μ * b)(n) ≥ 0` for every `n ≥ 1`.
