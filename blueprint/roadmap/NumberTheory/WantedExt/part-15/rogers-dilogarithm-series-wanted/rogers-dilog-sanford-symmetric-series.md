# Rogers Dilog sanford symmetric series

The symmetric specialization `a ↦ 1/2 + a/2`, `b ↦ 1/2 - a/2`: for `a ∈ (0, 1)`,
`∑_{n ≥ 1} L(a²(1/2 - a/2)ⁿ(1/2 + a/2)ⁿ / ((1/2 + a/2)^{n+1} - (1/2 - a/2)^{n+1})²)
  = L((1 - a)/(1 + a))`.
