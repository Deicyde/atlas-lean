# Rogers Dilog lucas U series

Sanford's Lucas-sequence generalization of Bridgeman, equation (8): for integer parameters
`P > 0`, `Q > 0` with `D = P² - 4Q > 0`, `α = (P + √D)/2`, and `k ≥ 1`,
`∑_{n ≥ 1} L(U_k² Q^{kn} / U_{k(n+1)}²) = L(Q^k/α^{2k})`.
