# Card sq add sq eq

Theorem 16 (Jacobi's two-square theorem): for `n ≥ 1`, the number
`r_2(n)` of pairs `(a, b) ∈ ℤ²` with `a² + b² = n` is
`4 (d_{1,4}(n) - d_{3,4}(n))`.
