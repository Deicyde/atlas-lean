# Round div sequence card ge

Lemma 13: in the rounding sequence `⌊n/1 + 1/2⌋, …, ⌊n/n + 1/2⌋`, all
`n` terms are at least `1`, and for `k ≥ 2` exactly `⌊2n/(2k-1)⌋` terms are
at least `k`.
