# Card sq add two mul sq eq

Proposition 48 (Dickson, Exercise XXII.1): for `n ≥ 1`, the number of
pairs `(x, y) ∈ ℤ²` with `x² + 2y² = n` is
`2 (d_{1,8}(n) + d_{3,8}(n) - d_{5,8}(n) - d_{7,8}(n))`.
