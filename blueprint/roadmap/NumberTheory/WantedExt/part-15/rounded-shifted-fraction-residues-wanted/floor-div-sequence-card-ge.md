# Floor div sequence card ge

Lemma 1: for `k ≥ 1`, exactly `⌊n/k⌋` terms of the floor sequence
`⌊n/1⌋, …, ⌊n/n⌋` are at least `k`. Stated for every `n : ℕ` (the case
`n = 0` is the empty sequence).
