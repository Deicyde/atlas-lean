# Shifted Floor Residue Count

The count `𝓝_{n,α,ν,r,m}` of terms of the `α`-shifted, `ν`-offset floor
sequence `⌊(n-ν)/1 + α⌋, …, ⌊(n-ν)/n + α⌋` that are congruent to `r`
modulo `m`.
