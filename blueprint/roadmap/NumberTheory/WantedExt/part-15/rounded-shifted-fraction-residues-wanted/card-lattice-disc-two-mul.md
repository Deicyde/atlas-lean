# Card lattice disc two mul

Proposition 17: the closed disc of radius `√(2n)` about the origin
contains `4 𝓡_n + 4n + 1` lattice points, i.e. `C(2n) = 4 𝓡_n + 4n + 1`.
Stated for every `n : ℕ` (both sides are `1` at `n = 0`).
