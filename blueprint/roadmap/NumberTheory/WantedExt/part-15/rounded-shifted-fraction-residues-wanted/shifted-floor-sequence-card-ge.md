# Shifted floor sequence card ge

Lemma 24: for `α, ν ∈ [0, 1)` and `nα ≥ ν`, all `n` terms of the
`α`-shifted, `ν`-offset floor sequence of length `n` are at least `1`, and
for `k ≥ 2` exactly `⌊(n-ν)/(k-α)⌋` of them are at least `k`.
