# Card eisenstein form eq

Proposition 42 (Dickson, Exercise XXII.2): for `n ≥ 1`, the number of
pairs `(x, y) ∈ ℤ²` with `x² + xy + y² = n` is `6 (d_{1,3}(n) - d_{2,3}(n))`.
