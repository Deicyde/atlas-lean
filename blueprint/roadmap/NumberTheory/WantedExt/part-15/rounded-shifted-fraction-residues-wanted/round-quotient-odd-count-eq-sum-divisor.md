# Round Quotient Odd Count eq sum divisor difference

Lemma 15: `𝓡_n = -n + ∑_{k=1}^{2n} (d_{1,4}(k) - d_{3,4}(k))`, where
`d_{r,4}(k)` counts the positive divisors of `k` congruent to `r` mod `4`.
