# Shadow Markoff Mutate vertex 001

The first two mutations of the tree of `(1, 1, 1 + ε)` (the paper's vertex `(0:0:1)`):
`(1,1,1 | 0,0,1) ↦ (2,1,1 | 2,0,1) ↦ (2,5,1 | 2,10,1)`, matching the labels `2 2` and `5 10` of
the paper's figure.
