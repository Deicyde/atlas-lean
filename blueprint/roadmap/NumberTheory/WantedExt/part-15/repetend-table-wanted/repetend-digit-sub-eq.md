# Repetend digit sub eq

Digit differences: with `b_x = b^x mod n` and `r_{x,j} = ⌊b_x^(j+1)/n⌋ mod b_x`,
`n (r_{ym,e} - r_{y,d}) = b_{ym} b_{yme} - b_{ym(e+1)} - b_y b_{yd} + b_{y(d+1)}`.
