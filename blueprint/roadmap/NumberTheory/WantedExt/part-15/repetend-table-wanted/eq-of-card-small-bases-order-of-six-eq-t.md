# Eq of card small bases order Of six eq two

If exactly two small bases have order `6` modulo `n`, then `n = 3^k t ≥ 9` or `n = p^k t` with
`p ≡ 1 (mod 6)` prime, `k ≥ 1`, and `t ∈ {1, 2}`.
