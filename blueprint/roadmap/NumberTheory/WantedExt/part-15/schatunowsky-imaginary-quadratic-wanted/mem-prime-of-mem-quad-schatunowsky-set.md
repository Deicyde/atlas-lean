# Mem prime of mem quad Schatunowsky Set

Lemma 4: for `α ∈ Q_{m,ℓ}` with `N(π_n)^{ℓ+1} < N(α)`, `π_n# ∣ α`, i.e. `α` lies in every
nonzero prime ideal of norm at most `N(π_n)`.
