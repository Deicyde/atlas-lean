# Quad Schatunowsky Set norm bound and finite

Theorem 4: if `n₀` has the property of Proposition 4 (`N(π_{n+1})^{ℓ+1} < N(π_n#)` for all
`n ≥ n₀`), then no `α` with `N(π_{n₀})^{ℓ+1} < N(α)` lies in `Q_{m,ℓ}`; and `Q_{m,ℓ}` is finite.
