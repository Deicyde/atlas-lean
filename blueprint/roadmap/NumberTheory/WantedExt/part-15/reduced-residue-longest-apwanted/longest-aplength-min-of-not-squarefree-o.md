# Longest APLength min of not squarefree of not is Prime Pow

Theorem 3, `n` neither squarefree nor a prime power:
`n / rad(n) ≤ min_{m ∈ ℤ} f_m(n) ≤ max(n / rad(n), gpf(n) - 1)`, and the minimum equals
`n / rad(n)` when `n / rad(n) ≥ gpf(n) - 1`.
