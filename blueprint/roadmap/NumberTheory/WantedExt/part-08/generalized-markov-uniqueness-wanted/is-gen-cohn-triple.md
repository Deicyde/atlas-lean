# Is Gen Cohn Triple

`(P, Q, R)` is a `k`-generalized Cohn triple associated with `(a, b, c)`: `P, Q, R` are
`k`-generalized Cohn matrices, `Q = PR - S`, and their `(1, 2)`-entries `(a, b, c)` form a
`k`-generalized Markov triple.
