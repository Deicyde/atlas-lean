# Special Linear Group two trace identities

Lemma 3.6: for `A, B ∈ SL(2, ℤ)`, `tr A = tr A⁻¹`, `tr(AB) = tr A tr B - tr(AB⁻¹)` and
`A² = (tr A) A - I`.
