# Is Gen Markov Triple

A `k`-generalized Markov triple: a positive integer solution of the `k`-generalized
Markov equation `GME(k)`: `x² + y² + z² + k(yz + zx + xy) = (3 + 3k) x y z`. For `k = 0`
these are the classical Markov triples.
