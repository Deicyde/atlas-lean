# Not dvd sq sub four of large prime

Proposition 4.15: for an odd prime `p`, if `k ≥ 4` is even and `p > k/2 + 1`, or `k` is
odd and `p > k + 2`, then `k² - 4 ≢ 0 (mod p)`.
