# Gen Markov Vieta

One Vieta jumping of `GME(k)`: `false` is the left jumping
`(a, b, c) ↦ (a, (a² + kab + b²)/c, b)`, `true` the right jumping
`(a, b, c) ↦ (b, (b² + kbc + c²)/a, c)`.
