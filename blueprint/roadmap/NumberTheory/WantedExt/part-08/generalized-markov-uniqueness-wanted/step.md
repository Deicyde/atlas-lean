# Step

Theorem 3.5: if `(P, Q, R)` is a `k`-generalized Cohn triple associated with `(a, b, c)`,
then `(P, PQ - S, Q)` is one associated with `(a, (a² + kab + b²)/c, b)` and `(Q, QR - S, R)`
one associated with `(b, (b² + kbc + c²)/a, c)`.
