# Gen Cohn Tree neg snd eq

Lemma 4.5: for an irreducible fraction `t ∈ (0, 1)` with Farey vertex `(r, t, s)`,
`C_t(k, -k) = [[u_t, m_t], [*, (3 + 3k)m_t - u_t - k]]`, where `u_t` is the characteristic
number of `(m_r, m_t, m_s)`.
