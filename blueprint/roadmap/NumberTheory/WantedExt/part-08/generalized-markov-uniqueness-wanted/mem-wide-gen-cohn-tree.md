# Mem wide Gen Cohn Tree

Proposition 3.10: every `k`-generalized Cohn triple associated with `(a, b, c)`,
`b ≥ max {a, c}`, is a vertex of `WGCT(k, ℓ)` for some `ℓ ∈ ℤ`.
