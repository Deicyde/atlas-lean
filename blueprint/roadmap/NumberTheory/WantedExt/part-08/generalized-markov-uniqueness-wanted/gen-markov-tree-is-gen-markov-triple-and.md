# Gen Markov Tree is Gen Markov Triple and once

Corollary 2.2: every vertex of `MT(k)` is a `k`-generalized Markov triple, and every
`k`-generalized Markov triple `(a, b, c)` with `b > max {a, c}` appears exactly once in
`MT(k)`.
