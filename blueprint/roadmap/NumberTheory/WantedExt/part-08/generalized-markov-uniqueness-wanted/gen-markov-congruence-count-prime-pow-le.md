# Gen Markov Congruence Count prime pow le two

Lemma 4.11: for an odd prime `p`, `m ≥ 2` and `k² - 4 ≢ 0 (mod p)`,
`x² + k x + 1 ≡ 0 (mod p^m)` has at most two solutions.
