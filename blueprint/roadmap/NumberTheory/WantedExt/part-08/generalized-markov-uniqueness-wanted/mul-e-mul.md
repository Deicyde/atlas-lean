# Mul E mul

Lemma 3.7: for a `k`-generalized Cohn matrix `M` and `E = [[0, 0], [3 + 3k, 0]]`,
`M E M = (tr M + k) M + E` and `M⁻¹ E M⁻¹ = -(tr M⁻¹ + k) M⁻¹ + E`.
