# The congruence $x^2 + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod 5$ has two solutions

**The congruence $x^2 + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod 5$ has two solutions.** Exactly two integers $x$
with $0 \le x < 5$ satisfy $x^2 + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod 5$.

They are $x = 2$ and $x = 3$.
