# Farey Tree

The vertex of the Farey tree `FT`, rooted at `(0/1, 1/1, 1/0)` with children
`(a/b, (a+c)/(b+d), c/d)` and `(c/d, (c+e)/(d+f), e/f)`, reached along the path `w`.
A fraction `n/d` is the pair `(n, d)`, and `1/0` stands for `∞`.
