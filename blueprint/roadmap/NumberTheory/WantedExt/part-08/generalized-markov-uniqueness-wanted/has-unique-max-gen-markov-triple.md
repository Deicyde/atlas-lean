# Has Unique Max Gen Markov Triple

`b` is the maximum of a unique `k`-generalized Markov triple up to order: there is a
triple `(a, b, c)` with `max {a, b, c} = b`, and any other such triple `(a', b, c')` has
`{a', c'} = {a, c}`.
