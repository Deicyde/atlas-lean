# Markov has Unique Max Triple of congruence Count le two

Theorem 1.2 (classical criterion, `k = 0`): for a Markov number `b ∉ {1, 2}`, if
`x² + 1 ≡ 0 (mod b)` has at most two solutions, then `b` is the maximum of a unique Markov
triple up to order.
