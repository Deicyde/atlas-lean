# Has Unique Max Gen Markov Triple odd prime pow

Theorem 4.10: a `k`-generalized Markov number `b = p^m` or `2p^m` with `p` an odd prime,
`m ≥ 2` and `k² - 4 ≢ 0 (mod p)` is the maximum of a unique triple up to order.
