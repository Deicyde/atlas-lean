# Generalized Markov triples are pairwise coprime

**Generalized Markov triples are pairwise coprime.** If $(a, b, c)$ is a $k$-generalized
Markov triple, then $\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(b, c) = \gcd(a, c) = 1$.
