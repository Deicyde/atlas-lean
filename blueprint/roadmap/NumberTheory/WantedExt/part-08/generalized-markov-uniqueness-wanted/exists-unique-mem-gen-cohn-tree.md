# Exists Unique mem gen Cohn Tree

Proposition 3.17: a `k`-generalized Cohn triple associated with `(a, b, c)`,
`b > max {a, c}`, is the vertex of `GCT(k, ℓ)` for a unique pair of `ℓ ∈ ℤ` and path.
