# Pow add pow ne pow of prime five le

Corollary 1 (quoted by the paper from Bartolomé and Mihăilescu, arXiv:2108.08572): for primes
`5 ≤ p ≤ q`, `x^p + y^p = z^q` has no solution in pairwise coprime nonzero integers.
