# Solvable generalized Fermat equation

**Solvable generalized Fermat equation.** For natural numbers $m$, $n$, $r$, the equation
$x^m + y^n = z^r$ is called solvable when there are nonzero integers $x$, $y$, $z$ with
$x^m + y^n = z^r$.
