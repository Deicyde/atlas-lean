# Generalized Tau succ mod Eq card frequency two

Parity of `τ_k(n + 1)`: with `A = {a : 1 ≤ a ≤ k, C(k, a) odd}`, `τ_k(n + 1) ≡ F_A(n) (mod 2)`,
where `F_A(n)` counts partitions of `n` whose every frequency lies in `A`.
