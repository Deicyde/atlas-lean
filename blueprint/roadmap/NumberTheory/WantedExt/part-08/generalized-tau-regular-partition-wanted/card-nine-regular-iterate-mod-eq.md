# Card nine regular iterate mod Eq

`R_9((r - 1) 4^(s-1) + (4^s - 1)/3) ≡ R_9(r) (mod 3)`, with the particular cases
`R_9((4^s - 1)/3) ≡ 1`, `R_9(4^(s-1) + (4^s - 1)/3) ≡ 2`, `R_9(2·4^(s-1) + (4^s - 1)/3) ≡ 0`.
