# Ramanujan Tau succ mod Eq triangular sum seven

`τ(n + 1) ≡ ∑ (-1)^(m + r) (2m + 1)(2r + 1) (mod 7)`, the sum over `m, r ≥ 0` with
`n = m(m + 1)/2 + 7 r(r + 1)/2`.
