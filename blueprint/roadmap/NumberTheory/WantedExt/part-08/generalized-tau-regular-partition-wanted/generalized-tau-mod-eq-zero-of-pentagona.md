# Generalized Tau mod Eq zero of pentagonal

If `k - 1` is prime and `k - 1 ∤ n - 1 - P` for every generalized pentagonal number
`P ≤ n - 1`, then `τ_k(n) ≡ 0 (mod k - 1)`.
