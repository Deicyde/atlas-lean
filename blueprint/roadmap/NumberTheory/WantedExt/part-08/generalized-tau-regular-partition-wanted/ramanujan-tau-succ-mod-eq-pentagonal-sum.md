# Ramanujan Tau succ mod Eq pentagonal sum eleven

`τ(n + 1) ≡ ∑ (-1)^(l + m + s + r) (mod 11)`, the sum over generalized pentagonal indices
with `n = P(l) + P(m) + 11 P(s) + 11 P(r)`, `P(r) = r(3r - 1)/2`.
