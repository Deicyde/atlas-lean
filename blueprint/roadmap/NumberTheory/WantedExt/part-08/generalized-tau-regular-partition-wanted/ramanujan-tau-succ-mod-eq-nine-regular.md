# Ramanujan Tau succ mod Eq nine regular

Ramanujan's `τ` modulo `3`: `τ(n + 1) ≡ R_9(n/3)` if `3 ∣ n` and `≡ 0` otherwise. Here
`τ = generalizedTau 24`.
