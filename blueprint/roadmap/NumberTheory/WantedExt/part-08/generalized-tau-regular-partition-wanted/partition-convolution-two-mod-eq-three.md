# Partition convolution two mod Eq three

`∑_{a + b = n} p(a) p(b) ≡ ∑_{n = t + 3s} ω(t) p(s) (mod 3)`, where `ω(t)` is the `q^t`
coefficient of `∏ (1 - q^m)` (Euler's pentagonal series).
