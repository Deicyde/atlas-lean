# Generalized Tau prime pow succ mod Eq

`τ_{p^s}(n + 1) ≡ (-1)^t (mod p)` if `n = p^s P(t)` and `≡ 0` otherwise, written as a sum
over `t` that has at most one nonzero term (`pentagonal` is injective).
