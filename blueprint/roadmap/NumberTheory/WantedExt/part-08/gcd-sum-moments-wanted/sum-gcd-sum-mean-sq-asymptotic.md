# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1** (`k = 2`). There are a real polynomial `P` and a constant `C` with
`∑_{n ≤ x} A(n)^2 = x P(log x) + O(x^{1/2} exp(C (log log x)^2))` as `x → ∞`.
