# Theorem 2

**Theorem 2** (`k = 3`). There are a real polynomial `P` and a constant `C` with
`∑_{n ≤ x} A(n)^3 = x P(log x) + O(x^{5/8} exp(C (log log x)^2))` as `x → ∞`.
