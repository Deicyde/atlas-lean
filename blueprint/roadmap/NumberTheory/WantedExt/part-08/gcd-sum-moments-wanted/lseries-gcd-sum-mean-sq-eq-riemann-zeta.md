# Theorem 5

**Theorem 5**, the case `k = 2`: `F_2(s) = ζ(s)^4 / ζ(2s) · G̃_{2,2}(s)` for `ℜ s > 1`,
where `G̃_{2,2}(s) = ∑ h(n) n^{-s}` converges absolutely in every half-plane `ℜ s > 1/n`, that
is, for `ℜ s > 0`.
