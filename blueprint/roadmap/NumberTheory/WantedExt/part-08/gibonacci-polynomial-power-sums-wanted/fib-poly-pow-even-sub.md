# Fib Poly pow even sub

Lemma 3.1: for `k ≤ n`,
`Δ^{2(m-1)}(f_{n+k}^{2m} - f_{n-k}^{2m})
  = Σ_{j=0}^{m-1} C(2m, j)(-1)^{(n+k+1)j} f_{2k(m-j)} f_{2n(m-j)}`.
