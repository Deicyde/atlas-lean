# Lucas Poly pow even sub

Lemma 3.2: for `k ≤ n`,
`ℓ_{n+k}^{2m} - ℓ_{n-k}^{2m} = Δ² Σ_{j=0}^{m-1} C(2m, j)(-1)^{(n+k)j} f_{2k(m-j)} f_{2n(m-j)}`.
