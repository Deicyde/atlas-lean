# Gibonacci Poly mul sub sq

Lemma 2.1 (quoted from Koshy): for `k ≤ n`,
`f_{n+k} f_{n-k} - f_n² = (-1)^{n+k+1} f_k²` and `ℓ_{n+k} ℓ_{n-k} - ℓ_n² = (-1)^{n+k} Δ² f_k²`.
