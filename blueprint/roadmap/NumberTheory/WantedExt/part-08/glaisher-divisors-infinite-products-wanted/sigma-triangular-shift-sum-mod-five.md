# Sigma triangular shift sum mod five

For `n ≥ 1`, `∑_{j ≥ 0} σ(2n + 1 - j(j+1))` is `1` mod 5 when `n = 5k(k+1)/2` for
some `k`, and `0` mod 5 otherwise.
