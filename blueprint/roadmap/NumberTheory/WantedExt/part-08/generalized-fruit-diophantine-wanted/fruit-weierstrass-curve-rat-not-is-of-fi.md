# Fruit Weierstrass Curve rat not is Of Fin Order of two dvd num

Over `ℚ`: for an integer `α` not a root of `x⁸ + 5x⁶ - 432x⁴ - 4320x² - 10800`, `E_α(ℚ)` has
no torsion point `P = (x₀, y₀) ≠ O` whose `x₀` has even numerator in lowest terms.
