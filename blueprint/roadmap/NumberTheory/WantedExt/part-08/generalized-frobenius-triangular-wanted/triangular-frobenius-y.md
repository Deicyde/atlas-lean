# Triangular Frobenius Y

The integers `y_s` of the paper's Definition 1 for `s = k(k+1) + i`, `0 ≤ i ≤ 2k+1`:
for even `n`, `y_s^{even} = 2(k - i)` (`i ≤ k`) or `4k - 2i + 3` (`i > k`); for odd `n`,
`y_s^{odd} = k - i` (`i ≤ k`) or `2k - i + 1` (`i > k`).
