# Triangular Frobenius X

The integers `x_s` of the paper's Definition 1 for `s = k(k+1) + i`, `0 ≤ i ≤ 2k+1`:
for even `n`, `x_s^{even} = i` (`i ≤ k`) or `i - k - 1` (`i > k`); for odd `n`, `x_s^{odd} = 2i`
(`i ≤ k`) or `2(i - k) - 1` (`i > k`).
