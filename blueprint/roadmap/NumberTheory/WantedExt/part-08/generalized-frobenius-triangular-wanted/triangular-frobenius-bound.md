# Triangular Frobenius Bound

The bounds `N_s^{even} = 6⌊√(s+1)⌋ - 6` (for even `n`) and
`N_s^{odd} = 6⌊(√(4s+5) - 1)/2⌋ - 3` (for odd `n`), as integers.
