# Bisector equation symm

Symmetries of (1): if `(a, b, c)` is a real solution of
`(a - c)² (b² + 1) = (b - c)² (a² + 1)`, then so are `(a, b, -c⁻¹)` (for `c ≠ 0`), `(-a, -b, -c)`
and `(b, a, c)`.
