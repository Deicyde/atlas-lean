# Abs sq sub mul sq ne of two mod four

If `d ≡ 1 (mod 4)` and `z ≡ 2 (mod 4)`, then `|x² - d y²| = z` has no integral solutions.

The source's `z > 1` is not needed. The second sentence of part (a) (on `S(d)` and `l_2`) is not
formalized.
