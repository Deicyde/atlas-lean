# Neg Pell F

The `x`-sequence `f_n` of the powers `ε^n = f_n + g_n √d` of the least positive unit
`ε = X + Y √d` of norm `-1` in `ℤ[√d]` (`(X, Y)` the least positive solution of `x² - d y² = -1`).
