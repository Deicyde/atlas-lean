# Bisector equation form two

Converse of Theorem 1(i): for integers `l, m, n` with `|l| ≠ |m|`, `l m ≠ n²`, `l m n ≠ 0`,
both triples of Form (2) solve `(a - c)² (b² + 1) = (b - c)² (a² + 1)`.
