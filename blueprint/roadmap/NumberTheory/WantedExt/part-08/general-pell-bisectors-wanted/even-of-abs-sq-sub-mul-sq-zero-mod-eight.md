# Even of abs sq sub mul sq zero mod eight

If `d ≡ 5 (mod 8)` and `z ≡ 0 (mod 8)`, then every integral solution of `|x² - d y²| = z` is a
pair of even integers.

The source's `z > 1` is not needed.
