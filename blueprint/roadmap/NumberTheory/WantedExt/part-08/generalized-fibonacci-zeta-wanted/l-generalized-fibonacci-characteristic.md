# L Generalized Fibonacci Characteristic

The characteristic polynomial `φ_ℓ(X) = X^ℓ - ∑_{i < ℓ} X^i`, which is the
paper's `X^ℓ - X^(ℓ-1) - ⋯ - X - 1`, over `ℂ`.
