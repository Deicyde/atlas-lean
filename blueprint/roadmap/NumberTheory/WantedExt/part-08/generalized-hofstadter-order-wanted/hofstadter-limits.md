# Hofstadter limits

For `j ≥ 0` and `k ≥ 1`, with `α = α_k` and `β = β_k`: `F_k^j(n)/n → α^j`,
`L_k^j(n)/n → β^j`, the letter `i` has frequency `α^{k+i-1}` in `x_k` for `1 ≤ i < k`, and
the letter `k` has frequency `α^{k-1} = β - 1`.
