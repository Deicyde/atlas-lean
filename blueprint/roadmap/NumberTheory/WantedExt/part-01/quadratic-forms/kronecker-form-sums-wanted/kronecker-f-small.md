# Kronecker's sums at $n = 1$ and $n = 3$

**Kronecker's sums at $n = 1$ and $n = 3$.** Kronecker's sums $F_o$ and $F_e$ satisfy
$F_o(1) = 1/2$, $F_e(1) = 0$, $F_o(3) = 1$ and $F_e(3) = 1/3$.
