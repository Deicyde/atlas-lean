# Hbc Admissible prod le of ne floor

Under the hypotheses of Theorem 1 with `k ≥ 2`, unless `y_1 = ⌊1 + α⌋` (`α = (a + d)/d`),
`a ∏_{i=1}^k y_i ≤ ((a + d)/d^{3/4})^{2^k} - ((a + d)/d^{3/4})^{2^{k-1}}` (27).
