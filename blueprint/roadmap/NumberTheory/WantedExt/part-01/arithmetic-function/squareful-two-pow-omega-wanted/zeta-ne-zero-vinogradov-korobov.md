# Zeta ne zero vinogradov korobov

Lemma 6 (quoted classical result, Vinogradov–Korobov zero-free region):
there is `C* > 0` with `ζ(s) ≠ 0` for
`σ ≥ 1 - C* / ((log |t|)^{2/3} (log log |t|)^{1/3})`, `|t| ≥ t₀`.
