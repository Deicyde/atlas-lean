# Zeta le rpow of riemann Hypothesis

Lemma 8, equation (24) (quoted classical result): the Riemann hypothesis
implies `ζ(σ + it) = O(|t|^ε)` uniformly for `1/2 ≤ σ ≤ 1`, `|t| ≥ t₀`.
