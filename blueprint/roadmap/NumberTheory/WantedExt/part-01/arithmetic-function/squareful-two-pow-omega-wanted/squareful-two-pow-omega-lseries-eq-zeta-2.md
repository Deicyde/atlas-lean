# Squareful two pow omega LSeries eq zeta quotient five

Lemma 2: for `Re s > 1/2`, `F(s) = ζ²(2s)ζ²(3s)/ζ(4s) · H₂(s)`, where `H₂` is a
Dirichlet series converging absolutely for `Re s > 1/5`.
