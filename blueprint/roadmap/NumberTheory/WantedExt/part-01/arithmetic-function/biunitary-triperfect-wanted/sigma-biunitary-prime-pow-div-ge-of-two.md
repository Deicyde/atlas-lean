# Sigma Biunitary prime pow div ge of two mul sub one le

For a prime `p` and positive integers `m`, `e` with `e ≥ 2m - 1`,
`σ**(p^e) / p^e ≥ σ**(p^{2m}) / p^{2m}`, and, unless `e = 2m`,
`σ**(p^e) / p^e ≥ 1 + 1/p + ⋯ + 1/p^m` (in `ℚ`).
