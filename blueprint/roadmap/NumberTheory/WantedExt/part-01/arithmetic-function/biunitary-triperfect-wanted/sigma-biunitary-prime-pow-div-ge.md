# Sigma Biunitary prime pow div ge

Lower bounds for `σ**(p^e) / p^e` (in `ℚ`) for a prime `p` and `e ≥ 1`: it is at least
`1 + 1/p²`; at least `1 + 1/p` unless `e = 2`; at least `(1 + 1/p)(1 + 1/p³)` if `e ≥ 3`.
