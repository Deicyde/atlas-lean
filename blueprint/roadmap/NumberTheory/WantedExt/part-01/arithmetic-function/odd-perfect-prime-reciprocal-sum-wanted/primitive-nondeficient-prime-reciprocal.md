# Primitive Nondeficient prime Reciprocal Sum ge

If `n` is primitive non-deficient with least prime factor `p₁` and surplus
`S = H(n) - 2`, then `T(n) ≥ log 2 - 25/(64 p₁) + S/2 - S²/4`.
