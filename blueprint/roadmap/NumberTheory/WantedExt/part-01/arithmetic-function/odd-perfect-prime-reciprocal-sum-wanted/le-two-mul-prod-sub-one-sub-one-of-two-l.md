# Le two mul prod sub one sub one of two lt

If `p₁, …, p_m` are distinct odd primes with `∏ pᵢ/(pᵢ-1) ≤ 2`, and `q` is an odd
prime with `(∏ pᵢ/(pᵢ-1)) · q/(q-1) > 2`, then `q ≤ 2 ∏ (pᵢ - 1) - 1`.
