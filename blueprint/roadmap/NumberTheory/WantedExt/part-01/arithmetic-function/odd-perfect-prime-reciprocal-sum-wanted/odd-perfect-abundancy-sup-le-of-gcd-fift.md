# Odd Perfect abundancy Sup le of gcd fifteen

(Suryanarayana) Upper bounds for `H(n)` for an odd perfect number `n`, by
`gcd(15, n)`: `2.014754` for `1`, `2.096234` for `3`, `2.031002` for `5`, and `2.165439`
for `15`.
