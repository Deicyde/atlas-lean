# Multiplicative nonagonal additive three pow

Theorem 2 (the step for powers of `3`): under the hypotheses of Theorem 1, with
`α(x) = (14/17)(2/3)x - 12/17`, if `f n = n` for every `n < α(3^r)`, then `f (3^r) = 3^r`.
