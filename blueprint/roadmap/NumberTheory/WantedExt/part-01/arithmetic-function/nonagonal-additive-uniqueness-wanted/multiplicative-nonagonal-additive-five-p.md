# Multiplicative nonagonal additive five pow

Theorem 3 (the step for powers of `5`): under the hypotheses of Theorem 1, with
`β(x) = x/4`, if `r ≥ 3` and `f n = n` for every `n ≤ β(5^r)`, then `f (5^r) = 5^r`.
