# Beatty floor inv sub eq floor two sub

Identity (7): for complementary Beatty sequences `a n = ⌊nα⌋`, `b n = ⌊nβ⌋` with
`1 < α < 2` and every positive integer `n`,
`⌊β⁻¹ (b (n - 1) + 1)⌋ - ⌊β⁻¹ a n⌋ = ⌊(2 - α) n⌋`.
