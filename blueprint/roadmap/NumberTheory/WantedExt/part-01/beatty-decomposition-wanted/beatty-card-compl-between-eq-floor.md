# Beatty card compl between eq floor

For complementary Beatty sequences with slopes `1 < α < 2` and `β`, the number
`r n` of elements of `B` strictly between `⌊nα⌋` and `⌊nβ⌋` equals `⌊(2 - α) n⌋` for
every positive integer `n`.
