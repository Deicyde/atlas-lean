# Beatty card self between eq floor

For complementary Beatty sequences with slopes `1 < α < 2` and `β`, the number
`c n` of elements of `A` strictly between `⌊nα⌋` and `⌊nβ⌋` equals `⌊(β - 2) n⌋` for
every positive integer `n`.
