# Beatty floor fiber card eq or eq succ

If `1 / (k + 1) < α < 1 / k`, every non-negative integer `t` is attained by
`⌊nα⌋` (`n ≥ 1`) exactly `k` or `k + 1` times, and both multiplicities occur for
infinitely many `t`.
