# Beatty floor fiber card sets is Beatty

For irrational `α` with `1 / (k + 1) < α < 1 / k`, the positive integers attained by
`⌊nα⌋` (`n ≥ 1`) exactly `k + 1` times and those attained exactly `k` times are two
positive Beatty sets.
