# Tsum inv primes of almost Prime index sub one is Big O

The reciprocal sum of the primes of `k`-almost prime index tends to `1` exponentially fast:
`∑_{n ∈ N_{1k}} 1/n = 1 + O(e^{-k/2})` as `k → ∞`.
