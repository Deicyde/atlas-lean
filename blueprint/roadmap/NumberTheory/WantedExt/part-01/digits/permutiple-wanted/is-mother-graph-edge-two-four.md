# Is Mother Graph Edge two four

Characteristic values: in the `(2, 4)`-mother graph, `(0, 2)` is an edge
(`λ(0 + 2·2) = 0`) and `(0, 1)` is not (`λ(0 + 2·1) = 2 > 1`).
