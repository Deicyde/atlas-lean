# Tsum max LStar Block mul eq

Shifted maximal blocks: with `Q̃` the fundamental sequence of `Ẽ*` and
`ρ = ∑ β̄^1 Q̃`, `∑ β̄^ℓ Q̃ = ω̃^{ℓ-1} ρ` where `ω̃ = Q̃_1`. Zero-based (`maxLStarBlock L s` is the
paper's `β̄^{s+1}`, `Q' 0` is `Q̃_1`).
