# Is LStar Block Sum

`ε` is the sum of the disjoint proper `L*`-blocks with support intervals `[p.1, p.2]`,
`p ∈ I` (finitely or infinitely many): on each interval `ε` is a proper block, and `ε` vanishes
off the intervals.
