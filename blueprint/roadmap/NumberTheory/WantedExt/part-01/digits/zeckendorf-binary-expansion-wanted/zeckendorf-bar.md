# Zeckendorf Bar

`μ̄` for a nonzero finitely supported `μ` (Definition 20): with `μ̌` the largest element of
`E` below `μ` (`<_a`), `μ̄` is the immediate successor of `μ̌` in `E` (`0` when these do not
exist).
