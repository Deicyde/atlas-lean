# Zeckendorf Rev Bar

`rev̄(ε)` (Definition 23): `β̄^1` if `β̄^1 ≤_d ε`, and otherwise the coordinatewise limit of
`rev_n(\overline{rev_n(ε)})` as `n → ∞` (each coordinate is eventually constant by Lemma 10; `0`
for a coordinate that is not).
