# Ten pow block Length mod Eq two pow

Proposition 1: let `2^p - 1` be prime and let `p` divide the period `L` of `1/(2^p - 1)`;
with blocks of length `ℓ = L/p`, `10^ℓ ≡ 2^r (mod 2^p - 1)` for some `r ∈ {1, …, p - 1}`.
