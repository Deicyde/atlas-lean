# Kaprekar Four Digit Step examples

Characteristic values: in base `11`, `(5, 1) ↦ (9, 1)` and `(6, 0) ↦ (5, 5) ↦ (2, 0)`;
in base `7`, the terminal cycle `(3, 1) ↦ (5, 1) ↦ (5, 3) ↦ (3, 1)`.
