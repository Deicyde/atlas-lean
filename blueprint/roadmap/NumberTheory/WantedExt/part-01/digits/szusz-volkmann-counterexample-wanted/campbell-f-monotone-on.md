# Monotonicity of Campbell's interpolation

**Monotonicity of Campbell's interpolation.** Campbell's interpolation $f$ is monotone
(non-decreasing) on $[0, \infty)$: for all real numbers $x$ and $y$ with $0 \le x \le y$,
$f(x) \le f(y)$.
