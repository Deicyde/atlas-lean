# Digit Power Sum

`γ_j` is the sum of the `j`-th powers of all base-`b` digits other than
`d`. Source: Jean-François Burnol, *Measures for the Summation of Irwin Series*,
INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A11, Theorem 1.
