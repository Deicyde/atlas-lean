# Three mul dvd one sub neg two pow cube

If `n ∣ (1 - (-2)ᵏ)/3` then `3n ∣ (1 - (-2)^(3k))/3`, stated in the integral form
`3n ∣ 1 - (-2)ᵏ → 9n ∣ 1 - (-2)^(3k)` for all `n ∈ ℤ`, `k ∈ ℕ` (the paper has positive
`n`, `k`).
