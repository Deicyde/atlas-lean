# Ecsd neg two descendant mod Eq

In `G(-2n + a₁, -2n + a₂)` every `k`-descendant of `m` is congruent to
`(-2)ᵏm + a₁(1 - (-2)ᵏ)/3` modulo `|a₁ - a₂|` (the quotient is exact). Stated for all
`a₁, a₂ ∈ ℤ`; the exact-covering condition is not needed.
