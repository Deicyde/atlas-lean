# The $1$-descendants are the out-neighbours

**The $1$-descendants are the out-neighbours.** Let $R$ be a digraph on $\mathbb{Z}$ and let
$m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then $n$ is a $1$-descendant of $m$ in $R$ if and only if $R$ has an edge
$m \to n$.
