# Knott expansions of Lucas numbers

**Knott expansions of Lucas numbers.** For every $n \ge 1$, each of the Lucas numbers $L_{2n}$
and $L_{2n+1}$ (with $L_0 = 2$, $L_1 = 1$) has exactly $2n + 1$ Knott expansions.
