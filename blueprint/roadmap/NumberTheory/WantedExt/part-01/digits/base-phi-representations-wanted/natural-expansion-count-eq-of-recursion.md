# Natural Expansion Count eq of recursion

Recursion for `Tot_ν(N)`: the same as for `Tot_κ(N)` except
`r 1 = (s_1 + 1) / 2` when `s_1` is odd. Notation as in
`knottExpansionCount_eq_of_recursion`.
