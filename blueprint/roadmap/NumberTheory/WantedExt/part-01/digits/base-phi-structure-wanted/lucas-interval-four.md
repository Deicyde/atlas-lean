# Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_4$

**Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_4$.** The Lucas interval $\Lambda_4$ has left end
$7$ and right end $11$, that is, $\Lambda_4 = [7, 11]$.
