# Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_5$

**Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_5$.** The Lucas interval $\Lambda_5$ has left end
$12$ and right end $17$, that is, $\Lambda_5 = [12, 17]$.
