# Generalized Beatty comp wythoff

Composition with the lower and upper Wythoff sequences
`A(n) = ⌊n φ⌋` and `B(n) = ⌊n φ⌋ + n`: for `n ≥ 1`,
`V(p,q,r)(A(n)) = V(p+q, p, r-p)(n)` and `V(p,q,r)(B(n)) = V(2p+q, p+q, r)(n)`.
