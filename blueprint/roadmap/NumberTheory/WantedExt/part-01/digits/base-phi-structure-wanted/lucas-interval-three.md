# Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_3$

**Endpoints of the Lucas interval $\Lambda_3$.** The Lucas interval $\Lambda_3$ has left end
$5$ and right end $6$, that is, $\Lambda_3 = [5, 6]$.
