# Has Binomial Form two pow

If `n = xk + r` with `k ≥ 1`, `r ≥ 0` and `2^r C(k, ⌈k/2⌉) < 2^x - 1`, then `2^n`
has a representation in base `2^x - 1` in binomial form.
