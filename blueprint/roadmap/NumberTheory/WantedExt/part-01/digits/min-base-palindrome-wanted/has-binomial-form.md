# Has Binomial Form

`N` has a base-`b` representation in binomial form with at least two digits:
`N = (α C(k,k), α C(k,k-1), …, α C(k,0))_b` for some `α ≥ 1` and `k ≥ 1` (the
one-digit case `k = 0` is excluded as degenerate).
