# Three digit binomial Form two pow iff

The only 3-digit binomial form representations of `2^n` are
`2^i (1, 2, 1)_{2^{(n-i)/2} - 1}` with `0 ≤ 3i < n - 2` and `n ≡ i (mod 2)`.
