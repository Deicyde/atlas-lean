# Is Mother Graph Edge four ten

Characteristic edges of the `(4, 10)`-mother graph: `(2, 8)` and `(9, 9)` are edges
(`λ(2 + 6 · 8) = 0`, `λ(9 + 6 · 9) = 3`) and `(2, 1)` is not (`λ(2 + 6 · 1) = 8 > 3`).
