# Mem hoey Sloane Language 87912

Characteristic example: the inputs `(2, 8)(1, 7)(9, 9)(7, 1)(8, 2)` of
`87912 = 4 · 21978` form a member of the `(4, 10)` language, through the states
`0, 3, 3, 3, 0, 0`.
