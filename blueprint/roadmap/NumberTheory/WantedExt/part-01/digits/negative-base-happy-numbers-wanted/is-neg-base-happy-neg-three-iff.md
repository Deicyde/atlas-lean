# Is Neg Base Happy neg three iff

Grundman–Harris, part (2): there is an infinite arithmetic progression with difference `2` of
`-3`-happy numbers; indeed `a ∈ ℤ` is `-3`-happy iff `a ≡ 1 (mod 2)`.
