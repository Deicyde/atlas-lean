# Is Neg Base Happy neg two iff

Grundman–Harris, part (1): there is an infinite arithmetic progression with difference `3` of
`-2`-happy numbers; indeed `a ∈ ℤ` is `-2`-happy iff `a ≡ 1 (mod 3)`.
