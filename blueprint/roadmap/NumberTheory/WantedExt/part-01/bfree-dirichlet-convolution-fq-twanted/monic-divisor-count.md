# The $h$-fold divisor function

**The $h$-fold divisor function.** Let $F$ be a field and $h$ a natural number. For
$f \in F[T]$, $d_h(f)$ is the number of $h$-tuples $(g_1, \dots, g_h)$ of monic polynomials in
$F[T]$ with $g_1 \cdots g_h = f$.
