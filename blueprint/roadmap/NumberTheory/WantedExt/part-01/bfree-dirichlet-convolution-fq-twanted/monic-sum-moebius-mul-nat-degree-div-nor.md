# Monic sum moebius mul nat Degree div norm

For monic `f`, `∑_{g ∈ M, g ∣ f} μ(g) deg(g) / |g| = (Φ(f) / |f|) ∑_{P ∣ f} deg P / (1 - |P|)`,
with `Φ(f) / |f| = ∏_{P ∣ f} (1 - 1/|P|)` written as the product.
