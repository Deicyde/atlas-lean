# The $\kappa$-power of $1$

**The $\kappa$-power of $1$.** Let $F$ be a field and let $\kappa$ be a function from $F[T]$
to the natural numbers. Then the $\kappa$-power of the constant polynomial $1$ is $1^\kappa = 1$.
