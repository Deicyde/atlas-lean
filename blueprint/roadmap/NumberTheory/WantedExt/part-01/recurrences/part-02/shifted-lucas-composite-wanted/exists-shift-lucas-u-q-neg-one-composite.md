# Exists shift lucas U q neg one composite

For `P ≥ 1` and `Q = -1` there is a positive integer `K` such that
`U_n(P, -1) + K - 1`, `U_n(P, -1) + K` and `U_n(P, -1) + K + 1` are composite for every
`n ≥ 0`.
