# Sylvester numbers modulo 36

**Sylvester numbers modulo 36.** Let $(S_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Sylvester sequence,
$S_0 = 2$ and $S_{n+1} = S_n^2 - S_n + 1$. For every $n \ge 2$, $S_n \equiv 7 \pmod{36}$.
