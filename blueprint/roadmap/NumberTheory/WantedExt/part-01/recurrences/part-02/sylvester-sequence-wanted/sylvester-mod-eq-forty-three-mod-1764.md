# Sylvester numbers modulo 1764

**Sylvester numbers modulo 1764.** Let $(S_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Sylvester sequence,
$S_0 = 2$ and $S_{n+1} = S_n^2 - S_n + 1$. For every $n \ge 3$, $S_n \equiv 43 \pmod{1764}$.
