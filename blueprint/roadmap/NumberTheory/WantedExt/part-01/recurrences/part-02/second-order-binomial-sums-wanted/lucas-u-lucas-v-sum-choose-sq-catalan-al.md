# Lucas U lucas V sum choose sq catalan alternating

`∑_{k=0}^{2n} (-1)^k binom(2n, k) binom(4n, 2k) C_k C_{2n-k} U_{2n-k} V_k =
(-1)^n binom(3n+1, n) C_n C_{2n} U_{2n}`.
