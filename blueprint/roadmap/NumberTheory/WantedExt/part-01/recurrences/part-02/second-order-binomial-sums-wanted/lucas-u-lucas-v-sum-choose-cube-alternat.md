# Lucas U lucas V sum choose cube alternating

Dixon-type analogue: `∑_{k=0}^{2n} binom(2n, k)³ (-1)^k U_{2n-k} V_k =
(-1)^n binom(3n; n, n, n) U_{2n}`, with the trinomial coefficient written as
`binom(3n, n) binom(2n, n)`.
