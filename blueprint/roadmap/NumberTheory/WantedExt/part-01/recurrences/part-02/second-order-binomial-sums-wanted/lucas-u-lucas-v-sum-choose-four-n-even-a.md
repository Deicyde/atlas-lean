# Lucas U lucas V sum choose four n even alternating

`∑_{k=0}^{2n} binom(2n, k) binom(4n, 2k) (-1)^k U_k V_{2n-k} =
(-1)^n binom(6n, 2n) binom(2n, n) binom(3n, n)⁻¹ U_{2n}`, over `ℚ`.
