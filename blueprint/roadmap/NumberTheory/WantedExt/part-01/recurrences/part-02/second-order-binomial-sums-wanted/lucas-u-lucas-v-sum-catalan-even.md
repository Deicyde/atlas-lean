# Lucas U lucas V sum catalan even

`∑_{k=0}^{n} C_{2n-2k} C_{2k} U_k V_{n-k} = 2^{2n-1} (n + 2)/(2n + 1) C_{n+1} U_n`, over `ℚ`
(with `2^{2n-1}` written `2^{2n}/2`).
