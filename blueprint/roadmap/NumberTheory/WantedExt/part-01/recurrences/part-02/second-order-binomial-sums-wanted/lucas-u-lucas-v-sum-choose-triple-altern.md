# Lucas U lucas V sum choose triple alternating

`∑_{k=0}^{2n} binom(2n, k) binom(4n, k) binom(4n, 2n+k) (-1)^k U_k V_{2n-k} =
(-1)^n binom(2n, n) binom(5n, 2n) U_{2n}`.
