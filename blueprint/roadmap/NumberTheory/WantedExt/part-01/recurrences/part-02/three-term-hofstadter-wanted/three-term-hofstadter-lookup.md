# Three Term Hofstadter Lookup

The value at index `j` of the sequence whose first terms are `xs = #[B(1), …, B(m)]`:
`xs[j - 1]` for `1 ≤ j ≤ m`, `0` for `j ≤ 0` when `pad`, and undefined otherwise.
