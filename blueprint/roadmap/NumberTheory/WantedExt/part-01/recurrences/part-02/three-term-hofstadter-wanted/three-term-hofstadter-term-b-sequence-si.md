# Three Term Hofstadter Term b Sequence six seven

The `B`-sequence `⟨1, 2, 3, 4, 5⟩` continues with `B(6) = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6` and
`B(7) = B(1) + B(2) + B(3) = 6`.
