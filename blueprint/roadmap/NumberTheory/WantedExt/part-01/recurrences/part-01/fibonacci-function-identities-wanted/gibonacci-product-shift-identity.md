# Gibonacci product shift identity

For gibonacci sequences `G`, `H` and integers `s`, `k`:
`G_(k+1) H_(s+1) + G_(k-2) H_(s-2) = 2 G_k H_s + 2 G_(k-1) H_(s-1)`.
