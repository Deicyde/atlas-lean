# Lucas mul gibonacci seed sub eq

For gibonacci sequences `G`, `H` and integers `r`, `s`, `t`:
`L_r (G_0 H_(s+t-1) + G_1 H_(s+t)) - (-1)^r G_(s-r) (H_(t+1)+H_(t-1))
 = H_(r+t) (G_(s+1)+G_(s-1))`.
