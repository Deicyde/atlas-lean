# Gibonacci two mul add eq fib sq combination

For a gibonacci sequence `G` and integers `k`, `s`:
`G_(2k+s) = F_(k+1)^2 G_(s+1) + F_k^2 G_s - F_(k-1)^2 G_(s-1)`.
