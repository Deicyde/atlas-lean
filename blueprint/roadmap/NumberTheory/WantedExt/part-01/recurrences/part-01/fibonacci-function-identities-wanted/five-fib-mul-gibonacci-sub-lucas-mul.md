# Five fib mul gibonacci sub lucas mul

For a gibonacci sequence `G` and integers `k`, `r`:
`5 F_k G_(k+r) - L_k (G_(k+r+1) + G_(k+r-1)) = 2 (-1)^(k-1) (G_(r+1) + G_(r-1))`.
