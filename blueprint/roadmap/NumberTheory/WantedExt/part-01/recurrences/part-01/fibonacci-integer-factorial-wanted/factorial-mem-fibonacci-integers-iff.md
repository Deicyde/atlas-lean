# Factorial mem fibonacci Integers iff

The largest factorial which is a Fibonacci integer is `36!`: `n!` lies in the
subgroup of `ℚˣ` generated by the positive Fibonacci numbers `F_m` (`m ≥ 1`) if and only
if `n ≤ 36`.

The group `G_F` is the multiplicative group generated by the Fibonacci numbers; the
statement records both the paper's claim that `36!` is a Fibonacci integer and that no
`n!` with `n ≥ 37` is.
