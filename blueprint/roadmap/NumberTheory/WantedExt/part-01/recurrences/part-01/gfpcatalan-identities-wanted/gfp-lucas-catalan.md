# Gfp lucas catalan

Theorem 3.3 (2) (Catalan identity for GFPs of Lucas type): for `n ≥ 0`,
`α 2^{n-1} L_n = ∑_{i=0}^{⌊n/2⌋} C(n, 2i) d^{n-2i} (4g + d²)^i`, stated as
`2^n α L_n = 2 ∑ …` so that `n = 0` is included.
