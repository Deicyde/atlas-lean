# Gfp fib eval eq integral

Proposition 5.1: for `n ≥ 2` and a real `x` with `d(x)² + 4 g(x) ≥ 0`,
`F_n(x) = (n / 2^n) ∫_{-1}^{1} (d(x) + y √(d(x)² + 4 g(x)))^{n-1} dy`.
