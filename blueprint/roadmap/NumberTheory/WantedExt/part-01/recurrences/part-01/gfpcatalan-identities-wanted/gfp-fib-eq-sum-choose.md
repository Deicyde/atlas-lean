# Gfp fib eq sum choose

Lemma 3.1 (1) (quoted from Flórez and Saunders): for `n ≥ 1`, the Fibonacci-type GFP is
`F_n = ∑_{i=0}^{⌊(n-1)/2⌋} C(n-i-1, i) d^{n-2i-1} g^i`.
