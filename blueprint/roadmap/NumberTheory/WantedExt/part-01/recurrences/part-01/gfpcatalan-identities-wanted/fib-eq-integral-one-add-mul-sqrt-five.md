# Fib eq integral one add mul sqrt five

Corollary 5.1 (Stewart): `F_n = (n / 2^n) ∫_{-1}^{1} (1 + y √5)^{n-1} dy`. The corollary
states no range; it is stated for `n ≥ 1`, where the exponent `n - 1` is a natural number.
