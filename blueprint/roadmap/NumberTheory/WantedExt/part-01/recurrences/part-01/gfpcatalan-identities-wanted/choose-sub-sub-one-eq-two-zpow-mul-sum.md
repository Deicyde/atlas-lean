# Choose sub sub one eq two zpow mul sum

Lemma 3.2 (1) (Webb and Parberry): for `0 ≤ m < n`,
`C(n-m-1, m) = 2^{2m-n+1} ∑_{j=m}^{⌊(n-1)/2⌋} C(j, m) C(n, 2j+1)`.
