# Gfp fib catalan

Theorem 3.3 (1) (Catalan identity for GFPs of Fibonacci type): for `n ≥ 0`,
`2^{n-1} F_n = ∑_{i=0}^{⌊(n-1)/2⌋} C(n, 2i+1) d^{n-2i-1} (4g + d²)^i`.
