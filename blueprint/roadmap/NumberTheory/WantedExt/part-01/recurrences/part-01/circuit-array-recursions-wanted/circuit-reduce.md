# Circuit Reduce

One row reduction of a labelled triangular grid: left boundary edges by `𝒫`, other left edges
by `ℒ`, right boundary edges (`d = r`) by the mirror-image series, other right edges by `ℛ`, base
edges by `ℬ`.
