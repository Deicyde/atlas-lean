# Theorem 6

**Theorem 6.** With `S_{k,n}^(p) = ∑_{i=0}^n i^p F_{k,i}` (Eq. (24), `0^0 = 1`),
`k S_{k,n}^(p) = C_k^(p)(n) F_{k,n+1} + C_k^(p)(n+1) F_{k,n} - C_k^(p)(0)` for all `p, n ≥ 0`.
Stated for every nonzero integer `k`.
