# Corollary 9

**Corollary 9.** For odd `m` and all `p, n ≥ 0`,
`L_m ∑_{i=1}^n i^p F_{mi} = C_{L_m}^(p)(n) F_{m(n+1)} + C_{L_m}^(p)(n+1) F_{mn} -
C_{L_m}^(p)(0) F_m`.
