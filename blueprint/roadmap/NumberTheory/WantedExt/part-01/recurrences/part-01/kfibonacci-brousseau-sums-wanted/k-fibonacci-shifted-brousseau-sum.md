# Theorem 11

**Theorem 11.** For all `m, p, n ≥ 0`,
`k ∑_{i=1}^n i^p F_{k,m+i} = C_k^(p)(n) F_{k,m+n+1} + C_k^(p)(n+1) F_{k,m+n} -
C_k^(p)(0) F_{k,m+1} - C_k^(p)(1) F_{k,m}`. Stated for every nonzero integer `k`.
