# Brousseau A

The numbers `A_k^(p)` of Definition 1: `A_k^(0) = 1` and
`A_k^(p) = (2/k) ∑_{j=1}^{⌈p/2⌉} binom(p, 2j-1) A_k^(p-2j+1)` for `p ≥ 1` (written with
`p + 1` in place of `p` and `j + 1` in place of `j`).
