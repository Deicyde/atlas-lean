# The polynomial $C_k^{(0)}$ is constant $1$

**The polynomial $C_k^{(0)}$ is constant $1$.** For all rational numbers $k$ and $n$, the
coefficient polynomial $C_k^{(0)}$ for $k$-Fibonacci Brousseau sums satisfies $C_k^{(0)}(n) = 1$.
