# Lucas V quadratic eq pm discr zpow

Lemma 2.7: for `y = -1` (`D = x² + 4`) and positive `p, q, k, x` with `p` odd, if
`V_p(x) k² + (V_p(x) - 2)k - 1 = ±D^{p-q} V_p(x)²` (in `ℚ`, `D^{p-q}` an integer power), then
`(p, q, k, x) ∈ {(1, 1, 1, 1), (1, 1, 2, 1), (1, 1, 2, 5)}`.
