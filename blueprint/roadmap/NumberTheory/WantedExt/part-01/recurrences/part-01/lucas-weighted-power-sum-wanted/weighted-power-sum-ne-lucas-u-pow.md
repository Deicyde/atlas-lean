# Weighted power sum ne lucas U pow

Theorem 1.1, second part: for `y = ±1` with `x ≥ 2` when `y = -1` and `x ≥ 3` when `y = 1`,
and positive `n, p, q` with `max(p, q) ≤ 11` and `k ≥ 3`, `Σ_{j=1}^k j U_j^p ≠ U_n^q`.
