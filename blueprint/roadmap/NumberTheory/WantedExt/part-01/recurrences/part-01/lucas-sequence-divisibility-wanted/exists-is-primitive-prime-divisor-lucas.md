# Exists is Primitive Prime Divisor lucas U of discr pos

Carmichael: for nondegenerate `U(P, Q)` with `gcd(P, Q) = 1` and `D > 0`, `U_n` has a
primitive prime divisor (a prime `p` with `ρ(p) = n`) for every `n ≥ 1` outside `{1, 2, 6, 12}`.
