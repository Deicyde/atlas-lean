# Exists is Primitive Prime Divisor lucas U

Bilu–Hanrot–Voutier: for nondegenerate `U(P, Q)` with `gcd(P, Q) = 1`, `U_n` has a primitive
prime divisor for every `n ≥ 1` outside `{1, …, 8, 10, 12, 13, 18, 30}`.
