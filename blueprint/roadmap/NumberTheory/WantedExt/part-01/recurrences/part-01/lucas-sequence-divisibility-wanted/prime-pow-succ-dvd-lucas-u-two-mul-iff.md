# Prime pow succ dvd lucas U two mul iff

Let `gcd(P, Q) = 1` and let `p` be a prime with `p^i ∥ P`, `i ≥ 1`. Then for every
positive `n`, `p^{i+1} ∣ U_{2n}` if and only if `p ∣ n`.
