# Infinite strong frobenius pseudoprimes two primes

Rotkiewicz: for nondegenerate `U(P, Q)` with `Q = ±1`, infinitely many products `p₁p₂` of
distinct odd primes are both strong Lucas and Frobenius pseudoprimes.
