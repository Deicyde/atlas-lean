# Is Strong Lucas Pseudoprime

Strong Lucas pseudoprime: odd composite `N`, `gcd(N, QD) = 1`, `N - (D/N) = 2^s d` with `d`
odd, and `U_d ≡ 0` or `V_{2^r d} ≡ 0 (mod N)` for some `0 ≤ r < s`.
