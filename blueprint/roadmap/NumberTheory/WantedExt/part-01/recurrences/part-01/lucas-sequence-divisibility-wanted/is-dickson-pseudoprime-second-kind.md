# Is Dickson Pseudoprime Second Kind

Dickson pseudoprime of the second kind: positive odd composite `N`, `gcd(N, QD) = 1`,
`V_{N-(D/N)} ≡ 2Q^{(1-(D/N))/2} (mod N)`.
