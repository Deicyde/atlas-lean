# Is Lucas Frobenius Pseudoprime of is Strong Lucas Pseudoprime

For nondegenerate `U(P, Q)` with `Q = ±1`, a strong Lucas pseudoprime `N` with
`gcd(N, PD) = 1` is a Frobenius pseudoprime.
