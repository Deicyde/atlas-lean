# Odd lucas U coprime discr

Let `gcd(P, Q) = 1`, `P ≠ 0`, and let `m` be odd with `gcd(m, PQD) = 1` and
`3 ∤ m` when `P ≡ Q ≡ 1 (mod 2)`. Then `N₁ = U_m` and `N₂ = U_{2m}/P` are odd and
coprime to `D`.
