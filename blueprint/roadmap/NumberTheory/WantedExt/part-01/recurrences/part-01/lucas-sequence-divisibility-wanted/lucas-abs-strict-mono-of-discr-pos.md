# Lucas abs strict Mono of discr pos

For nondegenerate `U(P, Q)`, `V(P, Q)` with `D > 0`: `|U_n|` is strictly increasing
for `n ≥ 2` and `|V_n|` for `n ≥ 1`; if `P > 0` then `U_n > 0` for `n ≥ 1` and `V_n > 0`
for `n ≥ 0`; and `|U_3| ≥ 3` unless `|P| = -Q = 1`.
