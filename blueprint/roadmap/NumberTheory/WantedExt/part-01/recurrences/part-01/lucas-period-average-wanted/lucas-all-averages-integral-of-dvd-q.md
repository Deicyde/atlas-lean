# Lucas All Averages Integral of dvd q

Theorem 2.10: let `n > 1` and `m ∈ S'(U)`. If `n ∣ b` and `a ≡ 1 (mod n)`, or `n` is even,
`n ∣ b` and `a ≡ -1 (mod n)`, then `A_W(n)` and `A_W(m n)` are integers for all `W(a, b)`.
