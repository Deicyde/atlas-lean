# Lucas U sum odd of even p

Theorem 2.14: for nondegenerate `U(a, b)` with `a` even and `b` odd, `B_U(n)` is odd and
`A_U(n)` is not an integer when `n ≡ 2 (mod 4)`, and `C(2 ^ k) = 2 ^ k` for `k ≥ 0`.
