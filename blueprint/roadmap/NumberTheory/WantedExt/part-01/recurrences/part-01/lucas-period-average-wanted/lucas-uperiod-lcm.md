# Lucas UPeriod lcm

Theorem 1.2: for `gcd(m n, b) = 1`, `π_U(lcm(m, n)) = lcm(π_U(m), π_U(n))` and
`ρ_U(lcm(m, n)) = lcm(ρ_U(m), ρ_U(n))` (1.17), and the same for the iterates
`π_U^i` and `ρ_U^i` (1.18), where `π_U^0(m) = m` and `π_U^(i+1)(m) = π_U(π_U^i(m))`.
