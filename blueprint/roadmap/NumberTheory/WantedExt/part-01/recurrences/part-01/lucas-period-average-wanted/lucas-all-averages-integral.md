# Lucas All Averages Integral

"`A_W(n)` is an integer for all recurrences `W(a, b)`": every integer sequence `w`
satisfying `w (k + 2) = a * w (k + 1) + b * w k` has `n ∣ w 1 + ⋯ + w n`.
