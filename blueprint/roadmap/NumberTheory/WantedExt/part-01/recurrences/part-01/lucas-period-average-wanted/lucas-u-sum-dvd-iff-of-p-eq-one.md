# Lucas U sum dvd iff of p eq one

Theorem 2.6: for `a = 1`, `b ≠ 0` and `gcd(n, b) = 1`, `B_U(n) ≡ 0 (mod n)` if and only if
`U (n + 2) ≡ 1 (mod n)`.
