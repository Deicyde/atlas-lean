# Restricted period of the Fibonacci sequence modulo 2

**Restricted period of the Fibonacci sequence modulo 2.** The least restricted period
modulo $2$ of the Fibonacci sequence $F = U(1, -1)$ equals $3$ (note $F_3 = 2$).
