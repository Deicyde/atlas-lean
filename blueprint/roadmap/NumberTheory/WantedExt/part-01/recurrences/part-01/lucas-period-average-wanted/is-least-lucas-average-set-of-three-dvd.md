# Is Least lucas Average Set of three dvd

Theorem 2.22: for nondegenerate `U(a, b)` and `n` with `3 ∣ n`, `a ≡ -1` and
`b ≡ -1 (mod rad(n))`: `C'(n) = C(n) = n` and `A_W(n)` is an integer for all `W(a, b)`.
