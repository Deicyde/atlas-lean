# Lucas U sum formula

Corollary 2.1: for `b ≠ 0`, `(a + b - 1) B_U(n) = U (n + 1) - 1 + b U n` if
`a + b - 1 ≠ 0`, and `(-b - 1) B_U(n) = U (n + 1) - 1 - n` if `a + b - 1 = 0`.
