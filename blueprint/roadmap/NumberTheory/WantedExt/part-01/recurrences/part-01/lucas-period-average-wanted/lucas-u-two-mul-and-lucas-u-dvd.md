# Lucas U two mul and lucas U dvd

Lemma 1.1: `U (2 n) = U n * V n`, `U (2 n + 1) = b * U n ^ 2 + U (n + 1) ^ 2`, and
`U m ∣ U n` whenever `m ∣ n`. The record's trailing expository text on pure periodicity is not
part of the lemma.
