# Restricted period of a Lucas sequence modulo 1

**Restricted period of a Lucas sequence modulo 1.** For every Lucas sequence $U = U(P, Q)$,
the least restricted period $\rho_U(1)$ of $U$ modulo $1$ equals $1$.
