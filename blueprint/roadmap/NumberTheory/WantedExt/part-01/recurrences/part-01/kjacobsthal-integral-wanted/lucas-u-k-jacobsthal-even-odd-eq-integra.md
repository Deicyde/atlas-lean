# Lucas U k Jacobsthal even odd eq integral

`J_{k,2n} = (kn/2ⁿ) ∫_{-1}^{1} (k² + 4 + kΔ_k x)^{n-1} dx` and
`J_{k,2n+1} = 2^{-(n+1)} ∫_{-1}^{1} (nk² + k² + 4 + k(n+1)Δ_k x)(k² + 4 + kΔ_k x)^{n-1} dx`.
