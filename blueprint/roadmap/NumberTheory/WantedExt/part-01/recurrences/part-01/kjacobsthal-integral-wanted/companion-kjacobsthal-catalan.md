# Companion KJacobsthal catalan

Catalan's identity: for `r ≤ n`,
`𝒥_{k,n-r} 𝒥_{k,n+r} - 𝒥_{k,n}² = (2b - ak + aΔ_k)(ak - 2b + aΔ_k)(-2)^{n-r-2} J_{k,r}²`, with
`(2b - ak + aΔ_k)(ak - 2b + aΔ_k) = a²Δ_k² - (2b - ak)²` and `(-2)^{n-r-2}` an integer power in
`ℚ`.
