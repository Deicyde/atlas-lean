# The second companion $k$-Jacobsthal number

**The second companion $k$-Jacobsthal number.** For every natural number $k$ and all integers
$a, b$, the companion $k$-Jacobsthal numbers satisfy $\mathcal{J}_{k,2}(a, b) = bk + 2a$.
