# K Jacobsthal Params

The Lucas-sequence parameters `(P, Q) = (k, -2)` of the `k`-Jacobsthal numbers
`J_{k,n} = U_n(k, -2)` and the `k`-Jacobsthal-Lucas numbers `j_{k,n} = V_n(k, -2)`.
