# Lucas V k Jacobsthal even odd eq integral

`j_{k,2n} = 2^{-n} ∫_{-1}^{1} (k² + 4 + k(n+1)Δ_k x)(k² + 4 + kΔ_k x)^{n-1} dx` and
`j_{k,2n+1} = (k/2^{n+1}) ∫_{-1}^{1} (nΔ_k² + k² + 4 + k(n+1)Δ_k x)(k² + 4 + kΔ_k x)^{n-1} dx`.
