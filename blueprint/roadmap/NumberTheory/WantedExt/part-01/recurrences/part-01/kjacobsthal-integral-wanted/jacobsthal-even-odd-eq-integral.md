# Jacobsthal even odd eq integral

Jacobsthal case `k = 1`: `J_{2n} = (n/2ⁿ) ∫_{-1}^{1} (5 + 3x)^{n-1} dx` and
`J_{2n+1} = 2^{-(n+1)} ∫_{-1}^{1} (n + 5 + 3(n+1)x)(5 + 3x)^{n-1} dx`.
