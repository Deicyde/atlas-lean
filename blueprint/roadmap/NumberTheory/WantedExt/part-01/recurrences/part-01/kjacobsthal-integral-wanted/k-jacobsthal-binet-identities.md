# K Jacobsthal binet identities

Binet-type identities: `j_{k,n} + Δ_k J_{k,n} = 2σ_kⁿ`,
`j_{k,n} - Δ_k J_{k,n} = 2(-2)ⁿ/σ_kⁿ`, and `j_{k,n}² - Δ_k² J_{k,n}² = (-2)^{n+2}`.
