# Companion KJacobsthal

The companion `k`-Jacobsthal numbers `𝒥_{k,n}(a, b)`: `𝒥_{k,0} = a`, `𝒥_{k,1} = b`,
`𝒥_{k,n} = k 𝒥_{k,n-1} + 2 𝒥_{k,n-2}`.
