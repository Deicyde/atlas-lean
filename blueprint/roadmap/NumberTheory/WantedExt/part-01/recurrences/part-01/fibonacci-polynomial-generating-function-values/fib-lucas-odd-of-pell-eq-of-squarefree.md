# Fib Lucas odd of pell eq of squarefree

Proposition 2.2: if `b ∣ a`, `b` is squarefree and `P, Q ∈ ℕ` satisfy
`P² - (a² + 4b) Q² = -4b`, then `b^n P = L_{2n+1}` and `b^n Q = F_{2n+1}` for some `n ∈ ℕ`, where
`F_i = F_i(1)` and `L_i = L_i(1)`.
