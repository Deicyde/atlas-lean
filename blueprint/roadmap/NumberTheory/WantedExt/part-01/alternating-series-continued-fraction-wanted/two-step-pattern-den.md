# Two Step Pattern Den

The convergent denominators `q_n` of the two-step pattern
`[0; ω₀, 1, ω₁ q₁^β, 1, ω₂ q₃^β, 1, …]` (display (e2)): `q₀ = 1`, `q₁ = ω₀`,
`q_{2n+2} = q_{2n+1} + q_{2n}` and `q_{2n+3} = ω_{n+1} q_{2n+1}^β q_{2n+2} + q_{2n+1}`
(display (e22)).
