# Is DCarmichael mul iff

Williams: for `D ≡ 0, 1 (mod 4)` and primes `p₁ < p₂`, the product `p₁p₂` is `D`-Carmichael
iff `p₂ = p₁ + 2`, `(D | p₁) = -1` and `(D | p₂) = 1`.
