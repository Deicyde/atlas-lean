# Rosser's lower bound for the $n$th prime

**Rosser's lower bound for the $n$th prime.** For every positive integer $n$, the $n$th prime
$p_n$ (counting from $p_1 = 2$) satisfies $p_n > n \log n$.

For $n = 1$ the bound reads $2 > 0$.
