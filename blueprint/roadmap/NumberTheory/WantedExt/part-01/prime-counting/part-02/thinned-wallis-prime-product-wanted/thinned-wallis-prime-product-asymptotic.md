# Thinned wallis prime product asymptotic

For `q = 2^m` with `m ≥ 2` and a set `S` of reduced residues modulo `q`, there is
`K(q, S) ∈ (0, ∞)` with `P_S(x) = K(q, S) (log x)^{-2μ(S)/φ(q)} (1 + o(1))` as `x → ∞`.
