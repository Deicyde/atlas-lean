# Nth Prime sq log bound

Proposition 1: for every `n ≥ 1`, with one-indexed `pₙ`,
`937.5 pₙ² log pₙ + 5674.5 pₙ² > 118.676119576 n² log³ pₙ + 709.7856 n pₙ log² pₙ`.
