# An auxiliary inequality in $\log n$ and $\log\log n$

**An auxiliary inequality in $\log n$ and $\log\log n$.** For every integer $n \ge 2$, with
$L = \log n$,

$$\frac{4.151332(\log L - 2)}{L} + \frac{3.151332(\log L - 1)}{L} +
\frac{3.151332(\log L - 2)}{L^2} \le 1.$$
