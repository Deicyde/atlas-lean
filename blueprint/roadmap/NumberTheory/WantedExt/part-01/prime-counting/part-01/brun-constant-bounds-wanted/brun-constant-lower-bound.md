# Lower bound for Brun's constant

**Lower bound for Brun's constant.** Brun's constant $B$, the sum of
$\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{p + 2}$ over all primes $p$ such that $p + 2$ is also prime, satisfies
$B > 1.840503$.
