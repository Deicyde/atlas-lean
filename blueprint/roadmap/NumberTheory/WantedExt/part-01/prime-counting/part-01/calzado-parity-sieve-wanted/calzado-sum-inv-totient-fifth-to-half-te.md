# Calzado sum inv totient fifth to half tendsto

Lemma 1, equation (11): `∑_{x^{1/5} < p ≤ x^{1/2}} 1 / φ(p) → log (5/2)`.
The paper prints the limit as `0.916…`; the exact value `log (5/2) = 0.91629…`
is forced by Mertens' theorem.
