# Calzado sum inv totient near sqrt is Big O

Lemma 1, equation (12): for fixed `B > 0`,
`∑_{x^{1/2} (log x)^{-B} < p ≤ x^{1/2}} 1 / φ(p) ≪_B log log x / log x`.
