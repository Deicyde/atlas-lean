# Abs prime Counting sub logarithmic Integral lt

Johnston's unconditional range for Schoenfeld's inequality:
`|π(x) - li(x)| < √x log x / (8π)` for every real `x` with `2657 ≤ x ≤ 1.101 · 10²⁶`.
