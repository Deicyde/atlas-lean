# Abs theta sub lt div log cube

The paper's inequality (6) for Chebyshev's function:
`|ϑ(x) - x| < 0.024334 x / log³ x` for every real `x ≥ 1757126630797` (the prime
`p₆₄₇₀₇₈₆₅₁₄₃`). `ϑ` is Mathlib's `Chebyshev.theta`.
