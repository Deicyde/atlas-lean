# Prime Counting lt div log sub four terms

For every real `x ≥ 29.53`,
`π(x) < x / (log x - 1 - 1/log x - 3/log² x - 70.935/log³ x)`.
`π(x)` is `Nat.primeCounting ⌊x⌋₊`.
