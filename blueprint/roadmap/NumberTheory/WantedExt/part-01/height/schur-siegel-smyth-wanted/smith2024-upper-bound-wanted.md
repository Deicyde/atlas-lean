# Smith's upper bound for the Schur–Siegel–Smyth trace constant

**Smith's upper bound for the Schur–Siegel–Smyth trace constant.** The
Schur–Siegel–Smyth trace constant $C_{86}$, an extended real number, satisfies
$C_{86} < 1.89831$.
