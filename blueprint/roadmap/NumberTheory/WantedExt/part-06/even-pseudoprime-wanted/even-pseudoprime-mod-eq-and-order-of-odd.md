# Lemma 2

**Lemma 2.** If an odd prime `p` divides an even pseudoprime `N`, then
`N ≡ p (mod p ℓ₂(p))`, `ℓ₂(p)` is odd, `p ≡ ±1 (mod 8)` and `p ≤ N / 14`.
