# Lemma 5

**Lemma 5.** If `p² ∣ N` for an even pseudoprime `N` and a prime `p`, then `p` is a
Wieferich prime, `2^{p-1} ≡ 1 (mod p²)`; in particular `p ≥ 3511`.
