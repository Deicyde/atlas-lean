# Ramanujan K

Ramanujan's function `k(q) = r(q) r²(q²)`, through its product representation
`k(q) = q (q, q², q⁸, q⁹; q¹⁰)_∞ / (q³, q⁴, q⁶, q⁷; q¹⁰)_∞`.
