# Rat Pythagorean Triples comm Group

`E` with `⋆` is an infinite commutative group: `⋆` is associative and commutative on `E`,
`(1, 0, 1) ∈ E` is an identity, every element of `E` has an inverse in `E`, and `E` is infinite.
