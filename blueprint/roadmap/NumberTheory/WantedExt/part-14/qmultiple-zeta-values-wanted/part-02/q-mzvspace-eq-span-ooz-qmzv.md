# Q MZVSpace eq span ooz QMZV

The spans of the OOZ model: (i) `𝒵_{q,1}` is spanned by the `ζ_q^OOZ(k)` with
`k₁ ≥ 2`, `kᵢ ≥ 1`; (ii) `𝒵_q` is spanned by the `ζ_q^OOZ(k)` with `k₁ ≥ 1`,
`kᵢ ≥ 0`.
