# Stanley Partitions

The set `𝒫_r(N)` of partitions of `N` of length `r` in Stanley coordinates:
pairs `((m₁, …, mᵣ), (n₁, …, nᵣ))` of positive integers with `m₁ > ⋯ > mᵣ` and
`m₁ n₁ + ⋯ + mᵣ nᵣ = N` (all coordinates are then at most `N`).
