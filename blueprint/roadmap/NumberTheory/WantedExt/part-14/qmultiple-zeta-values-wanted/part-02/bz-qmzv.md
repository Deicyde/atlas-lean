# Bz QMZV

The Bradley–Zhao qMZV `ζ_q^BZ(k) = ζ_q(k₁, …, kᵣ; X^{k₁-1}, …, X^{kᵣ-1})
= ∑_{m₁ > ⋯ > mᵣ > 0} ∏ⱼ q^{mⱼ(kⱼ-1)} / (1 - q^{mⱼ})^{kⱼ}` of an admissible
index (`k₁ ≥ 2`, `kᵢ ≥ 1`); junk for other indices.
