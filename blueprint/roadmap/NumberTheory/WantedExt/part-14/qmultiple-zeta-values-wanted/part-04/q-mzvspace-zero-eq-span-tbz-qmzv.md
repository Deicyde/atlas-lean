# Q MZVSpace zero eq span tbz QMZV

The TBZ model spans `𝒵_q`: `𝒵_q` is the `ℚ`-span of the `ζ_q^TBZ(k₁, …, kᵣ)` with
`kᵢ ∈ ℕ̄` (entries `some kᵢ` have `kᵢ ≥ 1`) and `k₁ ≠ 1`.
