# Eulerian QSeries

The `q`-series `∑_{n ≥ 1} n^{k-1}/(k-1)! q^{m n} = P_k(q^m)/(1 - q^m)^k` (`P_k` the `k`-th
Eulerian polynomial), the inner sum of a bi-bracket.
