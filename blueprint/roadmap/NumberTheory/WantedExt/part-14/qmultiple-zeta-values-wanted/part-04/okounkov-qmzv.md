# Okounkov QMZV

The Okounkov qMZV `ζ_q^Oko(k₁, …, kᵣ) = ζ_q(k₁, …, kᵣ; p_{k₁}, …, p_{kᵣ})` for indices with
every entry `≥ 2`; junk otherwise.
