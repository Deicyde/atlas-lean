# Okounkov polynomials

**Okounkov polynomials.** For $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the Okounkov polynomial
$p_k \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ is $p_k(X) = X^{k/2}$ if $k$ is even and $p_k(X) = X^{(k-1)/2}(1 + X)$ if
$k$ is odd.
