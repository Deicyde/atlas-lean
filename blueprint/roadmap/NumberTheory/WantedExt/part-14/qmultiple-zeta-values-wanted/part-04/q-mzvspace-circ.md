# Q MZVSpace Circ

The space `𝒵°_{q,d}`: the `ℚ`-span of the general qMZVs `ζ_q(k₁, …, kᵣ; Q₁, …, Qᵣ)` with
`kⱼ ≥ 1`, `deg Qⱼ ≤ kⱼ - d` (encoded as `natDegree Qⱼ + d ≤ kⱼ`) and every `Qⱼ ∈ X ℚ[X]`.
