# Coeff sz Gen

The coefficient of `∏ⱼ Xⱼ^{aⱼ} Yⱼ^{bⱼ}` in `s(X; Y)` is
`ζ_q^SZ(a₁ + 1, {0}^{b₁}, …, aᵣ + 1, {0}^{bᵣ})`.
