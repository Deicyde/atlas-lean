# Sz Shuffle

The SZ-qshuffle product on words in the letters `p` (`true`) and `y` (`false`):
`𝟏 ш w = w ш 𝟏 = w`, `y u ш v = y (u ш v)`, `p u ш y v = y (p u ш v)`,
`p u ш p v = p (u ш p v) + p (p u ш v) + p (u ш v)`.
