# Sz QMZV mul eq sum sz Shuffle

`ζ_q^SZ` is an algebra homomorphism on `(𝔎¹, ш_SZ)`: for SZ-admissible indices
`u, v`, `ζ_q^SZ(u) ζ_q^SZ(v) = ζ_q^SZ(u ш_SZ v)`.
