# Sz QMZV mul eq sum stuffle

`ζ_q^SZ` is an algebra homomorphism on `(𝔎¹, ∗_SZ)`: for SZ-admissible `v, w`,
`ζ_q^SZ(v) ζ_q^SZ(w) = ζ_q^SZ(v ∗_SZ w)`, with the stuffle `∗_SZ` on indices.
