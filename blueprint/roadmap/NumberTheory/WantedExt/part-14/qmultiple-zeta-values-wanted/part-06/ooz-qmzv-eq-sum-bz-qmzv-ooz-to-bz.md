# Ooz QMZV eq sum bz QMZV ooz To BZ

Restricted OOZ-qMZVs in the BZ model: for an admissible index `k` (`kᵢ ≥ 1`, `k₁ ≥ 2`),
`ζ_q^OBZ(k) = ζ_q^BZ(U(k))`, with `ζ_q^BZ` extended `ℚ`-linearly.
