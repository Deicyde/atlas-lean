# Ooz QMZV eq sum sz QMZV ooz To SZ

OOZ-qMZVs in the SZ model: for an SZ-admissible index `k` (empty or `k₁ ≥ 1`),
`ζ_q^OSZ(k) = ζ_q^SZ(V(k))`, with `ζ_q^SZ` extended `ℚ`-linearly.
