# Weighted Quasi Shuffle

The quasi-shuffle product with weighted letter product `d : α → α → (α →₀ ℚ)` on formal
`ℚ`-linear combinations of words: `𝟏 ∗ w = w ∗ 𝟏 = w` and
`a u ∗ b v = a (u ∗ b v) + b (a u ∗ v) + ∑_c d(a, b)_c · c (u ∗ v)`.
