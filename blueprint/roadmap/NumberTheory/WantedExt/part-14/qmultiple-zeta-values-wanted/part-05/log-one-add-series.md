# The logarithm series $\ln(1 + T)$

**The logarithm series $\ln(1 + T)$.** The formal power series $\ln(1 + T)$ over
$\mathbb{Q}$ is $\sum_{n \ge 1} (-1)^{n+1} T^n / n$; its constant term is $0$.
