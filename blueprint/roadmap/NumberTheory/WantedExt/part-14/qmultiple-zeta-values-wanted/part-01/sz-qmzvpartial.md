# Sz QMZVPartial

Partial sums of SZ-qMZVs: for an index `(k₁, …, kᵣ)` and a bound `M`,
`∑_{M > m₁ > ⋯ > mᵣ > 0} ∏ⱼ q^{mⱼ kⱼ} / (1 - q^{mⱼ})^{kⱼ}`.
