# Ooz QMZVPartial

Partial OOZ sums without the leading `q^{m₁}`:
`∑_{M > m₁ > ⋯ > mᵣ > 0} ∏ⱼ 1 / (1 - q^{mⱼ})^{kⱼ}`.
