# Q MZVPartial

Partial sums of general qMZVs: for data `[(k₁, Q₁), …, (kᵣ, Qᵣ)]` and a bound
`M`, `∑_{M > m₁ > ⋯ > mᵣ > 0} ∏ⱼ Qⱼ(q^{mⱼ}) / (1 - q^{mⱼ})^{kⱼ}`.
