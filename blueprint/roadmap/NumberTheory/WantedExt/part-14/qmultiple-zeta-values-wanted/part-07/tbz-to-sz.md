# Tbz To SZ

The map `U_TBZ : 𝔥^TBZ → 𝔎¹` on a word (letters `none = 1̄`, `some k`): each `1̄` becomes the SZ
letter `1`, each `k` becomes `k` or `k - 1` (sum over both choices), as a formal `ℚ`-combination of
SZ indices.
