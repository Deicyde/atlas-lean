# Sz Star QMZVPartial

Partial sums of SZ-star qMZVs: for an index `(k₁, …, kᵣ)` and a bound `M`,
`∑_{M ≥ m₁ ≥ ⋯ ≥ mᵣ > 0} ∏ⱼ q^{mⱼ kⱼ} / (1 - q^{mⱼ})^{kⱼ}`.
