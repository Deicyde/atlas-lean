# Q mul ramanujan F one three five fifteen

For `‖q‖ < 1`, with Ramanujan's `f(q) = (-q;-q)_∞`,
`q f(q)f(q³)f(q⁵)f(q¹⁵) = q f₁f₃f₅f₁₅ + 2q² f₂f₆f₁₀f₃₀ + 4q⁴ f₄f₁₂f₂₀f₆₀`.
