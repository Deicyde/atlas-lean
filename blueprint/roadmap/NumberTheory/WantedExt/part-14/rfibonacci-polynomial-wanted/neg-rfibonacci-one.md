# The first (-r)-Fibonacci polynomial

**The first (-r)-Fibonacci polynomial.** For every commutative ring $A$, natural number
$r$, $x \in A$ and unit $y$ of $A$, the $(-r)$-Fibonacci polynomial satisfies
$U^{(-r)}_1(x, y) = 1$.
