# Neg RLucas eq

Proposition 1: for `r ≥ 2` and `n ≥ r`,
`V^{(-r)}_n = r U^{(-r)}_{n+1} - x y^{-1} U^{(-r)}_{n-r}`. The paper takes `x` a unit;
the identity holds for every `x`.
