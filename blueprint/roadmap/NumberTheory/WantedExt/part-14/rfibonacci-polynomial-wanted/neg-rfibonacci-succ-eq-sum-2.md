# Neg RFibonacci succ eq sum'

Theorem 6, second form: for `r ≥ 2`,
`U^{(-r)}_{n+1} = ∑ C((n-k)/r, k) y^{-(n-k)/r} (-x)^k` over `0 ≤ k ≤ ⌊n/r⌋` with
`r ∣ n - k`.
