# Prime pair pow sub two pow odd mul pow eq sq iff

If `p` and `2p - 1` are primes with `p ≡ 3 (mod 8)` and `k ≥ 0`, then
`pˣ - (2^{2k+1}(2p - 1))ʸ = z²` has the unique non-negative solution `(x, y, z) = (0, 0, 0)`.
