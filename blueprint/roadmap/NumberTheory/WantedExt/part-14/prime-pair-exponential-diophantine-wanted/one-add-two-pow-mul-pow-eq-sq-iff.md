# One add two pow mul pow eq sq iff

If `p` and `2p - 1` are odd primes and `k ≥ 1`, then `1 + (2ᵏ(2p - 1))ʸ = z²` has the unique
non-negative solution `(k, p, y, z) = (4, 3, 1, 9)`.
