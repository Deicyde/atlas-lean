# Prime pair pow add two pow odd mul pow ne sq

If `p` and `2p - 1` are primes with `p ≡ 5, 7 (mod 8)`, then for every `k ≥ 0`,
`pˣ + (2^{2k+1}(2p - 1))ʸ = z²` has no non-negative solutions.
