# Prime pair two pow even mul pow sub pow eq sq iff

If `p` and `2p - 1` are primes with `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` and `k ≥ 1`, then
`(2^{2k}(2p - 1))ˣ - pʸ = z²` has the unique non-negative solution `(x, y, z) = (0, 0, 0)`.
