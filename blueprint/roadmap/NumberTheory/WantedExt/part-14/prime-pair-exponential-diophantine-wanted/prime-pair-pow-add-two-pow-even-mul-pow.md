# Prime pair pow add two pow even mul pow eq sq iff

If `p` and `2p - 1` are primes with `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` and `k ≥ 1`, then the non-negative
solutions of `pˣ + (2^{2k}(2p - 1))ʸ = z²` are `(p, x, y, z) = (3, 1, 0, 2)` (any `k`) and
`(p, k, x, y, z) = (3, 2, 0, 1, 9)`.
