# Zero is a fixed point of the q-analog 3x+1 map

**Zero is a fixed point of the q-analog 3x+1 map.** For all power series
$A, B \in \mathbb{F}_2[[q]]$, the $q$-analog $3x+1$ map satisfies $T_{A,B}(0) = 0$.
