# Q Collatz conjugate two Adic Collatz

For odd `A, B ∈ 𝔽₂[[q]]`, `T_{A,B}` is conjugate to the `2`-adic `3x+1` map `T`: there is a
bijection `h : ℤ₂ → 𝔽₂[[q]]` with `h⁻¹ ∘ T_{A,B} ∘ h = T`.
