# Q Collatz iterate parity flip

For odd `A, B`, `n ∈ ℕ`, and `U, V ∈ 𝔽₂[[q]]` with `V` odd, `T_{A,B}^n(U + qⁿ V)` and
`T_{A,B}^n(U)` have opposite parity.
