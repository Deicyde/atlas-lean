# The q-analog 3x+1 map on multiples of $q$

**The q-analog 3x+1 map on multiples of $q$.** For all power series
$A, B, y \in \mathbb{F}_2[[q]]$, the $q$-analog $3x+1$ map satisfies $T_{A,B}(qy) = y$.
