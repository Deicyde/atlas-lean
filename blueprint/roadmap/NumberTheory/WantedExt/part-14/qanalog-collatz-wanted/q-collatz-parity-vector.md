# Q Collatz Parity Vector

The parity vector `PV_{A,B}(x) = ∑ₖ xₖ 2ᵏ ∈ ℤ₂`, where `xₖ ∈ {0, 1}` is the parity (constant
coefficient) of `T_{A,B}^k(x)`.
