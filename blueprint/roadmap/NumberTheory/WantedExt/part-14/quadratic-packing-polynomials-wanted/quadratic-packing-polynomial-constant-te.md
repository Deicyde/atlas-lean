# Quadratic Packing Polynomial constant term

Lemma 20: if `P` is a QPP on `I(n/m)` with step difference `k > 0`, then its constant term
is `F = P(0, 0) = (l²/n)(k - 1)(k + 1)/12`, `l = gcd(m - 1, n)`.
