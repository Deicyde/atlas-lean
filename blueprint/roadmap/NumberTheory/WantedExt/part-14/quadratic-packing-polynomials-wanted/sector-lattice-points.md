# Sector Lattice Points

The integer lattice points `I(α) = S(α) ∩ ℤ²` of the sector
`S(α) = {(x, y) ∈ ℝ² : 0 ≤ y ≤ α x}`, viewed as a subset of `ℝ × ℝ`.
