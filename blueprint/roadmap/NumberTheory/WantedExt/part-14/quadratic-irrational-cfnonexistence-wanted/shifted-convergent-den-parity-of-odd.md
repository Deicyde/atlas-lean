# Shifted convergent Den parity of odd

Corollary 2 (ii): for odd `l ≥ 5` and a symmetric sequence `a₁, …, a_{l-1}`,
`r_{l-2} ≡ r_{(l-1)/2} + r_{(l-3)/2} (mod 2)` with `r_j = K(a₂, …, a_j)`. The case `l = 3`,
which needs `r_0 = 0`, is omitted.
