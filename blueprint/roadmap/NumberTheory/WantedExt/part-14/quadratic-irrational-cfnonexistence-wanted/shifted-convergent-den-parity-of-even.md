# Shifted convergent Den parity of even

Corollary 1 (ii): for even `l ≥ 4` and a symmetric sequence `a₁, …, a_{l-1}`,
`r_{l-2} ≡ a_{l/2} r_{l/2-1} (mod 2)` with `r_j = K(a₂, …, a_j)`. The case `l = 2`,
which needs `r_0 = 0`, is omitted.
