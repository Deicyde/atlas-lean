# Convergent Den parity of odd

Corollary 2 (i): for odd `l ≥ 3` and a symmetric sequence `a₁, …, a_{l-1}`,
`q_{l-1} ≡ q_{(l-1)/2} + q_{(l-3)/2} (mod 2)`.
