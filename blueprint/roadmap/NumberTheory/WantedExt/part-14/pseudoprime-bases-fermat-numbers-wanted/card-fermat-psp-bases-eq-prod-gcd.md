# Monier; Baillie–Wagstaff

**Monier; Baillie–Wagstaff.** For a composite `n > 1`, the number of pseudoprime bases
`1 ≤ b < n` is `∏_{p ∣ n} gcd(n - 1, p - 1)` over the distinct prime divisors `p` of `n`.
