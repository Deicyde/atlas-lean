# Exists generalized Tribonacci basis

There are Tribonacci basis sequences `u, v, w` with initial conditions
`(1, 0, 0)`, `(0, 1, 0)`, `(0, 0, 1)` such that `Q_n = u_n * a + v_n * b +
w_n * c` for every generalized Tribonacci sequence.
