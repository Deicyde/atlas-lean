# Prod zpow le lcm zpow mul prod gcd

Cohen's inequality: for positive integers `d_1, …, d_k` and every `t ∈ ℤ`,
`[d_1, …, d_k]^{t(t+1)/2} ∏_{i<j} (d_i, d_j) ≥ ∏_i d_i^t` (in `ℚ`; `t(t+1)/2` is an integer).
