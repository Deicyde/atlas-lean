# Exists lower bound conjecture constant

If `k` bounds `D_n(n^θ, n^{θ-ε})` for every `n ≥ 1` (Conjecture 2), then
`k ≫ √ε (θ(1 - θ))^{3/2} (θ^θ (1 - θ)^{1-θ})^{-1/ε}`, with an absolute implied constant.
