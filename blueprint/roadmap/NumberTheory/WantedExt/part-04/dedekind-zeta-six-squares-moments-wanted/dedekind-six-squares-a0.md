# Dedekind Six Squares A0

The exponent `a_{0,ℓ} = 1 + ∑_{i=1}^{⌊ℓ/2⌋} C(ℓ, 2i) (2i)!/(i!(i+1)!)`, the multiplicity of
the constant term in `a_K(p)^ℓ`; `(2i)!/(i!(i+1)!)` is the Catalan number `catalan i`.
