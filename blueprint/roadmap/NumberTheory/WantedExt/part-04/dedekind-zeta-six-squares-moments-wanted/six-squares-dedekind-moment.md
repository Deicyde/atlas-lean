# Six Squares Dedekind Moment

The moment `S_ℓ(x) = ∑_{1 ≤ n ≤ x} r₆(n) a_K(n)^ℓ`, with `r₆(n)` the number of
representations of `n` as a sum of six squares of integers (`sumOfSquaresRepCount 6 n`).
