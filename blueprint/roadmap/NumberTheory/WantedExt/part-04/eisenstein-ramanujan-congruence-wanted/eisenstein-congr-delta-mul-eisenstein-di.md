# Corollary 5

**Corollary 5.** For `k ∈ {12, 16, 18, 20, 22, 26}`, `G_k ≡ Δ E_{k-12} (mod N'_k)`
(coefficientwise, all `n ≥ 0`) and `σ_{k-1}(n) ≡ (τ * σ_{k-13})(n) / σ_{k-13}(0) (mod N'_k)`
for `n ≥ 1` (notation of Theorem 1).
