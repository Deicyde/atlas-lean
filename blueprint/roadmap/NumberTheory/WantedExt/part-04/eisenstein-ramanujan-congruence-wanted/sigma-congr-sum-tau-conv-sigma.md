# Corollary 3

**Corollary 3.** With the notation of Theorem 1, for all `n ≥ 1`,
`σ_{k-1}(n) ≡ ∑_{i=1}^m (γ_i / σ_{k-12i-1}(0)) (τ^{*i} * σ_{k-12i-1})(n) (mod N'_k)`.
