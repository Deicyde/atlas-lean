# Eq fib two pow of ratio le of has Sum millin

A sequence of positive integers with `a_n² / a_(n+1) ≤ 2/3` and
`∑ 1 / a_n = (5 - √5) / 2` is `F_2, F_4, F_8, …` (the Millin series).
