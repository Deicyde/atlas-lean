# Convergence of the pseudo-greedy expansion

**Convergence of the pseudo-greedy expansion.** For every real number $r > 0$, the
pseudo-greedy expansion $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of $r$ satisfies $\sum_n 1 / a_n = r$.
