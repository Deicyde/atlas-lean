# Eq sylvester of ratio mem of has Sum one

A sequence of positive integers with `2/3 ≤ a_n² / a_(n+1) ≤ 4/3` and `∑ 1 / a_n = 1` is
the Sylvester sequence `2, 3, 7, 43, …`.
