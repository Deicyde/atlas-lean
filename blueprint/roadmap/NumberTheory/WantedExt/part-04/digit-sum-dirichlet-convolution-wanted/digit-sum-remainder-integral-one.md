# The remainder integral vanishes at $1$

**The remainder integral vanishes at $1$.** For every natural number $q$, let $J$ be the
remainder of the Trollope–Delange formula in base $q$ and $K(x) = \int_1^x J(u) \, du / u^2$.
Then $K(1) = 0$.
