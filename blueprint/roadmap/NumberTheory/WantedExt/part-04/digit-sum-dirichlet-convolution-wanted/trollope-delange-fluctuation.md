# Trollope Delange Fluctuation

The Trollope-Delange fluctuation
`F(x) = (q - 1)/2 · (1 + ⌊x⌋ - x) + q^{1 + ⌊x⌋ - x} h(q^{x - ⌊x⌋ - 1})`.
