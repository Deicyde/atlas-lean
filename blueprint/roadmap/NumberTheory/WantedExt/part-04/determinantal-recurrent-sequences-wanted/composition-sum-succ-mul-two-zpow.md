# Composition sum succ mul two zpow

For `n ≥ 1`, `∑ m_n(α) 1^{α_1} 3^{α_2} ⋯ ((n+1) 2^{n-2})^{α_n}` equals
`5^{(n-1)/2} F_{n+1}` for odd `n` and `5^{(n-2)/2} L_{n+1}` for even `n`.
