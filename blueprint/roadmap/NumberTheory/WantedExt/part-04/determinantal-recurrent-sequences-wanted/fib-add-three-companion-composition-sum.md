# Fib add three companion composition sum

The companion of identity (13) (minus sign for `lam` in (30) with `m = 3`): for `n ≥ 1`,
`(5/9) F_{n+3} = (-1/3)^n ∑_α m_n(α) ∏_i (5i - 10)^{α_i}`.
