# Horadam composition sum recurrence

Converse part of Theorem 1: if `(q - r)^2 = 4s(p - 1) ≠ 0`, the sequence `w_n` defined for
`n ≥ 1` by the composition sum of identity (12) satisfies `w_n = r w_{n-1} + s w_{n-2}` for
`n ≥ 3`.
