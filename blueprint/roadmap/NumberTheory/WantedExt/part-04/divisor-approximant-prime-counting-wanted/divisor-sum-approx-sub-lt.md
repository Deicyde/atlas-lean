# Divisor Sum Approx sub lt

Theorem 3: with `A` as in Lemma 1, `σ̃(n) = ∑_{k=1}^{n} k exp(-n k A(k, n) sin²(nπ/k))`
satisfies `σ̃(n) - σ(n) < n/(eⁿ - 1)` for all `n ≥ 1`.
