# Prime Counting Approx sub lt

Lemma 3: `π₀(n) = ∑_{m=2}^{n} exp(-m (τ(m) - 2))` satisfies
`π₀(n) - π(n) < 1/(e³ (e - 1))` for all `n ≥ 2`.
