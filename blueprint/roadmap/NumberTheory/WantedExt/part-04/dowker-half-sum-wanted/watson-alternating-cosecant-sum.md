# Watson's alternating cosecant sum

**Watson's alternating cosecant sum.** For a natural number $n$, the alternating cosecant sum is
the real number

$$
C_n = \sum_{l=1}^{n-1} \frac{(-1)^{l+1}}{\sin(\pi l / n)}.
$$

For $n \le 1$ the sum is empty and $C_n = 0$.
