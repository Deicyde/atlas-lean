# Watson's alternating cosecant sum vanishes for odd $n$

**Watson's alternating cosecant sum vanishes for odd $n$.** For every odd natural number $n$,

$$
\sum_{l=1}^{n-1} \frac{(-1)^{l+1}}{\sin(\pi l / n)} = 0.
$$
