# Watson's cosine sum at $n = 2$, $\nu = 1$

**Watson's cosine sum at $n = 2$, $\nu = 1$.** The cosine sum
$C_n(\nu) = \sum_{l=1}^{n-1} \cos(2 \pi \nu l / n) / \sin(\pi l / n)$ satisfies $C_2(1) = -1$.
