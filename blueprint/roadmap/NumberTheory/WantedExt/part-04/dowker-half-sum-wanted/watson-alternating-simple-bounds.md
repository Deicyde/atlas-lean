# Watson alternating simple bounds

Corollary 3 replaces the correction in Theorem 6 by decimal bounds: for every
even `n ≥ 4`, `C_n` lies strictly between `2n log 2 / π + 0.252/n` and
`2n log 2 / π + 0.262/n`.
