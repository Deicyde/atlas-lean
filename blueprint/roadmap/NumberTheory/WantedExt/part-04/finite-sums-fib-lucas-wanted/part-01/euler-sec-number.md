# Euler Sec Number

The paper's Euler (secant) numbers `Eₙ` (exponential
generating function `2/(eᵗ + e⁻ᵗ)`): `0` at odd indices and
MathlibExt's `chapter5EulerEven` at even indices
(`E₀ = 1`, `E₂ = -1`, `E₄ = 5`).
