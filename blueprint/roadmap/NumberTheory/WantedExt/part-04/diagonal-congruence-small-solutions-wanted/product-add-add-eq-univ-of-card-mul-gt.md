# Product add add eq univ of card mul gt

Sumset-product covering in `ℤ/pℤ`: if `A₁, A₂, A₃, D ⊆ ℤ/pℤ` satisfy
`|A₁| |A₂| |A₃| |D| > p³`, then `A₁ D + A₂ + A₃ = ℤ/pℤ`.
