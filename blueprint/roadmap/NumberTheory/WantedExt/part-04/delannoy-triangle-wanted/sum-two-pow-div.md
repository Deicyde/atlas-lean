# Lemma 6

**Lemma 6**: for a prime `p > 3`,
`∑_{k=1}^{p-1} 2^k / k ≡ -2 q_p(2) (mod p^2)` and
`∑_{k=1}^{p-1} 2^k / k^2 ≡ -q_p(2)^2 (mod p)`.
