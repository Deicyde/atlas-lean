# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4**: for a prime `p ≥ 5` and a positive
integer `n`,
`C(np-1, p-1)_{[2]}
  ≡ 1 + (n-1) p q_p(2) (2 - p q_p(2)) (mod p^3)`.
