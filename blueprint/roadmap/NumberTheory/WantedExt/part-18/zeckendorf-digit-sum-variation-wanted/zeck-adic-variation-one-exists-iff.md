# Zeck Adic Variation one exists iff

`Δ^{(1)}(x) = lim_k Δ_k^{(1)}(x)` exists for `x ∈ 𝕏` iff `x ∈ C_{00(10)^d} ∪ C_{001(01)^d}`
for some `d ≥ 0`.
