# Zeck NIZ fib variation order succ

For `r = F_ℓ`, `ℓ ≥ 3`: among the levels (cylinders of order `ℓ + 1`, represented by
`v < F_{ℓ+3}`) in `NIZ_{ℓ+1}`, exactly `F_{ℓ-1}` carry `Δ^{(F_ℓ)} = 0` and exactly `F_{ℓ-2}`
carry `Δ^{(F_ℓ)} = 1`.
