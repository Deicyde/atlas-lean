# Zeck NIZ cylinder shift

For `r ≥ 1`, `k ≥ 2` and an admissible word `n_{k-1} ⋯ n_2` (the digits of `y ∈ 𝕏`):
`C_{00 n_{k-1} ⋯ n_2} ⊆ NIZ_k` implies `C_{0010 n_{k-1} ⋯ n_2} ⊆ NIZ_{k+2}`.
