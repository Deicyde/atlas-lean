# Zeck NIZ variation shift

For `r ≥ 1`, `ℓ ≥ 2` with `F_ℓ ≤ r < F_{ℓ+1}`, `k ≥ ℓ`, and a cylinder
`C_{00 n_{k-1} ⋯ n_2} ⊆ NIZ_k` on which `Δ^{(r)} = d`: `Δ^{(r)} = d - 1` on
`C_{0010 n_{k-1} ⋯ n_2}`.
