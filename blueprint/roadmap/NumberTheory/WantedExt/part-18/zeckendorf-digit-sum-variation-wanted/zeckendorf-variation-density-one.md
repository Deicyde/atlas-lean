# Zeckendorf variation density one

The distribution of `Δ^{(1)}`: `μ^{(1)}(d) = 0` for `d ≥ 2`, `μ^{(1)}(1) = 1/φ²`, and
`μ^{(1)}(d) = 1/φ^{2 - 2d}` for `d ≤ 0`.
