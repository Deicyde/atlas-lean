# Zeck NIZ leading zeros

For `r ≥ 1` and `k ≥ 2`, the cylinders of order `k` in `NIZ_k` have names starting with
`00`: `x_{k+1} = x_k = 0` on `𝕏 ∩ NIZ_k`.
