# Zeck Odometer invariant measure phi mixing

`φ`-mixing of the digits under `ℙ`: for the coordinate process `X_j = x_{j+1}` (`j ≥ 1`),
every term defining `φ(k)` is at most `2/φ^{2k}`, i.e. `φ(k) ≤ 2/φ^{2k}` for `k ≥ 1`.
