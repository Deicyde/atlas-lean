# Zeck Odometer uniquely ergodic

`(𝕏, T)` is uniquely ergodic: there is exactly one `T`-invariant Borel probability measure
carried by `𝕏` (measures on `𝕏` are taken as measures on `ℕ → Bool` with `μ 𝕏ᶜ = 0`).
