# Zeck CST

`CST_k`: the union of the levels of the large and small towers of order `k`, except the `r`
highest levels of each tower, i.e. `v_k + r < F_{k+1}` or `F_{k+1} ≤ v_k` and
`v_k + r < F_{k+2}`.
