# Compositions of $w$ into $r$ parts

**Compositions of $w$ into $r$ parts.** For natural numbers $w$ and $r$, $\mathfrak{S}_w$
is the set of $r$-tuples $(s_1, \dots, s_r)$ of positive integers with $s_1 + \dots + s_r = w$.
