# Extended Cunningham Chain

The extended Cunningham chain generated by `p`: `e₀ = p` and `eₖ = 2 eₖ₋₁ + 1`, i.e. the
numbers obtained from `p` by repeatedly appending the binary digit `1` on the right. The paper
requires the initial value to be prime; the definition accepts any natural number and the
theorems below add the primality hypothesis.
