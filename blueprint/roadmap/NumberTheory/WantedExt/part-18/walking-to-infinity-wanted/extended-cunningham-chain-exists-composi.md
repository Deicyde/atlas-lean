# Extended Cunningham Chain exists composite run

Every extended Cunningham chain generated by a prime contains arbitrarily long runs of
consecutive composite terms: for every `n ≥ 1` there is `i ≥ 1` with `eᵢ, …, eᵢ₊ₙ₋₁` all
composite. Since `eᵢ ≥ 2p + 1 ≥ 5` for `i ≥ 1`, compositeness is rendered as `¬ Prime`.
