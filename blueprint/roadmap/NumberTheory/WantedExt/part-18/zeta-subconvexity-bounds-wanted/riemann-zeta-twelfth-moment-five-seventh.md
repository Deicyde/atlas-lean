# Riemann Zeta twelfth moment five sevenths le

Twelfth moment of `ζ` on the line `σ = 5/7`: for every `ε > 0`,
`∫_0^T |ζ(5/7 + i t)|^{12} dt ≪_ε T^{1 + ε}` uniformly for `T ≥ 1`.
