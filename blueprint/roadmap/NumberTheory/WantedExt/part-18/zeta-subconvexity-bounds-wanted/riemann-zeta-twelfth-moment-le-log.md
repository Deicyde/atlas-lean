# Riemann Zeta twelfth moment le log

Heath-Brown's twelfth-moment bound on the critical line with the logarithmic factor:
`∫_1^T |ζ(1/2 + i t)|^{12} dt ≪ T^2 (log T)^{17}` for all sufficiently large `T`.
