# Riemann Zeta twelfth moment le

Twelfth moment of `ζ` on the critical line (Heath-Brown's bound, as quoted): for every
`ε > 0`, `∫_1^T |ζ(1/2 + i t)|^{12} dt ≪_ε T^{2 + ε}` uniformly for `T ≥ 1`.
