# A generalized Wilson criterion for primality

**A generalized Wilson criterion for primality.** Let $n \ge 1$ and $k < n$ be natural
numbers. Then $(-1)^k \, k! \, (n - k - 1)! \equiv -1 \pmod{n}$ if and only if $n = 1$ or $n$
is prime.
