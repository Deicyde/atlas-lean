# Morozov's Wilson quotient sum

**Morozov's Wilson quotient sum.** For a natural number $n$, $Z(n)$ is the rational number
$Z(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} M_k(n)$, where $M_k(n)$ are Morozov's Wilson quotients.
