# Square Split Conv

The square-splitting `A`-convolution of Narkiewicz type,
`(f ⋆ g)(n) = ∑_{d ∈ A(n)} f(d) g(n/d)` for `n ≥ 1` (and `0` at `0`, as for
every `ArithmeticFunction`).
