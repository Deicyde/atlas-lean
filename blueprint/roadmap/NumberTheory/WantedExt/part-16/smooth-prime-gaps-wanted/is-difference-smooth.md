# Difference-smooth sets of integers

**Difference-smooth sets of integers.** Let $y$ be a natural number. A finite set $H$ of
integers is difference $y$-smooth if for all distinct $h, h' \in H$ every prime factor of
$h - h'$ is at most $y$.
