# Not is Difference Smooth of is Admissible Finset

An admissible `k`-tuple, `k ≥ 2`, is not difference `ℓ`-smooth for any
`ℓ < z_k`, `z_k` the largest prime `≤ k`; equivalently, for every prime
`z ≤ k` and every `ℓ < z`.
