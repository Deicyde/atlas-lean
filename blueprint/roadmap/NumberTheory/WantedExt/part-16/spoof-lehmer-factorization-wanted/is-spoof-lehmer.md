# Is Spoof Lehmer

`F` is a (spoof) `k`-Lehmer factorization: `F` is a spoof factorization (positive bases and
exponents), `k ⋅ φ̃(F) = ẽ(F) - 1`, and `F` has at least two factors or a factor with exponent
greater than `1`.
