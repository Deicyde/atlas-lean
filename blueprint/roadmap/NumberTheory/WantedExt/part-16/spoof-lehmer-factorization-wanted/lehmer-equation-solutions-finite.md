# Lehmer Equation solutions finite

For every `r ≥ 2`, Equation (1), `k ∏ (x_j - 1) = ∏ x_j - 1` with `k ∈ ℤ_{>0}`, has only
finitely many solutions with `x_1, …, x_r ≥ 2`. Omitted: the meta-claim that the solutions can
be explicitly computed.
