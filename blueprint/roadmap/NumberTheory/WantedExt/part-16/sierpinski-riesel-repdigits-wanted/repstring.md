# Repstring

The `b`-repstring `k_b^{(z;t)} = k (b^{(z+ℓ)t} - 1)/(b^{z+ℓ} - 1)`, `ℓ` the number of base-`b`
digits of `k`; `repstring b k 0 t` is the repinteger `k_b^{(t)}`.
