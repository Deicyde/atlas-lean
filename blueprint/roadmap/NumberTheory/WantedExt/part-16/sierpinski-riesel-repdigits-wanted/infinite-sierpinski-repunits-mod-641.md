# Corollary 3.12

**Corollary 3.12.** If `b > 2`, `b ≡ 𝔟 (mod 641)` for some `𝔟 ∈ {1, 147, 265, 378}`, and
`b ≢ 1 (mod 5)`, there are infinitely many `b`-repunit Sierpiński numbers.
