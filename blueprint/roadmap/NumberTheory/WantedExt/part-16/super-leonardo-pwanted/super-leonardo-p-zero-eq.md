# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4**: the homogeneous case `k = 0`: `𝓛_{p,0,x}(n) = x_n` for `n ≤ p` and
`F_p(n-p+1) x_p + ∑_{i=0}^{min(p-1, n-p-1)} F_p(n-p-i) x_i` for `n > p`.
