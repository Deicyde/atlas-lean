# Świerczkowski's lower bound $C_{1b} \ge (4 - \sqrt{6})/5$

**Świerczkowski's lower bound $C_{1b} \ge (4 - \sqrt{6})/5$.** The continuous Erdős
minimum-overlap constant $C_{1b}$ satisfies $(4 - \sqrt{6})/5 \le C_{1b}$.
