# Moser's lower bound $C_{1b} \ge \sqrt{4 - \sqrt{15}}$

**Moser's lower bound $C_{1b} \ge \sqrt{4 - \sqrt{15}}$.** The continuous Erdős minimum-overlap
constant $C_{1b}$ satisfies $\sqrt{4 - \sqrt{15}} \le C_{1b}$.
