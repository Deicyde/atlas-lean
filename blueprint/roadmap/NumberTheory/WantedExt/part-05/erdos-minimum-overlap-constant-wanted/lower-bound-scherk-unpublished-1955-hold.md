# Scherk's lower bound for the minimum-overlap constant

**Scherk's lower bound for the minimum-overlap constant.** The continuous Erdős
minimum-overlap constant $C_{1b}$ satisfies $1 - 1/\sqrt{2} \le C_{1b}$.
