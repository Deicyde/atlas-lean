# E

Definition of `Eₙ(H)` as the number of monic integer polynomials `f(x) = xⁿ + a₁ xⁿ⁻¹ + … aₙ` of
degree `n` with `|aᵢ| ≤ H` for all `i` such that the Galois group of `f` is not `Sₙ`.
