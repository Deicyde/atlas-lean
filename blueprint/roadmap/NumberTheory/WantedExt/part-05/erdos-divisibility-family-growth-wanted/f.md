# Maximum size of a good subset

**Maximum size of a good subset.** For a natural number $n$, $F(n)$ is the largest size of a
good subset of $\{1, \dots, n\}$, defined as the supremum of the sizes of the good sets
$A \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$.
