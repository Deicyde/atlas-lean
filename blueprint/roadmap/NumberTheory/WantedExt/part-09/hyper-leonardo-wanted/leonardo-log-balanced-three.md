# Leonardo log Balanced three

Proposition 11: the Leonardo numbers are log-balanced at `n = 3`:
`Le_2 Le_4 ≥ Le_3²` and `(Le_3/3!)² ≥ (Le_2/2!)(Le_4/4!)`.
