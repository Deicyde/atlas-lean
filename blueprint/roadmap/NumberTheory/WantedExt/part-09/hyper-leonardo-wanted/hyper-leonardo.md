# Hyper Leonardo

The hyper-Leonardo numbers `Le^{(r)}_n`: `Le^{(0)}_n` is the Leonardo number
`genLeonardo 1 n`, and `Le^{(r+1)}_n = ∑_{k=0}^{n} Le^{(r)}_k`.
