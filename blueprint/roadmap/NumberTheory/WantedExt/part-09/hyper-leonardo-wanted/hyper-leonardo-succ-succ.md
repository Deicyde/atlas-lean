# Hyper Leonardo succ succ

Proposition 3: `Le^{(r)}_n = Le^{(r)}_{n-1} + Le^{(r-1)}_n` for `r ≥ 1` and `n ≥ 1`
(the cases `r = 0` or `n = 0` refer to undefined terms and are omitted).
