# Hyper Leonardo convex

Theorem 3: `Le^{(r)}` is convex: `Le^{(r)}_{n-1} + Le^{(r)}_{n+1} ≥ 2 Le^{(r)}_n` for
`r ≥ 0` and `n ≥ 1`.
