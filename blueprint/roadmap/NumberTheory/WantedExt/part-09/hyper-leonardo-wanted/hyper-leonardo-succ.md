# Hyper-Leonardo numbers as partial sums

**Hyper-Leonardo numbers as partial sums.** For all natural numbers $r$ and $n$, the
hyper-Leonardo numbers $Le^{(r)}_n$ satisfy $Le^{(r+1)}_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} Le^{(r)}_k$.
