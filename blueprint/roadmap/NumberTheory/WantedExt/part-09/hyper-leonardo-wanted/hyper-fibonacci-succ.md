# Hyper-Fibonacci numbers as partial sums

**Hyper-Fibonacci numbers as partial sums.** For all natural numbers $r$ and $n$, the
hyper-Fibonacci numbers $F^{(r)}_n$ satisfy $F^{(r+1)}_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} F^{(r)}_k$.
