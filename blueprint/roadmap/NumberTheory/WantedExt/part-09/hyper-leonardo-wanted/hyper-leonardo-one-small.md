# First hyper-Leonardo numbers of order one

**First hyper-Leonardo numbers of order one.** The hyper-Leonardo numbers $Le^{(1)}_n$ of
order $1$ satisfy $Le^{(1)}_0 = 1$, $Le^{(1)}_1 = 2$, $Le^{(1)}_2 = 5$ and $Le^{(1)}_3 = 10$.
