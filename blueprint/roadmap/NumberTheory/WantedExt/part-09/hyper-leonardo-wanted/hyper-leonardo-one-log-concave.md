# Hyper Leonardo one log Concave

Proposition 13: `Le^{(1)}` is log-concave from `n = 2` on:
`Le^{(1)}_{n-1} Le^{(1)}_{n+1} ≤ (Le^{(1)}_n)²` for `n ≥ 2`.
