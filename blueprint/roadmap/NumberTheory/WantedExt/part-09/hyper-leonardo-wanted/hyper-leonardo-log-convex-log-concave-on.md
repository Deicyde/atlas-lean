# Hyper Leonardo log Convex log Concave one

Proposition 12: at `n = 1`, `Le^{(1)}` satisfies the log-convexity inequality
`Le^{(1)}_0 Le^{(1)}_2 ≥ (Le^{(1)}_1)²`, and `Le^{(r)}` for `r ≥ 2` the log-concavity
inequality `(Le^{(r)}_1)² ≥ Le^{(r)}_0 Le^{(r)}_2`.
