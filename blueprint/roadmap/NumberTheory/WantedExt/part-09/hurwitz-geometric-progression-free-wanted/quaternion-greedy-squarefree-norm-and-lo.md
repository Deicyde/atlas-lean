# Quaternion Greedy squarefree norm and lower Density

The quaternion greedy set contains every Hurwitz quaternion of squarefree norm, and its
lower density is at least `60 / (7 π² ζ(3))`, with `ζ(3) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} n⁻³`.
