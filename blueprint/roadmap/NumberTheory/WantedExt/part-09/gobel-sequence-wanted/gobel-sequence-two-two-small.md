# Göbel's original sequence

**Göbel's original sequence.** The $(2, 2)$-Göbel sequence $g_{2,2}$ satisfies
$g_{2,2}(2) = 3$, $g_{2,2}(3) = 5$, $g_{2,2}(4) = 10$ and $g_{2,2}(5) = 28$.
