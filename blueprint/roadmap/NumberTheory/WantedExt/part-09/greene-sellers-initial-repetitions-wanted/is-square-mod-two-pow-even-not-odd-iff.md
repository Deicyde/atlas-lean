# Is Square mod two pow even not odd iff

For `k ≥ 1`, `2^(2k) + 2^(2k-2)` is the unique residue modulo `2^(2k+1)` that is a square
modulo `2^(2k)` but not a square modulo `2^(2k+1)`. Stated for all `k`; at `k = 0` both sides are
false.
