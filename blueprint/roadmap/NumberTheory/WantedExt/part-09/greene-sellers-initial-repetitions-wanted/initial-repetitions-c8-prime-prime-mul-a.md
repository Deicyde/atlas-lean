# Initial Repetitions C8 Prime prime mul add even

For a prime `p ≥ 7` and `1 ≤ r ≤ p - 1` such that `15r + 4` is a quadratic nonresidue
modulo `p` (not a square in `ZMod p`), `c₈'(pn + r)` is even for all `n ≥ 0`.
