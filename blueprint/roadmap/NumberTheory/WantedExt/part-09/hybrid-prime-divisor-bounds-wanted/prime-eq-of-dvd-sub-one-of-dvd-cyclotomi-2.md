# Prime eq of dvd sub one of dvd cyclotomic eval pow

If primes `ρ` and `p` satisfy `ρ ∣ y - 1` and `ρ ∣ Φ_p(y^{p^{a-1}})` for a positive integer
`y` and some `a ≥ 1`, then `ρ = p`.
