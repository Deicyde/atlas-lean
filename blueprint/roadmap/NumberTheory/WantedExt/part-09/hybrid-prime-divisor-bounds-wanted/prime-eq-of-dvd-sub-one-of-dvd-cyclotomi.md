# Prime eq of dvd sub one of dvd cyclotomic eval

If primes `ρ` and `p` satisfy `ρ ∣ x - 1` and `ρ ∣ Φ_p(x)` for a positive integer `x`,
then `ρ = p`. Here `Φ_p` is Mathlib's `cyclotomic p ℤ`.
