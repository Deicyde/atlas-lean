# The delay of $1/p$ is $1$

**The delay of $1/p$ is $1$.** For every prime $p$, the delay of the base-$p$ expansion of
$1/p$ is $D_{1/p} = -v_p(1/p) = 1$, where $v_p$ is the $p$-adic valuation.
