# The prime-to-$p$ parts of $1$

**The prime-to-$p$ parts of $1$.** For every natural number $p$, the prime-to-$p$ part $x_D$
of the denominator and the prime-to-$p$ part $x_N$ of the numerator of $x = 1$ are both $1$.
