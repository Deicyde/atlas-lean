# Floor Sum Correction

The periodic correction `A_{x^{-1}}(n)` of Proposition 4:
`½ (-(1 - 1/x_D) + x (1 - {x^{-1} p^n})) {x^{-1} p^n} +
∑_{k=1}^{⌊x^{-1} p^n⌋ mod x_D} ({x k} - ½ (1 - 1/x_D))`.
