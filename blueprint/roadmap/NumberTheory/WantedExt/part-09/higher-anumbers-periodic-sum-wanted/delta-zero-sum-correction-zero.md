# The correction $F_y$ vanishes at $y = 0$

**The correction $F_y$ vanishes at $y = 0$.** For all natural numbers $p$, $d$ and $e$, the
correction $F_y(e)$ of Lemma 9, taken at $y = 0$, is $0$.
