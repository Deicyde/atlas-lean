# Base-5 digits of 2/3

**Base-5 digits of 2/3.** Writing $[5^j](x)$ for the $j$-th base-$5$ digit of a rational
number $x$, we have $[5^{-1}](2/3) = 3$, $[5^{-2}](2/3) = 1$ and $[5^0](2/3) = 0$.
