# $\tau_D = 1$ when $d = 1$

**$\tau_D = 1$ when $d = 1$.** For every natural number $p$, the denominator $\tau_D$ of
$\tau = (p + 1)/d$ in lowest terms equals $1$ when $d = 1$.
