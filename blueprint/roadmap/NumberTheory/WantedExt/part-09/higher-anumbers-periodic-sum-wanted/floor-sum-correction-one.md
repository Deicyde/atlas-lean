# The correction $A_{x^{-1}}$ vanishes at $x = 1$

**The correction $A_{x^{-1}}$ vanishes at $x = 1$.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $n$, the
periodic correction $A_{x^{-1}}(n)$ of Proposition 4 satisfies $A_1(n) = 0$.
