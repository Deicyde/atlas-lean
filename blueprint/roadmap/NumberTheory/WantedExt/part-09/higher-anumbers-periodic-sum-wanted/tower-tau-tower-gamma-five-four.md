# Values of $\tau$ and $\gamma$ for $p = 5$, $d = 4$, $r = 2$

**Values of $\tau$ and $\gamma$ for $p = 5$, $d = 4$, $r = 2$.** With $\tau = (p + 1)/d$ and
$\gamma = ((p - 1)r + (p + 1))/d$, taking $p = 5$, $d = 4$ and $r = 2$ gives $\tau = 3/2$ and
$\gamma = 7/2$.
