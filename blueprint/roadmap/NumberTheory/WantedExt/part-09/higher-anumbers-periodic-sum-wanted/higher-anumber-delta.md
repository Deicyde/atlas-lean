# Higher ANumber Delta

The indicator `δ(i)`: `0` if the integer-part digits `([p^n](τ i))_{n ≥ 0}` are
lexicographically larger than the fractional digits `([p^{-1-n}](τ i))_{n ≥ 0}`, and `1`
otherwise, with `τ = (p + 1)/d`.
