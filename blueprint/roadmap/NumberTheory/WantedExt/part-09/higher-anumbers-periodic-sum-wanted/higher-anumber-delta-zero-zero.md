# The indicator $\delta_0$ vanishes at $0$

**The indicator $\delta_0$ vanishes at $0$.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $d$, the variant
$\delta_0$ of the rounding indicator for $p$ and $d$ satisfies $\delta_0(0) = 0$.
