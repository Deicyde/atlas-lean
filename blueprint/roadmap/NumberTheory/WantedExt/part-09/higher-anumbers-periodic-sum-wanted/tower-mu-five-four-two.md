# $\mu(2) = 3$ for $p = 5$ and $d = 4$

**$\mu(2) = 3$ for $p = 5$ and $d = 4$.** For $p = 5$ and $d = 4$ the lattice boundary
$\mu(2)$ equals $3$; here $\tau = (p + 1)/d = 3/2$, so $\tau \cdot 2 = 3$ is an integer and
its floor and ceiling agree.
