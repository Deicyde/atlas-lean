# Delta Zero Sum Correction average tower Tau

The coefficient of `n` in Proposition 5 vanishes for `x = τ`:
`F̄_{τ^{-1}} - τ̄^{-1}/(2p) (1 - 1/τ_D) = 0`, with `F̄_{τ^{-1}}` the average of
`F_{τ^{-1}}(e)` over `1 ≤ e ≤ L_{τ^{-1}}` (the delay `D_{τ^{-1}}` is `0`).
