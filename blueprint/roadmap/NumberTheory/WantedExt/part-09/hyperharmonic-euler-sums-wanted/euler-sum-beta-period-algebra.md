# Euler Sum Beta Period Algebra

The `ℚ`-algebra `ℚ[log 2, π, ζ(2j+1)_{j ≥ 1}, β(2j)_{j ≥ 1}] ⊆ ℂ`, where
`β(s) = ∑_{n ≥ 0} (-1)^n / (2n+1)^s` is the Dirichlet beta function.
