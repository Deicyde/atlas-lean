# Vanishing of odd multiple harmonic numbers of large depth

**Vanishing of odd multiple harmonic numbers of large depth.** For natural numbers $k$ and
$n$ with $k > n$, the depth-$k$ odd harmonic number $O_n(k)$ equals $0$.
