# Hyperharmonic Euler Sum

The generalized hyperharmonic Euler sum `S(k, r; p; a) = ∑_{n ≥ k} H_n^{[r]}(k) / (n + a)^p`,
written as a sum over `n + k`; the exponent `p` is an integer.
