# Left Factorial eq floor kurepa Partial Integral

Corollary 15: `!n = ⌊β n⌋ + 1` for every positive integer `n`, and for `n > 4`,
`!n = ⌊b n⌋ + 1 = ⌊B n m + 1/m⌋` for every positive `m`.
