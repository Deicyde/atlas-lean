# Alternating Generalized Harmonic

Alternating generalized harmonic numbers `H̄_n^{(s)} = ∑_{j=1}^n (-1)^{j-1} / j^s` for real `s`;
for `s = -p` with `p ∈ ℕ` this is `∑_{j=1}^n (-1)^{j-1} j^p`.
