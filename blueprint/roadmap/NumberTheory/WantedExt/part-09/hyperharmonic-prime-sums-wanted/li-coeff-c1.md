# Li Coeff C1

`c₁(m, j) = 1/(2(m+1)) ∑_{k=0}^{m-j} C(m+1, k) B_k 2^k C(m+1-k, j) (-1)^{m-k-j}` for `j ≤ m`
(`0` otherwise), with `B = bernoulli'`.
