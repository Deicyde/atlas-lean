# Norm incomplete Sum lt sup mul log

Corollary 1.1: for a prime `p ≥ 5`, a polynomial `f` over `ℤ` with `d_p(f) ≥ 2`, and
`1 ≤ B ≤ p`, `|S_p(f, B)| < Φ(f, p) log p`.
