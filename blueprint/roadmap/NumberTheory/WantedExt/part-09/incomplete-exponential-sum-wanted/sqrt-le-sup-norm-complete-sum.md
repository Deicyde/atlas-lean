# Sqrt le sup norm complete Sum

Lemma 2.2: for a prime `p` and a polynomial `f` over `ℤ`,
`Φ(f, p) = max_{0 ≤ y ≤ p-1} |∑_{x=1}^{p} e_p(f(x) + y x)| ≥ √p`.
