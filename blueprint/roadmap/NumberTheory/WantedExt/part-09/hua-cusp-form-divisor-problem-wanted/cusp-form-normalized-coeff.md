# Cusp Form Normalized Coeff

The normalized Fourier coefficient `λ_f(n) = a_f(n) / n^{(k-1)/2}` of a level-one cusp form
`f` of weight `k`, where `a_f(n)` is the `n`-th coefficient of the `q`-expansion
`f(z) = ∑ a_f(n) e(nz)`.
