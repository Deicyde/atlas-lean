# Exists period from preperiod bound

Theorem 2: if `k ≤ m` attains `A_k = A_max`, then `k m (m + 1) / 2` is a
preperiod of `η̃`: the tail `(e_i)_{i > k m (m + 1) / 2}`, i.e. `η j` for
`j ≥ k m (m + 1) / 2`, is periodic.
