# Gcd max Average Indices dvd

Lemma 2: `GCD {i ≤ m | A_i = A_max} = GCD {i ∈ ℕ | A_i = A_max}`, stated as:
the left gcd divides every `i ≥ 1` with `A_i = A_max` (the other divisibility
is immediate).
