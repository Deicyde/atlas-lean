# Binomid Average le

Lemma 1: for the lex-minimal extension of a finite binomid index of length
`m`, `A_max = max {A_i | 1 ≤ i ≤ m}` is the maximum of all averages: `A_i ≤ A_j`
for every `i ≥ 1` and every maximizing index `j ≤ m`.
