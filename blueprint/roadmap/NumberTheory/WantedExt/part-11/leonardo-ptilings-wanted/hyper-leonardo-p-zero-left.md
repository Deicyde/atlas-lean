# Hyper Leonardo $p$-numbers at $n = 0$

**Hyper Leonardo $p$-numbers at $n = 0$.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $k$, the hyper
Leonardo $p$-number $\mathcal{L}^{(k)}_{p,0}$ equals $1$.
