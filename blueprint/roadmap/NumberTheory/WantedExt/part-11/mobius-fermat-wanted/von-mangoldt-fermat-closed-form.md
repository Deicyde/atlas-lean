# Von Mangoldt Fermat closed form

Theorem 10 (closed form of `Λ_M`): with `l` and `r` as in Theorem 9,
`Λ_M l = (-1) ^ (r - 1) * ∑_{j=0}^{min(⌊m₁/2⌋, m₂)} (r - 2j - 1)! /
((m₁ - 2j)! (m₂ - j)! j! m_3! ⋯ m_k!) * log l`. (For `l = 1` both sides vanish.)
