# The odd squarefree part of $360$

**The odd squarefree part of $360$.** The number $5$ is the odd squarefree part of $360$:
$5$ is odd and squarefree, and $360 = 2^a m^2 \cdot 5$ for some natural number $a$ and some
integer $m$.
