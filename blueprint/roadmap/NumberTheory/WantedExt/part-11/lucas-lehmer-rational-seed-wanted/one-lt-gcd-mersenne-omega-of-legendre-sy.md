# One lt gcd mersenne Omega of legendre Sym eq zero

Let `p` be one of the primes `r_i`, `q_i` of `s_1^- s_1^+`. If `η_n(p) = (M_n | p) = 0`, then
`gcd(n, Ω) > 1` with `Ω = Ω(s_1^- s_1^+)`.
