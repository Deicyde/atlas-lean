# Lehmer V two neg one

For `R = 2`, `Q = -1` (the parameters giving the seed `4`), `V̄_2 = 4`, `V̄_4 = 14 = 4² - 2`
and `V̄_8 = 194 = 14² - 2`, as the doubling formula (LehmerV2n) predicts.
