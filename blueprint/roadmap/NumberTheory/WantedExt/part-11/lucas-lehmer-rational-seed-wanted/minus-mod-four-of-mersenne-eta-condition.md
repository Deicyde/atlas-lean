# Minus mod four of mersenne Eta Condition all

If `q'(s_1) = 1`, i.e. condition (etar) holds for all `φ(Ω)` reduced residues `n` modulo `Ω`,
then `s_1^- ≡ 1 (mod 4)` and `s_1^+ ≡ -1 (mod 4)`.
