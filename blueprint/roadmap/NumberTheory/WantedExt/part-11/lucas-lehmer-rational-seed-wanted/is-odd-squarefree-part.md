# Is Odd Squarefree Part

`k` is the odd squarefree part `N^*` of `N`: `k` is odd and squarefree and
`N = 2^a m² k`. For positive `N` this `k` is unique: it is `sqf(N)` when that is odd and
`sqf(N)/2` otherwise.
