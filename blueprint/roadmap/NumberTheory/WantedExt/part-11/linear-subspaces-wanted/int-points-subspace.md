# Integer points of a subspace

**Integer points of a subspace.** For a subspace $V$ of $\mathbb{R}^d$, this is the set
$V \cap \mathbb{Z}^d$ of integer points of $V$, as a $\mathbb{Z}$-submodule of $V$.
