# Coeff ramanujan Theta Monomial one one three

Characteristic values of `ψ(q) = f(q, q^3) = 1 + q + q^3 + ⋯`: the coefficients of `q^0`,
`q^1`, `q^2`, `q^3` are `1, 1, 0, 1`.
