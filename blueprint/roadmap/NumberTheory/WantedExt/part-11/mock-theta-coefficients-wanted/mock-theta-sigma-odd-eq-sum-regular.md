# Mock Theta Sigma odd eq sum regular

Theorem 7: `P_σ(2n+1) = ∑_{m ≥ 0} b_2^2(n - (3m^2+3m)/2)`, where `b_2^2(k)` is the
coefficient of `q^k` in `(f_2/f_1)^2` (Equation (8)) and vanishes for `k < 0`.
