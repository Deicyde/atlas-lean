# Lower Order At Infinity

The lower order at infinity `liminf_{q → ∞} -log ψ(q) / log q` of `1/ψ`, valued in
`ℝ≥0∞` (for `ψ` with values in `(0, 1]` the ratio is nonnegative once `q ≥ 2`, and the
order may be infinite).
