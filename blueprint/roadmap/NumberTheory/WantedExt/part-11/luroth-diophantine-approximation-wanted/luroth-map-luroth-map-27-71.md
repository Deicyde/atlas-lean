# The Lüroth orbit of $27/71$ has period two

**The Lüroth orbit of $27/71$ has period two.** The Lüroth map $T$ sends $27/71$ to $20/71$
and sends $20/71$ to $27/71$; in particular $T(T(27/71)) = 27/71$.
