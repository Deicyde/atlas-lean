# The order ratio of $q^{-\tau}$

**The order ratio of $q^{-\tau}$.** For every real $\tau$ and all sufficiently large natural
numbers $q$, $\max(0, -\log((q^{\tau})^{-1})/\log q) = \max(0, \tau)$.
