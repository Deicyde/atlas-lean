# Mallows Child

The left, middle and right child gaps of a Mallows-tree vertex inserted in the gap
`(a, b)`: if `⟨p, q⟩` is the pair inserted in `(a, b)`, the children are the pairs inserted in
`(a, p)`, `(p, q)` and `(q, b)`.
