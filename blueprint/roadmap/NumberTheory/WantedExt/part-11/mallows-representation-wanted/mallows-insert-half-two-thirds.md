# Mallows insertion between $1/2$ and $2/3$

**Mallows insertion between $1/2$ and $2/3$.** Mallows' insertion rule inserts the pair
$\langle 4/7, 3/5 \rangle$ between the fractions $1/2$ and $2/3$ (here the left fraction $1/2$
has odd numerator).
