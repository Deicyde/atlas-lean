# Mallows Rep ne M iff

For `α = [0; a_1, a_2, …]` irrational in `(0, 1)`, `M(α)` contains no `M` iff `a_{2k}` is
even for all `k ≥ 1`; `a k = a_{k+1}`, so `a_{2k}` is `a (2k - 1)`.
