# Mallows Refine

One refinement step `{0/1, 1/1} ∪ MT_1 ∪ ⋯ ∪ MT_i ↦ {0/1, 1/1} ∪ MT_1 ∪ ⋯ ∪ MT_{i+1}` of an
increasing list of fractions: Mallows' insertion between each pair of adjacent fractions.
