# Transitive mallows Rep iff cont Fract

The Mallows representation of an irrational `α = [0; a_1, a_2, …] ∈ (0, 1)` is transitive
iff every finite word over `ℤ_{>0}` occurs in `⟨a_1, a_2, …⟩`; `a k = a_{k+1}`.
