# Mallows Admissible

The set `I` of infinite sequences over `{O_L, E_R, O_R, E_L}` starting with `O_L` and
containing none of the factors `⟨O_L,O_R⟩, ⟨O_L,E_L⟩, ⟨E_R,E_L⟩, ⟨E_R,E_R⟩, ⟨O_R,O_L⟩,
⟨O_R,E_R⟩, ⟨E_L,E_L⟩, ⟨E_L,E_R⟩` and no tail `⟨O_L⟩^∞`, `⟨O_R⟩^∞`, `⟨O_L,E_R⟩^∞`,
`⟨O_R,E_L⟩^∞`.
