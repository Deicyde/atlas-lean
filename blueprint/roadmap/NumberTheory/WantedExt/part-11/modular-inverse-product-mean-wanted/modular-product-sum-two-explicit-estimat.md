# Modular Product Sum two explicit estimate

Theorem 3: for `n ≥ 2` and `m` coprime to `n`,
`|S_2(n, m) - n² φ(n) / 4| ≤ n² σ_{1/2}(n) (τ(n) log(e n))² + n φ(n) / 4`, where
`σ_{1/2}(n) = ∑_{d ∣ n} √d` and `τ(n)` is the number of divisors.
