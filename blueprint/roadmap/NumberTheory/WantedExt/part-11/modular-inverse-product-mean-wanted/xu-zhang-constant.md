# Xu Zhang Constant

The constant `C_q := ∏_{p^α ∥ q} (1 + (2p³ + p² - 1) / ((p² + 1)(p² + p + 1))
- 1 / (p^{3(α-1)} (p² + p + 1)))` of Xu and Zhang.
