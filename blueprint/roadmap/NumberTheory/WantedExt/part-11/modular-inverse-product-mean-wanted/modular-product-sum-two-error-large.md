# Modular Product Sum two error large

Theorem 4, consequence: there are `c > 0` and `N` such that every odd `n ≥ N` admits
`m ∈ {1, …, n}` coprime to `n` with `|S_2(n, m) - n² φ(n) / 4| ≥ c n^{3/2} φ(n)`.
