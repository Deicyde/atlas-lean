# Sum inv norm one sub additive character

Lemma 6: for `q ≥ 2`, `ℓ ≥ 1` and either sign `s = ±1`,
`∑_{a mod q, q ∤ a ℓ} 1 / |1 - e_q(s a ℓ)| ≤ (q/2) log(e q / (2 (ℓ, q))) < (q/2) log(e q)`.
