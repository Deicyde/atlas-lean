# Sum mul additive character

Lemma 3: for `q ≥ 2` and `q ∤ k`,
`∑_{b=1}^{q-1} b e_q(-k b) = -q / (1 - e_q(-k))`. The paper's `k ≥ 1` follows from `q ∤ k`.
