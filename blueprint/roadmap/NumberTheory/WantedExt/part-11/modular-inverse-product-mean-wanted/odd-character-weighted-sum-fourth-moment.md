# Odd character weighted sum fourth moment

Corollary 1: there is `C` such that for every odd `n ≥ 3`,
`∑_{χ mod n, χ(-1) = -1} |∑_{a=1}^n a χ(a)|⁴ ≤ C n³ φ(n)⁴ (log log n)²`.
