# Modular Product Sum odd estimate

Theorem 2: for fixed `k ≥ 4` there is `C_k` such that for every odd `n ≥ 3` and every `m`
coprime to `n`, `|S_k(n, m) - 2^{-k} n^k φ(n)^{k-1}|
≤ C_k n^{3(k-2)/2} φ(n)³ (log n)^{k-4} (log log n)²`.
