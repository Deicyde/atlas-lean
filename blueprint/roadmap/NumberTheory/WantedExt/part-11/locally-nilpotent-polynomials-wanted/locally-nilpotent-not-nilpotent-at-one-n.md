# Locally Nilpotent not nilpotent At one neg one

The locally nilpotent but non-nilpotent polynomials at `1` and at `-1` are exactly
`x + 1` and `x - 1` respectively (`S_1 = {x + 1}`, `S_{-1} = {x - 1}`).
