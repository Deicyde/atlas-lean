# Locally Nilpotent not nilpotent At zero

The locally nilpotent but non-nilpotent polynomials at `0` (`S_0`) are exactly
`x + b` with `b ≠ 0` and `ax + b` with `b ≠ 0` and `P(b) ⊇ P(a) ≠ ∅`.
