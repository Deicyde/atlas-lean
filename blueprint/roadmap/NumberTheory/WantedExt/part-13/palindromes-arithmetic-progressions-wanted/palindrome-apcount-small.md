# Palindrome APCount small

Small values: `A_10(100, 1, 0) = 18` (the palindromes `1, …, 9, 11, …, 99`) and
`A_2(9, 2, 1) = 5` (`1, 3, 5 = (101)_2, 7 = (111)_2, 9 = (1001)_2`).
