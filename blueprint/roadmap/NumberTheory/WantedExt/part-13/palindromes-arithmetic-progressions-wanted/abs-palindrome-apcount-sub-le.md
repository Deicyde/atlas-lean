# Abs palindrome APCount sub le

Corollary 12: for `b ≥ 2`, odd `q ≥ 1`, `0 ≤ r < q` and `b ≡ 1 (mod q)`, uniformly in `m ≥ 1`,
`A_b(m, q, r) = A_b(m)/q + O*(log m/(2 log b) + 2)`, i.e. the error is at most
`log m/(2 log b) + 2` in absolute value.
