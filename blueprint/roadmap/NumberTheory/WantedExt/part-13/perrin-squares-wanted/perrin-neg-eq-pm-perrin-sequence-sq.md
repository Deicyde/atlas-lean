# Perrin Neg eq pm perrin Sequence sq

Theorem 2: the solutions of `R_{-n} = ± R_m ^ 2` in positive integers are
exactly `R_{-5} = 4 = R_2 ^ 2 = R_4 ^ 2` and `R_{-18} = 25 = R_5 ^ 2 = R_6 ^ 2`.
