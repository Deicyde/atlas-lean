# Perrin Neg abs bounds

Lemma 1 (quoted from Bravo, Bravo and Luca): with `α` the real zero of
`X ^ 3 - X - 1`, for every integer `k ≥ 6`,
`α ^ (k / 2 - 3 × 10 ^ 15 log k) < |R_{-k}| < 2.01 α ^ (k / 2)`.
