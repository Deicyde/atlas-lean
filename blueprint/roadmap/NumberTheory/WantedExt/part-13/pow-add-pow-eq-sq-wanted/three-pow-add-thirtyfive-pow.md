# Solutions of $3^x + 35^y = z^2$

**Solutions of $3^x + 35^y = z^2$.** The natural numbers $x$, $y$, $z$ with
$3^x + 35^y = z^2$ are exactly $(x, y, z) = (1, 0, 2)$ and $(x, y, z) = (0, 1, 6)$.
