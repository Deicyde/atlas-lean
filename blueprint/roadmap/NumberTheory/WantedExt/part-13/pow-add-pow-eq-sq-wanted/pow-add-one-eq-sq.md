# Solutions of $n^x + 1 = z^2$ for $n \ne 2$

**Solutions of $n^x + 1 = z^2$ for $n \ne 2$.** Let $n \ge 1$ be a natural number with
$n \ne 2$. For natural numbers $x$ and $z$, $n^x + 1 = z^2$ holds if and only if $x = 1$ and
$z^2 = n + 1$.
