# No integer solutions of $64x^6 - 1 = p^3 q^3 y^2$ with $p$, $q$ prime

**No integer solutions of $64x^6 - 1 = p^3 q^3 y^2$ with $p$, $q$ prime.** There are no
integers $x$, $y$ and primes $p$, $q$ with $64 x^6 - 1 = p^3 q^3 y^2$.
