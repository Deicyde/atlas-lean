# Exact divisibility of $x^2 - x + 1$ by $3$

**Exact divisibility of $x^2 - x + 1$ by $3$.** If $x$ is an integer with
$x \equiv 2 \pmod 3$, then $3$ divides $x^2 - x + 1$ but $9$ does not.
