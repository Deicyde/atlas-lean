# Square Pell–Lucas numbers

**Square Pell–Lucas numbers.** For every natural number $n$, the Pell–Lucas number $Q_n$ is a
perfect square if and only if $n = 0$ or $n = 1$; thus the only square terms of the sequence are
$Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$.
