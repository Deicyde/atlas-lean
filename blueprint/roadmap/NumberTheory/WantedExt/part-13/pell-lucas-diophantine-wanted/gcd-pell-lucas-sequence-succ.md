# Consecutive Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime

**Consecutive Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime.** For every natural number $n$, the Pell–Lucas
numbers $Q_n$ and $Q_{n+1}$ satisfy $\gcd(Q_n, Q_{n+1}) = 1$.
