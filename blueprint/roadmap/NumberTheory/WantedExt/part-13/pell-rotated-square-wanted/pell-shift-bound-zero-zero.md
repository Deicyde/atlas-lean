# The bound $K$ of the unshifted Pell equation

**The bound $K$ of the unshifted Pell equation.** For every integer $D$, the constant
$K = |a| + \max(|b| + 1, \sqrt{1 + D b^2})$ with $a = b = 0$ equals $1$.
