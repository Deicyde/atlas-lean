# Pell shifted intercepts max

For integers `a ≠ 0` and `b`, the largest `L¹` norm of an intercept of the shifted Pell curve
`(x - a)² - D (y - b)² = 1`, among `(a ± √(1 + D b²), 0)` and `(0, b ± √((a² - 1) / D))`, is
`|a| + √(1 + D b²)`.
