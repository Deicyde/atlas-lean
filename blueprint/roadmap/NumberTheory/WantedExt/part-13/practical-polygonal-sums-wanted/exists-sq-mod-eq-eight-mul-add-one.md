# Square roots modulo powers of two

**Square roots modulo powers of two.** For all natural numbers $m > 0$ and $n > 0$ there is
a natural number $x$ with $1 \le x \le 2^m - 1$ and $x^2 \equiv 8n + 1 \pmod{2^{m+2}}$.
