# Exists polygonal add polygonal mod Eq two

For positive `n` and `s > 3` there are positive `x, y` such that
`P_s(x₁) + P_s(y₁) ≡ n (mod 2)` for all positive `x₁ ≡ x`, `y₁ ≡ y (mod 4)`.
