# Q Pochhammer Inf five dissection

Ramanujan's 5-dissection of Euler's product:
`f_1 = f_{25} (R(q^5)⁻¹ - q - q^2 R(q^5))` with `R(q) = f(-q, -q^4) / f(-q^2, -q^3)`, for
complex `‖q‖ < 1`.
