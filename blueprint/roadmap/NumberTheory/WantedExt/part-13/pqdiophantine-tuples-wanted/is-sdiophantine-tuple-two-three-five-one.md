# Is SDiophantine Tuple two three five one three eight

`(1, 3, 8)` is a `{2, 3, 5}`-Diophantine triple: `1 * 3 + 1 = 2 ^ 2`,
`1 * 8 + 1 = 3 ^ 2`, `3 * 8 + 1 = 5 ^ 2`.
