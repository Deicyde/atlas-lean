# Pell Lucas Sequence oresme convolution

The Pell–Lucas–Oresme convolution, for the odd Pell–Lucas numbers `Q_0 = Q_1 = 1`:
`∑_{k=0}^n Q_k O_{n-k} = (2/49)(5 Q_{n+1} - 4 Q_n) + (1/49)(7n-2)/2^n` for every `n ≥ 0`.
