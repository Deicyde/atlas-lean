# Pell Number oresme convolution

The Pell–Oresme convolution:
`∑_{k=0}^n P_k O_{n-k} = (2/49)(5 P_{n+1} - 4 P_n) - (2/49)(7n+5)/2^n` for every `n ≥ 0`.
