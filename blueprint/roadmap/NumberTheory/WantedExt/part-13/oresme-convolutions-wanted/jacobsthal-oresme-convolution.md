# Jacobsthal oresme convolution

The Jacobsthal–Oresme convolution, with `J = lucasU (1, -2)`:
`∑_{k=0}^n J_k O_{n-k} = (2/9) J_{n+1} - 2(n+1)/(9 · 2^n)` for every `n ≥ 0`.
