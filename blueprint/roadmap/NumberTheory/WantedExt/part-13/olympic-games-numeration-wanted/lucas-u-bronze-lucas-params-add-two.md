# Recurrence of the bronze numbers

**Recurrence of the bronze numbers.** Let $B_n$ be the bronze numbers, the terms of the Lucas
sequence $U(3, -1)$. For every natural number $n$, $B_{n+2} = 3B_{n+1} + B_n$.
