# Bit length of $\binom{2^n}{2^{n-1}}$

**Bit length of $\binom{2^n}{2^{n-1}}$.** For every integer $n \ge 1$, the binary expansion of
the binomial coefficient $\binom{2^n}{2^{n-1}}$ has exactly $2^n - \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ digits.
