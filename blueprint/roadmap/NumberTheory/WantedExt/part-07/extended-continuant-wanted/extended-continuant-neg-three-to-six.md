# Extended Continuant neg three to six

Lemma 1: `K_{-3}(x)_h = 1`, `K_{-4}(x)_h = -x_{h-2}`, `K_{-5}(x)_h = 1 + x_{h-3} x_{h-2}`, and
`K_{-6}(x)_h = -(x_{h-4} + x_{h-2} + x_{h-4} x_{h-3} x_{h-2})`.
