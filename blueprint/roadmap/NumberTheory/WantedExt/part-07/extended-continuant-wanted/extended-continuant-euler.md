# Extended Continuant euler

Corollary 1 (Euler's continuant identity, extended): for all `h, k, ℓ, m ∈ ℤ`,
`K_{ℓ+m}(x)_h K_{k-1}(x)_{ℓ+h+1} - K_{m-1}(x)_{ℓ+h+1} K_{k+ℓ}(x)_h
= (-1)^k K_{m-k-2}(x)_{k+ℓ+h+2} K_{ℓ-1}(x)_h`.
