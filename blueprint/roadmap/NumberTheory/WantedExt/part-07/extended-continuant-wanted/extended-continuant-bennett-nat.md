# Extended Continuant bennett nat

Theorem 2 (Bennett): for nonnegative integers `k, ℓ, m, n`,
`(-1)^{k+ℓ} K_{n-k-2}(x)_k K_{m-ℓ-2}(x)_ℓ + (-1)^{ℓ+m} K_{n-ℓ-2}(x)_ℓ K_{k-m-2}(x)_m
+ (-1)^{m+k} K_{n-m-2}(x)_m K_{ℓ-k-2}(x)_k = 0`.
