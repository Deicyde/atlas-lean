# Three Silent terms

Theorem 6 (3-silent BpCFs), with the paper's choice `x_{-1} = 0`: if `k_i ≐ c_i` and
`k_{-i-4} ≐ d_i` for all `i ≥ 0` and `x_{-1} = 0`, then `x_n = y_n` for `n ≥ 0` and
`x_{-n} = -z_{n-2}` for `n ≥ 2`.
