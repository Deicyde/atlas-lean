# No product of distinct odd primes is near $F_k$-perfect

**No product of distinct odd primes is near $F_k$-perfect.** Let $k > 1$ and let $s$ be a
finite set of odd primes. Then $\prod_{p \in s} p$ is not near $F_k$-perfect.
