# Odd primes are not $(k,\ell)$-deficient-perfect

**Odd primes are not $[k,\ell]$-deficient-perfect.** Let $p$ be an odd prime. Then for all
natural numbers $k$ and $\ell$, $p$ is not $[k,\ell]$-deficient-perfect.
