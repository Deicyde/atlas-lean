# Gcd radical expansion coeffs eq one

Under the hypotheses of Lemma 3, the coefficients `a_{n,0}, …, a_{n,N-1}` of
`(u + v^{1/N})^n` have greatest common divisor `1` for every `n ≥ 1`.
