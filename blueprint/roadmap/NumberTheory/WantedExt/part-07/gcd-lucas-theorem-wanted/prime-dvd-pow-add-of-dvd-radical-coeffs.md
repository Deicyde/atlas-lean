# Prime dvd pow add of dvd radical coeffs gcd

If a prime `p` divides `D_n` for some `n ≥ 1`, then `p ∣ u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v` (the norm of
`u + v^{1/N}`). The section's `N ≥ 4` and the coprimality of `u, v` are not needed (norm
argument; probe).
