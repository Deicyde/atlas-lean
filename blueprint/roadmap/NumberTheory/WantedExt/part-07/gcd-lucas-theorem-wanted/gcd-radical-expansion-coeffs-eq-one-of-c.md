# Gcd radical expansion coeffs eq one of coprime

For coprime `u, v`, `N ≥ 2` and `x^N - v` irreducible over `ℚ`, if
`u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v` and `N` are coprime then `D_n = 1` for all `n ≥ 1`. The section assumes
`N ≥ 4`; `N = 2, 3` also hold, `N = 1` fails.
