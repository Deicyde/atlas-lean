# $u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v$ is coprime to $u$ and to $v$

**$u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v$ is coprime to $u$ and to $v$.** Let $u, v$ be coprime positive integers
and let $N \ge 1$. Then $\gcd(u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v, u) = 1$ and $\gcd(u^N + (-1)^{N+1} v, v) = 1$.
