# Card h Full h Free asymptotic

Lemma 5, Equation (51): for `0 < h < ℓ`, a primitive `h`-th root of unity `ξ` and `ε > 0`,
`|N_h ∩ M_n ∩ S_ℓ| = (q^{n/h}/h) ∑_{j<h} ξ^{jn} H_{N_h,S_ℓ}((q^{1/h}ξ^j)^{-1})
+ O_ε(q^{n/(h+1) + εn})` for `n ≥ ℓ`.
