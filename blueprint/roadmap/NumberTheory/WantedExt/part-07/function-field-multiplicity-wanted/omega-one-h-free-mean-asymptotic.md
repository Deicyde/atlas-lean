# Omega One h Free mean asymptotic

Theorem 1, first moment: for `h ≥ 2`, the mean of `ω_1` over `S_h ∩ M_n` is
`log n + C_1(S_h, 1) + O(1/n)` with `C_1(S_h, 1) = B_1 - T_h`.
