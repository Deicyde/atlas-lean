# The polynomial norm $q^{\deg f}$ over a finite field

**The polynomial norm $q^{\deg f}$ over a finite field.** Let $F$ be a finite field with
$q$ elements. The norm of a polynomial $f \in F[X]$ is the real number $|f| = q^{\deg f}$ if
$f \neq 0$, and $|0| = 0$.
