# Omega Mult h Free mean asymptotic

Theorem 3, first moment: for `1 < k < h` and every `ε > 0`, the mean of `ω_k` over
`S_h ∩ M_n` is `S_{h,k} + O_ε(q^{n/k - n + εn})`.
