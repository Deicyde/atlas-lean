# Omega One h Free variance asymptotic

Theorem 1, variance: for `h ≥ 2` and every `ε > 0`, the variance of `ω_1` over
`S_h ∩ M_n` is `log n + C_4(S_h, 1) + O_ε(n^{ε-1})` with
`C_4(S_h, 1) = B_1 - ζ(2) - U_h - T_h`, `ζ(2) = π^2/6`.
