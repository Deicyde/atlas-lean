# A nonzero polynomial has norm $q^{\deg f}$

**A nonzero polynomial has norm $q^{\deg f}$.** Let $F$ be a finite field with $q$
elements. Every nonzero polynomial $f \in F[X]$ has norm $|f| = q^{\deg f}$.
