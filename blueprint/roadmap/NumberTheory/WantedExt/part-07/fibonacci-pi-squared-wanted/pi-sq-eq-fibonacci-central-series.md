# Pi sq eq fibonacci central series

Lemma 2.2 (the case `n = 1`):
`π² = (250 / (8√5)) Σ_k (k!)² F_(2k+2) / (2k+2)!` (2.3) and
`π² = (250 / (25 - 4√5)) Σ_k (k!)² F_(2k+1) / (2k+2)!` (2.4).
