# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4**: the Lucas analogues of the generalized
FiboCatalan numbers `J_{{r}} {2n}!/({n}! {n+r+1}!)`, where
`J_{{r}} = {2r+1}!/{r}!`, are polynomials with non-negative
integer coefficients.
