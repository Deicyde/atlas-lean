# Proposition 3

**Proposition 3.** For odd `k ≥ 3`, in `𝒜`:
`2 ζ^{[2]}_{𝒜,12}(k) = (2^{-k} - 3^{-k} - 4^{-k} + 12^{-k}) Z(k)` and
`2 ζ^{[3]}_{𝒜,12}(k) = (3^{-k} - 4^{-k} - 6^{-k} + 12^{-k}) Z(k)`, where
`Z(k) = (B_{p-k} / k mod p)_p`.
