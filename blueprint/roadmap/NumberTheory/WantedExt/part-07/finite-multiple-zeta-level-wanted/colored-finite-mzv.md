# Colored Finite MZV

The colored finite multiple harmonic sum `ζ^α_{p,N}(k) ∈ ℤ/pℤ`: the sum of
`∏ᵢ mᵢ^{-kᵢ}` over `0 < m₁ < ⋯ < m_r < p` with `mᵢ ≡ αᵢ (mod N)`.
