# Lemma 1

**Lemma 1.** For an index `k` of weight `w`, `N ≥ 1` and `0 ≤ j < N`,
`∑_{jp/N < m₁ < ⋯ < m_r < (j+1)p/N} 1/(m₁^{k₁} ⋯ m_r^{k_r}) ≡ N^w ζ^{[j]}_{𝒜,N}(k)` in `𝒜`.

The entries of `k` may be `0`; the paper's indices have positive entries.
