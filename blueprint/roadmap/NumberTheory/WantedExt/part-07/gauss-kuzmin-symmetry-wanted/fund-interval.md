# Half-open interval between two reals

**Half-open interval between two reals.** For real numbers $\alpha$ and $\beta$, the interval
$\langle \alpha, \beta \rangle$ is $[\alpha, \beta)$ if $\alpha < \beta$, and $(\beta, \alpha]$
otherwise.
