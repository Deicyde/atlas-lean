# Gcd eq neg emod emod five sub two

For all `a, b ≥ 1`,
`gcd(a, b) = (((-5^{ab(ab+a+b)}) mod ((5^{a²b} - 1)(5^{ab²} - 1))) mod 5^{ab}) - 2` over `ℤ`,
with the nonnegative remainder.
