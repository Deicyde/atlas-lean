# Tendsto farey Zoom sub at rational

Theorem 3: for `x ≥ 0` and a positive reduced fraction `p/q`,
`B_n(x + (p/q) n) - B_n((p/q) n) → (1/q) Σ_{k=1}^{⌊qx⌋} log(qx/k)` as `n → ∞`.
