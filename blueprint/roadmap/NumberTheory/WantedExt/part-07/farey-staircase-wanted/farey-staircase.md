# Farey Staircase

The Farey staircase of order `n`: `A_n(x) = (1/n) Σ_{k=1}^n ⌊kx⌋/k`, the average of the first `n`
lower rational approximations `⌊kx⌋/k` of `x` (`A_0 = 0`).
