# Floor gaps inter Iic finite

Theorem 5 (ii) (D. R. Richman): for every `ε > 0` only finitely many values
`f_n(x) = ⌊nx⌋ - n A_n(x)` (`n ≥ 1`, `x ∈ ℝ`) are at most `1 - log 2 - ε`.
