# Farey Staircase sub eq

Proposition 7: for `n ≥ 2`, `D_n = A_n - A_{n-1}` satisfies
(a) `D_n(x) = (1/n)(⌊nx⌋/n - A_{n-1}(x))` and (b) `D_n(x) = (1/(n-1))(⌊nx⌋/n - A_n(x))`.
