# Tendsto dilation quotient

Proposition 12: if `f` has derivative `f'` at `x`, the dilation derivative
`lim_{λ → 1} (λ⁻¹ f(λx) - f(x))/(λ - 1)` exists and equals `x f' - f(x)`.
