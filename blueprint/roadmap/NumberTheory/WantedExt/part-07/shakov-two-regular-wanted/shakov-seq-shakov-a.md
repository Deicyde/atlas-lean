# Proposition 9

**Proposition 9**: with `a` as in `shakovA`,
`s(a(n)) = F_n`, the `n`-th Fibonacci number (the
paper's `F_n` is `Nat.fib (n - 1)`; `a(n)` is a
positive integer, written `Int.toNat` here).
