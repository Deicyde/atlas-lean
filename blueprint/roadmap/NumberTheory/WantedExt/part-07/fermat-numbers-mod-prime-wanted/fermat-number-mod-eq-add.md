# Fermat Number mod Eq add

A congruence `F_i ≡ F_j (mod m)` propagates to `F_{i+t} ≡ F_{j+t} (mod m)` for every
`t`; stated for every modulus and all `i, j` (the paper takes `p` prime and `i < j`).
