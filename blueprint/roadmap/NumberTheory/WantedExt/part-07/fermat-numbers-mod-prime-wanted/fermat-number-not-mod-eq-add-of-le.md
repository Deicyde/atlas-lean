# Fermat Number not mod Eq add of le

If `F_j ≢ F_{j+t} (mod m)` for every `t ≥ 1` and `i ≤ j`, then `F_i ≢ F_{i+t} (mod m)`
for every `t ≥ 1`; stated for every modulus and `i ≤ j` (the paper takes `p` prime and
`i < j`).
