# Order Of two two Adic of mod eight

The `2`-part of `ord_p(2)` by residue class: if `p ≡ 7 (mod 8)` then `ord_p(2)` is odd;
if `p ≡ 3 (mod 8)` then `2 ∥ ord_p(2)`; if `p ≡ 5 (mod 8)` then `4 ∥ ord_p(2)`.
