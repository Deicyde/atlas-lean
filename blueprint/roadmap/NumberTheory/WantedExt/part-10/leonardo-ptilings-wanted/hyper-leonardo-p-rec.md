# Proposition 10

**Proposition 10**: `𝓛^{(k)}_{p,n} = 𝓛^{(k)}_{p,n-1} + 𝓛^{(k-1)}_{p,n}` for `n, k ≥ 1`,
with `𝓛^{(0)}_{p,n} = 𝓛_{p,n}` (and `𝓛^{(k)}_{p,0} = 1`, proved as
`hyperLeonardoP_zero_left`).
