# Proposition 14

**Proposition 14**: `R_{p,n} = (p + 1) 𝓛_{p,n} - p 𝓛_{p,n-1}`, with
`𝓛_{p,-1} = -p` (the backward value of the recurrence, `leonardoPKZ`) at `n = 0`.
