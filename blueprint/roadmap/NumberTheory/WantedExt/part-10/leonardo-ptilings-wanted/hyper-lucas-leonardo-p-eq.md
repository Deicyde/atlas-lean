# Proposition 19

**Proposition 19**: for `n > p ≥ 1` and `k ≥ 1`,
`R^{(k)}_{p,n} = R_{p,n+(p+1)k} - R_{p,n+(p+1)k}(k - 1) - p C(n + k, k)`.
