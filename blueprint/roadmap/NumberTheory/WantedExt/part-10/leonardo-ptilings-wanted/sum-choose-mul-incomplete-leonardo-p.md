# Proposition 7

**Proposition 7**: for `n ≥ p + r` and `0 ≤ k ≤ ⌊(n - p - r)/(p+1)⌋`,
`∑_{i=0}^{r} C(r, i) 𝓛_{p,n+pi}(k + i) + (2^r - 1) p = 𝓛_{p,n+(p+1)r}(k + r)`.
