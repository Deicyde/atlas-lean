# Lucas P

The Lucas `p`-numbers: `L_{p,0} = p + 1`, `L_{p,i} = 1` for `1 ≤ i ≤ p`, and
`L_{p,n} = L_{p,n-1} + L_{p,n-p-1}` for `n > p` (evaluated by `lucasTypeSeq`).
