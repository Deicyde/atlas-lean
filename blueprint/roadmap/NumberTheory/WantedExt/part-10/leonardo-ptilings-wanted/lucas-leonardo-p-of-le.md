# Lucas-Leonardo $p$-numbers at indices $1$ to $p$

**Lucas-Leonardo $p$-numbers at indices $1$ to $p$.** For every natural number $p$ and every
$n$ with $1 \le n \le p$, the Lucas-Leonardo $p$-number $R_{p,n}$ equals $1$.
