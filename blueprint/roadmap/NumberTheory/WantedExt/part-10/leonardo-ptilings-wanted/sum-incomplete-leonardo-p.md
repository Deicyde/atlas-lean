# Proposition 8

**Proposition 8**: for `n ≥ (p + 1)(k + 1)`,
`∑_{i=0}^{r-1} 𝓛_{p,n-p+i}(k) + rp = 𝓛_{p,n+r}(k + 1) - 𝓛_{p,n}(k + 1)`.
