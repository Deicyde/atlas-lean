# Lucas Leonardo P

The Lucas-Leonardo `p`-numbers: `R_{p,0} = p² + p + 1`, `R_{p,i} = 1` for
`1 ≤ i ≤ p`, and `R_{p,n} = R_{p,n-1} + R_{p,n-p-1} + p` for `n > p` (evaluated by
`lucasTypeSeq`).
