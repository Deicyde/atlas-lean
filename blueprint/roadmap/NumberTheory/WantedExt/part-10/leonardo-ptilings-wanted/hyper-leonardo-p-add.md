# Proposition 9

**Proposition 9**: for `m, n ≥ 1` with `m ≤ k`,
`𝓛^{(k)}_{p,n+m} = ∑_{i=0}^{m} C(m, i) 𝓛^{(k-i)}_{p,n+i}`.
