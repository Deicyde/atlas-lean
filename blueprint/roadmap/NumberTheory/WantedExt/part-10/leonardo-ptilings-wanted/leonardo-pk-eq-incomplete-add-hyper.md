# Proposition 11

**Proposition 11**: for `n > p` and `k ≥ 1`,
`𝓛_{p,n+(p+1)k} = 𝓛_{p,n+(p+1)k}(k - 1) + 𝓛^{(k)}_{p,n} + p C(n + k, k)`.
