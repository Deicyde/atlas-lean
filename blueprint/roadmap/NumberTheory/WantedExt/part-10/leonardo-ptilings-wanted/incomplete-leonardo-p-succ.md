# Proposition 6

**Proposition 6**: for `n > p` and `0 ≤ k ≤ ⌊n/(p+1)⌋`,
`𝓛_{p,n}(k + 1) = 𝓛_{p,n-1}(k + 1) + 𝓛_{p,n-p-1}(k) + p`.
