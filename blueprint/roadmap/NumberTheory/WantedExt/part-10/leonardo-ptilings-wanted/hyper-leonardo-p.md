# Hyper Leonardo P

The hyper Leonardo `p`-numbers
`𝓛^{(k)}_{p,n} = (p + 1) ∑_{i=0}^{⌊n/(p+1)⌋} C(n + k - pi, i + k) - p C(n + k, k)`.
