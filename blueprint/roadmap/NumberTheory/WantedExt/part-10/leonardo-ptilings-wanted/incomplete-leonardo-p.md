# Incomplete Leonardo P

The incomplete Leonardo `p`-numbers
`𝓛_{p,n}(k) = (p + 1) ∑_{i=0}^{k} C(n - pi, i) - p` (natural-number subtraction; the
paper uses `0 ≤ k ≤ ⌊n/(p+1)⌋`, where it does not truncate).
