# Theorem 6

**Theorem 6.** For `k ≥ 2` and every integer `a`,
`∑_{ℓ ≥ 0} binom((k + 1)ℓ + a, ℓ) 2^{-(k + 1)ℓ} = 2^{a + 1} ρ_k^{-a} / ((k + 1)ρ_k - 2k)`.
