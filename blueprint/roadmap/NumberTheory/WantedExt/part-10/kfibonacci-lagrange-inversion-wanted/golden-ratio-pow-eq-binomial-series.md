# Corollary 5

**Corollary 5.** For `n ≥ 1`, with `φ` the golden ratio,
`φⁿ = 2ⁿ - n 2^{n - 3} ∑_{ℓ ≥ 0} 1/(ℓ + 1) binom(3ℓ + 2 - n, ℓ) 2^{-3ℓ}`; in particular
`φ = 2 - ∑_{ℓ ≥ 0} 1/(ℓ + 1) binom(3ℓ + 1, ℓ) 2^{-(3ℓ + 2)}`.
