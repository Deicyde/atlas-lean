# Jacobi consecutive constant

For odd squarefree `n`: if `n` is prime then `C_{S(n)}(n) = 3`; if `n` is not prime and
every prime divisor of `n` is at least seven then `C_{S(n)}(n) = 2^{Ω(n)}`.
