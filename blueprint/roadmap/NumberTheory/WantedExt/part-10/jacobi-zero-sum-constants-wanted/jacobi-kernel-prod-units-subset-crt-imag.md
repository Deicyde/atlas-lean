# Jacobi kernel prod units subset crt image

For odd squarefree `n`, a prime divisor `p'` and `n' = n/p'`, the Chinese remainder
isomorphism `ψ = (f_{n,n'}, f_{n,p'}) : U(n) → U(n') × U(p')` satisfies
`S(n') × U(p') ⊆ ψ(L(n;p'))`.
