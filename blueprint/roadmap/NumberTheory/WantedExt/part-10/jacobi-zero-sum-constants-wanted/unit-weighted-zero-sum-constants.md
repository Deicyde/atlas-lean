# Unit weighted zero sum constants

For every positive integer `n`, `D_{U(n)}(n) = Ω(n) + 1` and `C_{U(n)}(n) = 2^{Ω(n)}`,
with `Ω(n)` the number of prime factors counted with multiplicity.
