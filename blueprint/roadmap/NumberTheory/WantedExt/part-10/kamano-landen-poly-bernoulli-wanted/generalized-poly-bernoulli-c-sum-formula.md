# Sum formula for `C_n`

**Sum formula for `C_n`**, Identity (16): for `k ≥ 1` and every real `μ`,
`∑_{|k| = k} μ^{d(k) - 1} C_n^{(k, g, (β, γ))} = B_n^{((k), g, (β + μγ, β))}
= C_n^{({1}^k, g, (β, β + μγ))}`, with `{1}^k = (1, …, 1)` (`k` ones).
