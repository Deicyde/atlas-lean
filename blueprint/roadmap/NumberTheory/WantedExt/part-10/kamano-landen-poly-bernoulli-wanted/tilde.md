# Tilde

The transformed data `g̃`, with `g̃_i = (1 0; c₀ - d₀ -1) g_i`; thus
`g̃₀ = (1 -1; -d₀ -c₀)` and `g̃_i = (a_i b_i; (c₀ - d₀) a_i - c_i (c₀ - d₀) b_i - d_i)`.
