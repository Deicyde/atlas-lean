# Depth-one multiple polylogarithm

**Depth-one multiple polylogarithm.** For every natural number $a$ and every integer
$m \ge 1$, the coefficient of $z^m$ in the multiple polylogarithm $\mathrm{Li}_{(a)}(z)$ is
$1/m^a$.
