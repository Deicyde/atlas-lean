# Sum formula for `B_n`

**Sum formula for `B_n`**, Identity (17): for `k ≥ 1` and every real `μ`,
`∑_{|k| = k} μ^{d(k) - 1} B_n^{(k, g, (β, γ))} = B_n^{((k), g, (β + μγ, γ))}
= C_n^{({1}^k, g, (γ, β + μγ))}`.
