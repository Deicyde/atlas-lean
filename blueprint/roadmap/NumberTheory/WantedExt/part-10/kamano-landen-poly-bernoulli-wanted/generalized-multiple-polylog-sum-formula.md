# Sum formula

**Sum formula**: for `k ≥ 1`, every real `μ` and every `δ`,
`∑_{|k| = k} μ^{d(k) - 1} Li_k(g; (β, γ); z) = Li_{(k)}(g; (β + μγ, δ); z)`.
The paper takes `μ` an indeterminate; both sides are polynomials in `μ`, so this
is equivalent.
