# Generalized Poly Bernoulli B

Kamano's generalized poly-Bernoulli numbers of the second kind:
`d/dt Li_{k→}(g; (β, γ); z(t)) = ∑_n B_n^{(k, g, (β, γ))} tⁿ / n!`.
