# $7$ is a large Zsigmondy prime for $(2, 1, 3)$

**$7$ is a large Zsigmondy prime for $(2, 1, 3)$.** The prime $7$ is a large Zsigmondy prime
for $(a, b, n) = (2, 1, 3)$: it divides $2^3 - 1 = 7$, divides neither $2^1 - 1$ nor $2^2 - 1$,
and $7 > 3 + 1$.
