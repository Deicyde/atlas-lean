# Is Large Zsigmondy Prime

A large Zsigmondy prime for `(a, b, n)`: a prime `p` dividing `aⁿ - bⁿ` but no
`aᵐ - bᵐ` with `1 ≤ m < n`, such that `p² ∣ aⁿ - bⁿ` or `p > n + 1`.
