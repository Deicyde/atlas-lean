# Laurent Has Bounded Partial Quotients

`α` has bounded partial quotients: the degrees in `x` of all partial quotients are bounded,
i.e. some `B` has `(b_n).coeff m = 0` (coefficient of `x⁻ᵐ`) for all `n` and `m < -B`.
