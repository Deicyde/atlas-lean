# Laurent Conv Den

Denominators of the convergents, shifted by one: `laurentConvDen α (n + 1) = Q_n`, with
`Q_{-1} = 0`, `Q₀ = 1`, `Q_{n+1} = b_{n+1} Q_n + Q_{n-1}`.
