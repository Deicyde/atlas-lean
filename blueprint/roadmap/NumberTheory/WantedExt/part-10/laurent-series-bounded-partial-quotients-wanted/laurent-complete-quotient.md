# Laurent Complete Quotient

The complete quotients `α₀ = α`, `α_{n+1} = (α_n - [α_n])⁻¹` of the continued fraction
expansion of `α ∈ 𝔽_q((x⁻¹))` (they become `0` after a finite expansion ends).
