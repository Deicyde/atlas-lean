# Laurent Conv Num

Numerators of the convergents, shifted by one: `laurentConvNum α (n + 1) = P_n`, with
`P_{-1} = 1`, `P₀ = b₀`, `P_{n+1} = b_{n+1} P_n + P_{n-1}`.
