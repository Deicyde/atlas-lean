# The variable $x$ times $x^{-1}$ is one

**The variable $x$ times $x^{-1}$ is one.** Let $K$ be a field. In $K((x^{-1}))$, the
product of the variable $x$ and the series $x^{-1}$ (coefficient $1$ at $x^{-1}$ and $0$
elsewhere) is $1$.
