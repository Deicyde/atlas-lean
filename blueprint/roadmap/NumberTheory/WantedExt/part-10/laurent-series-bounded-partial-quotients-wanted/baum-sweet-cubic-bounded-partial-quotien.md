# Baum Sweet cubic bounded Partial Quotients

Baum–Sweet: a root `f ∈ 𝔽₂((x⁻¹))` of `f³ + p⁻¹ f + 1 = 0`, `p ∈ 𝔽₂[x]`, `deg p ≥ 1`, has
bounded partial quotients (quoted result).
