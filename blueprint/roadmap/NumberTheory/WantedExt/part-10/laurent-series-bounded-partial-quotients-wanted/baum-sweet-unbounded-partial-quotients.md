# Baum Sweet unbounded Partial Quotients

Baum–Sweet: if `f ∈ 𝔽₂((x⁻¹))` satisfies `f^{2ⁿ+1} + g⁻¹ f + 1 = 0` with `n > 1`,
`g ∈ 𝔽₂[x]`, `deg g ≥ 1`, then `f` has unbounded partial quotients (quoted result).
