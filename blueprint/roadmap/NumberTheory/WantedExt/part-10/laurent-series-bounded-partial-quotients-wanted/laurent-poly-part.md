# Laurent Poly Part

The polynomial part `[α]` of `α ∈ 𝔽_q((x⁻¹))`: the terms `c_n x⁻ⁿ` with `n ≤ 0`, i.e. the
coefficients of `Xⁿ`, `n ≤ 0`, in `LaurentSeries K`.
