# Lemma 9

**Lemma 9.** If `|Q^{(k)}_n - 2^m| < 2^{m/2}` with `k ≥ 2`, positive `n`, `m` and
`n ≥ k + 1`, then `n < 1.7 · 10¹⁵ · k⁴ · log³ k` and `m < 2.37 · 10¹⁵ · k⁴ · log³ k`.
