# Lemma 8

**Lemma 8.** If `|Q^{(k)}_n - 2^m| < 2^{m/2}` with `k ≥ 2`, `n ≥ 1`, `m ≥ 2` and
`n ≥ k + 1`, then `0.69n - 1.69 < m < 1.39n + 3.78`.
