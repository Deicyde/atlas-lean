# K Pell Lucas

The `k`-generalized Pell–Lucas numbers `Q^{(k)}_n` for `n ≥ 0`: `Q_0 = Q_1 = 2` and
`Q_{n+2} = 2 Q_{n+1} + Q_n + Q_{n-1} + ⋯ + Q_{n+2-k}`, where the terms of negative index (the
`k - 2` initial zeros) are dropped.
