# Corollary 1

**Corollary 1.** For `k ≥ 2` and positive `n`, `m`, `Q^{(k)}_n = 2^m` only for
`Q^{(k)}_1 = 2^1` (every `k ≥ 2`) and `Q^{(k)}_3 = 2^4` (`k ≥ 3`).
