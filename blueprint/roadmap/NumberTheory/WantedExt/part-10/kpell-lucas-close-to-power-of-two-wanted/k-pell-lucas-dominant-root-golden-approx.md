# Lemma 2

**Lemma 2.** Let `α` be the positive real root of `Φ_k` and `g_k` as in Lemma 1. If
`k ≥ 50` and `1 < n < φ^{k/2}`, then (i) `|(2α - 2) αⁿ - 2φ^{2n+1}| < 4φ^{2n} / φ^{k/2}` and
(ii) `|g_k(α) - g_k(φ²)| < 4k / φ^k`.
