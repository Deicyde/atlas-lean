# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3.** Let `α` be the positive real root of `Φ_k` and `g_k` as in Lemma 1. If
`k ≥ 50` and `n < φ^{k/2}`, then `(2α - 2) g_k(α) αⁿ = 2φ^{2n+1} / (φ + 2) · (1 + ξ)` with
`|ξ| < 1.25 / φ^{k/2}`.
