# Xu–Zhang constant at a prime

**Xu–Zhang constant at a prime.** For every prime $p$, the Xu–Zhang constant of $p$ is

$$C_p = 1 + \frac{2p^3 + p^2 - 1}{(p^2 + 1)(p^2 + p + 1)} - \frac{1}{p^2 + p + 1}.$$
