# Nagell ljunggren cube of five dvd

The Nagell–Ljunggren equation `(xⁿ - 1)/(x - 1) = y³` (Equation (1) with `q = 3`) has no
solution with `5 ∣ n`. Ranges as in `nagell_ljunggren_exponent_structure`: `x, y ≥ 2`,
`n ≥ 3`.
