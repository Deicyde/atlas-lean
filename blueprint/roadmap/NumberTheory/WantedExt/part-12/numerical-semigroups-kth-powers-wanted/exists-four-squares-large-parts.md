# Exists four squares large parts

There is a constant `C > 0` such that every positive integer `n` is `a₁² + a₂² + a₃² + a₄²`
with integers `aᵢ`, each `aᵢ = 0` or `aᵢ ≥ √n / C` (quoted by the paper from Moscariello).
