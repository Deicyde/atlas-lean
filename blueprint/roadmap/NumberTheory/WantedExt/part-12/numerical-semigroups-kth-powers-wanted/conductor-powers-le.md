# Conductor powers le

For positive integers `n` and `k`, with `S_n = ⟨n^k, (n+1)^k, …⟩`:
`τ(S_n) ≤ 2^k τ(S_{⌈n/2⌉}) + (2^k - 1)(2n)^k`, `τ` the conductor (quoted by the paper from
Dutch and Rickett); `⌈n/2⌉ = (n + 1) / 2`.
