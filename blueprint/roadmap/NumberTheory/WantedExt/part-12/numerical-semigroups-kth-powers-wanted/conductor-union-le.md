# Conductor union le

For nonempty `A, B ⊆ ℕ` with `gcd(A ∪ B) = 1` and `t` dividing every element of `A`, with
`C = {a / t : a ∈ A}`: `τ(A ∪ B) ≤ t · τ(C ∪ B) + τ(B ∪ {t})`, where `τ(X)` is the conductor
of `⟨X⟩` (quoted by the paper from Dutch and Rickett).
