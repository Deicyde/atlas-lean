# N Approximability eq sup divisors

For each `n ≥ 1` and irrationals `ξ ≠ ξ'`, `λₙ(ξ) = max_{g ∣ n} λ(gξ)` and
`μₙ(ξ, ξ') = max_{g ∣ n} μ(gξ, gξ')`.
