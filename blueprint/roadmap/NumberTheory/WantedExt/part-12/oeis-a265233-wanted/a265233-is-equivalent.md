# Asymptotics of A265233

**Asymptotics of A265233.** The sequence $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ A265233 satisfies

$$a_n \sim \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6\pi} \cdot \frac{12^n}{n} \quad \text{as } n \to \infty.$$
