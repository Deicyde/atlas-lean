# Nagell ljunggren cube no solution of mod six eq five

Theorem 2: for `n ≡ 5 (mod 6)`, the equation `(xⁿ - 1)/(x - 1) = y³` has no integer
solutions `(x, y)` with `x ≡ 2, 3, 5, 6 (mod 9)` (the paper's `x = 9z + 2, 9z + 3, 9z + 5,
9z + 6` with `z ∈ ℤ`).
