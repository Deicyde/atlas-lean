# The odd-case cube sum $L_{m,o}$

**The odd-case cube sum $L_{m,o}$.** For a natural number $m$ and a real number $x$,

$$L_{m,o}(x) = \sum_{j=1}^{m} (2j - 1)^3 + x^3 +
\sum_{j=2}^{m} \bigl((2j - 2) + (j - 1)x\bigr)^3.$$
