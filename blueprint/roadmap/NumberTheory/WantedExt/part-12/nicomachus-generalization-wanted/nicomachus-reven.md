# The even-case square $R_{m,e}$

**The even-case square $R_{m,e}$.** For a natural number $m$ and a real number $x$,

$$R_{m,e}(x) = \bigl(T_{2m} + (1 + T_m)\,x\bigr)^2,$$

where $T_n = n(n+1)/2$ is the $n$-th triangular number.
