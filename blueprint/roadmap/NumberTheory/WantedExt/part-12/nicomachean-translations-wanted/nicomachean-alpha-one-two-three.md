# Nicomachean Alpha one two three

The paper's specializations: `α(n, 1, a) = 2n^2 + a - a^2`,
`α(n, 2, a) = n^2 + (1 - a)n - a^2 + 2a`, and `α(n, 3, a) = -(a + n - 3)(a + n)`.
