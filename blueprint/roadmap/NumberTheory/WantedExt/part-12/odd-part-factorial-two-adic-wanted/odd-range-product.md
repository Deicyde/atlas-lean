# Odd Range Product

`oddRangeProduct l m` is the product of the odd natural numbers in the closed
interval `[l,m]`.  This is the paper's `odpr(l,m)`; a filtered `Finset.Icc`
makes both endpoints and the oddness restriction explicit.
