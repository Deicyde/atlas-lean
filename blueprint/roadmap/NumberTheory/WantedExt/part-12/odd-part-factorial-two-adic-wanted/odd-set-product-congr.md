# Product of the odd numbers below $2^e$

**Product of the odd numbers below $2^e$.** For every natural number $e \ge 3$, the product of the
odd integers $i$ with $1 \le i < 2^e$ is congruent to $2^e + 1$ modulo $2^{e+1}$.
