# Multiplicative three additive two squares base

If `f` is multiplicative, `3`-additive on `E₂ = {a² + b² : a, b ≥ 1}` (Equation (2) with
`k = 3`) and `f(2) ≠ 0`, then `f(2) = 2`, `f(3) = 3` and `f(5) = 5`.
