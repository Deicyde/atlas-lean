# Sq add add one eq three mul cube iff

The equation `u² + u + 1 = 3 v³` has exactly the integer solutions
`(-2, 1)` and `(1, 1)`, and `u² - u + 1 = 3 v³` has exactly `(2, 1)` and
`(-1, 1)`.
