# Not cube sub one eq prime sq mul cube and cube add one

There are no integers `x, a, b` and primes `p, q` with `x³ - 1 = p² a³` and
`x³ + 1 = q² b³`; as in the paper, `x`, `a`, `b` range over all integers (no
positivity assumption).
