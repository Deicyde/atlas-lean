# Cube split of mul eq prime sq mul cube of gcd eq one

Coprime splitting: if `R * S = p ^ 2 * C ^ 3` for a prime `p` and
`gcd(R, S) = 1`, then one of `R`, `S` is a perfect cube and the other is `p ^ 2`
times a perfect cube.
