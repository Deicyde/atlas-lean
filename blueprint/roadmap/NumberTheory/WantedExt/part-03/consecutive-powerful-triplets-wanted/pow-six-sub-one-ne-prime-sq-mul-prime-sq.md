# $x^6 - 1$ is not $p^2 q^2$ times a nonzero cube

**$x^6 - 1$ is not $p^2 q^2$ times a nonzero cube.** For all primes $p$, $q$ and all integers
$x$ and $a$ with $a \ne 0$, $x^6 - 1 \ne p^2 q^2 a^3$.
