# Constant Term tilde pow mul mod Eq laurent Cartier

Rowland–Zeilberger congruence mod `p^a` for `P̃` with `P̃(x)^p ≡ P̃(x^p) (mod p^a)`:
`ct[P̃^{pn+k} Q] ≡ ct[P̃^n Λ_p(P̃^k Q)] (mod p^a)`, for all natural `k` (the paper states
`k ∈ 𝔽_p`).
