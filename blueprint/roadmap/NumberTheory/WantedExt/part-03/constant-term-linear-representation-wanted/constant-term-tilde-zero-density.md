# Constant Term tilde zero density

If `P̃(x)^p ≡ P̃(x^p) (mod p^a)` and `p ∣ ct[P̃^{n₀}]` for some `n₀`, then for every `Q`
the zeros of `ct[P̃^n Q] mod p^a` have density `1`.
