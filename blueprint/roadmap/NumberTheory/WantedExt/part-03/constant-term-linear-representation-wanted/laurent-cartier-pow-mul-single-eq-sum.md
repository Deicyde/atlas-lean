# Laurent Cartier pow mul single eq sum

Bookkeeping identity: for a digit `k < p` and `i ∈ T = [-m, m]^r` with
`m ≥ deg P - 1`, `Λ_p(P^k x^i) = ∑_{j ∈ T} a^k_{i - p j} x^j`, where
`a^k_{i-pj} = coef_{pj-i}[P^k]`.
