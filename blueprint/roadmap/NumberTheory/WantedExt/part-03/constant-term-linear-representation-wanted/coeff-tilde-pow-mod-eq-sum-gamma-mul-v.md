# Coeff tilde pow mod Eq sum gamma mul V

The step `γ̃(k) · Ṽ(n) = Ṽ(pn + k)` over `ℤ/p^aℤ`, entrywise, for `P̃` with
`P̃(x)^p ≡ P̃(x^p) (mod p^a)`, `m ≥ deg P̃ - 1`, a digit `k < p` and `i ∈ [-m, m]^r`.
