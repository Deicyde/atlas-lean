# Constant Term pow mul mod Eq tilde

Conversion to `P̃`: `ct[P^{p^{a-1} n + k} Q] ≡ ct[P̃^n Λ_{p^ℓ}(P^k Q)] (mod p^a)`, for all
natural `k` (the paper restricts to `k < p^{a-1}`).
