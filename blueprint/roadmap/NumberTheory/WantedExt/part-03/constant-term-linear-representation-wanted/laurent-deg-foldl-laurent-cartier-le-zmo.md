# Laurent Deg foldl laurent Cartier le zmod

Degree bound mod `p^a`: for Laurent polynomials `P̃` and `Q₀` over `ℤ/p^aℤ`, iterating
`Q ↦ Λ_p(P̃^k Q)` with digits `k < p` never raises the degree above
`max(deg P̃ - 1, deg Q₀)`.
