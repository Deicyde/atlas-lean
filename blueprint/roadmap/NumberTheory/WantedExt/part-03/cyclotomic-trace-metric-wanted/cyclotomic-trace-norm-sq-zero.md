# Squared trace norm of zero

**Squared trace norm of zero.** Let $p$ be a positive integer and let $K$ be a $p$-th
cyclotomic extension of $\mathbb{Q}$. The squared trace norm of $0 \in K$ is $0$, that is,
$\lVert 0 \rVert^2 = 0$.
