# Paperfolding Sum cont Frac recursion

Corollary 15: under the hypotheses of Theorem 14, `⌊S⌋ = 1` and the partial denominators
of `S` begin with the word `Aₖ` of the recursion `A₀ = [1, 1]`, `Aₖ₊₁ = Aₖ (xₖ₊₁ - 1) Aₖ^r`,
for every `k`.
