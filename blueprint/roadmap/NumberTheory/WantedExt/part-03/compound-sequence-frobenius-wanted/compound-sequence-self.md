# Last term of a compound sequence

**Last term of a compound sequence.** For every natural number $n$ and all sequences $a$, $b$
of natural numbers, the compound sequence $c$ of $a$ and $b$ satisfies $c_n = b_1 b_2 \cdots b_n$.
