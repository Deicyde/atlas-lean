# Compound Sequence embedding Dimension

The embedding dimension of the semigroup generated by the compound sequence
`C = {c_0, …, c_n}` is `n - k + 1`, where `k = |{i : a_i = 1}|`. The embedding dimension is
the cardinality of the minimal generating set, the nonzero elements of `⟨C⟩` that are not a
sum of two nonzero elements of `⟨C⟩`.
