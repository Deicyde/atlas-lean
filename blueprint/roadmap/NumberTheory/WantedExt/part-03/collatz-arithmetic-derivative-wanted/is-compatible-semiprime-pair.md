# Is Compatible Semiprime Pair

Semiprimes `pq` and `rs` are compatible: `p, q, r, s` are primes and `r, s` are the roots of
`2x² - (p + q)x + 3pq + 1 = 0`, i.e. `2(r + s) = p + q` and `2rs = 3pq + 1`.
