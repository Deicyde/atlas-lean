# Girstmair's coefficient at index (2, 2)

**Girstmair's coefficient at index (2, 2).** Girstmair's cotangent power coefficient
$c_{2,2}$ equals $-1$, matching the identity $(i \cot)^2 = 1 - i^2 \cot'$ with
$\cot' = -1 - \cot^2$.
