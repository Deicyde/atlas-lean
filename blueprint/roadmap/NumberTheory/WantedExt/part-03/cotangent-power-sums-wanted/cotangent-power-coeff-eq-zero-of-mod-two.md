# Cotangent Power Coeff eq zero of mod two ne

Parity of Girstmair's coefficients: `c_{r,j} = 0` when `1 ≤ j ≤ r` and `r ≢ j (mod 2)`.
The source deduces this from the `ℚ`-linear independence of the functions `i^j cot^{(j-1)}`.
