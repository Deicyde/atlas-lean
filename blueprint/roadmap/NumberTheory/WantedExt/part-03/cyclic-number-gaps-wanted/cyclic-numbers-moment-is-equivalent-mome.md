# Cyclic Numbers moment is Equivalent moment rpow

Campbell and Cohen, Theorem 1, relation between moments: for `j ≥ 1` and every `k`,
`μ'(c,n,k) ~ (j+1)^{k/j} / (k+1) · μ'(c,n,j)^{k/j}` as `n → ∞`.
