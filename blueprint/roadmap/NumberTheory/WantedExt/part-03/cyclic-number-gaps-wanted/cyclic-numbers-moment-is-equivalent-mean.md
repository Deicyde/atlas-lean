# Cyclic Numbers moment is Equivalent mean pow

Campbell and Cohen, Theorem 1, generalized Taylor's law: for every `k`,
`μ'(c,n,k) ~ 2^k / (1+k) · m(c,n)^k` as `n → ∞`.
