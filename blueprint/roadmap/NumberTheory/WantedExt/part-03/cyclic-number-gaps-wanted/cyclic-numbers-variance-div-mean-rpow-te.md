# Cyclic Numbers variance div mean rpow tendsto

Campbell and Cohen, Theorem 1, display (5) (Taylor's law): `v(c,n) / m(c,n)^b` tends to
`∞` if `b < 2`, to `1/3` if `b = 2`, and to `0` if `b > 2`.
