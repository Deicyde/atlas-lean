# The divisor sum of $\chi_4$ at one

**The divisor sum of $\chi_4$ at one.** For the nonprincipal Dirichlet character $\chi_4$
modulo 4, the sum $\sum_{d \mid 1} \chi_4(d)$ over the divisors of $1$ equals $1$.
