# Weak approximation for the rationals

Let $S$ be a finite set of places of $\mathbb{Q}$ and, for each $v \in S$, let $x_v \in \mathbb{Q}_v$. Then
for every $\epsilon > 0$ there is $x \in \mathbb{Q}$ with $|x - x_v|_v < \epsilon$ for every $v \in S$,
where $|\cdot|_v$ is the norm of the completion $\mathbb{Q}_v$: the usual absolute value on
$\mathbb{Q}_\infty = \mathbb{R}$ and the $p$-adic norm on $\mathbb{Q}_p$.

Equivalently, the image of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\prod_{v \in S} \mathbb{Q}_v$ under the diagonal map
$q \mapsto (q)_{v \in S}$ is dense in the product topology.
