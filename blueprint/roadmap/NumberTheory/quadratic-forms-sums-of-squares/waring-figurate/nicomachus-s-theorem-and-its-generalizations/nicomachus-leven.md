# The even-case cube sum $L_{m,e}$

**The even-case cube sum $L_{m,e}$.** For a natural number $m$ and a real number $x$,

$$L_{m,e}(x) = \sum_{j=1}^{m} (2j - 1)^3 + x^3 + \sum_{j=1}^{m} (2j + jx)^3.$$
