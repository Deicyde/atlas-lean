# Lucas tetrahedral numbers

**Lucas tetrahedral numbers.** For nondegenerate Lucas parameters $(s, t)$ and a natural
number $n$, the $n$-th Lucas tetrahedral number is the Lucasnomial coefficient
$\binom{n+2}{3}_{s,t}$.
