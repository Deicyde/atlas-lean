# Lucas pentachoron numbers

**Lucas pentachoron numbers.** For nondegenerate Lucas parameters $(s, t)$ and a natural
number $n$, the $n$-th Lucas pentachoron number is the Lucasnomial coefficient
$\binom{n+3}{4}_{s,t}$.
