# Lucas triangular numbers

**Lucas triangular numbers.** For nondegenerate Lucas parameters $(s, t)$ and a natural
number $n$, the $n$-th Lucas triangular number is the Lucasnomial coefficient
$\binom{n+1}{2}_{s,t}$.
