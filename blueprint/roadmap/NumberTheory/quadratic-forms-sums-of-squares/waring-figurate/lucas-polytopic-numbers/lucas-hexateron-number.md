# Lucas hexateron numbers

**Lucas hexateron numbers.** For nondegenerate Lucas parameters $(s, t)$ and a natural
number $n$, the $n$-th Lucas hexateron number is the Lucasnomial coefficient
$\binom{n+4}{5}_{s,t}$.
