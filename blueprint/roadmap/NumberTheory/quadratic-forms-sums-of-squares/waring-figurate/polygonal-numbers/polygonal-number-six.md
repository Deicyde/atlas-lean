# Closed form of the hexagonal numbers

**Closed form of the hexagonal numbers.** For every natural number $n$, the $n$-th hexagonal
number is $p_6(n) = 2n^2 - n$, where $p_s(n)$ is the $n$-th $s$-gonal number.
