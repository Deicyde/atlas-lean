# Euler polynomials

**Euler polynomials.** Let $B_m(x)$ denote the $m$-th Bernoulli polynomial. For $n \ge 0$,
the Euler polynomial $E_n(x)$ is the polynomial with rational coefficients
$E_n(x) = \frac{2}{n + 1} \left(B_{n+1}(x) - 2^{n+1} B_{n+1}(x/2)\right)$.
