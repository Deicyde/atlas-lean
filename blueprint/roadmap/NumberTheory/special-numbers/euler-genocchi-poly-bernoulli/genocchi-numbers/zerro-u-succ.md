# Recurrence for the Zerroukhat–Khaldi sums $U$

**Recurrence for the Zerroukhat–Khaldi sums $U$.** Let $U(m, k)$ be the Zerroukhat–Khaldi
sum. For all natural numbers $r$ and $k$ with $k \ge 1$,
$U(r+1, k) = U(r, k+1) + k \, U(r, k)$.
