# Bounds for $1/a + 1/b - 1/(a+b)$

**Bounds for $1/a + 1/b - 1/(a+b)$.** Let $a$ and $b$ be natural numbers with $a, b \ge 1$
and $a + b \ge 3$, and put $g(a,b) = 1/a + 1/b - 1/(a+b)$. Then $0 < g(a,b) \le 7/6$.
