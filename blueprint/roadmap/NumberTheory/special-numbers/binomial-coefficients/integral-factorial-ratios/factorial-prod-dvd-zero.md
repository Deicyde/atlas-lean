# Scaled factorial products at index zero divide

**Scaled factorial products at index zero divide.** For all lists $A$ and $B$ of natural
numbers, $\prod_{b \in B} (b \cdot 0)!$ divides $\prod_{a \in A} (a \cdot 0)!$, since both
products equal $1$.
