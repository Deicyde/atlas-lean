# Exponent vector of $x^n y^n z^n$

**Exponent vector of $x^n y^n z^n$.** For a natural number $n$, the exponent vector assigning
exponent $n$ to each of the three variables $x, y, z$, that is, the monomial $x^n y^n z^n$.
