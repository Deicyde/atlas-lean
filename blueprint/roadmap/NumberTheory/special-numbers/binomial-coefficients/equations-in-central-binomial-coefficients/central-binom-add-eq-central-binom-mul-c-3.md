# Central binomial products with $x = 1$

**Central binomial products with $x = 1$.** Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers. Then
$\binom{2(a+b)}{a+b} = \binom{2a}{a}\binom{1+2b}{b}$ if and only if $a = 1$.
