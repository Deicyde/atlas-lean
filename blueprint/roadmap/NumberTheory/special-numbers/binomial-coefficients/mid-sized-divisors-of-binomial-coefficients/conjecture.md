# Erdős Problem 387

**Erdős Problem 387.** The proposition that there is a real number $c > 0$ such that for all
natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $1 \le k < n$, the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has a
divisor $d$ with $cn < d \le n$.
