# Golden factorial exponent of $1$

**Golden factorial exponent of $1$.** Let $\varphi$ be the golden ratio and, for a natural
number $n$, let $\alpha(n)$ be the least positive integer $m$ with $n! \le \varphi^m$. Then
$\alpha(1) = 1$.
