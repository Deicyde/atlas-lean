# First values of the alternating-sign Fibonacci sequence

**First values of the alternating-sign Fibonacci sequence.** The alternating-sign Fibonacci
sequence $d$ satisfies $d_0 = 0$, $d_1 = 1$, $d_2 = 2$, $d_3 = 2$ and $d_4 = 5$.
