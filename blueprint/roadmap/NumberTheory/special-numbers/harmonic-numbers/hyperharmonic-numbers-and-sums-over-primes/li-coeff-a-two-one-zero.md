# The coefficient $a(2, 1, 0)$

**The coefficient $a(2, 1, 0)$.** The coefficient $a(2, 1, 0)$ of Li's Lemma 3 equals $-1$.

By the recursion, $a(2, 1, 0) = -a(1, 0, 0) = -1$.
