# Li's coefficient $c(0, 0)$

**Li's coefficient $c(0, 0)$.** Li's coefficient $c(0, 0)$ equals $\frac{1}{2}$ (this is the
Bernoulli number $B_1$ in the convention $B_1 = +\frac{1}{2}$).
