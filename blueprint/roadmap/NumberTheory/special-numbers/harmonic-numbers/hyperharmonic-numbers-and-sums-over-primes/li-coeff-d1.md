# Li's coefficients $d_1(m, j)$

**Li's coefficients $d_1(m, j)$.** For natural numbers $m$ and $j$,
$d_1(m, j) = \sum_{k=j}^{m} \binom{k}{j} (-1)^{k-j} c_1(m, k)$, where $c_1$ denotes Li's
coefficients $c_1(m, k)$; in particular $d_1(m, j) = 0$ when $j > m$.
