# Generalized harmonic numbers

**Generalized harmonic numbers.** For a real number $s$ and a natural number $n$, the
generalized harmonic number of order $s$ is $H_n^{(s)} = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 1/k^s$, where $k^s$ is the
real power.
