# Fibonacci Jordan quotient at zero

**Fibonacci Jordan quotient at zero.** The Jordan-type quotient $J_{r,F} = F_{2r+1}! / F_r!$
of Fibonacci factorials takes the value $J_{0,F} = F_1! / F_0! = 1$.
