# Fibonacci factorial at three

**Fibonacci factorial at three.** The Fibonacci factorial $F_3! = F_1 F_2 F_3$ of the
Fibonacci numbers $F_1, F_2, F_3$ equals $2$, since $F_1 = F_2 = 1$ and $F_3 = 2$.
