# Cross-product identity for Lucas polynomials

**Cross-product identity for Lucas polynomials.** For every natural number $n$, the Lucas
polynomials $\{k\}$ in $s$ and $t$ satisfy
$\{2n\}\{n + 2\} - \{2n + 2\}\{n\} = (-1)^n \{2\} t^n \{n\}$.
