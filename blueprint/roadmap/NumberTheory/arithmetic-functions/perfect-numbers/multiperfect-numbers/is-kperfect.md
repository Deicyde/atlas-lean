# $k$-perfect numbers

**$k$-perfect numbers.** For natural numbers $k$ and $n$, the number $n$ is $k$-perfect when
$n > 0$ and $\sigma(n) = kn$, where $\sigma(n)$ is the sum of the positive divisors of $n$.
