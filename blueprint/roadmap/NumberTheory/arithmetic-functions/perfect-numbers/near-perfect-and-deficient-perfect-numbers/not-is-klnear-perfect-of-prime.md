# Primes are not $(k,\ell)$-near-perfect

**Primes are not $[k,\ell]$-near-perfect.** Let $k$ and $\ell$ be natural numbers with
$\ell \ge 1$ and let $p$ be a prime. Then $p$ is not $[k,\ell]$-near-perfect.
