# Twice an odd prime is not $(k,\ell)$-near-perfect

**Twice an odd prime is not $[k,\ell]$-near-perfect.** Let $k$ and $\ell$ be natural numbers
with $\ell \ge 1$ and let $p$ be an odd prime. Then $2p$ is not $[k,\ell]$-near-perfect.
