# $2$ is $(k,1)$-deficient-perfect

**$2$ is $[k,1]$-deficient-perfect.** For every natural number $k$, the number $2$ is
$[k,1]$-deficient-perfect.

The divisor is $d = 1$, since $1 + 2^k + 1^k = 2^k + 1 \cdot 2$.
