# No $(2,\ell)$-near-perfect number of the form $2^a p$

**No $[2,\ell]$-near-perfect number of the form $2^a p$.** Let $\ell$ and $a$ be natural
numbers with $\ell \ge 1$, and let $p$ be an odd prime. Then $2^a p$ is not
$[2,\ell]$-near-perfect.
