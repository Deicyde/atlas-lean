# $3 \cdot 3$ is a $2$-Lehmer factorization

**$3 \cdot 3$ is a $2$-Lehmer factorization.** The spoof factorization $3^1 \cdot 3^1$ is a
$2$-Lehmer factorization; indeed $2 \cdot (3 - 1)(3 - 1) = 3 \cdot 3 - 1$.
