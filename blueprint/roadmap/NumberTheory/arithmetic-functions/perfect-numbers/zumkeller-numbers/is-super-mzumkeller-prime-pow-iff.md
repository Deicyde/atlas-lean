# Super-m-Zumkeller prime powers

**Super-m-Zumkeller prime powers.** Let $p$ be a prime and $\alpha \ge 1$ an integer. Then
$p^\alpha$ is super-m-Zumkeller if and only if
$\alpha \equiv 0, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13$ or $15 \pmod{16}$.
