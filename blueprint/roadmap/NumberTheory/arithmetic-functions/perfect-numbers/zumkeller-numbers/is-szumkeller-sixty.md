# $60$ is s-Zumkeller

**$60$ is s-Zumkeller.** The number $60$ is s-Zumkeller: the squares of its proper divisors
$1, 2, 5, 30$ sum to $1^2 + 2^2 + 5^2 + 30^2 = 930$, which is half of the sum of the squares of
all proper divisors of $60$.
