# Consecutive polynomial family

**Consecutive polynomial family.** Let $k$ be a natural number. A family
$L_0, \dots, L_{k-1}$ of polynomials in $\mathbb{Z}[X]$ is consecutive when
$L_{i+1} - L_i = 1$ for every $i$ with $i + 1 < k$.
