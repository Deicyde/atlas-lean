# $k$-full numbers

**$k$-full numbers.** Let $k$ be a natural number. A natural number $n$ is $k$-full if
$n \ne 0$ and $p^k$ divides $n$ for every prime $p$ dividing $n$.

The definition makes sense for every $k$; the source considers only $k \ge 2$.
