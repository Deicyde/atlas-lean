# A logarithmic-integral sum

**A logarithmic-integral sum.** For every real $\beta > 0$,

$$\sum_{i=2}^{m} \frac{i^{\beta - 1}}{\log i} \sim \frac{m^\beta}{\beta \log m}$$

as $m \to \infty$ through the natural numbers.
