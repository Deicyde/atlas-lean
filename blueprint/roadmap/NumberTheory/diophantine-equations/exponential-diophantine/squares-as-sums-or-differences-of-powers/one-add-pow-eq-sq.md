# The case $x = 0$ of $n^x + (5p)^y = z^2$

**The case $x = 0$ of $n^x + (5p)^y = z^2$.** Let $p$ be a prime with $p \ne 2$. For natural
numbers $y$ and $z$, $1 + (5p)^y = z^2$ holds if and only if $y = 1$ and $z^2 = 5p + 1$.
