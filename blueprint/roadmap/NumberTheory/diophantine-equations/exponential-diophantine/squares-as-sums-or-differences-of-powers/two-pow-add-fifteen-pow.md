# Solutions of $2^x + 15^y = z^2$

**Solutions of $2^x + 15^y = z^2$.** The natural numbers $x$, $y$, $z$ with
$2^x + 15^y = z^2$ are exactly $(x, y, z) = (3, 0, 3)$, $(0, 1, 4)$ and $(6, 2, 17)$.
