# Squaring $(3/5, 4/5)$ under Amato's product

**Squaring $(3/5, 4/5)$ under Amato's product.** For Amato's product
$(x, y) \odot (x', y') = (x x' / (1 + y y'), (y + y') / (1 + y y'))$ on $\mathbb{Q}^2$, we have
$(3/5, 4/5) \odot (3/5, 4/5) = (9/41, 40/41)$.
