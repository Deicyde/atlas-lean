# At most $2(N - 1)$ solutions with $c - a = N$

**At most $2(N - 1)$ solutions with $c - a = N$.** For every integer $N > 1$,
there are at most $2(N - 1)$ solutions $(a, b, c)$ of the sum of consecutive squares problem
with $c - a = N$.
