# The matrix $Q_{1;\ell}$

**The matrix $Q_{1;\ell}$.** For a natural number $k$ and an integer $\ell$, $Q_{1;\ell}$ is
the $2 \times 2$ integer matrix

$$\begin{pmatrix} k + \ell + 1 & 1 \\ k^2 - \ell^2 + 3k + \ell + 1 & k - \ell + 2 \end{pmatrix}.$$
