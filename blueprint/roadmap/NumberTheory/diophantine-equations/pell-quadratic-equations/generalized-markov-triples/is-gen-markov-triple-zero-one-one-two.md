# The Markov triple $(1, 1, 2)$

**The Markov triple $(1, 1, 2)$.** The triple $(1, 1, 2)$ is a $0$-generalized Markov
triple, that is, a classical Markov triple: $1^2 + 1^2 + 2^2 = 3 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 2$.
