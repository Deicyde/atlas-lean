# The fraction $1/1$ labels $k + 2$

**The fraction $1/1$ labels $k + 2$.** For every natural number $k$, the number $k + 2$
carries the label $1/1$ in the fraction labeling of $k$-generalized Markov numbers, that is,
$m_{1/1} = k + 2$.
