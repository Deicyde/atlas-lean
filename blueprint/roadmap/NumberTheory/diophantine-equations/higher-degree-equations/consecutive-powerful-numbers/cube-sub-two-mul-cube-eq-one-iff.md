# Integer solutions of $u^3 - 2v^3 = 1$

**Integer solutions of $u^3 - 2v^3 = 1$.** For all integers $u$ and $v$, $u^3 - 2v^3 = 1$ if
and only if $(u, v) = (1, 0)$ or $(u, v) = (-1, -1)$.
