# The inclusion-exclusion-like sum in two variables

**The inclusion-exclusion-like sum in two variables.** For every function
$F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{Z}$ and all real numbers $x$ and $y$,
$g^{(F)}(x, y) = F(x) + F(y) - F(x + y)$.
