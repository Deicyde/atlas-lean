# The Baker–Wüstholz constant

**The Baker–Wüstholz constant.** For natural numbers $n$ and $d$, the Baker–Wüstholz constant
is the real number
$C(n, d) = 18 \cdot (n + 1)! \cdot n^{n+1} \cdot (32 d)^{n+2} \cdot \log(2nd)$.
