# Bounds for the 2-complexity of $2^m \cdot 10^5$

**Bounds for the 2-complexity of $2^m \cdot 10^5$.** Write $\|n\|_2$ for the $2$-complexity
of $n$. For every natural number $m$, one has $m + 19 \le \|2^m \cdot 10^5\|_2 \le m + 20$.
