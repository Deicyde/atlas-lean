# The $l$-complexity of $l^m + l^{m'}$

**The $l$-complexity of $l^m + l^{m'}$.** Write $\|n\|_l$ for the $l$-complexity of $n$.
For natural numbers $l > 1$ and $m \ge m' \ge 1$, $\|l^m + l^{m'}\|_l = m + 1$.
