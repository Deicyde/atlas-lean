# Initial value of the $(k, l)$-Göbel sequence

**Initial value of the $(k, l)$-Göbel sequence.** For all natural numbers $k$ and $l$, the
$(k, l)$-Göbel sequence $g_{k,l}$ satisfies $g_{k,l}(1) = l$.
