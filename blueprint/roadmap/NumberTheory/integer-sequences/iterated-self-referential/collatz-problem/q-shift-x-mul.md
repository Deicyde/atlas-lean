# Dividing a multiple of $q$ by $q$

**Dividing a multiple of $q$ by $q$.** For every power series $y \in \mathbb{F}_2[[q]]$,
applying the shift $\sum_n x_n q^n \mapsto \sum_n x_{n+1} q^n$ to $qy$ gives back $y$.
