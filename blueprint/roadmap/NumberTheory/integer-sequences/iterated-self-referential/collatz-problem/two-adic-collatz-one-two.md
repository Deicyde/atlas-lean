# The $2$-adic $3x+1$ map swaps $1$ and $2$

**The $2$-adic $3x+1$ map swaps $1$ and $2$.** The $2$-adic $3x+1$ map
$T : \mathbb{Z}_2 \to \mathbb{Z}_2$ satisfies $T(1) = 2$ and $T(2) = 1$.
