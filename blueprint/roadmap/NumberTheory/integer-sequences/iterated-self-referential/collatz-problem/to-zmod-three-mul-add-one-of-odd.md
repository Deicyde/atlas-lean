# $3x + 1$ is even for odd $2$-adic $x$

**$3x + 1$ is even for odd $2$-adic $x$.** For every $2$-adic integer $x \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ whose
residue modulo $2$ is nonzero, the residue of $3x + 1$ modulo $2$ is zero.
