# Divisibility by $2$ from the residue modulo $2$

**Divisibility by $2$ from the residue modulo $2$.** If the residue modulo $2$ of a $2$-adic
integer $y \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ is zero, then $2$ divides $y$ in $\mathbb{Z}_2$.
