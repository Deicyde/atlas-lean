# Integral of $\exp(Lx)$

**Integral of $\exp(Lx)$.** For real numbers $L \ne 0$, $a$ and $b$,

$$\int_a^b \exp(Lx)\,dx = \frac{\exp(Lb) - \exp(La)}{L},$$

where the integral is oriented: $\int_a^b = -\int_b^a$ when $a > b$.
