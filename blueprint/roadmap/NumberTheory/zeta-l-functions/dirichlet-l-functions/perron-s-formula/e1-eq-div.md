# The averaging operator as a difference quotient

**The averaging operator as a difference quotient.** Let $E_1(u) = \int_0^1 e^{tu}\,dt$ for
$u \in \mathbb{C}$. Then $E_1(u) = (e^u - 1)/u$ for every nonzero complex number $u$.
