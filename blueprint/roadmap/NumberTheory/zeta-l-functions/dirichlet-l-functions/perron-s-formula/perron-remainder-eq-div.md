# The Perron remainder as a difference quotient

**The Perron remainder as a difference quotient.** For every real $y > 0$ and every complex
$s \neq 0$, the Perron remainder $R_y$ satisfies $R_y(s) = (y^s - 1)/s$, where
$y^s = e^{s \log y}$.
