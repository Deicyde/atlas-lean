# The coefficient of $q$ in $\zeta_q^{SZ,\star}(1)$

**The coefficient of $q$ in $\zeta_q^{SZ,\star}(1)$.** The coefficient of $q$ in the SZ-star
qMZV $\zeta_q^{SZ,\star}(1) = \sum_{m > 0} q^m / (1 - q^m)$ is $1$.
