# The Okounkov polynomials $p_2$ and $p_3$

**The Okounkov polynomials $p_2$ and $p_3$.** The Okounkov polynomials of index $2$ and $3$
are $p_2(X) = X$ and $p_3(X) = X(1 + X)$.
