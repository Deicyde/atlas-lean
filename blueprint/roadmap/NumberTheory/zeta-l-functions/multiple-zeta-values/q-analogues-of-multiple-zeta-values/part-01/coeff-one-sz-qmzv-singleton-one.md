# The coefficient of $q$ in $\zeta_q^{\mathrm{SZ}}(1)$

**The coefficient of $q$ in $\zeta_q^{\mathrm{SZ}}(1)$.** The coefficient of $q$ in the
Schlesinger–Zudilin qMZV $\zeta_q^{\mathrm{SZ}}(1) = \sum_{m > 0} q^m / (1 - q^m)$ is $1$.
