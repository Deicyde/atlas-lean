# Words of $\mathfrak{H}^0$

**Words of $\mathfrak{H}^0$.** A word $w$ in the two letters $x$ and $y$ is an
$\mathfrak{H}^0$-word when $w$ is empty or $w = xuy$ for some word $u$, that is, when $w$ is
empty or begins with $x$ and ends in $y$.
