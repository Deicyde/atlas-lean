# Unfolding the greatest common monic divisor

**Unfolding the greatest common monic divisor.** For polynomials $h$, $g_1$ and $g_2$
over a commutative ring $R$, $h$ is a greatest common monic divisor of $g_1$ and $g_2$ if and
only if $h$, $g_1$ and $g_2$ are monic and the ideal of $R[X]$ generated by $g_1$ and $g_2$
equals the principal ideal generated by $h$.
