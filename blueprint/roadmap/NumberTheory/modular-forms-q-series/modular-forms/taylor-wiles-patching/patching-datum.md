# Taylor–Wiles patching datum

**Taylor–Wiles patching datum.** A Taylor–Wiles patching datum: a patching level for every `n`, with the
relation ideal at level `n` generated by `(1 + Xⱼ)^(ℓⁿ) - 1`.
This structure records an indexed family of finite-level data.
