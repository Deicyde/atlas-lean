# Reduction mod p maps Γ(N) onto SL(2, ℤ/pℤ) when p does not divide N

**Reduction mod p maps Γ(N) onto SL(2, ℤ/pℤ) when p does not divide N.** If `p` does not divide `N`, then reduction modulo `p` maps `Γ(N)` onto
`SL(2, ZMod p)`.
