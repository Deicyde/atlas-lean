# 1 is a strict period of Γ₀(N)

**1 is a strict period of Γ₀(N).** `1` is a strict period of `Γ₀(N)`: the translation matrix `T` lies in
`Γ₀(N)`, and the strict periods then contain the integer multiples of `1`.
