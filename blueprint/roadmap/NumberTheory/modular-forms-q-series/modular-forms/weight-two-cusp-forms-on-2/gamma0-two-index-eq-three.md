# Γ₀(2) has index 3 in SL(2, ℤ)

**Γ₀(2) has index 3 in SL(2, ℤ).** The index of `Γ₀(2)` in `SL(2, ℤ)` is 3: the cosets are classified by
the first column mod 2, and there are three nonzero vectors in `(ZMod 2)²`.
