# Norm trick for modular forms

**Norm trick for modular forms.** The norm trick: two modular forms satisfying a commutation relation under
the slash action of `ℋ` give rise to a modular form `Φ` of level `ℋ` with
`Φ * G = F * norm ℋ G`.
