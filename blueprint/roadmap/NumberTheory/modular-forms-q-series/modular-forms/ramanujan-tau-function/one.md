# Normalisation of the Ramanujan τ-function

**Normalisation of the Ramanujan τ-function.** The Ramanujan τ-function satisfies
$\tau(1) = 1$: the coefficient of $q$ in the $q$-expansion of the modular discriminant $\Delta$
is $1$.
