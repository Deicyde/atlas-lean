# Hecke operators on weight-two cusp forms of level $N$: the steps

Shimura's [Hecke operators $T_n$](hecke-operators-of-level-n.md) on the cusp forms of weight $2$ for a
group $\Gamma$ of level $N$, $\Gamma_1(N) \subset \Gamma \subset \Gamma_0(N)$, and the steps of their
construction: a double coset $\Gamma \alpha \Gamma$ of $\alpha \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q})$ with positive
determinant is a finite union of right cosets, the double coset operator $[\Gamma \alpha \Gamma]_2$, the sum of
the slashes by their representatives, maps $S_2(\Gamma)$ into itself, and the double cosets in the
definition of $T_q$ are finitely many. The normalization of $[\Gamma \alpha \Gamma]_2$ is that of Shimura's
*Introduction*, §3.4, which his relation (1.3) of 1973 matches with the Hecke endomorphisms of the Jacobian
([Hecke operators on cusp forms and Hecke endomorphisms of the Jacobian](../../../../AlgebraicGeometry/algebraic-curves/shimura-construction-of-the-abelian-variety/hecke-operators-and-hecke-endomorphisms-under-mu.md)).
