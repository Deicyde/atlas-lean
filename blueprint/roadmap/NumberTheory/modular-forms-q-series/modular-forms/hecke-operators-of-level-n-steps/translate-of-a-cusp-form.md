# The translate of a cusp form

Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, $f$ a
[cusp form](../cusp-form.md) of weight $k$ for $\mathcal{G}$, and $g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$. Then the
translate $f \mid_k g$, for the weight-$k$ slash action of the [cusp form](../cusp-form.md) page, is a
cusp form of weight $k$ for the conjugate group $g^{-1} \mathcal{G} g$: it is invariant under
$g^{-1} \mathcal{G} g$, holomorphic, and vanishes at every cusp of $g^{-1} \mathcal{G} g$.
