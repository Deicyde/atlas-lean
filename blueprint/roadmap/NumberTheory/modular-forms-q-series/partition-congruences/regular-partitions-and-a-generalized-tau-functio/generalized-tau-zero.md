# Generalized tau function at zero

**Generalized tau function at zero.** For every integer $k$, $\tau_k(0) = 0$, where $\tau_k$
is the generalized tau function: the series $q \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^m)^k$ has no constant term.
