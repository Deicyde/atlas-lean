# Theorem 5.15 (Vélu)

Let $E: y^2 = x^3 + Ax + B$ be an elliptic curve over $k$ and let $G$ be a
 finite subgroup of $E(\bar{k})$ of odd order. For each nonzero $Q = (x_Q,
 y_Q)$ in $G$ define $t_Q \coloneqq 3x_Q^2 + A$, $u_Q \coloneqq 2y_Q^2$, $w_Q
 \coloneqq u_Q + t_Q x_Q$ and let $t \coloneqq \sum_{Q \in G_{\neq 0}} t_Q$,
 $w \coloneqq \sum_{Q \in G_{\neq 0}} w_Q$, $r(x) \coloneqq x + \sum_{Q \in
 G_{\neq 0}} \left(\frac{t_Q}{x - x_Q} + \frac{u_Q}{(x - x_Q)^2}\right)$. The
 rational map $\phi(x, y) \coloneqq (r(x), r'(x) y)$ is a separable isogeny
 from $E$ to $E': y^2 = x^3 + A'x + B'$, where $A' \coloneqq A - 5t$ and $B'
 \coloneqq B - 7w$, with $\ker \phi = G$.
