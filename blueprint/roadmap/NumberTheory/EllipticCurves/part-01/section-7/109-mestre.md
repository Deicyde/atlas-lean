# Theorem 7.7 (Mestre)

Let $p>229$ be prime, and let $E/\mathbb{F}_{p}$ be an elliptic curve with
 quadratic twist $\widetilde{E}/\mathbb{F}_{p}$. At least one of the integers
 $\lambda(E(\mathbb{F}_{p}))$ and $\lambda(\widetilde{E}(\mathbb{F}_{p}))$
 has a unique multiple in the Hasse interval
 $\mathcal{H}(p)=[(\sqrt{p}-1)^{2},(\sqrt{p}+1)^{2}]$.
