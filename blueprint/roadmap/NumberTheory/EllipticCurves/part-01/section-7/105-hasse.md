# Theorem 7.3 (Hasse)

Let $E/\mathbb{F}_q$ be an elliptic curve over a finite field. Then $\#
 E(\mathbb{F}_q)=q+1-t$ where $t\coloneqq\operatorname{tr} \pi_E$ is the
 trace of the Frobenius endomorphism $\pi_E$ and $|t| \leq 2\sqrt{q}$.
