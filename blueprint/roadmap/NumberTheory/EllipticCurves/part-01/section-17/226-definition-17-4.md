# Definition 17.4

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order in a number field $K$ and let $\mathfrak{a}$ be
 a nonzero $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. The (absolute) norm of the ideal
 $\mathfrak{a}$ is $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{a} \coloneqq [\mathcal{O} :
 \mathfrak{a}] = \# \mathcal{O}/\mathfrak{a} \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.
