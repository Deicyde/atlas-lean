# Definition 17.13

Let $E / k$ be any elliptic curve with CM by an imaginary quadratic order
 $\mathcal{O}$, and let $\mathfrak{a}$ be an $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. The
 $\mathfrak{a}$-torsion subgroup of $E$ is defined by
 $E[\mathfrak{a}]\coloneqq\{P \in E(\bar{k}) \mid \alpha(P)=0 \text{ for all
 } \alpha \in \mathfrak{a}\}$ where we are viewing each $\alpha \in
 \mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathcal{O} \simeq \operatorname{End}(E)$ as an
 endomorphism.
