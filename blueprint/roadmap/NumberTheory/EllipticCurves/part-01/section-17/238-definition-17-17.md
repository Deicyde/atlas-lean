# Definition 17.17

A negative integer $D$ that is a square modulo 4 is an (imaginary quadratic)
 discriminant. Discriminants not of the form $u^{2} D'$ for some integer
 $u>1$ and discriminant $D'$ are said to be fundamental. Every discriminant
 can be written uniquely as the product of a square and a fundamental
 discriminant.
