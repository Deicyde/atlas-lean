# Corollary 17.11

The ideal class group $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ is the group of
 invertible fractional $\mathcal{O}$-ideals modulo its subgroup of principal
 fractional $\mathcal{O}$-ideals (in particular
 $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ is a group).
