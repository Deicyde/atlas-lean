# Corollary 17.7

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order in a number field, let $\alpha \in \mathcal{O}$
 be nonzero, and let $\mathfrak{a}$ be a nonzero $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. Then
 $\mathrm{N}(\alpha \mathfrak{a}) = \mathrm{N}(\alpha)
 \mathrm{N}\mathfrak{a}$.
