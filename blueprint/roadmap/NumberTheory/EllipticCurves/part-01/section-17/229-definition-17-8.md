# Definition 17.8

Let $\mathfrak{b} = \frac{1}{b} \mathfrak{a}$ be a nonzero fractional ideal
 in an order $\mathcal{O}$ of a number field, with $b \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.
 The (absolute) norm of $\mathfrak{b}$ is $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{b} \coloneqq
 \frac{\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{a}}{\mathrm{N} b} \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}^{\times}$.
