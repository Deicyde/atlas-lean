# Theorem 14.24

Suppose $\{f_{n}\}$ is a sequence of functions holomorphic on an open set
 $\Omega$, and that $\{f_{n}\}$ converges to a function $f$ uniformly on
 every compact subset of $\Omega$. Then $f$ is holomorphic on $\Omega$.
