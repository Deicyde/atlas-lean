# Theorem 14.28

Let $L$ be a lattice. The Laurent series expansion for $\wp(z)=\wp(z ; L)$ at
 $z=0$ is given by $$\wp(z)=\frac{1}{z^{2}}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(2 n+1) G_{2
 n+2}(L) z^{2 n}$$ where $G_{k}(L)$ denotes the Eisenstein series of weight
 $k$.
