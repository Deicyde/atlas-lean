# Definition 15.7

The $j$-function $j: \mathbb{H} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is defined by
 $j(\tau)=j([1, \tau])$. We similarly define $g_{2}(\tau)=g_{2}([1, \tau])$,
 $g_{3}(\tau)=g_{3}([1, \tau])$, and $\Delta(\tau)=\Delta([1, \tau])$.
