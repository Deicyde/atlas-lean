# Definition 15.2

The $j$-invariant of a lattice $L$ is defined by $$j(L)=1728
 \frac{g_{2}(L)^{3}}{\Delta(L)}=1728 \frac{g_{2}(L)^{3}}{g_{2}(L)^{3}-27
 g_{3}(L)^{2}}$$
