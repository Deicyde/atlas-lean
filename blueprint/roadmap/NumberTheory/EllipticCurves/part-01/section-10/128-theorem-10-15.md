# Theorem 10.15

Let $E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b$ be an elliptic curve over a field $k$. Suppose
 $E(k)$ contains a point $P$ of order 4, and let $2P = (x_0, 0)$. Then
 $3x_0^2 + a$ is a square in $k$ and $E$ can be put in Montgomery form $E':
 By^2 = x^3 + Ax^2 + x$ by setting $B = 1/\sqrt{3x_0^2 + a}$ and $A = 3x_0
 B$; the map $(x, y) \mapsto (B(x - x_0), By)$ defines an isomorphism from
 $E$ to $E'$.
