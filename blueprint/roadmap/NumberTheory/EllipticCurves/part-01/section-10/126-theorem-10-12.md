# Theorem 10.12

Assume $4a^3 + 27b^2$ is not divisible by $N$, and let $P_1$ and $P_2$ be the
 reductions of $P$ modulo distinct primes $p_1$ and $p_2$ dividing $N$, with
 $p_1 \leq M$. Suppose $|P_1|$ is $\ell_1$-smooth and $|P_2|$ is not, for
 some prime $\ell_1 \leq B$. Then Algorithm 10.11 succeeds.
