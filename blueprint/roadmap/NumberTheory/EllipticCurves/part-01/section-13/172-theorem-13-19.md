# Theorem 13.19

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a field $k$ of characteristic $p$ for which
 $\operatorname{End}^{0}\left(E_{\bar{k}}\right)$ is a quaternion algebra.
 Then $E$ is supersingular.
