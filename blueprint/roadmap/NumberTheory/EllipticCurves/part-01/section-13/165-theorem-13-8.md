# Theorem 13.8

Let $E/\mathbb{F}_{q}$ be an elliptic curve for which
 $\operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ is an imaginary quadratic field $K$ with ring of
 integers $\mathcal{O}_{K}$. Then $\mathbb{Z}[\pi_{E}] \subseteq
 \operatorname{End}(E) \subseteq \mathcal{O}_{K}$, and the conductor of
 $\operatorname{End}(E)$ divides $[\mathcal{O}_{K} : \mathbb{Z}[\pi_{E}]]$.
