# Theorem 13.13

Elliptic curves $E: y^{2}=x^{3}+Ax+B$ and $E^{\prime}:
 y^{2}=x^{3}+A^{\prime}x+B^{\prime}$ defined over $k$ are isomorphic (over
 $k$) if and only if $A^{\prime}=\mu^{4} A$ and $B^{\prime}=\mu^{6} B$, for
 some $\mu \in k^{\times}$.
