# Corollary 13.20

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a finite field of characteristic $p$.
 Either $E$ is supersingular, $\operatorname{tr} \pi_{E} \equiv 0 \bmod p$,
 and $\operatorname{End}^{0}\left(E_{\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}\right)$ is a
 quaternion algebra, or $E$ is ordinary, $\operatorname{tr} \pi_{E}
 \not\equiv 0 \bmod p$, and
 $\operatorname{End}^{0}\left(E_{\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}\right)=\operatorname{End}^{0}\left(E_{\mathbb{F}_{q}}\right)$
 is an imaginary quadratic field.
