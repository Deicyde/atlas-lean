# Theorem 6.20

Let $\alpha$ be an endomorphism of an elliptic curve $E/k$ and let $n$ be a
 positive integer prime to the characteristic of $k$. Then $\operatorname{tr}
 \alpha \equiv \operatorname{tr} \alpha_n \bmod n$ and $\operatorname{deg}
 \alpha \equiv \operatorname{det} \alpha_n \bmod n$.
