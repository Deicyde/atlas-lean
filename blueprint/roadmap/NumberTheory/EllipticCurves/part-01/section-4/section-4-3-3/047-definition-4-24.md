# Definition 4.24

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_{q}$. The
 Frobenius endomorphism of $E$ is the map $\pi_{E}:(x: y: z) \mapsto (x^{q}:
 y^{q}: z^{q})$.
