# Corollary 16.7

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve defined over $\mathbb{C}$. Then
 $\operatorname{End}(E)$ is commutative and therefore isomorphic to either
 $\mathbb{Z}$ or an order in an imaginary quadratic field.
