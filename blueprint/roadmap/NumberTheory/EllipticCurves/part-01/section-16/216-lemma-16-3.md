# Lemma 16.3

Let $L \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be a lattice. The following hold: (i)
 $\mathbb{C}(L)=\mathbb{C}(\wp, \wp')$; (ii)
 $\mathbb{C}(L)^{\text{even}}=\mathbb{C}(\wp)$; (iii) if $f \in
 \mathbb{C}(L)^{\text{even}}$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{C}-L$ then $f \in
 \mathbb{C}[\wp]$.
