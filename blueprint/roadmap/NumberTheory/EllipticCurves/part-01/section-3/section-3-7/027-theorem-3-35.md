# Theorem 3.35

Let $q = p^e$ be a prime power, and assume that $\log e = O(\log p)$. The
 time to multiply two elements of $\mathbb{F}_q$ is $O(\operatorname{M}(n)) =
 O(n \log n)$, where $n = \lg q$.
