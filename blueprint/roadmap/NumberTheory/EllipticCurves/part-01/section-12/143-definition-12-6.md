# Definition 12.6

Let $\alpha \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$. The (reduced) norm of $\alpha$ is
 $\mathrm{N} \alpha = \alpha \hat{\alpha}$ and the (reduced) trace of
 $\alpha$ is $\mathrm{T} \alpha=\alpha+\hat{\alpha}$.
