# Corollary 12.11

For any nonzero $\alpha \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$, if $\mathrm{T}
 \alpha=0$ then $\alpha^{2}=-\mathrm{N} \alpha<0$. An element $\alpha \in
 \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ is fixed by the Rosati involution if and only if
 $\alpha \in \mathbb{Q}$.
