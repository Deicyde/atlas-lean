# Theorem 6.1

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve and let $p\coloneqq\operatorname{char}(k)$.
 For each prime $\ell$: $E[\ell^{e}] \simeq \begin{cases}
 \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{e}\mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{e}\mathbb{Z} &
 \text{if } \ell \neq p \\ \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{e}\mathbb{Z} \text{ or } \{0\} &
 \text{if } \ell=p \end{cases}$
