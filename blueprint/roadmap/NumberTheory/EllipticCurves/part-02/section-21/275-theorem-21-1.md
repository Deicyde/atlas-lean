# Theorem 21.1

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order of discriminant $D$ and let
 $L$ be the splitting field of $H_{D}(X)$ over
 $K\coloneqq\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$. The map $\Psi: \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)
 \rightarrow \operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ that sends each $\sigma \in
 \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ to the unique $\alpha_{\sigma} \in
 \operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ such that $j(E)^{\sigma}=\alpha_{\sigma}
 j(E)$ for all $j(E) \in \operatorname{Ell}_{\mathcal{O}}(L)$ is a group
 isomorphism compatible with the actions of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ and
 $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ on $\operatorname{Ell}_{\mathcal{O}}(L)$.
