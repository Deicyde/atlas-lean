# Corollary 21.8

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order of discriminant $D$ in an imaginary quadratic
 field $K$. The splitting field $L$ of $H_{D}(X)$ over $K$ is unramified at
 all primes that do not divide the conductor of $\mathcal{O}$. In particular,
 every rational prime $p \nmid D$ is unramified in $L$.
