# Corollary 21.2

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order with discriminant $D$. The
 Hilbert class polynomial $H_{D}(x)$ is irreducible over
 $K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$ and for any elliptic curve $E/\mathbb{C}$ with CM
 by $\mathcal{O}$ the field $K(j(E))$ is a finite abelian extension of $K$
 with $\operatorname{Gal}(K(j(E))/K) \simeq \operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$.
