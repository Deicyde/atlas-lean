# Corollary 21.9

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order with discriminant $D$ and
 ring class field $L$, and let $p \nmid D$ be an odd prime satisfying
 $4p=t^{2}-v^{2}D$. Every $j(E) \in
 \operatorname{Ell}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathbb{C})$ is the $j$-invariant of an
 elliptic curve $E/L$ with good reduction $\bar{E}$ modulo a prime
 $\mathfrak{q}$ of $L$ lying above $p$. Provided $j(\bar{E}) \neq 0, 1728$,
 we have $\operatorname{tr} \pi_{\bar{E}} = \pm t \not\equiv 0 \bmod p$ and
 $\bar{E}$ is ordinary.
