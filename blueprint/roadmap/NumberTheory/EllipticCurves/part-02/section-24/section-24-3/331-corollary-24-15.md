# Corollary 24.15

For any modular form $f \in S_{k}(\Gamma_{0}(1))$ and integers $m \perp n$ we
 have $a_{m}(T_{n} f)=a_{mn}(f)$; in particular, $a_{1}(T_{n} f)=a_{n}(f)$.
