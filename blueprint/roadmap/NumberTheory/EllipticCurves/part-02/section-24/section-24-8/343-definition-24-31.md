# Definition 24.31

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be a semi-stable elliptic curve with minimal discriminant
 $\Delta_{\min}(E)$. The conductor $N_{E}$ of $E$ is the product of the prime
 divisors of $\Delta_{\min}(E)$.
