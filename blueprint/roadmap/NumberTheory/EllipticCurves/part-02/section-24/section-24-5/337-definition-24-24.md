# Definition 24.24

The $L$-function (or $L$-series) of a cusp form $f(\tau)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 a_{n} q^{n}$ of weight $k$ is the complex function defined by the Dirichlet
 series $L(f, s)\coloneqq\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n} n^{-s}$ which converges
 locally uniformly to a holomorphic function on $\operatorname{re}(s)>1+k/2$.
