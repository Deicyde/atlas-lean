# Theorem 24.37 (Eichler-Shimura, Carayol)

Let $f = \sum a_n q^n \in S_2^{\text{new}}(\Gamma_0(N))$ be a newform with
 $a_n \in \mathbb{Z}$. There exists an elliptic curve $E / \mathbb{Q}$ of
 conductor $N$ for which $f_E = f$.
