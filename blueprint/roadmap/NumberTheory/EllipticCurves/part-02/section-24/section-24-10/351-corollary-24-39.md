# Corollary 24.39

Elliptic curves $E, E' / \mathbb{Q}$ are isogenous if and only if $L(E, s) =
 L(E', s)$, equivalently, if and only if $E_p$ and $E_p'$ are isogenous
 modulo sufficiently many good primes $p$.
