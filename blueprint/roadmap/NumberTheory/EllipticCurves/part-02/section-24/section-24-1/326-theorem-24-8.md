# Theorem 24.8

Let $\Gamma$ be a congruence subgroup. For $k=0$ we have $\dim
 M_{k}(\Gamma)=1$ and $\dim S_{k}(\Gamma)=0$. For any even integer $k>0$ we
 have $\dim
 M_{k}(\Gamma)=(k-1)(g(\Gamma)-1)+\left\lfloor\frac{k}{4}\right\rfloor
 \nu_{2}+\left\lfloor\frac{k}{3}\right\rfloor \nu_{3}+\frac{k}{2}
 \nu_{\infty}$ and if $k>2$ we also have $\dim
 S_{k}(\Gamma)=(k-1)(g(\Gamma)-1)+\left\lfloor\frac{k}{4}\right\rfloor
 \nu_{2}+\left\lfloor\frac{k}{3}\right\rfloor
 \nu_{3}+\left(\frac{k}{2}-1\right) \nu_{\infty}$. For $k=2$ we have $\dim
 S_{k}(\Gamma)=g(\Gamma)$.
