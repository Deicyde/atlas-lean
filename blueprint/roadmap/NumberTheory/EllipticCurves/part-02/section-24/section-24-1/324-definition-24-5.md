# Definition 24.5

A modular form $f$ is a weak modular form that is holomorphic at the cusps.
 Equivalently, $f$ is a weak modular form that extends to a holomorphic
 function on the extended upper half plane $\mathbb{H}^{*}=\mathbb{H} \cup
 \mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q})$.
