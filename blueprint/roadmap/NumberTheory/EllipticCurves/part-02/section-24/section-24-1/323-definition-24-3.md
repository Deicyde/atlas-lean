# Definition 24.3

A holomorphic function $f: \mathbb{H} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is a weak
 modular form of weight $k$ for a congruence subgroup $\Gamma$ if $f(\gamma
 \tau)=(c \tau+d)^{k} f(\tau)$ for all $\gamma=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a & b
 \\ c & d\end{smallmatrix}\right) \in \Gamma$.
