# Definition 23.18

Let $C/k$ be a smooth projective curve, and let $f \in k(C)^{\times}$. For
 each divisor $D \in \operatorname{Div} C$ with support disjoint from
 $\operatorname{div} f$ we define $f(D)\coloneqq\prod_{P \in
 \operatorname{supp}(D)} f(P)^{v_{P}(D)} \in k^{\times}$, which satisfies
 $f(D_{1}+D_{2})=f(D_{1})f(D_{2})$ for any $D_{1}, D_{2}$ with support
 disjoint from $\operatorname{div} f$.
