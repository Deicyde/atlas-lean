# Lemma 23.26

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve, let $n$ be a positive integer not divisible
 by the characteristic of $k$, and let $P, Q \in E(k)[n]$. For any point $T
 \notin \{0, Q, -P, Q-P\}$ on $E$ we have $$e_n(P, Q) = \frac{f_{n,Q}(T)
 f_{n,P}(Q-T)}{f_{n,P}(-T) f_{n,Q}(P+T)}.$$
