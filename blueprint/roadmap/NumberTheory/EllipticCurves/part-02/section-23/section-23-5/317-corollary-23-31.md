# Corollary 23.31

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve and let $P \in E(\bar{k})$ be a point of order
 $n$ prime to the characteristic of $k$. For every $Q \in E[n]$ the order of
 $e_n(P, Q)$ in $\mu_n$ is the largest integer $m$ for which $E[m] \subseteq
 \langle P, Q \rangle$, equivalently, the least integer $m$ for which $mQ \in
 \langle P \rangle$. In particular, $e_n(P, Q) = 1$ if and only if $\langle
 P, Q \rangle$ is cyclic.
