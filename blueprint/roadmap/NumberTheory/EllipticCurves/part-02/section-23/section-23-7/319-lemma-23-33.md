# Lemma 23.33

Let $E / \mathbb{F}_{q}$ be an elliptic curve, let $n \perp q$ be a prime
 divisor of $\# E(\mathbb{F}_{q})$, and let $\pi_{n}$ denote the restriction
 of the Frobenius endomorphism $\pi_{E}$ to $\operatorname{End}(E[n]) \simeq
 \operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z})$. Then either $E[n]
 \subseteq E(\mathbb{F}_{q})$ or $E[n] \simeq \ker(\pi_{n}-1) \oplus
 \ker(\pi_{n}-q)$, and the embedding degree of $E$ with respect to $n$ is the
 least integer $k>0$ such that $q^{k} \equiv 1 \bmod n$.
