# Corollary 23.10

Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field $k$.
 For every $f \in k(C)^{\times}$ we have $\sum_{P \in C(k)} v_{P}(f) = 0$ and
 $v_{P}(f)=0$ for all but finitely many $P$; we have $v_{P}(f)=0$ for all $P$
 if and only if $f \in k^{\times}$.
