# Corollary 22.7

Let $E/\mathbb{F}_q$ be an elliptic curve with CM by an order $\mathcal{O}$
 of discriminant $D \perp q$ in an imaginary quadratic field $K$, and let
 $\ell \nmid q$ be prime. Then $E$ admits $1 + \left(\frac{D}{\ell}\right)$
 horizontal $\ell$-isogenies and one or zero ascending $\ell$-isogenies,
 depending on whether $\ell \mid [\mathcal{O}_K : \mathcal{O}]$ or not. The
 number of descending $\ell$-isogenies admitted by $E$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ is
 either zero or $\ell - \left(\frac{D}{\ell}\right)$, depending on whether
 $\operatorname{Ell}_{\mathcal{O}'}(\mathbb{F}_q)$ is empty or not, where
 $\mathcal{O}'$ is the order of index $\ell$ in $\mathcal{O}$.
