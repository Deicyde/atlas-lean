# Lemma 22.13

Let $v$ be a vertex in an ordinary component $V$ of depth $d$ in
 $G_{\ell}(\mathbb{F}_q)$. Either $\deg v \leq 2$ and $v \in V_d$, or $\deg v
 = \ell + 1$ and $v \notin V_d$.
