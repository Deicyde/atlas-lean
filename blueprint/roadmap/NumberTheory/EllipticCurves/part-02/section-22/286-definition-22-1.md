# Definition 22.1

The $\ell$-isogeny graph $G_{\ell}(k)$ is the directed graph with vertex set
 $k$ and edges $(j_1, j_2)$ present with multiplicity equal to the
 multiplicity of $j_2$ as a root of $\Phi_{\ell}(j_1, Y)$.
