# Theorem 25.4 (Taylor-Wiles)

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be a semistable elliptic curve. If $\bar{\rho}_{E,\ell}$
 is modular, then $\rho_{E,\ell}$ is also modular (and therefore $E$ is
 modular).
