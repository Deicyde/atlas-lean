# Corollary 22.22

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ for a number field $K$ and let $n\coloneqq \left[\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: \mathcal{C}\right]$. For every ideal $I \in \mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ the set $\{\mathfrak{p} \in I\mathcal{C}\}$ has Dirichlet density $\frac{1}{n}$, and for every $\chi \neq \mathbb{1}$ in $X(\mathcal{C})$ we have $L(1, \chi) \neq 0$.
