# Lemma 22.26

Let $L_{1}/K$ and $L_{2}/K$ be two finite abelian extensions of a local or global field $K$. If $L_{1} \subseteq L_{2}$ then $\mathfrak{c}(L_{1}/K)$ divides $\mathfrak{c}(L_{2}/K)$.
