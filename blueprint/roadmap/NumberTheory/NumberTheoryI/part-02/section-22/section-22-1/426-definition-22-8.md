# Definition 22.8

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a congruence subgroup for a number field $K$. The unique modulus $\mathfrak{c}\coloneqq \mathfrak{c}(\mathcal{C})$ given by Corollary 22.7 is the conductor of $\mathcal{C}$, and we say that $\mathcal{C}$ is primitive if $\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{C} \mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{c}}$ (the unique congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{c}$ equivalent to $\mathcal{C}$).
