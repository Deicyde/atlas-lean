# Theorem 22.29 (Norm Index Inequality)

Let $L / K$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$ divisible by the conductor of $L / K$. Then $[\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: T_{L / K}^{\mathfrak{m}}] \leq [L: K]$.
