# Definition 28.1

Let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension of global fields. The idele norm $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}: \mathbb{I}_{L} \rightarrow \mathbb{I}_{K}$ is defined by $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(b_{w})=(a_{v})$, where each $a_{v}\coloneqq \prod_{w \mid v} \mathrm{N}_{L_{w}/K_{v}}(b_{w})$ is a product over places $w$ of $L$ that extend the place $v$ of $K$ and $\mathrm{N}_{L_{w}/K_{v}}: L_{w} \rightarrow K_{v}$ is the field norm of the corresponding finite separable extension of local fields $L_{w}/K_{v}$.
