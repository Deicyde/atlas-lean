# Theorem 26.24 (Fundamental theorem of Galois theory)

Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension and let $G\coloneqq \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ be endowed with the Krull topology. The maps $F \mapsto \operatorname{Gal}(L/F)$ and $H \mapsto L^{H}$ define an inclusion reversing bijection between subextensions $F/K$ of $L/K$ and closed subgroups $H$ of $G$. Under this correspondence, subextensions of finite degree $n$ correspond to subgroups of finite index $n$, and normal subextensions $F/K$ correspond to normal subgroups $H \subseteq G$ such that $\operatorname{Gal}(F/K) \simeq G/H$ as topological groups.
