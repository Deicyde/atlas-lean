# Theorem 26.23

Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension. Under the Krull topology, the restriction maps induce a natural isomorphism of topological groups $\phi: \operatorname{Gal}(L/K) \rightarrow \varprojlim \operatorname{Gal}(F/K)$, where $F$ ranges over finite Galois extensions of $K$ in $L$. In particular, $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is a profinite group whose open normal subgroups are precisely those of the form $\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)$ for some finite normal extension $F/K$.
