# Corollary 26.25

Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension and let $H$ be a subgroup of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ with fixed field $F$. The closure $\bar{H}$ of $H$ in the Krull topology is $\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)$.
