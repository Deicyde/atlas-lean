# Theorem 21.19

Let $L/K$ be an abelian extension of number fields and $\mathfrak{m}$ a modulus divisible by all ramified primes. Then the Artin map $\psi_{L/K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: \mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} \rightarrow \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is surjective.
