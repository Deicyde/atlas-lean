# Theorem 21.20

Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for a number field $K$ and let $L$ and $M$ be finite abelian extensions of $K$ unramified at all primes not in the support of $\mathfrak{m}$. If $\operatorname{ker} \psi_{L/K}^{\mathfrak{m}}=\operatorname{ker} \psi_{M/K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ then $L=M$. In particular, ray class fields are unique whenever they exist.
