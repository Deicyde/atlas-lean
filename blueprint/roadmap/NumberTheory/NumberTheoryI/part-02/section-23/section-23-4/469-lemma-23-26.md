# Lemma 23.26

Let $G$ be a group and $A$ an abelian group. Then $H_{0}(G, \operatorname{Ind}^{G}(A)) \simeq A$ and $H_{n}(G, \operatorname{Ind}^{G}(A))=0$ for all $n \geq 1$.
