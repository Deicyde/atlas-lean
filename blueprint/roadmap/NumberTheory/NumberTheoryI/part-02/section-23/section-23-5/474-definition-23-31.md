# Definition 23.31

Let $A$ be a $G$-module for a finite group $G$. For $n \geq 0$ the Tate cohomology and homology groups are defined by $\hat{H}^{n}(G, A)\coloneqq \begin{cases} \operatorname{coker} \hat{N}_{G} & \text{for } n=0 \\ H^{n}(G, A) & \text{for } n>0 \end{cases}$ and $\hat{H}_{n}(G, A)\coloneqq \begin{cases} \ker \hat{N}_{G} & \text{for } n=0 \\ H_{n}(G, A) & \text{for } n>0 \end{cases}$ with $\hat{H}^{-n}(G, A)\coloneqq \hat{H}_{n-1}(G, A)$ and $\hat{H}_{-n}(G, A)\coloneqq \hat{H}^{n-1}(G, A)$.
