# Theorem 23.70

Let $P$, $Q$ be projective resolutions of an $R$-module $M$, let $A$ be an $R$-module, and let $\bullet_{A}^{*}$ denote $\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(\bullet, A)$. Then $H^{n}(P_{A}^{*}) \simeq H^{n}(Q_{A}^{*})$ for all $n \geq 0$.
