# Definition 23.71

Let $A$ and $M$ be $R$-modules. $\operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{n}(M, A)$ is the abelian group $H^{n}(P_{A}^{*})$ uniquely determined by Theorem 23.70 using any projective resolution $P$ of $M$. If $\alpha: A \rightarrow B$ is a morphism of $R$-modules the map $\varphi \mapsto \alpha \circ \varphi$ induces a morphism of cochain complexes $P_{A}^{*} \rightarrow P_{B}^{*}$ and morphisms $\operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{n}(M, \alpha): \operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{n}(M, A) \rightarrow \operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{n}(M, B)$ for each $n \geq 0$.
