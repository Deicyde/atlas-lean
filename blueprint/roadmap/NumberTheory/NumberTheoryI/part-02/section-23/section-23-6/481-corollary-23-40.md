# Corollary 23.40

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group. Given an exact sequence of $G$-modules $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$ we have a corresponding exact hexagon relating $\hat{H}^{0}(G, A)$, $\hat{H}^{0}(G, B)$, $\hat{H}^{0}(G, C)$, $\hat{H}_{0}(G, A)$, $\hat{H}_{0}(G, B)$, $\hat{H}_{0}(G, C)$.
