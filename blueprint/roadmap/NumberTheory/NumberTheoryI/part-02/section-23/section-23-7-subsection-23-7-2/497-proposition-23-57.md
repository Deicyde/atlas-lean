# Proposition 23.57

Let $M$ and $N$ be $R$-modules with projective resolutions $P$ and $Q$ and injective resolutions $I$ and $J$, respectively. Every $R$-module morphism $\alpha_{0}: M \rightarrow N$ extends to a morphism $\alpha: P \rightarrow Q$ of chain complexes, and to a morphism $\alpha: I \rightarrow J$ of cochain complexes, both of which are unique up to homotopy.
