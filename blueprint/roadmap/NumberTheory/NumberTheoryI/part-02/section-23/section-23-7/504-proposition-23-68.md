# Proposition 23.68

Let $A$ be an $R$-module and let $\bullet^{*}$ denote the application of the functor $\operatorname{Hom}(\bullet, A)$. Let $f, g: C \rightarrow D$ be homotopic morphisms of chain complexes of $R$-modules. Then $f^{*}, g^{*}: D^{*} \rightarrow C^{*}$ are homotopic morphisms of cochain complexes of $\mathbb{Z}$-modules.
