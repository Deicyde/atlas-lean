# Theorem 23.8

Every short exact sequence of $G$-modules $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$ induces a long exact sequence of cohomology groups $0 \rightarrow H^{0}(G, A) \xrightarrow{\alpha^{0}} H^{0}(G, B) \xrightarrow{\beta^{0}} H^{0}(G, C) \xrightarrow{\delta^{0}} H^{1}(G, A) \longrightarrow \cdots$ and commutative diagrams of short exact sequences of $G$-modules induce corresponding commutative diagrams of long exact sequences of cohomology groups.
