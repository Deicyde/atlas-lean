# Lemma 23.7

Suppose that we have a short exact sequence of $G$-modules $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$. Then for every $n \geq 0$ we have a corresponding exact sequence of $n$-cochains $0 \longrightarrow C^{n}(G, A) \xrightarrow{\alpha^{n}} C^{n}(G, B) \xrightarrow{\beta^{n}} C^{n}(G, C) \longrightarrow 0$.
