# Definition 24.4

Let $L / K$ be a Galois extension of global fields and let $w$ be a place of $L$. The decomposition group of $w$ is its stabilizer in $\operatorname{Gal}(L / K)$: $D_{w} \coloneqq \{\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(L / K) : \sigma(w) = w\}$. If $w$ corresponds to a prime $\mathfrak{q}$ of $\mathcal{O}_{L}$ then $D_{w} = D_{\mathfrak{q}}$ is also the decomposition group of $\mathfrak{q}$.
