# Lemma 24.9

Let $L / K$ be a cyclic extension of global fields with Galois group $\langle\sigma\rangle$. We have $h_{0}(\mathcal{I}_{L}) = 1$ and $h(\mathcal{I}_{L}) = h^{0}(\mathcal{I}_{L}) = e_{0}(L / K)[\mathcal{I}_{K} : N(\mathcal{I}_{L})]$.
