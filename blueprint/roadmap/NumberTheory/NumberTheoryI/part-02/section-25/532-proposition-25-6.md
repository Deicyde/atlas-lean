# Proposition 25.6

Let $(X_{i})_{i \in I}$ be a family of locally compact topological spaces and let $(U_{i})_{i \in I}$ be a corresponding family of open subsets $U_{i} \subseteq X_{i}$ almost all of which are compact. Then the restricted product $X \coloneqq \coprod(X_{i}, U_{i})$ is locally compact.
