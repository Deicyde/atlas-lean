# Proposition 25.10

Let $L$ be a finite separable extension of a global field $K$. There is a natural isomorphism of topological rings $\mathbb{A}_{L} \simeq \mathbb{A}_{K} \otimes_{K} L$ that makes the diagram with canonical embeddings $L \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}_{L}$ and $L \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}_{K} \otimes_{K} L$ commute.
