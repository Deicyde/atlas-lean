# Lemma 25.14 (Adelic Blichfeldt-Minkowski lemma)

Let $K$ be a global field. There is a positive constant $B_{K}$ such that for any $a \in \mathbb{A}_{K}$ with $\|a\|>B_{K}$ there exists a nonzero principal adele $x \in K \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}$ for which $\|x\|_{v} \leq \|a\|_{v}$ for all $v \in M_{K}$.
