# Definition 27.3

A norm group of a local field $K$ is a subgroup of the form $\mathrm{N}\left(L^{\times}\right)\coloneqq \mathrm{N}_{L / K}\left(L^{\times}\right) \subseteq K^{\times}$ for some finite abelian extension $L / K$.
