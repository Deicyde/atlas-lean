# Theorem 27.9 (Main Theorem of Local Class Field Theory)

Let $K$ be a local field. The local Artin homomorphism induces a canonical isomorphism $\widehat{\theta}_{K}: \widehat{K^{\times}} \xrightarrow{\sim} \operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{ab}}/K)$ of profinite groups.
