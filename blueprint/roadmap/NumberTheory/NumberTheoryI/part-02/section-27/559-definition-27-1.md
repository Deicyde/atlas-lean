# Definition 27.1

Let $K$ be a local field with separable closure $K^{\text{sep}}$. The field $K^{\mathrm{ab}}\coloneqq \bigcup_{\substack{L \subseteq K^{\text{sep}} \\ L / K \text{ finite abelian}}} L$ is the maximal abelian extension of $K$ (in $K^{\text{sep}}$). We also define $K^{\mathrm{unr}}\coloneqq \bigcup_{\substack{L \subseteq K^{\mathrm{sep}} \\ L / K \text{ finite unramified}}} L$ the maximal unramified extension of $K$ (in $K^{\text{sep}}$).
