# Proposition 27.12

Let $K$ be a local field and assume every finite index open subgroup of $K^{\times}$ is a norm group. There is at most one homomorphism $\theta: K^{\times} \rightarrow \operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{ab}}/K)$ of topological groups that has the properties given in Theorem 27.2.
