# Theorem 27.8 (Local Existence Theorem)

Let $K$ be a local field and let $H$ be a finite index open subgroup of $K^{\times}$. There is a unique extension $L/K$ in $K^{\text{ab}}$ with $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(L^{\times})=H$.
