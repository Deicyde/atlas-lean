# Proposition 8.11

Let $K$ be a field with absolute value $|\,|_v$ induced by a discrete valuation $v$, let $A$ be the valuation ring of $K$, and let $\pi$ be a uniformizer. The valuation ring of the completion $K_v$ of $K$ with respect to $|\,|_v$ is a complete discrete valuation ring $A_v$ with uniformizer $\pi$, and we have an isomorphism of topological rings $A_v \simeq \varprojlim_{n} A / \pi^n A$.
