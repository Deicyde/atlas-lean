# Corollary 10.6

Assume AKLB and that $A$ is a complete DVR with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ and let $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$. Then $v_{\mathfrak{q}}(x)=\frac{1}{f_{\mathfrak{q}}} v_{\mathfrak{p}}(\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(x))$ for all $x \in L$.
