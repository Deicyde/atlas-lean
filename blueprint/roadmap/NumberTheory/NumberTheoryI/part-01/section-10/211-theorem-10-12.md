# Theorem 10.12

Assume $AKLB$, with $A$ and $B$ DVRs with residue fields $k\coloneqq A/\mathfrak{p}$ and $l\coloneqq B/\mathfrak{q}$. If $l/k$ is separable then $B=A[\alpha]$ for some $\alpha \in B$; if $L/K$ is unramified this holds for every lift $\alpha$ of any generator $\bar{\alpha}$ for $l=k(\bar{\alpha})$.
