# Corollary 10.17

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$ and finite residue field $\mathbb{F}_q$, and let $L$ be a degree $n$ extension of $K$. Then $L/K$ is unramified if and only if $L \simeq K(\zeta_{q^n-1})$. When this holds, $A[\zeta_{q^n-1}]$ is the integral closure of $A$ in $L$ and $L/K$ is a Galois extension with $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K) \simeq \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$.
