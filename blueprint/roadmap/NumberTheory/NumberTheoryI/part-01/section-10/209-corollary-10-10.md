# Corollary 10.10

Let $A$ be a local noetherian ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, let $g \in A[x]$, and let $B\coloneqq A[x]/(g(x))$. Every maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $B$ contains the ideal $\mathfrak{p} B$.
