# Theorem 10.23

Assume $AKLB$ with $A$ a complete DVR and separable residue field extension $l/k$. Let $e$ and $f$ be the ramification index and residue field degrees, respectively, and let $\mathfrak{q}$ be the unique prime of $B$. The following hold:
(i) There is a unique intermediate field extension $E/K$ that contains every unramified extension of $K$ in $L$ and it has degree $[E:K]=f$.
(ii) The extension $L/E$ is totally ramified and has degree $[L:E]=e$.
(iii) If $L/K$ is Galois then $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is the decomposition group $D_{\mathfrak{q}}$, $\operatorname{Gal}(L/E)$ is the inertia subgroup $I_{\mathfrak{q}}$, and $E/K$ is Galois with $\operatorname{Gal}(E/K) \simeq D_{\mathfrak{q}}/I_{\mathfrak{q}} \simeq \operatorname{Gal}(l/k)$.
