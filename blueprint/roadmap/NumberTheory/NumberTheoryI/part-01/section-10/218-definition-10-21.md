# Definition 10.21

Let $L/K$ be a separable extension. The maximal unramified extension of $K$ in $L$ is the subfield $\bigcup_{\substack{K \subseteq E \subseteq L \\ E/K \text{ fin. unram.}}} E \subseteq L$ where the union is over finite unramified subextensions $E/K$. When $L=K^{\text{sep}}$ is the separable closure of $K$, this is the maximal unramified extension of $K$, denoted $K^{\mathrm{unr}}$.
