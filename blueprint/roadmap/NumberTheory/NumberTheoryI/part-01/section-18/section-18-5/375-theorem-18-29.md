# Theorem 18.29

For $x \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 1}$, as $x \to \infty$ we have $H(x) = \log x + \gamma + O\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$ where $\gamma = \lim_{x \to \infty}(H(x) - \log x) = 0.577216\ldots$ is Euler's constant.
