# Lemma 18.8

Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character of period $m$. Then $\chi$ is a Dirichlet character of modulus $m'$ if and only if $m \mid m' \mid m^k$ for some $k$ (which holds in particular for $m' = m$).
