# Proposition 12.4

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{q}|\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $B$. Then $\mathcal{D}_{\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}/\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}} = \mathcal{D}_{B/A}\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}$ where $\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}$ and $\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}$ are the completions of $A$ and $B$ at $\mathfrak{p}$ and $\mathfrak{q}$, respectively.
