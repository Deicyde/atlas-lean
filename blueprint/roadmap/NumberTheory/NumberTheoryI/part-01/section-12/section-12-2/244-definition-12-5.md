# Definition 12.5

Let $S/R$ be a ring extension in which $S$ is a free $R$-module of rank $n$. For any $x_1, \ldots, x_n \in S$ we define the discriminant $\operatorname{disc}(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \coloneqq \operatorname{disc}_{S/R}(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \coloneqq \det[\mathrm{T}_{S/R}(x_i x_j)]_{i,j} \in R$.
