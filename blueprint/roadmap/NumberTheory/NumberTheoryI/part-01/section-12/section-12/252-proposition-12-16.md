# Proposition 12.16

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. Then $$D_{B/A} \hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}} = \prod_{\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}} D_{\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}/\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}}$$ where $\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}$ and $\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}$ are the completions of $A$ and $B$ at $\mathfrak{p}$ and $\mathfrak{q}$, respectively.
