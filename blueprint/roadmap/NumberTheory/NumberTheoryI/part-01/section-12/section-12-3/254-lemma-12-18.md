# Lemma 12.18

Let $k$ be a field and let $R$ be a commutative $k$-algebra with $k$-basis $r_1, \ldots, r_n$. Then $R$ is a finite étale $k$-algebra if and only if $\operatorname{disc}(r_1, \ldots, r_n) \neq 0$.
