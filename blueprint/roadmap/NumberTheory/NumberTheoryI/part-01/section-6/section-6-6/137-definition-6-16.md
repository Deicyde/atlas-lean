# Definition 6.16

Let $S/R$ be an extension of commutative rings. The conductor of $R$ in $S$ is the largest $S$-ideal that is also an $R$-ideal; equivalently, it is the largest ideal of $S$ contained in $R$. It can be written as $\mathfrak{c}\coloneqq \{\alpha \in S: \alpha S \subseteq R\}=\{\alpha \in R: \alpha S \subseteq R\}$. If $R$ is an integral domain, the conductor of $R$ is the conductor of $R$ in its integral closure.
