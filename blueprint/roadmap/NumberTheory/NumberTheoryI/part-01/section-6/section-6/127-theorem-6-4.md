# Theorem 6.4

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $N \subseteq M$ be $A$-lattices in a $K$-vector space $V$ of dimension $r$ for which the quotient module $M / N$ is a direct sum of cyclic $A$-modules: $M / N \simeq A / I_{1} \oplus \cdots \oplus A / I_{n}$ where $I_{1}, \ldots, I_{n}$ are nonzero ideals of $A$. Then $[M: N]_{A}=I_{1} \cdots I_{n}$.
