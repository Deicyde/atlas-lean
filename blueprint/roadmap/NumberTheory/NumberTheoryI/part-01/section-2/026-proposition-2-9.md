# Proposition 2.9

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain. The following are equivalent: (i) For every nonzero prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \subset A$ the local ring $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a DVR. (ii) The ring $A$ is integrally closed and $\dim A \leq 1$.
