# Lemma 14.29

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$. For each prime number $p$ we have $v_p(D_K) \leq n\lfloor \log_p n \rfloor + n - 1$. In particular, $v_p(D_K) \leq n\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + n - 1$ for all $p$.
