# Lemma 14.19

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$ with $r$ real and $s$ complex places. For each $t \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$, the measure of the convex symmetric set $S_{t}\coloneqq \{(z_{\sigma}) \in K_{\mathbb{R}}: \sum |z_{\sigma}| \leq t\} \subseteq K_{\mathbb{R}}$ with respect to the normalized Haar measure $\mu$ on $K_{\mathbb{R}}$ is $\mu(S_{t})=2^{r} \pi^{s} \frac{t^{n}}{n!}$.
