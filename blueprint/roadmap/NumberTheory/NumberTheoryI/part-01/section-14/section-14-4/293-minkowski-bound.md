# Theorem 14.18 (Minkowski bound)

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$ with $s$ complex places. Define the Minkowski constant $m_{K}$ for $K$ as the positive real number $m_{K}\coloneqq \frac{n!}{n^{n}}\left(\frac{4}{\pi}\right)^{s} \sqrt{|D_{K}|}$. For every nonzero fractional ideal $I$ of $\mathcal{O}_{K}$ there is a nonzero $a \in I$ for which $\mathrm{N}(a) \leq m_{K} \mathrm{N}(I)$.
