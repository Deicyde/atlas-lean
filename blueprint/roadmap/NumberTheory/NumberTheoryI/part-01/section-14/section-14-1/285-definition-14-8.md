# Definition 14.8

Let $\Lambda$ be a lattice in $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$. A fundamental domain for $\Lambda$ is a measurable set $F \subseteq V$ such that $V=\bigsqcup_{\lambda \in \Lambda}(F+\lambda)$.
