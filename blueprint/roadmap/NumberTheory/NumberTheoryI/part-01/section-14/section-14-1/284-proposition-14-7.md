# Proposition 14.7

Let $T: V \rightarrow V$ be a linear transformation of $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$. For any Haar measure $\mu$ on $V$ and every measurable set $S \subseteq V$ we have $\mu(T(S))=|\det T| \mu(S)$.
