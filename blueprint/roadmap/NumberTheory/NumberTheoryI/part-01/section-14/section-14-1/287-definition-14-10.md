# Definition 14.10

Let $\Lambda$ be a lattice in $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and fix a Haar measure $\mu$ on $V$. The covolume $\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda) \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ of $\Lambda$ is the measure $\mu(F)$ of any fundamental domain $F$ for $\Lambda$.
