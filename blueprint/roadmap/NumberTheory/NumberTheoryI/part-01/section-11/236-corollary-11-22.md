# Corollary 11.22

For every finite Galois extension $\hat{L}/\hat{K}$ of local fields there is a finite Galois extension of global fields $L/K$ and an absolute value $|\,|$ on $L$ such that $\hat{L}$ is the completion of $L$ with respect to $|\,|$, $\hat{K}$ is the completion of $K$ with respect to the restriction of $|\,|$ to $K$, and $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K) \simeq \operatorname{Gal}(\hat{L}/\hat{K})$.
