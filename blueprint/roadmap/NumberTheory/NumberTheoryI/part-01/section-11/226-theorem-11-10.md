# Theorem 11.10

Assume $AKLB$ with $A$ a complete DVR and separable residue field extension of characteristic $p \geq 0$ not dividing $n\coloneqq [L:K]$. The extension $L/K$ is totally tamely ramified if and only if $L=K(\pi_A^{1/n})$ for some uniformizer $\pi_A$ of $A$.
