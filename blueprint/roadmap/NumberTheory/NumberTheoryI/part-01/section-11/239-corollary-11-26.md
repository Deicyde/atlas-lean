# Corollary 11.26

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. Let $\mathfrak{p}B = \prod \mathfrak{q}^{e_{\mathfrak{q}}}$ be the factorization of $\mathfrak{p}B$ in $B$. Let $\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}$ denote the completion of $A$ with respect to $|\,|_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and for each $\mathfrak{q}|\mathfrak{p}$, let $\hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}$ denote the completion of $B$ with respect to $|\,|_{\mathfrak{q}}$. Then $B \otimes_A \hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}} \simeq \prod_{\mathfrak{q}|\mathfrak{p}} \hat{B}_{\mathfrak{q}}$, as $\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}}$-algebras.
