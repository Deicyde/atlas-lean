# Lemma 11.4

Let $A$ be a DVR and let $f \in A[x]$ be an Eisenstein polynomial. Then $B=A[\pi]\coloneqq A[x]/(f)$ is a DVR with uniformizer $\pi$, where $\pi$ is the image of $x$ in $A[x]/(f)$.
