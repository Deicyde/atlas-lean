# Lemma 11.13

Let $K$ be the fraction field of a complete DVR with algebraic closure $\bar{K}$ and absolute value $|\;|$ extended to $\bar{K}$. For all $\alpha \in \bar{K}$ and $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_{K}(\bar{K})$ we have $|\sigma(\alpha)|=|\alpha|$.
