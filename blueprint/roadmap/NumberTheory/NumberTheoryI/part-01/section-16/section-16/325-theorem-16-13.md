# Theorem 16.13

Let $f: \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a bounded piecewise continuous function, and suppose its Laplace transform extends to a holomorphic function $g(s)$ on $\operatorname{Re}(s) \geq 0$. Then the integral $\int_{0}^{\infty} f(t) dt$ converges and is equal to $g(0)$.
