# Proposition 5.3

Let $A$ be an integral domain with fraction field $K$ and let $M$ be a nonzero $A$-submodule of $K$. Then $M^{\vee} \simeq (A:M) \coloneqq \{x \in K : xM \subseteq A\}$; in particular, if $M$ is an invertible fractional ideal then $M^{\vee} \simeq M^{-1}$ and $M^{\vee\vee} \simeq M$.
