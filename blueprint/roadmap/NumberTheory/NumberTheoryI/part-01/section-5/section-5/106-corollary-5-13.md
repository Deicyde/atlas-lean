# Corollary 5.13

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$. If $M_1, M_2$ are $A$-lattices in $K$-vector spaces $V_1, V_2$ with perfect pairings $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle_1, \langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle_2$ (resp.), then $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle_1 + \langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle_2$ defines a perfect pairing on $V_1 \oplus V_2$ and $(M \oplus N)^* \simeq M^* \oplus N^*$.
