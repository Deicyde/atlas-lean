# Theorem 5.12

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, let $V$ be a $K$-vector space with a perfect pairing $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle$, and let $M$ be an $A$-lattice in $V$. The dual lattice $M^*$ is an $A$-lattice in $V$ isomorphic to $M^{\vee}$.
