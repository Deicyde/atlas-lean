# Proposition 5.18

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite extension of fields, and let $B$ be the integral closure of $A$ in $L$. Then $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(b) \in A$ and $\mathrm{T}_{L/K}(b) \in A$ for all $b \in B$.
