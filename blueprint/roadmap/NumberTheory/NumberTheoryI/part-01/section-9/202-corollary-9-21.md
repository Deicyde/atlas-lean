# Corollary 9.21

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$, and let $L/K$ be a finite extension of degree $n$. Then $\alpha \in L$ is integral over $A$ if and only if $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(\alpha) \in A$.
