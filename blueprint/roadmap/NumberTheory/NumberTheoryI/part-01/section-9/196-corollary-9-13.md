# Corollary 9.13

Let $R$ be a ring, $f \in R[x]$, and $a \in R$. Then $f(a)=f'(a)=0$ if and only if $a$ is (at least) a double root of $f$, that is, $f(x)=(x-a)^{2} g(x)$ for some $g \in R[x]$.
