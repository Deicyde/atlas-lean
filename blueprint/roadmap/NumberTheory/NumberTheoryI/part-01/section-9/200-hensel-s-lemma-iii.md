# Lemma 9.19 (Hensel's lemma III)

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ and residue field $k$, let $f \in A[x]$ have image $\bar{f}$ in $k[x]$, and suppose $\bar{f}=\bar{g}\bar{h}$ for some coprime $\bar{g}, \bar{h} \in k[x]$. Then there exist polynomials $g, h \in A[x]$ for which $f=gh$ with $g \equiv \bar{g} \bmod \mathfrak{p}$ and $h \equiv \bar{h} \bmod \mathfrak{p}$ such that $\deg g=\deg \bar{g}$.
