# Definition 1.6

Two absolute values $|\cdot|$ and $|\cdot|'$ on the same field $k$ are equivalent if there exists an $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ for which $|x|' = |x|^\alpha$ for all $x \in k$.
