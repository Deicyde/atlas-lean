# Definition 7.19

Assume $AKLBG$ with finite residue fields and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be an unramified prime of $A$. The $G$-conjugacy class $\{\sigma_{\mathfrak{q}} : \mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}\}$ is the Frobenius class of $\mathfrak{p}$, denoted $\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak{p}}$.
