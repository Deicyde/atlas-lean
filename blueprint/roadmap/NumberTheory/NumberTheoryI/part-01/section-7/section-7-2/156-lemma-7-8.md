# Lemma 7.8

Assume $AKLBG$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. The decomposition groups $D_{\mathfrak{q}}$ for $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ are all conjugate in $G$, with $\# D_{\mathfrak{q}}=e_{\mathfrak{p}} f_{\mathfrak{p}}$ and $[G: D_{\mathfrak{q}}]=g_{\mathfrak{p}}$.
