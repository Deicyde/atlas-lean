# Proposition 7.9

Assume $AKLBG$ and let $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $B$. The group homomorphism $\pi_{\mathfrak{q}}: D_{\mathfrak{q}} \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}_{A/\mathfrak{p}}(B/\mathfrak{q})$ defined by $\sigma \mapsto \bar{\sigma}$ is surjective and $B/\mathfrak{q}$ is normal over $A/\mathfrak{p}$.
