# Theorem 13.21 (Product Formula)

Let $L$ be a global field. For all $x \in L^{\times}$ we have $\prod_{v \in M_{L}}\|x\|_{v}=1$ where $\|\,\|_{v}$ denotes the normalized absolute value for each place $v \in M_{L}$.
