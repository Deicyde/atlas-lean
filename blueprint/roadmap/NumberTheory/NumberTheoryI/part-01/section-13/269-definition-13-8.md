# Definition 13.8

Let $K$ be a number field. Elements of $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Q}}(K, \mathbb{R})$ are real embeddings, and elements of $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Q}}(K, \mathbb{C})$ whose image does not lie in $\mathbb{R}$ are complex embeddings.
