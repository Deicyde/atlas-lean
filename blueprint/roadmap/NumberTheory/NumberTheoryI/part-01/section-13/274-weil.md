# Theorem 13.14 (Weil)

Every locally compact group $G$ has a Haar measure. If $\mu$ and $\mu^{\prime}$ are two Haar measures on $G$, then there is a positive real number $\lambda$ such that $\mu^{\prime}(S)=\lambda \mu(S)$ for all measurable sets $S$.
