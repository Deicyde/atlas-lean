# Corollary 13.7

Let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension of global fields and $v$ a place of $K$. We have a bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_{K}(L, \bar{K}_v)/\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{K}_v/K_v) \longleftrightarrow \{w \mid v\}$, between $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{K}_v/K_v)$-orbits of $K$-embeddings of $L$ into $\bar{K}_v$ and the places of $L$ above $v$.
