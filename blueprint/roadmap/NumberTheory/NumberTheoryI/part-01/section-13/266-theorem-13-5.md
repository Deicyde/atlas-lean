# Theorem 13.5

Let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension of global fields and let $v$ be a place of $K$. We have an isomorphism of finite étale $K_v$-algebras $L \otimes_{K} K_v \xrightarrow{\sim} \prod_{w \mid v} L_w$ defined by $\ell \otimes x \mapsto (\ell x, \ldots, \ell x)$.
