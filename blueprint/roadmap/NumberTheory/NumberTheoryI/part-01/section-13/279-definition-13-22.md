# Definition 13.22

A global field is a field $K$ with at least one place whose completion at each of its places $v \in M_{K}$ is a local field $K_{v}$, and which has a product formula of the form $\prod_{v \in M_{K}}\|x\|_{v}=1$ where each normalized absolute value $\|\,\|_{v}: K_{v} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ satisfies $\|\,\|_{v}=|\,|_{v}^{m_{v}}$ for some absolute value $|\,|_{v}$ representing $v$ and some fixed $m_{v} \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$.
