# Proposition 20.7

For odd $p$ every totally wildly ramified Galois extension of $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ is cyclic. In particular, there is no abelian extension of $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ with Galois group $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{3}$ when $p$ is odd.
