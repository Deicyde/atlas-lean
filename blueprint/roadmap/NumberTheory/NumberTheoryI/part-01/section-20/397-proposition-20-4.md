# Proposition 20.4

Let $K/\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ be a cyclic extension of degree $\ell^{r}$ for some prime $\ell \neq p$. Then $K$ lies in a cyclotomic field $\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\zeta_{m})$.
