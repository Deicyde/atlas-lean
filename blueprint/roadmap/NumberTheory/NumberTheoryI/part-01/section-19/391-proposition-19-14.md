# Proposition 19.14

Let $p$ be a prime, let $m$ be a positive integer, and let $m^{\prime}=m / p^{v_{p}(m)}$. Then $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{m^{\prime}})$ is the maximal extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{m})$ unramified at $p$. In particular, if $p$ does not divide $m$ then $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{m})$ is unramified at $p$.
