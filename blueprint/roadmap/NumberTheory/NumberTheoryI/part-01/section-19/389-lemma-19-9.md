# Lemma 19.9

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ be a sequence of complex numbers and let $\sigma$ be a real number. Suppose that $a_{1}+\cdots+a_{t}=O\left(t^{\sigma}\right)$ as $t \rightarrow \infty$. Then the Dirichlet series $\sum a_{n} n^{-s}$ defines a holomorphic function on $\operatorname{Re} s>\sigma$.
