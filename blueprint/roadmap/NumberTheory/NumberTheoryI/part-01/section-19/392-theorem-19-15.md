# Theorem 19.15

Let $H \subseteq X(m)$ be a group of primitive Dirichlet characters and let $K= \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{m})^{\phi(H)}$ be the corresponding subfield of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{m})$, with $\phi(H)$ defined as above. Then $$\zeta_{K}(s)=\prod_{\chi \in H} L(s, \chi).$$
