# Definition 4.17

Let $L/K$ be an algebraic field extension. The field $F$ in Corollary 4.16 is the separable closure of $K$ in $L$. When $L$ is an algebraic closure of $K$ it is simply called a separable closure of $K$ and denoted $K^{\text{sep}}$.
