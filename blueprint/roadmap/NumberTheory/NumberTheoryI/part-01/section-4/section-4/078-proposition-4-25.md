# Proposition 4.25

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$. If $L / K$ is purely inseparable of degree $p$ then $L=K\left(a^{1 / p}\right) \simeq K[x] /\left(x^{p}-a\right)$ for some $a \in K-K^{p}$.
