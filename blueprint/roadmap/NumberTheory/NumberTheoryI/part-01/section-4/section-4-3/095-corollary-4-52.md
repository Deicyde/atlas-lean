# Corollary 4.52

Let $A$ be an integrally closed domain with fraction field $K$ and let $L/K$ be a finite extension. If $\alpha \in L$ is integral over $A$ then $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(\alpha) \in A$ and $\mathrm{T}_{L/K}(\alpha) \in A$.
