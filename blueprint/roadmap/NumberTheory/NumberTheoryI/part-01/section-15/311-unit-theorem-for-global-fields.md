# Theorem 15.13 (Unit Theorem for Global Fields)

Let $K/F$ be a finite separable extension, with $F=\mathbb{Q}$ or $F=\mathbb{F}_{q}(t)$, let $S \subseteq M_{K}$ be the set of places of $K$ lying above the unique infinite place of $F$, and define $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}\coloneqq \{x \in K^{\times}: v(x)=0 \text{ for all } v \in M_{K}-S\}$. Then $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \simeq \mu_{K} \times \mathbb{Z}^{\# S-1}$ is a finitely generated abelian group.
