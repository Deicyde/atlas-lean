# Theorem 15.12 (Dirichlet's Unit Theorem)

Let $K$ be a number field with $r$ real and $s$ complex places. Then $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \simeq \mu_{K} \times \mathbb{Z}^{r+s-1}$ is a finitely generated abelian group.
