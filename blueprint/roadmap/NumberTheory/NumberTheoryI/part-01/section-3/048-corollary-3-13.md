# Corollary 3.13

In a Dedekind domain every nonzero fractional ideal $I$ has a unique factorization $I = \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathfrak{p}^{v_{\mathfrak{p}}(I)}$ into nonzero prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}$.
