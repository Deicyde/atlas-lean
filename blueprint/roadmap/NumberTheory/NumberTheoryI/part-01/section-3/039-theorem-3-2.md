# Theorem 3.2

Let $I$ be a fractional ideal of a noetherian domain $A$. Then $I$ is invertible if and only if its localization at every maximal ideal of $A$ is invertible, equivalently, if and only if its localization at every prime ideal of $A$ is invertible.
