# Embedding a cyclotomic extension of the rationals

**Embedding a cyclotomic extension of the rationals.** A cyclotomic extension `K` of `ℚ` embeds into any characteristic-zero
field `L` containing a primitive `n`-th root of unity, sending the
distinguished root `ζ` to it.
