# Enumeration of the units of ℤ(√−2)

**Enumeration of the units of ℤ[√−2].** The map $\{0, 1\} \to \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ sending
$0$ to $1$ and $1$ to $-1$, which lists the two units of $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$.
