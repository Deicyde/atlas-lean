# x² + 2y² is never 5 or 7 modulo 8

**x² + 2y² is never 5 or 7 modulo 8.** For all $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$,
$x^2 + 2y^2 \ne 5$ and $x^2 + 2y^2 \ne 7$.
