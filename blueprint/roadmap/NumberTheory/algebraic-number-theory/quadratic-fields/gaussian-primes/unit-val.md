# Enumeration of the Gaussian units

**Enumeration of the Gaussian units.** The map $u$ on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ with $u_0 = 1$,
$u_1 = -1$, $u_2 = i$ and $u_3 = -i$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, listing the four units of
$\mathbb{Z}[i]$.
