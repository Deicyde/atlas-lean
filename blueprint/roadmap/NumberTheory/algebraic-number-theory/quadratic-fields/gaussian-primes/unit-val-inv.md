# Inverses of the Gaussian units

**Inverses of the Gaussian units.** The map $v$ on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ with $v_0 = 1$,
$v_1 = -1$, $v_2 = -i$ and $v_3 = i$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, listing the inverses of the units
$1$, $-1$, $i$ and $-i$ in that order.
