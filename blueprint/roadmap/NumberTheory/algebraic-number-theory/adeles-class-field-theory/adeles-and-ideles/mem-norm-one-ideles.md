# Membership in the norm-one ideles

**Membership in the norm-one ideles.** Let $K$ be a number field. An idele $a$ of $K$ (with
$R = \mathcal{O}_K$) lies in the subgroup of norm-one ideles if and only if its adelic norm
is $1$.
