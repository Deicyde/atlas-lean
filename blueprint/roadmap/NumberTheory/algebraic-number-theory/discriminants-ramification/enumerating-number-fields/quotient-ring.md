# Quotient of a polynomial ring by $r$ polynomials

**Quotient of a polynomial ring by $r$ polynomials.** For a natural number $r$ and polynomials
$E_1, \dots, E_r \in \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]$, the quotient ring
$\mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]/(E_1, \dots, E_r)$ of the polynomial ring by the ideal generated by
$E_1, \dots, E_r$.
