# Quotients of the unit filtration of a discretely valued field

Let $L$ be a discretely valued field with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_L$, maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_L$ and residue
field $k_L$, let $U_L = \mathcal{O}_L^\times$ and $U_L^i = 1 + \mathfrak{m}_L^i$ for $i \ge 1$. Then:

1. reduction modulo $\mathfrak{m}_L$ induces an isomorphism $U_L/U_L^1 \cong k_L^\times$;
2. for $i \ge 1$, the map $1 + y \mapsto y \bmod \mathfrak{m}_L^{i+1}$ induces an isomorphism of
   $U_L^i/U_L^{i+1}$ with the additive group $\mathfrak{m}_L^i/\mathfrak{m}_L^{i+1}$, which does not depend on any
   choice; and multiplication by $\pi_L^{-i}$ for a uniformizer $\pi_L$ identifies $\mathfrak{m}_L^i/\mathfrak{m}_L^{i+1}$
   with the additive group of $k_L$, an isomorphism that depends on $\pi_L$.
