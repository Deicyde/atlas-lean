# Ramification groups of local fields

The lower numbering filtration of the Galois group of a finite Galois extension of local fields and the structure
results on its quotients, which give the structure of the inertia and wild inertia groups of a $p$-adic field. Let
$L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete discretely valued fields whose residue fields are perfect of
characteristic $p > 0$, $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, and for $i \ge -1$ let
$G_i = \{ s \in G : v_L(s(x) - x) \ge i + 1 \text{ for all } x \in \mathcal{O}_L \}$.

Sources: J.-M. Fontaine and Y. Ouyang, *Theory of p-adic Galois Representations*, book draft (PDF of 2022-05-26),
archived at <http://web.archive.org/web/20261014003209/http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~yiouyang/galoisrep.pdf>, §1.3.1,
pp. 21–24, Lemma 1.58 to Remark 1.65 and diagram (1.35), and §1.3.3, p. 25, Proposition 1.67; J.-P. Serre, *Local
Fields*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 67, Springer, 1979, Chapter IV, §§1–2 (cited, not consulted for these pages).
