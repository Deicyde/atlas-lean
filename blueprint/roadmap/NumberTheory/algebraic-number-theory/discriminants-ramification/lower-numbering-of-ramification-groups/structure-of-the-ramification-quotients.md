# Structure of the quotients of the ramification filtration

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete discretely valued fields whose residue fields are perfect of
characteristic $p > 0$, and $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ with ramification groups $G_i$. Then:

1. $G_0/G_1$ is cyclic of order prime to $p$, and $\theta_0$ maps it isomorphically onto a subgroup of the group
   $\mu(k_L)$ of roots of unity of $k_L$;
2. for $i \ge 1$, $G_i/G_{i+1}$ is an abelian group of $p$-power order, in fact a direct product of cyclic groups of
   order $p$.

Here $\theta_i \colon G_i/G_{i+1} \hookrightarrow U_L^i/U_L^{i+1}$ are the embeddings of
[Embeddings of the quotients of the ramification filtration into the unit filtration](embeddings-of-the-ramification-quotients.md).

## Depends on

- [Ramification groups of a finite Galois extension of local fields](ramification-groups-of-a-finite-galois-extension.md), for the groups $G_i$

## Proof depends on

- [Embeddings of the quotients of the ramification filtration into the unit filtration](embeddings-of-the-ramification-quotients.md), for the injections $\theta_i$
- [Quotients of the unit filtration of a discretely valued field](quotients-of-the-unit-filtration.md), for $U_L/U_L^1 \cong k_L^\times$, whose finite subgroups are cyclic of order prime to $p$ as $k_L$ is a field of characteristic $p$, and $U_L^i/U_L^{i+1} \cong (k_L, +)$ for $i \ge 1$, a group of exponent $p$
