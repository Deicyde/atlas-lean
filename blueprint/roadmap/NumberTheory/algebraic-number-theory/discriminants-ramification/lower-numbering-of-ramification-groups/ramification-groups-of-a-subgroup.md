# Ramification groups of a subgroup

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete discretely valued fields whose residue fields are perfect of
characteristic $p > 0$, $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, $H$ a subgroup of $G$ and $K' = L^H$, so that
$H = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K')$. Then the ramification groups of $H$, those of the extension $L/K'$, are

$$H_i = G_i \cap H \qquad (i \ge -1).$$

In particular the ramification groups $G_i$, $i \ge 0$, of $L/K$ are those of $L/L_0$, where $L_0$ is the fixed field
of the inertia subgroup $G_0$. Moreover a generator $x$ of $\mathcal{O}_L$ as an $\mathcal{O}_K$-algebra also generates
$\mathcal{O}_L$ as an $\mathcal{O}_{K'}$-algebra.
