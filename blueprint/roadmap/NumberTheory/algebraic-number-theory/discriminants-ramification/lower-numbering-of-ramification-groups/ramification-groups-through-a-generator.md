# Ramification groups through a generator of the ring of integers

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete discretely valued fields whose residue fields are perfect of
characteristic $p > 0$, $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, $v_L$ the normalized valuation of $L$, and let
$x \in \mathcal{O}_L$ generate $\mathcal{O}_L$ as an $\mathcal{O}_K$-algebra. For $s \in G$ and an integer
$i \ge -1$ the following are equivalent:

1. $s$ acts trivially on the quotient ring $\mathcal{O}_L/\mathfrak{m}_L^{i+1}$;
2. $v_L(s(a) - a) \ge i + 1$ for all $a \in \mathcal{O}_L$;
3. $v_L(s(x) - x) \ge i + 1$.

So $s$ lies in the ramification group $G_i$ of
[Ramification groups of a finite Galois extension of local fields](ramification-groups-of-a-finite-galois-extension.md)
if and only if $v_L(s(x) - x) \ge i + 1$.
