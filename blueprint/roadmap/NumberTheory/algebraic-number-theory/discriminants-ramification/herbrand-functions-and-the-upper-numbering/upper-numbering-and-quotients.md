# The upper numbering is compatible with quotients

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete discretely valued fields whose residue fields are perfect of
characteristic $p > 0$, $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, $K'/K$ a Galois subextension and $H = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K')$.
With the ramification groups in the upper numbering $G^v$ of $L/K$ and $(G/H)^v$ of $K'/K$, as on
[Upper numbering of the ramification groups of a finite Galois extension](upper-numbering-of-a-finite-galois-extension.md),

$$G^v H / H = (G/H)^v \qquad (v \ge -1).$$

## Depends on

- [Upper numbering of the ramification groups of a finite Galois extension](upper-numbering-of-a-finite-galois-extension.md), for $G^v$ and $(G/H)^v$

## Proof depends on

- [Herbrand's theorem](herbrand-s-theorem.md), for $G_u H/H = (G/H)_{\varphi_{L/K'}(u)}$ with $u = \psi_{L/K}(v)$
- [Transitivity of the Herbrand functions](transitivity-of-the-herbrand-functions.md), for $\varphi_{L/K'} \circ \psi_{L/K} = \psi_{K'/K}$
- [Upper numbering of the ramification groups of a finite Galois extension](upper-numbering-of-a-finite-galois-extension.md), for $G^v = G_{\psi_{L/K}(v)}$ and $(G/H)^v = (G/H)_{\psi_{K'/K}(v)}$
