# Least common multiple of $1, \dots, n$

**Least common multiple of $1, \dots, n$.** For a natural number $n$, $L_n$ is the least common
multiple $\operatorname{lcm}(1, 2, \dots, n)$ of the integers from $1$ to $n$; in particular
$L_0 = 1$.
