# Kronecker–Jacobi bridge for 4m with m ≡ 3 mod 4

Even subcase `m ≡ 3 (mod 4)` for odd `p`: reduce `4 * m` to `m`
([Jacobi symbol of $4m$ at an odd denominator](jacobi-sym-four-mul-of-odd.md)), identify the
Kronecker symbol with the Jacobi symbol at odd denominators, then apply the bridge for
`D ≡ 3 (mod 4)`
([Kronecker symbol at odd numbers for integers congruent to 3 mod 4](chi4-mul-jacobi-sym-nat-abs-eq-kronecker.md))
to `m` itself. Covers prime and composite odd denominators alike.
