# Odd squares modulo 16

**Odd squares modulo 16.** For every odd natural number $k$, either $k^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{16}$
and $k \equiv 1$ or $7 \pmod 8$, or $k^2 \equiv 9 \pmod{16}$ and $k \equiv 3$ or $5 \pmod 8$.
