# Euler–Fermat congruence modulo prime powers

**Euler–Fermat congruence modulo prime powers.** For every natural number $a$, every prime $p$
and every natural number $r \ge 1$, we have $a^{p^r} \equiv a^{p^{r-1}} \pmod{p^r}$.
