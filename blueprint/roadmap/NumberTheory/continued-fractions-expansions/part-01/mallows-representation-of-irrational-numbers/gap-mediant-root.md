# The mediant of $0/1$ and $1/1$

**The mediant of $0/1$ and $1/1$.** With fractions encoded as pairs (numerator, denominator)
of natural numbers, the mediant of the gap bounded by $0/1$ and $1/1$ is $1/2$, that is, the
pair $(1, 2)$.
