# Zero is absorbing for the Engel trajectory

**Zero is absorbing for the Engel trajectory.** Let $n$, $a$ and $j$ be natural numbers
and let $t_0, t_1, \dots$ be the Engel remainder trajectory of $a$ with respect to $n$. If
$t_j = 0$, then $t_{j+1} = 0$.
