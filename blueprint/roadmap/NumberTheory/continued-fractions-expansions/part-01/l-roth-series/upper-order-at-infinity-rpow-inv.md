# Upper order at infinity of $q^{-\tau}$

**Upper order at infinity of $q^{-\tau}$.** For every real $\tau$, the upper order at infinity
of $q \mapsto (q^{\tau})^{-1}$ is $\max(0, \tau)$; in particular it is $\tau$ when $\tau \ge 0$.
