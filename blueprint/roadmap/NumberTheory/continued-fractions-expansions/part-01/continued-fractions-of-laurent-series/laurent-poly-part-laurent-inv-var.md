# Polynomial parts of the variable and its inverse

**Polynomial parts of the variable and its inverse.** Let $K$ be a field. In
$K((x^{-1}))$ the polynomial part of $x$ is $x$, and the polynomial part of $x^{-1}$ is $0$.
