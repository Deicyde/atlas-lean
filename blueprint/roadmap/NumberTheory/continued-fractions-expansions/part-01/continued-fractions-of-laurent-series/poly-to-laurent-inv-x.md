# The polynomial variable embeds as the Laurent variable

**The polynomial variable embeds as the Laurent variable.** Let $K$ be a field. The
embedding $K[x] \to K((x^{-1}))$ sends the polynomial $x$ to the element $x$ of
$K((x^{-1}))$.
