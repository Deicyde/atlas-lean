# Perrin recurrence at negative indices

**Perrin recurrence at negative indices.** Let $R_{-k}$ denote the Perrin sequence extended
to negative indices. For every natural number $n$, $R_{-n} = R_{-(n+2)} + R_{-(n+3)}$.
