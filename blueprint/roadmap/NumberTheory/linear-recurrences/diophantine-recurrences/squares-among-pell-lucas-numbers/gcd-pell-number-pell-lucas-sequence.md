# Pell and Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime

**Pell and Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime.** For every natural number $n$, the Pell number
$P_n$ and the Pell–Lucas number $Q_n$ satisfy $\gcd(P_n, Q_n) = 1$.
