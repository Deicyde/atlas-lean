# The Lucas-balancing number $C_2$ is $3$

**The Lucas-balancing number $C_2$ is $3$.** For the Lucas-balancing numbers defined by
$C_0 = 3$, $C_1 = 1$ and $C_{n+2} = 6C_{n+1} - C_n$, we have $C_2 = 3$.
