# Two values of the $3$-generalized Pell–Lucas numbers

**Two values of the $3$-generalized Pell–Lucas numbers.** The $3$-generalized Pell–Lucas
numbers $Q^{(3)}_n$ satisfy $Q^{(3)}_3 = 16$ and $Q^{(3)}_6 = 260$.
