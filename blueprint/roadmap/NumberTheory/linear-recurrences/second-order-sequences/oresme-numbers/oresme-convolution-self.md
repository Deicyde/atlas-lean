# Self-convolution of the Oresme numbers

**Self-convolution of the Oresme numbers.** Let $O_m = m/2^m$ denote the Oresme numbers. For
every natural number $n$, $\sum_{k=0}^{n} O_k O_{n-k} = \frac{(n+1) n (n-1)}{6 \cdot 2^n}$.
