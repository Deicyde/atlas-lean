# The zeroth (-r)-Fibonacci polynomial

**The zeroth (-r)-Fibonacci polynomial.** For every commutative ring $A$, natural number
$r$, $x \in A$ and unit $y$ of $A$, the $(-r)$-Fibonacci polynomial satisfies
$U^{(-r)}_0(x, y) = 0$.
