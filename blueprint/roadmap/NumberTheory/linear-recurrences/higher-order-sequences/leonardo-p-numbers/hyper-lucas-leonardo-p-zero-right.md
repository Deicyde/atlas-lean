# Hyper Lucas-Leonardo p-numbers of order zero

**Hyper Lucas-Leonardo p-numbers of order zero.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $n$, the
hyper Lucas-Leonardo $p$-number of order $0$ equals the Lucas-Leonardo $p$-number:
$R^{(0)}_{p,n} = R_{p,n}$.
