# Incomplete Leonardo p-numbers at zero

**Incomplete Leonardo p-numbers at zero.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $n$, the
incomplete Leonardo $p$-number $\mathcal{L}_{p,n}(0)$ equals $1$.
