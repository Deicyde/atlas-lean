# Lucas $p$-numbers at indices $1$ to $p$

**Lucas $p$-numbers at indices $1$ to $p$.** For every natural number $p$ and every $n$ with
$1 \le n \le p$, the Lucas $p$-number $L_{p,n}$ equals $1$.
