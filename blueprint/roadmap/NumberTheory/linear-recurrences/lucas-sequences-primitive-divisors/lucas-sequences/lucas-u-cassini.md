# Cassini's identity for Lucas sequences

**Cassini's identity for Lucas sequences.** For Lucas sequence parameters $(P, Q)$ and every
natural number $n$, the Lucas sequence $U = U(P, Q)$ satisfies
$U_{n+1}^2 - U_n U_{n+2} = Q^n$.
