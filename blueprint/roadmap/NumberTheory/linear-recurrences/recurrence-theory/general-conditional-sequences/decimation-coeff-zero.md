# Zeroth decimation coefficient

**Zeroth decimation coefficient.** For sequences $(A_j)_{j \ge 0}$ and $(s_n)_{n \ge 0}$ in
a commutative ring and natural numbers $m$, $r$, $i$, the decimation coefficient $v_0^{(i)}$
equals $s_i$.
