# Floor quotient of a negated non-multiple

**Floor quotient of a negated non-multiple.** Let $A$ and $B$ be integers with $A \ge 1$ and
$B \ge 2$ such that $B$ does not divide $A$. Then
$-\lfloor -A/B \rfloor = \lfloor A/B \rfloor + 1$.
