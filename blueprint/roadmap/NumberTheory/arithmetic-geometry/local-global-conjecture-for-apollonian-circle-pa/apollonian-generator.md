# The four Apollonian generators as a family

The four generators $S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4$ of the [Apollonian group](apollonian-group.md) of the local-global
statements, collected as a family indexed by $i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ so that they can be compared with the flips
of the proved Apollonian algebra.
