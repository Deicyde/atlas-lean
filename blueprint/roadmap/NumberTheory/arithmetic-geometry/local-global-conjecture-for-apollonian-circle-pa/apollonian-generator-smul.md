# Each Apollonian generator acts as an Apollonian flip

Each generator $S_i$ of the [Apollonian group](apollonian-group.md) of the local-global statements acts on integer
quadruples as the [Apollonian flip](../../../Geometry/euclidean-geometry/circles-and-conics/apollonian-group/apollonian-flip.md),
which replaces the $i$-th entry by twice the sum of the other three minus itself. Since the flips are the
[orthogonal reflections of the Descartes form](../../../Geometry/euclidean-geometry/circles-and-conics/apollonian-group/apollonian-flip-eq-reflection.md),
this group is generated by reflections.

## Sources

- A. Kontorovich and H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings and closed horospheres on hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 24 (2011) 603–648, arXiv:0811.2236v5, equation (1.3) and the matrices $S_1, \dots, S_4$ of the introduction.
