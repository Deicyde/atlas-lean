# The Descartes matrix represents the Descartes form

For every integer quadruple $v$, the quadratic form of the [Descartes matrix](descartes-matrix.md) is the
[Descartes quadratic form](../../../Geometry/euclidean-geometry/circles-and-conics/apollonian-group/descartes-form.md):
$v^{\mathsf T} M v = 2(v_1^2 + v_2^2 + v_3^2 + v_4^2) - (v_1 + v_2 + v_3 + v_4)^2$. This identifies the matrix used
in the local-global statements with the form of the proved Apollonian algebra.

## Sources

- A. Kontorovich and H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings and closed horospheres on hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 24 (2011) 603–648, arXiv:0811.2236v5, Section 2.1.
