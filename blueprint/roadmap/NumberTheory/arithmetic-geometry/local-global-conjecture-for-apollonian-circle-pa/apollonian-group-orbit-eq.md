# The Apollonian group orbit is the Apollonian orbit

For every integer quadruple $v$, the orbit $\{g v : g \in A\}$ of $v$ under the [Apollonian group](apollonian-group.md)
$A$ of the local-global statements equals the
[Apollonian orbit](../../../Geometry/euclidean-geometry/circles-and-conics/apollonian-group/apollonian-orbit.md) of
$v$, the quadruples obtained from $v$ by finitely many flips. Hence everything proved about Apollonian orbits (they
stay on a level set of the Descartes form and preserve integrality) applies to the orbits used in the local-global
statements.

## Sources

- A. Kontorovich and H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings and closed horospheres on hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 24 (2011) 603–648, arXiv:0811.2236v5, Section 2.1.
