# The roots $x_i$ are pairwise distinct

**The roots $x_i$ are pairwise distinct.** For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the elements
$x_1, \dots, x_n$ of the function field $\mathbb{C}(x_1, \dots, x_n)$ are pairwise distinct:
$x_i = x_j$ implies $i = j$.
