# Galois groups over the reals have order 1 or 2

**Galois groups over the reals have order 1 or 2.** If $E$ is a finite Galois extension of
$\mathbb{R}$, then the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{R})$ has order $1$ or $2$.
