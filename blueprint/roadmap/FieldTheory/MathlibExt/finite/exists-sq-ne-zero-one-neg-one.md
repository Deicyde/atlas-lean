# A square avoiding $0$, $1$ and $-1$ in a finite field

**A square avoiding $0$, $1$ and $-1$ in a finite field.** If $F$ is a finite field with
more than five elements, then there is an element $r \in F$ with $r^2 \neq 0$, $r^2 \neq 1$
and $r^2 \neq -1$.
