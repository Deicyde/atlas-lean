# Sep Closed Tensor Hom

The `Ω`-algebra homomorphism `Ω ⊗[K] L → (L →ₐ[K] Ω → Ω)` given on pure
tensors by `ω ⊗ₜ l ↦ (σ ↦ ω * σ l)`.
