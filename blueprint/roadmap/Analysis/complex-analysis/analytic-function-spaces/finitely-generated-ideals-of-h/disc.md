# The open unit disc

**The open unit disc.** The open unit disc
$\mathbb{D} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$, the open ball of radius $1$ about $0$ in
$\mathbb{C}$, taken as a space in its own right whose points are the points of the disc.
