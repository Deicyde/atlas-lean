# Closed form of $\sum u^n/(n+1)$ for complex $u$ in the unit disc

**Closed form of $\sum u^n/(n+1)$ for complex $u$ in the unit disc.** The complex form: `∑' n, uⁿ / (n + 1)` sums to `-log (1 - u) / u`
for `u ≠ 0` in the unit disc.
