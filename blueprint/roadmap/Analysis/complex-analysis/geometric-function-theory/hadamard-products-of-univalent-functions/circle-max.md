# Sup norm of a polynomial on the unit circle

**Sup norm of a polynomial on the unit circle.** For a complex polynomial $Q$, this is the
real number $\sup_{|z| = 1} |Q(z)|$, the supremum of $|Q(z)|$ over all $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with
$|z| = 1$.
