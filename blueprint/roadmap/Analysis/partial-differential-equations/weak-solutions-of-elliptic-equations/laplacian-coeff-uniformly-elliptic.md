# The Laplacian is uniformly elliptic

For every $x$ and every $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^n$,
$1 \cdot \sum_i \xi_i^2 \le \sum_{i,j} \delta^{ij} \xi_i \xi_j$. So the coefficients of $-\Delta$
satisfy the uniform ellipticity hypothesis of interior $H^2$ regularity with $\theta = 1$; with
constant (hence bounded and $C^1$) principal coefficients and $b = c = 0$, this shows that its
hypotheses are jointly satisfiable.
