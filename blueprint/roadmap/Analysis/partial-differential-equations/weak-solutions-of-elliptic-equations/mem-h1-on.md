# Sobolev space $H^1(U)$

A function $u : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ belongs to $H^1(U)$ when $u \in L^2(U)$ and, for every
$i$, the weak partial derivative $\partial_i u$ on $U$ exists and lies in $L^2(U)$.
