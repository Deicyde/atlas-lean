# Parabolic boundary of a space-time cylinder

For $U \subseteq E$ and $T \ge 0$, the parabolic cylinder is $U_T = (0, T] \times U$ (time first)
and its parabolic boundary is

$$\Gamma_T = \big(\{0\} \times \overline U\big) \cup \big([0, T] \times \partial U\big),$$

the closure of $U_T$ minus $U_T$ itself. It does not contain the points $(T, x)$ with $x \in U$.
