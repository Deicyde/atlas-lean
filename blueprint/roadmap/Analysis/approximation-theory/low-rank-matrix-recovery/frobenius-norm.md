# Frobenius norm

The Frobenius norm of a real matrix $X$ is

$$
\lVert X\rVert_F = \Big(\sum_{i, j} X_{ij}^2\Big)^{1/2} = \sqrt{\langle X, X\rangle},
$$

the Euclidean norm of $X$ viewed as a vector of entries; it equals $(\sum_i \sigma_i(X)^2)^{1/2}$.

## Sources

- B. Recht, M. Fazel and P. A. Parrilo, *Guaranteed minimum-rank solutions of linear matrix equations via nuclear norm minimization*, SIAM Review 52 (2010), 471–501, arXiv:0706.4138, Section 2.
