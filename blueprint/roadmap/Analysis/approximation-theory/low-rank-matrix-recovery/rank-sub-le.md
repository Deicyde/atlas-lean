# Subadditivity of the rank

For real matrices $A$ and $B$ of the same size, $\operatorname{rank}(A - B) \le \operatorname{rank} A + \operatorname{rank} B$, since the column space of $A - B$ lies in the sum of the column spaces of $A$ and $B$.

## Sources

- B. Recht, M. Fazel and P. A. Parrilo, *Guaranteed minimum-rank solutions of linear matrix equations via nuclear norm minimization*, SIAM Review 52 (2010), 471–501, arXiv:0706.4138, proof of Theorem 3.2 ("the sub-additivity of the rank").
