# Uniqueness of sparse solutions under `δ_{2s} < 1`

If `Φ` has the restricted isometry property of order `2 s` with a constant `δ < 1`, then two `s`-sparse vectors `x`, `x'` with `Φ x = Φ x'` are equal.

## Sources

- E. J. Candès, *The restricted isometry property and its implications for compressed sensing*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris, Ser. I 346 (2008) 589–592, remark after Theorem 1.2: "if `δ_{2s} < 1`, the `ℓ₀` problem has a unique `s`-sparse solution"
