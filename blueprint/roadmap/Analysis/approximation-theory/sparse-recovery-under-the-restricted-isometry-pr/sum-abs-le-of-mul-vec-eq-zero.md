# The null-space property under restricted isometry

Let `s ≥ 1` and let `Φ` have the restricted isometry property of order `2 s` with a constant `δ < 1`. For every `h` in the nullspace of `Φ` and every set `T₀` of at most `s` indices, `‖h_{T₀}‖₁ ≤ ρ ‖h_{T₀ᶜ}‖₁` with `ρ = √2 δ / (1 - δ)`.

Candès states the lemma for `#T₀ = s`; the proof covers `#T₀ ≤ s` unchanged.

## Sources

- E. J. Candès, *The restricted isometry property and its implications for compressed sensing*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris, Ser. I 346 (2008) 589–592, Lemma 2.2, eq. (15)
