# Existence of bisection sequences

**Existence of bisection sequences.** For every function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and
all reals $a$, $b$, there are sequences $u, v$ forming a bisection sequence for $f$ from $a$ to
$b$.
