# Terms of a convergent series tend to zero

**Terms of a convergent series tend to zero.** If $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ and the
series $\sum_n f(n)$ converges in order to some $s \in \mathbb{R}$, then $f(n) \to 0$ as
$n \to \infty$.
