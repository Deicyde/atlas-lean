# Membership in the polar

**Membership in the polar.** Let $E$ be a real inner product space, $A \subseteq E$ and
$y \in E$. Then $y$ lies in the polar $A^\circ$ of $A$ if and only if $\langle x, y \rangle \le 1$
for every $x \in A$.
