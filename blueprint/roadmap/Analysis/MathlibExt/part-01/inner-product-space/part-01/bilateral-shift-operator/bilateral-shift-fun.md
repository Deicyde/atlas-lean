# Bilateral shift of a sequence

**Bilateral shift of a sequence.** For a function $x : \mathbb{Z} \to K$ with values in any
set $K$, the bilateral (forward) shift of $x$ is the function $Sx : \mathbb{Z} \to K$ given by
$(Sx)(n) = x(n - 1)$.
