# Volume of a domain

**Volume of a domain.** For an open set $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, the volume $|\Omega|$
is the Lebesgue measure of $\Omega$ taken as a real number, with $|\Omega| = 0$ when the
Lebesgue measure of $\Omega$ is infinite.
