# Volume of the unit ball

**Volume of the unit ball.** For $n \ge 0$, $\omega_n$ is the Lebesgue measure of the open
unit ball $\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : |x| < 1\}$ of Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$, as a real
number.
