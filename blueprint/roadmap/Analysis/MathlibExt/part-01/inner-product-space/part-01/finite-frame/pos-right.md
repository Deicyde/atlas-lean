# The upper frame bound is positive

**The upper frame bound is positive.** If a finite family $(f_i)_{i \in \iota}$ in an inner
product space $E$ over $\mathbb{K}$ ($\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$) is a frame with frame bounds $A$
and $B$, then $0 < B$.
