# Conjugates of unimodular scalars are nonzero

**Conjugates of unimodular scalars are nonzero.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or
$\mathbb{C}$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| = 1$, then its conjugate $\bar{\mu}$ is
nonzero.
