# Norm has Eigenvalue le

The norm of an eigenvalue of a bounded linear operator is at most the
operator norm: if `T x = μ • x` for a nonzero vector `x`, then
`‖μ‖ * ‖x‖ = ‖T x‖ ≤ ‖T‖ * ‖x‖`.
