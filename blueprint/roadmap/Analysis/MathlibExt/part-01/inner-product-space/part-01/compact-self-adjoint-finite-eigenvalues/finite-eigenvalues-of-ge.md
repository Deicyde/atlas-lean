# Finiteness of eigenvalues bounded away from zero

**Finiteness of eigenvalues bounded away from zero.** For a compact
self-adjoint operator `T` and any `ε > 0`, the set of eigenvalues `μ` of `T`
with `ε ≤ ‖μ‖` is finite.
