# Riesz–Schauder theorem on a Hilbert space, adjoint form

**Riesz–Schauder theorem on a Hilbert space, adjoint form.** The range of
`μ • 1 - T` is the orthogonal complement of the kernel of `star μ • 1 - T†`.
