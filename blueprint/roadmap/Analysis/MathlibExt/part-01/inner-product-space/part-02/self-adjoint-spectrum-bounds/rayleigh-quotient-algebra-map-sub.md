# Rayleigh Quotient algebra Map sub

Auxiliary lemma: for `t ∈ ℝ` and a nonzero vector `x`, the Rayleigh
quotient of `t • 1 - T` at `x` equals `t` minus the Rayleigh quotient of `T`
at `x`.
