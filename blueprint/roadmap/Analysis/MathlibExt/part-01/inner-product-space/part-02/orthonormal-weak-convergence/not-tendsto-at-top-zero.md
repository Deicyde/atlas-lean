# An orthonormal sequence does not converge strongly to `0`

**An orthonormal sequence does not converge strongly to `0`.**
Weak convergence does not upgrade to norm convergence here: every term of
an orthonormal sequence has norm `1`, so the sequence cannot tend to `0`
in norm.
