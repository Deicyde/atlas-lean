# The identity on ℓ²(ℕ) has norm at most one

**The identity on ℓ²(ℕ) has norm at most one.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or
$\mathbb{C}$. The operator norm of the identity operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is
at most $1$.
