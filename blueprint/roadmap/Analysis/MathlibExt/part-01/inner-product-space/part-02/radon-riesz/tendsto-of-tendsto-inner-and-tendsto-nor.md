# Radon–Riesz theorem, inner-product form

**Radon–Riesz theorem, inner-product form.** If the inner products
`⟪u n, y⟫` converge to `⟪x, y⟫` for every `y` and `‖u n‖ → ‖x‖`, then
`u n → x` in norm.
