# The unilateral shift is injective

**The unilateral shift is injective.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The
unilateral shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is injective.
