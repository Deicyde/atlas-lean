# Adjoint of the unilateral shift

**Adjoint of the unilateral shift.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The
adjoint of the unilateral shift $S$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is the backward shift
$B$: $S^* = B$.
