# Uniform convergence implies pointwise convergence

**Uniform convergence implies pointwise convergence.** If a sequence $(u_n)$ in
$C([0, 1], \mathbb{R})$ converges to $e$ in the sup norm, then $u_n(x) \to e(x)$ for every
$x \in [0, 1]$.
