# Midpoints of distinct $\ell^p$ unit vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ have norm less than one

**Midpoints of distinct $\ell^p$ unit vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ have norm less than one.**
Let $1 < p < \infty$ and equip $\mathbb{R}^2$ with the $\ell^p$ norm. If $x \neq y$ are vectors
with $\|x\| = \|y\| = 1$, then $\|\tfrac{1}{2} (x + y)\| < 1$.
