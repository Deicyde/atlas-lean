# The $\ell^p$ norm of $(1, 1)$ for finite $p$

**The $\ell^p$ norm of $(1, 1)$ for finite $p$.** For every real $p$ with $1 \le p < \infty$,
the vector $(1, 1)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ has $\ell^p$ norm $\|(1, 1)\|_p = 2^{1/p}$, where
$\|(x, y)\|_p = (|x|^p + |y|^p)^{1/p}$.
