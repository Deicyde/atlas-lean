# The $\ell^\infty$ norm of $(1, -1)$

**The $\ell^\infty$ norm of $(1, -1)$.** In $\mathbb{R}^2$ with the $\ell^\infty$ norm
$\|(x, y)\|_\infty = \max(|x|, |y|)$, the vector $(1, -1)$ has norm $\|(1, -1)\|_\infty = 1$.
