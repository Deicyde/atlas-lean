# Norm of $(1, 1) + (1, -1)$ in $\ell^p_2$

**Norm of $(1, 1) + (1, -1)$ in $\ell^p_2$.** For every $p \in [1, \infty]$, let $\ell^p_2$ be
$\mathbb{R}^2$ with the $\ell^p$ norm, and let $u_p = (1, 1)$ and $v_p = (1, -1)$. Then
$\|u_p + v_p\| = 2$.
