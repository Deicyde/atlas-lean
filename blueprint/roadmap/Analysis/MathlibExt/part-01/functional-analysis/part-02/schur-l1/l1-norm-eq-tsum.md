# The $\ell^1$ norm is the sum of the coordinate norms

**The $\ell^1$ norm is the sum of the coordinate norms.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or
$\mathbb{C}$. Every $x \in \ell^1(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ satisfies
$\|x\| = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} |x_i|$.
