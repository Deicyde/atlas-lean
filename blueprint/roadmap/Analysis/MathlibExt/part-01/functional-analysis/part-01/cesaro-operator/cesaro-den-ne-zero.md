# The Cesàro denominator $n + 1$ is nonzero

**The Cesàro denominator $n + 1$ is nonzero.** Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$.
For every natural number $n$, the element $n + 1$ of $\mathbb{K}$ is nonzero.
