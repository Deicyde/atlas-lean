# The Hanner cross-polytopes are Hanner polytopes

**The Hanner cross-polytopes are Hanner polytopes.** For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Hanner
cross-polytope $P_n \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ is a Hanner polytope.
