# Sine Basis Fun

The `k`-th orthonormal sine basis function on `[0, 1]`:
`u_k(x) = √2 sin(kπx)`.
These form an orthonormal eigenbasis of the Green's operator with Dirichlet
boundary conditions on the unit interval.
