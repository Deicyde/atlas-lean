# The space $L^2((0, 1))$

**The space $L^2([0, 1])$.** $L^2([0, 1])$ is the real Hilbert space of square-integrable
real-valued functions on the unit interval, up to equality almost everywhere, with Lebesgue
measure restricted to $[0, 1]$.
