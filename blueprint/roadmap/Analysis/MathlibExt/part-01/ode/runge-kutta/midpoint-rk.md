# Midpoint tableau

**Midpoint tableau.** The explicit Runge–Kutta method with two stages, Butcher tableau
entries $a_{21} = 1/2$ and $a_{11} = a_{12} = a_{22} = 0$, and weights $b_1 = 0$, $b_2 = 1$.
