# Sum of the Green's eigenvalues

**Sum of the Green's eigenvalues.** For the Green's eigenvalues $\lambda_k = 1/(k^2\pi^2)$,
indexed by the positive integers $k$, we have $\sum_{k \ge 1} \lambda_k = 1/6$.
