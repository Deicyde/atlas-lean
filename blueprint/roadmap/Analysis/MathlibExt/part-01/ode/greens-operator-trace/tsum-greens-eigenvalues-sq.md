# Sum of the squared Green's eigenvalues

**Sum of the squared Green's eigenvalues.** For the Green's eigenvalues
$\lambda_k = 1/(k^2\pi^2)$, indexed by the positive integers $k$, we have
$\sum_{k \ge 1} \lambda_k^2 = 1/90$.
