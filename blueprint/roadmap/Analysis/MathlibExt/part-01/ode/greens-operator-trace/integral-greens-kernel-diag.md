# Diagonal integral of the Green's kernel

**Diagonal integral of the Green's kernel.** The Green's kernel $G$ on $[0, 1]$ satisfies
$\int_0^1 G(x, x)\,dx = 1/6$, since $G(x, x) = x(1 - x)$.
