# Uniqueness for the Sturm–Liouville Dirichlet problem on `(0, 1)`:

**Uniqueness for the Sturm–Liouville Dirichlet problem on `[0, 1]`:**
any two `C²` solutions `u₁, u₂` of `-u'' + V u = f` on `[0, 1]` with the
Dirichlet boundary conditions `u(0) = u(1) = 0` (where `V ≥ 0` is continuous)
must agree on `[0, 1]`.
