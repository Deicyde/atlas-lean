# Phase of an exponent vector

**Phase of an exponent vector.** For an angle vector $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and an exponent
vector $e \in \mathbb{Z}^n$, the real phase of $e$ at $\theta$ is $\sum_{j} e_j \theta_j$.
