# Integer vectors of length one

**Integer vectors of length one.** The additive isomorphism from $\mathbb{Z}^1$, the group of
functions $\{0\} \to \mathbb{Z}$ under pointwise addition, to $\mathbb{Z}$ that sends a function
$e$ to its value $e(0)$.
