# Arctangent integral

**Arctangent integral.** For real numbers $a$, $b$ and $d$ with $d \ne 0$,
$\int_a^b \frac{d}{x^2 + d^2}\,dx = \arctan\frac{b}{d} - \arctan\frac{a}{d}$.
