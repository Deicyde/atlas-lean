# Complex Hessian

The complex Hessian `u_{j k̄} = ∂²u/∂z_j∂z̄_k
= (u_{x_j x_k} + u_{y_j y_k} + i (u_{x_j y_k} - u_{y_j x_k})) / 4` of `u : ℂⁿ → ℝ` at `z`.
