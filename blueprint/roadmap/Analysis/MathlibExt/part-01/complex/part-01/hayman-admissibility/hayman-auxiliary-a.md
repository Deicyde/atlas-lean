# Hayman auxiliary function $A$

**Hayman auxiliary function $A$.** For a function $G : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ and a real
number $r$, $A(r)$ is the real part of $r\,G'(r)/G(r)$, where $G'$ is the complex derivative of
$G$.
