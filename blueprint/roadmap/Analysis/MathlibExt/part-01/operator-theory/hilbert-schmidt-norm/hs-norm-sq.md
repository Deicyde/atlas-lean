# Hs Norm Sq

The squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of `T` computed in the family `u`, as an extended
nonnegative real: `∑' n, ‖T (u n)‖ ^ 2` in `ℝ≥0∞`. It is `⊤` exactly when the series of
squares diverges.
