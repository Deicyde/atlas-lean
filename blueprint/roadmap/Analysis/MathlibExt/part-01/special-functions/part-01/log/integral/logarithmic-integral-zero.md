# The logarithmic integral vanishes at zero

**The logarithmic integral vanishes at zero.** The logarithmic integral $\mathrm{li}$
satisfies $\mathrm{li}(0) = 0$; here $0$ lies in its domain since $0 \ge 0$ and $0 \ne 1$.
