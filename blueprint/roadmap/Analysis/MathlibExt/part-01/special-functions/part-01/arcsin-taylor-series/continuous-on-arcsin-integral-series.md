# Continuity of the integrated arcsine series

**Continuity of the integrated arcsine series.** The integrated arcsine series
$F(t) = \sum_{n \ge 0} \binom{2n}{n} 4^{-n}\, t^{2n+1}/(2n+1)^2$ is continuous on the closed
interval $[-1, 1]$.
