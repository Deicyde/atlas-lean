# The integrated arcsine series vanishes at the origin

**The integrated arcsine series vanishes at the origin.** The integrated arcsine series
$F(t) = \sum_{n \ge 0} \binom{2n}{n} 4^{-n}\, t^{2n+1}/(2n+1)^2$ satisfies $F(0) = 0$.
