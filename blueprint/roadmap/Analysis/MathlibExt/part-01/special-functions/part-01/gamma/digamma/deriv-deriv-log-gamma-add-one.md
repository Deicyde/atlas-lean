# Trigamma series

**Trigamma series.** For every real $y > 0$, the second derivative of $\log \Gamma$ at
$y + 1$ equals $\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(y + j + 1)^2}$, where $\Gamma$ is the real Gamma
function.
