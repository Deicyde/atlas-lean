# Mittag-Leffler expansion of $1/(e^u - 1)$

**Mittag-Leffler expansion of $1/(e^u - 1)$.** For every real number $u \neq 0$,

$$
\frac{1}{e^u - 1} = \frac{1}{u} - \frac{1}{2} + \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} \frac{2u}{u^2 + 4\pi^2 m^2}.
$$
