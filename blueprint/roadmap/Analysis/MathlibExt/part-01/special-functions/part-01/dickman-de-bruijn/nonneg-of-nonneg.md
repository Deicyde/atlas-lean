# Nonnegativity of the Dickman–de Bruijn function

**Nonnegativity of the Dickman–de Bruijn function.** If $\rho : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$
is a Dickman–de Bruijn function, then $\rho(u) \ge 0$ for every real $u \ge 0$.
