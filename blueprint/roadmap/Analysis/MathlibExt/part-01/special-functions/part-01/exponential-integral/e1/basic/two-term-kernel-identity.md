# Two-term expansion of $1/(t + z)$

**Two-term expansion of $1/(t + z)$.** Let $z \ne 0$ be a complex number and $t$ a real
number with $t + z \ne 0$. Then
$\frac{1}{t + z} = \frac{1}{z} - \frac{t}{z^2} + \frac{t^2}{z^2 (t + z)}$.
