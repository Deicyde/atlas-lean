# Integrability of $e^{-t} t^n$ on $(0, \infty)$

**Integrability of $e^{-t} t^n$ on $(0, \infty)$.** For every natural number $n$, the
function $t \mapsto e^{-t} t^n$ is Lebesgue integrable on the open interval $(0, \infty)$.
