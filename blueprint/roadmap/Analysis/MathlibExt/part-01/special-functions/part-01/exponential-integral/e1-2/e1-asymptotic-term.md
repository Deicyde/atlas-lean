# Asymptotic term of $E_1$

**Asymptotic term of $E_1$.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a nonzero complex number $z$, the
$n$-th term of the asymptotic expansion of the exponential integral $E_1$ is
$\frac{e^{-z}}{z} \cdot \frac{(-1)^n \, n!}{z^n}$.
