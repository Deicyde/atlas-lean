# The Lambert family $L_m$

**The Lambert family $L_m$.** For a complex number $q$ with $|q| < 1$ and an integer $m \ge 1$,
the Lambert family is $L_m(q) = \sum_{n \ge 1} q^{mn} / (1 - q^{2n})^m$.
