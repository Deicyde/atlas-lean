# Lambert series summand

**Lambert series summand.** For a complex number $q$ with $|q| < 1$ and a natural number $k$,
the $k$-th summand of the basic Lambert series is $q^{k+1} / (1 - q^{k+1})$, that is, the term
$q^n / (1 - q^n)$ with $n = k + 1$.
