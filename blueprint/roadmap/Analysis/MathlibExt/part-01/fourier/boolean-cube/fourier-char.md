# Fourier Char

The Fourier character on the subset lattice: `χ_S(T) = (−1)^|S ∩ T|`.
This is the `Finset`-indexed form of the uniform-cube transform;
`fourierChar_eq_cubeChi` identifies it with the canonical `cubeChi`
after reindexing.
