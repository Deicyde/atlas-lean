# Young's convolution inequality

**Young's convolution inequality** on a finite abelian additive group: for
`1 < p`, `1 < q`, `1 < r` with `1 / p + 1 / q = 1 + 1 / r`,
`‖f * g‖_r ≤ ‖f‖_p * ‖g‖_q` in the finite-sum `ℓ^p` norms.
