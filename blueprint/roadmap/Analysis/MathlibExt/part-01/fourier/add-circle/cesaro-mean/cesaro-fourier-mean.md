# Cesaro Fourier Mean

The `N`-th Cesàro–Fourier mean of `f`, given by
`σ_N f(x) = (N+1)⁻¹ • ∑_{k=0}^{N} S_k f(x)`,
where `S_k f` is the `k`-th partial Fourier sum.
