# Sum range fourier shift pos

Companion to `sum_range_fourier_shift_neg`: the sum
`∑_{k=0}^{N} fourier ((N+1 : ℤ) - (k : ℤ)) x` equals the sum over the positive range
`n ∈ [1, N+1]`.
