# Symmetry of the Fejér kernel

**Symmetry of the Fejér kernel.** Let $T > 0$. For every natural number $N$ and every
$x \in \mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$, the $N$-th Fejér kernel satisfies $K_N(-x) = K_N(x)$.
