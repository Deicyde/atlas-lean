# Continuity of the Fejér kernel

**Continuity of the Fejér kernel.** Let $T > 0$. For every natural number $N$, the $N$-th
Fejér kernel $K_N$ is a continuous function from the circle $\mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$ to
$\mathbb{C}$.
