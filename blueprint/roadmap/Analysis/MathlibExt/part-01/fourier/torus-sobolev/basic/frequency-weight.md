# Squared frequency

**Squared frequency.** For a frequency $k = (k_1, k_2) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the frequency
weight is $|k|^2 = k_1^2 + k_2^2$, the squared frequency of the mode $e^{i k \cdot x}$ on the
torus $(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$.
