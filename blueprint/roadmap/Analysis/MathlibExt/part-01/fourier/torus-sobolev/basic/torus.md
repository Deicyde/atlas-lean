# The torus of period $2\pi$

**The torus of period $2\pi$.** The two-dimensional torus
$\mathbb{T}^2 = (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$, the product of two copies of the circle
$\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$.
