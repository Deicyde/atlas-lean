# Integer frequencies

**Integer frequencies.** The frequencies are the elements of the lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$,
that is, the pairs $k = (k_1, k_2)$ of integers; they index the Fourier modes of the torus
$(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$.
