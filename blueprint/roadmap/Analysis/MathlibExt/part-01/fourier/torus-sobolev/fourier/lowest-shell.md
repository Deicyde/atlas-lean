# Lowest frequency shell

**Lowest frequency shell.** The lowest shell is the set $\{e_1, -e_1, e_2, -e_2\}$ of
frequencies in $\mathbb{Z}^2$, where $e_1 = (1, 0)$ and $e_2 = (0, 1)$; these are the four nonzero
frequencies of smallest length.
