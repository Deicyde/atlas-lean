# The lowest-shell witness has mean zero

**The lowest-shell witness has mean zero.** The lowest-shell witness, the function
$w(x_1, x_2) = (\cos x_1 + \cos x_2)/(2\pi)$ on the torus $(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$, has
mean $0$.
