# Sum norm sq dft Fun le of eq zero of not Mem

Concentration of `f̂`: if `f` vanishes outside `A`, then the squared
`ℓ²` mass of `f̂` on any set `B` is at most `(|A| * |B| / |G|)` times the
total squared `ℓ²` mass of `f̂`.
