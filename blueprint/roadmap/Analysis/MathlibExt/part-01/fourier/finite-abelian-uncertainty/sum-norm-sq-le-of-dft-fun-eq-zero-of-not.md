# Sum norm sq le of dft Fun eq zero of not Mem

Concentration of `f`: if `f̂` vanishes outside `B`, then the squared
`ℓ²` mass of `f` on any set `A` is at most `(|A| * |B| / |G|)` times the
total squared `ℓ²` mass of `f`.
