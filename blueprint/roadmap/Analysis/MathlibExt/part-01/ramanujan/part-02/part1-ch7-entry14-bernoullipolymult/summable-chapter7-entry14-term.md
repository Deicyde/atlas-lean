# Convergence of $\sum 1/(n(n^x - 1))$

**Convergence of $\sum 1/(n(n^x - 1))$.** For every real $x > 0$ the series
$\sum_{n \ge 2} \frac{1}{n(n^x - 1)}$ converges absolutely.
