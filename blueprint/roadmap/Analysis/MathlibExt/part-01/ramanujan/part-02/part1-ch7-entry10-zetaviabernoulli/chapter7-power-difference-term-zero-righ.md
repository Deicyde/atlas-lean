# Power-difference terms vanish at $x = 0$

**Power-difference terms vanish at $x = 0$.** For every $r \in \mathbb{C}$ and every natural
number $j$, the power-difference term $(j + 1)^r - (j + 1 + x)^r$ vanishes at $x = 0$.
