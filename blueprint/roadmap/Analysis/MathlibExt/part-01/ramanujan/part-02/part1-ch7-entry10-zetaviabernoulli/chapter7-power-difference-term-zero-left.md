# Power-difference terms vanish at exponent zero

**Power-difference terms vanish at exponent zero.** For every $x \in \mathbb{C}$ and every
natural number $j$, the power-difference term $(j + 1)^r - (j + 1 + x)^r$ vanishes at $r = 0$.
