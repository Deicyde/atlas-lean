# Summability of the Lambert series $\sum_k 1/(e^{kx}+1)$

**Summability of the Lambert series $\sum_k 1/(e^{kx}+1)$.** For every real $x > 0$, the
series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{e^{kx} + 1}$ is summable.
