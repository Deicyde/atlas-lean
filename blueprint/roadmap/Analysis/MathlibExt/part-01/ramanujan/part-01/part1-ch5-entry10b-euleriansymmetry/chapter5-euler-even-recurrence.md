# Binomial recurrence for the even-index Euler numbers

**Binomial recurrence for the even-index Euler numbers.** For every natural number $m$,
$\sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{2m}{2k} E_{2k}$ is $1$ if $m = 0$ and $0$ if $m \ge 1$.
