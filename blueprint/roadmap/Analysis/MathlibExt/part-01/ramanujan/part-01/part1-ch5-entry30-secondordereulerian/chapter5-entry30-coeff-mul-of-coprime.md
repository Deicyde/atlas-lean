# Entry 30 coefficients are multiplicative

**Entry 30 coefficients are multiplicative.** For every function $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$
and all coprime $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Entry 30 coefficients $c_a$ satisfy
$c_a(mn) = c_a(m)\,c_a(n)$.
