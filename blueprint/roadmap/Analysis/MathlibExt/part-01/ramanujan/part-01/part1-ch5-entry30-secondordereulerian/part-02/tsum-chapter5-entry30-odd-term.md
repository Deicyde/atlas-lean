# Tsum chapter5 Entry30 Odd Term

`∑ n ^ (-s)` over squarefree `n ≥ 1` with an odd number of prime factors is
`(ζ(s) ^ 2 - ζ(2s)) / (2 ζ(s) ζ(2s))` for `1 < s.re`.
