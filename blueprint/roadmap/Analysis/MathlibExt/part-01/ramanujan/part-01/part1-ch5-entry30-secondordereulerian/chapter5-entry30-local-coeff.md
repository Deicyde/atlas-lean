# Local factor

**Local factor.** Given $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$ and natural numbers $p$ and $e$,
the local factor of $a$ at $p$ with exponent $e$ is $1$ if $e = 0$, $a(p)$ if $e = 1$, and $0$
if $e \ge 2$.
