# Sum of $n^{-s}$ over squarefree $n$

**Sum of $n^{-s}$ over squarefree $n$.** For every complex number $s$ with
$\operatorname{Re} s > 1$, the sum of $n^{-s}$ over the squarefree $n \ge 1$ equals
$\zeta(s)/\zeta(2s)$.
