# Entry 30 coefficients vanish at higher prime powers

**Entry 30 coefficients vanish at higher prime powers.** For every function
$a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$, every prime $p$ and every $e \ge 2$, the Entry 30 coefficient
satisfies $c_a(p^e) = 0$.
