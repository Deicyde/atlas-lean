# Sum of $n^{-s}$ over non-squarefree $n$

**Sum of $n^{-s}$ over non-squarefree $n$.** For every complex number $s$ with
$\operatorname{Re} s > 1$, the sum of $n^{-s}$ over the non-squarefree $n \ge 1$ equals
$\zeta(s)(\zeta(2s) - 1)/\zeta(2s)$.
