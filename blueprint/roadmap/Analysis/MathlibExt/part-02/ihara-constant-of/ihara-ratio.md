# Normalized Ihara ratio

**Normalized Ihara ratio.** For a sequence $N : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and
$g \in \mathbb{N}$, the normalized ratio of $N$ at $g$ is the extended nonnegative real $N(g)/g$
when $g > 0$, and $0$ when $g = 0$.
