# Continuity of the averaging operator at zero

**Continuity of the averaging operator at zero.** The function
$E_1 : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ given by $E_1(u) = \int_0^1 e^{tu}\,dt$ is continuous at $0$.
