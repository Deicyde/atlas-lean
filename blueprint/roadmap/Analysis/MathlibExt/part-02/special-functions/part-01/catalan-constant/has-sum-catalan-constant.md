# The series for Catalan's constant converges to it

**The series for Catalan's constant converges to it.** The series
$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{(2n+1)^2}$ converges unconditionally, and its sum is Catalan's
constant $G$.
