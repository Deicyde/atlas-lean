# Homothety of period pairs is transitive

**Homothety of period pairs is transitive.** For period pairs $L_1$, $L_2$, $L_3$,
if $L_1$ is homothetic to $L_2$ and $L_2$ is homothetic to $L_3$, then $L_1$ is homothetic
to $L_3$.
