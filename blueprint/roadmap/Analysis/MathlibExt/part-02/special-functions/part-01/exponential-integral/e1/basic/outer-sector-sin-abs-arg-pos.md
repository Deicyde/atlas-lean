# Positivity of $\sin |\arg z|$ in the outer sector

**Positivity of $\sin |\arg z|$ in the outer sector.** Let $z \ne 0$ be a complex number with
$\pi/2 \le |\arg z| < \pi$, where $\arg z \in (-\pi, \pi]$ is the principal argument. Then
$\sin |\arg z| > 0$.
