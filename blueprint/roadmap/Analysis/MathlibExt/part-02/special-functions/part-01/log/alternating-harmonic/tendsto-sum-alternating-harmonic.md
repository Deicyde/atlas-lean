# The alternating harmonic series sums to $\log 2$

**The alternating harmonic series sums to $\log 2$.** The partial sums
$\sum_{i=0}^{k-1} (-1)^i / (i + 1)$ of real numbers converge to $\log 2$ as $k \to \infty$.
