# Logarithmic derivative of the real Gamma function

**Logarithmic derivative of the real Gamma function.** For every real $u > 0$, the
derivative of $\log \Gamma$ at $u$ equals $\Gamma'(u) / \Gamma(u)$, where $\Gamma$ is the real
Gamma function.
