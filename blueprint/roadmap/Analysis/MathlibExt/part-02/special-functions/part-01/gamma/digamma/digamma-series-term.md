# Terms of the complex digamma series

**Terms of the complex digamma series.** For $j \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s \in \mathbb{C}$, the
$j$-th term of the complex digamma series at $s$ is $\frac{1}{j+1} - \frac{1}{j+s}$, where
$1/0$ is read as $0$.
