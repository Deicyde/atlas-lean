# Kantorovich's inequality

**Kantorovich's inequality**, scalar weighted form: if the weights are
nonnegative, sum to `1`, every `λ i` lies in `[m, M]`, and `0 < m`, then
`(∑ w i * λ i) * (∑ w i / λ i) ≤ (M + m)² / (4 * m * M)`.
