# Shifted unilateral shift is invertible outside the unit disk

**Shifted unilateral shift is invertible outside the unit disk.** For `‖μ‖ > 1`, `S - μ • 1` is a unit: it equals
`(algebraMap (-μ)) * (1 - μ⁻¹ • S)`, a product of units.
