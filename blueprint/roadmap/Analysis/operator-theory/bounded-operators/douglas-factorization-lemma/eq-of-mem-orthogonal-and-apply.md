# Injectivity of an operator on the orthogonal complement of its kernel

**Injectivity of an operator on the orthogonal complement of its kernel.** Two points of `(ker B)ᗮ` with the same image under `B` are equal, since
their difference lies in `ker B ∩ (ker B)ᗮ`.
