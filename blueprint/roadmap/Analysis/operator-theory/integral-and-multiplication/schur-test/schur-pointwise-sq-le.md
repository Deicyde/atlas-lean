# Per-row estimate in Schur's test

**Per-row estimate in Schur's test.** The per-row estimate in Schur's test: the `i`-th coordinate of `A x`
satisfies `‖(A x) i‖² ≤ R * ∑ j, ‖A i j‖ * ‖x j‖²` when every row sum of
`‖A i j‖` is at most `R`.
