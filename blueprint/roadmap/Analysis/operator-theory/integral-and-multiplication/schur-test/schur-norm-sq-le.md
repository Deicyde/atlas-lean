# Squared-norm estimate in Schur's test

**Squared-norm estimate in Schur's test.** The squared-norm estimate in Schur's test: `‖A x‖² ≤ R * C * ‖x‖²`
when every row sum of `‖A i j‖` is at most `R` and every column sum is at
most `C`.
