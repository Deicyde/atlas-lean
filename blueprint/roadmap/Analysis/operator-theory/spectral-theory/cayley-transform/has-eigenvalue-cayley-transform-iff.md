# Eigenvalues of the Cayley transform

**Eigenvalues of the Cayley transform.** The eigenvalue correspondence: for `μ ≠ 1`, `μ` is an eigenvalue of the
Cayley transform `U` iff `i * (1 + μ) / (1 - μ)` is an eigenvalue of `A`.
