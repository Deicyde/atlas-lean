# Resolvent factorization for the Cayley transform

**Resolvent factorization for the Cayley transform.** The resolvent-type factorization behind the spectral mapping: for
`μ ≠ 1` and `λ = i * (1 + μ) / (1 - μ)`,
`μ • 1 - U = ((μ - 1) • (A - λ • 1)) * (A + i • 1)⁻¹`.
