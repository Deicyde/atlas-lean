# Eigenvectors for nonzero eigenvalues lie in the range

**Eigenvectors for nonzero eigenvalues lie in the range.** An eigenvector for a nonzero eigenvalue lies in the range: if
`T v = μ • v` with `μ ≠ 0`, then `v = T (μ⁻¹ • v)`.
