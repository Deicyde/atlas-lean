# Hilbert–Schmidt matrix maps ℓ² vectors into ℓ²

**Hilbert–Schmidt matrix maps ℓ² vectors into ℓ².** The coordinate series of a Hilbert–Schmidt matrix applied to an `ℓ²` vector is in `ℓ²`.
