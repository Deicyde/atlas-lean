# Klein's inequality

**Klein's inequality** (sum form): for a convex function `f`
differentiable at the eigenvalues of `S`, the tangent-line sum at the
eigenvalues of `S` evaluated on the diagonal of `T` is at most the sum
of `f` over the eigenvalues of `T`.

When the basis is an eigenbasis of $S$ the left side is $\operatorname{tr} f(S) + \operatorname{tr}(f'(S)(T - S))$, and the inequality reads $\operatorname{tr}[f(T) - f(S) - (T - S) f'(S)] \ge 0$, Klein's inequality in Carlen's form.

## Sources

- E. A. Carlen, Trace inequalities and quantum entropy: an introductory course, lecture-notes draft of May 3, 2009 (published in Contemp. Math. 529, 2010), Theorem 2.11. Theorem numbers refer to that draft.
