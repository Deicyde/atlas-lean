# Cauchy interlacing

**Cauchy interlacing.** For a codimension-one subspace `S`, the
eigenvalues `μₖ` of the compression of a self-adjoint operator `T`
interlace the eigenvalues `λₖ` of `T`:
`λₖ₊₁ ≤ μₖ ≤ λₖ`.
