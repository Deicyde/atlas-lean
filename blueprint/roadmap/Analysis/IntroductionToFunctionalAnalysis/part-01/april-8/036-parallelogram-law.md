# Proposition (Parallelogram law)

Let $H$ be a pre-Hilbert space. Then for any $u, v \in H$, we have $||u+v||^2 + ||u-v||^2 = 2\left(||u||^2 + ||v||^2\right)$. In addition, if $H$ is a normed vector space satisfying this equality, then $H$ is a pre-Hilbert space.
