# Theorem (April 27, 2021)

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space, and let $A: H \to H$ be a bounded linear operator. Then $(\operatorname{Ran}(A))^\perp = \operatorname{Null}(A^\ast)$, where $\operatorname{Ran}(A)$ is the range of $A$ (the set of all vectors of the form $Au$), and $\operatorname{Null}(A^\ast)$ is the nullspace of $A^\ast$ (the set of all vectors for which $A^\ast u = 0$).
