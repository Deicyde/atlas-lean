# Theorem (Parseval's identity)

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space, and let $\{e_n\}$ be a countable orthonormal basis of $H$. Then for all $u \in H$, $\sum_{n} |\langle u, e_n \rangle|^2 = ||u||^2$.
