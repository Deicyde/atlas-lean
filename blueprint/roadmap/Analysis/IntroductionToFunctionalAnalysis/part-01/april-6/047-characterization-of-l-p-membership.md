# Proposition (Characterization of $L^p$ membership)

Let $E \subset \mathbb{R}$ be measurable. Then $f \in L^p(E)$ if and only if (letting $n$ range over positive integers) $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{[-n, n] \cap E} |f|^p < \infty$.
