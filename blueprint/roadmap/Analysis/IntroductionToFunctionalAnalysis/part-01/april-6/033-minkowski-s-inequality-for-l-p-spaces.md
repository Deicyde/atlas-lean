# Theorem (Minkowski's inequality for $L^p$ spaces)

If $1 \leq p \leq \infty$ and $f, g: E \to \mathbb{C}$ are two measurable functions, then $||f+g||_{L^p(E)} \leq ||f||_{L^p(E)} + ||g||_{L^p(E)}$.
