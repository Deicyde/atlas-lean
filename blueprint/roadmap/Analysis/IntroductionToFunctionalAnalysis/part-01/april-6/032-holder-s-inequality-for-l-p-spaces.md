# Theorem (Holder's inequality for $L^p$ spaces)

If $1 \leq p \leq \infty$ and $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} = 1$, and $f, g: E \to \mathbb{C}$ are measurable functions, then $\int_E |fg| \leq ||f||_{L^p(E)} ||g||_{L^q(E)}$.
