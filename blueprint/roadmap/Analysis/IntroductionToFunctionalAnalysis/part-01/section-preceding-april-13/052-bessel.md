# Theorem (Bessel)

Let $\{e_n\}$ be a countable (finite or countably infinite) orthonormal subset of a pre-Hilbert space $H$. Then for all $u \in H$, we have $\sum_n |\langle u, e_n \rangle|^2 \leq ||u||^2$.
