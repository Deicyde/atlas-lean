# Hardy–Littlewood Tauberian theorem

**Hardy–Littlewood Tauberian theorem** (1914). If `aₙ ≥ 0` and the power series
`∑ aₙ rⁿ` converges for every `r ∈ (0, 1)` with Abel limit `A` as `r → 1⁻`, then
the partial sums of `a` converge to `A`.
