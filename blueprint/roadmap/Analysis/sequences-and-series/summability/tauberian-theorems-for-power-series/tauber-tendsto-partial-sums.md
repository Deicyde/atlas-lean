# Tauber's theorem

**Tauber's theorem** (1897). If the power series `∑ aₙ rⁿ` converges (in the
Abel sense) to `A` as `r → 1⁻` and `n * aₙ → 0`, then the partial sums of `a`
converge to `A`.
