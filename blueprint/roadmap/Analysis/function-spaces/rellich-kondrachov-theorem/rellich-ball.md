# Rellich ball of weighted square-summable sequences

**Rellich ball of weighted square-summable sequences.** The Rellich ball of radius `M`: sequences in `ℓ²(ℕ, 𝕜)` whose weighted
`ℓ²` energy is at most `M ^ 2`.
