# Proposition 12.14

For any $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $\mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) \subset \partial(\mathbb{B}^{n} \times \mathbb{B}^{n}) = (\mathbb{B}^{n} \times \mathbb{S}^{n-1}) \cup (\mathbb{S}^{n-1} \times \mathbb{B}^{n}) = (\mathbb{R}^{n} \times \mathbb{S}^{n-1}) \cup (\mathbb{S}^{n-1} \times \mathbb{S}^{n-1}) \cup (\mathbb{S}^{n-1} \times \mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $\mathrm{WF}(u) \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ are closed sets and under projection onto the first variable $\pi_{1}(\mathrm{WF}(u)) = \operatorname{sing\,supp}(u) \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$, $\pi_{1}(\mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u)) = \operatorname{Css}(u) \subset \mathbb{B}^{n}$.
