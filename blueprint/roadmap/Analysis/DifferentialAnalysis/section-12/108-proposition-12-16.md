# Proposition 12.16

For any $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $(p, q) \in \partial(\mathbb{B}^{n} \times \mathbb{B}^{n})$, $(p, q) \notin \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) \Longleftrightarrow u = u_{1} + u_{2}$, $u_{1}, u_{2} \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $p \notin \operatorname{Css}(u_{1})$, $q \notin \operatorname{Css}(\widehat{u_{2}})$.
