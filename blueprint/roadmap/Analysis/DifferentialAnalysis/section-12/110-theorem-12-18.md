# Theorem 12.18

For $u, v \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $uv \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is unambiguously defined provided $(p, \omega) \in \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) \cap (\mathbb{B}^{n} \times \mathbb{S}^{n-1}) \Longrightarrow (p, -\omega) \notin \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(v)$; and $u * v \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is unambiguously defined provided $(\theta, q) \in \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) \cap (\mathbb{S}^{n-1} \times \mathbb{B}^{n}) \Longrightarrow (-\theta, q) \notin \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(v)$.
