# Lemma 12.13

If $(p, q) \notin \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u)$ then if $p \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$ there exists a neighbourhood $U \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ of $p$ and a neighbourhood $U' \subset \mathbb{B}^{n}$ of $q$ such that for all $\phi \in \mathcal{C}_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ with support in $U$, $U' \cap \operatorname{Css}(\widehat{\phi u}) = \emptyset$; similarly if $p \in \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$ then there exists a neighbourhood $\tilde{U} \subset \mathbb{B}^{n}$ of $p$ such that $U' \cap \operatorname{Css}(\widehat{\psi_{R} u}) = \emptyset$ if $\operatorname{Csp}(\omega_{R}) \subset \tilde{U}$.
