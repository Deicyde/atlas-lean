# Lemma 12.5

If $K_{i} \subset \mathbb{B}^{n}$, $i=1,2$, are two disjoint closed (hence compact) subsets then we can define an unambiguous pairing $\{u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n}) \mid \operatorname{Css}(u) \subset K_{1}\} \times \{u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n}) \mid \operatorname{Css}(u) \subset K_{2}\} \ni (u_{1}, u_{2}) \longmapsto u_{1}(u_{2}) \in \mathbb{C}$.
