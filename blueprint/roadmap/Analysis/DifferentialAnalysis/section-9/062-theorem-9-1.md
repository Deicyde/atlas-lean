# Theorem 9.1

Fourier transform $\mathcal{F} \colon \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n) \to \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is an isomorphism with inverse $\mathcal{G} \colon \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n) \to \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $\mathcal{G}\psi(y) = (2\pi)^{-n} \int e^{iy \cdot \xi} \psi(\xi)\, d\xi$.
