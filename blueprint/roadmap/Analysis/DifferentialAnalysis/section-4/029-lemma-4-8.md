# Lemma 4.8

If $f$ and $g$ are measurable then $\left|\int f g \, d\mu\right| \leq \|f\|_{p}\|g\|_{q}$ for any $1 < p < \infty$, with $\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1$.
