# Theorem 4.6

If $f_n$ is a sequence of integrable functions, $f_k \to f$ a.e. and $|f_n| \leq g$ for some integrable $g$ then $f$ is integrable and $$\int f\, d\mu = \lim_{n \to \infty} \int f_n\, d\mu.$$
