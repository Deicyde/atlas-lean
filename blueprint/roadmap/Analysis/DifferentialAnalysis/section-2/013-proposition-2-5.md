# Proposition 2.5

If $0 \leq u \in (\mathcal{C}_0(X))'$, for $X$ a locally compact metric space, then each open subset of $X$ is $\mu^*$-measurable for the outer measure defined by (1.11) and (1.12) and $\mu$ in (1.11) is its measure.
