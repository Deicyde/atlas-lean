# Theorem 11.6 (Hörmander, Theorem 4.1.1)

If $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $\varphi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ then $u * \varphi \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n}) \cap \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and if $\operatorname{supp}(\varphi) \Subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ then $\operatorname{supp}(u * \varphi) \subset \operatorname{supp}(u) + \operatorname{supp}(\varphi)$. For any multi-index $\alpha$, $D^{\alpha}(u * \varphi) = D^{\alpha} u * \varphi = u * D^{\alpha} \varphi$.
