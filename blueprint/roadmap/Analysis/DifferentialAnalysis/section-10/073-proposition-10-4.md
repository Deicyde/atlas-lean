# Proposition 10.4

Schwartz space can be written in terms of weighted Sobolev spaces: $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n}) = \bigcap_{k} \langle x \rangle^{-k} H^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
