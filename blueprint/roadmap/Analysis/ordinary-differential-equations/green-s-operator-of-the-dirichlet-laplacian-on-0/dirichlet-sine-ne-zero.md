# Dirichlet sine eigenfunctions are nonzero

**Dirichlet sine eigenfunctions are nonzero.** The sine eigenfunctions are nonzero (so they are genuine
eigenfunctions, not the trivial solution). The witness point is
`x = 1 / (2k)`, where `u_k = √2`.
