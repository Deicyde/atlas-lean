# Dirichlet eigenvalue equation in Sturm-Liouville form

**Dirichlet eigenvalue equation in Sturm-Liouville form.** The Dirichlet eigenvalue equation in Sturm-Liouville form:
`-u_k'' = λ_k u_k` with `λ_k = (kπ)²`.
