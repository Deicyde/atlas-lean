# Exponential decay of a Hurwitz semigroup

**Exponential decay of a Hurwitz semigroup.** If `A` is Hurwitz, there are `M, α > 0`
with `‖exp(t A) v‖ ≤ M e^{-α t} ‖v‖` for all `t ≥ 0` and all vectors `v`.
