# Theorem (BKT 2)

With the same setting as BKT, there exists $t \in D$ such that for any subset $Y \subset X$ with $|Y| \geq p^{-\epsilon} |X|$, we have $|\pi_t(Y)| \geq p^\epsilon |X|^{1/2}$.
