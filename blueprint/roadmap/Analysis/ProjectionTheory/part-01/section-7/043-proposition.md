# Proposition (Section 7, Subsection 7.3: Multiplicative Convolution and Projections)

$$\lVert (\pi_q(f *_M g))^*_h \rVert_{L^\infty} \leq \lVert (\pi_q f)_h \rVert_{L^2} \lVert (\pi_q g)_h \rVert_{L^2}.$$
