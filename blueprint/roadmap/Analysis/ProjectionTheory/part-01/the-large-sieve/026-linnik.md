# Theorem (Linnik)

If $f \colon [N] \to \mathbb{C}$ and $M \leq N^{1/2}$, then $$\sum_{p \in P_M} \|(\pi_p f)_H\|_{L^2}^{2} \lesssim \frac{N}{M} \sum_{n} |f_H(n)|^2.$$
