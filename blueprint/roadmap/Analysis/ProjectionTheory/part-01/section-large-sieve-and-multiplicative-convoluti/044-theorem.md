# Theorem (Section 'Large Sieve and Multiplicative Convolution')

If $f \colon [N_1] \to \mathbb{C}$ and $g \colon [N_2] \to \mathbb{C}$, then $f *_M g \colon [N] \to \mathbb{C}$, where $N = N_1 N_2$, and $$\sum_{p \in P_M} \lVert (\pi_q(f *_M g))_h^* \rVert_{L^\infty}^2 \lesssim \left(\left(\frac{N_1}{M} + M\right) \left(\frac{N_2}{M} + M\right)\right)^{1/2} \lVert f \rVert_{L^2} \lVert g \rVert_{L^2}.$$
