# Summability of convolution terms for $\ell^1$ and $\ell^p$ sequences

**Summability of convolution terms for $\ell^1$ and $\ell^p$ sequences.** The terms of a convolution of an `ℓ¹` sequence with an `ℓ^p` sequence are
summable.
