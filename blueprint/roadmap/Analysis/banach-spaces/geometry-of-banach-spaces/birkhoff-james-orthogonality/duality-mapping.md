# Duality mapping of a normed space

**Duality mapping of a normed space.** The **duality mapping** (duality set) at `x`: the functionals `f` with
`f x = ‖x‖ ^ 2` and `‖f‖ = ‖x‖`. At `x = 0` this is the singleton `{0}`.
