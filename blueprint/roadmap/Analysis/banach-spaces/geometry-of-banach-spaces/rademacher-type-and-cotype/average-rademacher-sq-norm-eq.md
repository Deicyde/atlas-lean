# Average squared norm of a Rademacher sum

**Average squared norm of a Rademacher sum.** The average (uniform over sign vectors) of `‖∑ᵢ εᵢ • xᵢ‖²` is exactly
`∑ᵢ ‖xᵢ‖²`.
