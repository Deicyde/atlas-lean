# Bound on the average Rademacher norm

**Bound on the average Rademacher norm.** The average Rademacher norm is at most `(∑ᵢ ‖xᵢ‖²)^{1/2}`
(Cauchy–Schwarz for the finite average, plus the exact second moment).
