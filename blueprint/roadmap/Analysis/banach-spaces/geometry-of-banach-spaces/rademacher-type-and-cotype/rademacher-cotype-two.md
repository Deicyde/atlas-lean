# Hilbert spaces have Rademacher cotype 2

**Hilbert spaces have Rademacher cotype 2.** Rademacher cotype 2 with constant `1`: `∑ᵢ ‖xᵢ‖²` is at most the
average of the squared Rademacher sums.
