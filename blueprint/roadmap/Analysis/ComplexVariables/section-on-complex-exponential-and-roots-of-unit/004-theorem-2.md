# Theorem 2 [1]

The roots of $z^{n} = 1$ are $1, \omega, \omega^{2}, \cdots, \omega^{n-1}$, where $\omega = \cos \frac{2\pi}{n} + i \sin \frac{2\pi}{n}$.
