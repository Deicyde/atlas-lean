# Hilbert Adjoint Closed Range Wanted

Show that if $X, Y$ are Hilbert spaces, $A \in L(X, Y)$, and $\mathrm{Ran}\,A$ is closed then
$\mathrm{Ran}\,A^*$ is closed (where $A^* \in L(Y, X)$ is the Hilbert space adjoint).
