# The space $L^2((1,2))$

**The space $L^2([1,2])$.** $L^2([1,2])$ is the space of real-valued functions on
$\mathbb{R}$ that are square-integrable for Lebesgue measure restricted to $[1,2]$, taken modulo
equality almost everywhere on $[1,2]$.
