# Sum sin pi mul div of even of even

Lemma 7: for `N` positive and even and `q` even,
`∑_{ℓ=1}^{N/2} sin(q ℓ π / N)` is `0` for `q ≡ 0 (mod 4)` and `cot(q π / (2N))`
for `q ≡ 2 (mod 4)`. The paper's positivity of `q` is dropped.
