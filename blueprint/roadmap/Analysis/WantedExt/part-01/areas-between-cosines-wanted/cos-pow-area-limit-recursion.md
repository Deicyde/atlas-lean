# Cos Pow Area limit recursion

Theorem 3: for `n ≥ 3` the limits `A_n` and `A_{n-2}` exist and
`A_n = (n - 1)/n · A_{n-2} + 8/(n² π)` for `n` odd, `+ 16/(n² π)` for `n` even.
