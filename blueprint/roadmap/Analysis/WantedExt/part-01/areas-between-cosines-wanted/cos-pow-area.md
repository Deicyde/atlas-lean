# Cos Pow Area

The area `∫₀^π |cos^n x - cos^n (k x)| dx` between `cos^n x` and
`cos^n (k x)` on `[0, π]`; the paper's `A_n` is its limit as `k → ∞`
(Eq. (1)), with `k` running over the natural numbers.
