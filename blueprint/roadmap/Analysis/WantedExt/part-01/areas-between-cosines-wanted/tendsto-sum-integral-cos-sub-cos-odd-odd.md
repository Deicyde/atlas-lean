# Tendsto sum integral cos sub cos odd odd

Lemma 9: for `q` odd, with `k = 2m + 1` odd tending to infinity,
`∑_{ℓ=1}^{(k-1)/2} ∫_{2πℓ/(k+1)}^{2πℓ/(k-1)} (cos qkx - cos qx) dx → 4/(q² π)`.
