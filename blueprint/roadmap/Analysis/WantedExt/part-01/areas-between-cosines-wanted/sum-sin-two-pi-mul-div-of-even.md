# Sum sin two pi mul div of even

Lemma 5: for `N` even and positive,
`∑_{ℓ=1}^{N/2} sin(2π q ℓ / N)` is `0` for `q` even and `cot(q π / N)` for `q`
odd. The paper's positivity of `q` is dropped (`q = 0` holds trivially).
