# The map $x \mapsto e^{x-1} - e^{1-x}$ has a unique fixed point

**The map $x \mapsto e^{x-1} - e^{1-x}$ has a unique fixed point.** There is exactly one
real number $x$ such that $e^{x-1} - e^{1-x} = x$.
