# Continuous Multiplicative Functional Eq Exp Wanted

Suppose $f(x)f(y)=f(x+y)$ for all real $x$ and $y$. Assuming that $f$ is continuous and not
zero, prove that

\[
 f(x)=e^{cx},
 \]
 where $c$ is a constant.
