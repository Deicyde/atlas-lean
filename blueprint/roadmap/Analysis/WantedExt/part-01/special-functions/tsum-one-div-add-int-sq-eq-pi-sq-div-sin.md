# Tsum Inv Sq Shift Eq Pi Sq Div Sin Sq Wanted

Suppose $u \notin \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that
\[
\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(u+n)^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{(\sin \pi u)^2}.
\]
