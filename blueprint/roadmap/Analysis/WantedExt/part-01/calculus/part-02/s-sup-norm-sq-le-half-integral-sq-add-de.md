# Sup Norm Sq Le Half H1 Norm Wanted

Suppose $f\in C^{1}(I,\mathbb{R})$ has $f(\alpha)=f(\beta)=0$. Show that

\[
\|f\|_{\infty}^{2}\le \frac{1}{2}\int_{\alpha}^{\beta}\bigl(f^{2}+(f')^{2}\bigr)\,dx.
\]
