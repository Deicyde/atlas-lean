# XSq Sin Inv Sq Nonintegrable Deriv Wanted

Consider the function $F(x)=x^2\sin(1/x^2)$, $x\ne 0$, with $F(0)=0$. Show that $F'(x)$ exists
for every $x$, but $F'$ is not integrable on $[-1,1]$.
