# Even C2 Square Factor Wanted

Let $a>0$ and $f\in C^{2}([-a,a],\mathbb{R})$ be even.

Show that there exists $g\in C^{1}([0,a^{2}],\mathbb{R})$ such that
$f(x)=g(x^{2})$ for all $x\in[-a,a]$.
