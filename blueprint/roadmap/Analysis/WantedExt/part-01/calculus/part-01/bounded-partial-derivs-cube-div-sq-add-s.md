# Cubic Over Sum Squares Partials Bounded Wanted

Define $f(0,0)=0$ and

\[
f(x,y)=\frac{x^3}{x^2+y^2}\qquad \text{if }(x,y)\ne(0,0).
\]
Prove that $D_1f$ and $D_2f$ are bounded functions in $R^2$.
