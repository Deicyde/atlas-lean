# Monotone Pointwise Uniform Wanted

Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of monotone functions on a compact interval $I$ which converges
pointwise to a continuous function $f$. Show that $f$ is monotone and that $(f_n)$ converges
uniformly to $f$.
