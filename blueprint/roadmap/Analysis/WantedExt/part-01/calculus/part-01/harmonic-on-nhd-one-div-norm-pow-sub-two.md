# Norm Pow Two Sub Dim Harmonic Wanted

Verify that $g_m:\mathbb R^m\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb R$ defined by

\[
g_m(x):=|x|^{2-m}, & m>2,
\]
is harmonic in $\mathbb R^m\setminus\{0\}$.
