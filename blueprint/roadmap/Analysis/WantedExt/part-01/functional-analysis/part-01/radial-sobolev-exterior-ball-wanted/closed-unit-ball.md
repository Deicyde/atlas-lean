# Closed unit ball of $\mathbb{R}^n$

**Closed unit ball of $\mathbb{R}^n$.** For a natural number $n$, the closed unit ball of
$\mathbb{R}^n$ is the set $\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : \|x\| \le 1\}$, where $\|\cdot\|$ is the Euclidean
norm.
