# Lp Norm Tendsto Ess Sup Wanted

Assume $|\Omega|\lt \infty$. Let $f\in L^{\infty}(\Omega)$. Prove that
$\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_{p}=\|f\|_{\infty}$.
