# Schwartz Weighted L2 Deriv Inequality Wanted

Prove that the following inequality holds for any $f \in S(\mathbb{R})$:
\[
\|f\|_{L^2}^2 \le 2\|xf\|_{L^2}\|f'\|_{L^2}.
\]
