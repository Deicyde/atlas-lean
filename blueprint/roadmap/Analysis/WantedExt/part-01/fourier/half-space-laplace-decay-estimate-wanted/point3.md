# Appending a third coordinate

**Appending a third coordinate.** For a point $x = (x_1, x_2)$ of the Euclidean plane
$\mathbb{R}^2$ and a real number $z$, this is the point $(x_1, x_2, z)$ of Euclidean three-space
$\mathbb{R}^3$.
