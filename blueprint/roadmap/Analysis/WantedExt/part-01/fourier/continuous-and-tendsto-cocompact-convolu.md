# L2 Convolution Continuous Vanishing Wanted

Let $f, g \in L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$. Show that $f \ast g \in C(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and
$\lim_{|x|\to\infty}(f \ast g)(x) = 0$.
