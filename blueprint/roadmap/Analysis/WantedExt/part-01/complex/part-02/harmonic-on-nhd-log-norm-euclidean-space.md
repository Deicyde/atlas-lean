# Log Norm Harmonic Plane Wanted

Verify that $g:\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb R$ defined by

\[
g(x):=
\log|x|,
\]
is harmonic in $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}$.
