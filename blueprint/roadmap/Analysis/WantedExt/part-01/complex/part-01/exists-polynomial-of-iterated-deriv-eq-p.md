# Entire Iterated Deriv Poly Wanted

Fix a positive integer $k$. Suppose that $f$ is an entire function such that, for some $k$, the
$k$th derivative $f^{(k)}$ of $f$ is a polynomial.

Prove that $f$ is a polynomial.
