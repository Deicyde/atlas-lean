# Finite Zeros In Disc Of Holomorphic Wanted

Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of $\overline{D}(P,r)$. Suppose that $f$ is not
identically zero on $D(P,r)$. Prove that $f$ has at most finitely many zeros in $D(P,r)$.
