# Holomorphic Disk Diameter Deriv Bound Wanted

Suppose $f:D\to\mathbb{C}$ is holomorphic. Show that the diameter $d=\sup_{z,w\in D}|f(z)-f(w)|$
of the image of $f$ satisfies

\[
2|f'(0)|\le d.
\]
