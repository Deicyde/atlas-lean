# A lacunary power series

**A lacunary power series.** For $z \in \mathbb{C}$, let
$f(z) = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} 2^{-j} z^{2^j}$, where $f(z)$ is taken to be $0$ when the series is
not summable.
