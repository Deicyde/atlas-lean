# Differentiable On ball continuous On closed Ball lacunary

Prove that the function

\[
f(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} 2^{-j} z^{(2^j)}
\]
is holomorphic on $D(0,1)$ and continuous on $\overline{D}(0,1)$.
