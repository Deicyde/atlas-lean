# The antiholomorphic quadratic map

**The antiholomorphic quadratic map.** For $c \in \mathbb{C}$, the map
$T_c : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is $T_c(z) = \bar z^2 + c$, where $\bar z$ is the complex
conjugate of $z$.
