# Multivariate monomial

**Multivariate monomial.** For a multi-index $n = (n_1, \dots, n_d) \in \mathbb{N}^d$ and a
point $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$, the monomial $x^n$ is the real number $\prod_{i=1}^{d} x_i^{n_i}$.
