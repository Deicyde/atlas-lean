# Multi-index binomial coefficient

**Multi-index binomial coefficient.** For multi-indices $n, k \in \mathbb{N}^d$, the binomial
coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ is the natural number $\prod_i \binom{n_i}{k_i}$.
