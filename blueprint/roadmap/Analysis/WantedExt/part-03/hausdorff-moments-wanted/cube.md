# Unit cube of the Hausdorff moment problem

**Unit cube of the Hausdorff moment problem.** For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the closed unit cube
$I^d = [0,1]^d$ is the set of points $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ with $0 \le x_i \le 1$ for every
coordinate $i$.
