# Weighted sum fib rpow le mul rpow

Upper bounds of Theorem 5(ii) (weighted Fibonacci), with `α = (r + s) / 2`: for
weights `w_k > 0`, `W = ∑_{k=1}^n w_k` and `0 < α < 2`,
`∑ w_k F_k ^ α ≤ W (∑ w_k F_k ^ 2 / W) ^ (α / 2) ≤ ∑ w_k F_k ^ 2`.
