# Sum fib rpow le of two le

Upper bounds of Theorem 3 for Fibonacci numbers, with `α = (r + s) / 2`: if
`α ≥ 2` then `∑_{k=1}^n F_k ^ α ≤ (F_n F_{n+1}) ^ (α / 2)`.
