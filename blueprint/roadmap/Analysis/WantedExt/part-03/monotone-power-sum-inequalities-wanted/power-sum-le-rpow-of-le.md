# Power sum le rpow of le

Upper bound of Theorem 2(i): for positive `x_k` and `0 < β ≤ α`,
`S^{[α]} ≤ (S^{[β]}) ^ (α / β)` (in the paper `α = (r + s) / 2` with `r, s > 0`, and
the sequence is monotone; neither is needed here).
