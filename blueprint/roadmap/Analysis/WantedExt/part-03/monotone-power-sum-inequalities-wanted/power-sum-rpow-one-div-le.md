# Power sum rpow one div le

Power sums of a nonnegative finite sequence are monotone in the exponent after
normalisation: if `α > β > 0` then `(∑ x_k ^ α) ^ (1 / α) ≤ (∑ x_k ^ β) ^ (1 / β)`.
