# Upward neighbour

**Upward neighbour.** For a positive integer $L$, the upward neighbour of a site $(i, j)$ of
the periodic $L \times L$ square lattice is $(i, j + 1)$, with addition in
$\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$.
