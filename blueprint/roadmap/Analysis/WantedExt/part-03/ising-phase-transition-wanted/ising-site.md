# Sites of the periodic square lattice

**Sites of the periodic square lattice.** For a positive integer $L$, the sites of the
periodic $L \times L$ square lattice are the pairs $(i, j)$ with
$i, j \in \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$.
