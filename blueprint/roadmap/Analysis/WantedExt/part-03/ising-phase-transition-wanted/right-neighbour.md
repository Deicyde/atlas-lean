# Right neighbour

**Right neighbour.** For a positive integer $L$, the right neighbour of a site $(i, j)$ of
the periodic $L \times L$ square lattice is $(i + 1, j)$, with addition in
$\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$.
