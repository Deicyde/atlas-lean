# Straight lines in the complex plane

**Straight lines in the complex plane.** A set $s \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is a straight line if
there are $a, v \in \mathbb{C}$ with $v \ne 0$ such that $s = \{a + tv : t \in \mathbb{R}\}$.
