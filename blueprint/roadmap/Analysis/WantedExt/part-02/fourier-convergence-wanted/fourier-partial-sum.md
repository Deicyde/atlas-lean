# Fourier Partial Sum

The symmetric Fourier partial sum of `f : ℝ → ℂ` on the period `[0, 2π]`:
`S_N(f)(x) = ∑_{n = -N}^{N} f̂_n · e^{inx}`, with `f̂_n = fourierCoeffOn _ f n`.
