# Standard basis vectors of ℝ³

**Standard basis vectors of ℝ³.** For $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, $e_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ is the
$i$-th standard basis vector: its $i$-th coordinate is $1$ and its other coordinates are $0$.
