# Iterate rpow succ lt Gamma iterate rpow

For `n > 0` and `b ≥ e`, `Γ(E_n(b, b)) > E_{n+1}(b, b)`, where `E_m(b, b)` is the
`m`-fold iterate of `x ↦ b ^ x` from `b`.
