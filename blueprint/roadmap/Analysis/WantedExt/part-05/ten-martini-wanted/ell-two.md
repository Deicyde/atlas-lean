# The sequence space $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$

**The sequence space $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$.** We write $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ for the Hilbert
space of square-summable sequences $(u_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ of complex numbers.
