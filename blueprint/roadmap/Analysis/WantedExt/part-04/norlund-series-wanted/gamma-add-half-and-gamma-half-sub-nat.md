# Gamma add half and Gamma half sub nat

Lemma 2: `(r - 1/2)! = (√π/2^{2r}) C(2r, r) r!` for `r ∈ ℂ` off the negative integers, with
`w! = Γ(w + 1)` and `C(2r, r) r! = Γ(2r + 1)/Γ(r + 1)`; and
`(-r - 1/2)! = √π (-1)^r 2^{2r}/(r! C(2r, r))` for `r ∈ ℕ`.
