# Tsum odd mul catalan div four pow mul binom

Theorem 5 (general identity): for `Re z > 1/2`,
`Σ_{n≥0} (2n + 1) C_n/(2^{2n} C(n + z, n)) = 2z(H_{z-1} - H_{z-3/2}) = 2z(ψ(z) - ψ(z - 1/2))`.
