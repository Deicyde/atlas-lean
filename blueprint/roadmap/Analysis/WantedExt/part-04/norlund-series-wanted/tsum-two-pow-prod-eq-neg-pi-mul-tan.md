# Tsum two pow prod eq neg pi mul tan

Theorem 2: for `z ∈ ℂ` with `-1/2 < Re z < 1/2`,
`Σ_{n≥0} (2^{n+1}/(n+1)) ∏_{j=0}^n (2z - j)/(2z - (2j + 1)) = -π tan(πz)`.
