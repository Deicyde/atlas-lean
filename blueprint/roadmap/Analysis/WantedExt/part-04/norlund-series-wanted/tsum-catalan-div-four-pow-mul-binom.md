# Tsum catalan div four pow mul binom

Theorem 3 (general identity): for `Re z > 0`,
`Σ_{n≥0} C_n/(2^{2n} C(n + z, n)) = -2z(H_{z-1} - H_{z-1/2}) = -2z(ψ(z) - ψ(z + 1/2))`.
