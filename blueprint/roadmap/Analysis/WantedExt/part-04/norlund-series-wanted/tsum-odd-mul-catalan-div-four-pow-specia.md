# Tsum odd mul catalan div four pow special

Theorem 5 (special values): `Σ_{n≥0} ((2n + 1)/(n + 1)) C_n/2^{2n} = 4 ln 2` and
`Σ_{n≥0} ((2n + 1)/((n + 1)(n + 2))) C_n/2^{2n} = 4 ln 2 - 2`.
