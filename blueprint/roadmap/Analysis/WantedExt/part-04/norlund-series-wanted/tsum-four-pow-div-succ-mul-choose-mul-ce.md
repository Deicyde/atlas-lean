# Tsum four pow div succ mul choose mul central Binom

Theorem 10: for a positive integer `m`,
`Σ_{n≥0} 2^{2n}/((n + 1) C(n + m, m) C(2(n + m), n + m))
  = (m/4)(3ζ(2) - 4O^{(2)}_{m-1})/C(2(m - 1), m - 1)`,
with `ζ(2) = π²/6`.
