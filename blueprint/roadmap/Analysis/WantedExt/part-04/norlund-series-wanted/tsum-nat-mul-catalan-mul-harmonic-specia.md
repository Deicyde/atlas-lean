# Tsum nat mul catalan mul harmonic special

Theorem 7 (special values, `z = 1` and `z = 3/2`):
`Σ_{n≥1} (n C_n/2^{2n}) H_{n+1}/(n + 1) = 2π²/3 - 4` and
`Σ_{n≥1} n O_{n+2}/((n + 1)(2n + 1)(2n + 3)) = 13/4 - 2 ln 2 - π²/6` (the `n = 0` terms vanish).
