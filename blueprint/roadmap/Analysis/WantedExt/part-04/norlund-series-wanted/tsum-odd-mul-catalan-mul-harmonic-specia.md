# Tsum odd mul catalan mul harmonic special

Theorem 6 (special values): `Σ_{n≥0} ((2n + 1)/(n + 1)) C_n H_{n+1}/2^{2n} = 2π²/3`,
`Σ_{n≥0} ((2n + 1)/((n + 2)(n + 1))) C_n H_{n+2}/2^{2n} = 2π²/3 + 4 ln 2 - 8`, and
`Σ_{n≥0} O_{n+2}/((n + 1)(2n + 3)) = 4 - 2 ln 2 - π²/6`.
