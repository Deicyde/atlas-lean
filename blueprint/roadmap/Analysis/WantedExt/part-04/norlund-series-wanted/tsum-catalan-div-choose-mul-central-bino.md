# Tsum catalan div choose mul central Binom

Theorem 12: for a positive integer `m`,
`Σ_{n≥0} C_n/(C(n + m, n) C(2(n + m), n + m))
  = (m/2)(H_{m-1} - 2O_{m-1} + 2 ln 2)/C(2(m - 1), m - 1)`.
