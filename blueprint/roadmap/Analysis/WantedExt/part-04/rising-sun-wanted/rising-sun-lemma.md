# Riesz's rising sun lemma

**Riesz's rising sun lemma.** Let $a < b$ be real numbers and let
$f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on $[a, b]$. Then $f$ has the rising sun property
on $[a, b]$.
