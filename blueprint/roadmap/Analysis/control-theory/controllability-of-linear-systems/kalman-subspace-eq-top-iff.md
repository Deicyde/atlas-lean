# Kalman rank condition, dual form

The Kalman subspace of $(A, B)$ is all of $E$ if and only if the only vector $\eta \in E$ orthogonal to $A^k B v$ for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $v \in U$ is $\eta = 0$. Powers $k \ge \dim E$ add nothing to the Kalman subspace, by the Cayley–Hamilton theorem.

## Informally uses

- [Kalman subspace](kalman-subspace.md): its orthogonal complement is $\{0\}$ exactly when it is the whole space.
