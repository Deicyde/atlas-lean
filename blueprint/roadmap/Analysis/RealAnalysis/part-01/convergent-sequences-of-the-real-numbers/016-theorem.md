# Theorem (Convergent Sequences of the Real Numbers section) (1)

Let $\{x_n\}$ be a monotone decreasing function. Then, $\{x_n\}$ is convergent if and only if $\{x_n\}$ is bounded. Moreover, $\lim_{n\to \infty} x_n = \inf\{x_n \mid n\in \mathbb{N}\}$.
