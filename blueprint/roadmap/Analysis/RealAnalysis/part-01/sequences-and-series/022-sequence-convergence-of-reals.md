# Definition (Sequence Convergence of Reals) [1]

A sequence $\{x_n\}$ converges to $x\in \mathbb{R}$ if $\forall \epsilon >0$, $\exists M\in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\forall n\geq M$, $|x_n - x| <\epsilon$.
