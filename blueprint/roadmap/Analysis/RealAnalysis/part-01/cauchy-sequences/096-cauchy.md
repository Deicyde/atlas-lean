# Definition (Cauchy) (Cauchy Sequences section) (2)

A sequence $\{x_n\}$ is Cauchy if $\forall \epsilon>0$ $\exists M \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $n,k \geq M$, $|x_n - x_k| <\epsilon.$
