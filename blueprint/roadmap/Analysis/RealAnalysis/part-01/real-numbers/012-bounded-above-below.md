# Definition (Bounded Above/Below) [1]

Let $S$ be an ordered set and let $E \subset S$. Then, (1) If there exists a $b \in S$ such that $x \leq b$ for all $x \in E$, then $E$ is bounded above and $b$ is an upper bound of $E$. (2) If $\exists c \in S$ such that $x \geq c$ for all $x \in E$, then $E$ is bounded below and $c$ is a lower bound of $E$.
