# Theorem (Continuous Functions, Limits of Functions)

Let $c$ be a cluster point of $S\subset \mathbb{R}$, and let $f: S\to \mathbb{R}$. If $f(x) \to L_1$ and $f(x)\to L_2$ as $x\to c$, then $L_1 = L_2$.
