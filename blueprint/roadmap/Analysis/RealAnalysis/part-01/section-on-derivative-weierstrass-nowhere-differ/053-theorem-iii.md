# Theorem (Theorem III)

We will show the following: (1) $\forall x\in \mathbb{R}$, $\sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{\cos(160^k x)}{4^k}$ is absolutely convergent. (2) The function $f:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{\cos(160^k x)}{4^k}$ is bounded and continuous.
