# Theorem (Integral Bounds)

Let $f\in C([a,b])$, and $m_f = \inf\{f(x) \mid x\in [a,b]\} \in \mathbb{R}$, $M_f = \sup \{f(x) \mid x\in [a,b]\}\in \mathbb{R}$. Then, $m_f(b-a) \leq \int_a^b f \leq M_f(b-a).$
