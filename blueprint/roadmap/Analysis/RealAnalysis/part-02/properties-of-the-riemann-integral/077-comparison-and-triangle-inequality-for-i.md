# Theorem (Comparison and Triangle Inequality for Integrals)

Suppose $f\in C([a,b])$ and $g\in C([a,b])$. (1) If $\forall x\in [a,b]$ $f(x) \leq g(x)$, then $\int_a^b f \leq \int_a^b g.$ (2) (Triangle Inequality for integrals): $|\int_a^b f| \leq \int_a^b |f|$.
