# Theorem (Change of Variables) [2]

Let $\varphi:[a,b]\to [c,d]$ be continuously differentiable with $\varphi'>0$ on $[a,b]$, $\varphi(a) = c$, and $\varphi(b) = d$. Then, $\int_c^d f(u)\,du = \int_a^b f(\varphi(x))\varphi'(x)\,dx$.
