# Theorem (Sequences of Function, Interchange of Limits)

If $f_n:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}$ is continuously differentiable, $f:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}$, $g:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}$, and $f_n \to f$ pointwise, $f_n' \to g$ uniformly, then $f$ is continuously differentiable and $g = f'$.
