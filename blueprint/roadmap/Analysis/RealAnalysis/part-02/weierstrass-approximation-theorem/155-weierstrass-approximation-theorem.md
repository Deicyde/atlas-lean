# Theorem (Weierstrass Approximation Theorem) (Weierstrass Approximation Theorem section) (2)

If $f\in C([a,b])$, there exists a sequence of polynomials $\{P_n\}$ such that $P_n\to f$ uniformly on $[a,b]$.
