# Definition (Refinement) [2]

If $(\underline{x}, \underline{\xi})$ and $(\underline{x}', \underline{\xi}')$ are tagged partitions of $[a,b]$ such that $\underline{x}\subset \underline{x}'$, we say $\underline{x}'$ is a refinement of $\underline{x}$.
