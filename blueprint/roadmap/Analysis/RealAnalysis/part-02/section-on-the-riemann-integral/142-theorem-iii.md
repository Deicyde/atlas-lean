# Theorem III [2]

If $(\underline{x}, \underline{\xi})$ and $(\underline{x}', \underline{\xi}')$ are any two tagged partitions of $[a,b]$ and $f\in C([a,b])$, then $|S_f(\underline{x}, \underline{\xi}) - S_f(\underline{x}', \underline{\xi}')| \leq (w_f(\lVert \underline{x}\rVert) + w_f(\lVert \underline{x}'\rVert))(b-a).$
