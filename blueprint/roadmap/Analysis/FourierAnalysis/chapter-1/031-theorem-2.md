# Theorem 2 [3]

Let $W$ be given by (3). Then $W(0) = 0$ and (a) $B(t) = W(t)$ satisfies (2) (or equivalently (1)) on $0 \leq t \leq \pi$. (b) $W$ is almost surely continuous in $t$.
