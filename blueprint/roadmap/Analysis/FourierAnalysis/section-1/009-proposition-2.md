# Proposition 2 [1]

If $h \in L^1(\mathbb{R}) \cap L^2(\mathbb{R})$, then the inverse Fourier transform obtained by continuity in the $L^2$ norm coincides with the $L^1$ definition: $\mathcal{G}(h)(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} h(\xi) e^{ix\xi} \, d\xi$.
