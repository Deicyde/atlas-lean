# Corollary 2 [1]

The functions $e^{inx}$, $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, form an orthonormal basis for $L^2(\mathbb{T})$. In particular, for all $f \in L^2(\mathbb{T})$, $\lim_{N \to \infty} \|s_N - f\|_2 = 0$, and $\|f\|_2^2 = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} |\hat{f}(n)|^2$.
