# Theorem 1 (Fourier Series, Continued (part 3))

Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{T})$. Then $\lim_{N \to \infty} \|f - \sigma_N f\|_1 = 0$. In particular, trigonometric polynomials are dense in $L^1(\mathbb{T})$.
