# Multi-index binomial coefficient

**Multi-index binomial coefficient.** For multi-indices $n, k \in \mathbb{N}^d$, the
binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ is the natural number $\prod_{i=1}^{d} \binom{n_i}{k_i}$.
