# Coefficient identity for a `C¹` periodic function

**Coefficient identity for a `C¹` periodic function.** For `n ≠ 0`,
`ĉₙ(f) = (T / (2π i n)) · ĉₙ(f')`. The boundary term in integration by parts
vanishes by periodicity.
