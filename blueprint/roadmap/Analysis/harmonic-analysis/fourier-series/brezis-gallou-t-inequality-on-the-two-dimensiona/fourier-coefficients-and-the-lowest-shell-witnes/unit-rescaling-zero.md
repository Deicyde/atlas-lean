# The unit rescaling fixes the origin

**The unit rescaling fixes the origin.** The coordinate rescaling from the torus
$(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$ to the unit torus $(\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^2$ maps $0$ to $0$.
