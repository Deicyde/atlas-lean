# Heisenberg's uncertainty principle

**Heisenberg's uncertainty principle** in Fourier form: with Mathlib's
Fourier normalization, the Fourier second moment about zero is `∫ ξ² * ‖𝓕 f ξ‖²`, and
`(∫ ‖f x‖²)² ≤ 16 * π² * (∫ x² * ‖f x‖²) * (∫ ξ² * ‖𝓕 f ξ‖²)`.
