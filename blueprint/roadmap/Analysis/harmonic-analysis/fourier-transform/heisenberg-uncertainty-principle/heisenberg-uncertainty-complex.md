# Heisenberg's uncertainty principle

**Heisenberg's uncertainty principle** for complex-valued Schwartz functions:
`(∫ ‖f x‖²)² ≤ 4 * (∫ x² * ‖f x‖²) * (∫ ‖f' x‖²)`.
