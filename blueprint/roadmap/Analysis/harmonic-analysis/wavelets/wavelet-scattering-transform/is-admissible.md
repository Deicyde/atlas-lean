# Admissible scattering wavelet in dimension one

A scattering wavelet $\psi$ (with low-pass $\varphi$) is admissible (S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286), Theorem 2.6, conditions (29)–(30), with $d = 1$ and $G = \{1, -1\}$) if it satisfies the [standing assumptions](is-scattering-wavelet.md) and there are $\eta \in \mathbb{R}$ and an integrable $\rho \ge 0$ with $|\hat\rho(\omega)| \le |\hat\varphi(2\omega)|$ and $\hat\rho(0) = 1$ such that
$$\alpha = \inf_{1 \le |\omega| \le 2} \sum_{j \in \mathbb{Z}} \sum_{r = \pm 1} \hat\Psi(2^{-j} r \omega)\, |\hat\psi(2^{-j} r \omega)|^2 > 0,$$
where $\hat\Psi$ is the [admissibility function](admissibility-function.md). The Lean definition adds two conditions that make the series meaningful: the series defining $\hat\Psi$ converges at every $\omega$ (its terms are nonnegative, since $|\hat\rho| \le 1$ under the standing assumptions), and the lower bound $\alpha$ is required of sums over $\mathbb{Z}$ that converge unconditionally. Mallat reports that the analytic cubic-spline Battle–Lemarié wavelet is admissible with $\eta = 3\pi/2$, by a numerical evaluation giving $\alpha = 0.2766$; no example is formalized here.
