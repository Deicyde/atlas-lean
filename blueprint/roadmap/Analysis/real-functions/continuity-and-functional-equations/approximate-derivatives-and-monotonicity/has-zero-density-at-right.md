# Right density zero at the origin

**Right density zero at the origin.** A set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ has right-hand density
$0$ at $0$ if the Lebesgue outer measure of $S \cap (0, \delta]$, divided by $\delta$, tends to
$0$ as $\delta \to 0^+$.
