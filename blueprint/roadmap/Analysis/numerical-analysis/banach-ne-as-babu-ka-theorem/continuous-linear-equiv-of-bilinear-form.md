# Banach–Nečas–Babuška theorem

**Banach–Nečas–Babuška theorem.** The **Banach–Nečas–Babuška theorem**: a bilinear form satisfying the
inf–sup condition and nondegeneracy is itself a continuous linear equivalence
`E ≃L[ℝ] (F →L[ℝ] ℝ)`.
