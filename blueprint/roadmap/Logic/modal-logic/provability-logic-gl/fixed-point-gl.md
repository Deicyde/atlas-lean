# The de Jongh–Sambin fixed point theorem

Let $p$ be modalised in $A$. There is a formula $H$ in which $p$ does not occur, all of whose atoms
occur in $A$, such that $\mathrm{GL} \vdash H \leftrightarrow A(H)$.

Wanted statement (no proof in the library yet). Uniqueness is proved, and so is the case of a single
boxed occurrence; the general case follows from that case by induction on the number of boxed
subformulas of $A$ containing $p$ (de Jongh and Sambin, 1975; algorithmic proofs are listed in the
source below, among them Sambin and Valentini's and Reidhaar-Olson's).
