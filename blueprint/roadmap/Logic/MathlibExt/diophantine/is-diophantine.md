# Is Diophantine

`S ⊆ Rᵏ` is Diophantine over `R` with coefficients from `A` (through `φ : A →+* R`): for some
`n` and finitely many polynomials `P` over `A` in the variables `x₁, …, x_k, y₁, …, yₙ`,
`x ∈ S ↔ ∃ y, ∀ p ∈ P, p(x, y) = 0`.
