# Satisfaction at position zero

**Satisfaction at position zero.** For every valuation of atoms on letters, ω-word $w$ and LTL
formula $\varphi$, $\varphi$ holds at position $0$ of $w$ if and only if $w$ satisfies $\varphi$.
