# Satisfaction of a CNF formula

**Satisfaction of a CNF formula.** An assignment $v : \mathbb{N} \to \{\mathrm{true},
\mathrm{false}\}$ satisfies a CNF formula $\varphi$ when $v$ satisfies every clause of $\varphi$.
