# Craig interpolation

**Craig interpolation** (semantic form): if every valuation with `⊥` false that makes
`A` true makes `B` true, there is `I` whose atoms are common to `A` and `B` (or `⊥`) with
`A ⊨ I` and `I ⊨ B`.
