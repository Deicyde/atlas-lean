# Closed At

`ClosedAt φ d` means every free variable of `φ` is `< d`: variables bound
by the `d` binders enclosing `φ` are `< d`. Source: C. Stirling, *Modal and
Temporal Properties of Processes*, Springer 2001, Chapter 6 (de Bruijn
variables).
