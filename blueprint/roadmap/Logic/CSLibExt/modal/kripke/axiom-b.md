# Axiom B

**Axiom B.** For a modal formula $\varphi$, the symmetry axiom B is the formula
$\varphi \to \Box\Diamond\varphi$, where $\Diamond\psi$ is $\neg\Box\neg\psi$ and $\neg\chi$ stands
for $\chi \to \bot$.
