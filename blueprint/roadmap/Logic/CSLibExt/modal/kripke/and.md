# Conjunction

**Conjunction.** For modal formulas $\varphi$ and $\psi$, the conjunction $\varphi \wedge \psi$
is the formula $\neg(\varphi \to \neg\psi)$, where $\neg\chi$ stands for $\chi \to \bot$.
