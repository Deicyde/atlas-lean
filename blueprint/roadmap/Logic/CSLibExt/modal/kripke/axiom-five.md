# Axiom 5

**Axiom 5.** For a modal formula $\varphi$, the euclidean axiom 5 is the formula
$\Diamond\varphi \to \Box\Diamond\varphi$, where $\Diamond\psi$ is $\neg\Box\neg\psi$ and $\neg\chi$
stands for $\chi \to \bot$.
