# Axiom D

**Axiom D.** For a modal formula $\varphi$, the seriality axiom D is the formula
$\Box\varphi \to \Diamond\varphi$, where $\Diamond\psi$ is $\neg\Box\neg\psi$ and $\neg\chi$ stands
for $\chi \to \bot$.
