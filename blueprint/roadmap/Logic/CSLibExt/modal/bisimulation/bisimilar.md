# Bisimilar worlds

**Bisimilar worlds.** Let $M$ and $N$ be Kripke models over the same atoms. A world $w$ of
$M$ and a world $v$ of $N$ are bisimilar if some bisimulation between $M$ and $N$ relates $w$
to $v$.
