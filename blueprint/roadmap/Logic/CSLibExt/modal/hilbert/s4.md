# Extra axioms of S4

**Extra axioms of S4.** The extra axioms of the modal logic S4 are the formulas
$\Box\varphi \to \varphi$ (axiom T) and $\Box\varphi \to \Box\Box\varphi$ (axiom 4), for all
modal formulas $\varphi$.
