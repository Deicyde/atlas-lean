# Proposition 1.16.2

Let $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{D}$ be multiring categories and $F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a quasi-tensor functor. Then $F$ defines a homomorphism of unital rings $[F]: \operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{D})$.
