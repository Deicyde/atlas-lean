# Lemma 1.16.1

The multiplication on $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C})$ defined by $X_{i} X_{j} \coloneqq [X_{i} \otimes X_{j}] = \sum_{k \in I} [X_{i} \otimes X_{j} : X_{k}] X_{k}$ is associative.
