# Proposition 1.4.3

For any monoidal functor $(F, J) \colon \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$, the diagrams involving the unit constraints $l, r, l', r'$ and the isomorphism $\varphi \colon \mathbf{1}' \to F(\mathbf{1})$ are commutative for all $X \in \mathcal{C}$.
