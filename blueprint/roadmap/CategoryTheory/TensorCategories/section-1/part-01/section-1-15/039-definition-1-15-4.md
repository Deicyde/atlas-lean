# Definition 1.15.4

The subcategories $\mathcal{C}_{ij} \coloneqq \mathbf{1}_{i} \otimes \mathcal{C} \otimes \mathbf{1}_{j}$ will be called the component subcategories of $\mathcal{C}$.
