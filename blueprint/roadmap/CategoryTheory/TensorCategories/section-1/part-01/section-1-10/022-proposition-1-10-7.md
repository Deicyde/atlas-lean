# Proposition 1.10.7

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category. (i) Let $U, V, W$ be objects in $\mathcal{C}$ admitting right (respectively, left) duals, and let $f: V \rightarrow W, g: U \rightarrow V$ be morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$. Then $(f \circ g)^{*}=g^{*} \circ f^{*}$ (respectively, ${}^{*}(f \circ g)={}^{*}g \circ {}^{*}f$). (ii) If $U, V$ have right (respectively, left) duals then the object $V^{*} \otimes U^{*}$ (respectively, ${}^{*}V \otimes {}^{*}U$) has a natural structure of a right (respectively, left) dual to $U \otimes V$.
