# Theorem 1.22.11

The assignments $(C, F) \mapsto H = \operatorname{End}(F)$, $H \mapsto (\operatorname{Rep}(H), \operatorname{Forget})$ are mutually inverse bijections between 1) finite tensor categories $C$ with a fiber functor $F$, up to monoidal equivalence and isomorphism of monoidal functors; 2) finite dimensional Hopf algebras over $k$ up to isomorphism.
