# Corollary 1.51.3

One has: $P_{i^{**}} = L_\rho^* \otimes P_{{}^{**}i} \otimes L_\rho$; $L_{i^{**}} = L_\rho^* \otimes L_{{}^{**}i} \otimes L_\rho$.
