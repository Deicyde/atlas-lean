# Corollary 1.50.3

If $\mathcal{C}$ is integral, and $F \colon \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ is a surjective quasi-tensor functor then $\mathcal{D}$ is also integral, and the object $F(R_{\mathcal{C}})$ is free of rank $\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{C})/\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{D})$ (which is an integer).
