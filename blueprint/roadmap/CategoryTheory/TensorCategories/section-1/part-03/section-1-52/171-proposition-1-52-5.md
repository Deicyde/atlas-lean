# Proposition 1.52.5

The following conditions on a finite dimensional quasi-Hopf algebra $H$ are equivalent: (i) $H$ is semisimple; (ii) $\varepsilon(I) \neq 0$ (where $I$ is a left integral in $H$); (iii) $I^2 \neq 0$; (iv) $I$ can be normalized to be an idempotent.
