# Proposition 1.45.4

Let $X \in I$. (1) The number $\alpha = \mathrm{FPdim}(X)$ is an algebraic integer, and for any algebraic conjugate $\alpha'$ of $\alpha$ we have $\alpha \geq |\alpha'|$. (2) $\mathrm{FPdim}(X) \geq 1$.
