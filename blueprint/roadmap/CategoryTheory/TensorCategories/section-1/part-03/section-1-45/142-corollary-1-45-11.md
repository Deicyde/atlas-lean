# Corollary 1.45.11

Let $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{D}$ be tensor categories with finitely many classes of simple objects. If $F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a quasi-tensor functor, then $\operatorname{FPdim}_{\mathcal{D}}(F(X))=\operatorname{FPdim}_{\mathcal{C}}(X)$ for any $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$.
