# Definition 1.48.1

A transitive unital $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-ring $A$ of finite rank is said to be integral if FPdim: $A \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ (i.e. the Frobenius-Perron dimensions of elements of $\mathcal{C}$ are integers). A tensor category $\mathcal{C}$ is integral if $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C})$ is integral.
