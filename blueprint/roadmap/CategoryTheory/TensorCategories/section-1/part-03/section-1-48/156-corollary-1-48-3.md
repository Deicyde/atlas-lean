# Corollary 1.48.3

The assignment $H \mapsto \operatorname{Rep}(H)$ defines a bijection between integral finite tensor categories $\mathcal{C}$ over $k$ up to monoidal equivalence, and finite dimensional quasi-Hopf algebras $H$ over $k$, up to twist equivalence and isomorphism.
