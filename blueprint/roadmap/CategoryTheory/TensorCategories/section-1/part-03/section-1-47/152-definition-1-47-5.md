# Definition 1.47.5

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finite tensor category. Then the Frobenius-Perron dimension of $\mathcal{C}$ is defined by $\mathrm{FPdim}(\mathcal{C}):=\mathrm{FPdim}(R_{\mathcal{C}})=\sum_{i \in I} \mathrm{FPdim}(X_{i}) \mathrm{FPdim}(P_{i})$.
