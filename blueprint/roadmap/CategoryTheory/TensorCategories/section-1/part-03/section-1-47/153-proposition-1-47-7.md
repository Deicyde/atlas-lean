# Proposition 1.47.7

(1) $Z \otimes R_{\mathcal{C}}=R_{\mathcal{C}} \otimes Z=\mathrm{FPdim}(Z) R_{\mathcal{C}}$ for all $Z \in \operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C})$. (2) The image of $R_{\mathcal{C}}$ in $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{C}$ is a regular element.
