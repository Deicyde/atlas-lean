# Proposition 1.35.1

One has $\sum \Phi_i^1 \beta S(\Phi_i^2) \alpha \Phi_i^3 = 1$ and $\sum S(\bar{\Phi}_i^1) \alpha \bar{\Phi}_i^2 \beta S(\bar{\Phi}_i^3) = 1$.
