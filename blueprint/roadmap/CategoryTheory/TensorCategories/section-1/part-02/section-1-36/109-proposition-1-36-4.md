# Proposition 1.36.4

Let $H$ be a finite dimensional bialgebra. Then $J \mapsto (\operatorname{Id}, J)$ is a bijection between: 1) gauge equivalence classes of bialgebra twists for $H$, and 2) fiber functors on $C = \operatorname{Rep}(H)$, up to isomorphism.
