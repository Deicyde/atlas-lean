# Definition 1.39.1

A pivotal structure $a$ on a tensor category $\mathcal{C}$ is spherical if $\operatorname{dim}_{a}(V)=\operatorname{dim}_{a}(V^{*})$ for any object $V$ in $\mathcal{C}$. A tensor category is spherical if it is equipped with a spherical structure.
