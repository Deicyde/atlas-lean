# Theorem 1.25.2

There exists a unique Hopf algebra structure on $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$, given by $\Delta(K) = K \otimes K$ (thus $K$ is a grouplike element); $\Delta(E) = E \otimes K + 1 \otimes E$; $\Delta(F) = F \otimes 1 + K^{-1} \otimes F$ (thus $E$, $F$ are skew-primitive elements).
