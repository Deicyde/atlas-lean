# Definition 1.34.1

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian monoidal category over $k$, and $(F, J) \colon \mathcal{C} \to \operatorname{Vec}$ be a quasi-fiber functor. $(F, J)$ is said to be normalized if $J_{\mathbf{1} X} = J_{X \mathbf{1}} = \operatorname{Id}_{F(X)}$ for all $X \in \mathcal{C}$.
