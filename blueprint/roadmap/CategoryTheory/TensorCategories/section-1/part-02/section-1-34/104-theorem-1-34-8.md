# Theorem 1.34.8

The assignments $(C, F) \mapsto H = \operatorname{End}(F)$, $H \mapsto (\operatorname{Rep}(H), \operatorname{Forget})$ are mutually inverse bijections between 1) finite $k$-linear abelian monoidal categories $C$ admitting a quasi-fiber functor, up to monoidal equivalence of categories, and 2) finite dimensional quasi-bialgebras $H$ over $k$ up to twist equivalence and isomorphism.
