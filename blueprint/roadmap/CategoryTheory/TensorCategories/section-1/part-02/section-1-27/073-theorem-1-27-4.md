# Theorem 1.27.4

Assume that $k$ has characteristic $0$. Let $C$ be a finite ring category over $k$ with simple object $\mathbf{1}$. Then $\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{1}) = 0$.
