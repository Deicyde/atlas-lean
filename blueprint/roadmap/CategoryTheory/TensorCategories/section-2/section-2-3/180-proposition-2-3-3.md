# Proposition 2.3.3

Structures of a $\mathcal{C}$-module category on $\mathcal{M}$ are in a natural one-to-one correspondence with exact monoidal functors $F \colon \mathcal{C} \to \operatorname{End}(\mathcal{M})$.
