# Definition 2.8.3

A $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-module $M$ over a $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-ring $K$ is called irreducible if it has no proper $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-submodules (in other words, the $\mathbb{Z}$-span of any proper subset of the basis of $M$ is not a $K$-submodule).
