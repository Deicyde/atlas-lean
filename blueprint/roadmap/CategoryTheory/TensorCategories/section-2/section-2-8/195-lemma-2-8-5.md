# Lemma 2.8.5

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be an indecomposable exact module category over $\mathcal{C}$. Then $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{M})$ is an irreducible $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-module over $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C})$.
