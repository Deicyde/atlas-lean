# Definition 2.9.22

Let $A$ be an algebra in $\mathcal{C}$ and let $(M, p_M)$ be a right $A$-module, and $(N, p_N)$ be a left $A$-module. A tensor product over $A$, $M \otimes_A N \in \mathcal{C}$, is the quotient of $M \otimes N$ by the image of morphism $p_M \otimes \operatorname{id} - \operatorname{id} \otimes p_N \colon M \otimes A \otimes N \to M \otimes N$.
