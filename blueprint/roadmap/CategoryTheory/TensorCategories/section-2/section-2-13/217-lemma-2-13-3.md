# Lemma 2.13.3

Let $\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2}$ be exact module categories over $\mathcal{C}$. Any functor $F \in \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2})$ has both right and left adjoint.
