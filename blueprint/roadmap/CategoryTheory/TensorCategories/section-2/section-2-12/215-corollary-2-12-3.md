# Corollary 2.12.3

The category $Fun_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2})$ of right exact module functors from $\mathcal{M}_{1}$ to $\mathcal{M}_{2}$ is abelian.
