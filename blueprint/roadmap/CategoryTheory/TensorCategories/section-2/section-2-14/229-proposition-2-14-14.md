# Proposition 2.14.14

(Basic identity) Let $X, Y, Z \in \mathcal{M}$. There is a canonical isomorphism $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, Y) \otimes Z \simeq {}^{*}\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{\mathcal{C}^{*}_{\mathcal{M}}}(Z, X) \otimes Y$.
