# Equality case of Fischer's inequality

**Equality case of Fischer's inequality**: for `A` and `D`
positive definite and `[[A, B], [Bᴴ, D]]` positive semidefinite,
`det [[A, B], [Bᴴ, D]] = det A * det D` iff `B = 0`.
