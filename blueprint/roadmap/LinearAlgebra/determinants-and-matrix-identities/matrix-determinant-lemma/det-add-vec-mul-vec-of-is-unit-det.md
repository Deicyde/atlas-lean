# Multiplicative matrix determinant lemma for invertible matrices

The matrix determinant lemma in multiplicative form, when `det A` is a unit: for vectors `u`, `v`,
`det (A + u vᵀ) = det A · (1 + vᵀ A⁻¹ u)`. This recovers the classical form of the lemma for
invertible `A` from the general version,
[Matrix determinant lemma without invertibility](det-add-vec-mul-vec.md).
