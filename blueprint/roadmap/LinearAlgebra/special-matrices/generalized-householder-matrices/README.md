# Generalized Householder matrices

Matrices of the form $I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$ for a unit vector $v$, an identity minus a multiple of the orthogonal projector onto the line spanned by $v$: their spectrum, determinant and norm, with the orthogonal projections onto a hyperplane and the Householder reflections as special cases. They are the state transitions of [delta-rule linear attention](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/attention-and-low-rank-adaptation/delta-rule-linear-attention.md). The pages are expositions without Lean formalization.
