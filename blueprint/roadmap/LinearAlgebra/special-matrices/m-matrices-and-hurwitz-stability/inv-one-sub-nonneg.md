# Neumann series

**Neumann series.** If `K` is entrywise nonnegative with spectral radius `< 1`, then
`(1 - K)⁻¹` is entrywise nonnegative (the proof identifies it with the limit of the partial
sums `∑_{n<N} Kⁿ`).
