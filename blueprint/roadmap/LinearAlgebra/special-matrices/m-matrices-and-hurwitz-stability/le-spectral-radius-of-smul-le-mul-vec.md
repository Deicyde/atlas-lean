# Collatz–Wielandt lower bound

**Collatz–Wielandt lower bound.** If `K ≥ 0` entrywise, `z ≥ 0`, `z ≠ 0` and
`r • z ≤ K *ᵥ z` with `r ≥ 0`, then `r` is at most the spectral radius of `K`.
