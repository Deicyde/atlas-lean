# Inverse positivity of M-matrices

**Inverse positivity of M-matrices.** The inverse of a nonsingular M-matrix is entrywise
nonnegative (Berman–Plemmons, Ch. 6, Theorem 2.3, (G20) ⇒ (N38)).
