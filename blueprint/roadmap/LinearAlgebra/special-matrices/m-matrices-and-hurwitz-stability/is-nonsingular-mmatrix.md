# Nonsingular M-matrix

**Nonsingular M-matrix.** A **nonsingular M-matrix** is a Z-matrix all of whose complex eigenvalues have positive
real part (Berman–Plemmons, Ch. 6, Theorem 2.3, condition (G20)).
