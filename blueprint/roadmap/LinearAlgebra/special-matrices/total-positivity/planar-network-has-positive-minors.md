# Total positivity of totally connected planar networks

In the setting of
`planarNetwork_isTotallyNonneg`, with real edge weights, suppose the weights are positive and
the network is totally connected: for all row indices `r 0 < ⋯ < r (k-1)` and column indices
`c 0 < ⋯ < c (k-1)` there is a family of pairwise vertex-disjoint paths from `A (r a)` to
`B (c a)`. Then every minor of the weight matrix is positive (Fomin and Zelevinsky, *Total
positivity: tests and parametrizations*, Math. Intelligencer 22 (2000), no. 1, 23–33,
arXiv:math/9912128v1, Corollary 3).

## Background

- [Total nonnegativity of planar networks](planar-network-is-totally-nonneg.md): the same argument with nonnegative weights; total connectedness makes every sum nonempty, hence positive.
- [Gasca–Peña criterion for total positivity](has-positive-minors-iff-initial-minors-p.md): the test of total positivity by the $n^2$ initial minors (Fomin–Zelevinsky, Theorem 9), which they derive from the converse parametrisation of totally positive matrices by planar networks.
