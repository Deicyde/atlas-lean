# Semiseparable matrices

Lower-triangular matrices whose submatrices on or below the diagonal have bounded rank, and the sequentially semiseparable representation by products of small matrices. These are the matrices by which [linear state-space sequence models](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/linear-state-space-sequence-models/README.md) act along the sequence. The pages are expositions without Lean formalization.
