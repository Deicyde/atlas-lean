# Generalized Hyper Fibonacci succ succ

For `r ≥ 1` and `n ≥ 1`, `W_{p,n}^{(r)} = p W_{p,n-1}^{(r)} + W_{p,n}^{(r-1)}`, stated for
all integers `p`, `a`, `b`.
