# Generalized PFibonacci

The generalized Fibonacci sequence `W_{p,n}`: `W_{p,0} = a`, `W_{p,1} = b`,
`W_{p,n} = p W_{p,n-1} + W_{p,n-2}` for `n ≥ 2`.
