# Generalized Hyper Fibonacci eventually log Concave

For `r ≥ 1`, `(W_{p,n}^{(r)})_n` is eventually log-concave: for all large `n` the terms
are positive and `W_{p,n}^{(r)} W_{p,n+2}^{(r)} ≤ (W_{p,n+1}^{(r)})²`.
