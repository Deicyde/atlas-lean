# Integral of $x^r (1 - x)^r$ over $(0, 1)$

**Integral of $x^r (1 - x)^r$ over $[0, 1]$.** For every natural number $r$ (including
$r = 0$),

$$\int_0^1 x^r (1 - x)^r \, dx = \frac{1}{(2r + 1) \binom{2r}{r}}.$$
