# Eigenvectors of fg map to eigenvectors of gf

**Eigenvectors of fg map to eigenvectors of gf.** An eigenvector of `f ∘ₗ g` for a nonzero eigenvalue is sent by `g` to an
eigenvector of `g ∘ₗ f` for the same eigenvalue.
