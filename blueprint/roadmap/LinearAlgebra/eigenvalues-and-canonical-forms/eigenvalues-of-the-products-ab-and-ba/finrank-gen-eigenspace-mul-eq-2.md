# Generalized eigenspaces of AB and BA have equal dimension

**Generalized eigenspaces of AB and BA have equal dimension.** The generalized `μ`-eigenspaces of `A * B` and of `B * A` have equal
`Module.finrank`, for every exponent `k : ℕ∞`.
