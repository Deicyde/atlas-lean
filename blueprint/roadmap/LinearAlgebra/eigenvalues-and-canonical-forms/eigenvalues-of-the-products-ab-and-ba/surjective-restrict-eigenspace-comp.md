# Surjectivity of g between eigenspaces of fg and gf

**Surjectivity of g between eigenspaces of fg and gf.** The map `g` restricted to the eigenspaces is surjective when `μ ≠ 0`: an
eigenvector `y` of `g ∘ₗ f` is the image of `μ⁻¹ • f y`.
