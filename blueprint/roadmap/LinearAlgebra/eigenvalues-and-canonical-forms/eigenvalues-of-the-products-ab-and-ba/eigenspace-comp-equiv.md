# Linear equivalence of eigenspaces of fg and gf

**Linear equivalence of eigenspaces of fg and gf.** For a nonzero eigenvalue, the eigenspaces of `f ∘ₗ g` and of `g ∘ₗ f`
are linearly equivalent, via `g` in one direction.
