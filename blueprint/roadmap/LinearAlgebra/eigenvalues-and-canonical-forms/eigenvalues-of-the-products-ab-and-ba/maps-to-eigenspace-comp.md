# Eigenspace of fg maps into the eigenspace of gf

**Eigenspace of fg maps into the eigenspace of gf.** `g` sends the `μ`-eigenspace of `f ∘ₗ g` into the `μ`-eigenspace of
`g ∘ₗ f`: if `(f ∘ₗ g) x = μ • x` then `(g ∘ₗ f) (g x) = μ • (g x)`.
