# Generalized eigenspaces of fg and gf are finite-dimensional together

**Generalized eigenspaces of fg and gf are finite-dimensional together.** For a nonzero scalar, the generalized eigenspaces of `f ∘ₗ g` and of
`g ∘ₗ f` are finite-dimensional together, for every exponent `k : ℕ∞`.
