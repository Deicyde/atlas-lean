# The bias-variance split of a weighted quadratic form

**The bias-variance split of a weighted quadratic form**: for symmetric `A`,
`∑ w (x - μ)ᵀ A (x - μ) = ∑ w (x - m)ᵀ A (x - m) + (∑ w) (m - μ)ᵀ A (m - μ)` with `m` the
weighted mean.
