# Realized sign matrices have entries $\pm 1$

**Realized sign matrices have entries $\pm 1$.** For every Boolean $n \times n$ matrix $A$ and
all indices $i, j$, the $(i, j)$ entry of the real realization of $A$ is $1$ or $-1$.
