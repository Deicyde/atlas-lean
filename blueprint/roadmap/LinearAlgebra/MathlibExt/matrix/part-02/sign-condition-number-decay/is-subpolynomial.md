# Subpolynomial sequences

**Subpolynomial sequences.** A sequence $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to [0, \infty)$ is subpolynomial
when for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ we have $f(n) = o(n^{\varepsilon})$ as $n \to \infty$.
