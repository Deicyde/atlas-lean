# No submodules are $1$-neighbors

**No submodules are $1$-neighbors.** For any submodules $L_1$ and $L_2$ of a module $M$
over a commutative ring $R$, $L_1$ and $L_2$ are not $1$-neighbors, since $1$ is not prime.
