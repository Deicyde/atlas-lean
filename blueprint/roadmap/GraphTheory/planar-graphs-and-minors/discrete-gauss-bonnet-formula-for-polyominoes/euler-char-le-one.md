# Euler characteristic bound via the open-top skeleton

If the open-top skeleton of `S` (its 1-skeleton with the top edge of every cell removed) is
connected, then the Euler characteristic `V - E + F` of the closed cell complex formed by the
cells of `S` is at most `1`. See also
[Euler characteristic at most 1 for a connected skeleton](../hopf-s-umlaufsatz-for-lattice-polygons/part-10/euler-char-le-one-of-skeleton-connected.md),
which assumes connectivity of the full skeleton.
