# Bramble of order greater than `k`

A vertex set covers a bramble $\mathcal{B}$ if it meets every member of $\mathcal{B}$; the order of
$\mathcal{B}$ is the least size of a cover. The bramble has order greater than $k$ when no set of at
most $k$ vertices covers it.

## Sources

- R. Diestel, *Graph Theory*, 6th edition, author's online preview of Chapter 12 (PDF dated 28 June 2024, https://diestel-graph-theory.com/), Section 12.4.
