# A graph with an edge has tree-width at least one

If a graph has an edge $uv$, it does not have tree-width at most $0$: by (T2) some bag contains both
$u$ and $v$, so it has at least two vertices.

## Sources

- R. Diestel, *Graph Theory*, 6th edition, author's online preview of Chapter 12 (PDF dated 28 June 2024, https://diestel-graph-theory.com/), Section 12.3, condition (T2), and Section 12.4 (definition of width).
