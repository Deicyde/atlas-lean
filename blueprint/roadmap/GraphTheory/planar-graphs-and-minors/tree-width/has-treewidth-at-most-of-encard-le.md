# The one-bag decomposition

A graph with at most $k + 1$ vertices has tree-width at most $k$: a tree with one node whose bag is
the whole vertex set is a tree-decomposition.

## Sources

- R. Diestel, *Graph Theory*, 6th edition, author's online preview of Chapter 12 (PDF dated 28 June 2024, https://diestel-graph-theory.com/), Section 12.4 (definition of tree-width).
