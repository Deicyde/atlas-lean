# Unit lattice step in a given direction

**Unit lattice step in a given direction.** The unit step of `ℤ²` in direction `w`: `0 ↦ (1, 0)` (east), `1 ↦ (0, 1)` (north), `2 ↦ (-1, 0)`
 (west), `3 ↦ (0, -1)` (south).
