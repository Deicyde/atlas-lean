# Isolation is avoidance of the pairs at a vertex

For an indicator $\omega$ of a set of pairs of vertices of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the vertex $v$ is isolated in the graph of $\omega$ exactly when $\omega$ contains none of the [pairs at $v$](pairs-at.md).

## Sources

- A. Frieze and M. Karoński, *Introduction to Random Graphs*, Cambridge University Press (2016), proof of Theorem 4.1, where an isolated vertex is a component of order one.
