# First moment of isolated vertices at the connectivity threshold

If
`pₙ = (log n + c)/n` for all large `n`, then the expected number `n (1 - pₙ)^(n - 1)` of isolated
vertices of `G(n, pₙ)` tends to `e^{-c}` (Frieze and Karoński, *Introduction to Random Graphs*,
2016, proof of Theorem 4.1: `E X₁ = n(1 - p)^{n-1} ≈ e^{-c}`).
