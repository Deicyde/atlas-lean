# Cartesian Grid opposability

For `k ≥ 1` and all `nᵢ ≥ 1`, the Cartesian grid `□_{i=1}^k P_{nᵢ}` is almost
opposable iff at most one `nᵢ` is even, and otherwise it is opposable.
