# Strong Prod

The strong product `G ⊠ H`: the formula product for
`(g₁ = g₂ ∧ h₁h₂ ∈ E) ∨ (g₁g₂ ∈ E ∧ h₁ = h₂) ∨ (g₁g₂ ∈ E ∧ h₁h₂ ∈ E)`.
