# Formula Product

The graph product `G φ H` on `V × W` given by a Boolean function `φ` of the
atoms `g₁g₂ ∈ E(G)`, `h₁h₂ ∈ E(H)`, `g₁ = g₂`, `h₁ = h₂` (symmetric in the two
endpoints, so `fromRel` only removes loops).
