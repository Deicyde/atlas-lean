# Petersen coloring conjecture

**Petersen coloring conjecture.** For every finite vertex type $V$ and every simple graph $G$
on $V$, if every vertex of $G$ has exactly three neighbors and $G$ is bridgeless, then $G$ admits
a Petersen coloring.
