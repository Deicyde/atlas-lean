# Erdős's lower bound, graphRamsey form

If $\binom nk 2^{1 - \binom k2} < 1$, then $n < R(k, k)$ for the repository's [classical Ramsey number](graph-ramsey.md) `graphRamsey k k`. The least $N$ with the Ramsey property exists by the [existence of Ramsey numbers](exists-classical-ramsey.md), and it exceeds $n$ by [Erdős's lower bound](lt-of-classical-ramsey-of-choose-mul-lt.md). For example $R(5, 5) > 11$.

## Informally uses

- [Erdős's lower bound for diagonal Ramsey numbers](lt-of-classical-ramsey-of-choose-mul-lt.md): applied to the least $N$ with the Ramsey property.
- [Existence of Ramsey numbers for cliques](exists-classical-ramsey.md): that least $N$ exists.
