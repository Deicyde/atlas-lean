# Moon's count of circular triads

**Moon's count of circular triads.** In a tournament on $n$ players with scores
$s_1, \dots, s_n$, the number of circular triads is
$\binom{n}{3} - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \binom{s_i}{2}$.
