# Easy direction of Cheeger's inequality, eigenvalue form

For nonnegative weights and every
vertex set `S`, `λ₂ · |S| · |V \ S| ≤ |V| · w(∂S)`; with `s = |S|/|V|` this reads
`w(∂S)/|S| ≥ λ₂ (1 - s)`, hence the isoperimetric ratio satisfies `θ_G ≥ λ₂/2`
(D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Theorem 20.1.1, stated there
for unit weights).
