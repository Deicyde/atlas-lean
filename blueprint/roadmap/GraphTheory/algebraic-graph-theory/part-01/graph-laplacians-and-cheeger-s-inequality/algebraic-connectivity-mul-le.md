# `λ₂` bounds the Rayleigh quotient on vectors orthogonal to the constants

For nonnegative
weights and every real `x` with `∑ x = 0`, `λ₂ · xᵀx ≤ xᵀ L x` (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and
Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Section 3.2: the Courant–Fischer theorem, Theorem 2.0.1,
gives `λ₂ = min_{x ⊥ 1} xᵀLx / xᵀx` because `1` spans an eigenvector of `λ₁ = 0`).
