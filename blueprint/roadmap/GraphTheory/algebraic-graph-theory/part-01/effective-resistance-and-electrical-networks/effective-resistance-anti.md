# Rayleigh's monotonicity law for effective resistance

If `0 ≤ w ≤ w'` edgewise and the
effective conductance between `s` and `t` is positive for `w`, then the effective resistance
between them for `w'` is at most that for `w`: raising resistances cannot decrease an effective
resistance (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, the remark after
Theorem 12.3.1).
