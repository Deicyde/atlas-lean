# Thomson's principle, Pythagorean form

For positive conductances, if `L v = δ_s - δ_t`
and `θ` is a unit flow from `s` to `t`, then
`E(θ) = (v(s) - v(t)) + E(θ - i)`, where `i` is the current of `v`. This is the orthogonal
decomposition `‖θ‖²_r = ‖i‖²_r + ‖θ - i‖²_r` of R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and
Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026,
equation (2.10), together with `E(i) = v(s) - v(t)` from equation (2.7).

The source states (2.10) for any flow between vertex sets `A` and `Z` with the same divergence as the current; this page covers single vertices `A = {s}`, `Z = {t}` and unit strength, and it holds for any potential `v` solving `L v = δ_s - δ_t`, without a looplessness or connectivity hypothesis.
