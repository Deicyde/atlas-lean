# Mem Lp Of Lq Integrable On Finite Measure Sets Wanted

Fix $1 \le p < q < \infty$.
Suppose $f \in L^p(\mathbb{R})$ and $\int_A |f|^q < \infty$ for any measurable $A$ with $|A| <
\infty$. Show that $f \in L^q(\mathbb{R})$.
