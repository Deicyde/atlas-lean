# Lp Two Lp One Incomparable Wanted

Neither the inclusion $L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d}) \subset L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d})$ nor the inclusion
$L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d}) \subset L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})$ is valid.
