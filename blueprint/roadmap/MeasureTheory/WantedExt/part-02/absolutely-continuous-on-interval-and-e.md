# Lipschitz One Abs Cont Deriv Linfty Wanted

If $f\in \operatorname{Lip}1$ on $[a,b]$ (i.e. $f$ is Lipschitz continuous on $[a,b]$ with
Lipschitz constant $1$), prove that $f$ is absolutely continuous and that $f'\in L^\infty$.
