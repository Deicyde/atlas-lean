# Dilated L1 Function Decay Ae Wanted

Suppose $f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})$ and $\lambda>0$. Show that $\lim_{n\to +\infty}
n^{-\lambda}f(nx)=0$ for almost all $x\in\mathbb{R}$ (where $n\in\mathbb{N}$).
