# Cube Abs Sum Integral Asymptotic Wanted

Let $I_n$ be the integral of $\left|\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right|$ over the unit $n$-cube $-1/2\le
x_i\le 1/2$. Prove that $n^{-1/2}I_n\to (6\pi)^{-1/2}$ as $n\to\infty$.
