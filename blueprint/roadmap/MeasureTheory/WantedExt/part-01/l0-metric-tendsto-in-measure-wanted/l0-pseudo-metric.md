# L0 Pseudo Metric

`∫ x in [0, 1], |f x - g x| / (1 + |f x - g x|)`. On representatives this is a pseudometric
(functions that agree a.e. on `[0, 1]` are at distance `0`); it is the source's metric `d` on the
a.e.-classes of `L⁰([0, 1])`.
