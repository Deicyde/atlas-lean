# Volume of the cross-polytope

**Volume of the cross-polytope.** For every integer $n \ge 1$, the cross-polytope
$\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : |x_1| + \dots + |x_n| \le 1\}$ in the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ has
volume $2^n / n!$.
