# The vanishing cutoff factor vanishes at the right endpoint

**The vanishing cutoff factor vanishes at the right endpoint.**
For all real numbers $a$, $b$ and $\delta$, the vanishing cutoff factor with endpoints $a$, $b$
and width $\delta$ takes the value $0$ at $b$.
