# The vanishing cutoff factor is at most one

**The vanishing cutoff factor is at most one.** For all real numbers $a$, $b$, $\delta$
and $x$, the value at $x$ of the vanishing cutoff factor with endpoints $a$, $b$ and width
$\delta$ is at most $1$.
