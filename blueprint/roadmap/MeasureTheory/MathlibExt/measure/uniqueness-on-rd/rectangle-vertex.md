# Rectangle Vertex

The vertex of the rectangle `(a, b] ⊆ Fin d → ℝ` obtained by selecting
the lower coordinate `a i` for indices `i ∈ S` and the upper coordinate
`b i` otherwise. Used to compute inclusion-exclusion sums over all `2 ^ d`
vertices.
