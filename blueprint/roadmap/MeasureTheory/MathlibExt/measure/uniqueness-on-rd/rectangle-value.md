# Rectangle Value

The alternating sum of `F` over the `2 ^ d` vertices of the rectangle
`(a, b]`. For a distribution function `F` on `ℝᵈ`, this equals the mass
`μ ((a, b])` of the associated measure (signed inclusion-exclusion).
