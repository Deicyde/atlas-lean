# Injectors

**Injectors.** Let $\mathfrak{X}$ be a class of groups and $G$ a group. A subgroup $V$ of $G$ is
an $\mathfrak{X}$-injector of $G$ if, for every subnormal subgroup $N$ of $G$, the intersection
$V \cap N$ is $\mathfrak{X}$-maximal in $N$.
