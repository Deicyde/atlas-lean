# Rigid level stabilizer (Kourovka 15.15)

**Rigid level stabilizer (Kourovka 15.15).** Let $\rho$ be a homomorphism from a group $G$ to
the permutations of the vertices of the tree with branching sequence $m$, and let $n$ be a natural
number. The $n$-th rigid level stabilizer is the subgroup of $G$ generated by the rigid vertex
stabilizers of all vertices at level $n$.
