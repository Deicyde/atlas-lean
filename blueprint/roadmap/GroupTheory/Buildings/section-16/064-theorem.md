# Theorem (Section 16.10)

Let $f \colon X \to X$ be a simplicial-complex automorphism of the thick affine building $X$, with its maximal apartment system. Then the geometric realization $|f|$ of $f$ maps conical cells to conical cells in $|X|$, and defines a simplicial-complex automorphism $f_\infty$ of $X_\infty$ by $f_\infty(c_\infty) = (fc)_\infty$ where $c$ is a conical cell and $c_\infty$ is its face at infinity. If $f$ is label-preserving, then so is the induced map $f_\infty$.
