# Nilpotent Class Four Formations Distributive refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 10.56 "No": the lattice of varieties of nilpotent
`2`-groups of class `≤ 4` is not distributive (Belov; Bryce), and `𝔙 ↦ 𝔙 ∩ 𝔉` (`𝔉` the class of
finite groups) embeds it in the lattice of formations (Kovács), with image among the formations of
finite nilpotent groups of class `≤ 4`. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved
Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of
Solved Problems, Problem 10.56 (A. N. Skiba); resolution: Yu. A. Belov, Algebra and Logic 9, no. 6
(1970), 371-374; R. A. Bryce, Philos. Trans. Roy. Soc. London (A) 266 (1970), 281-355, footnote on
p. 335; L. Kovács, letter of 12 May 1988, Kourovka Archive, Problem 10.56 ("No"): the lattice of
varieties of nilpotent 2-groups of class ≤ 4 is non-distributive, and V ↦ V ∩ F embeds it in the
lattice of formations.
