# Nilpotent Class Four Formations Distributive

Kourovka 10.56 (A. N. Skiba): the lattice of formations of finite nilpotent groups of class at
most `4` is distributive: `A ∧ (B ∨ C) = (A ∧ B) ∨ (A ∧ C)` for all such formations.
