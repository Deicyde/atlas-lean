# Kourovka Problem 12.24 (free subgroups of the Nottingham group)

**Kourovka Problem 12.24 (free subgroups of the Nottingham group).** For every prime $p$ there
is an injective group homomorphism from the free group $F_2$ on two generators to the group of
ring automorphisms of $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})[[x]]$ whose image consists of automorphisms
$\varphi$ with $\varphi(x) - x \in (x^2)$, that is, of elements of the Nottingham group
$N(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$.
