# Is Nottingham

A ring automorphism `φ` of `R⟦x⟧` lies in the Nottingham group `N(R)` when it sends `x` to
`x (1 + a₁ x + a₂ x² + ⋯)`, that is, `φ x - x ∈ (x²)`.
