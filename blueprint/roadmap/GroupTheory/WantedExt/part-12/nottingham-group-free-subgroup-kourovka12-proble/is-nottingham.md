# Nottingham condition on automorphisms of power series

**Nottingham condition on automorphisms of power series.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A
ring automorphism $\varphi$ of the power series ring $R[[x]]$ satisfies the Nottingham condition
when $\varphi(x) - x$ lies in the ideal generated by $x^2$, that is, when
$\varphi(x) = x(1 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + \cdots)$ for some $a_1, a_2, \ldots \in R$.

For a prime $p$ and $R = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, every ring automorphism of $R[[x]]$ is
$R$-linear and preserves the maximal ideal, so it is the substitution $x \mapsto \varphi(x)$; the
automorphisms satisfying the Nottingham condition then form the Nottingham group $N(R)$.
