# Nilpotent Three Conjugates In Fitting

Kourovka 17.40 (E. P. Vdovin): for every nilpotent subgroup `N` of a finite group `G` there are
`x, y ∈ G` with `N ∩ N^x ∩ N^y ≤ F(G)`.
