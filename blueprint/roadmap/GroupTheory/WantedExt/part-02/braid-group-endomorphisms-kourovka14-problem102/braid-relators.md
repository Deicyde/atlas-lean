# Braid Relators

Artin's relators for the braid group on `n` strands, in the free group on the generators
`σ₀, …, σ_{n-2}` (indexed by `Fin (n - 1)`): `σᵢ σⱼ = σⱼ σᵢ` when `j ≥ i + 2`, and
`σᵢ σᵢ₊₁ σᵢ = σᵢ₊₁ σᵢ σᵢ₊₁`.
