# Block Lattices Generate All Lattices

Kourovka 11.93 (D. M. Smirnov): the variety of all lattices is generated by the block lattices
of finite groups, i.e. every lattice identity `p = q` that holds in the block lattice of every
finite group holds in every lattice.
