# Block Lattices Generate All Lattices refuted

Resolved false: Smirnov shows that the variety generated by the block lattices of groups does not
contain all finite lattices (the filters `{x ∈ C(G) | g ∈ x}` are modular, even arguesian), so some
lattice identity holds in the block lattice of every (finite) group but fails in some lattice; the
Kourovka Archive answers 11.93 "No, it is not". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 11.93 (D. M. Smirnov); resolution: D. M. Smirnov, Siberian Math.
J. 33, no. 4 (1992), 663-668, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.93: "No, it is not"; Archive 9.62: the
variety generated by the block lattices of groups does not contain all finite lattices,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
