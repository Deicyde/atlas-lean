# Lattice terms

**Lattice terms.** Lattice terms in the variables $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$ are built
inductively: each variable $x_n$ is a term, and for terms $s$ and $t$ their join $s \vee t$ and
their meet $s \wedge t$ are terms.
