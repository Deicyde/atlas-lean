# Aut Iso Aut Of Proper Subgroup

Kourovka 15.27 (Ya. G. Berkovich): for some prime `p` there are a finite `p`-group `G` of order
greater than `2` and a proper subgroup `H < G` with `Aut G ≅ Aut H`.
