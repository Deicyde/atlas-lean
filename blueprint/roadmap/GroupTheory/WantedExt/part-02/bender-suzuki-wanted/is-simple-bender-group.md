# Simple Bender groups

**Simple Bender groups.** A group $G$ is a simple Bender group if it is isomorphic to
$\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$, to the Suzuki group $\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$ for some
$n \ge 1$, or to $\mathrm{PSU}(3, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$.
