# Scottish Book Problem62 Claim

Mazur and Ulam's Problem 62 from the Scottish Book: if a group `G`
is the union of an increasing chain of subgroups `H n`, each isomorphic
to `H 1`, is `G` itself isomorphic to `H 1`?
