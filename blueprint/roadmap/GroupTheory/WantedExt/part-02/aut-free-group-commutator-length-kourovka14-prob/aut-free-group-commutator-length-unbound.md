# Aut Free Group Commutator Length Unbounded

Kourovka 14.15 (V. G. Bardakov), answer `kₙ = ∞`: for every `n ≥ 3`, the commutator length is
unbounded on the derived subgroup of `Aut F_n`, so the supremum `kₙ` is infinite.
