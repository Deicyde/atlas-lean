# Boundary of the square

**Boundary of the square.** The boundary $\partial I^2$ of the closed square
$I^2 = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ is the set of points $(x, y) \in I^2$ with $x = 0$, $x = 1$, $y = 0$
or $y = 1$.
