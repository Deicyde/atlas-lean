# Aut Order Prime Power Bound

Kourovka 18.82 (R. M. Patne): there is a function `f : ℕ → ℕ` such that for every prime `p`
and every finite group `G`, if `p ^ f(n)` divides `|G|` then `p ^ n` divides `|Aut G|`.
