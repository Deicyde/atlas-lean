# Torsion Free Three Classes Index Two holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 11.42 "Yes, there does" (Minasyan): there is a
torsion-free group with exactly three conjugacy classes that has a subgroup of index 2. Source: E.
I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st
issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 11.42 (A. V. Izosov);
resolution: A. Minasyan, Comment. Math. Helv. 84 (2009), 259-296, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.42:
"Yes, there does", https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
