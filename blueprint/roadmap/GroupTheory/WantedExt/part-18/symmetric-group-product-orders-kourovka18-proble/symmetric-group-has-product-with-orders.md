# Symmetric Group Has Product With Orders

Kourovka 18.49 (S. Kohl): for every `n` and all `a, b, c` with `1 < a, b, c ≤ n - 2`, the
symmetric group `Sₙ` has elements `x`, `y` of orders `a` and `b` whose product `x y` has order
`c`.
