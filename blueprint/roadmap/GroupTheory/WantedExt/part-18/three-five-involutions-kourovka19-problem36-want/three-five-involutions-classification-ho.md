# Three Five Involutions Classification holds

Resolved true: Bettio proved Jabara's conjecture, even without the hypothesis that `G` is periodic:
a group generated by involutions in which the product of two involutions has order 1, 2, 3 or 5, all
four occurring, is isomorphic to `A₅` or to `PSU(3, 4)`; the Kourovka Archive records the proof.
