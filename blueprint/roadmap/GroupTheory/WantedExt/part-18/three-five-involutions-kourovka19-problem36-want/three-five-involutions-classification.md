# Three Five Involutions Classification

Kourovka 19.36 (E. Jabara): a periodic group `G` with `{3, 5}`-involutions, that is, generated
by its involutions, with the order of `xy` in `{1, 2, 3, 5}` for all involutions `x, y` and each
of these four values attained, is isomorphic to `A₅` or to `PSU(3, 4)`.
