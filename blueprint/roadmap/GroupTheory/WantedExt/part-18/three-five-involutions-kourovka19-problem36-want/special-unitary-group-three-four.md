# Special Unitary Group Three Four

The special unitary group `SU₃(4)`: the matrices `A ∈ GL₃(𝔽₁₆)` of determinant `1` with
`Āᵀ A = 1`, where `Ā` applies the involutive field automorphism `x ↦ x⁴` of `𝔽₁₆` entrywise (the
isometries of the Hermitian form `x₁ȳ₁ + x₂ȳ₂ + x₃ȳ₃`). Written as the subgroup generated by these
matrices, which already form a subgroup.
