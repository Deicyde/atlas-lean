# Thompson Relators

The relators of the standard presentation `⟨x₀, x₁ | [x₀x₁⁻¹, x₀⁻¹x₁x₀], [x₀x₁⁻¹, x₀⁻²x₁x₀²]⟩`
of Thompson's group `F`, with `[a, b] = a b a⁻¹ b⁻¹`.
