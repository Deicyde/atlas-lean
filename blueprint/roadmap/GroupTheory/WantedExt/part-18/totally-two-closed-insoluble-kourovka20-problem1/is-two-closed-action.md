# Is Two Closed Action

The action of `G` on `Ω` is `2`-closed: every permutation `σ` of `Ω` that preserves each
`G`-orbit on ordered pairs (for all `α, β` some `g ∈ G` agrees with `σ` at `α` and at `β`) is
induced by an element of `G`.
