# Exists Massive Normally Independent Set refuted

Resolved false: Koch-Hyde and Olive (arXiv:2609.00382, Theorem 1.7) prove that a subset `S` of the
ball `Bₙ` of `F_r` that is independent (no `x ∈ S` lies in the subgroup generated by `S \ {x}`) has
`|S| = O((2r - 1)^{n/2})`. Condition (1) implies independence, so every such `E ⊆ F₂` has `|Eₙ| =
O(3^{n/2})` and growth rate at most `√3 < 3`. The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 10.13 solved,
"No, there is no such set". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in
Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 10.13 (R. I.
Grigorchuk); resolution: L. Koch-Hyde and É. Olive, Two problems about bases and independent sets in
free groups, arXiv:2609.00382 (2026), Theorem 1.7 (an independent set S ⊆ B_n ⊆ F_r has |S| = O((2r
- 1)^{n/2})) and the Solution to Problem 1.5; the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 10.13 solved,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00382.
