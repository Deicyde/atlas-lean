# Maximal Soluble Subgroup Normalizer Supplement

Kourovka 14.62 (V. S. Monakhov): if `H` is a non-soluble normal subgroup of a finite group `G`,
then some maximal soluble subgroup `S` of `H` satisfies `G = H N_G(S)`.
