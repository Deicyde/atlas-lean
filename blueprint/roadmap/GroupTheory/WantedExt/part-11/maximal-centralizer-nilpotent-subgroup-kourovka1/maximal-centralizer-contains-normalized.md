# Maximal Centralizer Contains Normalized Nilpotent

Kourovka 14.67 (I. T. Mukhamet'yanov, A. N. Fomin): if `a ≠ 1` in a finite group `G` has
`|C_G(a)| ≥ |C_G(x)|` for every `x ≠ 1`, then every nilpotent subgroup `H` of `G` normalized by
`C_G(a)` lies in `C_G(a)`.
