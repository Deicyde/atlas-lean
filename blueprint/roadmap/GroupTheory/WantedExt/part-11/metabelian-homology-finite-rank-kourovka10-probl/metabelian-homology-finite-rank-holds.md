# Metabelian Homology Finite Rank holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers "Yes, it is", citing Kochloukova (2003): every finitely
generated metabelian group whose integral homology groups are all finitely generated has finite rank
(Groves had proved this when `G` splits over its derived subgroup). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 10.37 (G. A. Noskov); resolution: D.
H. Kochloukova, Groups with finitely generated integral homologies, in: Groups St. Andrews 2001 in
Oxford, Vol. II, Cambridge Univ. Press, 2003, 332-343, Kourovka Archive, Problem 10.37 ("Yes, it
is").
