# Metabelian Homology Finite Rank

Kourovka 10.37 (G. A. Noskov): if `G` is a finitely generated metabelian group all of whose
integral homology groups `Hₙ(G, ℤ)` (trivial coefficients) are finitely generated, then `G` has
finite (Prüfer) rank: for some `r`, every finitely generated subgroup of `G` is generated by at most
`r` elements.
