# Locally nilpotent group (Kourovka 13.16)

**Locally nilpotent group (Kourovka 13.16).** A group $G$ is locally nilpotent if for every
finite subset $S \subseteq G$ the subgroup generated by $S$ is nilpotent, that is, every finitely
generated subgroup of $G$ is nilpotent.
