# Is Hypercentral

`G` is hypercentral: every nontrivial quotient `G ⧸ N` has nontrivial centre, i.e. some
`g ∉ N` commutes with all of `G` modulo `N` (equivalently, the transfinite upper central series
reaches `G`).
