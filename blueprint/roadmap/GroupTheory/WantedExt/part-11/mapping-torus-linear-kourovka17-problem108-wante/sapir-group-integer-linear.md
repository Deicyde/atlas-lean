# Sapir Group Integer Linear

Kourovka 17.108 (M. Sapir), in the form of its resolution: the group
`⟨a, b, t | a^t = ab, b^t = ba⟩` is `ℤ`-linear, that is, it embeds in `GL_n(ℤ)` for some `n`.
