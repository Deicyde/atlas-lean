# Sapir's group

**Sapir's group.** Sapir's group is the group with presentation
$\langle a, b, t \mid a^t = ab, b^t = ba \rangle$, where $a^t = t^{-1} a t$: the quotient of the
free group on $a$, $b$, $t$ by the normal subgroup generated by $t^{-1} a t (ab)^{-1}$ and
$t^{-1} b t (ba)^{-1}$.
