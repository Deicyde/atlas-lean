# Hirsch–Plotkin radical

**Hirsch–Plotkin radical.** The Hirsch–Plotkin radical of a group $G$ is the subgroup
generated by all locally nilpotent normal subgroups of $G$. By the Hirsch–Plotkin theorem it is
the largest locally nilpotent normal subgroup of $G$.
