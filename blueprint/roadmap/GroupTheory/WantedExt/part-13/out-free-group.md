# Out Free Group Shared

`Out(F_n) = Aut(F_n) / Inn(F_n)`, with `Aut(F_n) = MulAut (FreeGroup (Fin n))`. The inner
automorphisms (the image of `MulAut.conj`) form a normal subgroup, so the normal closure below is
`Inn(F_n)` itself.
