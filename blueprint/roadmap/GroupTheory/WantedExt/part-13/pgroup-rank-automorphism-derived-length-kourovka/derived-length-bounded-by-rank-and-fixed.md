# Derived Length Bounded By Rank And Fixed Points

Kourovka 13.56 (A. Shalev; E. I. Khukhro): there is a function `f(r, m)` such that every
finite `p`-group `G` of sectional rank at most `r` with an automorphism `φ` having exactly `m`
fixed points has derived length at most `f(r, m)`: `derivedSeries G (f r m) = ⊥`.
