# Derived Length Bounded By Rank And Fixed Points holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 13.56 "Yes, it is", citing Jaikin-Zapirain: the derived
length of a finite `p`-group of sectional rank `r` with an automorphism having exactly `m` fixed
points is bounded by a function of `r` and `m`. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 13.56 (A. Shalev; E. I. Khukhro); resolution: A.
Jaikin-Zapirain, Israel J. Math. 129 (2002), 209-220, Kourovka Archive, Problem 13.56 (answer: Yes,
it is).
