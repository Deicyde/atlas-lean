# Order Divisibility Bijection To Cyclic holds

Resolved true: Amiri proves that for every finite group `G` of order `n` there is a bijection `f`
from `G` onto the cyclic group of order `n` with `o(x) ∣ o(f(x))` for all `x ∈ G`; the Kourovka
Notebook (21st issue) answers 18.1 "Yes, it does". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Problem 18.1 (I. M. Isaacs); resolution: M. Amiri, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 228 (2024), no. 7, 107632
(preprint arXiv:2002.11455), abstract (for any finite group G of order n there is a bijection f from
G onto C_n with o(x) | o(f(x)) for all x); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 18.1 solved
(Yes, it does), https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11455.
