# Gl2 Trace Range Contains Ray

Kourovka 16.69(a) (J. Mycielski): for every nontrivial word `w(x, y)` the range of
`tr w(x, y)` over `x, y ∈ GL(2, ℝ)` contains the interval `[-2, ∞)`.
