# Two Closure

The `2`-closure `G⁽²⁾` of `G ≤ Sym(Ω)`: the permutations that map every pair `(α, β)` into its
`G`-orbit, which form the largest subgroup of `Sym(Ω)` containing `G` with the same orbits on
`Ω × Ω`. Written as the subgroup generated by these permutations, which already form a subgroup.
