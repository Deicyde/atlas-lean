# Solvable Two Closure Factors Cyclic Or Alternating refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 19.67 "No, it is not true", citing Skresanov's solvable
permutation group whose `2`-closure has a composition factor that is neither cyclic nor alternating.
