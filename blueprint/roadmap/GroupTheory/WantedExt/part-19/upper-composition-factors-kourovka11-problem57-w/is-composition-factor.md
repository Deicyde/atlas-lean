# Is Composition Factor

The simple group `T` is a composition factor of the finite group `Q`: `T` is a homomorphic
image of a subnormal subgroup of `Q`. For simple `T` and finite `Q` this is the same as `T`
occurring as a factor of a composition series of `Q` (refine `ker ≤ H` and the subnormal chain).
