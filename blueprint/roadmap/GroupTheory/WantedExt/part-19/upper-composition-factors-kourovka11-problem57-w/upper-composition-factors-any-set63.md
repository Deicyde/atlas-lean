# Upper Composition Factors Any Set63

Kourovka 11.57 (A. Mann, D. Segal), first part of the answer: for every family `(T i)` of
finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group generated by 63 elements whose upper
composition factors are, up to isomorphism, exactly the `T i`.
