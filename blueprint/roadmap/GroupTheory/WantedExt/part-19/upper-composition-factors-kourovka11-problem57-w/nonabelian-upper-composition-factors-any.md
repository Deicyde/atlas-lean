# Nonabelian Upper Composition Factors Any Set3

Kourovka 11.57 (A. Mann, D. Segal), second part of the answer: for every family `(T i)` of
finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group generated by 3 elements whose non-abelian upper
composition factors are, up to isomorphism, exactly the `T i`.
