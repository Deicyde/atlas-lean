# Kourovka Problem 11.57, 3-generator form (statement)

**Kourovka Problem 11.57, 3-generator form (statement).** The statement that for every family
$(T_i)_{i \in I}$ of finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group $G$ generated by $3$
elements such that a finite non-abelian simple group $Q$ is an upper composition factor of $G$ if
and only if $Q$ is isomorphic to $T_i$ for some $i \in I$. Abelian upper composition factors of $G$
are not restricted.
