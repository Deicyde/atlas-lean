# Upper Composition Factors Any Set63 holds

Resolved true: Segal proves that every set of non-abelian finite simple groups is exactly the set of
upper composition factors of some 63-generator group, as recorded in the Kourovka Archive ("There
are no restrictions"). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems,
Problem 11.57 (A. Mann, D. Segal); resolution: D. Segal, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 82 (2001),
597-613, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.57: any set of non-abelian finite simple groups is the set of
upper composition factors of a 63-generator group; with abelian upper composition factors allowed, 3
generators suffice, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
