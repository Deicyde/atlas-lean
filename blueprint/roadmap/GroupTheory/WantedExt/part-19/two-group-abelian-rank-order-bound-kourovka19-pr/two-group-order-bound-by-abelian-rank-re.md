# Two Group Order Bound By Abelian Rank refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 19.75 "No, it is not true", as follows from
Olshanskii's examples of finite `p`-groups whose abelian subgroups are small compared to the group
(pointed out by Mann). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems,
Problem 19.75 (B. Sambale); resolution: A. Yu. Olshanskii, The number of generators and orders of
abelian subgroups of finite p-groups, Math. Notes 23 (1978), no. 3, 183-185; A. Mann, letter of 23
April 2018, abstract: the least f(n) such that every finite p-group whose abelian subgroups are
generated by at most n elements has at most f(n) generators grows quadratically in n; Kourovka
Archive, Problem 19.75: No, it is not true, as follows from the examples constructed by Olshanskii
(Mann), https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01651428.
