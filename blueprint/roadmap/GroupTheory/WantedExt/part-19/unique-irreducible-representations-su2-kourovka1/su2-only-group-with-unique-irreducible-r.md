# Su2 Only Group With Unique Irreducible Reps

Kourovka 16.58 (J. McKay): `SU₂(ℂ)` is the only group, up to isomorphism, that has exactly one
irreducible complex representation of dimension `n` for each `n = 1, 2, …`.
