# Relatively Free Group

The free group `F_Θ(X)` of the variety `Θ` defined by the laws `W` (words in `x₀, x₁, …`), on
the finite set `X ⊆ ℕ` of free generators: `F(X)` modulo its verbal subgroup, the subgroup
generated by the values `φ w` (`w ∈ W`, `φ : FreeGroup ℕ →* F(X)`); this set of values is closed
under conjugation, so the verbal subgroup is its normal closure.
