# Is Locally Ultrapower Image

Every element of `B` lies in a subgroup of `B` that is a homomorphic image of the ultrapower
`ℤ^ω / U`, i.e. of `ℤ^ω` by a homomorphism killing the sequences that vanish `U`-almost
everywhere.
