# Is Polycyclic

`G` is polycyclic: it has a finite subnormal series `1 = H₀ ≤ H₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ Hₙ = G`, each `Hᵢ`
normal in `Hᵢ₊₁`, with cyclic factors `Hᵢ₊₁ / Hᵢ`.
