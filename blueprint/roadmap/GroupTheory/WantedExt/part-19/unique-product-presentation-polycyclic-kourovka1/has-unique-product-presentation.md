# Has Unique Product Presentation

`G` has generators `a₀, …, aₙ₋₁` such that every element of `G` is `a₀ ^ k₀ ⋯ aₙ₋₁ ^ kₙ₋₁`
for exactly one `(k₀, …, kₙ₋₁) ∈ ℤⁿ`.
