# Uncountably Many Infinite Two Group Quotients

Kourovka 16.101 (J. Wiegold): there are uncountably many pairwise non-isomorphic infinite
`2`-groups that are quotients of `⟨x, y | x² = y⁴ = (x y)⁸ = 1⟩`.
