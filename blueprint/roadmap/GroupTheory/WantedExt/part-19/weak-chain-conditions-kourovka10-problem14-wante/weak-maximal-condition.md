# Weak Maximal Condition

The weak maximal condition on subgroups: there is no infinite ascending chain
`H₀ ≤ H₁ ≤ ⋯` of subgroups with every index `|Hᵢ₊₁ : Hᵢ|` infinite (`relIndex = 0`).
