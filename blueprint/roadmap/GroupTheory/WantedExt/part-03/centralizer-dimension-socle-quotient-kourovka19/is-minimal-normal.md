# Minimal normal subgroups

**Minimal normal subgroups.** A subgroup $M$ of a group $Q$ is a minimal normal subgroup if $M$
is normal in $Q$, $M \ne \{1\}$, and every normal subgroup $N$ of $Q$ with $N \le M$ is either
$\{1\}$ or $M$.
