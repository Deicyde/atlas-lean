# Braid Roots Unique Up To Conjugacy

Kourovka 10.23 (G. S. Makanin): extraction of roots in braid groups is unique up to
conjugation: if `α ^ k = β ^ k` in `Bₙ` for some `k ≥ 1`, then `α` and `β` are conjugate.
