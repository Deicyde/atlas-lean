# Branch Group Maximal Subgroups Finite Index refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 15.15 "No, not necessarily" (Bondarenko): some finitely
generated branch group has a maximal subgroup of infinite index. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 15.15 (L. Bartholdi, R. I.
Grigorchuk, Z. Šunić); resolution: I. V. Bondarenko, Finite generation of iterated wreath products,
Arch. Math. (Basel) 95 (2010), no. 4, 301-308, arXiv:1002.0320, abstract and the example after
Corollary 3 (a finitely generated branch group with maximal subgroups of infinite index, answering
Question 14 of Bartholdi, Grigorchuk and Šunić, Branch groups); Kourovka Archive, Problem 15.15
(answer: No, not necessarily), https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.0320.
