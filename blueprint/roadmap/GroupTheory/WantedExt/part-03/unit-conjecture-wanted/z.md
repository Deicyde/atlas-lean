# The element z of Gardam's group

**The element z of Gardam's group.** In the group $P$ with presentation
$\langle a, b \mid b^{-1}a^2b = a^{-2},\ a^{-1}b^2a = b^{-2} \rangle$, $z = (ab)^2$.
