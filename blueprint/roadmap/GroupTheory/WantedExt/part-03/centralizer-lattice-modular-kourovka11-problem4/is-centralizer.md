# Centralizer subgroups

**Centralizer subgroups.** A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is a centralizer if $H = C_G(X)$ for
some subset $X \subseteq G$, where $C_G(X)$ is the subgroup of elements of $G$ that commute with
every element of $X$.
