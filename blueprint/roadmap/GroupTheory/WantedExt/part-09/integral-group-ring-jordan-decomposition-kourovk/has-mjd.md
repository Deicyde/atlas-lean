# Has MJD

`ℤ[G]` has the multiplicative Jordan decomposition property: for every unit `a` of `ℤ[G]`, the
factors of the decomposition `a = s u` in `ℚ[G]` (`s` semisimple over `ℚ`, `u` unipotent,
`s u = u s`) lie in `ℤ[G]`.
