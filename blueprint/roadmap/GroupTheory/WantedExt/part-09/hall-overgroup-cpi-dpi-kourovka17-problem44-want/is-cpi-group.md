# $C_\pi$-group

**$C_\pi$-group.** Let $\pi$ be a set of natural numbers. A group $G$ is a $C_\pi$-group when it
has a Hall $\pi$-subgroup and any two Hall $\pi$-subgroups $H$ and $K$ of $G$ are conjugate, that
is, $K = gHg^{-1}$ for some $g \in G$.
