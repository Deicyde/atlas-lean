# Relatively Free Finite Word Values Finite Verbal refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive records that the conjecture is disproved for relatively free
groups (A. Storozhev, 1996): some word takes only finitely many values on some relatively free group
whose verbal subgroup for that word is infinite. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 12.31 (S. V. Ivanov); resolution: A. Storozhev, Proc. Amer.
Math. Soc. 124 (1996), no. 10, 2953-2954, Kourovka Archive, Problem 12.31: the conjecture is
disproved.
