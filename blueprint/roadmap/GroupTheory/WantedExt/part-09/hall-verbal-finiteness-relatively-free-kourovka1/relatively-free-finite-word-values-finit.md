# Hall's verbal finiteness conjecture for relatively free groups (Kourovka 12.31)

**Hall's verbal finiteness conjecture for relatively free groups (Kourovka 12.31).** The
statement that for every relatively free group $G$, every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every word $v$ in
the free group on $n$ generators, if $v$ takes only finitely many values on $G$, then the verbal
subgroup $v(G)$ of $G$ generated by these values is finite.
