# Relatively Free Finite Word Values Finite Verbal

Kourovka 12.31 (S. V. Ivanov; P. Hall's conjecture for relatively free groups): if a word `v`
takes only finitely many values on a relatively free group `G`, then the verbal subgroup `v(G)`
is finite.
