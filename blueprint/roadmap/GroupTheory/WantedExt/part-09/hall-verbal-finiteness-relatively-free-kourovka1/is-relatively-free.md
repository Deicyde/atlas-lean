# Relatively free group

**Relatively free group.** A group $G$ is relatively free if it has a family $(x_i)_{i \in I}$
of elements that generates $G$ such that for every family $(y_i)_{i \in I}$ of elements of $G$
there is an endomorphism $\varphi$ of $G$ with $\varphi(x_i) = y_i$ for all $i \in I$.

Equivalently, $G$ is free on $(x_i)$ in the variety of groups it generates.
