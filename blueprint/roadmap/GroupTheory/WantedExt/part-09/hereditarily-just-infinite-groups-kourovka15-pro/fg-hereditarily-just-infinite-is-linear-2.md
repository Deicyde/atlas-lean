# Fg Hereditarily Just Infinite Is Linear refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 15.18(b) "No, not every" (Ershov and Jaikin-Zapirain).
As the problem notes, a positive answer to (b) would contradict (a): a finitely generated linear
torsion group is finite, while a just infinite group is infinite. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 15.18 (L. Bartholdi, R. I.
Grigorchuk, Z. Šunić); resolution: M. Ershov and A. Jaikin-Zapirain, Groups of positive weighted
deficiency and their applications, J. Reine Angew. Math. 677 (2013), 71-134, arXiv:1007.1489,
Kourovka Archive, Problem 15.18 ((a) Yes, such groups exist; (b) No, not every); the abstract
constructs finitely generated residually finite p-torsion groups and shows that every group of
positive weighted deficiency has a hereditarily just infinite quotient,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.1489.
