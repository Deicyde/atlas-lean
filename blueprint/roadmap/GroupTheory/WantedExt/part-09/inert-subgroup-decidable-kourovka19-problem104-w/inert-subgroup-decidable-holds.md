# Inert Subgroup Decidable holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers 19.104 "Yes, there is" (A.
Jaikin-Zapirain, 2024, Corollary 1.3(2)): inertness of a finitely generated subgroup is equivalent
to compressedness, which is known to be decidable. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Problem 19.104 (E. Ventura); resolution: A. Jaikin-Zapirain, Free groups are L^2-subgroup rigid,
preprint, arXiv:2403.09515 (2024), Corollary 1.3(2) (citing Kourovka 19.104): it is algorithmically
decidable, for a finitely generated subgroup of a free group, whether it is inert (compressedness is
decidable and equivalent to inertness by Corollary 1.2); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks
19.104 solved, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09515.
