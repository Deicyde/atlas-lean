# Power Quotient Infinite holds

Resolved true: Ivanov and Olshanskii prove that for every non-elementary hyperbolic group `G` the
quotient `G / G^n` is infinite for some `n`; the Kourovka Archive answers 11.74(a) "Yes, it is".
