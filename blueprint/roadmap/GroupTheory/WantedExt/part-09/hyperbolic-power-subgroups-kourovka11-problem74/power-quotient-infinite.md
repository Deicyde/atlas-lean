# Power Quotient Infinite

Kourovka 11.74(a) (M. Gromov; A. Yu. Olshanskii): for every non-elementary hyperbolic group
`G` there is `n ≥ 1` such that `G / G^n` is infinite, i.e. `G^n` has infinite index.
