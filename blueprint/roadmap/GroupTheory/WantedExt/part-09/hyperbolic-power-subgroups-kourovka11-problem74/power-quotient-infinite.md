# Kourovka Problem 11.74(a) (infinite power quotients of hyperbolic groups)

**Kourovka Problem 11.74(a) (infinite power quotients of hyperbolic groups).** For every
word-hyperbolic group $G$ that is not virtually cyclic (non-elementary), there is an integer
$n \ge 1$ such that the subgroup $G^n$ generated by the $n$-th powers of the elements of $G$ has
infinite index in $G$, i.e. the quotient $G / G^n$ is infinite.

The problem is due to M. Gromov and A. Yu. Olshanskii.
