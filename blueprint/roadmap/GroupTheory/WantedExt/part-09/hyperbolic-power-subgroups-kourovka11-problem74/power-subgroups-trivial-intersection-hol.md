# Power Subgroups Trivial Intersection holds

Resolved true: Ivanov and Olshanskii prove that `⋂_{n ≥ 1} G^n = 1` for every non-elementary
hyperbolic group `G`; the Kourovka Archive answers 11.74(b) "Yes, it is". Source: E. I. Khukhro and
V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 11.74 (A. Yu. Olshanskii);
resolution: S. V. Ivanov and A. Yu. Olshanskii, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 348, no. 6 (1996),
2091-2138, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.74: "a) Yes, it is; b) Yes, it is",
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
