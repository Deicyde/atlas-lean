# Involution Cayley Graph Integral

Kourovka 19.50(a) (D. V. Lytkina): if a finite group `G` is generated by a normal subset `R`
consisting of involutions, then the Cayley graph `Cay(G, R)` (Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.mulCayley R`)
is integral.
