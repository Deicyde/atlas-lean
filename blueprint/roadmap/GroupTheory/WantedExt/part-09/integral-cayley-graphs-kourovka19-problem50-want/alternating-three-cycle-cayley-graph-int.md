# Alternating Three Cycle Cayley Graph Integral

Kourovka 19.50(b) (D. V. Lytkina): for `S = {(123), (124), …, (12n)}` and `R = S ∪ S⁻¹`, the
Cayley graph `Cay(Aₙ, R)` (Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.mulCayley R`) is integral (`n ≥ 3`).
