# Hall Subgroup For Intersection Of Prime Sets

Kourovka 18.38 (A. V. Zavarnitsine): `E_{π₁} ∩ E_{π₂} ⊆ E_{π₁ ∩ π₂}` for all sets of primes
`π₁`, `π₂`, that is, a finite group with a Hall `π₁`-subgroup and a Hall `π₂`-subgroup has a Hall
`(π₁ ∩ π₂)`-subgroup.
