# Hall π-subgroups

**Hall π-subgroups.** Let $\pi$ be a set of natural numbers and let $H$ be a subgroup of a
group $G$. Then $H$ is a Hall $\pi$-subgroup when its order $|H|$ is a $\pi$-number and no prime
divisor of its index $|G : H|$ lies in $\pi$.
