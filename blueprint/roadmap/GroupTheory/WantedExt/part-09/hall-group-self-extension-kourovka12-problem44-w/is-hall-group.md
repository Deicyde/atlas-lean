# Is Hall Group

`G` is isomorphic to every extension of itself by itself: whenever `N` is a normal subgroup
of a group `H` with `N ≅ G` and `H ⧸ N ≅ G`, also `H ≅ G` (a Hall group in the sense of Göbel and
Shelah).
