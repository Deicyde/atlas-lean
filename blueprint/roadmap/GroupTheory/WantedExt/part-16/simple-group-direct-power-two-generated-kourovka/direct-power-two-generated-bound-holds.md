# Direct Power Two Generated Bound holds

Resolved true: Maróti and Tamburini (Theorem 1.1) prove `2√|G| < h(G)` for every non-abelian finite
simple group `G`, where `h(G)` is the largest `n` such that `Gⁿ` is 2-generated; the Kourovka
Archive answers 17.116 "Yes, it is, even with `n(G) > 2√|G|`". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 17.116 (A. Erfanian, J. Wiegold);
resolution: A. Maróti and M. C. Tamburini Bellani, A solution to a problem of Wiegold, Comm. Algebra
41 (2013), 34-49, Theorem 1.1 (for any non-abelian finite simple group G, 2√|G| < h(G), the largest
n with G^n 2-generated); Kourovka Archive, Problem 17.116 (Yes, it is, even with n(G) > 2√|G|),
https://www.renyi.hu/~maroti/centralizer12.pdf.
