# Direct Power Two Generated Bound

Kourovka 17.116 (A. Erfanian, J. Wiegold), in the form of its resolution: for every non-abelian
finite simple group `G` there is `n > 2√|G|` (written `4|G| < n²`) such that the direct power `Gⁿ`
is generated by two elements. Since `Gⁿ` maps onto `Gᵐ` for `m ≤ n`, this says that the largest
`n(G)` with `G^{n(G)}` 2-generated satisfies `n(G) > 2√|G|`.
