# Sigma Subnormal From Joins With Conjugates

Kourovka 19.84 (A. N. Skiba): for every partition `σ` of the primes, a subgroup `A` of a finite
group `G` that is `σ`-subnormal in `⟨A, A^x⟩` for every `x ∈ G` is `σ`-subnormal in `G`.
