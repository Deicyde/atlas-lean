# Schmidt Sigma Subnormal Cyclic Quotient holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 19.85 "Yes, it is true", citing Yi and Kamornikov, whose
theorem gives `G / F_σ(G)` cyclic, `F_σ(G)` the largest normal `σ`-nilpotent subgroup (for nilpotent
`G` take `N = G`); the case of the partition into single primes was known. Source: E. I. Khukhro and
V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 19.85 (A. N. Skiba); resolution: X.
Yi and S. F. Kamornikov, Finite groups with σ-subnormal Schmidt subgroups, J. Algebra 560 (2020),
181-191, main result, quoted as Lemma 8 (reference [11]) in A. Ballester-Bolinches, S. F. Kamornikov
and X. Yi, Finite groups with σ-subnormal Schmidt subgroups, Bull. Malays. Math. Sci. Soc. (2022):
if every Schmidt subgroup of a non-nilpotent group G is σ-subnormal, then G / F_σ(G) is cyclic,
F_σ(G) the largest normal σ-nilpotent subgroup; Kourovka Archive, Problem 19.85: Yes, it is true,
https://doi.org/10.1007/s40840-022-01369-y.
