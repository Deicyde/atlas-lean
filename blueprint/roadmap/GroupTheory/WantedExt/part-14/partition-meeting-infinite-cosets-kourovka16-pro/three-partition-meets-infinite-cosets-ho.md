# Three Partition Meets Infinite Cosets holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 16.43 "Yes", citing Zelenyuk (2008), and records more:
every infinite abelian group with finitely many involutions can be partitioned into infinitely many
subsets such that every coset modulo an infinite subgroup meets each subset of the partition.
