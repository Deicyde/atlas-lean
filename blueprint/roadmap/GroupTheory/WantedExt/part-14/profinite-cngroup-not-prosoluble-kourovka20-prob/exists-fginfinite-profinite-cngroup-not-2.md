# Exists FGInfinite Profinite CNGroup Not Prosoluble holds

Resolved true: Kinyon and Urban (Section 3.35) give an infinite two-generated closed subgroup of
`SL₂(F₄[[t]])` that maps onto `SL₂(F₄) ≅ A₅` and in which every non-identity element has abelian,
hence pronilpotent, centralizer. The 21st issue of the Kourovka Notebook marks 20.90 solved ("An
example is found"; AI solution confirmed by P. Shumyatsky). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov
(eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49
(2026), Problem 20.90 (P. Shumyatsky); resolution: M. Kinyon and J. Urban, Results on problems from
the Kourovka Notebook, preprint (September 2026), Kourovka Notebook site, Section 3.35: a
two-generated closed subgroup G of SL₂(F₄[[t]]) that is infinite, maps onto SL₂(F₄) ≅ A₅ by
reduction modulo t (so it is not prosoluble), and in which the centralizer of every non-identity
element is abelian; an AI solution confirmed by P. Shumyatsky, Kourovka Notebook (21st issue),
Problem 20.90: An example is found,
https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/kourovka-mathematics.pdf.
