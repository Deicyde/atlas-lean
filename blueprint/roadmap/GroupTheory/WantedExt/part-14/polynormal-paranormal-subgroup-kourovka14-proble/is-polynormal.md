# Is Polynormal

`A` is polynormal (Z. I. Borevich): `A^x ≤ ⟨A^u | u ∈ A^⟨x⟩⟩` for every `x ∈ G`, where
`A^⟨x⟩ = ⟨A^(xⁿ) | n ∈ ℤ⟩`.
