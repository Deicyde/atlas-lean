# Permuting Conjugates Not Simple

Kourovka 10.66 (A. N. Fomin, V. P. Shunkov): a group `G` containing nontrivial subgroups `A`
and `B` with `AB ≠ G` and `A B^g = B^g A` for every `g ∈ G` is not simple.
