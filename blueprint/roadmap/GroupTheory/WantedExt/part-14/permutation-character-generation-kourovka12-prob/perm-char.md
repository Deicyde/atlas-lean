# Complex permutation character on the cosets of a subgroup

**Complex permutation character on the cosets of a subgroup.** For a subgroup $H$ of a group
$G$, the permutation character $1_H^G : G \to \mathbb{C}$ sends $g \in G$ to the number of left
cosets $xH$ of $H$ with $gxH = xH$, and to $0$ when $g$ fixes infinitely many cosets. For finite
$G$ and the trivial subgroup $H = 1$ this is the regular character $1^G$.
