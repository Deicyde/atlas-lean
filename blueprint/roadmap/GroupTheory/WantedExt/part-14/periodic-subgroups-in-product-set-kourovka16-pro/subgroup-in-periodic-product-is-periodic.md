# Subgroup In Periodic Product Is Periodic

Kourovka 16.38 (F. de Giovanni): if `A` and `B` are periodic subgroups of a soluble group `G`,
then every subgroup of `G` contained in the set `AB = {a b | a ∈ A, b ∈ B}` is periodic.
