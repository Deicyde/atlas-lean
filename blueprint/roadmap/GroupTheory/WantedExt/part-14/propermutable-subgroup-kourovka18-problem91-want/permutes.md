# Permuting subgroups

**Permuting subgroups.** Let $H$ and $X$ be subgroups of a group $G$. Then $H$ permutes
with $X$ when $HX = XH$ as subsets of $G$, where $HX = \{hx : h \in H, x \in X\}$ and
$XH = \{xh : x \in X, h \in H\}$.
