# Nilpotent By Bounded Rank Of Centralizer Rank holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 13.58 "Yes, it does", citing Khukhro (the case `p = 2`
was proved earlier by Shumyatsky): `G` has a normal subgroup `N` of `p`-bounded nilpotency class
with `G / N` of `(r, p)`-bounded sectional rank. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 13.58 (E. I. Khukhro); resolution: E. I. Khukhro, J. London
Math. Soc. 77 (2008), 130-148 (the case p = 2: P. Shumyatsky, Arch. Math. 71 (1998), 425-432),
Kourovka Archive, Problem 13.58 (answer: Yes, it does).
