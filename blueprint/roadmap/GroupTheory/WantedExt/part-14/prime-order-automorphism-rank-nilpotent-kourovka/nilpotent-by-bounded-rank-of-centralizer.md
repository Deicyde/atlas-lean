# Nilpotent By Bounded Rank Of Centralizer Rank

Kourovka 13.58 (E. I. Khukhro): there are functions `f(p)` and `g(r, p)` such that if `φ` is an
automorphism of prime order `p` of a periodic nilpotent group `G` and `C_G(φ)` has finite sectional
rank `r`, then `G` has a normal subgroup `N`, nilpotent of class at most `f(p)`, such that `G / N`
has sectional rank at most `g(r, p)`.
