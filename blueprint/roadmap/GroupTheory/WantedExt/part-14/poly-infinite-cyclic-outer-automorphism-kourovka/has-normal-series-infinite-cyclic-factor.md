# Has Normal Series Infinite Cyclic Factors

`G` has a finite series `1 = H₀ ≤ H₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ Hₙ = G` of normal subgroups of `G` whose factors
`Hᵢ₊₁ ⧸ Hᵢ` are infinite cyclic.
