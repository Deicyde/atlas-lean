# Product Of Three Large Classes Is Alternating Group

Kourovka 20.23 (M. Garonzi, A. Maróti): there are `δ ∈ (0, 1)` and `N` such that whenever
`n ≥ N` and `A`, `B`, `C` are conjugacy classes of `Alt(n)` each of size at least `|Alt(n)|^δ`,
then `ABC = Alt(n)`.
