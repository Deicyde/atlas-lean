# Rota–Baxter operators on groups

**Rota–Baxter operators on groups.** A Rota–Baxter operator (of weight $1$) on a group $G$ is
a map $B \colon G \to G$ such that $B(g)\,B(h) = B\bigl(g\,B(g)\,h\,B(g)^{-1}\bigr)$ for all
$g, h \in G$.
