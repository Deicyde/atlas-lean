# Sylow Maximal Sigma Nilpotent Supplement holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 19.88 "Yes, it is true", citing Liu, Guo, Safonova and
Skiba (and a second proof using CFSG by Kamornikov and Tyutyanov). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 19.88 (A. N. Skiba); resolution:
A.-M. Liu, W. Guo, I. N. Safonova and A. N. Skiba, G-covering subgroup systems for some classes of
σ-soluble groups, J. Algebra 585 (2021), 280-293; another solution using CFSG by S. F. Kamornikov
and V. N. Tyutyanov, Trudy Inst. Mat. Mekh. UrO RAN 27 (2021), no. 1, 98-102, abstract: a set of
subgroups of G containing a supplement to each maximal subgroup of every Sylow subgroup of G is a
G-covering subgroup system for the classes of σ-soluble and of σ-nilpotent groups, which answers
19.87 and 19.88; Kourovka Archive, Problem 19.88: Yes, it is true,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2021.06.010.
