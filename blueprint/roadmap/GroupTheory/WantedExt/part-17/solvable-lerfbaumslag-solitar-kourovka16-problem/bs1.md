# The Baumslag–Solitar group BS(1, n)

**The Baumslag–Solitar group BS(1, n).** For a natural number $n$, $\mathrm{BS}(1, n)$ is the
group with presentation $\langle a, b \mid b a b^{-1} = a^n \rangle$.
