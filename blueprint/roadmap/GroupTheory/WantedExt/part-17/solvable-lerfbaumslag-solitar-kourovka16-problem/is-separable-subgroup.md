# Separable subgroup

**Separable subgroup.** A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is separable when for every element
$x \in G$ with $x \notin H$ there are a finite group $F$ and a group homomorphism
$f \colon G \to F$ with $f(x) \notin f(H)$.
