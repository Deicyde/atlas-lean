# Stable Series Shared

A set `S` of linear automorphisms of `V` is stable: there is a finite chain of subspaces
`0 = W₀ ≤ W₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ W_m = V` with `g w - w ∈ W_i` for all `g ∈ S` and `w ∈ W_{i+1}`.
