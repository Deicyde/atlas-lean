# Delta

`δ_φ(x) = x⁻¹ φ(x)`, the commutator `[x, φ]` (Kourovka convention `[x, y] = x⁻¹ y⁻¹ x y`, with
`φ` acting as `x ↦ φ x`). Its iterates are `[x, ₙ φ]`.
