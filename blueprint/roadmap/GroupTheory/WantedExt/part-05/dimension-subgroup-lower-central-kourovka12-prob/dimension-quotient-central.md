# Dimension Quotient Central

Kourovka 12.22(a) (N. D. Gupta, Yu. V. Kuz'min): for every group `G` and `n ≥ 1`,
`D_n(G) / γ_n(G)` is central in `G / γ_n(G)`; here `(⊤ : Subgroup G).lowerCentralSeries (n - 1)`
is `γ_n(G)`.
