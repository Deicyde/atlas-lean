# Abelian Subgroup Generators Le Twice Normal

Kourovka 20.75 (L. Pyber): for every prime `p` and every `k`, if all abelian normal
subgroups of a finite `p`-group `G` can be generated by `k` elements, then every abelian subgroup
of `G` can be generated by `2k` elements.
