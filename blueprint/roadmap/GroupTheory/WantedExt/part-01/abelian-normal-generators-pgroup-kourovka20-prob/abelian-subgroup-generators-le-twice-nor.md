# Pyber's question on generators of abelian subgroups (Kourovka 20.75)

**Pyber's question on generators of abelian subgroups (Kourovka 20.75).** The proposition that
for every prime $p$, every natural number $k$ and every finite $p$-group $G$, if every abelian
normal subgroup of $G$ can be generated by at most $k$ elements, then every abelian subgroup of $G$
can be generated by at most $2k$ elements.
