# Kourovka Problem 18.89 for $n \ge 3$

**Kourovka Problem 18.89 for $n \ge 3$.** The proposition that for every $n \ge 3$ the
Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$ (the subgroup of the permutation group of the set of $n$-tuples of
elements of the free group $F_n$ generated by the elementary Andrews–Curtis moves) is finitely
presented.
