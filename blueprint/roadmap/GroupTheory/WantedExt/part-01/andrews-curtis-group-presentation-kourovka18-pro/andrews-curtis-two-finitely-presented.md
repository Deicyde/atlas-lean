# Kourovka Problem 18.89 for $n = 2$

**Kourovka Problem 18.89 for $n = 2$.** The proposition, asked by J. Swan and A. Lisitsa,
that the Andrews–Curtis group $AC_2$ (the subgroup of the permutation group of the set of pairs of
elements of the free group $F_2$ generated by the elementary Andrews–Curtis moves) is finitely
presented.
