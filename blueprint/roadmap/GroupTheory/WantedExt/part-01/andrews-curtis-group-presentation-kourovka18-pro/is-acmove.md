# Is ACMove

`f` is an elementary Andrews–Curtis move on `n`-tuples `(u₁, …, uₙ)` of elements of the free
group `Fₙ`: `uᵢ ↦ uᵢ uⱼ^{±1}` or `uᵢ ↦ uⱼ^{±1} uᵢ` (`i ≠ j`), `uᵢ ↦ uᵢ⁻¹`, or `uᵢ ↦ w uᵢ w⁻¹`
(`w ∈ Fₙ`), all other entries unchanged.
