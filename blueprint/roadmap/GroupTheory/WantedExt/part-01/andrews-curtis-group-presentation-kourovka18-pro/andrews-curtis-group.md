# Andrews Curtis Group

The group `ACₙ` of Andrews–Curtis moves on balanced presentations `⟨x₁, …, xₙ | u₁, …, uₙ⟩`:
the subgroup of `Sym(Fₙⁿ)` generated by the elementary moves (Nielsen transformations of the relator
tuple together with conjugations of relators).
