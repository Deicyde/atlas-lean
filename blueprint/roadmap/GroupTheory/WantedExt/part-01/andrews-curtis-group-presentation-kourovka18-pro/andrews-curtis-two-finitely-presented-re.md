# Andrews Curtis Two Finitely Presented refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) records that `AC₂` is not finitely presented
(Krstić-McCool, 1997; Roman'kov, 2023, who also shows that the group of moves on all relator tuples
is isomorphic to the Andrews-Curtis group `AC(F₂) ≅ A₂,₂`). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov
(eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49
(2026), Problem 18.89 (J. Swan, A. Lisitsa); resolution: S. Krstić and J. McCool, J. London Math.
Soc. (2) 56 (1997), no. 2, 264-274; V. A. Roman'kov, On the Andrews-Curtis groups: non-finite
presentability, preprint, arXiv:2305.11838 (2023); G. Kiralis, S. Krstić and J. McCool, Proc. London
Math. Soc. (3) 73 (1996), no. 3, 575-622; M. Ershov, letter of 16 October 2025, AC_2 is not finitely
presented (Krstić-McCool, Theorem 1; Roman'kov, Theorem B: AC(F_2) ≅ A_{2,2} is not finitely
presented); AC_n, n ≥ 3, is finitely presented by Kiralis-Krstić-McCool, Theorem 3.1, since AC_n ≅
A_{n,n} ≅ Φ_n(F_n) (Ershov's letter); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 18.89 solved,
https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/kourovka1889.pdf.
