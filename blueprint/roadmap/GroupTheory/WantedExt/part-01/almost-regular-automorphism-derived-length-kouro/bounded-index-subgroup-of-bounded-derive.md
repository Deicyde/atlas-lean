# Bounded Index Subgroup Of Bounded Derived Length

Kourovka 14.96 (E. I. Khukhro): there are functions `f(p, n, m)` and `g(m)` such that every
finite `p`-group `P` with an automorphism of order `pⁿ` having exactly `pᵐ` fixed points has a
subgroup of index at most `f(p, n, m)` that is soluble of derived length at most `g(m)`.
