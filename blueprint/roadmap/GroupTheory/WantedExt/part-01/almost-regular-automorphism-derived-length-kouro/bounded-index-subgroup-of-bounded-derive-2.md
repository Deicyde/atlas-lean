# Bounded Index Subgroup Of Bounded Derived Length holds

Resolved true: Jaikin-Zapirain proved that a finite `p`-group with an automorphism of order `pⁿ`
having exactly `pᵐ` fixed points has a subgroup of `(p, n, m)`-bounded index and `m`-bounded derived
length; the Kourovka Archive answers 14.96 "Yes, it is true". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 14.96 (E. I. Khukhro); resolution: A.
Jaikin-Zapirain, On almost regular automorphisms of finite p-groups, Adv. Math. 153 (2000), no. 2,
391-402, main theorem (a subgroup of (p, n, m)-bounded index and m-bounded derived length); Kourovka
Archive, Problem 14.96 (answer: Yes, it is true).
