# Growth rate of a $k$-element generating family (Kourovka 14.7)

**Growth rate of a $k$-element generating family (Kourovka 14.7).** Let $\Gamma$ be a group
and $S = (s_1, \dots, s_k)$ a family of $k$ elements generating $\Gamma$. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ let
$\beta_S(n)$ be the number of elements of $\Gamma$ that are products of at most $n$ elements of
$S \cup S^{-1}$. The growth rate of $\Gamma$ with respect to $S$ is the real number
$\omega(\Gamma, S) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \beta_S(n)^{1/n}$.
