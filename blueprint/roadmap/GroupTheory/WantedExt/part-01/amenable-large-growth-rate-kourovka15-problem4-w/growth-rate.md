# Growth Rate

The growth rate `ω(Γ, S) = limsup β_S(n)^(1/n)` of a finite generating family `S` (Kourovka
14.7), where `β_S(n)` is the number of elements that are products of at most `n` elements of
`S ∪ S⁻¹`.
