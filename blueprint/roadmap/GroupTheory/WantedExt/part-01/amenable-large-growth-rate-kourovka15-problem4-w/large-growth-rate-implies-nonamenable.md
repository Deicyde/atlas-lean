# Kourovka Problem 15.4 (large growth rate implies non-amenability)

**Kourovka Problem 15.4 (large growth rate implies non-amenability).** The proposition, asked
by P. de la Harpe, that there are $k \ge 2$ and a real $\varepsilon > 0$ such that every group
$\Gamma$ generated by a family $S$ of $k$ distinct elements whose growth rate satisfies
$\omega(\Gamma, S) \ge 2k - 1 - \varepsilon$ is non-amenable (amenability being the Følner
condition).
