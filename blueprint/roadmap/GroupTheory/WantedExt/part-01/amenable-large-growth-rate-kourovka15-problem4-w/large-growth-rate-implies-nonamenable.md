# Large Growth Rate Implies Nonamenable

Kourovka 15.4 (P. de la Harpe), for some `k ≥ 2`: there is `ε > 0` such that every group `Γ`
generated by a set `S` of `k` elements with `ω(Γ, S) ≥ 2k - 1 - ε` is non-amenable.
