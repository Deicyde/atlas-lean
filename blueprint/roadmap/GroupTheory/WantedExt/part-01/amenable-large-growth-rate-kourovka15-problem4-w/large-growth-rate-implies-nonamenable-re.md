# Large Growth Rate Implies Nonamenable refuted

Resolved false: Arzhantseva, Guba and Guyot showed that for every `m ≥ 2` the growth rate of
`F_m/R'` tends to `2m - 1` as the shortest relation in `R` gets longer, so `m`-generated amenable
groups (abelian-by-nilpotent ones, for instance) have growth rates arbitrarily close to `2m - 1`;
the Kourovka Archive answers 15.4 "No, it does not". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 15.4 (P. de la Harpe); resolution: G. N. Arzhantseva, V. S. Guba
and L. Guyot, Growth rates of amenable groups, J. Group Theory 8 (2005), 389-394,
arXiv:math/0406013, abstract and main result (the growth rate of an m-generated amenable group,
already in the abelian-by-nilpotent and metabelian-by-finite classes, can be arbitrarily close to 2m
- 1); Kourovka Archive, Problem 15.4 (answer: No, it does not), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406013.
