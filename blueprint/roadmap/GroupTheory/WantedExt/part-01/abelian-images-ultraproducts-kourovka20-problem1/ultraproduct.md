# Ultraproduct of a sequence of groups

**Ultraproduct of a sequence of groups.** For an ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ and a sequence
$(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of groups, the ultraproduct $\left(\prod_i G_i\right)/U$ is the quotient
of the direct product $\prod_i G_i$ by the ultraproduct kernel, the normal subgroup of sequences
$(g_i)_i$ with $\{i : g_i = 1\} \in U$.
