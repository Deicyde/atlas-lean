# Almost Simple Gen Num Eq

Kourovka 11.27 (K. Gruenberg), the formula of the answer: if `S` is a finite non-abelian
simple group and `S ≤ G ≤ Aut S` (with `S` identified with the inner automorphism group
`Inn S`), then `d(G) = max (2, d(G / S))`.
