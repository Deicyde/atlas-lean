# Almost Simple Gen Num Le Three holds

Resolved true (modulo the classification of finite simple groups, as accepted by the Kourovka
Archive): Dalla Volta and Lucchini prove that every `G` with `S ≤ G ≤ Aut S`, `S` finite simple
non-abelian, satisfies `d(G) ≤ 3`. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems
in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved
Problems, Problem 11.27 (K. Gruenberg); resolution: F. Dalla Volta and A. Lucchini, J. Algebra 178,
no. 1 (1995), 194-223, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.27: "The number `d(G)` is found (mod CFSG) for
every such a group `G`. In particular, `d(G) = max {2, d(G/S)}` and `d(G) ≤ 3`",
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
