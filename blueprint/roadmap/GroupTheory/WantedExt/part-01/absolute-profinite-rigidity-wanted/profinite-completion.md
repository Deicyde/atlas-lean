# Profinite completion

**Profinite completion.** The profinite completion of a group $G$ is the inverse limit of the
finite quotient groups $G/N$, where $N$ ranges over the normal subgroups of finite index in $G$
and the maps are the quotient maps $G/N \to G/M$ for $N \le M$. It is a profinite group.
