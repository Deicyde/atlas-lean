# Polynomial Folner Exhaustion Implies Polynomial Growth refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 14.27 "No, not always": by Bardakov, every finitely
generated group of intermediate growth (such groups exist by Grigorchuk) has a sequence with
properties (i)-(iv), but no polynomial growth. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.),
Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026),
Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 14.27 (A. G. Vaillant); resolution: V. G. Bardakov, Algebra and
Logic 40 (2001), no. 1, 12-16, Kourovka Archive, Problem 14.27: every group of intermediate growth
has such a sequence (answer: No, not always).
