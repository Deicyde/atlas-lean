# Kourovka Problem 16.31 for finite groups

**Kourovka Problem 16.31 for finite groups.** The statement (V. A. Vedernikov) that for every
finite group $G$ only finitely many formations of finite groups are contained in the formation
generated by $G$. Here formations may be empty, and a formation $\mathfrak{F}$ is contained in the
formation generated by $G$ when every finite group in $\mathfrak{F}$ belongs to the formation
generated by $G$.
