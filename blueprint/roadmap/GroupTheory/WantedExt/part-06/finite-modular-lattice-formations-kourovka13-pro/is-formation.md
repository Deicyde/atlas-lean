# Is Formation

`𝔉` is a formation of finite groups: it is closed under homomorphic images, and
`G / N₁, G / N₂ ∈ 𝔉` implies `G / (N₁ ∩ N₂) ∈ 𝔉` (closure under finite subdirect products). The
empty class is a formation.
