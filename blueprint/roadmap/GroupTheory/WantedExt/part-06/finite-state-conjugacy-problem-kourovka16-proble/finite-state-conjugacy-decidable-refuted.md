# Finite State Conjugacy Decidable refuted

Resolved false: Šunić and Ventura (Theorem 1.1) construct automaton groups, finitely generated
groups generated by finite self-similar sets of finite-state automorphisms of a regular rooted tree,
with unsolvable conjugacy problem; the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers 16.34 "No, not
always". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The
Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 16.34 (R. I. Grigorchuk, V. V.
Nekrashevich, V. I. Sushchanskiĭ); resolution: Z. Šunić and E. Ventura, The conjugacy problem in
automaton groups is not solvable, J. Algebra 364 (2012), 148-154, arXiv:1010.1993, Theorem 1.1
(there exist automaton groups with unsolvable conjugacy problem; the examples are (free
abelian)-by-free groups generated by finite self-similar sets of tree automorphisms); the Kourovka
Notebook (21st issue) marks 16.34 solved (No, not always), https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1993.
