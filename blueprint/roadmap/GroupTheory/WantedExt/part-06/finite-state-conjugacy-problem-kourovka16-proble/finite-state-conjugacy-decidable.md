# Finite State Conjugacy Decidable

Kourovka 16.34 (R. I. Grigorchuk, V. V. Nekrashevich, V. I. Sushchanskiĭ): every finitely
generated group of finite-state automorphisms of a regular rooted tree has decidable conjugacy
problem (with respect to its finite generating set).
