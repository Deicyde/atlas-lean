# Finite PGroup Quasivarieties Sublattice refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 10.9 "No, not always", citing Shakhova (1993): for some
prime `p`, `L_p` is not a sublattice. Since joins stay in `L_p` (the join is generated by `G × H`),
the intersection of two quasivarieties generated by finite `p`-groups is then not generated by any
finite `p`-group. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory:
The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem
10.9 (A. I. Budkin); resolution: S. A. Shakhova, Math. Notes 53, no. 3 (1993), 345-347, Kourovka
Archive, Problem 10.9 ("No, not always").
