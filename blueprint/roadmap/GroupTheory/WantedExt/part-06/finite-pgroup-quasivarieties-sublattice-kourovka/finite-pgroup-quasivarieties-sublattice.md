# Finite PGroup Quasivarieties Sublattice

Kourovka 10.9 (A. I. Budkin): for every prime `p`, the set `L_p` of quasivarieties generated by
a single finite `p`-group is a sublattice of the lattice of all quasivarieties of groups: the meet
(intersection) and the join of two members of `L_p` are again generated by a finite `p`-group.
