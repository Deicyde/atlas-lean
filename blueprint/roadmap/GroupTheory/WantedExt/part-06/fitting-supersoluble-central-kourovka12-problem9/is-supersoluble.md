# Is Supersoluble

`G` is supersoluble: it has a series `⊥ = s 0 ≤ ⋯ ≤ s k = ⊤` of normal subgroups of `G` with
cyclic factors (`s (i + 1) = ⟨x⟩ ⊔ s i`).
