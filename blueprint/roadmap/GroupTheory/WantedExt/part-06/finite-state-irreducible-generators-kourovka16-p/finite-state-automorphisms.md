# Finite State Automorphisms

The group of finite-state automorphisms of the `d`-regular rooted tree: the subgroup of
permutations of the words generated by the tree automorphisms with finitely many distinct
sections. These automorphisms are closed under products and inverses, so the subgroup consists of
exactly them.
