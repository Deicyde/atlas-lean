# Finite State Automorphisms Irreducible Generators holds

Resolved true: Lavrenyuk (Theorem 3.8) proves that for a regular rooted tree over any alphabet of at
least two letters the group of all finite-state automorphisms has a minimal (irreducible) generating
set; the Kourovka Archive answers 16.86 "Yes, it does". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov
(eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49
(2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 16.86 (V. I. Sushchanskiĭ); resolution: Ya. Lavrenyuk,
The group of all finite-state automorphisms of a regular rooted tree has a minimal generating set,
Geom. Dedicata 183 (2016), 59-67 (preprint: Minimal generating sets in wreath products,
arXiv:1210.0150), arXiv:1210.0150, Theorem 3.8 (for a regular rooted tree over any alphabet of at
least two letters, the group of finite-state automorphisms has a minimal generating set); Kourovka
Archive, Problem 16.86 (Yes, it does), https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.0150.
