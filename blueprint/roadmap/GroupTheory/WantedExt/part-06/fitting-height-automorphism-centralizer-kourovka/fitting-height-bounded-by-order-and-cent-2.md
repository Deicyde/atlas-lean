# Fitting Height Bounded By Order And Centralizer holds

Resolved true: Khukhro proved that the Fitting height of a finite soluble group with an automorphism
`φ` is bounded in terms of `|φ|` and `|C_G(φ)|`; the 21st issue of the Kourovka Notebook marks 19.43
solved ("Yes, it is"). Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 19.43 (E. I. Khukhro);
