# Group matrix

**Group matrix.** For a finite group $G$, the group matrix of Dedekind and Frobenius is the
$G \times G$ matrix over the polynomial ring $\mathbb{C}[x_g : g \in G]$ whose entry at $(g, h)$ is
the variable $x_{gh}$.
