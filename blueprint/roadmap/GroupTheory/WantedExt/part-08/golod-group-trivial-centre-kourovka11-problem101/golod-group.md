# Golod Group

The Golod group of `y : Fin r → N` (Kourovka 9.76): the subgroup of the units of the
unitization `k ⊕ N` generated by the units with value `1 + yᵢ`, that is, the subgroup
`⟨1 + y₁, …, 1 + y_r⟩` of the adjoint group `1 + N`.
