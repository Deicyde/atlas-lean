# Fewer Generator Subgroups Free Generic holds

Resolved true: Arzhantseva and Olshanskii prove that for presentations with `m ≥ 2` generators and
`n` cyclically reduced relators of total length `d`, the proportion whose group has all `(m -
1)`-generated subgroups free tends to `1` as `d → ∞`; the Kourovka Archive records 11.75 as proved
by this paper. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory:
The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem
11.75 (A. Yu. Olshanskii); resolution: G. N. Arzhantseva and A. Yu. Olshanskii, The class of groups
all of whose subgroups with lesser number of generators are free is generic, Math. Notes 59 (1996),
no. 4, 350-355 (Mat. Zametki 59 (1996), 489-496), Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.75: This is proved
(Arzhantseva-Olshanskii); paper, abstract ("this solves Problem 11.75 from the Kourovka Notebook")
and main theorem as summarized in zbMATH Zbl 0877.20021 (lim N_d^f / N_d = 1 over presentations with
cyclically reduced relators of total length d), https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02308683.
