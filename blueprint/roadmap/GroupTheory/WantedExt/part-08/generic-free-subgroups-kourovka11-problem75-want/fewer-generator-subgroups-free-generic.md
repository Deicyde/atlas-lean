# Fewer Generator Subgroups Free Generic

Kourovka 11.75 (A. Yu. Olshanskii), in the form proved by Arzhantseva and Olshanskii: for
`m ≥ 2` generators and `n ≥ 1` relators, the proportion of presentations of total relator length
`d` whose group has all subgroups generated by fewer than `m` elements free tends to `1` as
`d → ∞`.
