# Free Product Aut Free Group Embeds

Kourovka 18.11 (V. G. Bardakov): for any subgroups `G, H` of `Aut(Fₙ)`, `n ≥ 2`, the free
product `G ∗ H` embeds in `Aut(Fₘ)` for some `m`.
