# Ribes' question on generator counts (Kourovka 12.71)

**Ribes' question on generator counts (Kourovka 12.71).** Write $d(X)$ for the minimal number
of generators of a finite group $X$. This is the proposition that for all finite groups $A$ and
$B$ there are a finite group $G$ and subgroups $H, K \le G$ with $H \cong A$, $K \cong B$ and
$\langle H, K \rangle = G$ such that $d(G) = d(A) + d(B)$.
