# Rank Additive Generation refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 12.71 "No, not always" (A. Lucchini, 2001): for some
finite groups `A` and `B`, every finite group generated by subgroups isomorphic to `A` and `B` needs
fewer than `d(A) + d(B)` generators (Kovács and Sim had shown this within soluble groups). Source:
E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook,
21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 12.71 (L. Ribes);
resolution: A. Lucchini, J. Group Theory 4 (2001), no. 1, 53-58, Kourovka Archive, Problem 12.71:
No, not always.
