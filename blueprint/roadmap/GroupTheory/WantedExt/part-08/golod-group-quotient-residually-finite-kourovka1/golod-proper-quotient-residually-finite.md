# Golod Proper Quotient Residually Finite

Kourovka 12.102 (V. P. Shunkov): every proper factor group `Γ / K` (`K ≠ 1` normal) of a Golod
group `Γ` (Kourovka 9.76) is residually finite, i.e. every element outside `K` lies outside some
normal subgroup of finite index containing `K`. Here `Γ` is generated by `1 + y₁, …, 1 + y_r`
(`r ≥ 2`) in a non-unital algebra `N = F / I` over a field of characteristic `p > 0` generated by
`y₁, …, y_r`, nil (every element nilpotent) and not nilpotent (nonzero products of every length).
