# Golod Proper Quotient Residually Finite refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 12.102 "No, not every" (Hammoudi): some Golod group has
a quotient by a nontrivial normal subgroup that is not residually finite. Source: E. I. Khukhro and
V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 12.102 (V. P. Shunkov); resolution:
L. Hammoudi, Algebra Colloq. 5 (1998), 371-376, Kourovka Archive, Problem 12.102: "No, not every",
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
