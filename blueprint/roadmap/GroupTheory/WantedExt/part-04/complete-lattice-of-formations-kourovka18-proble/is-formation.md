# Is Formation

A formation of finite groups: its members are finite, it contains the trivial group, it is
closed under quotients, and `G ⧸ N₁, G ⧸ N₂ ∈ X` implies `G ⧸ (N₁ ⊓ N₂) ∈ X`.
