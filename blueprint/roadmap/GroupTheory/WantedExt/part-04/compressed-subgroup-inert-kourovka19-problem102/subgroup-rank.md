# Rank of a subgroup

**Rank of a subgroup.** For a subgroup $H$ of a group $G$, the rank
$r(H) \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$ is the least size of a finite subset of $G$ that generates
$H$, with $r(H) = \infty$ when $H$ is not finitely generated.
