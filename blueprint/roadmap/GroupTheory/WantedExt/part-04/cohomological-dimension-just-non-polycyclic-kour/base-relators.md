# Base Relators

The relators of `K = F / F''(F')²`, `F = ⟨x, y⟩` (the values of `[[a, b], [c, d]]` and
`[a, b]²`, which generate `F''(F')²` as a normal subgroup), written over `⟨x, y, t⟩` with
`x = of 0`, `y = of 1`.
