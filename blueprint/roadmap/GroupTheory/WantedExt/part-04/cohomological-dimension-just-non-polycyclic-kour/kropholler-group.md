# Kropholler Group

`G = (F / F''(F')²) ⋊ ⟨φ⟩` with `φ : x ↦ y, y ↦ x y`, presented as the mapping torus
`⟨x, y, t | relators of K, t x t⁻¹ = φ(x), t y t⁻¹ = φ(y)⟩` (`φ` has infinite order on `K`).
