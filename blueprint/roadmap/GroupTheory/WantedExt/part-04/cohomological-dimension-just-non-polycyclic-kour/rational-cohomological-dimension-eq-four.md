# Rational Cohomological Dimension Eq Four

Kourovka 12.41 (P. H. Kropholler), answer: the cohomological dimension of `G` over `ℚ` is
`4`: `H⁴(G, M) ≠ 0` for some `ℚG`-module `M`, and `Hⁿ(G, M) = 0` for all `n > 4` and all `M`.
