# Three Hall Subgroups Intersect In Core refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 18.31 "No, it is not true" (V. I. Zenkov, 2022), for
`G` an extension of `L₂(27)` by a field automorphism of order `3`, in which `H` is an extension of a
Borel subgroup of `L₂(27)` by a field automorphism of order `3`. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D.
Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue,
arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 18.31 (E. P. Vdovin, D. O. Revin);
resolution: V. I. Zenkov, Siberian Math. J. 63 (2022), no. 4, 720-722, Kourovka Archive, Problem
18.31: No; G an extension of L_2(27) by a field automorphism of order 3, H an extension of a Borel
subgroup of L_2(27) by a field automorphism of order 3, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
