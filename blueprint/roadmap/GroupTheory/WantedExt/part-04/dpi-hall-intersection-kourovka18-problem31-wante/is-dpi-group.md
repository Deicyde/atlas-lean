# Dπ-groups

**Dπ-groups.** Let $\pi$ be a set of natural numbers. A group $G$ is a
$D_\pi$-group when any two maximal $\pi$-subgroups $H$ and $K$ of $G$ are conjugate, that is,
$K = gHg^{-1}$ for some $g \in G$.
