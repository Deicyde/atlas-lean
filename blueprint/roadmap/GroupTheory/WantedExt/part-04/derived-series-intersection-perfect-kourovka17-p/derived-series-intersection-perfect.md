# Derived Series Intersection Perfect

Kourovka 17.82 (R. Mikhailov): in every finitely presented group `G` the intersection
`D = ⋂ₙ G⁽ⁿ⁾` of the derived series has trivial abelianization, `[D, D] = D`.
