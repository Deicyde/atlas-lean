# Coprime Generated Rank Bound refuted

Resolved false: Muliarchyk (Theorem 1) constructs, for every `d ≥ 2`, a finite group `G = ⟨A, B⟩`
with `|A|` and `|B|` coprime, `d(A) = d(B) = d` and `d(G) = d + 2` (`A` and `B` metabelian of
exponents 6 and 55, inside a finite power of `PSL₂(11)`); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers
16.53 "No, not always". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 16.53 (A. Lucchini);
resolution: K. Muliarchyk, Coprime generation through coset counts: a counterexample to Kourovka
Notebook Problem 16.53, preprint (2026), Theorem 1 (for every d ≥ 2 finite groups A_d, B_d ≤ G_d =
⟨A_d, B_d⟩ with coprime orders, d(A_d) = d(B_d) = d and d(G_d) = d + 2); the Kourovka Notebook (21st
issue) marks 16.53 solved (No, not always),
https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/kourovka_16_53_solution.pdf.
