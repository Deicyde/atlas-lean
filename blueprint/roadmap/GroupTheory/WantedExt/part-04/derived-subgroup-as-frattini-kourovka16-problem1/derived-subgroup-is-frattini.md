# Derived Subgroup Is Frattini

Kourovka 16.11 (Ya. G. Berkovich): for every prime `p` and every finite `p`-group `G` there is a
finite `p`-group `H` whose Frattini subgroup `Φ(H)` is isomorphic to the derived subgroup `[G, G]`.
