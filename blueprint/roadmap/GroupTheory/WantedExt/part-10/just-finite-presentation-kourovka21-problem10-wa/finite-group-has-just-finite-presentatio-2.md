# Finite Group Has Just Finite Presentation holds

Resolved true: Lackenby (Theorem 1) proves that every finite group admits a finite presentation from
which the removal of any relation gives an infinite group; the 21st issue of the Kourovka Notebook
marks 21.10 solved ("Yes, it is true"). The paper acknowledges assistance from the Google DeepMind
AI co-mathematician tool. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.10 (Y. Barnea);
resolution: M. Lackenby, Every finite group admits a just finite presentation, preprint,
arXiv:2605.10402 (2026), Theorem 1: every finite group admits a finite, just finite presentation (a
consequence of Theorem 2 on just-(FA) presentations); Kourovka Notebook (21st issue), Problem 21.10:
Yes, it is true, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10402.
