# Involution Engel Two Groups Bounded Derived Length refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 18.57 "No, it is not" (A. Abdollahi, 2015): such
2-groups have unbounded derived length. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved
Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of
Solved Problems, Problem 18.57 (D. V. Lytkina); resolution: A. Abdollahi, J. Group Theory 18 (2015),
no. 1, 111-114, Kourovka Archive, Problem 18.57: No, it is not, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
