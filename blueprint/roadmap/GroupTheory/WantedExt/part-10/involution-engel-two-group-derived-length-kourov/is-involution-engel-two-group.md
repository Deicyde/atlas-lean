# Is Involution Engel Two Group

`G` is a 2-group generated by involutions in which `[x, u, u] = 1` for every `x ∈ G` and every
involution `u`, where `[x, u] = x⁻¹ u⁻¹ x u` and `[x, u, u] = [[x, u], u]`.
