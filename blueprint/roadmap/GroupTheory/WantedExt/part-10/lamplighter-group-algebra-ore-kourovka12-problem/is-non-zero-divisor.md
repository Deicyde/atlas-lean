# Non-zero-divisor

**Non-zero-divisor.** An element $s$ of a ring $R$ is a non-zero-divisor when it is neither a
left nor a right zero divisor: for every $z \in R$, $zs = 0$ implies $z = 0$ and $sz = 0$ implies
$z = 0$.
