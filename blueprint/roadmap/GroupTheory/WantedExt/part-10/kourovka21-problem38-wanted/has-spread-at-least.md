# Spread at least $k$

**Spread at least $k$.** A group $G$ has spread at least $k$, for a natural number $k$, when for
every family $x_1, \dots, x_k$ of nontrivial elements of $G$, not necessarily distinct, there is an
element $y \in G$ such that the subgroup $\langle x_i, y \rangle$ generated by $x_i$ and $y$ is
all of $G$ for every $i$.
