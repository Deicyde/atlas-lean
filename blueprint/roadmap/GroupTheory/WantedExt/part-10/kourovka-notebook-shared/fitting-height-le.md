# Fitting Height LE

`G` has Fitting height (nilpotency length) at most `h`: there are normal subgroups
`1 = G₀ ≤ G₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ G_h = G` of `G` whose factors `G_{i+1} / G_i` are nilpotent (some term of the
lower central series of `G_{i+1}`, computed in `G`, lies in `G_i`).
