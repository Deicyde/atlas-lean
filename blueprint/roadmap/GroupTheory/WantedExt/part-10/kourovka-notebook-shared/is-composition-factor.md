# Is Composition Factor

`S` is a composition factor of the finite group `K`: `S` is simple and some subnormal subgroup
of `K` maps onto `S`. For finite `K` this is the same as `S` occurring as a factor of a composition
series of `K` (refine the subnormal chain through the kernel).
