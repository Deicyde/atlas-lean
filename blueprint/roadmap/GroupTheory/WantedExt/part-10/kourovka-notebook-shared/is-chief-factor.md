# Chief factors

**Chief factors.** For subgroups $A$ and $B$ of a group $G$, $A / B$ is a chief factor of $G$
if $A$ and $B$ are normal in $G$, $B < A$, and every normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ with
$B \le N \le A$ equals $B$ or $A$.
