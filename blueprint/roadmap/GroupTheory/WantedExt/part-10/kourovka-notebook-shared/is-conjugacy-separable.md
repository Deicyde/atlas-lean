# Is Conjugacy Separable

`G` is conjugacy separable: elements whose images are conjugate in every finite quotient
`G / N` (`N` normal of finite index, written as `g x g⁻¹ y⁻¹ ∈ N` for some `g`) are conjugate in
`G`.
