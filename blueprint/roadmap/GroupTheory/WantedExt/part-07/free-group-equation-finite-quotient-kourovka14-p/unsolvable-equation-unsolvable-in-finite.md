# Unsolvable Equation Unsolvable In Finite Quotient

Kourovka 14.48 (L. Comerford): if an equation `w ∈ F ∗ F(x₀, …, xₙ₋₁)` over a free group `F`
has no solution in `F`, then it has no solution in some finite quotient `F / N`.
