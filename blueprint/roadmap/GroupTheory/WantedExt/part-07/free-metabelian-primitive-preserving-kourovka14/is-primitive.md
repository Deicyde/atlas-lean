# Is Primitive

`y ∈ M_n` is primitive: it belongs to a free basis of `M_n`, that is, `y = σ(xᵢ)` for an
automorphism `σ` of `M_n` and a basis element `xᵢ` (the image of a free generator of `F_n`).
