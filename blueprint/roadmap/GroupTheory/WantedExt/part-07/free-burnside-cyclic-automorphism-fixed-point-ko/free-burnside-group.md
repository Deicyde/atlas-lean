# Free Burnside group

**Free Burnside group.** For natural numbers $m$ and $n$, the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ is
the quotient of the free group $F_m$ on $m$ generators by the normal subgroup generated by all
$n$-th powers $w^n$ with $w \in F_m$.
