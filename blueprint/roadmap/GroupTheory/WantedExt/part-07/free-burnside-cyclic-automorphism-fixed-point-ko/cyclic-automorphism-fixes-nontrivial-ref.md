# Cyclic Automorphism Fixes Nontrivial refuted

Resolved false: Atabekyan (Theorem 1.1) proves that for every odd `m ≥ 3` (in particular every prime
`m ≥ 3`) there is `q_m` such that for every prime `q > q_m` the automorphism of the free Burnside
group `B(m, q)` cyclically permuting the `m` free generators has no nontrivial fixed points, and
these groups are infinite by Adian's theorem (`B(m, n)` is infinite for odd `n ≥ 665`, `m > 1`). In
the notation of the problem the answer is negative for every prime `q ≥ 3` and all large primes `p`;
the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers 17.70 "No, it does not for all `q ≥ 3` and all
sufficiently large `p`". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group
Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 17.70 (V. D. Mazurov);
resolution: V. S. Atabekyan, Mazurov's question on fixed points of automorphisms of free Burnside
groups, preprint, arXiv:2609.33941 (2026), Theorem 1.1 (for every odd m ≥ 3 there is q_m such that
for every prime q > q_m the automorphism of B(m, q) of order m cyclically permuting the free
generators has no nontrivial fixed points; B(m, n) is infinite for all odd n ≥ 665 and m > 1 by
Adian's theorem, as recalled there); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 17.70 solved (No, it
does not for all q ≥ 3 and all sufficiently large p), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33941.
