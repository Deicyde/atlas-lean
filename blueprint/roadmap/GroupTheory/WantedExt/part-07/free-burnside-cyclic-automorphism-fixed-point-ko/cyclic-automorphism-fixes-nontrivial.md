# Cyclic Automorphism Fixes Nontrivial

Kourovka 17.70 (V. D. Mazurov): for primes `q, p` with `B(q, p)` infinite, the automorphism of
`B(q, p)` induced by the cyclic permutation `xᵢ ↦ xᵢ₊₁ (mod q)` of the free generators fixes some
nontrivial element; the fixed points are written through representatives `w ∈ F_q`.
