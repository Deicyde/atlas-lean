# Is Free Pro POn

`F` is a free pro-`p` group on `x₀, …, x_{d-1}`: a pro-`p` group topologically generated by
the `xᵢ` such that every assignment of the `xᵢ` in a finite `p`-group `P` extends to a continuous
homomorphism `F → P` (one with open kernel).
