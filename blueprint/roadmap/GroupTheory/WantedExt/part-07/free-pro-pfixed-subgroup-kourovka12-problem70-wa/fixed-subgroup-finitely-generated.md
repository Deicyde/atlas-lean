# Fixed Subgroup Finitely Generated

Kourovka 12.70 (L. Ribes): if `F` is a free pro-`p` group of finite rank and `Δ ≠ 1` is a
(continuous) automorphism of `F` of `p`-power order, then `Fix_F(Δ)` has finite rank, i.e. it is
the closure of the subgroup generated by finitely many of its elements.
