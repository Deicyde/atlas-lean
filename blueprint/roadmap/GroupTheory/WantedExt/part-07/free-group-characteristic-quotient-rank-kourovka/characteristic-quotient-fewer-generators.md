# Characteristic Quotient Fewer Generators

Kourovka 17.62 (J. Conrad), for every rank where it can hold: for every `n ≥ 3` the free group
`Fₙ` has a proper characteristic subgroup `C` such that `Fₙ / C` is generated by two (fewer than
`n`) elements.
