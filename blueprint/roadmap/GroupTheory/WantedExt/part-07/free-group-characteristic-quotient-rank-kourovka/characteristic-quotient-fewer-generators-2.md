# Characteristic Quotient Fewer Generators holds

Resolved true: Coulon and Fournier-Facio (Theorem 1.1) prove that for all `n ≥ 2` the free group
`Fₙ` admits continuum many pairwise non-isomorphic 2-generated infinite simple characteristic
quotients; for `n ≥ 3` such a quotient `Fₙ/C` is generated by fewer than `n` elements, and `C` is
proper since the quotient is infinite. The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers 17.62 "Yes, it is
possible". Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The
Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 17.62 (J. Conrad); resolution: R.
Coulon and F. Fournier-Facio, Infinite simple characteristic quotients, preprint, arXiv:2312.11684,
Theorem 1.1 (for all n ≥ 2, the free group of rank n admits continuum many pairwise non-isomorphic,
2-generated, infinite, simple, characteristic quotients; Section 1 notes that this answers Problem
17.62); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 17.62 solved (Yes, it is possible; moreover, for
all n ≥ 2, F_n admits continuum many pairwise non-isomorphic infinite simple characteristic
2-generated quotients), https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11684.
