# Derived Normal Implies Normal

Kourovka 11.124 (V. E. Shpilrain): in a non-cyclic free group `F` (of any rank), every
non-cyclic subgroup `R` whose derived subgroup `[R, R]` is normal in `F` is itself normal in `F`.
