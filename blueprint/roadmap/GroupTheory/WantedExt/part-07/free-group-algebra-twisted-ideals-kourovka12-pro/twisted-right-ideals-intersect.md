# Twisted Right Ideals Intersect

Kourovka 12.46 (P. A. Linnell): for every nonzero `α ∈ ℂF` there are `a, b ∈ ℂ ∖ {1}` with
`|a| = |b| = 1` such that the right ideals `α ℂF` and `ϑ_{a,b}(α) ℂF` intersect nontrivially.
