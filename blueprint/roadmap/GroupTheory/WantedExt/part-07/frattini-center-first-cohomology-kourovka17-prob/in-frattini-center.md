# Centre of the Frattini subgroup

**Centre of the Frattini subgroup.** An element $x$ of a group $G$ lies in the centre
$Z(\Phi(G))$ of the Frattini subgroup $\Phi(G)$ when $x \in \Phi(G)$ and $xy = yx$ for every
$y \in \Phi(G)$.
