# Free Action Embeds In Skew Field

Kourovka 16.54 (V. D. Mazurov): a group `G` that acts freely by automorphisms on a nontrivial
abelian group `V` (`g • v ≠ v` for all `g ≠ 1` and `v ≠ 0`) embeds in the multiplicative group of
a division ring.
