# Free Action Embeds In Skew Field refuted

Resolved false: the Kourovka Archive answers 16.54 "No, it is not" (Nedrenko): the group `2.A₅.2`
with a quaternion Sylow 2-subgroup acts freely on an elementary abelian group of order `7⁴`, but by
Theorem 7 of Amitsur's classification of finite subgroups of division rings it does not embed in the
multiplicative group of any skew-field. Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved
Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of
Solved Problems, Problem 16.54 (V. D. Mazurov); resolution: D. Nedrenko, letter of 20 January 2014,
as recorded in the Kourovka Archive; S. A. Amitsur, Finite subgroups of division rings, Trans. Amer.
Math. Soc. 80 (1955), 361-386, Kourovka Archive, Problem 16.54 (No: 2.A_5.2 with a quaternion Sylow
2-subgroup acts freely on an elementary abelian group of order 7^4 but does not embed in the
multiplicative group of a skew-field, by Amitsur's Theorem 7).
