# Kourovka Problem 17.3 (Abdollahi's question on nilpotent pairs)

**Kourovka Problem 17.3 (Abdollahi's question on nilpotent pairs).** The proposition that for
every group $G$ in which every $4$-element subset contains two distinct elements generating a
nilpotent subgroup, every subgroup of $G$ generated by two elements is nilpotent.
