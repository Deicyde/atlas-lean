# Free Group Cayley Hamilton

Kourovka 17.32 (O. V. Bogopolski), an analogue of the Cayley-Hamilton theorem for the free group
`Fₙ`: if `w ∈ Fₙ` and `φ ∈ Aut(Fₙ)` satisfy `⟨w, φ(w), …, φⁿ(w)⟩ = Fₙ`, then
`⟨w, φ(w), …, φⁿ⁻¹(w)⟩ = Fₙ`.
