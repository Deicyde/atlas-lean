# Free central extensions need not be residually nilpotent

**Free central extensions need not be residually nilpotent.** There exist a free group $F$ and a
normal subgroup $R$ of $F$ such that $F/R$ is finitely presented and residually torsion-free
nilpotent, while $F/[F, R]$ is not residually nilpotent; here $[F, R]$ is the subgroup of $F$
generated by all commutators $[f, r]$ with $f \in F$ and $r \in R$.
