# Residually nilpotent group

**Residually nilpotent group.** A group $G$ is residually nilpotent if for every element
$g \ne 1$ of $G$ there is a normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ with $g \notin N$ such that the quotient
$G/N$ is nilpotent.
