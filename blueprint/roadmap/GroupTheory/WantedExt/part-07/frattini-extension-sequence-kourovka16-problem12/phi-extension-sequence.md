# Phi Extension Sequence

Kourovka 16.12 (Ya. G. Berkovich): for every prime `p` and every nontrivial finite `p`-group
`G` there is an infinite sequence `G = G₀, G₁, G₂, …` of groups in which every `G_{i+1}` is a
`Φ`-extension of `G_i`.
