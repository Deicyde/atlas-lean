# Schmidt subgroup

**Schmidt subgroup.** A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is a Schmidt subgroup when $H$ is not
nilpotent but every proper subgroup of $H$ is nilpotent.

Finiteness of $H$ is not part of this definition.
