# Pure Braid Unique Roots

Kourovka 11.55 (G. S. Makanin): extraction of roots in the group of coloured braids is unique:
if `α ^ k = β ^ k` for coloured braids `α`, `β` and some `k ≥ 1`, then `α = β`.
