# Finitely Generated Group Infinitely Many Maximal Subquasivarieties holds

Resolved true: the Kourovka Archive answers 14.25 "Yes, it is", citing Bludov: some finitely
generated group `G` has infinitely many maximal proper subquasivarieties in `qG`. Source: E. I.
Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st
issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 14.25 (A. I. Budkin);
resolution: V. V. Bludov, Algebra and Logic 41 (2002), no. 1, 1-8, Kourovka Archive, Problem 14.25
(answer: Yes, it is).
