# Finitely Generated Group Infinitely Many Maximal Subquasivarieties

Kourovka 14.25 (A. I. Budkin): some finitely generated group `G` has infinitely many maximal
proper subquasivarieties in `qG`: for every `k` there are `k` pairwise distinct ones.
