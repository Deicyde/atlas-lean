# Budkin's problem on maximal subquasivarieties (Kourovka 14.25)

**Budkin's problem on maximal subquasivarieties (Kourovka 14.25).** The statement that there
is a finitely generated group $G$ such that the quasivariety $qG$ generated by $G$ has infinitely
many maximal proper subquasivarieties: for every natural number $k$ there are sets of
quasi-identities $Q_0, \dots, Q_{k-1}$, each defining a maximal proper subquasivariety of $qG$,
such that for $i \ne j$ the quasivarieties defined by $Q_i$ and $Q_j$ are different.
