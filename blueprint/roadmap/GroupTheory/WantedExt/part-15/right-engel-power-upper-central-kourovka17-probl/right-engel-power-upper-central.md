# Right Engel Power Upper Central

Kourovka 17.12 (A. Abdollahi): there are functions `e, c : ℕ → ℕ` with `e n > 0` such that,
whenever a normal subgroup `H` of a nilpotent group `G` consists of right `n`-Engel elements of
`G`, the subgroup `H^{e(n)}` generated by the `e(n)`-th powers of elements of `H` lies in the
`c(n)`-th term `ζ_{c(n)}(G)` of the upper central series.
