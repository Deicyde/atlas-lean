# Right Engel Power Upper Central holds

Resolved true: Crosby and Traustason prove that there are positive integers `e(n)`, `c(n)` such that
`[H^{e(n)}, _{c(n)} G] = 1`, that is `H^{e(n)} ≤ ζ_{c(n)}(G)`, whenever `H` is a normal subgroup of
`G` consisting of right `n`-Engel elements and lying in some term of the upper central series; in a
nilpotent group every subgroup does. The Kourovka Archive answers 17.12 "Yes, there are". Source: E.
I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st
issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 17.12 (A. Abdollahi);
resolution: P. G. Crosby and G. Traustason, J. Algebra 324 (2010), 875-883, main theorem as
presented by G. Traustason, Right Engel subgroups (Ischia Group Theory 2010): for a normal right
n-Engel subgroup H of G with H ≤ ζ_m(G) for some m, there are positive integers e(n), c(n) with
[H^{e(n)}, _{c(n)} G] = 1; Kourovka Archive, Problem 17.12 (Yes, there are),
http://www.dipmat2.unisa.it/ischiagrouptheory/IGT2010/talks/Traustason.pdf.
