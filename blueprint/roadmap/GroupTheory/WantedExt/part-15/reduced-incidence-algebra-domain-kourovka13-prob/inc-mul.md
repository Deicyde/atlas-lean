# Inc Mul

The product of the reduced incidence algebra on functions `Finset Ω → ℚ`:
`(fg)(X) = ∑_{Y ⊆ X} f(Y) g(X ∖ Y)`. For `f ∈ Vₙ`, `g ∈ Vₘ` (vanishing off `n`- resp. `m`-subsets)
this is the product of the problem, landing in `V_{n+m}`.
