# Pth Powers Subgroup Powerful

Kourovka 21.137 (L. Wilson), main question, for some prime `p`: whenever the set of `p`-th
powers of a finite `p`-group `G` is a subgroup `P`, the subgroup `P` is powerful, i.e.
`[P, P] ≤ P^p` for odd `p` and `[P, P] ≤ P⁴` for `p = 2`, where `P^m` is generated by the `m`-th
powers of elements of `P`.
