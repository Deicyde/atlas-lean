# Pth Powers Subgroup Abelian Exponent PSq

Kourovka 21.137 (L. Wilson), odd-prime form, for some odd prime `p`: whenever the set of
`p`-th powers of a finite `p`-group of exponent `p²` is a subgroup, that subgroup is abelian.
