# Homomorphisms trivial on the upper unitriangular matrices factor through the determinant

Let $F$ be a field, $n \ge 1$, $U_n(F)$ the group of upper unitriangular matrices of
$\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$, and $\varphi \colon \mathrm{GL}_n(F) \to A$ a homomorphism to an abelian group $A$
that is trivial on $U_n(F)$. Then there is a unique homomorphism $\chi \colon F^\times \to A$ with

$$\varphi = \chi \circ \det.$$

Proof. As $A$ is abelian, $\varphi(g u g^{-1}) = \varphi(u) = 1$ for $u \in U_n(F)$ and
$g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F)$, so $\varphi$ is trivial on the subgroup generated by the conjugates of
$U_n(F)$, which is $\mathrm{SL}_n(F)$ by
[the generation by conjugates](special-linear-group-generated-by-conjugates-of-unitriangular-matrices.md),
the kernel of $\det$. The determinant $\det \colon \mathrm{GL}_n(F) \to F^\times$ is surjective, as
$\mathrm{diag}(t, 1, \dots, 1)$ has determinant $t$, so $\varphi$ factors uniquely through it.

## Sources

- T. Wedhorn, "The local Langlands correspondence for GL(n) over p-adic fields", lecture notes,
  arXiv:math/0011210 (version 2): (2.1.18) and its proof, pp. 19–20 (a one-dimensional smooth
  representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$, $K$ a non-archimedean local field, is trivial on the unipotent
  upper triangular matrices $U$, hence on the subgroup generated by the conjugates of $U$, which is
  $\mathrm{SL}_n(K)$, and is of the form $\chi \circ \det$).
