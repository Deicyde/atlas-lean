# Criterion for a cyclic sublattice

**Criterion for a cyclic sublattice.** Let $L'$ and $L$ be subgroups of an abelian group $A$.
If $L' \subseteq L$ and the quotient group $L/L'$ is cyclic, then $L'$ is a cyclic sublattice
of $L$.
