# Order of $\mathrm{PGL}_2$ over a finite field

**Order of $\mathrm{PGL}_2$ over a finite field.** If $F$ is a finite field with $q$ elements,
then the projective general linear group $\mathrm{PGL}_2(F)$ has order $q(q^2 - 1)$.
