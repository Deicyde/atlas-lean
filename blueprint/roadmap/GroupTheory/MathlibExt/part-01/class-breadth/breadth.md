# Breadth of a finite group

**Breadth of a finite group.** For a finite group $G$ and a natural number $p$, the breadth
$\operatorname{br}_p(G)$ of $G$ is the maximum of the element breadths $\operatorname{br}_p(g)$
over all $g \in G$.
