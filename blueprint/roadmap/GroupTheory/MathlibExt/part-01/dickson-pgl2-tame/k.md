# Algebraic closure of the field with p elements

**Algebraic closure of the field with p elements.** For a prime $p$, $K_p$ denotes a fixed
algebraic closure of the finite field $\mathbb{F}_p = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.
