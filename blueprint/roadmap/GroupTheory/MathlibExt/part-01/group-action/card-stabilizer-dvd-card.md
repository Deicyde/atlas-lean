# Stabilizer Divides

If a subgroup `H` of `G` stabilizes a finite subset `U` of `G` under left
multiplication, then `|H|` divides `|U|`. The `H`-orbits partition `U` and
each orbit has exactly `|H|` elements, since left multiplication is injective.
