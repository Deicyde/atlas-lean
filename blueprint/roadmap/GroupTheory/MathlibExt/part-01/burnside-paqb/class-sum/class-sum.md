# Class sum of a finite subset

**Class sum of a finite subset.** Let $G$ be a group. The class sum of a finite subset
$C \subseteq G$ is the element $\widehat{C} = \sum_{x \in C} x$ of the complex group algebra
$\mathbb{C}[G]$.
