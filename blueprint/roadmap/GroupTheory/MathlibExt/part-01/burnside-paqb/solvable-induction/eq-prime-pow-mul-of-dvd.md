# Divisors of $p^a q^b$

**Divisors of $p^a q^b$.** Let $p$ and $q$ be primes and let $a$ and $b$ be natural numbers.
Every natural number $d$ dividing $p^a q^b$ has the form $d = p^{a'} q^{b'}$ for some natural
numbers $a'$ and $b'$.
