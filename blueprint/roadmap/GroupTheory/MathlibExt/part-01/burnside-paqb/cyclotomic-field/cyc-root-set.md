# Complex roots of $X^m - 1$

**Complex roots of $X^m - 1$.** For every natural number $m \neq 0$, the set of complex roots
of the rational polynomial $X^m - 1$ is exactly the set of $m$-th roots of unity in $\mathbb{C}$.
