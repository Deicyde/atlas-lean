# Burnside paqb zero

Burnside's `p^a q^b` theorem for small-universe groups: if `G` is finite with
`Fintype.card G = p ^ a * q ^ b` for primes `p, q`, then `G` is solvable.
Proved via the simple-prime-power corollary and induction.
