# Burnside's $p^a q^b$ theorem

**Burnside's $p^a q^b$ theorem.** Let $G$ be a finite group, let $p$ and $q$ be primes and
let $a$ and $b$ be natural numbers. If $G$ has order $p^a q^b$, then $G$ is solvable.
