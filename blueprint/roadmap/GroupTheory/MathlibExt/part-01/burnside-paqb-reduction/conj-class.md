# Conjugacy class

**Conjugacy class.** For an element $g$ of a finite group $G$, the conjugacy class of $g$ is
the finite set $\{h g h^{-1} : h \in G\}$, the orbit of $g$ under the action of $G$ on itself
by conjugation.
