# Conjugacy classes are closed under conjugation

**Conjugacy classes are closed under conjugation.** Let $g$, $h$ and $x$ be elements of a
finite group $G$. If $x$ lies in the conjugacy class of $g$, then so does $h x h^{-1}$.
