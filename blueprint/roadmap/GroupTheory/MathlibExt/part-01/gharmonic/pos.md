# Entries of a G-harmonic tuple are positive

**Entries of a G-harmonic tuple are positive.** Let $G$ be a group. If a tuple
$a = (a_1, \dots, a_n)$ of natural numbers is $G$-harmonic, then $a_i > 0$ for every $i$.
