# The tuple (1) is G-harmonic

**The tuple (1) is G-harmonic.** For every group $G$, the one-entry tuple $(1)$ is
$G$-harmonic; the subgroup $G$ itself, of index $1$, with coset representative $1$ witnesses
this.
