# Tuples with a zero entry are not G-harmonic

**Tuples with a zero entry are not G-harmonic.** Let $G$ be a group. If a tuple
$a = (a_1, \dots, a_n)$ of natural numbers has $a_i = 0$ for some $i$, then $a$ is not
$G$-harmonic.
