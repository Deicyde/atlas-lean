# The subgroup $2^kA$

**The subgroup $2^kA$.** For an abelian group $A$, written additively, and a natural number
$k$, $2^kA$ is the subgroup $\{2^k a : a \in A\}$ of $A$, the image of multiplication by $2^k$.
