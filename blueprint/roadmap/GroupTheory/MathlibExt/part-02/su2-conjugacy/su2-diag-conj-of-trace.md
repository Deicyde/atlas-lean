# Su2 diag conj of trace

Two diagonal elements `diag(z₁, conj z₁)` and `diag(z₂, conj z₂)` of `SU₂`
with the same trace are conjugate in `SU₂`: either `z₁ = z₂`, or
`z₁ = conj z₂` and conjugation by the anti-diagonal matrix swaps the
diagonal entries.
