# Trace is invariant under conjugation in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$

**Trace is invariant under conjugation in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$.** For all $P$ and $A$ in
$\mathrm{SU}(2)$, the trace of $P A P^{-1}$ equals the trace of $A$.
