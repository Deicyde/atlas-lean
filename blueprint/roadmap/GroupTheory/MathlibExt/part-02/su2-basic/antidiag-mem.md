# Antidiag mem

The anti-diagonal matrix `!![0, 1; -1, 0]` lies in `SU₂`. It is used to
show that a central element of `SU₂` has equal diagonal entries, and to
conjugate one diagonal element of `SU₂` to its conjugate.
