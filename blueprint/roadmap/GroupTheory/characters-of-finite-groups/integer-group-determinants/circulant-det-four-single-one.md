# The 4 × 4 circulant determinant of (1, 0, 0, 0)

**The 4 × 4 circulant determinant of (1, 0, 0, 0).** Over every commutative ring $R$, the
$4 \times 4$ circulant determinant satisfies $D_4(1, 0, 0, 0) = 1$.
