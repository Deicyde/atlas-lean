# Definition 1 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 10, Section 2 (Complex Vector Bundles and Chern Classes))

The first Chern class of $L$ is $c_{1}(L) = \left[\frac{1}{2\pi} R^{\nabla}\right] \in H^{2}(M, \mathbb{R})$.
