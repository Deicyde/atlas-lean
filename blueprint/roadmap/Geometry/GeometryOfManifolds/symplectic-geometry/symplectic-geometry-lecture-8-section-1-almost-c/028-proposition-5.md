# Proposition 5

$\operatorname{Sp}(2n) \cap O(2n) = \operatorname{Sp}(2n) \cap \operatorname{GL}(n, \mathbb{C}) = O(2n) \cap \operatorname{GL}(n, \mathbb{C}) = U(n)$.
