# Proposition 3 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 8, Section 1 (Almost-complex Structures))

For $(M, J)$ an almost-complex manifold, $\omega_{0}, \omega_{1}$ two symplectic forms compatible with $J$, $\omega_{t} = (1-t)\omega_{0} + t\omega_{1}$ is symplectic and $J$-compatible for all $t \in [0,1]$ (i.e.\ the space of $J$-compatible $\omega$ is convex).
