# Corollary 2 [2]

$\exists G \colon \Omega^{k} \rightarrow \Omega^{k}$ and $H \colon \Omega^{k} \rightarrow \mathcal{H}^{k} = \operatorname{Ker} \Delta$ such that $G\Delta = \Delta G = \operatorname{id} - H$ and $\operatorname{Im}(G\Delta) = (\mathcal{H}^{k})^{\perp}$.
