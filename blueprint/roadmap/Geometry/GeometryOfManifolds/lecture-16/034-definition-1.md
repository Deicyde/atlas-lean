# Definition 1 [3]

For $L \colon \Gamma(E) \rightarrow \Gamma(F)$ a differential operator, $P \colon \Gamma(F) \rightarrow \Gamma(E)$ is called a parametrix (or pseudoinverse) if $L \circ P - \operatorname{id}_{E}$ and $P \circ L - \operatorname{id}_{F}$ are smoothing operators, i.e.\ they extend continuously to $W^{s}(E) \rightarrow W^{s+1}(E)$.
