# Theorem 1 (Lecture 25, Section 2 (Seiberg-Witten Equations))

For $(g, \mu)$ generic, $\mathcal{M}$ (if nonempty) is a smooth, compact, orientable manifold of dimension $d(S) = \frac{1}{4}(c_{1}(L)^{2} \cdot [X] - (2\chi + 3\sigma))$.
