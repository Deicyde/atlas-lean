# Theorem 2 [1]

For $(M, \omega)$ symplectic, $X \subset (M, \omega)$ compact and Lagrangian, if $H^{1}(X, \mathbb{R}) = 0$, then every Lagrangian submanifold of $M$ which is $C^{1}$ close to $X$ intersects $X$ in $\geq 2$ points.
