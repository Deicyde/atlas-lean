# Proposition 2 [5]

Any two spin$^{c}$ connections differ by a 1-form on $X$ of the type $ia \otimes \operatorname{id}_{S^{\pm}}$, and the induced connection $A$ on $L = \bigwedge^{2} S^{\pm}$ defines the spin$^{c}$ connection uniquely.
