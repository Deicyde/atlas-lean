# Definition 4 (Lecture 24, Section 3 (Dirac Operator))

The Seiberg-Witten equations are the equations $D_{A}\psi = 0 \in \Gamma(S^{-})$ and $\gamma(F_{A}^{+}) = (\psi^{*} \otimes \psi)_{0} \in \Gamma(\operatorname{End}(S^{+}))$, where $A$ is a Hermitian connection on $L = \bigwedge^{2} S^{\pm}$ (corresponding to a spin$^{c}$ connection on $S^{\pm}$), $\psi \in \Gamma(S^{+})$ is a section, $F_{A}^{+} = \frac{1}{2}(F_{A} + \ast F_{A}) \in i\Omega_{+}^{2}$ for $F_{A} \in i\Omega^{2}$ the curvature of $A$, and $(\psi^{*} \otimes \psi)_{0} = \psi^{*} \otimes \psi - \frac{1}{2}|\psi|^{2}$ is the traceless part of $\psi^{*} \otimes \psi$.
