# Theorem 5 (Weinstein's Lagrangian Neighborhood)

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a symplectic manifold, $i \colon X \hookrightarrow M$ a closed Lagrangian submanifold, $i_{0} \colon X \rightarrow (T^{*}X, \omega_{0})$ the zero-section. Then $\exists U_{0}$ a neighborhood of $X$ in $T^{*}X$ and $U$ a neighborhood of $X$ in $M$ such that we have a symplectomorphism $(U_{0}, \omega_{0}) \xrightarrow{\sim} (U, \omega)$ which is the identity on $X$.
