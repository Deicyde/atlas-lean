# Theorem 4 (Local Moser)

Let $X \hookrightarrow M$ be a submanifold, $\omega_{0}, \omega_{1}$ symplectic forms on $M$ such that $(\omega_{0})_{p} = (\omega_{1})_{p}$ for all $p \in X$. Then $\exists$ neighborhoods $U_{0}, U_{1} \supset X$ and $\phi \colon U_{0} \xrightarrow{\sim} U_{1}$ such that $\phi^{*}\omega_{1} = \omega_{0}$ and $\phi|_{X} = \mathrm{id}$.
