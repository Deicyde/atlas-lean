# Proposition 3 (Lecture 3, Section 2 (Hamiltonian Vector Fields))

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a symplectic manifold, $H \colon M \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ a $C^{\infty}$ map, and $\rho_{t}$ the isotopy generated by the Hamiltonian vector field $X_{H}$. Then $\rho_{t}$ are symplectomorphisms.
