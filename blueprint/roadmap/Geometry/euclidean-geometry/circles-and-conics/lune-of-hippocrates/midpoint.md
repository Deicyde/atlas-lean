# Midpoint of two points in the plane

**Midpoint of two points in the plane.** For points $p = (p_1, p_2)$ and
$q = (q_1, q_2)$ of the coordinate plane, their midpoint is
$\left(\frac{p_1 + q_1}{2}, \frac{p_2 + q_2}{2}\right)$.
