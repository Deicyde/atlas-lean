# Configurations reachable by Apollonian swaps

The configurations reachable from $C_0$ are generated by swaps: in a reachable configuration $C$, replace the circle
$C_i$ by a different circle $D$ such that $D$ and the three circles $C_j$, $j \ne i$, again form a Descartes
configuration. By the theorem of Apollonius there are exactly two circles tangent to three mutually tangent circles
with distinct tangency points, so $D$ is the other one; in a bounded packing it is never a line.

- H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings: dynamics and number theory*, Japan. J. Math. 9 (2014), arXiv:1312.1383v2, Theorem 1.1 (Apollonius) and the construction after it.
