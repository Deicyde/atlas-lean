# Apollonian orbits stay on a level set of the Descartes form

Every quadruple in the
Apollonian orbit of `v` has the same Descartes form as `v`; in particular the orbit of a Descartes
quadruple consists of Descartes quadruples, which lie on the cone `Q = 0` (Kontorovich and Oh,
arXiv:0811.2236v5, Section 2.1).
