# Curvatures of tangent circles form a Descartes quadruple

Under the hypotheses of
Descartes' circle theorem (four circles of positive radii, either pairwise externally tangent or
with one circle internally tangent to the three others, which are pairwise externally tangent),
the signed curvatures `k` satisfy `Q(k) = 0` (Lagarias, Mallows and Wilks,
arXiv:math/0101066v1, Theorem 1.1).
