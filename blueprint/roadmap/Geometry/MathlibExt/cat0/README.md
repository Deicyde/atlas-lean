# CAT(0) comparison geometry

## Mathematical overview

The four-point comparison condition bounds the squared diagonals of a metric quadrilateral by the squared sides. This section defines that condition and studies its preservation under isometries.

The precise condition appears in [the four-point definition](is-cat0-four-point.md); its equivalent formulation is stated [here](is-cat0-four-point-iff.md).
