# Optimal area of ambidextrous sofas

**Optimal area of ambidextrous sofas.** The ambidextrous optimal area is the supremum, taken
in $[0, \infty]$, of the Lebesgue areas of all ambidextrous sofas $K \subseteq \mathbb{C}$.
