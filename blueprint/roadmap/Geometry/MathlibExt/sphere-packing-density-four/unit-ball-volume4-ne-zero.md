# The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has nonzero volume

**The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has nonzero volume.** The Lebesgue volume of the open unit
ball of radius $1$ about the origin in $\mathbb{R}^4$ is not $0$.
