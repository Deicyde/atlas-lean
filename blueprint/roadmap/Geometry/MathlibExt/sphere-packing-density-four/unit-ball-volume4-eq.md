# The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has volume $\pi^2/2$

**The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has volume $\pi^2/2$.** The Lebesgue measure of the open unit
ball centred at the origin of $\mathbb{R}^4$ equals $\pi^2/2$.
