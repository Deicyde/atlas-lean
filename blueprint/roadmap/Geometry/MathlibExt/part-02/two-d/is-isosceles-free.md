# Isosceles-free sets

**Isosceles-free sets.** A set $A$ in a space with a distance function $d$ is isosceles-free if
there are no three distinct points $x, y, z \in A$ such that two of the distances $d(x, y)$,
$d(y, z)$, $d(z, x)$ are equal.
