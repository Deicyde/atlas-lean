# Lattice Enumerator

Totalized enumerator: `ncard` of `latticePointSet`. For unbounded `P` the
intersection can be infinite and `ncard` returns junk `0`; use
`latticeEnumeratorPos` with a boundedness proof for source-faithful counts.
