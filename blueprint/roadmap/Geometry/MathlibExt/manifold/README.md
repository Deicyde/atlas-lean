# Smooth manifolds

## Mathematical overview

Smooth manifolds provide the setting for local parametrizations, immersions, submersions, and transversality. This section also brings together Morse functions and Morse theory, Sard-type results, collar neighborhoods, and holomorphic mapping results. Open an individual article for its dimensions, regularity assumptions, and precise conclusion.
