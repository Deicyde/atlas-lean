# Smoothness of the Morse quadratic form

**Smoothness of the Morse quadratic form.** For all natural numbers $k$ and $n$, the Morse
quadratic form $Q_{k,n} \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ of index $k$ is $C^\infty$.
