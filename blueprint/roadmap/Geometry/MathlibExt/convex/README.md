# Convex geometry

## Mathematical overview

Convex geometry connects convex hulls and separation with finite configurations, coverings, and lattice points. This section collects colorful Carathéodory results, Kirchberger separation, centerpoint and Tverberg statements, and questions about covering and illumination. Each linked article supplies its own hypotheses and statement.

The [colorful Carathéodory section](colorful-caratheodory/README.md) is a starting point; [covering](covering/README.md) and [illumination](illumination/README.md) give complementary geometric viewpoints.
