# Is Gradient Shrinking Ricci Soliton gaussian

The Gaussian shrinking soliton for `0 < a`, with constant `a`. For `a = 1 / 2`
this is the Gaussian shrinker `(ℝⁿ, g_E, ‖x‖ ^ 2 / 4)` of the cited report (Yokota,
p. 1652).
