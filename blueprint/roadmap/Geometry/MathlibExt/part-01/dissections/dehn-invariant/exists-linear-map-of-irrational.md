# Exists linear Map of irrational

If `α / π` is irrational, there is a `ℚ`-linear map `ℝ →ₗ[ℚ] ℝ` that vanishes at `π`
but not at `α`: `α` lies outside the `ℚ`-span of `π`, and a linear functional on the
quotient separates it.
