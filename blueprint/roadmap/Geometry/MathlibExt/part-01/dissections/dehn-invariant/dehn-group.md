# Dehn group

**Dehn group.** The Dehn group is the tensor product
$\mathbb{R} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} (\mathbb{R} / 2\pi\mathbb{Z})$ of abelian groups; it is the target
of the Dehn invariant.
