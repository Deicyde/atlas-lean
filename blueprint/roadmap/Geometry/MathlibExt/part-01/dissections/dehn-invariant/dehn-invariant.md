# Dehn invariant

**Dehn invariant.** The Dehn invariant of a list of edges is the sum, over the edges, of
$\ell \otimes \theta$ in the Dehn group, where $\ell$ is the length of the edge and $\theta$ its
dihedral angle taken modulo $2\pi$.
