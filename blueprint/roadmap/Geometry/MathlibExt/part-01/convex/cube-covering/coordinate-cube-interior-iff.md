# Interior of the coordinate cube

**Interior of the coordinate cube.** A point $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ lies in the interior of
the coordinate cube $[-1, 1]^n$ if and only if $-1 < x_i < 1$ for every coordinate $i$.
