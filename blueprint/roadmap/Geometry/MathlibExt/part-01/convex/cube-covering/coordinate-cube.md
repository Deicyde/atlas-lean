# Coordinate cube

**Coordinate cube.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the coordinate cube is the set $[-1, 1]^n$ of
points $x$ of the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ with $-1 \le x_i \le 1$ for every coordinate
$i$.
