# The coordinate cube is a convex body

**The coordinate cube is a convex body.** For every $n$, the coordinate cube $[-1, 1]^n$ in
$\mathbb{R}^n$ is a convex body: it is compact and convex, and its interior in $\mathbb{R}^n$ is
nonempty.
