# Integer unit cube

**Integer unit cube.** For $v \in \mathbb{Z}^n$, the integer unit cube at $v$ is the half-open
cube $Q_v = \prod_i [v_i, v_i + 1) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, the set of $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with
$v_i \le x_i < v_i + 1$ for every coordinate $i$.
