# The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has finite volume

**The unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has finite volume.** The Lebesgue volume of the open unit
ball of radius $1$ about the origin in $\mathbb{R}^4$ is not $\infty$.
