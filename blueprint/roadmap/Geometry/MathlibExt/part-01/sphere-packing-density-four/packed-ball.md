# Packed ball

**Packed ball.** For a packing $P$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$ with radius $r$ and a point
$c \in \mathbb{R}^4$, the packed ball of $P$ at $c$ is the closed ball
$\{x \in \mathbb{R}^4 : \|x - c\| \le r\}$, boundary included.
