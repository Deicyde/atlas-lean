# Ordered triple of points

**Ordered triple of points.** For points $p, q, r$ of Euclidean 3-space, the family indexed by
$\{0, 1, 2\}$ that sends $0 \mapsto p$, $1 \mapsto q$ and $2 \mapsto r$.
