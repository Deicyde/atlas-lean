# Quasi-inverse of a quasi-isometry

An `(L, C)`-quasi-isometry `f : X → Y` has a
quasi-inverse: an `(L, 3 L C)`-quasi-isometry `g : Y → X` with `d(f (g y), y) ≤ C` and
`d(g (f x), x) ≤ 2 L C` for all `x` and `y`. This is the "`f ∘ g` and `g ∘ f` are translations"
half of Gromov's definition (Proc. ICM 1983, §1.B).
