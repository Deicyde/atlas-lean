# Proposition 38.4

Write the geodesic equations in conjugate variables $x_{k}$ (position) and $p_{k} = \sum_{l} g_{kl}(x) v_{l}$ (momentum). Then they take on the Hamiltonian form $x_{k}^{\prime} = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_{k}}$, $p_{k}^{\prime} = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial x_{k}}$, where $H = \frac{1}{2} I(v,v) = \frac{1}{2} \langle p, G^{-1}(x) \cdot p \rangle = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{ij} p_{i} g^{ij}(x) p_{j}$.
