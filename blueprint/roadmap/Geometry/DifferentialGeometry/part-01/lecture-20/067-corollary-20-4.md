# Corollary 20.4

Let $f \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be a hypersurface patch, defined on a connected set. Suppose that for each point in $U$, the matrix $H_{x}$ has rank $\geq 3$. In that case, the intrinsic geometry of $f$ determines the extrinsic one. This means that if $\tilde{f} \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ is another hypersurface patch with the same first fundamental form as $f$, then necessarily $\tilde{f}(x) = Af(x) + c$ with $A$ an orthogonal matrix, and $c$ a constant.
