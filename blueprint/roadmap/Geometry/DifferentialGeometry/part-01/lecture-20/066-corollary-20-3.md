# Corollary 20.3

$\kappa_{\text{scalar}}$ and $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}^{n-1}$ are intrinsic. In particular, $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}$ is intrinsic for $n$ even, and $|\kappa_{\text{gauss}}|$ is intrinsic for $n \geq 3$ odd.
