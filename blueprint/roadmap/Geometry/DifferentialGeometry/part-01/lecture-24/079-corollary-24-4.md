# Corollary 24.4

Let $\psi, \tilde{\psi} \colon V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be two local defining functions for $M$. Then there is a unique smooth nowhere vanishing function $q \colon V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that $\tilde{\psi} = q\psi$.
