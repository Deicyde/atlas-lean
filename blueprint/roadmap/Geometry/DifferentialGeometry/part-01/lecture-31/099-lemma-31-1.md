# Lemma 31.1

Let $f$ be a partial parametrization, and $\nu^{f}$ the associated Gauss normal. Then $\operatorname{det}(G^{f}) = \operatorname{det}(\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f, \nu^{f})^{2}$. In particular, in the case of a surface, $\sqrt{\operatorname{det}(G^{f})} = \|\partial_{x_{1}} f \times \partial_{x_{2}} f\|$.
