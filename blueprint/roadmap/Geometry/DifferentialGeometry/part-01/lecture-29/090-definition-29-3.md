# Definition 29.3

Let $M$ be a hypersurface. A partial parametrization of $M$ consists of an open subset $V \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$, an open subset $U \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$, and a hypersurface patch $f \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ which is one-to-one (injective), and whose image is precisely $M \cap V$.
