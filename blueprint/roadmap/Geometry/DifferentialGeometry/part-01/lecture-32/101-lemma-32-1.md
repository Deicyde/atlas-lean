# Lemma 32.1

Let $M, \tilde{M}$ be hypersurfaces, with Gauss maps $\nu, \tilde{\nu}$, and $\phi \colon M \rightarrow \tilde{M}$ be a smooth map. Suppose that we have a parametrization $f \colon U \rightarrow M$ compatible with the orientation. Set $\phi^{f} = \phi \circ f \colon U \rightarrow \tilde{M} \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$, and let $G^{f}$ be the first fundamental form. Then for $y = f(x)$, $\operatorname{det}(D\phi)_{y} = \frac{\operatorname{det}(\partial_{x_{1}} \phi^{f}, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} \phi^{f}, \tilde{\nu}(\phi^{f}(x)))}{\sqrt{\operatorname{det}(G^{f}(x))}}$.
