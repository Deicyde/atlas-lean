# Definition 32.2

Let $M, \tilde{M}$ be compact hypersurfaces equipped with Gauss maps. Assume that $\tilde{M}$ is connected. Let $\phi \colon M \rightarrow \tilde{M}$ be a smooth map. The degree of $\phi$ is defined as $\operatorname{deg}(\phi) = \frac{1}{\operatorname{vol}(\tilde{M})} \int_{M} \operatorname{det}(D\phi_{y}) \, d\operatorname{vol}_{y}$.
