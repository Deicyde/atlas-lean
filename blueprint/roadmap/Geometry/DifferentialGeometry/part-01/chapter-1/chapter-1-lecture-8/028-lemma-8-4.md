# Lemma 8.4

Let $c$ be a closed curve of period $T$, and set $L = \int_{0}^{T} \left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\| \, dt$. Let $d$ be the unit speed reparametrization of $c$. Then $d$ is again a closed curve, of period $L$. Moreover, the total curvature of $d$ is the same as that of $c$.
