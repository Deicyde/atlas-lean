# Corollary 33.5

Let $M$ be a compact hypersurface with an orientation. Then $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}^{\text{tot}} = (-1)^{n} \operatorname{vol}(S^{n}) \operatorname{deg}(\nu)$. In particular, the total Gauss curvature is always an integer multiple of $\operatorname{vol}(S^{n})$.
