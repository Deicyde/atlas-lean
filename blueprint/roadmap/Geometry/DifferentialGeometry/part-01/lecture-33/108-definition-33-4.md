# Definition 33.4

Let $M$ be a compact hypersurface with an orientation. The total Gauss curvature is $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}^{\text{tot}} = \int_{M} \kappa_{\text{gauss}} \, d\operatorname{vol}$.
