# Lemma 33.2

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be a compact hypersurface with a Gauss map, and $\phi \colon M \rightarrow S^{n}$ a smooth map. If $\operatorname{deg}(\phi) \neq 0$, then $\phi$ is necessarily onto.
