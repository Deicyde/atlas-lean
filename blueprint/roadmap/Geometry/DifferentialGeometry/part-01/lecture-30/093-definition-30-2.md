# Definition 30.2

A hypersurface $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ is compact if it is bounded and closed (closedness means that if a sequence $y_{n} \in M$ converges to some point $y_{\infty} \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$, then that point must also lie in $M$).
