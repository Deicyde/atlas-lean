# Index of the $i$-th $q$-coordinate in $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$

**Index of the $i$-th $q$-coordinate in $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$.** For $i \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$,
the index of the $i$-th $q$-coordinate among the coordinates $0, \dots, 2n - 1$ of
$\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ is $i + n$.
