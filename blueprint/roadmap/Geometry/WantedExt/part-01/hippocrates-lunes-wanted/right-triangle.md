# Right triangle with legs on the axes

**Right triangle with legs on the axes.** For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the right triangle
$T(a, b)$ is the convex hull in the plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ of the three points $(0, 0)$, $(a, 0)$
and $(0, b)$.
