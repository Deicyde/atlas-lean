# Displacement vector between two points of the plane

**Displacement vector between two points of the plane.** For points $p = (p_1, p_2)$ and
$q = (q_1, q_2)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$, the displacement vector from $p$ to $q$ is
$(q_1 - p_1, q_2 - p_2)$.
