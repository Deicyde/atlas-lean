# Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^d$

**Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^d$.** For a natural number $d$, $E(d)$ is the $d$-dimensional
Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^d$, with coordinates indexed by $0, 1, \dots, d - 1$ and the standard
inner product.
