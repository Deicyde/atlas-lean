# Velocity of a space curve

**Velocity of a space curve.** For a curve $r \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^3$ and a real
number $t$, the velocity of $r$ at $t$ is its derivative $r'(t)$, taken to be $0$ when $r$ is not
differentiable at $t$.
