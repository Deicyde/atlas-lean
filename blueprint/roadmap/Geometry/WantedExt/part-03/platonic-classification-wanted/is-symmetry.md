# Symmetry of a convex polytope

**Symmetry of a convex polytope.** An affine isometry $\varphi$ of $\mathbb{R}^n$ is a
symmetry of a convex polytope $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ if $\varphi(P) = P$.
