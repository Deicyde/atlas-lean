# Milnor number

**Milnor number.** Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $f$ be a formal power series over $R$
in variables $z_i$ indexed by a set $\sigma$. The Milnor number $\mu(f) \in \mathbb{N} \cup
\{\infty\}$ is the rank, as an $R$-module, of the quotient of this power series ring by the ideal
generated by the partial derivatives $\partial f / \partial z_i$ for $i \in \sigma$, and $\infty$
when that rank is infinite. For $R = \mathbb{C}$ and $\sigma = \{1, \dots, n\}$ it is
$\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathbb{C}[[z_1, \dots, z_n]] / (\partial f / \partial z_1, \dots,
\partial f / \partial z_n)$.
