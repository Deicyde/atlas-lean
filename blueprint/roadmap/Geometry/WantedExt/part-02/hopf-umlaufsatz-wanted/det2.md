# Planar determinant

**Planar determinant.** For vectors $u = (u_0, u_1)$ and $v = (v_0, v_1)$ in the Euclidean
plane $\mathbb{R}^2$, $\det(u, v) = u_0 v_1 - u_1 v_0$, the signed area of the parallelogram
spanned by $u$ and $v$.
