# Liouville–Arnold coordinates: index of $q_i$

**Liouville–Arnold coordinates: index of $q_i$.** For $i \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the
coordinate $q_i$ of $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ is coordinate number $n + i$: this map sends $i$ to
$n + i \in \{0, \dots, 2n-1\}$.
