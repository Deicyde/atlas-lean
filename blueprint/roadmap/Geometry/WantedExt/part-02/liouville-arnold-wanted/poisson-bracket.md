# Poisson Bracket

Standard **Poisson bracket** on `ℝ^{2n}` for the symplectic form
`ω₀ = ∑ᵢ dpᵢ ∧ dqᵢ`:
`{F, G}(x) = ∑ᵢ ((∂F/∂pᵢ)(x)(∂G/∂qᵢ)(x) − (∂F/∂qᵢ)(x)(∂G/∂pᵢ)(x))`.
