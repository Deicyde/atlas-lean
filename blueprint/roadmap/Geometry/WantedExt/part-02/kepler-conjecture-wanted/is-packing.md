# Unit sphere packing

**Unit sphere packing.** For a natural number $d$, a set $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ is the
centre set of a unit sphere packing if the open balls of radius $1$ centred at any two distinct
points of $X$ are disjoint.
