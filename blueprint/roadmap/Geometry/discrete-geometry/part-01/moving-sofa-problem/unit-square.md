# Unit square

**Unit square.** The unit square is the set of complex numbers $z$ with
$0 \le \operatorname{Re} z \le 1$ and $0 \le \operatorname{Im} z \le 1$, that is, the square
$[0,1] \times [0,1]$ in the complex plane.
