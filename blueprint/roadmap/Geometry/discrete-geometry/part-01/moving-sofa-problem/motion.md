# Planar rigid motion

**Planar rigid motion.** A motion consists of a continuous map $t \mapsto u_t$ from $[0, 1]$
to the unit circle $\{u \in \mathbb{C} : |u| = 1\}$ (the rotation) and a continuous map
$t \mapsto c_t$ from $[0, 1]$ to $\mathbb{C}$ (the translation).
