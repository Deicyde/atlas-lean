# Continuity of the continuous Turán number

**Continuity of the continuous Turán number.** For every $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, the
continuous Turán number $\mathrm{opx}(n, P)$ is a continuous function of $n$ on $(0, \infty)$.
