# Lattice polygon

**Lattice polygon.** Given integer points $v_0, \dots, v_{n-1} \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the lattice
polygon on $v$ is the polygon in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $n$ vertices whose $i$-th vertex is $v_i$,
viewed as a point of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
