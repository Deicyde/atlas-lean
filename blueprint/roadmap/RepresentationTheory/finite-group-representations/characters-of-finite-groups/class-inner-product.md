# Class inner product

**Class inner product.** Let $G$ be a finite group and $k$ a field in which $|G|$ is
invertible. For functions $f_1, f_2 \colon G \to k$, their class inner product is
$\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle = |G|^{-1} \sum_{g \in G} f_1(g) f_2(g^{-1})$.
