# Wedderburn Alg Hom

The algebra map `k[G] → Module.End k (Fin (d i) → k)` underlying the
`i`-th Wedderburn block: the Wedderburn equivalence, evaluation at `i`,
and the matrix-to-linear-map equivalence.
