# Has Eigenvalue pow order Of eq one

Every eigenvalue `μ` of `ρ g` satisfies `μ ^ orderOf g = 1`: the
minimal polynomial of `ρ g` divides `X ^ orderOf g - 1`, and `μ` is a root
of the minimal polynomial.
