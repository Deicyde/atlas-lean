# Proposition 9.1

The simple $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-modules (or equivalently, Harish-Chandra modules) are $L_m$, $m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$, $M_m^-$, $M_{-m}^+$, $m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}$, and $P_+(s)$, $s \notin 2\mathbb{Z}+1$, $P_-(s)$, $s \notin 2\mathbb{Z}$, with the only isomorphisms $P_{\pm}(s) \cong P_{\pm}(-s)$.
