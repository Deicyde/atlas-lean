# Proposition 19.3

Let $X \in HC(\mathfrak{g})$. Then $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}-\operatorname{bimod}}(X, \mathbf{M}(\lambda, \mu)) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{(\mathfrak{b}_{-}, \mathfrak{b}_{+})-\operatorname{bimod}}(X \otimes \mathbb{C}_{\lambda-\rho}, \mathbb{C}_{\mu-\rho})$ where the $(\mathfrak{b}_{-}, \mathfrak{b}_{+})$-bimodule structure on $\mathbb{C}_{\mu-\rho}$ is defined by the character $(\mu-\rho, 0)$ and on $\mathbb{C}_{\lambda-\rho}$ by the character $(0, \lambda-\rho)$.
