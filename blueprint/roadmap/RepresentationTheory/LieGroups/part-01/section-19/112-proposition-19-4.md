# Proposition 19.4

The right action of $\mathfrak{g}$ on $\mathbf{M}(\lambda, \mu)$ is given by the formula $\Phi_{V}(v \otimes \ell) \cdot b=\Phi_{\mathfrak{g} \otimes V}\left([b \otimes v] \bigotimes\left[(\lambda-\rho) \otimes \ell+\sum_{\alpha \in R_{+}} f_{\alpha}^{*} \otimes f_{\alpha} \ell\right]\right)$.
