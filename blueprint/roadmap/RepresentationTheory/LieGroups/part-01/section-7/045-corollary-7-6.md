# Corollary 7.6

For a semisimple Lie group $G$, the assignment $V \mapsto V^{K\text{-fin}}$ is an equivalence of categories between unitary representations of $G$ of finite length and unitary Harish-Chandra modules of finite length (i.e., Harish-Chandra modules which admit an invariant positive Hermitian inner product).
