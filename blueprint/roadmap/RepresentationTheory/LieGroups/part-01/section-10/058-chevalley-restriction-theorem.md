# Theorem 10.1 (Chevalley restriction theorem)

(i) $\operatorname{Res}(F) \in \mathbb{C}[\mathfrak{h}]^W$. (ii) The map $\operatorname{Res}: \mathbb{C}[\mathfrak{g}]^{\mathfrak{g}} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}[\mathfrak{h}]^W$ is a graded algebra isomorphism.
