# Lemma 20.4

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional vector space, and suppose we have a short exact sequence in $\mathcal{O}$: $0 \rightarrow K \rightarrow E \otimes M_{\lambda} \rightarrow Z \rightarrow 0$ with $K[\lambda]=0$. If $\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal{O}}^{1}(Z, M_{\mu}^{\vee})=0$ for all $\mu \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ then $K=0$ and $Z \cong E \otimes M_{\lambda}$.
