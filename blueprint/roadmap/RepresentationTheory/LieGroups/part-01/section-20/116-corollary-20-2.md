# Corollary 20.2

If $X$ is standardly filtered then $\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal{O}}^{i}(X, M_{\mu}^{\vee})=0$ for all $\mu \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ and $i>0$.
