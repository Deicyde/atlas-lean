# Corollary 20.14

The following conditions on $\mu \leq \lambda$ are equivalent. (i) $\mu \preceq \lambda$. (ii) $L_{\mu-\rho}$ occurs in $M_{\lambda-\rho}$. (iii) $\dim \operatorname{Hom}(M_{\mu-\rho}, M_{\lambda-\rho}) \neq 0$.
