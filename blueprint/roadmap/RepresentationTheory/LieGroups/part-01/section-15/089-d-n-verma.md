# Theorem 15.11 (D. N. Verma)

Let $\lambda, \mu \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ and $\mu \preceq \lambda$. Then $\dim \operatorname{Hom}(M_{\mu-\rho}, M_{\lambda-\rho})=1$ and $M_{\mu-\rho}$ can be uniquely realized as a submodule of $M_{\lambda-\rho}$. In particular, $L_{\mu-\rho}$ occurs in the composition series of $M_{\lambda-\rho}$.
