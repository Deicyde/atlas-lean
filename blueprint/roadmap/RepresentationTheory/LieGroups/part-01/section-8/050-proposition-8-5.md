# Proposition 8.5

The map $\phi: U(\mathfrak{n}_{-}) \rightarrow M_{\lambda}$ given by $\phi(x)=xv_{\lambda}$ is an isomorphism of left $U(\mathfrak{n}_{-})$-modules.
