# Corollary 8.7

$M_{\lambda}$ has a weight decomposition with $P(M_{\lambda}) = \lambda - Q_+$, $\dim M_{\lambda}[\lambda] = 1$, and weight subspaces of $M_{\lambda}$ are finite-dimensional.
