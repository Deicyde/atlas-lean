# Proposition 17.6

(i) The orbit $O_{e}:=\operatorname{Ad}(G) e$ is open and dense in $\mathcal{N}$. (ii) All regular nilpotent elements in $\mathfrak{g}$ are conjugate to $e$. (iii) $\mathcal{N}$ is an irreducible affine variety. Thus $(S\mathfrak{g})_{0}$ is an integral domain.
