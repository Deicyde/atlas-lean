# Lemma 3.7

The map $\mathbb{C} G \times V \rightarrow V$ given by $g \mapsto \pi(g) v$ is continuous. Thus $\pi$ is continuous in the weak topology of $\mathbb{C} G$ and strong topology of $\operatorname{End}(V)$.
