# Corollary 16.1

The following conditions on a weight $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ are equivalent: (i) $\lambda$ is dominant for $\leq$; (ii) $\lambda$ is dominant for $\preceq$; (iii) For every root $\alpha \in R_{+}$, $(\lambda, \alpha^{\vee}) \notin \mathbb{Z}_{<0}$; (iv) For every $w \in W_{\lambda+Q}$, $w\lambda \preceq \lambda$; (v) For every $w \in W_{\lambda+Q}$, $w\lambda \leq \lambda$.
