# Theorem 2.4 (Peter-Weyl)

$L^{2}(K)^{\text{fin}}$ is a dense subspace of $L^{2}(K)$. Hence $\{\psi_{\rho i j}\}$ form an orthonormal basis of $L^{2}(K)$, and we have $L^{2}(K) = \widehat{\oplus}_{\rho \in \operatorname{Irr} K} \rho^{*} \otimes \rho$ (completed orthogonal direct sum under the Hilbert space norm).
