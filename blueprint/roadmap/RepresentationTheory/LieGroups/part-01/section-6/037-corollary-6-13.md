# Corollary 6.13

Let $V$ be an admissible representation of $G$. There is a bijection between subrepresentations of $V$ and $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-submodules of $V^{K\text{-fin}}$, given by $\alpha: U \subset V \mapsto U^{K\text{-fin}}$. The inverse is given by $\beta: W \mapsto \bar{W}$.
