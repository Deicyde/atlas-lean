# Corollary 4.8

$L^{2}(K)^{\text{fin}} \subset C(K)$ is a dense subspace. Moreover, if $K$ is a Lie group then $L^{2}(K)^{\text{fin}} \subset C^{k}(K)$ is a dense subspace for $0 \leq k \leq \infty$.
