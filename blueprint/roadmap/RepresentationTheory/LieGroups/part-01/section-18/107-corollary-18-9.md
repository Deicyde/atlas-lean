# Corollary 18.9

If $V$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module then the natural map $V \otimes U_{\chi_{\lambda+\rho}} \rightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_{\text{fin}}(M_{\lambda}, V \otimes M_{\lambda})$ is an isomorphism.
