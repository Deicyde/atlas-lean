# Theorem 25.8

(J. Bernstein--S. Gelfand) (i) If $\lambda$ is a regular weight then the functor $T_{\lambda}$ is an equivalence of categories, with quasi-inverse $H_{\lambda}$. (ii) In general, $T_{\lambda}$ is fully faithful and defines an equivalence $\operatorname{HC}_{\theta}^1 \cong \mathcal{O}(\lambda)$ with quasi-inverse $H_{\lambda}$.
