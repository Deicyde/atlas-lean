# Theorem 25.6

The simples (and indecomposable projectives) in $\operatorname{HC}_{\theta}^1$ are labeled by the set $\Xi$, via $\xi \in \Xi \mapsto L_{\xi}, P_{\xi}$. Namely, if $\xi = (\mu, \lambda)$ is a proper representation then $P_{\xi}$ is the unique indecomposable projective in $\operatorname{HC}_{\theta}^1$ such that $P_{\xi} \otimes_{U(\mathfrak{g})} M_{\lambda - \rho} = P_{\mu - \rho}$.
