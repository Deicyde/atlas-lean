# Theorem 22.4

Let $F_{1}, F_{2}$ be projective $\theta$-functors for $\theta=\chi_{\lambda}$. Let $i_{\lambda}: \operatorname{Hom}(F_{1}, F_{2}) \rightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(F_{1}(M_{\lambda-\rho}), F_{2}(M_{\lambda-\rho}))$. Then $i_{\lambda}$ is an isomorphism.
