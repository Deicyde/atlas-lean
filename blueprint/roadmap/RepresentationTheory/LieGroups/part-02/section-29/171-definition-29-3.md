# Definition 29.3

A smooth algebraic variety $X$ is said to be $D$-affine if the global sections functor $\Gamma: \mathcal{M}(X) \rightarrow D(X)-\bmod$ is an equivalence (hence $\operatorname{Loc}$ is its inverse).
