# Proposition 27.15

Let $Y$ be an irreducible normal affine algebraic variety and $p: X \rightarrow Y$ be a resolution of singularities. Then the homomorphism $p^{*}: \mathcal{O}(Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(X)$ is an isomorphism.
