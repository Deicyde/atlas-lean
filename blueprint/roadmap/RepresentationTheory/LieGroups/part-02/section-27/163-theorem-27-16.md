# Theorem 27.16

Let $p: T^{*} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{N}$ be the Springer resolution. Then the map $p^{*}: \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{N}) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}\left(T^{*} \mathcal{F}\right)$ is an isomorphism of graded algebras.
