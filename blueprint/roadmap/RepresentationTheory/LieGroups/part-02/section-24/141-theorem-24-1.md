# Theorem 24.1

If $W_{\lambda} = W_{\mu}$ and $V$ has extremal weight $\mu - \lambda$ then $F_{\chi, V, \theta} \colon \operatorname{Rep}(\mathfrak{g})_{\theta} \rightarrow \operatorname{Rep}(\mathfrak{g})_{\chi}$ is an equivalence of categories. A quasi-inverse equivalence is given by the functor $F_{\theta, V^*, \chi}$.
