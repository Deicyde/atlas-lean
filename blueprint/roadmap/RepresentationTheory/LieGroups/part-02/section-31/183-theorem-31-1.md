# Theorem 31.1

Let $X$ be a smooth variety and $K$ a connected algebraic group acting on $X$ with finitely many orbits. Then there are finitely many irreducible $K$-equivariant $D$-modules on $X$. Namely, they are parametrized by pairs $(O, V)$ where $O$ is an orbit of $K$ on $X$ and $V$ is an irreducible representation of the component group $H/H_{0}$ of the stabilizer $H:=K_{x}$ for $x \in O$, $(O, V) \mapsto M(O, V)$.
