# Objective of the MAX CUT semidefinite program

For a symmetric matrix $Y$ indexed by the vertices,

$$\frac12 \sum_{i<j} w_{ij} (1 - Y_{ij}),$$

the objective of the semidefinite form of the relaxation. Goemans's survey writes it as
$\frac14 L(G) \bullet Y$ with the weighted Laplacian $L(G)$; that form is not formalized here.
