# Weight satisfied by a truth assignment

For a truth assignment $a$, the total weight $\sum_{j : a \models C_j} w_j$ of the clauses it
satisfies, with satisfaction as in the repository's
[CNF semantics](../../../Logic/propositional-logic/conjunctive-normal-form/satisfies-2.md). MAX SAT
maximizes it over $a$.
