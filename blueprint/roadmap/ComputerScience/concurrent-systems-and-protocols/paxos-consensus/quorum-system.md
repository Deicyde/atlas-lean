# Quorum system

A family of finite sets of acceptors, the quorums, any two of which
share an acceptor (Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, Section 2.2: "any two majorities have at least
one acceptor in common", and the "obvious generalization of a majority").

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
