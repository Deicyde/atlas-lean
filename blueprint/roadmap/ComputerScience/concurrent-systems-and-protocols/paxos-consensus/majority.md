# Majorities form a quorum system

**Majorities form a quorum system** (Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, Section 2.2): the quorums
are the sets containing more than half of the finitely many acceptors.

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
