# Generalization bounds and concentration

Uniform deviation bounds between the empirical and the expected risk, obtained from concentration inequalities: for a finite class, Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound; for a general class, McDiarmid's bounded-differences inequality concentrates the supremum of the deviations around its mean. These pages are AI-authored expositions of published results without Lean formalization; each names what it relies on, and how, under Informally uses, and marks results not stated in its sources as derived.
