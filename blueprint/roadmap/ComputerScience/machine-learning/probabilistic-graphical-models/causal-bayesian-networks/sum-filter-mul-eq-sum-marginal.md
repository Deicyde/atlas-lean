# Splitting a sum by the values on `T`

A sum against `P` over the assignments agreeing with `x'` on a fixed set `S`, of a weight `g`
that depends only on the variables in `T ∪ S` (with `T` disjoint from `S`), is a sum over the
values `t` of the variables in `T` of `g` times the marginal of `P` on `T ∪ S`.
