# Relating two nested interventions

For `do(X_W = w)` and `do(X_{W'} = w)` with `W ⊆ W'`, on assignments agreeing with `w` on `W'`:
the joint of the smaller intervention is the joint of the larger one times the tables of the
nodes of `W' \ W`. With `W = ∅` this writes the joint law, on the event `X_{W'} = w`, as the
interventional law of `do(X_{W'} = w)` times the tables of `W'`.
