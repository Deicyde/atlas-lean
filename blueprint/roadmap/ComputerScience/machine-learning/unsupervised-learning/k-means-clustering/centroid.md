# Centroid

**Centroid.** Let $x_i$ be points in a real inner product space and $s$ a finite set of
indices. The centroid of the points indexed by $s$ is $\frac{1}{|s|} \sum_{i \in s} x_i$; when $s$
is empty it is the zero vector.
