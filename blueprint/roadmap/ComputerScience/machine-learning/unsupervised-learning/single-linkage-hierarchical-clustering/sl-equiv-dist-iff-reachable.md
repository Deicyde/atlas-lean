# Single-linkage clusters are the connected components of the Rips graph

In a
(pseudo)metric space, two points are `r`-equivalent exactly when they are joined
by a path in the `r`-neighbourhood graph, the one-skeleton of `VR(M, r)`. So the single-linkage
clusters at scale `r` are `π₀` of the Vietoris–Rips complex (Carlsson, *Topology and data*,
Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2).
