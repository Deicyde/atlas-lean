# Single linkage gives the maximal subdominant ultrametric

If `u` is symmetric, zero on
the diagonal, satisfies the ultrametric inequality and `u ≤ d`, then `u ≤ u*` (Carlsson and
Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Example 7, the remark after equation (4)). Together with `slDist_le` and
`slDist_le_max`, `u*` is the largest ultrametric below `d` when `d ≥ 0`.
