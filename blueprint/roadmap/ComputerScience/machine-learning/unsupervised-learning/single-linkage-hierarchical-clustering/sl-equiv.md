# `r`-equivalence (single-linkage clusters at scale `r`)

For a dissimilarity
`d : X → X → ℝ` and a scale `r`, `SLEquiv d r` is the equivalence relation generated by
`d x y ≤ r`: `x` and `y` are `r`-equivalent when they are joined by a finite chain
`x = x₀, x₁, …, x_k = y` whose consecutive points satisfy `d xᵢ xᵢ₊₁ ≤ r` (up to the order of
the arguments of `d`). Its classes are the single-linkage clusters at scale `r` (Carlsson and
Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Example 1; Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2).
