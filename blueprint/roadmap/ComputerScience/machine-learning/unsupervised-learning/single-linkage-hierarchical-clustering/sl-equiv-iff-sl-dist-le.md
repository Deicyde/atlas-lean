# The dendrogram is the family of closed balls of `u*`

**The dendrogram is the family of closed balls of `u*`.** On a finite set and for `r ≥ 0`,
`x` and `y` are `r`-equivalent if and only if `u*(x, y) ≤ r` (Carlsson and Mémoli, JMLR 11
(2010), Lemma 12 and Remark 13). Finiteness makes the infimum defining `u*` a minimum.
