# Nonnegative losses

**Nonnegative losses.** A finite sequence of loss vectors
$\ell_1, \dots, \ell_T \in \mathbb{R}^n$ on $n$ experts has nonnegative losses when
$\ell_t(i) \ge 0$ for every round $t$ and every expert $i$.
