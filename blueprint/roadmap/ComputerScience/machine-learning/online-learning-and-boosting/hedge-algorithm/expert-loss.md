# Total loss of an expert

**Total loss of an expert.** For an expert $i$ among $n$ experts and a finite sequence of loss
vectors $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_T \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the total loss of expert $i$ is
$\sum_{t=1}^T \ell_t(i)$.
