# Shattering

A class $H$ of functions $X \to \{0, 1\}$ shatters a finite set $A \subseteq X$ when every subset
$B \subseteq A$ is cut out of $A$ by some $h \in H$, that is, $h(x) = 1 \iff x \in B$ for all
$x \in A$; equivalently, all $2^{|A|}$ labellings of $A$ are restrictions of members of $H$.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Definition 6.3.
