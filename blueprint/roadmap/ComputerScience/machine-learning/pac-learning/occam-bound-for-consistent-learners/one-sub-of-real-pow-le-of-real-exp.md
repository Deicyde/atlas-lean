# The estimate $(1 - \varepsilon)^m \le e^{-\varepsilon m}$

For real $0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$ and every natural number $m$,
$(1 - \varepsilon)^m \le e^{-\varepsilon m}$, stated in $[0, \infty]$; it follows from
$1 - \varepsilon \le e^{-\varepsilon}$.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Section 2.3.1, the inequality $1 - \epsilon \le e^{-\epsilon}$ used in equation (2.9).
