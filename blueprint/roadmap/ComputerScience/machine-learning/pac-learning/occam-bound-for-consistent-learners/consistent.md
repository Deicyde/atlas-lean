# Consistency with a sample

A hypothesis $h$ is consistent with the target $c$ on a sample $S = (S_0, \dots, S_{m-1})$ of
instances when $h(S_i) = c(S_i)$ for every $i < m$, that is, when its empirical error on the
labelled sample $((S_i, c(S_i)))_{i<m}$ is zero.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Section 2.3.1.
