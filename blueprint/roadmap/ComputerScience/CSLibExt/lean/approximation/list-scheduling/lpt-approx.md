# Lpt approx

Graham (1969): LPT has makespan `C` with `3 * m * C ≤ (4 * m - 1) * M` for the makespan `M`
of every schedule `τ` of the same jobs, i.e. `C ≤ (4/3 - 1/(3m)) M`.
