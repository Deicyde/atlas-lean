# The mistake bound

**The mistake bound** (Littlestone and Warmuth): starting from unit weights with penalty
`β ∈ [0, 1]`, the weighted majority algorithm makes `M` mistakes with
`β ^ m * 2 ^ M ≤ n (1 + β) ^ M` for every expert with `m` mistakes.
