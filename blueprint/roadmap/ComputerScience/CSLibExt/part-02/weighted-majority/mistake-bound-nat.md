# The mistake bound in integers

**The mistake bound in integers**: `4 ^ M ≤ n 3 ^ M 2 ^ m`, i.e.
`M ≤ (m + log₂ n) / log₂ (4/3) ≈ 2.41 (m + log₂ n)`.
