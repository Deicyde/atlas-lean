# AdaBoost's hypothesis weight

**AdaBoost's hypothesis weight.** A weak hypothesis with weighted error
$\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$ gets the weight
$\alpha(\varepsilon) = \frac{1}{2} \log \frac{1 - \varepsilon}{\varepsilon}$.
