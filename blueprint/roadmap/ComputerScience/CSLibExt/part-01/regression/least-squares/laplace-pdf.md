# Laplace PDF

The density of the Laplace distribution with location `0` and scale `b`. As for Mathlib's
`gaussianPDFReal`, the scale is in `ℝ≥0`; `b = 0` gives the zero function, and the probabilistic
statements assume `b ≠ 0`.
