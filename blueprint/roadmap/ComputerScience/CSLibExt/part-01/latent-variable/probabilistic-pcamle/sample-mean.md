# Sample mean

**Sample mean.** For data points $x_1, \dots, x_N \in \mathbb{R}^d$, the sample mean is
$\bar x = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} x_n \in \mathbb{R}^d$.

When $N = 0$ the sample mean is $0$.
