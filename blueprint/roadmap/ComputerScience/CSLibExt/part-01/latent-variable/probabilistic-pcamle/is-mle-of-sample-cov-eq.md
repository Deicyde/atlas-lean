# Exact recovery

**Exact recovery**: if the sample covariance has the probabilistic PCA form `W₀ W₀ᵀ + σ₀² I`
with `σ₀² > 0`, then `(x̄, W₀, σ₀²)` is a maximum-likelihood estimate.
