# Conjugacy of the beta density

**Conjugacy of the beta density**: multiplying the `Beta(a, b)` density by `θ ^ k (1 - θ) ^ m`
gives `B(a + k, b + m) / B(a, b)` times the `Beta(a + k, b + m)` density.
