# Probability vectors

**Probability vectors.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the probability vectors are the
$\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ with $\theta_i \ge 0$ for every $i$ and $\sum_i \theta_i = 1$; they
form the parameter space of the categorical model.
