# Schnorr prover's response

**Schnorr prover's response.** Let $q$ be a natural number. For a secret key $x$, prover
randomness $k$ and challenge $r$, all in $\mathbb{Z}/q$, the prover's answer is
$s = rx + k \in \mathbb{Z}/q$.
