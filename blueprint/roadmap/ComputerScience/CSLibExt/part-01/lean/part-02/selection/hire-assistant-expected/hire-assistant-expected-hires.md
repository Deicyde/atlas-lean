# Hire Assistant expected Hires

The expected number of hires of `hireAssistant` under a uniformly
random permutation of a duplicate-free input is the harmonic sum
`∑ i, 1 / (i + 1)`, valued in `ℝ≥0∞` (CLRS Section 5.2).
