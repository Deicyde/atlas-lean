# Half-cleaner

**Half-cleaner.** For natural numbers $m$ and $b$, the half-cleaner on the block of wires
$[b, b + 2m)$ is the list of comparators $(b + i, b + m + i)$ for $i = 0, 1, \dots, m - 1$, in
this order: it compares wire $b + i$ with wire $b + m + i$.
