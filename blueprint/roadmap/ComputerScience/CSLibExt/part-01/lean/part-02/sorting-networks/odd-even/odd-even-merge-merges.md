# The odd-even merger merges

**The odd-even merger merges**: if wires `[0, 2 ^ k)` hold `a` zeros then ones, and wires
`[2 ^ k, 2 ^ (k + 1))` hold `c` zeros then ones, the merger leaves `a + c` zeros then ones.
