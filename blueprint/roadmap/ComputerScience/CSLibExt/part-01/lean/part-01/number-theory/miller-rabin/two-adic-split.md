# Splitting off the power of two

**Splitting off the power of two.** For a natural number $m > 0$, the two-adic split of $m$
is the pair $(t, u)$ of natural numbers with $m = 2^t u$ and $u$ odd. For $m = 0$ it is
$(0, 0)$.
