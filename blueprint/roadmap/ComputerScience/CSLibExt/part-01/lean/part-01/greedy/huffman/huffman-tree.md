# Huffman tree

**Huffman tree.** A Huffman tree over an alphabet $\alpha$ is either a leaf, which carries a
symbol $a \in \alpha$ and a natural-number weight $w$, or a fork, which joins a left and a right
Huffman tree over $\alpha$.
