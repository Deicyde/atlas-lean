# Pairs of suffixes

**Pairs of suffixes.** For lists $xs$ and $ys$, the list of all pairs $(u, v)$ where $u$ is
a suffix of $xs$ and $v$ is a suffix of $ys$, the empty lists and $xs$, $ys$ themselves
included.
