# Total weight of a knapsack selection

**Total weight of a knapsack selection.** For knapsack items $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$ with weights
$w_i$ and a set $S \subseteq \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ of item indices, the total weight of $S$ is
$\sum_{i \in S} w_i$.
