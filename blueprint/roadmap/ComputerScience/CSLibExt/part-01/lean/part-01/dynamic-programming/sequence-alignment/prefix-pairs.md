# Pairs of prefixes

**Pairs of prefixes.** For lists $xs$ and $ys$, the list of all pairs $(p, q)$ where $p$ is
a prefix of $xs$ and $q$ is a prefix of $ys$, the empty lists and $xs$, $ys$ themselves
included.
