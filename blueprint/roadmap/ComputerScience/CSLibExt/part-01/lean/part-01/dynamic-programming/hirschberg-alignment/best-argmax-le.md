# The argmax over $\{0, \dots, n\}$ is at most $n$

**The argmax over $\{0, \dots, n\}$ is at most $n$.** For every function
$f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the argmax of $f$ over
$\{0, \dots, n\}$ is at most $n$.
