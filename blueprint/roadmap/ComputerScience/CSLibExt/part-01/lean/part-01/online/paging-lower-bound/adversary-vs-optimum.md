# The adversary forces `n` misses while the optimum misses at most `k + ⌈n / k⌉` times

**The adversary forces `n` misses while the optimum misses at most `k + ⌈n / k⌉` times**:
for every deterministic online pager with cache size `k ≥ 1` on `k + 1` pages and every `n`.
