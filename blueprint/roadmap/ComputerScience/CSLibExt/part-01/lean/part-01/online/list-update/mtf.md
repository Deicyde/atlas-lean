# Move-to-front

**Move-to-front.** For a list $L$ and an item $x$, moving $x$ to the front gives the list
consisting of $x$ followed by $L$ with its first occurrence of $x$ removed; if $x$ does not
occur in $L$, this is $x$ followed by $L$.
