# Other endpoint of an edge

**Other endpoint of an edge.** For an edge $e = (a, b)$ and a vertex $v$, the other endpoint
of $e$ at $v$ is $b$ if $a = v$, and $a$ otherwise; in particular a loop $(v, v)$ gives $v$.
