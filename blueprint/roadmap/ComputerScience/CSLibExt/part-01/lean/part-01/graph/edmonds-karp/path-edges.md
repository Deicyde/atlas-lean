# Edges of a path

**Edges of a path.** The edges of a list $v_0, v_1, \dots, v_k$ are the list of its
consecutive ordered pairs $(v_0, v_1), (v_1, v_2), \dots, (v_{k-1}, v_k)$, in this order; the
empty list and one-element lists have no edges.
