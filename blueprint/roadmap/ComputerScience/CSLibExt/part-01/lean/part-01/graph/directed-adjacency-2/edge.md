# Edge of a directed adjacency graph

**Edge of a directed adjacency graph.** For a directed adjacency graph $G$ on $V$ and
vertices $s, t \in V$, there is an edge from $s$ to $t$ in $G$ when $t$ occurs in the adjacency
list of $s$.
