# Unordered edge of an ordered pair

**Unordered edge of an ordered pair.** The ordered pair $(a, b)$ of vertices, viewed as the
unordered pair $\{a, b\}$, so that $(a, b)$ and $(b, a)$ give the same unordered edge.
