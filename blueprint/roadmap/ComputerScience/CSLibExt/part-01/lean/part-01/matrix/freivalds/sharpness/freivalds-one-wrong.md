# The sharp Freivalds example has a wrong product

**The sharp Freivalds example has a wrong product.** Let $A = B = (1)$ and $C = (0)$ be
$1 \times 1$ matrices over $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$. Then $AB \neq C$.
