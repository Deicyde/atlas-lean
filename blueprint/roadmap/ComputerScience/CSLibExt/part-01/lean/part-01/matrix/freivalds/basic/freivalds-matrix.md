# Matrix of a Freivalds input

**Matrix of a Freivalds input.** An $n \times n$ array $A$ of rows over
$\mathbb{F}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2$ determines the $n \times n$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_2$ whose
$(i, j)$ entry is entry $j$ of row $i$ of $A$.
