# Values of opposite sign

**Values of opposite sign.** Two elements $d$ and $e$ of a field with a linear order straddle
zero when $d > 0$ and $e < 0$, or $d < 0$ and $e > 0$; that is, $d$ and $e$ are nonzero with
opposite signs.
