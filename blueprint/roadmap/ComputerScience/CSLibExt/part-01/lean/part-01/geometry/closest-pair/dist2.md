# Squared distance in the plane

**Squared distance in the plane.** Let $K$ be a linearly ordered field. The squared distance
between points $p = (p_1, p_2)$ and $q = (q_1, q_2)$ of $K^2$ is $(p_1 - q_1)^2 + (p_2 - q_2)^2$.
