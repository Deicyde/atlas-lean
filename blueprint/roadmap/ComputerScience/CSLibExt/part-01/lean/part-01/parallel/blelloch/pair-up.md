# Adjacent pair products of a sequence

**Adjacent pair products of a sequence.** For a sequence $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to M$ in a
monoid $M$, the sequence of adjacent pair products is the sequence whose term at $i$ is
$x(2i) \cdot x(2i+1)$.
