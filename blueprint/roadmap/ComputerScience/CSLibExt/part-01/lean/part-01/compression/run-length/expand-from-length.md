# Length of an expansion from a starting bit

**Length of an expansion from a starting bit.** For every bit $c$ and every list
$(n_1, \dots, n_k)$ of natural numbers, the expansion $E(c, (n_1, \dots, n_k))$ has length
$n_1 + \dots + n_k$.
