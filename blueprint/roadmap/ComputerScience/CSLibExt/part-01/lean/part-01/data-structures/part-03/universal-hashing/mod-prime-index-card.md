# Number of mod-prime indices

**Number of mod-prime indices.** For every positive natural number $p$, the set of pairs
$(a, b)$ of elements of $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ with $a \neq 0$ has exactly $p(p - 1)$
elements.
