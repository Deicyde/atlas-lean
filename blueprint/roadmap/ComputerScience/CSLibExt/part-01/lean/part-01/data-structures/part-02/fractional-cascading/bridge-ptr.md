# Bridge pointer

**Bridge pointer.** For a catalog $L$ and a value $v$, the bridge pointer of $v$ into $L$ is
the number of elements of $L$ that are $\le v$.

When $L$ is sorted, this is the index just past the elements of $L$ that are $\le v$.
