# Timed enqueue

**Timed enqueue.** Enqueueing a value $v$ onto a two-list queue $q$ returns the queue
obtained from $q$ by adding $v$ at the back, that is, by prepending $v$ to the enqueue list,
together with a recorded cost of $1$.
