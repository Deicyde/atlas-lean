# Collision count

**Collision count.** For a finite family $H$ of functions from a set $\alpha$ to
$\{0, 1, \dots, m - 1\}$ and keys $x, y \in \alpha$, the collision count of $x$ and $y$ in $H$
is the number of functions $h \in H$ with $h(x) = h(y)$.
