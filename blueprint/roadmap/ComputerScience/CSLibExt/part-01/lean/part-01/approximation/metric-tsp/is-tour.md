# Tour of a vertex list

**Tour of a vertex list.** A list $H$ is a tour of a list $V$ of vertices when the vertices of
$V$ are distinct and $H$ is a permutation of $V$: $H$ lists each vertex of $V$ exactly once and
nothing else.
