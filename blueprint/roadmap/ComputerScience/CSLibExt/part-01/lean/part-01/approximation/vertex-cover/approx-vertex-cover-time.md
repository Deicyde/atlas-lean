# Running time of APPROX-VERTEX-COVER

**Running time of APPROX-VERTEX-COVER.** For every list $L$ of ordered pairs of vertices, the
cost of APPROX-VERTEX-COVER on $L$ is the length of $L$: one unit per listed pair.
