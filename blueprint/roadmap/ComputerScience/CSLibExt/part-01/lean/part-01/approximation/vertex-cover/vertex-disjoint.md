# Vertex-disjoint pairs

**Vertex-disjoint pairs.** Two pairs $(a, b)$ and $(c, d)$ of vertices are vertex-disjoint
when they share no endpoint: $a \ne c$, $a \ne d$, $b \ne c$ and $b \ne d$.
