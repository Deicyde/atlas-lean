# RSA key

**RSA key.** A textbook RSA key consists of distinct primes $p$ and $q$ and natural numbers $e$
(the public exponent) and $d$ (the private exponent) such that $ed \equiv 1 \pmod{p - 1}$ and
$ed \equiv 1 \pmod{q - 1}$.
