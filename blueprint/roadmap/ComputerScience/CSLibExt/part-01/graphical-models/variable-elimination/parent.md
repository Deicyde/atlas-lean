# Parent of a vertex

**Parent of a vertex.** In a pairwise tree model on the vertices $0, \dots, n$, for $k < n$ the
parent of vertex $k + 1$ is the parent recorded by the model, an element of $\{0, \dots, k\}$,
regarded as one of the vertices $0, \dots, n$.
