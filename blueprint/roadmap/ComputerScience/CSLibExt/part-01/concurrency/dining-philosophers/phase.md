# Phase of a philosopher

**Phase of a philosopher.** For a state $s = (s_0, s_1, s_2)$ of the three dining
philosophers and a philosopher $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, the phase of philosopher $i$ in $s$ is the
component $s_i$.
