# Shifted sequence

**Shifted sequence.** For a sequence $s_0, s_1, s_2, \dots$ and a natural number $k$, the shift
of the sequence by $k$ is the sequence whose $i$-th term is $s_{i+k}$; that is, the suffix
starting at index $k$, re-indexed from $0$.
