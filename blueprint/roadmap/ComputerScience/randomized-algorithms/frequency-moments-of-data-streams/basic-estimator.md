# The AMS basic estimator of `F_k`

For a stream `l` of length `m` and a position `p`, the
value `X = m (r^k - (r - 1)^k)` with `r` the suffix count of `p` (N. Alon, Y. Matias and
M. Szegedy, J. Comput. System Sci. 58 (1999), proof of Theorem 2.1).

## Background

- [Suffix count of a stream position](suffix-count.md): the quantity $r$.
