# Pasting schedules of the components of a composition

A finite sequence $\beta$ of actions of a composition whose projections onto the two components are schedules of the components is a schedule of the composition (Lynch and Tuttle, 1989, Proposition 3, for two automata and finite schedules). With [the projection of schedules](sched-proj-left.md) this characterizes the finite schedules of a composition by those of its components.

## Informally uses

- [Pasting executions of the components of a composition](m-tr-of-proj.md): the executions behind the two schedules are pasted along $\beta$.
- [Actions of a composed signature](acts-compose.md): the actions of the composition are those of the components.
