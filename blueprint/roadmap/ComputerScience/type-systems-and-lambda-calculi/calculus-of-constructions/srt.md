# Sorts of CCω

**Sorts of CCω.** The sorts are $\mathrm{Prop}$ and $\mathrm{Type}_i$ for every natural number
$i$. In the calculus, $\mathrm{Prop}$ is impredicative and the $\mathrm{Type}_i$ form a
predicative hierarchy.
