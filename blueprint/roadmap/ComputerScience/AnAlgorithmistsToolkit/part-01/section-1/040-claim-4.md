# Claim 4 (Section 1.1 (A First Bound), Lecture 6)

For any $G$, $\delta \leq \frac{\ln(n)}{\lambda}$ where $\delta$ is the diameter of $G$, $n$ is the number of vertices in the graph, and $\lambda = 1 - \mu_2$, where $\mu_2$ is the second largest eigenvalue of the $M$ associated with $G$.
