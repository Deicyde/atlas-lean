# Definition 5 (Lecture 11, Section 4)

The Minkowski functional of an origin symmetric convex body $C$ is the map $p_C : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $p_C(x) = \inf\{\lambda > 0 \mid x \in \lambda C\}$.
