# Theorem 5 [2]

Let $p_0$ be an arbitrary initial distribution, and $p_t$ be the distribution after $t$ steps of the lazy random walk. Then, $\|p_t - \pi\|_2 \leq (1 - \lambda)^t \cdot \sqrt{\frac{\max_x d(x)}{\min_y d(y)}}$.
