# Soundness of full β-reduction in the graph model

One step of full β-reduction, contracting a redex anywhere in a term, including under λ, preserves the denotation in $P(\omega)$ under every valuation of free and bound variables. This is the content of Scott's conversion theorem for the rules β and ξ (D. Scott, *Data types as lattices*, SIAM J. Comput. 5(3) (1976), 522–587, Theorem 2.2): ξ is the rule that reduction under λ preserves meaning. The rule α is built into the locally nameless syntax.
