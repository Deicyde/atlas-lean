# Monotonicity of two-stage experiments

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability distributions and $f$, $g$ tests on pairs $(a, b)$. If, for every
$a$ in the support of $\mu$ and every $b$, $f(a, b) = 1$ implies $g(a, b) = 1$, then for $a$ drawn from
$\mu$ and $b$ drawn independently from $\nu$, $\Pr[f(a, b) = 1] \le \Pr[g(a, b) = 1]$.
