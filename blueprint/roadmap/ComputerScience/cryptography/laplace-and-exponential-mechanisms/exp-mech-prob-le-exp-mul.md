# Output probabilities on neighbouring databases

If $\varepsilon \ge 0$, $\Delta > 0$ and $|u(x, r) - u(y, r)| \le \Delta$ for all $r$, then
$p_x(r) \le e^{\varepsilon} p_y(r)$ for every output $r$: the weight of $r$ changes by a factor at
most $e^{\varepsilon/2}$, and so does the normalising sum.
