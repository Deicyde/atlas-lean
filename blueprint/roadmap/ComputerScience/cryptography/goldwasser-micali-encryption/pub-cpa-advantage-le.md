# Residuosity bounds transfer to Goldwasser–Micali

If the [quadratic-residuosity advantage](qr-advantage.md) of the [reduction](reduction.md) built from
an eavesdropper $A$ is at most $\varepsilon$, then the eavesdropping advantage of $A$ against the
[one-bit scheme](scheme.md) is at most $\varepsilon / 2$.
