# Encryption from a hard-core predicate is semantically secure

Under the hypothesis of the [previous result](ind-secure-of-predict-le.md), the [scheme](scheme.md) is
[semantically secure with error $2\varepsilon$](../semantic-security-and-indistinguishability/semantically-secure.md), for any types of a priori
information and of guesses.
