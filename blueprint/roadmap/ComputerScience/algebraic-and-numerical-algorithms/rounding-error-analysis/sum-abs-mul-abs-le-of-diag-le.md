# No element growth, max form

If $A = L L^T$ with $L$ real and every diagonal entry of $A$ is at most $c$, then every entry of $|L|\,|L^T|$ is at most $c$. In particular $\max_{ij} (|L|\,|L^T|)_{ij} \le \max_k A_{kk} = \max_{ij} |A_{ij}|$ for a symmetric positive semidefinite $A$.
