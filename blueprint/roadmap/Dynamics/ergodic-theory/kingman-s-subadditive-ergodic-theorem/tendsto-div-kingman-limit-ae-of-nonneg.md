# Kingman's subadditive ergodic theorem

**Kingman's subadditive ergodic theorem** for nonnegative cocycles: `X n / n` converges almost
everywhere to `kingmanLimit T X` (J. F. C. Kingman, 1968; proof of J. M. Steele, 1989).
