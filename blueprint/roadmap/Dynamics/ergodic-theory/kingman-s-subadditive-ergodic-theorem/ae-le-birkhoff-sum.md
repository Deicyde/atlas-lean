# Subadditive cocycle bounded by Birkhoff sums of its first term

**Subadditive cocycle bounded by Birkhoff sums of its first term.** Iterating subadditivity: `X n ≤ ∑_{k<n} X 1 ∘ T^[k]` almost everywhere, for all `n ≥ 1`.
