# Kolmogorov's non-isomorphism of Bernoulli schemes

The Bernoulli schemes with probability vectors $(\tfrac13, \tfrac13, \tfrac13)$ and $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$
are not measure-theoretically isomorphic: no measurable bijection with measurable inverse carries
$\bigl(\tfrac13, \tfrac13, \tfrac13\bigr)^{\mathbb{N}}$ to $\bigl(\tfrac12, \tfrac12\bigr)^{\mathbb{N}}$ and
conjugates the two shifts. Their entropies are $\log 3$ and $\log 2$.

Before entropy, all Bernoulli schemes were known to be spectrally isomorphic, and whether they are all
isomorphic was open; entropy settled the question. Ornstein later showed that entropy is a complete
invariant among Bernoulli schemes. The statement is wanted.
