# Sinai's generator theorem

Let `T` be an invertible measure-preserving transformation of
a probability space `(α, μ)` and let `ξ = {C₁, …, C_r}` be a finite measurable partition which is
a generator: the σ-algebra generated by all `Tⁿ Cⱼ`, `n ∈ ℤ`, is the whole σ-algebra modulo
`μ`. Then `h_μ(T) = h_μ(T, ξ)` (Ya. G. Sinai, *Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy*, Scholarpedia 4(3):2034,
Definition 2 and Theorem 1, proved in Sinai, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 124 (1959); O. Sarig,
*Lecture Notes on Ergodic Theory*, Definition 4.4 and Theorem 4.4, where finite partitions have
finite entropy). The integer powers are written as forward iterates of `T` and of `T.symm`.
