# Entropy of a Bernoulli shift

The Bernoulli scheme on a finite alphabet `S` with
probability vector `p` has Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy `-∑_{s ∈ S} p(s) log p(s)`
(O. Sarig, *Lecture Notes on Ergodic Theory*, Proposition 4.6, where `log = log₂`; with the
natural logarithm of `partitionEntropy` both sides are rescaled by the same factor). The
computation goes through the generator theorem applied to the partition into 1-cylinders.
