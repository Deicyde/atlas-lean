# Birkhoff limit of an ergodic map is the space average

For ergodic `T` on a probability space the Birkhoff limit equals the constant `∫ f dμ` almost everywhere; this is the
[Birkhoff pointwise ergodic theorem (ergodic case)](birkhoff-pointwise-ergodic.md) combined with
[Birkhoff averages converge almost everywhere to the Birkhoff limit](tendsto-birkhoff-average-ae.md).
