# Diophantine number

**Diophantine number.** A real number $\alpha$ is Diophantine (with exponent $2$, as used in
KAM theory) if there is $C > 0$ such that $C / q^2 \le |\alpha - p/q|$ for all integers $p$ and
$q$ with $q \neq 0$.
