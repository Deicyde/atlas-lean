# Moebius dual endpoint ne neg one

Lemma 2: the endpoint `η = -1` is impossible: for `α > 0`, `β < 1`, `δ ≥ 0`,
`V^#_{βδ}(-1) ≠ V^#_{αδ}(-1)` (the equation would force `δ = -1`).
