# Torus

**Torus.** For a natural number $d$, the $d$-dimensional torus is
$\mathbb{T}^d = (\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^d$, the product of $d$ copies of the circle
$\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$.
