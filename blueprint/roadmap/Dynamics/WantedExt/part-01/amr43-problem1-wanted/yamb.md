# The full 3-shift

**The full 3-shift.** The space of all two-sided sequences $y = (y_i)_{i \in \mathbb{Z}}$
with every $y_i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, carrying the product topology. It is the ambient space of the
proper 3-colouring shift.
