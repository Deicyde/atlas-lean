# Unit shift

**Unit shift.** The unit shift on the full shift on $N$ symbols is the map $\sigma$ from
$\{0, \dots, N-1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$ to itself given by $(\sigma x)_i = x_{i+1}$ for all
$i \in \mathbb{Z}$, the left translation by one step.
