# Forward orbit

**Forward orbit.** For a self-map $f$ of a set $X$ and a point $x \in X$, the forward orbit
of $x$ under $f$ is the set $\{f^n(x) : n \in \mathbb{N}\}$ of all iterates of $x$, including
$f^0(x) = x$.
