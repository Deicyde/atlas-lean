# Grand orbit relation

**Grand orbit relation.** For a self-map $f$ of a set $X$, points $x, y \in X$ are related,
that is, lie in the same grand orbit of $f$, when $f^m(x) = f^n(y)$ for some natural numbers
$m$ and $n$.
