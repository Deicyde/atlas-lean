# Theorem 5.10

(a) Fix $m \geq 1$. Let $p_{i}$ be the number of nonisomorphic simple graphs with $m$ vertices and $i$ edges. Then the sequence $p_{0}, p_{1}, \ldots, p_{\binom{m}{2}}$ is symmetric and unimodal. (b) Let $T$ be a collection of nonisomorphic simple graphs with $m$ vertices such that no element of $T$ is isomorphic to a subset of another element of $T$. Then $|T|$ is maximized by taking $T$ to consist of all nonisomorphic simple graphs with $\left\lfloor\frac{1}{2}\binom{m}{2}\right\rfloor$ edges.
