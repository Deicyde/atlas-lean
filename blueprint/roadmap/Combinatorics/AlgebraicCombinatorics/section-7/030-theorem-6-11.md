# Theorem 6.11

Let $S \in \binom{\mathbb{R}^{+}}{n}$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}^{+}$, and $k \in \mathbb{P}$. Then $f_{k}(S, \alpha) \leq f_{k}([n], \lfloor k(n+1) / 2\rfloor)$.
