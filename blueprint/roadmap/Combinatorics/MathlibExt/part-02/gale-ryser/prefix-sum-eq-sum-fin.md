# Prefix sums within the sequence length

**Prefix sums within the sequence length.** For a sequence $r = (r_0, \dots, r_{m-1})$ of
natural numbers and a natural number $k \le m$, the prefix sum of $r$ of length $k$ equals
$\sum_{i=0}^{k-1} r_i$.
