# The $p^r$-kernel lies in the $p$-kernel

**The $p^r$-kernel lies in the $p$-kernel.** For all natural numbers $p$ and $r$ and every
sequence $a$ indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, the $p^r$-kernel of $a$ is contained in the $p$-kernel of
$a$.
