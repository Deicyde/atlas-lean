# Circular Eulerian small

Characteristic values `C_{0,0} = 0`, `C_{1,0} = 1` and `C_{3,1} = C_{3,2} = 1`:
of the two 3-cycles, `1 ↦ 3 ↦ 2 ↦ 1` has one excedance and `1 ↦ 2 ↦ 3 ↦ 1` has two.
