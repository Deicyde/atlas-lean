# Modular valuation

**Modular valuation.** A function $f \colon L \to \mathbb{R}$ on a lattice $L$, with join
$\vee$ and meet $\wedge$, is a modular valuation when
$f(X) + f(Y) = f(X \vee Y) + f(X \wedge Y)$ for all $X, Y \in L$.
