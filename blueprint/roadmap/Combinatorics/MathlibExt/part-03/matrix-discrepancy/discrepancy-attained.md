# The discrepancy is attained

**The discrepancy is attained.** For every real $m \times n$ matrix $A$, including the cases
$m = 0$ and $n = 0$, there is a sign vector $x \in \{-1, 1\}^n$ such that the discrepancy of $A$
equals $\|Ax\|_\infty$.
