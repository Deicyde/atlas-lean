# $3$-abelian squares over a ternary alphabet

**$3$-abelian squares over a ternary alphabet.** A word $w$ over the alphabet $\{0, 1, 2\}$ is
a $3$-abelian square when $w = uv$ for two nonempty words $u$ and $v$ that are $3$-abelian
equivalent.
