# Is Self Conjugate

Let λ=[λ₁,…,λ_L]
be a partition, where L is an integer
with L≥ 0 and
λ₁≥⋯≥λ_L≥ 1,
and let λ' be its conjugate
partition. Then λ is
self-conjugate when

λ'=λ.

Equivalently, the Young diagram of
λ is equal to its own transpose.
The empty partition is self-conjugate.
