# Turán systems cover every $s$-set

**Turán systems cover every $s$-set.** Let $n$, $s$, $r$ be natural numbers and let $H$ be a
Turán $(n, s, r)$-system. Then every $s$-element subset $T$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ contains
some member $e$ of $H$.
