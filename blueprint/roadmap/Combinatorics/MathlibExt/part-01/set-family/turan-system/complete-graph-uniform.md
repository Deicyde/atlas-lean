# The complete $r$-graph is $r$-uniform

**The complete $r$-graph is $r$-uniform.** For all natural numbers $n$ and $r$, every member
of the complete $r$-graph on $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ has exactly $r$ elements.
