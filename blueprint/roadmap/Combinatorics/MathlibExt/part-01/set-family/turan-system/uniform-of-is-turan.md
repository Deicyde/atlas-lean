# Turán systems are uniform

**Turán systems are uniform.** Let $n$, $s$, $r$ be natural numbers. If $H$ is a Turán system
with parameters $n$, $s$, $r$, then $H$ is $r$-uniform: every member of $H$ has exactly $r$
elements.
