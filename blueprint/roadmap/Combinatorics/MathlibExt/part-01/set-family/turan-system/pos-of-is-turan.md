# Turán systems have positive uniformity

**Turán systems have positive uniformity.** Let $n$, $s$, $r$ be natural numbers and let $H$
be a Turán system with parameters $n$, $s$, $r$, whose definition includes $0 < r < s \le n$.
Then $0 < r$.
