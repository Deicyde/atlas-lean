# Uniform hypergraphs

**Uniform hypergraphs.** Let $H$ be a finite family of finite subsets of a type $V$, viewed as
a hypergraph with edge set $H$, and let $r \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $H$ is $r$-uniform when every
edge $e \in H$ has exactly $r$ elements.
