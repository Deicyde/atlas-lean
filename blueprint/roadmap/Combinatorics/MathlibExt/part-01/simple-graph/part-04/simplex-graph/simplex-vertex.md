# Simplex vertices

**Simplex vertices.** Let $G$ be a simple graph on a vertex type $V$. A simplex vertex
of $G$ is a finite set $s$ of vertices that is a clique of $G$, meaning that any two distinct
elements of $s$ are adjacent in $G$.
