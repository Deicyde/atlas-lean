# Vertex count of a $k$-vertex-connected graph

**Vertex count of a $k$-vertex-connected graph.** Let $k$ be a natural number. If a simple
graph $G$ on a finite vertex set $V$ is $k$-vertex-connected, then $V$ has more than $k$
elements.
