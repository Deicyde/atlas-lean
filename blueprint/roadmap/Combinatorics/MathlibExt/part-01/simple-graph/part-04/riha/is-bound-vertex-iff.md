# Characterization of bound vertices

**Characterization of bound vertices.** Let $c$ be a closed walk in a simple graph $G$. A
vertex $y$ is bound to $c$ if and only if $y$ lies on $c$ and every neighbor of $y$ in $G$ lies
on $c$.
