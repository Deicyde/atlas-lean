# Bound vertices of a closed walk

**Bound vertices of a closed walk.** Let $c$ be a closed walk in a simple graph $G$. A vertex
$y$ is bound to $c$ when $y$ lies on $c$ and every neighbor of $y$ in $G$ lies on $c$.
