# Dirac's theorem

**Dirac's theorem.** Let $G$ be a finite simple graph with $n \ge 3$ vertices in which every
vertex has degree at least $n/2$. Then $G$ is Hamiltonian, that is, it has a cycle that visits
every vertex exactly once.
