# $k$-degenerate graphs are $(k + 1)$-colorable

**$k$-degenerate graphs are $(k + 1)$-colorable.** Let $k$ be a natural number. Every
$k$-degenerate simple graph on a finite vertex set is $(k + 1)$-colorable.
