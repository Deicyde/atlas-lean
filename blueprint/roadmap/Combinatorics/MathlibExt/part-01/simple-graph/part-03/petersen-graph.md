# Petersen graph

**Petersen graph.** The Petersen graph is the generalized Johnson graph $J(5,2,0)$: its
vertices are the $2$-element subsets of $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$, and two of them are adjacent
exactly when they are disjoint.
