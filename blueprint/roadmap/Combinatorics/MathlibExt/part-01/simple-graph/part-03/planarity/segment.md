# Closed segment in the plane

**Closed segment in the plane.** For points $a$ and $b$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$, the closed segment
from $a$ to $b$ is the set of points $a + t(b - a)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$ and $0 \le t \le 1$.
