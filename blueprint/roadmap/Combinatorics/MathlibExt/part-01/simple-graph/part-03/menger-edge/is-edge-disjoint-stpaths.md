# Edge-disjoint paths

**Edge-disjoint paths.** Let $G$ be a simple graph with vertices $s$ and $t$. A family
$P_0, \dots, P_{n-1}$ of paths from $s$ to $t$ in $G$ is edge-disjoint when, for all indices
$i \ne j$, no edge of $P_i$ is an edge of $P_j$.
