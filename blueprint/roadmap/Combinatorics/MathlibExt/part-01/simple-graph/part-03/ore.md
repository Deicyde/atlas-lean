# Ore's theorem

**Ore's theorem.** Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$ with $|V| \ge 3$. If
$\deg u + \deg v \ge |V|$ for every pair of distinct nonadjacent vertices $u$ and $v$, then $G$
is Hamiltonian.
