# Balanced graph

**Balanced graph.** Let $G$ be a finite simple graph and $D$ a real number. The graph $G$ is
$D$-balanced if its maximum degree $\Delta(G)$ is at most $D$ times its minimum degree
$\delta(G)$, that is, $\Delta(G) \le D \cdot \delta(G)$.
