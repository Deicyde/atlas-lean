# Gaussian binomial coefficients with $k = 0$

**Gaussian binomial coefficients with $k = 0$.** Let $R$ be a semiring and $Q \in R$. For
every natural number $M$, the Gaussian binomial coefficient $\binom{M}{0}_Q$ equals $1$.
