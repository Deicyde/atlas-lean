# Diagonal Gaussian binomial coefficients

**Diagonal Gaussian binomial coefficients.** Let $R$ be a semiring and $Q \in R$. For every
natural number $M$, the Gaussian binomial coefficient $\binom{M}{M}_Q$ equals $1$.
