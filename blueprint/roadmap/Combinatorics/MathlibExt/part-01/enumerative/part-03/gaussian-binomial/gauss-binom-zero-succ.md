# Gaussian binomial coefficients with $M = 0$

**Gaussian binomial coefficients with $M = 0$.** Let $R$ be a semiring and $Q \in R$. For
every natural number $k$, the Gaussian binomial coefficient $\binom{0}{k+1}_Q$ equals $0$.
