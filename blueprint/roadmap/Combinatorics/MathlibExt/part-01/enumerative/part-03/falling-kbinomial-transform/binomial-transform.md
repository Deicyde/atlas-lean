# Binomial transform

**Binomial transform.** The binomial transform of an integer sequence $(a_i)_{i \ge 0}$ is the
falling k-binomial transform of $a$ with $k = 1$, the sequence whose $n$-th term is
$\sum_{i=0}^{n} \binom{n}{i} 1^{n-i} a_i$.
