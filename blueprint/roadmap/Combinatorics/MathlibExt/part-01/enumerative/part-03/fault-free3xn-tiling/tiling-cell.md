# Cells of the $3 \times n$ rectangle

**Cells of the $3 \times n$ rectangle.** A cell of the $3 \times n$ rectangle is a pair
$(r, c)$ of a row $r \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ and a column $c \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$.
