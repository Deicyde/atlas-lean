# Narayana's cows sequence

**Narayana's cows sequence.** The sequence $(N_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of natural numbers defined by
$N_0 = 0$, $N_1 = N_2 = 1$ and $N_n = N_{n-1} + N_{n-3}$ for $n \ge 3$.
