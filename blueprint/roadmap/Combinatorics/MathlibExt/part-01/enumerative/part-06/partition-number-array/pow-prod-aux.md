# Finite power product

**Finite power product.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and functions
$g, f \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, this is the product $\prod_{i=0}^{n-1} f(i)^{g(i)}$,
with the convention $0^0 = 1$.
