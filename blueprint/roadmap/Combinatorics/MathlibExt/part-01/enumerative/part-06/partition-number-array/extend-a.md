# Extension of a vector by zero

**Extension of a vector by zero.** A vector $(a_0, \dots, a_{n-1})$ of natural numbers
extends to the sequence $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ that sends $j$ to $a_j$ for $j < n$ and to $0$
for $j \ge n$.
