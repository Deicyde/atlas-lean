# Rising diagonal

**Rising diagonal.** For a real array $a = (a_{n,k})_{n,k \ge 0}$ and $n \ge 0$, the rising
diagonal of $a$ at level $n$ is the finite sequence $k \mapsto a_{n-k,k}$ indexed by
$0 \le k \le \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$.
