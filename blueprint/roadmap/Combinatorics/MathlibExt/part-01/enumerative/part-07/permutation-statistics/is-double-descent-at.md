# Double descent of a permutation

**Double descent of a permutation.** Let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$.
A position $i$ is a double descent of $\sigma$ if $i + 2 < n$ and
$\sigma(i) > \sigma(i+1) > \sigma(i+2)$.
