# Double ascent of a permutation

**Double ascent of a permutation.** Let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$.
A position $i$ is a double ascent of $\sigma$ if $i + 2 < n$ and
$\sigma(i) < \sigma(i+1) < \sigma(i+2)$.
