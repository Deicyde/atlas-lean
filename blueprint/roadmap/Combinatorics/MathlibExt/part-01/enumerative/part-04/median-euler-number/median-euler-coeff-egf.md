# Median Euler Coeff egf

Exponential-generating-function identity: `(exp x + exp (-x))`, that is `2 cosh x`,
times the EGF of `medianEulerCoeff` equals `2 + exp x - exp (-x)`, the `sech x + tanh x`
series.
