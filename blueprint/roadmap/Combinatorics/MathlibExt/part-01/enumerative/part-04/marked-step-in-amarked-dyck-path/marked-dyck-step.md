# Steps of a marked Dyck path

**Steps of a marked Dyck path.** A step of a marked Dyck path is one of three kinds: a
horizontal step $E = (1, 0)$, an unmarked vertical step $N = (0, 1)$, or a marked vertical step
$N^* = (0, 1)$.
