# Signed Robbins numbers

**Signed Robbins numbers.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the signed Robbins number is the integer
$(-1)^{\binom{n+1}{2}} A_{n+1}$, where $A_{n+1}$ is the Robbins number.
