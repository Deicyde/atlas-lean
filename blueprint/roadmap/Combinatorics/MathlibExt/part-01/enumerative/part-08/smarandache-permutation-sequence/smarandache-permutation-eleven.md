# Twelfth term of the Smarandache permutation sequence

**Twelfth term of the Smarandache permutation sequence.** Indexing the Smarandache permutation
sequence $(a_i)_{i \ge 0}$ from $0$, its twelfth term is $a_{11} = 2$.
