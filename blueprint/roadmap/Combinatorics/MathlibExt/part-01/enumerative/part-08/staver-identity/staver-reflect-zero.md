# Reflection of the alternating reciprocal binomial sum

**Reflection of the alternating reciprocal binomial sum.** For every natural number $n$,
$\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} \binom{n}{k}^{-1} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{k} \binom{n}{k}^{-1}$
in $\mathbb{Q}$.
