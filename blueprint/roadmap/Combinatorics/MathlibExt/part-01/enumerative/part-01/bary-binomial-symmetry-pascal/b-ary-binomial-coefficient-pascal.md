# B Ary Binomial Coefficient pascal

The `b`-ary binomial coefficients satisfy Pascal's recurrence where they are nonzero:
`C_b(n, k) = C_b(n - 1, k - 1) + C_b(n - 1, k)` for `0 < k ≤ n` and `C_b(n, k) ≠ 0`.
