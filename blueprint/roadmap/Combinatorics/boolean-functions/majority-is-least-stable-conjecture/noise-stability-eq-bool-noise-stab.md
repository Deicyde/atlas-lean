# The two noise-stability definitions agree

For every $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and every real $\rho$, the noise stability of the Majority
Is Least Stable module, defined by the Fourier sum $\sum_S \rho^{|S|} \hat f(S)^2$, equals the
correlation $\mathbb{E}[f \cdot T_\rho f]$ of
[noise stability on the Boolean cube](../../../Analysis/harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/influences-and-the-noise-operator-on-the-boolean/bool-noise-stab.md).
The proof is the [Fourier formula for noise stability](../../../Analysis/harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/influences-and-the-noise-operator-on-the-boolean/bool-noise-stab-eq-sum.md).
