# The standard cube in $\mathbb{F}_3^n$

**The standard cube in $\mathbb{F}_3^n$.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the standard cube is the set
$\{0, 1\}^n \subseteq \mathbb{F}_3^n$ of vectors each of whose coordinates is $0$ or $1$.
