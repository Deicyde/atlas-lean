# The $U^1$ norm is the modulus of the mean

For every $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, $\|f\|_{U^1} = |\mathbb{E} f|$. In particular $U^1$ vanishes on every function of mean zero, so it is a seminorm and not a norm; Green and Tao call the cases $d = 0, 1$ degenerate.
