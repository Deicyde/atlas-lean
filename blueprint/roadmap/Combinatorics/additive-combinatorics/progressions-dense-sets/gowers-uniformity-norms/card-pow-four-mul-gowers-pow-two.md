# Fourth moment of the Fourier transform

For every $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, with the unnormalised [discrete Fourier transform](../../../../Analysis/harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/fourier-transform-on-finite-abelian-groups/dft-fun.md),
$$|G|^4\,\|f\|_{U^2}^4 = \sum_{\psi} |\hat f(\psi)|^4.$$
On $\mathbb{Z}_N$ with Gowers's unnormalised transform this is identity (6) of his §2: $\sum_r |\hat A(r)|^4$ is $N$ times the number of additive quadruples in $A$. The normalised form is [$\|f\|_{U^2} = \|\hat f\|_{\ell^4}$](gowers-norm-two-eq.md).

## Sources

- W. T. Gowers, "A new proof of Szemerédi's theorem", Geom. Funct. Anal. 11 (2001), 465–588; numbering of the author's preprint `sz898.dvi` at https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~wtg10/papers.html (journal numbering not checked), §2, identity (6) and the remark after Lemma 2.1.
