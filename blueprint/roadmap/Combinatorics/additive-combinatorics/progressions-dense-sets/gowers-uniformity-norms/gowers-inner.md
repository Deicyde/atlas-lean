# Gowers inner product

For functions $F_\omega : G \to \mathbb{C}$ indexed by $\omega \in \{0,1\}^d$,
$$\langle (F_\omega)_\omega \rangle_{U^d} = \mathbb{E}_{x \in G,\ h \in G^d} \prod_{\omega} C^{|\omega|} F_\omega(x + \omega \cdot h),$$
the left-hand side of Gowers's Lemma 3.8, with averages in place of sums and $+$ in place of $-$ in the shifts. When every $F_\omega$ equals $f$ it is $\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}$, by the [cube formula](gowers-pow-eq-cube-average.md). For $d = 2$ it is the [$U^2$ inner product](gowers-inner-two.md) up to the indexing of $\{0,1\}^2$.
