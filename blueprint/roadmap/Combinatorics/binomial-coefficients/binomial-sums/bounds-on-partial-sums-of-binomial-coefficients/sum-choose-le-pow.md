# Number of subsets of size at most $r$ is at most $(3D/r)^r$

**Number of subsets of size at most $r$ is at most $(3D/r)^r$.** For `1 ≤ r ≤ D`, the number of subsets of size at most `r` is at most `(3 * D / r) ^ r`.
