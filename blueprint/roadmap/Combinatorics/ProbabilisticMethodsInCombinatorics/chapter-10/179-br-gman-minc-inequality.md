# Theorem 10.2.1 (Brégman–Minc inequality)

Let $A = (a_{ij}) \in \{0,1\}^{n \times n}$, whose $i$-th row has sum $d_i$. Then $\operatorname{per} A \leq \prod_{i=1}^{n} (d_i!)^{1/d_i}$.
