# Theorem 4.1.5 (Chebyshev's inequality)

Let $X$ be a random variable with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. For any $\lambda > 0$, $\mathbb{P}(|X - \mu| \geq \lambda\sigma) \leq \lambda^{-2}$.
