# Theorem 6.2.4

Let $H$ be a (non-uniform) hypergraph where every edge has size $\geq 3$. Suppose $\sum_{f \in E(H) \setminus \{e\} \colon e \cap f \neq \emptyset} 2^{-|f|} \leq \frac{1}{8}$ for each edge $e$, then $H$ is $2$-colorable.
