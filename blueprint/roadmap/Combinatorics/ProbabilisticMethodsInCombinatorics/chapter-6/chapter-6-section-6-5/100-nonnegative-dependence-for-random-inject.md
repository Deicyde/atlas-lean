# Theorem 6.5.5 (Nonnegative dependence for random injections)

In Setup 6.5.4, let $F_0$ be a matching in $K_{X,Y}$ such that $F_0$ is vertex disjoint from $F_1 \cup \cdots \cup F_k$. Then $\mathbb{P}(A_{F_0} \mid \overline{A_{F_1}} \cdots \overline{A_{F_k}}) \leq \mathbb{P}(A_{F_0})$.
