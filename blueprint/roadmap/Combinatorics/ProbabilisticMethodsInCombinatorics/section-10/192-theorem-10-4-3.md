# Theorem 10.4.3

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ be a finite set, and $\pi_{xy}(S)$ be its projection on the $xy$-plane, etc. Then $|S|^2 \leq |\pi_{xy}(S)| \cdot |\pi_{xz}(S)| \cdot |\pi_{yz}(S)|$.
