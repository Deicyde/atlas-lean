# Corollary 9.4.16 (Concentration of measure for Gaussian vectors)

If $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ is $1$-Lipschitz, and $Z$ is a vector of i.i.d. standard normals, then $X = f(Z)$ satisfies, for some $m$, $\mathbb{P}(|X - m| \geq t) \leq 2e^{-t^2/2}$.
