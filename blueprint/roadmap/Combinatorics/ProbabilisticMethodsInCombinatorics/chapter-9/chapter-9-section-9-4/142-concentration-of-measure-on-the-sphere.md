# Corollary 9.4.12 (Concentration of measure on the sphere)

Let $A$ be a measurable subset of the unit sphere in $\mathbb{R}^n$, equipped with the metric inherited from $\mathbb{R}^n$. If $A \subseteq S^{n-1}$ has $\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(A) / \operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(S^{n-1}) \geq 1/2$, then $\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(A_t) / \operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(S^{n-1}) \geq 1 - e^{-nt^2/4}$.
