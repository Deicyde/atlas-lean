# Theorem 9.4.5 (Rapid expansion from half to $1 - \varepsilon$)

Let $t > 0$. For every $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^n$ with $|A| \geq 2^{n-1}$, we have $|A_t| > (1 - e^{-2t^2/n}) 2^n$.
