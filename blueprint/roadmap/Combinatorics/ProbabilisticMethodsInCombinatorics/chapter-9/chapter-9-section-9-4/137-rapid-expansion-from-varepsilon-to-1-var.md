# Theorem 9.4.6 (Rapid expansion from $\varepsilon$ to $1 - \varepsilon$)

Let $\varepsilon > 0$ and $C = \sqrt{2\log(1/\varepsilon)}$. If $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^n$ with $|A| \geq \varepsilon \cdot 2^n$, then $|A_{C\sqrt{n}}| \geq (1-\varepsilon) 2^n$.
