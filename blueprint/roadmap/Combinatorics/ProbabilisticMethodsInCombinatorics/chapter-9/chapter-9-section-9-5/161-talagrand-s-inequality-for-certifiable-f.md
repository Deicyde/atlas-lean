# Theorem 9.5.21 (Talagrand's inequality for certifiable functions)

Let $\Omega = \Omega_1 \times \cdots \times \Omega_n$ be equipped with a product measure. Let $f \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be $1$-Lipschitz with respect to Hamming distance on $\Omega$. Suppose that $\{f \geq r\}$ is $s$-certifiable. Then, for every $t \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(f \leq r - t) \cdot \mathbb{P}(f \geq r) \leq e^{-t^2/(4s)}$.
