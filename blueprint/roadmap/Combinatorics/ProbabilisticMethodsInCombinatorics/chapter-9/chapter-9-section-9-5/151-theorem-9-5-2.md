# Theorem 9.5.2

Let $V$ be a fixed $d$-dimensional subspace in $\mathbb{R}^n$. For uniformly random $x \in \{-1, 1\}^n$, one has $\mathbb{P}(|\operatorname{dist}(x, V) - \sqrt{n - d}| \geq t) \leq Ce^{-ct^2}$, where $C, c > 0$ are some constants.
