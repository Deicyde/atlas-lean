# Corollary 9.5.23 (Longest increasing subsequence)

Let $X$ be the length of the longest increasing subsequence of a random permutation of $[n]$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists $C > 0$ so that $\mathbb{P}(|X - MX| \leq Cn^{1/4}) \geq 1 - \varepsilon$.
