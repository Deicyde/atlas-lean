# Theorem 9.6.3 (Rhee and Talagrand 1989)

$L_n$ is $O(1)$-subGaussian about its mean. That is, $\mathbb{P}(|L_n - \mathbb{E}L_n| \geq t) \leq e^{-ct^2}$ for all $t > 0$, where $c > 0$ is some constant.
