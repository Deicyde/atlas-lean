# Theorem 4.6.6 (Dubroff–Fox–Xu 2021)

If there is a $k$-element subset of $[n]$ with distinct subset sums, then $n \geq \binom{k}{\lfloor k/2 \rfloor} = \left(\sqrt{2/\pi} + o(1)\right) 2^k / \sqrt{k}$.
