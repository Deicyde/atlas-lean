# Theorem 4.4.2 (Second moment bound for clique number)

Let $k = k(n)$ be some sequence of positive integers. (a) If $f(n, k) \to 0$, then $\omega(G(n, 1/2)) < k$ whp. (b) If $f(n, k) \to \infty$, then $\omega(G(n, 1/2)) \geq k$ whp.
