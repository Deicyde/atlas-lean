# Ncard arithmetical Structure coconut Tree one leaf ne one

Proposition 5: for `p ≥ 1`, the number of arithmetical structures on
`CT(p, 1) = P_{p+1}` whose leaf cannot be smoothed (`d_{ℓ_1} ≠ 1`) is
`C_p - C_{p-1}`.
