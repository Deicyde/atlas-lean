# Ncard coconut Descendants

Proposition 4.5: for `s ≥ 2`, `1 ≤ i ≤ p` and a smooth arithmetical structure `(d, r)` on
`CT(i, s)`, exactly `B(p - 1, p - i)` arithmetical structures on `CT(p, s)` are descendants of
`(d, r)`.
