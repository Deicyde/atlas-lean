# Exists Unique smooth coconut Ancestor

Lemma 3.8: every arithmetical structure on `CT(p, s)` (`s ≥ 1`) with
`d_{ℓ_1}, …, d_{ℓ_s} ≥ 2` has a unique smooth ancestor on `CT(q, s)` for some `1 ≤ q ≤ p`.
