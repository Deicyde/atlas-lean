# Coconut Smooth Leaf example

Example 3.5: on `CT(4, 4)` with `r = (2, 2, 4, 6 | 3, 3, 2, 6)`
(so `d = (1, 3, 2, 3 | 2, 2, 3, 1)`),
smoothing at `v_1` and then at `v_{ℓ_4}` gives `d' = (2, 2, 2 | 2, 2, 3)`,
`r' = (2, 4, 6 | 3, 3, 2)` on `CT(3, 3)`.
