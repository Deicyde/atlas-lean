# Ncard arithmetical Structure coconut Tree star

Corollary 3: for `s ≥ 2`, the star `S_s = CT(1, s)` has
`|Arith(CT(1, s))| = 1 + ∑_{j=0}^{s-2} binom(s, j) |SArith(S_{s-j})|`.
