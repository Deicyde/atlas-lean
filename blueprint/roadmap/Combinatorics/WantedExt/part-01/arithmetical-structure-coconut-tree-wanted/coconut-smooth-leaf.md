# Coconut Smooth Leaf

Smoothing at the leaf `v_{ℓ_{l+1}}` of `CT(p, s + 1)`, giving labels on `CT(p, s)`: the
leaf is removed (re-indexed by `Fin.succAbove l`) and `d_p` drops by `1`.
