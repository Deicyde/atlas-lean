# The first zero of Kimberling's word

**The first zero of Kimberling's word.** Let $B_n$ be the position of the $n$-th $0$ in
Kimberling's word $k = k_1 k_2 k_3 \cdots$. Then $B_1 = 3$: the first $0$ of $k$ is $k_3$.
