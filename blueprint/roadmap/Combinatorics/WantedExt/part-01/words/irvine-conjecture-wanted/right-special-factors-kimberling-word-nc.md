# Right Special Factors kimberling Word ncard eq two iff

`k` has exactly two distinct right-special factors of length `n` iff for some `i ≥ 0`,
`x ≤ n < x + K_{2i}` with `x = K₁ + K₃ + ⋯ + K_{2i+1}`, or `y ≤ n < y + K_{2i+1}` with
`y = K₀ + K₂ + ⋯ + K_{2i+2}`.
