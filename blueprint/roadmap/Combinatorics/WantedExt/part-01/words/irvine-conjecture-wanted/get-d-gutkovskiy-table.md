# Entries of the Gutkovskiy table

**Entries of the Gutkovskiy table.** For natural numbers $k \le n$, the entry at index $k$
(counting from $0$) of the Gutkovskiy table $T_n$ is the term $g_k$ of Gutkovskiy's sequence.
