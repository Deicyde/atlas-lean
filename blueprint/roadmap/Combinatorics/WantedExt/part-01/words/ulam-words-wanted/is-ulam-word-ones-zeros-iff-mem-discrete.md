# Is Ulam Word ones zeros iff mem discrete Sierpinski

For `1 ≤ y ≤ x`, `1^y 0^{x-y}` is Ulam iff `(x, y) = (1, 1)` or `(x, y)` lies in the
discrete Sierpiński triangle `𝒮`.
