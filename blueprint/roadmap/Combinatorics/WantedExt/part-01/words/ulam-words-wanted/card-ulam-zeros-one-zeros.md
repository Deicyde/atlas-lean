# Card ulam zeros one zeros

For `n ≥ 1` there are `G(n - 1) = 2^{s₂(n-1)}` Ulam words of length `n` of the form
`0^a 1 0^b` (`G` is Gould's sequence, `s₂` the binary digit sum).
