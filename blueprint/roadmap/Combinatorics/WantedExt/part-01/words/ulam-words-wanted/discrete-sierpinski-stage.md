# Discrete Sierpinski Stage

The stages `S₀ = {(2, 1)}`, `S_{n+1} = S_n ∪ (S_n + (2ⁿ, 0)) ∪ (S_n + (2ⁿ, 2ⁿ))` of the
discrete Sierpiński triangle.
