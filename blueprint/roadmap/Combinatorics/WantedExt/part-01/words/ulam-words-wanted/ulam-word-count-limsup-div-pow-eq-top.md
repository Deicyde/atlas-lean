# Ulam Word Count limsup div pow eq top

`limsup #U_n / αⁿ = ∞` for every `1 < α < α₀`, where `α₀ = (#U₃₀ / (2 · 31))^{1/30}` is
the constant of the paper's proof (`n₁ = 30`); stated as: for every `C` and `N` some
`n ≥ N` has `C αⁿ ≤ #U_n`.
