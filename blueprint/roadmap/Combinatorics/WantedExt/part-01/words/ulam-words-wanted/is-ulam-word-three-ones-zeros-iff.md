# Ulam words with three leading ones

**Ulam words with three leading ones.** For every $n \ge 3$, the word $1^3 0^{n-3}$ (three
ones followed by $n - 3$ zeros) is an Ulam word if and only if $n \equiv 0 \pmod 4$ or
$n \equiv 1 \pmod 4$.
