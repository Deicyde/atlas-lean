# Thue Morse Quarto eq xor

Proposition 8: `𝒬(n) = t(n) ⊕ d(n)` where `t` is the Thue–Morse sequence and
`d(0) = 0`, `d(n) = ⌊log₂ n⌋ mod 2` (here `Nat.log 2 0 = 0` gives `d(0) = 0`).
