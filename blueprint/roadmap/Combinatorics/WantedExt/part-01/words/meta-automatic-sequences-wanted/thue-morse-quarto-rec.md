# Thue Morse Quarto rec

The recurrences of `𝒬`: `𝒬(4n) = 𝒬(n)` and `𝒬(4n+1) = 1 - 𝒬(n)` for `n ≥ 1`,
`𝒬(4n+2) = 𝒬(2n)` and `𝒬(4n+3) = 1 - 𝒬(2n)` for `n ≥ 0`.
