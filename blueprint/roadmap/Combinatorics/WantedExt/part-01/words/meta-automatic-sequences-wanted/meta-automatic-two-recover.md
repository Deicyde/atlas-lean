# Meta Automatic Two recover

Lemma 19: with `s(n) = (x, y) = (ℳ₂(n), ℳ₂(2n + ℳ₂(n)))`,
`ℳ₂(2n) = x ⊕ y` and `ℳ₂(2n+1) = x ⊕ y ⊕ 1`.
