# Meta Automatic Two complexity two pow

Proposition 36: for `k ≥ 3`, `p_{ℳ₂}(2^k) = (5/2) · 2^k - 2` (written `5 · 2^{k-1} - 2`)
if `3 ∣ k` and `3 · 2^k - 2` otherwise; and `p_{ℳ₂}(2^{k+3}) = 8 p_{ℳ₂}(2^k) + 14`.
