# Thue Morse Quarto complexity two pow

Theorem 31: for `k ≥ 4`, `p_𝒬(2^k) = 13 · 2^{k-2} - 2`, `p_𝒬(2^k) = p_t(2^k) + 2^{k-2}`,
and `|J_{2^k}| = 2^{k-2}`.
