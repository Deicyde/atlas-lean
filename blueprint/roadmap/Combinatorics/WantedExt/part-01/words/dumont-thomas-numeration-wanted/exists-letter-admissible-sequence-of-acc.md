# Exists letter Admissible Sequence of accepted

If the digit word `v_{k-1} v_{k-2} ⋯ v₀` (`k ≥ 1`) is accepted by `𝒜_{η,x}`, then there
is an `x`-admissible sequence `(mᵢ, aᵢ)_{i = 0, …, k-1}` with `|mᵢ| = vᵢ` for all `i < k`.
