# Dumont Thomas Rep iff strict Mono range

Characterization of `rep_u`: a map `f : ℤ → ℕ*` equals `rep_u` iff it is increasing for
`≺`, its image is `⋃_{ℓ ∈ ℕ} ℒ_{ℓp+1}(𝒜_{η,s}) \ {0 W_min, 1 W_max} 𝒟*`, and `f(0) = 0`.
