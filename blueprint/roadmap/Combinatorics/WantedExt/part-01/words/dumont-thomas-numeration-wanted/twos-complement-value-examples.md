# Two's complement values of small words

**Two's complement values of small words.** The two's complement values $\mathrm{val}_{2c}$
of the binary words $1$, $10$, $101$ and $011$ (most significant digit first) are $-1$, $-2$, $-3$
and $3$ respectively.
