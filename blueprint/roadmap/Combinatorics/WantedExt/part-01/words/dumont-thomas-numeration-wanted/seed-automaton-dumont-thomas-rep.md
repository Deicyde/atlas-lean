# Seed Automaton dumont Thomas Rep

Two-sided periodic points are automatic in the Dumont–Thomas complement numeration
system: every `n ∈ ℤ` has a representation `rep_u(n)`, and `u_n = 𝒜_{η,s}(rep_u(n))` for the
seed `s = u₋₁|u₀`.
