# Card quot keyword Equiv eq sum m Step Fibonacci

Main theorem: for `n, m ≥ 1` and every keyword `a` of length `m + 1`, the number of
`∼_a`-classes of binary words of length `n` is `F_0^(m) + F_1^(m) + ⋯ + F_n^(m)`.
