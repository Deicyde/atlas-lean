# Lame euclid Steps minimal

(Lamé, 1844) For `N ≥ 1`, if `u > v > 0` need exactly `N` division steps and `u` is the
smallest integer admitting such a `v`, then `u = F_{N+2}` and `v = F_{N+1}`.
