# Interior std Clean Parallelogram lattice Points

The lattice points in the interior of `P_{a,n}` are exactly
`⟨k(n-a)/n⟩ (1, 0) + (k/n) (a, n)` for `k = 1, …, n - 1`, where `⟨·⟩` is the fractional part.
