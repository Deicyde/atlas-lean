# Partial Quotient last ge two

The last partial quotient of the continued fraction `[q_0, …, q_m]` of `n / a`
(`1 ≤ a < n`) satisfies `q_m ≥ 2`. Here `m` is the least index at which `GenContFract.of (n / a)`
terminates.
