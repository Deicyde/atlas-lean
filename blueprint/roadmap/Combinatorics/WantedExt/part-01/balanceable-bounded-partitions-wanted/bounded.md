# $k$-bounded partition

**$k$-bounded partition.** A partition of $n$ is $k$-bounded if each of its parts is at most $k$.

For example, $12 = 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1$ is a $3$-bounded partition of $12$.
