# Tprod q Pochhammer three Adic parametric

Parametric `3`-adic dissection of the Euler function, for real `0 < q < 1` and
real `p`: `∏_{j ≥ 0} ((q^{3^j}; q^{3^{j+1}})_∞ (q^{2·3^j}; q^{3^{j+1}})_∞
/ (q^{3^{j+1}}; q^{3^{j+1}})_∞^{p-1})^{p^j} = (q; q)_∞`.
