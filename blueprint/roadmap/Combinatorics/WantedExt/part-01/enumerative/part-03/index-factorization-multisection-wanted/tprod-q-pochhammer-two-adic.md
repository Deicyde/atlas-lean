# Tprod q Pochhammer two Adic

`2`-adic dissection of the Euler function, first display: with
`(a; r)_∞ = ∏_{k ≥ 0} (1 - a r^k)` (`QPochhammer.qPochhammerInf`), for `|q| < 1`,
`∏_{j ≥ 0} ((q^{2^j}; q^{2^{j+1}})_∞ / (q^{2^{j+1}}; q^{2^{j+1}})_∞)^{2^j} = (q; q)_∞`.
