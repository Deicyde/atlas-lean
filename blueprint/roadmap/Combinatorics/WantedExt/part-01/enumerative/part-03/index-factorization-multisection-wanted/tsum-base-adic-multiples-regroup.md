# Tsum base Adic multiples regroup

The multiples of `B` regrouped by their `B`-adic factorization, sum form: for
`B ≥ 2`, `∑_{j ≥ 1, n ≥ 1} ∑_{k=1}^{B-1} c_{(Bn-k) B^j} = ∑_{m ≥ 1} c_{Bm}`, for `c`
summable along the multiples of `B`.
