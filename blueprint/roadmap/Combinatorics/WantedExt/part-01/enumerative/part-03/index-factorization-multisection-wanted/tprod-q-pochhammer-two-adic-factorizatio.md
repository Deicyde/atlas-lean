# Tprod q Pochhammer two Adic factorization

`2`-adic factorization of the Euler function (the case `p = 1`): for `|q| < 1`,
`∏_{j ≥ 0} (q^{2^j}; q^{2^{j+1}})_∞ = (q; q)_∞`.
