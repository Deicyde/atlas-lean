# Tsum two Adic truncated regroup

Truncated `2`-adic regrouping, sum form: for every `J`,
`∑_{j ≥ J, n ≥ 1} φ(j) c_{(2n-1) 2^j} = ∑_{p ≥ 1} φ(J + ν₂(p)) c_{p 2^J}`.
