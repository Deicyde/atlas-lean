# Tprod q Pochhammer three Adic factorization

`3`-adic factorization of the Euler function (the case `p = 1`): for `|q| < 1`,
`∏_{j ≥ 0} (q^{3^j}; q^{3^{j+1}})_∞ (q^{2·3^j}; q^{3^{j+1}})_∞ = (q; q)_∞`.
