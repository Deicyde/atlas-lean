# Tsum two Adic low regroup

The non-multiples of `2^J` regrouped by their `2`-adic factorization, sum form:
`∑_{0 ≤ j < J, n ≥ 1} c_{(2n-1) 2^j} = ∑_{p ≥ 1, 2^J ∤ p} c_p`.
