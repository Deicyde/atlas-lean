# Tprod q Pochhammer shifted two Adic

`2`-adic multisection of the `q`-Pochhammer symbol: for `a : ℂ` and `|q| < 1`,
`∏_{j ≥ 0} ((a q^{2^j - 1}; q^{2^{j+1}})_∞ / (a q^{2^{j+1} - 1}; q^{2^{j+1}})_∞)^{2^j}
= (a; q)_∞`.
