# Tsum two Adic high regroup

The multiples of `2^J` regrouped by their `2`-adic factorization, sum form:
`∑_{j ≥ J, n ≥ 1} c_{(2n-1) 2^j} = ∑_{p ≥ 1} c_{p 2^J}`.
