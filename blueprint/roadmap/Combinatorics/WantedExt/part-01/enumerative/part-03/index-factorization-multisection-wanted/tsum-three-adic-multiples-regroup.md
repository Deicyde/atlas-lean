# Tsum three Adic multiples regroup

The multiples of `3` regrouped by their `3`-adic factorization, sum form:
`∑_{j ≥ 1, n ≥ 1} (b_{(3n-1) 3^j} + b_{(3n-2) 3^j}) = ∑_{m ≥ 1} b_{3m}`, for `b`
summable along the multiples of `3`.
