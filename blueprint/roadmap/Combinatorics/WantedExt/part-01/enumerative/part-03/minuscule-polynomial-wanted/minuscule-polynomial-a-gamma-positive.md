# Minuscule Polynomial A gamma positive

Gamma-positivity: `N_n(x) = ∑_{k=0}^{⌊n/2⌋} γ_{n,k} x^k (1 + x)^{n-2k}` with non-negative
integers `γ_{n,k}`. Stated for every `n : ℕ` (`N_0 = 0`).
