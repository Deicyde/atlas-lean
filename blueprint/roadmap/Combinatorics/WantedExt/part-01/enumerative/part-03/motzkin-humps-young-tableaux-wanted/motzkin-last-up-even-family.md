# Motzkin Last Up Even Family

The finite disjoint union `⋃_{1 ≤ k ≤ ⌊n/2⌋} 𝓜^{*U}_{n,2k}`, retaining the
index `k`; its paths end at height `2k` and have last non-flat step `U`.
