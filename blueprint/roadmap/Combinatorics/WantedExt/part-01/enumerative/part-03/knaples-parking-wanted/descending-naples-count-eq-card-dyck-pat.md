# Descending Naples Count eq card dyck Path

Proposition 3: for `n ≥ 1`, descending `k`-Naples parking functions of length `n` are
equinumerous with Dyck paths of length `n + k` whose first `k` steps are Up and last `k + 1`
steps are Down.
