# The path $\xi(T)$ has $2n + 2$ steps

**The path $\xi(T)$ has $2n + 2$ steps.** For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every
semistandard Young tableau $T$ of shape $\theta^{(n)}$, the word $\xi(T)$ has length $2n + 2$.
