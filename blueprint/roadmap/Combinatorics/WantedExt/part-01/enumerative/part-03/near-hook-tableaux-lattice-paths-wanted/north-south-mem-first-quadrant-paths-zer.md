# The path $NS$ lies in $\mathcal{P}_0$

**The path $NS$ lies in $\mathcal{P}_0$.** The two-step word consisting of a north step
$(0, 1)$ followed by a south step $(0, -1)$ belongs to the set $\mathcal{P}_0$ of first-quadrant
paths.
