# Theorem 9

**Theorem 9.** (1) With `A_τ = {010, τ, 102, 120, 201}`, `A_100 ∼ A_110`. (2) With `A_τ = {010,
τ, 102, 120, 210}` and `B = {010, 101, 102, 120, 201}`, `A_100 ∼ A_101 ∼ A_110 ∼ B`
(I-Wilf-equivalence).
