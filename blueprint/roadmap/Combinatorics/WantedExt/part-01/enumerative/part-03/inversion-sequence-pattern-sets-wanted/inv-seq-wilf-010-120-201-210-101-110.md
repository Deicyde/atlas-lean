# Theorem 12

**Theorem 12**, part `A`: with `A_τ = {010, τ, 120, 201, 210}`, `A_101 ∼ A_110`. Partial: the
second part `B_101 ∼ B_110` with `B_τ = {100, τ, 120, 201, 210}` is false as printed
(`|I_5(B_101)| = 79 ≠ 81 = |I_5(B_110)|`) and is omitted.
