# A005773 Recurrence weighted log Concave

The sequences `(n A n)_{n ≥ 1}`, `(Δ(n A n))_{n ≥ 1}`, `(n ΔA n)_{n ≥ 1}` and
`(n Δ²A n)_{n ≥ 1}` are log-concave: `z (n - 1) z (n + 1) ≤ z n ^ 2` for every `n ≥ 2`.
