# A005773 Recurrence pow log Convex

The sequence `(nⁿ A nⁿ)_{n ≥ 1} = ((n A n)ⁿ)_{n ≥ 1}` is log-convex:
`z n ^ 2 ≤ z (n - 1) z (n + 1)` for every `n ≥ 2`.
