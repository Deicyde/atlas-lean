# Next free spot

**Next free spot.** Number the $n$ spots $0, \dots, n-1$. Given a set $S$ of occupied
spots and a spot $a$, the next free spot is the least spot $s \ge a$ with $s \notin S$; when
every spot $s \ge a$ is occupied, it is $a$ itself.
