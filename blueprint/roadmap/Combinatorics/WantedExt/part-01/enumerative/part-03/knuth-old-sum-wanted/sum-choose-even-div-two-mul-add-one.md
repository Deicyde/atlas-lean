# Even-index binomial sum with odd reciprocal weights

**Even-index binomial sum with odd reciprocal weights.** For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \frac{1}{2k+1} \binom{n}{2k} = \frac{2^n}{n+1}.$$
