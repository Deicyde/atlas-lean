# Sum inv gamma Binom of binomial poly identity

Equation (29): from the polynomial identity (28), for `ℜu, ℜv > -1`,
`∑ f(k) binom(p(k)+u+v+1, u+1)^{-1}
= (u+1)/(v+1) ∑ (-1)^{q(k)} g(k) binom(q(k)+u+v+1, v+1)^{-1}`.
