# Knuth old sum

Knuth's old sum (the Reed Dawson identity):
`∑_{k=0}^n (-1)^k binom(n,k) 2^{-k} binom(2k,k)` is `2^{-n} binom(n, n/2)` for even `n`
and `0` for odd `n`.
