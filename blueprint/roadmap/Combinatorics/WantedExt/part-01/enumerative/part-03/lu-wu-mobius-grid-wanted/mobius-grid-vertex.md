# Vertices of the Möbius grid

**Vertices of the Möbius grid.** For natural numbers $M$ and $N$, the vertices of the
$2M \times N$ Möbius grid are the pairs $(a, b)$ of natural numbers with $a < 2M$ and $b < N$.
