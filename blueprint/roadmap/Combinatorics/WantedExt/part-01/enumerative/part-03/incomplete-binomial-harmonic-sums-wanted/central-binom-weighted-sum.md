# Kollár's auxiliary sum $D_n$

**Kollár's auxiliary sum $D_n$.** For a natural number $n$, $D_n$ is the rational number
$D_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{\binom{2k}{k}}{2k \cdot 2^{2k}}$.
