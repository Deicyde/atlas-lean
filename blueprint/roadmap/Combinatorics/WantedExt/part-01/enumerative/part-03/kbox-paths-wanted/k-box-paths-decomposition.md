# K Box Paths decomposition

Decomposition: the `k`-box paths (of all sizes `n ≥ 1`) are exactly the words
`μ₁ U μ₂ U ⋯ μ_{k+1} U D^k L` with `μ₁, …, μ_{k+1}` augmented `(k+1)`-Dyck paths.
