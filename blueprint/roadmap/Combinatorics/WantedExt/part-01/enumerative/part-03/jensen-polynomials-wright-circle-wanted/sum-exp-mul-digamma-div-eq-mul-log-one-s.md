# Sum exp mul digamma div eq mul log one sub

For integers `1 ≤ k < p`, with `ζₚ = e^{2πi/p}`,
`∑_{α=1}^{p} ζₚ^{kα} ψ(α/p) = p Log(1 - ζₚ^k)`, where `ψ` is the digamma function and `Log` the
principal logarithm.
