# Vanishing of metered parking counts

**Vanishing of metered parking counts.** Let $m, n, t$ be natural numbers with $m > n$ and
$t \ge n$. Then there are no $t$-metered $(m, n)$-parking functions, that is,
$\mathrm{mpf}_{m,n}(t) = 0$.
