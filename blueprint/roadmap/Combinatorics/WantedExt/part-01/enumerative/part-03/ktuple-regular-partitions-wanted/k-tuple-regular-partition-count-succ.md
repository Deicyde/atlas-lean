# K Tuple Regular Partition Count succ

The convolution recurrence `T_{ℓ,k+1}(n) = ∑_{i=0}^{n} r_ℓ(i) · T_{ℓ,k}(n-i)`, with `r_ℓ` the `ℓ`-regular partition count.
