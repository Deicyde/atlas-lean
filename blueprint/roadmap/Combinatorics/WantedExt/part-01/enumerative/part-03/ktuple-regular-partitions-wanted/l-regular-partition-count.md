# Number of $\ell$-regular partitions

**Number of $\ell$-regular partitions.** For natural numbers $\ell$ and $n$, the number of
$\ell$-regular partitions of $n$ is the number of partitions of $n$ none of whose parts is
divisible by $\ell$.
