# Fibonacci Alternating Sum

The alternating sums of Fibonacci numbers `W_n = ∑_{k=0}^n (-1)^{n-k} F_k`
(`0, 1, 0, 2, 1, 4, 4, 9, …`), with `F_k = Nat.fib k`.
