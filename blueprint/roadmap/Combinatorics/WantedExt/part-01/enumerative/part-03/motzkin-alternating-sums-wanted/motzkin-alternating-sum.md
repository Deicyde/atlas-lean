# Motzkin Alternating Sum

The alternating sums of Motzkin numbers `T_n = ∑_{k=0}^n (-1)^{n-k} M_k` (OEIS A187306:
`1, 0, 2, 2, 7, 14, 37, …`), with `M_k = MetaMathlibExt.motzkinNumber k`.
