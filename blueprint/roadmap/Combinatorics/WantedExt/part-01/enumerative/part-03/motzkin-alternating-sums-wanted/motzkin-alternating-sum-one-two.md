# Alternating Motzkin sums at 1 and 2

**Alternating Motzkin sums at 1 and 2.** Let $M_k$ denote the $k$-th Motzkin number and let
$T_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} M_k$. Then $T_1 = 0$ and $T_2 = 2$.
