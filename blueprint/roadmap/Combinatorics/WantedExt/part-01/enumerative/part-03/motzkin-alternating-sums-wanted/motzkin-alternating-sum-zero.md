# The alternating Motzkin sum at 0

**The alternating Motzkin sum at 0.** Let $M_k$ denote the $k$-th Motzkin number and let
$T_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} M_k$. Then $T_0 = 1$.
