# Is Absolute Maximal Set Of Lanes

An MSL of the standard road intersection is absolute when it has no two U-turns `E_iX_i`,
`E_jX_j` (`i ≠ j`) whose replacement by `E_iX_j` and `E_jX_i` gives another MSL.
