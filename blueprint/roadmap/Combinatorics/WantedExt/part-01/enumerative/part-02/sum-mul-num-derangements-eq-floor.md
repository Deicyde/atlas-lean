# A sum rule for derangements

**A sum rule for derangements.** For every natural number $p$,
$\sum_{n=0}^{p} n\, D(n) = \lfloor (p + 1)!/e \rfloor$, where $D(n)$ is the number of
derangements of $n$ objects.
