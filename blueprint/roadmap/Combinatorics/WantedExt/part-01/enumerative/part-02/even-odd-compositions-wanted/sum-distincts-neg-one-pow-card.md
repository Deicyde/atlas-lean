# Sum distincts neg one pow card

Theorem 1 (Legendre): the number of even partitions of `n` into distinct
parts minus the number of odd ones is `(-1)^j` if `n = j(3j ± 1)/2` for some
`j ≥ 0`, and `0` otherwise.
