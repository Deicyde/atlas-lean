# Composition Odd Minus Even parts gt one

Theorem 5: `b_n = c_{n,o} - c_{n,e}`, the signed count of compositions of
`n + 1` with parts greater than one, is `(-1)^j` if `n ∈ {3j+1, 3j+2}` and `0`
otherwise.
