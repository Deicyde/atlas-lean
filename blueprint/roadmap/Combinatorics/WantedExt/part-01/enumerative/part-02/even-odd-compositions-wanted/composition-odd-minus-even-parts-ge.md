# Composition Odd Minus Even parts ge

Theorem 8: for `k, n ≥ 1`, the signed count `b_{k,n}` of compositions of
`n + k - 1` with parts at least `k` is
`∑_{0 ≤ j ≤ (n-1)/k} (-1)^j binom(n - 1 - j(k-1), j)`.
