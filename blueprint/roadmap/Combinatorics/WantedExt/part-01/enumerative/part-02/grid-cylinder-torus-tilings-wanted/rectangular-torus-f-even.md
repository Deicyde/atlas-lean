# Rectangular Torus f even

For even `n`, the `f` rectangular-torus formula is multiplied through by two.  In each
divisor summand, `tileFixedCount σ c` is `t_{f^c}`; `σ²=1` is the reflection-order hypothesis and
the division exponents follow the paper verbatim.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
