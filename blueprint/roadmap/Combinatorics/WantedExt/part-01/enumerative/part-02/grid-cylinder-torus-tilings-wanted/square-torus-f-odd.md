# Square Torus f odd

For odd `n`, the square-torus `f` formula is the divisor sum with one fixed-axis family.
`tileFixedCount σ d` is `t_{f^d}`, raised to `n/d`, and `σ²=1` encodes every valid tile action.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
