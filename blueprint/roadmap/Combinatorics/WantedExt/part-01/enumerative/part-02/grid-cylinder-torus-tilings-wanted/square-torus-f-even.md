# Square Torus f even

For even `n`, the square-torus `f` formula is multiplied through by two.  For each divisor
`d`, `tileFixedCount σ d` encodes `t_{f^d}`; `σ²=1` is the reflection-order hypothesis.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
