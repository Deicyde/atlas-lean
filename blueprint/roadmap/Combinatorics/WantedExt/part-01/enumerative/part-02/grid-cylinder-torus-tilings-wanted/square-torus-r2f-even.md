# Square Torus r2f even

For even `n`, the square-torus `r²f` formula is multiplied by two.  Its power-dependent
fixed count is `tileFixedCount σ d = t_{(r²f)^d}` and the design permutation satisfies `σ²=1`.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
