# Square Torus r2 even

For even `n`, the square-torus half-turn formula is multiplied through by four, replacing
the paper's `3/4` and `1/4` coefficients by integral coefficients.  The term
`(tileFixedCount σ 1)^4` is `t_{r²}^4`, and `σ²=1`.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
