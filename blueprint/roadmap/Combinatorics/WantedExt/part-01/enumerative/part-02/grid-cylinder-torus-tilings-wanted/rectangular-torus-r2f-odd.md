# Rectangular Torus r2f odd

For odd `m`, the `r²f` rectangular-torus shift-sum contains one fixed-row family.  The term
`tileFixedCount σ d` is exactly `t_{(r²f)^d}`, and `σ²=1` is the order-two hypothesis.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
