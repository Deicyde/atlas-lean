# Perm Orbit

The finite orbit of `c` under nonnegative powers of the cell permutation `φ`.  Because `φ`
is a permutation of a finite type, these are precisely the cyclic-subgroup orbits.  This explicit
encoding retains fixed one-cycles, unlike `Equiv.Perm.cycleType`.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
