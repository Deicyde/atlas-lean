# Fixed Tiling Count fin two identity

On two cells with two tile designs, the identity cell map and identity tile permutation fix
all four maps.  This concrete `decide` proof is a characteristic sanity check for
`fixedTilingCount`, not one of the paper's enumerative theorems.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
