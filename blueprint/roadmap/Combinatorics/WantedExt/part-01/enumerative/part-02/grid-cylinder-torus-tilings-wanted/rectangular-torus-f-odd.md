# Rectangular Torus f odd

For odd `n`, the `f` rectangular-torus shift-sum has one fixed column family.  The factor
`tileFixedCount σ c` is `t_{f^c}`, raised to `m/c`, and `σ²=1` records the design action's order.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
