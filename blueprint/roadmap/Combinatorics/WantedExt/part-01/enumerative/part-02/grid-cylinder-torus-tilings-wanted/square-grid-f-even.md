# Square Grid f even

For even `n`, either axial square-grid reflection `f` has no fixed column and the count is
`|T|^(n²/2)`.  Here the statement is for `f`, with `σ²=1` encoding its action on designs.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
