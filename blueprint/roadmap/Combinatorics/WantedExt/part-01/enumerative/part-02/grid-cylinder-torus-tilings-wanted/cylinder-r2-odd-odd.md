# Cylinder r2 odd odd

If both `m` and `n` are odd, the cylinder half-turn shift-sum is
`n t_id^((nm-1)/2)t_{r²}`.  `tileFixedCount σ 1` is the fixed-design count and `σ²=1` is the
required order condition.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
