# Square Torus r2 odd

For odd `n`, the square-torus half-turn sum is
`n²t_id^((n²-1)/2)t_{r²}`.  `tileFixedCount σ 1` supplies the fixed-design count and `σ²=1`
is the required order hypothesis.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
