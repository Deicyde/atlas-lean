# Cell R2

The cell map for the paper's right action of the half-turn `r²` on an `n × m` grid:
`(x,y) ↦ (n-1-x,m-1-y)`, with both modular subtractions represented by `-1`.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
