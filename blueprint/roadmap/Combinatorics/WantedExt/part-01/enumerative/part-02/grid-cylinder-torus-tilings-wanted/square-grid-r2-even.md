# Square Grid r2 even

For even `n`, the square-grid half-turn has only two-cycles, producing `|T|^(n²/2)`.
The tile permutation satisfies `σ²=1`, as required for `r²` in every `D₈`-set.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
