# Square Torus r odd

For odd `n`, the square-torus quarter-turn `r` shift-sum is
`n²t_id^((n²-1)/4)t_r`.  The term `tileFixedCount σ 1` is `t_r`, and `σ⁴=1` is the rotation's
order hypothesis for every tile action.

Formalization deviation: none beyond the representation choices stated above.
