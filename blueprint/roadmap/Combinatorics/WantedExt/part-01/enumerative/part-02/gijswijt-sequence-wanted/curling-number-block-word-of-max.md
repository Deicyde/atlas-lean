# Curling Number block Word of max

For `m, u ≥ 1` and `a_1, …, a_{u-1} ∈ {0, …, m+1}` with some `a_i = m + 1`, the prefix
`P = B_{u-1}^{a_{u-1}} ⋯ B_1^{a_1}` of `B_u^{(m+1)}` has `𝒞(P) = m + 1`.
