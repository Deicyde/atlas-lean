# Gijswijt Proper Prefix curling iff

For `m, u ≥ 1`, the proper prefixes `P` of `B_u^{(m+1)}` with `𝒞(P) ≤ m + 1` are exactly
the strings `B_{u-1}^{a_{u-1}} ⋯ B_1^{a_1}` (blocks of order `m + 1`) with
`a_1, …, a_{u-1} ∈ {0, …, m+1}`.
