# Gijswijt Glue Concat suffix block

For `m ≥ 1` and `t ≥ 2`, `m T_t^{(m)}` is a suffix of `B_t^{(m)}`, and this is the only
occurrence of `T_t^{(m)}` as a substring of `B_t^{(m)}`.
