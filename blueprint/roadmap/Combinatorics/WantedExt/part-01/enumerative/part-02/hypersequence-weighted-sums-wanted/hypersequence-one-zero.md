# First hypersequence at index zero

**First hypersequence at index zero.** Let $(a_n)$ be a sequence in an additive commutative
monoid and let $a^{(1)}_n$ denote its hypersequence of generation $1$. Then $a^{(1)}_0 = a_0$.
