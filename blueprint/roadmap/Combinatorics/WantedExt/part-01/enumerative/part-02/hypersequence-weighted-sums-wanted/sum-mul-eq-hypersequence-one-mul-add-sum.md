# Sum mul eq hypersequence one mul add sum

Theorem 7 (Abel's summation by parts):
`∑_{k=0}^{n} a_k b_k = a^{(1)}_n b_{n+1} + ∑_{k=0}^{n} a^{(1)}_k (b_k - b_{k+1})`.
