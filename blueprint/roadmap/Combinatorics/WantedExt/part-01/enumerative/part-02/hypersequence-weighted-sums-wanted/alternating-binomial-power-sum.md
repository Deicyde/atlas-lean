# Alternating binomial power sum

**Alternating binomial power sum.** For natural numbers $\ell, m$ and complex numbers $x, y$,

$$p_{\ell,m}(x, y) = \sum_{k=0}^{m} (-1)^k \binom{m}{k} (kx + y)^\ell,$$

with the convention $0^0 = 1$.
