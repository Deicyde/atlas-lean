# K Perm Fixed Point Polynomial real roots

Theorem 4: for `n ≥ k ≥ 1`, the polynomial `P(x) = ∑_{j=0}^{k} a(n, k, j) x^j` of the
fixed-point distribution of `k`-permutations of `[n]` has only real zeros.
