# Sum mul k Perm Fixed Point Count

Proposition 4 (mean): the mean `μ_{n,k} = n c(n - 1, k - 1) / c(n, k)` of the number of fixed
points of a uniform `k`-permutation of `[n]`, stated with the denominator `c(n, k)` cleared.
