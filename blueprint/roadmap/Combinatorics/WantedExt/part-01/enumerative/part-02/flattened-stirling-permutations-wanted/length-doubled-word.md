# Length of the doubled word

**Length of the doubled word.** For every finite set $S$ of natural numbers with elements
$s_1 < \dots < s_k$, the doubled word $f(S) = s_1 s_1 s_2 s_2 \cdots s_k s_k$ has length $2|S|$.
