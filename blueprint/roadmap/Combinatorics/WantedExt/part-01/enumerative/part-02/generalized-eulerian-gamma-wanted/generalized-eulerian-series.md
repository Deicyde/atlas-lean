# Generalized Eulerian Series

The generating function `𝒫(q, t; x, y; z) = ((x - y) / (x e^(yz) - y e^(xz)))^q e^((x+y)qtz)`
of Equation (2), the `q`-th power taken by Mathlib's binomial series (the base has constant
term `1` when `x ≠ y`).
