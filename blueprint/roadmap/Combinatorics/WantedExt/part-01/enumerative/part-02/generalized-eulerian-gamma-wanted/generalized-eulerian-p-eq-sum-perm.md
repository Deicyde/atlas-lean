# Theorem 2

**Theorem 2.** `P_n(q, t; x, y) = ∑_{w ∈ S_n} q^(cyc w) t^(fix w) x^(exc w) y^(exc w⁻¹)
(x + y)^(fix w)`, for rational `q, t, x, y` with `x ≠ y`.
