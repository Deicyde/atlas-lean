# Degenerate Harmonic Rank exp weighted sum

Theorem 3: for `n ≥ 0`,
`∑_{k=0}^n (-1)ᵏ (1)_{k,λ} / k! · H_λ(n + 1, k, α) = H_{n+1,λ}(α) - H_{n,λ}(α) / α`.
