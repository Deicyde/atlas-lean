# Degenerate Stirling Second

Degenerate Stirling numbers of the second kind:
`(e_λ(t) - 1)ᵏ / k! = ∑_{n ≥ k} S_{2,λ}(n, k) tⁿ / n!` (paper, Equation (8)).
