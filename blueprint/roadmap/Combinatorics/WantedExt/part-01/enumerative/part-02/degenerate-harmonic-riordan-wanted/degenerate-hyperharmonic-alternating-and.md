# Degenerate Hyperharmonic alternating and binomial sums

Theorem 1: for `n ≥ 0`,
`∑_{k=0}^n (-1)ᵏ H^{k+1}_{n-k+1,λ}(α) = (-1)ⁿ D_{n,λ} / (α^{n+1} n!)`, and for `r ≥ 0`,
`∑_{k=0}^n binom(k + r, r) H_{n-k+1,λ}(α) = H^{r+2}_{n+1,λ}(α)`.
