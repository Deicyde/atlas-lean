# Generalized Degenerate Harmonic

Dağlı's generalized degenerate harmonic numbers
`H_{n,λ}(α) = ∑_{k=1}^n (-1)^{k-1} binom(λ, k) / (λ αᵏ)`, with `H_{0,λ}(α) = 0`. At `α = 1` these
are the degenerate harmonic numbers `H_{n,λ}`.
