# Degenerate Harmonic Rank exp sum eq stirling First

Theorem 7: for `n ≥ 1`,
`∑_{k=1}^n (1)_{k,λ} / k! · H_λ(n, k - 1, α) =
  ∑_{k=1}^n ∑_{i=1}^k (-1)^{k+i} (1)_{i,λ} S_{1,λ}(k, i) / (αᵏ k!)`.
