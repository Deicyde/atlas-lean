# Degenerate Stirling First

Degenerate Stirling numbers of the first kind:
`(log_λ(1 + t))ᵏ / k! = ∑_{n ≥ k} S_{1,λ}(n, k) tⁿ / n!` (paper, Equation (8)), with
`log_λ(1 + t) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} binom(λ, n) tⁿ / λ` (paper, Equation (2)).
