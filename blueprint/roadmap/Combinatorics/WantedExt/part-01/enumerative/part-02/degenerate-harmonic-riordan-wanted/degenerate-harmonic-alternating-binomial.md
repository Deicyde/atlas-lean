# Degenerate Harmonic alternating binomial sum

Theorem 2: for `n ≥ 1`,
`∑_{k=0}^n (-1)^{k+1} binom(n, k) H_{k,λ} = binom(n + λ - 1, n) / λ`, where
`H_{k,λ} = H_{k,λ}(1)` are the degenerate harmonic numbers.
