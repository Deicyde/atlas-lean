# Degenerate Harmonic Rank succ partial sum

Theorem 4: for `n, r ≥ 0`,
`∑_{k=0}^n H_λ(k, r + 1, α) = ∑_{k=0}^n H_{n-k,λ}(α) H_λ(k, r, α)`.
