# MacMahon's partition of the factorization 2 · 3

**MacMahon's partition of the factorization 2 · 3.** MacMahon's bijection sends the ordered
factorization $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ to the partition $(1, 2^2) = (1, 2, 2)$ of $5$.
