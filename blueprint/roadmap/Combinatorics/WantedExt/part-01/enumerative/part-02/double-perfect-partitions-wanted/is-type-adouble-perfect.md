# Is Type ADouble Perfect

A Type-A double-perfect partition of `N` of order `r` (the set `D_r(N)`): a member of
`U_r(N)` obtained from a perfect partition of `N - r - 1` by inserting the parts `1^{r+1}` or the
part `r + 1`.
