# Perfect Partition Of Factorization

MacMahon's bijection (1) from ordered factorizations to perfect partitions: the factorization
`a₁ a₂ ⋯ a_k` maps to `(1^{a₁-1}, a₁^{a₂-1}, (a₁a₂)^{a₃-1}, …, (a₁⋯a_{k-1})^{a_k-1})`.
