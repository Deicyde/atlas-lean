# Small values of the ordered factorization count

**Small values of the ordered factorization count.** The number $f(n)$ of ordered
factorizations of $n$ into factors greater than $1$ takes the values $f(0) = 0$, $f(1) = 1$,
$f(6) = 3$ and $f(12) = 8$.
