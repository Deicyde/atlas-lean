# First five central trinomial coefficients

**First five central trinomial coefficients.** The central trinomial coefficients $T_n$, the
constant terms of $(x^{-1} + 1 + x)^n$, are $1, 1, 3, 7, 19$ for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4$.
