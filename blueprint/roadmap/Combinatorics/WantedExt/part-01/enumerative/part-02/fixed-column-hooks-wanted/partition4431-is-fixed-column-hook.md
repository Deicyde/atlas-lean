# Partition4431 is Fixed Column Hook

In `(4, 4, 3, 1)`, the first-column hook `h_{3,1} = 4 = 3 + 1` is a `1`-fixed hook, the
first-column hook `h_{4,1} = 1` is not `0`-fixed, and the third-column square `(4, 3)` lies
outside the diagram.
