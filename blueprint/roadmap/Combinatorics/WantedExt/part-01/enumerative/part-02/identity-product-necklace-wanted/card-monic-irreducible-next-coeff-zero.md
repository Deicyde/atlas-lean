# Card monic irreducible next Coeff zero

Corollary 35: for `n ≥ 1`, the number of monic irreducible polynomials of degree `n` over
`F` whose coefficient of `X^{n-1}` vanishes equals the number of aperiodic zero-sum
`n`-necklaces over `F`.
