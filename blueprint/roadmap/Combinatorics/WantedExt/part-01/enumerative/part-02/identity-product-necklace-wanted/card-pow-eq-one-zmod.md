# Card pow eq one zmod

Lemma 12: `[C_a ÷ b] = gcd(a, b)`: the cyclic group of order `a` has exactly `gcd(a, b)`
elements `x` with `x^b = 1`. Stated for every `b ≥ 0` (the paper takes `b` positive; for
`b = 0` both sides are `a`).
