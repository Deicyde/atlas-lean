# Aperiodic identity Product Necklace count

Theorem 16: the number of aperiodic identity-product `n`-necklaces (rotation orbits of
size `n`) is `(1/n) ∑_{d ∣ n} μ(n/d) [G ÷ n/d] |G|^{d-1}`.
