# Homogeneous Necklace count

Theorem 19: the orbits of `C_n × G` on `G^n` (rotate, and multiply every entry by `h` on
the left) are as many as the identity-product `n`-necklaces, namely
`(1/n) ∑_{d ∣ n} φ(n/d) [G ÷ n/d] |G|^{d-1}`.
