# Identity Product Necklace count smallest Period

Corollary 18: for `k ∣ n`, the number of identity-product `n`-necklaces with smallest
period `k` (rotation orbits of size `k`) is `(1/k) ∑_{d ∣ k} μ(k/d) [G ÷ n/d] |G|^{d-1}`.
