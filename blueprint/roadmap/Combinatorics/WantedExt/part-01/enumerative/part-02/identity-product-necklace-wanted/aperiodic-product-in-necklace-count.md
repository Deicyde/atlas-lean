# Aperiodic product In Necklace count

Theorem 24: for conjugation-closed `K`, the number of aperiodic `K`-product
`n`-necklaces is `(1/n) ∑_{d ∣ n} μ(n/d) [G ÷ K ÷ n/d] |G|^{d-1}`.
