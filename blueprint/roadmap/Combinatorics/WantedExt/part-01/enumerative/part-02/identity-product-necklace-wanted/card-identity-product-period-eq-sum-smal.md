# Card identity Product period eq sum smallest Period

Lemma 14: for every `k ≥ 1`, `|A_k| = ∑_{d ∣ k} |P_d|`, where `A_k` is the set of
identity-product `n`-tuples with period `k` and `P_d` those with smallest period `d`.
