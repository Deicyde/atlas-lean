# Exists normal Basis finite Field

Theorem 27 (normal basis theorem for finite fields): `K` has a normal basis over `F`, an
element whose Galois conjugates are linearly independent over `F` (hence a basis, as
`|Gal(K/F)| = [K : F]`).
