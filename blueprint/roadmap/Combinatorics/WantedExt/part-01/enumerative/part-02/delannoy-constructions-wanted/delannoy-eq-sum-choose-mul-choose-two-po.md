# Delannoy eq sum choose mul choose two pow

The Delannoy number `D(m, n)` (the Delannoy triangle entry
`T(m + n, m; 1)`) equals `∑_{j=0}^{min(m, n)} C(m, j) C(n, j) 2^j`.
