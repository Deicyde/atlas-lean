# Jacobsthal numbers

**Jacobsthal numbers.** The Jacobsthal numbers are the integers $J_n$ defined by $J_0 = 0$,
$J_1 = 1$ and $J_{n+2} = J_{n+1} + 2 J_n$ for all $n \ge 0$.
