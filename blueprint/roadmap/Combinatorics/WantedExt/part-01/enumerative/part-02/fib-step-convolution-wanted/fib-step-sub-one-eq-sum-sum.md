# Corollary 7

**Corollary 7**: for `n ≥ p ≥ 1`,
`F⁽ᵖ⁺¹⁾ₙ - 1 = ∑_{k=0}^{p-1} ∑_{j=0}^{n-2-k} F⁽ᵖ⁺¹⁾ⱼ`.
