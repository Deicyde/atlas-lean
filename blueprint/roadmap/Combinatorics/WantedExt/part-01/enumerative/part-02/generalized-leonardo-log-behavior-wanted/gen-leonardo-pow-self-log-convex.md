# Gen Leonardo pow self log Convex

For every `k ≥ 1`, the sequence `(L_{k,n}^n)_{n ≥ 3}` is log-convex:
`L_{k,n}^{2n} ≤ L_{k,n-1}^{n-1} L_{k,n+1}^{n+1}` for every `n ≥ 4`.
