# Gen Leonardo rpow inv log Convex

For every `k ≥ 1`, the sequence `(L_{k,n}^{1/n})_{n ≥ 9}` of `n`-th roots of the generalized
Leonardo numbers is log-convex: `(L_{k,n}^{1/n})² ≤ L_{k,n-1}^{1/(n-1)} L_{k,n+1}^{1/(n+1)}` for
every `n ≥ 10`.
