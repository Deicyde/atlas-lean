# Schreier Count

`|S^{p/q}_n|`, the number of `(p, q)`-Schreier sets with maximum `n`: subsets
`F ⊆ {1, …, n}` with `n ∈ F` and `p |F| ≤ q x` for all `x ∈ F` (that is, `q min F ≥ p |F|`).
