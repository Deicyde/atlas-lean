# Zurita SCoeff

The coefficients `s_k` of `S_n(x) = ∑_{k=0}^n s_k x^k`: `s_0 = 1/(1/2 - n)` and
`s_k = (k - 1 - n)/(k - n + 1/2) · s_{k-1}` for `1 ≤ k ≤ n`.
