# Euler Mahonian three two three

`E_3(x, y) = 1 + 2xy + 2xy² + x²y³`, checked at `x = 2`, `y = 3`: the six permutations of
`S_3` have `(des, maj) = (0,0), (1,1), (1,1), (1,2), (1,2), (2,3)`.
