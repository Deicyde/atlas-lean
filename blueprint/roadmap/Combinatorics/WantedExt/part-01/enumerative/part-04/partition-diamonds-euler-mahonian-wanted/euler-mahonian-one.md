# The first Euler–Mahonian polynomial is $1$

**The first Euler–Mahonian polynomial is $1$.** For every commutative ring $R$ and all
$x, y \in R$, the Euler–Mahonian polynomial $E_1$ satisfies $E_1(x, y) = 1$.
