# Diamond Link Pos

Position (counted from `0`) of the `k`th link of the multifold partition diamond whose
diamonds have `d₁, …, d_M` folds (`ds = [d₁, …, d_M]`): `∑_{m < k} (d_{m+1} + 1)`.
