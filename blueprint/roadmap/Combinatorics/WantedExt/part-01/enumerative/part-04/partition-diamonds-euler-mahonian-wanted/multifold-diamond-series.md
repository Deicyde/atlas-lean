# Multifold Diamond Series

The bivariate generating function `σ_{d₁,…,d_M}(a, b) = ∑ a^{fold sum} b^{link sum}` in
`ℤ⟦a, b⟧` (`a = X 0`, `b = X 1`); `σ_{d,M}` is the case `ds = [d, …, d]` (`M` copies).
