# Nondecreasing Rectangle Total

The total number of `r × s` rectangles over all nondecreasing words of length `n` over `[k]`
(the set `W^↗_{n,k}`); a word is a monotone map `Fin n → Fin k`, letter `i` read as `i + 1`.
