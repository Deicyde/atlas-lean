# Rectangle Capacity

The number of occurrences of an `r × s` rectangle (height `r`, width `s`) in the bargraph of
the word `w`: pairs `(i, y)` (0-indexed column, row) such that columns `i, …, i + s - 1` exist and
all have height at least `y + r`.
