# Unique stat Enc valley plateau eq one

If `α` is the unique parking function with its statistic encoding and
`α_{i-1} > α_i = ⋯ = α_j < α_{j+1}` for some `i ≤ j`, then `α_i = ⋯ = α_j = 1`.
