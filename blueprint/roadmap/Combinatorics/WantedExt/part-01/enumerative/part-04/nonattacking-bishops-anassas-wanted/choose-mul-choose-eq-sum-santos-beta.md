# Choose mul choose eq sum santos Beta

Equation (8): for natural `p`, `q` and complex `x`, `z`,
`binom(2x + z - q, p) binom(x, q) = ∑_{i=0}^{p+q} β_i(p, q, z) binom(2x + z, i)`.
