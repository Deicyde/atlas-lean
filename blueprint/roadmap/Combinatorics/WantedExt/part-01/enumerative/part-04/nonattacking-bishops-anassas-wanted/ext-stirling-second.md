# Ext Stirling Second

Knuth's extension of the Stirling numbers of the second kind to integer arguments:
`{n, k}` for `n, k ≥ 0`, the unsigned Stirling number of the first kind `[-k, -n]` for
`n, k < 0`, and `0` otherwise.
