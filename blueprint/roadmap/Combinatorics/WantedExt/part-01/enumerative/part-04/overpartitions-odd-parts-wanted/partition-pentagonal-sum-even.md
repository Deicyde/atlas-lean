# Partition pentagonal sum even

Parity form of Euler's pentagonal recurrence: for `n > 0`,
`∑_{k ∈ ℤ} p(n - k(3k+1)/2) ≡ 0 (mod 2)`; only `|k| ≤ n` can contribute.
