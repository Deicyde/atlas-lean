# Overpartition Odd eq sum parts Not Two Mod Four triangular

`po(n) = ∑_{k ≥ 0} P_2(n - k(k+1)/2)`. The paper states it for positive `n`; it also holds
at `n = 0` (both sides `1`), so it is stated for all `n`.
