# Theorem 10

**Theorem 10.** If `S[m] = CS[m]` for all `m < n` and `n ≥ 4`, the number of permutations
guessed in exactly three guesses with `ρ_S(π) = 1` is
`1 - 2^{n+1} + 3ⁿ + n²/2 + 5n/2 - n 2ⁿ`.
