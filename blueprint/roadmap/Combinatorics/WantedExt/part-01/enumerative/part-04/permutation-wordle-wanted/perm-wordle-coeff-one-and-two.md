# Theorem 5

**Theorem 5.** For every deranged strategy `S` of length `n` (each component `S m`,
`2 ≤ m ≤ n`, fixed-point-free), `[x¹] f_S = 1` and `[x²] f_S = A(n, 1) = 2ⁿ - n - 1`.
