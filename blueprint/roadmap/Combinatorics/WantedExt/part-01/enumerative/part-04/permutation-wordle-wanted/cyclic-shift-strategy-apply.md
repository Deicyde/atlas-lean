# Components of the cyclic shift strategy

**Components of the cyclic shift strategy.** For every $m$, the component of the cyclic shift
strategy on $m$ positions is the rotation $i \mapsto i + 1 \pmod m$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, m-1\}$.
