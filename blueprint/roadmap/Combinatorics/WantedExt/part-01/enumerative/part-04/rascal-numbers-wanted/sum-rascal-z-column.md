# Column sums of the Rascal triangle

**Column sums of the Rascal triangle.** For all natural numbers
$k$ and $r$, the Rascal numbers $R_{n,k} = k(n-k) + 1$
($0 \le k \le n$) satisfy
$\sum_{i=0}^{r} R_{k+i,k} = k \binom{r+1}{2} + r + 1$.
