# Alternating row sums of Rascal numbers on even rows

**Alternating row sums of Rascal numbers on even rows.** For every natural number $m$, the
Rascal numbers satisfy $\sum_{k=0}^{2m} (-1)^k R_{2m,k} = 1 - m$.
