# Number of ascents of a word

**Number of ascents of a word.** For a finite word
$w = w_1 w_2 \cdots w_n$ of natural numbers, the number of ascents
of $w$ is the number of indices $i$ with $1 \le i < n$ and
$w_i < w_{i+1}$.
