# Alternating row sums of the Rascal triangle, odd rows

**Alternating row sums of the Rascal triangle, odd rows.**
For every natural number $m$, the Rascal numbers
$R_{n,k} = k(n-k) + 1$ ($0 \le k \le n$) satisfy
$\sum_{k=0}^{2m+1} (-1)^k R_{2m+1,k} = 0$.
