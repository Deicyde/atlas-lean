# Row sums of the Rascal triangle

**Row sums of the Rascal triangle.** For every natural number
$n$, the Rascal numbers $R_{n,k} = k(n-k) + 1$ ($0 \le k \le n$)
satisfy $\sum_{k=0}^{n} R_{n,k} = \binom{n+1}{3} + n + 1$.
