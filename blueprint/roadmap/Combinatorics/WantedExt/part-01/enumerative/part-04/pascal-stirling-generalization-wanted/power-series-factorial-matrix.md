# Power Series Factorial Matrix

The leading `n × n` section of the paper's lower triangular matrix `M(f)` of a formal power
series `f`: 0-based entry `(a, b)` is `a!/b! · [t^{a-b}] f` for `b ≤ a` and `0` otherwise.
