# Pascal Diagonal Matrix

The paper's `𝖫_{d^{(m)}}(n)`: the `n × n` real matrix carrying the `m`-th diagonal
`d^{(m)}_b = C(m + b, b)` of Pascal's triangle on its `m`-th subdiagonal (0-based entry
`(b + m, b)`), zero elsewhere.
