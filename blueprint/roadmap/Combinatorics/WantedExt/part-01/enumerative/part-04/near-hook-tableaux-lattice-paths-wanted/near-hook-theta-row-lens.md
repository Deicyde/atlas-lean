# Row lengths of $\theta^{(n)}$

**Row lengths of $\theta^{(n)}$.** For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the row lengths of the Young
diagram $\theta^{(n)}$, from the top row down, are $n + 2$, $2$, and then $1$ repeated $n$ times.
