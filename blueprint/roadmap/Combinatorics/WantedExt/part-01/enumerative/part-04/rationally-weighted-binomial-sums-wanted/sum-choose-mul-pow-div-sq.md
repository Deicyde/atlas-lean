# Sum choose mul pow div sq

Identity (3.6): `∑_{k=1}^n binom(n, k) x^k / k² =
∑_{k=1}^n (1/k) ∑_{j=1}^k (1 + x)^j / j - ∑_{k=1}^n (1/k) ∑_{j=1}^k 1/j` for real `x`.
