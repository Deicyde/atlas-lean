# Sum pow mul coeff eq sum stirling Second derivative

Scherk's identity: for `f(x) = ∑_{k=0}^N a_k x^k` with real coefficients,
`∑_{k=0}^N k^r a_k x^k = ∑_{i=0}^r {r, i} x^i f^{(i)}(x)`.
