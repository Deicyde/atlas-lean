# Restricted Combination Count eq restricted Overlap Tiling Count

Theorem 1 (Theorem 2 of All24): `S^Q_{n,k} = B^Q_{n+q,k}` and `S^Q_n = B^Q_{n+q}`, where `q`
is the largest element of `Q` (`0` for `Q = ∅`).
