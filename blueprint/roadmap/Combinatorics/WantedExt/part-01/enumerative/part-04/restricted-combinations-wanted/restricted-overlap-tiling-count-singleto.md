# Two dominoes on a 4-board

**Two dominoes on a 4-board.** For $Q = \{1\}$ the $Q$-comb is a domino, and the number
$B^{\{1\}}_{4,2}$ of restricted-overlap tilings of a $4$-board with squares and two $Q$-combs is
$1$.
