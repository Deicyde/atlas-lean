# Initial value of the $(1,t)$-bonacci polynomials

**Initial value of the $(1,t)$-bonacci polynomials.** For every natural number $s$, the
$(1,s+1)$-bonacci polynomial of index $0$ is $f^{(s+1)}_0(x) = 1$.
