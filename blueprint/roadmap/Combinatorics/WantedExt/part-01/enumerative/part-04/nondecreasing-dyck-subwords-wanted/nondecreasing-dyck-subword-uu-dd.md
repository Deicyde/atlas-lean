# Nondecreasing Dyck subword UU DD

Subwords `UU` and `DD` in non-decreasing Dyck paths: for `w ∈ {UU, DD}`,
`F_w(x, y)`, `D_w(x)`, and the closed form of `d_w(n)` for `n ≥ 1`.
