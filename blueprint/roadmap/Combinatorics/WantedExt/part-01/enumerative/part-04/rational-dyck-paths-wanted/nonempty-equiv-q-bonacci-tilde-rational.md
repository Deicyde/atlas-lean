# Nonempty equiv q Bonacci tilde Rational

If `s = t r + 1` with `t ∈ ℕ` (and `r ≥ 1`), then `Q_n^{r/s}` and
`R̃_{n+t+1}^{r/s}` are in bijection for every `n ≥ 0`.
