# Is QBonacci Runs examples

The source's examples for `q = 4/5`: `U (UD) (DU)^2 (UD)^2 (DU)^2 D` is not in
`Q^{4/5}`, while `U (UD) (DU)^2 (UD)^4 (DU)^2 D` (last block `p₂ = 4 = r`) and
`U (UD) (DU)^2 (UD)^3 D` (`p₂ = 3 < r`, `v₂ = 0`) are.
