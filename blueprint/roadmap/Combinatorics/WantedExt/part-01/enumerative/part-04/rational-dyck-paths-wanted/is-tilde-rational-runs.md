# Is Tilde Rational Runs

The run condition of `R̃^{r/s}` (Definition 3): every block of `p` consecutive
`1`-peaks has `p ≤ r` and is followed by `v` consecutive `0`-valleys with
`p s ≤ r v`, i.e. `v ≥ ⌈p s / r⌉`.
