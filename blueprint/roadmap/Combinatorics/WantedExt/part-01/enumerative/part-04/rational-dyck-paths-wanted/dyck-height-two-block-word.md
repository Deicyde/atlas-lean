# Dyck Height Two Block Word

The block word of a Dyck word of height at most `2` and semilength `n`: for
`i < n - 1`, entry `i` is `true` when the steps `2i+1, 2i+2` form a `1`-peak `UD`
and `false` when they form a `0`-valley `DU`.
