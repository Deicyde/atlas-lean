# Genus bounds of reduced unicellular hypermonopole

Lemma 2: if `(σ, α)` is a reduced unicellular hypermonopole of genus `g` on `n`
points, then `2g + 1 ≤ n ≤ 4g`. The genus is given by Jacques' formula.
