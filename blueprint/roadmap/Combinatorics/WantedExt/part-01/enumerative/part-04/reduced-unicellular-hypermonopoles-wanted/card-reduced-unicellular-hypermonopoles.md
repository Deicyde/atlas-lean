# Card reduced unicellular hypermonopoles fin Rotate

Proposition 12: for `n ≥ 2` and `1 ≤ k ≤ n / 2`, the number `r(n, k)` of reduced
unicellular hypermonopoles `(σ, α)` with `σ = (1, …, n)` and `z(α) = k` is
`∑_{i=0}^{k-1} (-1)^i binom(n, i) H(n - i, k - i)`.
