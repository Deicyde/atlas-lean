# Sum choose mul two pow add succ

Lemma 1: for `n ≥ 1`, `V_n(2) + V_{n+1}(2) = 3 · 2^n C(2n+2, n+1)`, where
`V_n(α) = ∑_{k=0}^{n} C(n+k, k) α^k`.
