# Number of Schreier sparse sets with maximum $n$

**Number of Schreier sparse sets with maximum $n$.** For a natural number $n$, $|F_n|$ is the
number of sets $F \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ with $n \in F$ that are Schreier and sparse.
