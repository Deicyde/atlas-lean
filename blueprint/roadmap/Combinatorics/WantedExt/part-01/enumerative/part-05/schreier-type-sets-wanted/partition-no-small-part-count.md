# Partitions avoiding parts from 2 up to a bound

**Partitions avoiding parts from 2 up to a bound.** For natural numbers $n$ and $\ell$,
$|G_{n,\ell}|$ is the number of partitions of $n$ none of whose parts lies in
$\{2, \dots, \ell\}$.
