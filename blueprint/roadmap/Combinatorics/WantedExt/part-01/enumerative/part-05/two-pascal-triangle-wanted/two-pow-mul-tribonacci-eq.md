# Theorem 27

**Theorem 27.** `T_{n+2} = y_{3(n+1)} / 2^{n+1}`, with `T` the Tribonacci numbers
(`T_0 = T_1 = 0`, `T_2 = 1`) and `y` the sequence A077943,
`y_n = 2y_{n-1} - 2y_{n-2} + 2y_{n-3}`, `y_0 = y_1 = 0`, `y_2 = 1` (given by its recurrence).
