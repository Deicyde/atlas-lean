# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1** (Sprugnoli, fundamental theorem of Riordan arrays). For the Riordan
array `d_{n,k} = [t^n] d(t) h(t)^k` with `h(0) = 0` and any series `f`,
`∑_{k=0}^{n} d_{n,k} f_k = [t^n] d(t) f(h(t))`.
