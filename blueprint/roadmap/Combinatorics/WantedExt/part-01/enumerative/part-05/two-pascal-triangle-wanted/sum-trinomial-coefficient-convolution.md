# Theorem 20

**Theorem 20** (Vandermonde-type convolution). For `l ∈ ℤ`,
`∑_{k=0}^{2m} binom(n, l+k)_2 binom(m, k)_2 = binom(n+m, 2m+l)_2`, a negative lower index
giving `0`.
