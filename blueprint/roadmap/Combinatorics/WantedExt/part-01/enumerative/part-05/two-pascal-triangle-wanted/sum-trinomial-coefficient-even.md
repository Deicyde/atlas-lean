# Corollary 13

**Corollary 13.** `∑_{k≥0} binom(n, 2k)_2 z^k = ((1 + √z + z)^n + (1 - √z + z)^n)/2`,
stated for complex `z` and any `s` with `s^2 = z` in place of the indeterminate `z` and `√z`.
