# Corollary 49

**Corollary 49.** With `α`, `β` as in Theorem 6 and `g(t) = α(t) f(β(t))`,
`f_{2n} = [t^{2n}] (2-t)(1-t)^{2n} / (2(1-t+t^2)) · g(t / √(1-t+t^2))`. The square roots are
the power series `S`, `Q` of Theorem 6 and `R` with `R^2 = 1 - t + t^2`, `R(0) = 1`.
