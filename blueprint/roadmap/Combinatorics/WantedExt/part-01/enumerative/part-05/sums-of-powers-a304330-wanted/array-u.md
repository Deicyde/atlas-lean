# The array $U(k, m)$

**The array $U(k, m)$.** For natural numbers $k$ and $m$, $U(k, m)$ is the rational number
$\frac{2}{(2m)!} \sum_{j=0}^{m} (-1)^j \binom{2m}{j} (m - j)^{2k}$.
