# Sum of $2i \binom{2i+k-1}{2k-1}$

**Sum of $2i \binom{2i+k-1}{2k-1}$.** For all natural numbers $k \ge 1$ and $n \ge 1$,

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} 2i \binom{2i + k - 1}{2k - 1} = k \binom{2n + k + 1}{2k + 1}.$$
