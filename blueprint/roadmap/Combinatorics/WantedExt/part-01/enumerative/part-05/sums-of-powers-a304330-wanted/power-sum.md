# Power sums

**Power sums.** For natural numbers $k$ and $n$, the power sum $S_k(n)$ is the rational
number $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i^k = 1^k + 2^k + \cdots + n^k$, which is $0$ when $n = 0$.
