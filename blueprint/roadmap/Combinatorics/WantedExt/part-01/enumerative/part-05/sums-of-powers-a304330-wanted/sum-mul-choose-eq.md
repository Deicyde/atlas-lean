# Sum of $i \binom{i+k-1}{2k-1}$

**Sum of $i \binom{i+k-1}{2k-1}$.** For all natural numbers $k \ge 1$ and $n \ge 1$,

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i \binom{i + k - 1}{2k - 1} = \frac{k(2n + 1)}{2k + 1} \binom{n + k}{2k}.$$
