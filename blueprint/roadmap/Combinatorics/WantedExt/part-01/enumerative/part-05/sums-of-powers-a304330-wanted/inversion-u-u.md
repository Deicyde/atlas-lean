# Lemma 5 (Butzer et al., Proposition 2.3)

**Lemma 5 (Butzer et al., Proposition 2.3)**: the inversion
formula `a_k = Σ_{m=1}^k u(k, m)·b_m ⟺
b_k = Σ_{m=1}^k U(k, m)·a_m` for real sequences.
