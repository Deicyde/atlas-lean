# Perm Extrema small

The left-to-right and right-to-left extrema of `132` and `213`: `132` has
`lrmax = rlmax = rlmin = 2` and `lrmin = 1`; `213` has `lrmax = lrmin = rlmin = 2` and `rlmax = 1`.
