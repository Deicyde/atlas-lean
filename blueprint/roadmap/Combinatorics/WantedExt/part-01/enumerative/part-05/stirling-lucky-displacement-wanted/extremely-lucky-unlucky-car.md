# Extremely lucky unlucky car

Let `w ∈ Q_n` be extremely lucky. For `i ∈ [n]`, the `i`-th unlucky car `j`
(the unlucky car with exactly `i - 1` unlucky cars before it) (a) prefers spot
`n - i + 1` and (b) has displacement `2i - 1`.
