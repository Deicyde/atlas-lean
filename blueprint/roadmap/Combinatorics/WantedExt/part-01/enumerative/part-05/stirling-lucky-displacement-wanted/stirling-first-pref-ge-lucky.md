# Stirling first pref ge lucky

Let `w ∈ Q_n` with `lucky(w) ≥ 3` and `x` the second-smallest element of
`Lucky(w)`. If `x ≥ n`, then `w(1) ≥ lucky(w)`, and every lucky car parks in one
of the first `w(1)` spots.
