# The reversal of an extraTrib is not an extraTrib

**The reversal of an extraTrib is not an extraTrib.** If $S : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$
is an extraTrib, then the sequence $n \mapsto |S(-n)|$ is not an extraTrib.
