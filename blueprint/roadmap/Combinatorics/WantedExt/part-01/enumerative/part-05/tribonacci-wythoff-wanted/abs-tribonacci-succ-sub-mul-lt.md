# Abs tribonacci succ sub mul lt

With `α` the real root and `β`, `γ = conj β` the non-real roots of
`x³ = x² + x + 1`, `|T_{n+1} - α T_n| < 2 α^{-n/2} / |β - γ|` for every `n`.
