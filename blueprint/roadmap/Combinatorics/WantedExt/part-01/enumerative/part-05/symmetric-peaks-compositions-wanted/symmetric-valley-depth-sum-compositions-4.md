# Symmetric Valley Depth Sum compositions length three

`dsv(n, 3) = ∑_{m=1}^{⌊(n-2)/3⌋} d_m` with `d_m = (n - 3m) / 2` if `n - m` is even and
`d_m = 0` otherwise.
