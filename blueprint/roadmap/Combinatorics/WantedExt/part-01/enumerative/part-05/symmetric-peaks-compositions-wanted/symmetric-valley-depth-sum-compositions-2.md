# Symmetric Valley Depth Sum compositions closed form

Closed form of `dsv(n)`, with `ω± = 1 ± i√3`:
`dsv(n) = (-6n + 7)(-1)ⁿ / 108 + 2ⁿ (21n - 79) / 1323
+ (-33 - 15√3 i)(-2)ⁿ / (441 ω₊^{n+1}) + (-33 + 15√3 i)(-2)ⁿ / (441 ω₋^{n+1}) + 1/12`
for all `n ≥ 0`.
