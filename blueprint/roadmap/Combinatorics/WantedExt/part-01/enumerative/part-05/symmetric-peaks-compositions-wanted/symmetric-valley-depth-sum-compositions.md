# Symmetric Valley Depth Sum compositions gen Fun

Generating function of `dsv(n)`:
`∑ dsv(n) xⁿ = (x⁵ - 2x⁶ + x⁷) / ((1 - 2x)² (1 - x³)(1 - x²)²)`, stated after clearing
denominators.
