# The sum $\Psi_\alpha(k)$

**The sum $\Psi_\alpha(k)$.** For a sequence $\alpha = (\alpha_0, \alpha_1, \dots)$ of
rationals and a natural number $k$, $\Psi_\alpha(k) = \sum_{l=0}^{k-1} \frac{\alpha_l}{(k-1-l)!}$;
in particular $\Psi_\alpha(0) = 0$.
