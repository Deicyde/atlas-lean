# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4.** If a family `𝒰 ⊆ 𝒫(X)` is an upset (L1) and for every `A ⊆ X` exactly one of
`A` and `Aᶜ` lies in `𝒰` (L2), then `𝒰` is a maximal linked system.

The paper's hypothesis `n ≥ 1` is dropped: for `n = 0`, `∅ᶜ = ∅` and (L2) cannot hold.
