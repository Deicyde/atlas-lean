# Theorem 6

**Theorem 6.** For `n ≥ 0`, `λ_{n+1} = |{b ∈ D_n | b ≤ b*}|`, and
`λ_{n+1} = ∑_{b ∈ R_n, b ≤ b*} γ(b)`.
