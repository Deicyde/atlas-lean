# Corollary 16

**Corollary 16.** `Φ(π, Λ_8) = ∅` for `π` each of `(12345678)`, `(12)(345678)`,
`(1234)(5678)`, `(12)(34)(5678)` and `(12)(34)(56)(78)`.

Points `1, …, 8` are `0, …, 7` in `Fin 8`.
