# Theorem 10

**Theorem 10.** For `n ≥ 0`, `λ_{n+2}` is the number of pairs `b, d ∈ D_n` with
`d ≥ b | b*`: `λ_{n+2} = ∑_{b ∈ D_n} re(b | b*, ⊤)`, and
`λ_{n+2} = ∑_{b ∈ R_n} γ(b) re(b | b*, ⊤)`.
