# Stirling QMatrix

The strictly upper triangular matrix `Q^[N](n)` with
`Q^[N]_{i,j} = (-1)^{j-i+1} i^{N-1} / ((i-1)! (j-i-1)!)` for `j ≥ i + 1`, zero
otherwise.
