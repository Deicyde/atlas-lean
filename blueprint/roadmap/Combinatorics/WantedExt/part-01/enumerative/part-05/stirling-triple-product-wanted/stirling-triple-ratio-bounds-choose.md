# Stirling triple ratio bounds choose

Theorem 2.1: for positive integers `n ≥ p` and `j`,
`{p+j, j}{n+j, p+j}/{n+j, j} ≥ C(n, p) ≥ [p+j, j][n+j, p+j]/[n+j, j]`.
