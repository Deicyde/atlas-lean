# Mutually latin soulmate profiles card

The number of profiles whose men's and women's preferences both form Latin
squares and that have exactly `k` soulmate pairs is
`(L_n² / n!) C(n, k) ∑_{i=0}^{n-k} (-1)^i (n-k)!/i!`.
