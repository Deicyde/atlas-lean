# Soulmate profiles card

The number `F(k, n)` of preference profiles with exactly `k` soulmate pairs
is `C(n, k)² k! ((n-1)!)^{2k} S(k, n)`, with `S(k, n)` the inclusion-exclusion
sum of Lemma 4.
