# Theorem 17

**Theorem 17.** For `A` any of `{1̄0̄, 1̄0, 0̄1̄, 0̄0̄, 0̄0, 01̄}`, `{1̄0̄, 1̄0, 0̄1̄, 0̄0̄,
0̄0, 00̄}`, `{1̄0̄, 0̄1̄, 0̄0̄, 0̄0, 0̄1, 10̄}`, `|Ī_n(A)| = 2 · n!` for all `n ≥ 1`.
