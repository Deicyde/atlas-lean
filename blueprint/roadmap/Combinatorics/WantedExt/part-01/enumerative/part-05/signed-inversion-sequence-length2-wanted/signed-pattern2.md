# Signed Pattern2

A length-2 signed pattern `τ₁τ₂`: `ord` is the comparison of `|τ₁|` with `|τ₂|` (`.eq` for
`00`, `.lt` for `01`, `.gt` for `10`), and `bar₁`, `bar₂` say whether `τ₁`, `τ₂` are barred.
