# Ramanujan Theta order nine product

`f(-q, -q⁸) f(-q², -q⁷) f(-q⁴, -q⁵) = f(-q) f³(-q⁹) / f(-q³)` for `‖q‖ < 1`, stated with
`f(-q³)` cleared.
