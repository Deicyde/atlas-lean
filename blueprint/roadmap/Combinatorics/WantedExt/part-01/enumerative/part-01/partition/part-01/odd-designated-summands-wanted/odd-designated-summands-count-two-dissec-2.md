# Odd Designated Summands Count two dissection mod four

The 2-dissection modulo 4: `∑ PD_2(2n) q^n ≡ f_4^2 / (f_1^2 f_3^2) (mod 4)` and
`∑ PD_2(2n+1) q^n ≡ f_6^2 (mod 4)`, coefficientwise, the first with its denominator cleared.
