# Odd Designated Summands Count four dissection mod four

The 4-dissection modulo 4: `∑ PD_2(4n) q^n ≡ (f_2^3 / f_6)^2 + q f_12^2 (mod 4)` (the
paper's eq. (3)) and `∑ PD_2(4n+2) q^n ≡ 2 f_2^3 f_6 (mod 4)`, coefficientwise, the first with
its denominator `f_6^2` cleared.
