# Partition Ferrers Board

The Ferrers board of a partition `p` of `n`: cell `(i, j)` (row `i`,
column `j`, both zero-based) is present iff the `(i+1)`-st largest part
exceeds `j`, i.e. iff more than `i` parts exceed `j`.
