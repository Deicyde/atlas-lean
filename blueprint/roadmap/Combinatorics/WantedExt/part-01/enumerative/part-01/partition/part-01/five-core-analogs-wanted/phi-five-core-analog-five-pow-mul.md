# Phi Five Core Analog five pow mul

Theorem 2 recurrence: for `k ≥ 2` and `n ≥ 0`,
`ā_5(5^k n) = ((5^k - 1)/4) ā_5(5n) - ((5^k - 5)/4) ā_5(n)` (both divisions are exact).
