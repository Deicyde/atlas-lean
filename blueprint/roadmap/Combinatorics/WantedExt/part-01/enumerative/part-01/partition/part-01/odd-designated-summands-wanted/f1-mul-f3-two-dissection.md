# F1 mul f3 two dissection

`f_1 f_3 = f_2 f_8^2 f_12^4 / (f_4^2 f_6 f_24^2) - q f_4^4 f_6 f_24^2 / (f_2 f_8^2 f_12^2)`
(Hirschhorn, *The Power of q*, eq. (30.12.1)), stated with denominators cleared (both sides
multiplied by `f_2 f_4^2 f_6 f_8^2 f_12^2 f_24^2`). This is the second identity of the paper's
Lemma 3; the first is `one_div_f1_f3_two_dissection`.
