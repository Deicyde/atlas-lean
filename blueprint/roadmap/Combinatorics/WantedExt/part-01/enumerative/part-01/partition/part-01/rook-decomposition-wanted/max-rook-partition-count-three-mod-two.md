# Parity of $R_3(n)$

**Parity of $R_3(n)$.** Let $R_3(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ with max-rook number
$3$. For every natural number $n > 9$, $R_3(n)$ is even if $n$ is odd and odd if $n$ is even.
