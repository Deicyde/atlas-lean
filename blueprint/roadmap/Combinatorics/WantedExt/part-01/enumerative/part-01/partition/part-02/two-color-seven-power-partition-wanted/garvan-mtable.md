# Garvan MTable

Rows `1`-`7` of Garvan's matrix `M = (m_{i,j})` as printed in Tables 1 and 2 (columns
`1`-`14`); every other entry of these rows is `0`, and the value is `0` for `i = 0` or `j = 0`
or `i > 7`.
