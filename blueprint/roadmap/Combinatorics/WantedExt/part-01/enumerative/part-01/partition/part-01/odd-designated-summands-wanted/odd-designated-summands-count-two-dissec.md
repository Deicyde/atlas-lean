# Odd Designated Summands Count two dissection

The 2-dissection `∑ PD_2(2n) q^n = f_4^2 f_6^4 / (f_1^2 f_3^2 f_12^2)` and
`∑ PD_2(2n+1) q^n = f_2^6 f_12^2 / (f_1^4 f_4^2 f_6^2)`, stated with denominators cleared.
