# Perimeter Distinct Count shift inequalities

Proposition 1.6, `h` part: for positive `d, n, a`, `h_d^(a+1)(n) ≤ h_d^(a)(n)`,
`h_{d+1}^(a)(n) ≤ h_d^(a)(n)` and `h_d^(a)(n) ≤ h_d^(a)(n + 1)`.
