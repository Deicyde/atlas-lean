# Sum Distinct Parts small values

Characteristic values: `a_{r,m}(0) = s_m(0) = 0` (the empty partition has no parts), and
for the single partition `1` of `1`: `a_{0,1}(1) = s_1(1) = 1`, `s_2(1) = 0`.
