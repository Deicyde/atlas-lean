# Sigma eq sum n Color distinct Parts Diff

Corollary 8: for `m ≥ 1`, `σ(m) = ∑_{k=1}^{m} (C_o(D, k) - C_e(D, k)) C(m-k)`, with
`C_o(D, ·)`, `C_e(D, ·)` as in Corollary 7.
