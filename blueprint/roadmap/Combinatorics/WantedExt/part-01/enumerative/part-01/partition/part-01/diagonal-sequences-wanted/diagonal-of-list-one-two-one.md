# The list sequence of $(1, 2, 1)$

**The list sequence of $(1, 2, 1)$.** The sequence given by the list $(1, 2, 1)$ takes the
values $0, 1, 2, 1, 0, 0$ at the indices $0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$.
