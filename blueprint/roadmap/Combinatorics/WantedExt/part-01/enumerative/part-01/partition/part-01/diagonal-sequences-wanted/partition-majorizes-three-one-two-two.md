# Majorization of $(2, 2)$ by $(3, 1)$

**Majorization of $(2, 2)$ by $(3, 1)$.** The partition $(3, 1)$ majorizes the partition
$(2, 2)$: $3 \ge 2$ and $3 + 1 \ge 2 + 2$.
