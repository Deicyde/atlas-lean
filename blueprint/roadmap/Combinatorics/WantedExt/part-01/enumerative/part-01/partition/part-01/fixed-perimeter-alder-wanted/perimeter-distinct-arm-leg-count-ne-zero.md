# Perimeter Distinct Arm Leg Count ne zero iff and eq choose

Lemma 3.1: for positive `d, a, α, λ` with `a ≤ d + 1`, `h_d^(a)(α, λ) ≠ 0` iff
`α ≥ a + d(λ - 1)`, and then `h_d^(a)(α, λ) = C(α - a - (d - 1)(λ - 1), λ - 1)`.
