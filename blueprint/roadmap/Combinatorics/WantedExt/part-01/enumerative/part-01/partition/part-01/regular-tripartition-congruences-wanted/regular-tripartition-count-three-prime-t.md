# Regular Tripartition Count three prime three mod four

For a prime `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, `α, n ≥ 0` and `1 ≤ i ≤ p - 1`,
`T_3(3 p^{2α+1} (p n + i) + (p^{2α+2} - 1)/4) ≡ 0 (mod 3)` (the division is exact).
