# F2 div f1 sq dissection

`f_2 / f_1^2 = f_6^4 f_9^6 / (f_3^8 f_18^3) + 2 q f_6^3 f_9^3 / f_3^7
+ 4 q^2 f_6^2 f_18^3 / f_3^6`, stated with denominators cleared
(both sides multiplied by the product of all denominators).
