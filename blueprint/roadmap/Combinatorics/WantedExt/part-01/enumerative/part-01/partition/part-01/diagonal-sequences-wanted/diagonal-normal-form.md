# Diagonal Normal Form

The normal form `(1, 2, …, q - 1, q, q^{(s_q)}, (q-1)^{(s_{q-1})}, …, 1^{(s_1)})` of a
diagonal sequence, as a list: `1, …, q` followed by `s_v` copies of `v` for `v = q, q-1, …, 1`.
