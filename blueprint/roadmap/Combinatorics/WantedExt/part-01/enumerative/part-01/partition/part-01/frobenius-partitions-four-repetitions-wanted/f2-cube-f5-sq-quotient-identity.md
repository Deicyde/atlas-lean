# F2 cube f5 sq quotient identity

`f₂³ f₅²/(f₁⁵ f₁₀²) = f₅/(f₂² f₁₀) + 5q f₁₀²/(f₁³ f₂)`, stated with denominators cleared (both
sides multiplied by `f₁⁵ f₂² f₁₀²`). The lemma's other identity (16) is the last conjunct of
`theta_level_five_identities`.
