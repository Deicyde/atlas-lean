# Odd Designated Summands Count eight dissection mod four

`∑ PD_2(8n) q^n ≡ (f_1^3 / f_3)^2 (mod 4)` and `∑ PD_2(8n+4) q^n ≡ f_6^2 (mod 4)`,
coefficientwise, the first with its denominator `f_3^2` cleared.
