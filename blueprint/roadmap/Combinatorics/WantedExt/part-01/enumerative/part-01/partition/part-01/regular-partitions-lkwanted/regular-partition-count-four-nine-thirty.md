# $b_{4,9}(32n + 25)$ is divisible by $4$

**$b_{4,9}(32n + 25)$ is divisible by $4$.** Let $b_{4,9}(n)$ be the number of partitions of
$n$ with no part divisible by $4$ or by $9$. For every natural number $n$,
$b_{4,9}(32n + 25) \equiv 0 \pmod 4$.
