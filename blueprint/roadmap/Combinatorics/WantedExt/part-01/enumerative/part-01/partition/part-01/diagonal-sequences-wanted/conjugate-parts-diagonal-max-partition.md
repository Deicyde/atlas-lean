# Conjugate Parts diagonal Max Partition

Proposition 2: the conjugate of `ᾱ` is `α̲ = (q^{(s_q + 1)}, …, 1^{(s_1 + 1)})`. The
paper's hypothesis (a partition `α` with this normal form) only fixes `q` and `s` and is
omitted.
