# The conjugate of $(3, 1)$ is $(2, 1, 1)$

**The conjugate of $(3, 1)$ is $(2, 1, 1)$.** The conjugate of the partition with parts
$\{3, 1\}$ is the partition with parts $\{2, 1, 1\}$.
