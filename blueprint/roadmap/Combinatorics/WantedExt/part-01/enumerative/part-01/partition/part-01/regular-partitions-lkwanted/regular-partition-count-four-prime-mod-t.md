# Regular Partition Count four prime mod two

For a prime `p ≥ 5` and `r` with `8r + 1` a quadratic nonresidue modulo `p`,
`b_{4,p}(pn + r) ≡ 0 (mod 2)` for all `n ≥ 0`. Here `r ∈ ℕ`; every residue class mod `p`
has such a representative.
