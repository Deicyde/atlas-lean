# Lemma 5

**Lemma 5.** For `j, k ≥ 1` and `β ≥ 0`, `π(y^{(2k)}_{2β+1,j}) ≥ k + β + ⌊(7j-7)/4⌋` and
`π(y^{(2k)}_{2β+2,j}) ≥ k + β + 1 + ⌊(7j-7)/4⌋` (`π` the `7`-adic valuation, stated as
divisibility).
