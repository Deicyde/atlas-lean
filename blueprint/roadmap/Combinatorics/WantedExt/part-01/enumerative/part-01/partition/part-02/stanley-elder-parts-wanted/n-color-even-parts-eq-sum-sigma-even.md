# N Color even Parts eq sum sigma Even

Corollary 3: for `m ≥ 1`, `A_e(m) = ∑_{k=1}^{m} σ_even(k) C(m-k)`, where `A_e(m)` is the
total number of even parts over the `n`-color partitions of `m`.
