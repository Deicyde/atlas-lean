# Regular Tripartition Count

The number `T_ℓ(n)` of `ℓ`-regular tripartitions of `n`: triples `(λ₁, λ₂, λ₃)` of
partitions with `|λ₁| + |λ₂| + |λ₃| = n` in which no part of any `λᵢ` is divisible by `ℓ`.
