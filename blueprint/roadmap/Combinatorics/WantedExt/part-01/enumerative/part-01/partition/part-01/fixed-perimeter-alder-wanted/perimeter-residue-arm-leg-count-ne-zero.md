# Perimeter Residue Arm Leg Count ne zero iff and eq choose

Lemma 3.2: for positive `d, a, α, λ` with `a ≤ d + 1`, `f_d^(a)(α, λ) ≠ 0` iff `α ≥ a` and
`α ≡ a (mod d + 1)`, and then `f_d^(a)(α, λ) = C((α - a)/(d + 1) + λ - 1, λ - 1)`.
