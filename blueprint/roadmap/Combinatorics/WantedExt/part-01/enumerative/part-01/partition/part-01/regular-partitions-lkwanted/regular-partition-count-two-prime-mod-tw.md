# Regular Partition Count two prime mod two

For a prime `p ≥ 5` and `r` with `24r + 1` a quadratic nonresidue modulo `p`,
`b_{2,p}(pn + r) ≡ 0 (mod 2)` for all `n ≥ 0`. Here `r ∈ ℕ`.
