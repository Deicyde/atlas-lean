# Sigma Odd eq sum n Color parts Diff mul distinct

Corollary 2: for `m ≥ 1`, `σ_odd(m) = ∑_{k=1}^{m} (T_o(k) - T_e(k)) C(D, m-k)`, with
`T_o`, `T_e` as in Corollary 1 and `C(D, m)` the number of `n`-color partitions of `m` into
distinct parts.
