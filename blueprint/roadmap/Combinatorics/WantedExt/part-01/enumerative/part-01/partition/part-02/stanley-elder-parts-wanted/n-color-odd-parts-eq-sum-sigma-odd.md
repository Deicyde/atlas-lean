# N Color odd Parts eq sum sigma Odd

Corollary 4: for `m ≥ 1`, `A_o(m) = ∑_{k=1}^{m} σ_odd(k) C(m-k)`, where `A_o(m)` is the
total number of odd parts over the `n`-color partitions of `m`.
