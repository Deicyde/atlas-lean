# Sum Distinct Parts Mult At Least eq sum partition

Merca's identity (ii): for `m ≥ 1`, `s_m(n) = ∑_{j ≥ 0} j p(n - mj)`. The paper's
hypothesis `0 ≤ r < m` forces `m ≥ 1`; for `m = 0` the right side diverges.
