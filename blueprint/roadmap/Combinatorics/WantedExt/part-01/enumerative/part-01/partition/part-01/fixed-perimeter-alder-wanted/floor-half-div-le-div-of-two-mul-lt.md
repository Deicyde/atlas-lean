# Floor half div le div of two mul lt

Lemma 4.2: for positive `d, a, n` with `a < (d + 3)/2` and `n ≥ a`,
`⌊⌊(n - a)/(d + 1)⌋ / 2⌋ ≤ ⌊(n - a)/(d + 3)⌋` and
`⌊(⌊(n - a)/(d + 1)⌋ + 1) / 2⌋ ≤ ⌊(n + a)/(d + 3)⌋`, in natural-number division (the lower
bounds `0 ≤ …` are automatic).
