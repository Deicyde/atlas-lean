# Regular Tripartition Count two prime ge five even

For a prime `p ≥ 5`, `α, n ≥ 0` and `1 ≤ i ≤ p - 1`,
`T_2(3 p^{2α+1} (p n + i) + (p^{2α+2} - 1)/8) ≡ 0 (mod 2)` (the division is exact).
