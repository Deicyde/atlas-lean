# Sum Distinct Parts Mod Eq pentagonal eq pos Rank

For `0 ≤ r < m`, `∑_{j ≥ 1} (-1)^{j+1} a_{r,m}(n - j(3j+1)/2)
= ∑_{j ≥ 0} (mj + r) R(n - mj - r)`, with `R(k)` the number of partitions of `k` with positive
rank.
