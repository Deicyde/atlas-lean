# N Color even Parts generating Function

Theorem 4: with `A_e(m)` the total number of even parts over the `n`-color partitions of `m`,
`∑_{m ≥ 1} 2m q^{2m} / (1 - q^{2m}) = ∏_{m ≥ 1} (1 - q^m)^m ∑_{m ≥ 1} A_e(m) q^m`.
