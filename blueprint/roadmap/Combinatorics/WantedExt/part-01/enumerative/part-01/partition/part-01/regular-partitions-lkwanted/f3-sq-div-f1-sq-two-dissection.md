# F3 sq div f1 sq two dissection

`f_3^2 / f_1^2 = f_4^4 f_6 f_12^2 / (f_2^5 f_8 f_24) + 2q f_4 f_6^2 f_8 f_24 / (f_2^4 f_12)`,
stated with denominators cleared (both sides multiplied by `f_1^2 f_2^5 f_8 f_12 f_24`).
