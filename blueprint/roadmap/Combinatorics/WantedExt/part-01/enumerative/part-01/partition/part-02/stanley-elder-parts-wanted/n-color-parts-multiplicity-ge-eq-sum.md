# N Color parts Multiplicity Ge eq sum

Corollary 5: for `m ≥ t ≥ 1`, `N^t(m) = ∑_{k=1}^{⌊m/t⌋} k C(m - tk)`, where `N^t(m)` is
the number of distinct colored parts with multiplicity at least `t`, summed over the `n`-color
partitions of `m`.
