# F2 div f1 f4 dissection

`f_2 / (f_1 f_4) = f_18^9 / (f_3^2 f_9^3 f_12^2 f_36^3) + q f_6^2 f_18^3 / (f_3^3 f_12^3)
+ q^2 f_6^4 f_9^3 f_36^3 / (f_3^4 f_12^4 f_18^3)`, stated with denominators cleared
(both sides multiplied by the product of all denominators).
