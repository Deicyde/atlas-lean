# Vn Arrangement Count

The number of `k`-vn-arrangements of a finite multiset `M` of integers: orderings
`(v_1, …, v_b)` of `M` with `v_{i+1} - v_i ≤ k` for all `i`. For `k = 1` these are the
vn-arrangements.
