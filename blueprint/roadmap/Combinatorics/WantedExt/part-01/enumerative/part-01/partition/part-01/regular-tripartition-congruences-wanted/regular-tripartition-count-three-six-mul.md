# A congruence modulo 6 for 3-regular tripartitions

**A congruence modulo 6 for 3-regular tripartitions.** Let $T_3(m)$ be the number of
$3$-regular tripartitions of $m$. For every natural number $n$, $6 \mid T_3(6n + 4)$.
