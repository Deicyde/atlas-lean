# Lemma 6

**Lemma 6.** For `j, k ≥ 1` and `β ≥ 0`, `π(y^{(2k-1)}_{β+1,j}) ≥ k + ⌊(7j-7)/4⌋` (`π` the
`7`-adic valuation, stated as divisibility).
