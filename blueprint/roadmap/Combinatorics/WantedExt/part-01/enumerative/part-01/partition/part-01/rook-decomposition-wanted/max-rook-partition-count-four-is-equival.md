# Asymptotics of partitions with max-rook number 4

**Asymptotics of partitions with max-rook number 4.** Let $R_4(n)$ be the number of partitions
of $n$ with max-rook number $4$. Then $R_4(n) \sim n^3/9$ as $n \to \infty$.
