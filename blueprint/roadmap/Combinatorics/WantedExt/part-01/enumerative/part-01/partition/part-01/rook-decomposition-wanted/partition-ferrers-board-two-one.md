# Ferrers board of the partition $(2, 1)$

**Ferrers board of the partition $(2, 1)$.** With rows and columns numbered from $0$, the
Ferrers board of the partition $(2, 1)$ of $3$ consists of exactly the three cells $(0, 0)$,
$(0, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$.
