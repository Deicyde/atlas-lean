# Sum Distinct Parts Mod Eq pentagonal eq nonneg Rank

For `0 ≤ r < m`, `∑_{j ≥ 0} (-1)^j a_{r,m}(n - j(3j+1)/2) = ∑_{j ≥ 0} (mj + r) N(n - mj - r)`,
with `N(k)` the number of partitions of `k` with nonnegative rank.
