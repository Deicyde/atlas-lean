# One div f1 f2 dissection

`1 / (f_1 f_2) = f_9^9 / (f_3^6 f_6^2 f_18^3) + q f_9^6 / (f_3^5 f_6^3)
+ 3 q^2 f_9^3 f_18^3 / (f_3^4 f_6^4) - 2 q^3 f_18^6 / (f_3^3 f_6^5)
+ 4 q^4 f_18^9 / (f_3^2 f_6^6 f_9^3)`, stated with denominators cleared
(both sides multiplied by the product of all denominators).
