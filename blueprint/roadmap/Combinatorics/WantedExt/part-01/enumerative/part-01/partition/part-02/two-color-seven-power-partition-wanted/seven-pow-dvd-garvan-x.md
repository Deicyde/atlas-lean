# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4** (Garvan). `π(x_{1,1}) = 1`, `π(x_{1,2}) = 2`, and for `k, j ≥ 1`,
`π(x_{2k+1,j}) ≥ k + 1 + ⌊(7j-4)/4⌋` and `π(x_{2k,j}) ≥ k + 1 + ⌊(7j-6)/4⌋`, with `π` the
`7`-adic valuation (`π(0) = ∞`); the bounds are stated as divisibility.
