# F4 div f1 dissection

`f_4 / f_1 = f_12 f_18^4 / (f_3^3 f_36^2) + q f_6^2 f_9^3 f_36 / (f_3^4 f_18^2)
+ 2 q^2 f_6 f_18 f_36 / f_3^3`, stated with denominators cleared
(both sides multiplied by the product of all denominators).
