# F1 f2 dissection

`f_1 f_2 = f_6 f_9^4 / (f_3 f_18^2) - q f_9 f_18 - 2 q^2 f_3 f_18^4 / (f_6 f_9^2)`, stated with denominators cleared
(both sides multiplied by the product of all denominators).
