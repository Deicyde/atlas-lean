# Overcubic Triple Count five Hundred Seventy Six mul add twenty Four

For all `n ≥ 0`, `bt(576n+24) ≡ 4 [q^n] f_1 (mod 8)`, i.e.
`∑ bt(576n+24) q^n ≡ 4 f_1 (mod 8)`. This is the `α = 0` case of the paper's statement.
