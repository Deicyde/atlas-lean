# F1 cube three dissection

`f_1^3 = f_6 f_9^6 / (f_3 f_18^3) + 4 q^3 f_3^2 f_18^6 / (f_6^2 f_9^3) - 3 q f_9^3`,
stated with denominators cleared (both sides multiplied by `f_3 f_6^2 f_9^3 f_18^3`).
