# Sum Distinct Parts Mod Eq twice Square eq odd Distinct

For `0 ≤ r < m`, `a_{r,m}(n) + 2 ∑_{k ≥ 1} (-1)^k a_{r,m}(n - 2k²)
= ∑_{j ≥ 0} (mj + r) q_odd(n - mj - r)`, with `q_odd(k)` the number of partitions of `k` into
distinct odd parts.
