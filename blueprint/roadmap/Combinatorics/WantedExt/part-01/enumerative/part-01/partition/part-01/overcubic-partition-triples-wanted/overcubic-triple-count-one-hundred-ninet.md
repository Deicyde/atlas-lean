# Overcubic Triple Count one Hundred Ninety Two mul add eight

For all `n ≥ 0`, `bt(192n+8) ≡ 2 [q^n] f_1 (mod 4)`, i.e.
`∑ bt(192n+8) q^n ≡ 2 f_1 (mod 4)`. This is the `α = 0` case of the paper's statement.
