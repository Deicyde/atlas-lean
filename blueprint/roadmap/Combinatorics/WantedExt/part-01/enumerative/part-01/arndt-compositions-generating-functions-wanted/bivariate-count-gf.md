# Bivariate Count GF

The bivariate generating function `∑_{n, m} a(n, m) xⁿ yᵐ` of a two-index count `a` with
`a(n, m) = 0` for `m > n`, by weight (`x`) and second index (`y`), in `ℤ[y][[x]]`.
