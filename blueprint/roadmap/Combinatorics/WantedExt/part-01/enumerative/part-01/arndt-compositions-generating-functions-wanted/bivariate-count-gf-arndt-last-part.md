# Bivariate Count GF arndt Last Part

The generating function of Arndt compositions by weight (`x`) and last part (`y`) is
`B(x, y) = (1 - x - x² - x²y + 2x³y + 2x⁴y - x⁵y - x⁴y²)/((1 - x - x²)(1 - xy)(1 - x²y))`.
