# Sample values of the $k$-Arndt composition counts

**Sample values of the $k$-Arndt composition counts.** Writing $a_k(n, m)$ for the number of
$k$-Arndt compositions of $n$ with $m$ parts, $a_3(7, 2) = 1$, $a_3(7, 3) = 1$ and
$a_{-3}(4, 2) = 3$.
