# Leading coefficient of an S-polynomial

**Leading coefficient of an S-polynomial.** For every natural number $l$, the coefficient
$s_l = s_{l,l}$ of $x^l$ in Zurita's polynomial $S_l$ equals $8/(1 - 2l)$.
