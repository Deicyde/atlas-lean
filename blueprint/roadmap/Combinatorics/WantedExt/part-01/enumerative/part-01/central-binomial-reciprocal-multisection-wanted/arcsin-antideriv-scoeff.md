# Arcsin Antideriv SCoeff

Coefficient `s_k` of the paper's polynomial `S_l`: `s_l = 8/(1 - 2l)`,
`s_k = (8(k + 1)/(2k - 1)) s_{k+1}` for `0 ≤ k < l`, and `s_k = 0` for `k > l`.
