# Tsum div mul central Binom mul eq multisection

For `m ≥ 1` and `|x| < 4^m`,
`L_m(x) = ∑_{k ≥ 1} x^k / (k binom(2mk, mk)) = ∑_{j=0}^{m-1} L(w^j x^{1/m})` with
`L(y) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} y^n / (n binom(2n, n))` and `w = e^{2πi/m}`.
