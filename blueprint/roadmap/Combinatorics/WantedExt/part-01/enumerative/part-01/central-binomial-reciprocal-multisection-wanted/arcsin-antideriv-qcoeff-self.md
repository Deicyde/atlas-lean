# Leading coefficient of a Q-polynomial

**Leading coefficient of a Q-polynomial.** For every natural number $l \ge 1$, the coefficient
$q_l = q_{l,l}$ of $x^l$ in Zurita's polynomial $Q_l$ equals $-4/l$.
