# Arcsin Antideriv QCoeff

Coefficient `q_k` of the paper's polynomial `Q_l` (`l ≥ 1`): `q_l = -4/l`,
`q_k = ((4k + 6)/k) q_{k+1}` for `1 ≤ k < l`, `q_0 = -12 q_1`, and `q_k = 0` for `k > l`.
