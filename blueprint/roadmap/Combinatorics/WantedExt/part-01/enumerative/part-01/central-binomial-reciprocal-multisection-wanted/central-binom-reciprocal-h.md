# Central Binom Reciprocal H

The closed form `H(x) = 4√x arcsin(√x/2) / (4 - x)^{3/2} + 4/(4 - x)` of
`∑_{n ≥ 0} x^n / binom(2n, n)`, on the real interval `0 ≤ x < 4`.
