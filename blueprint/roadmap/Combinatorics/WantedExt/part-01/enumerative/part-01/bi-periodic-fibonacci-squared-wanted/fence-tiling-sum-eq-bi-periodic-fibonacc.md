# Fence Tiling Sum eq bi Periodic Fibonacci Sq

Theorem 2.2: for nonzero `a`, `b` and `n ≥ 0`, `q²_{n+1}` is the weighted number `S_n` of
tilings of an `n`-board by weighted half-squares and `(1/2, 1/2)`-fences.
