# Fib succ sq eq sum choose mul choose

Corollary 3.3: for `n ≥ 0`,
`F²_{n+1} = ∑_{i=0}^{⌊2n/3⌋} ∑_{j=0}^{i} C(i, j) C(2n-3i, j) 2^j`.
