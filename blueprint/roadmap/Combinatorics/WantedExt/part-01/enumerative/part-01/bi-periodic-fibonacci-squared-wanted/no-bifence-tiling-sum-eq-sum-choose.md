# No Bifence Tiling Sum eq sum choose

Lemma 3.15: for nonzero `a`, `b`, the weighted number `C_n` of tilings with no bifence is
`(a/b)^{ξ(n)} ∑_{i=0}^{⌊2n/3⌋} C(2n-2i, i) (ab)^{n-i}`.
