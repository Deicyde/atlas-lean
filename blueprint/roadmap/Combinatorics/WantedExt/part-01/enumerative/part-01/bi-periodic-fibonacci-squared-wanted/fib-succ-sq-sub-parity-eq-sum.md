# Fib succ sq sub parity eq sum

Corollary 3.10: for `n ≥ 1`,
`F²_{n+1} - ξ(n+1) = ∑_{k=1}^{n} (n-k+1) F²_k = ∑_{k=1}^{n} k F²_{n-k+1}`.
