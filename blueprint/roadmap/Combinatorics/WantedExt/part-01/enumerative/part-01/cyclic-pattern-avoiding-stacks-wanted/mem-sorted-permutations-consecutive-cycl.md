# Mem sorted Permutations consecutive Cyclic Stack Sort 321 iff

For `n ≥ 2` and every permutation `π` of `[n]`, `π ∈ Sort_n(s^[321])` if and only if
`π_1 = n` and `π_2 ⋯ π_n` is good.
