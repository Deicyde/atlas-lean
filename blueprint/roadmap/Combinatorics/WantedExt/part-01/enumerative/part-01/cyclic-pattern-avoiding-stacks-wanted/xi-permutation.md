# Xi Permutation

The permutation `ξ_n`: the entries of `[n]` of parity different from `n`, increasing, followed
by those of the parity of `n`, increasing (`ξ_n = 1,3,…,n-1,2,4,…,n` for even `n` and
`2,4,…,n-1,1,3,…,n` for odd `n`).
