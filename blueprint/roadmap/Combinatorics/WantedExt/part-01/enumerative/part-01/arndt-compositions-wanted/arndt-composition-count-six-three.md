# Arndt compositions of 6 with three parts

**Arndt compositions of 6 with three parts.** There are exactly $4$ Arndt compositions of $6$
with three parts.

They are $(4, 1, 1)$, $(3, 2, 1)$, $(3, 1, 2)$ and $(2, 1, 3)$.
