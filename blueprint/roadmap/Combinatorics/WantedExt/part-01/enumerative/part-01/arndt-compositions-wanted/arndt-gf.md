# Arndt GF

The bivariate generating function `A_S(x, y) = ∑_{n, k} a_S(n, k) xⁿ yᵏ` of Arndt
compositions with parts in `S`, by weight (`x`) and number of parts (`y`), in `ℤ[y][[x]]`.
