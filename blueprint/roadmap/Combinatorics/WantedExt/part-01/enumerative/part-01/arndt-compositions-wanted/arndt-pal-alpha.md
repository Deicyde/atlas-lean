# Arndt Pal Alpha

The series `α(x, y, w) = ∑_{n ≥ 0} (-1)ⁿ x^{n²+n} y^{2n} wⁿ / (xw; x)_{2n}` in
`ℚ[[x, y, z]]`, for a series `w` (the paper's third argument; `w = z` and `w = x` occur).
