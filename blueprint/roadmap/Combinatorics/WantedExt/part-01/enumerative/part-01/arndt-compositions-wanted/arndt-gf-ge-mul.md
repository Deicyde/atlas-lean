# Arndt GF ge mul

The generating function `B(x, y; ℓ)` of Arndt compositions with smallest part at least `ℓ`
is `(1 - x²)(1 - x + x^ℓ y) / (1 - x - x² + x³ - x^{2ℓ+1}y²)` for `ℓ ≥ 1`, stated
multiplicatively.
