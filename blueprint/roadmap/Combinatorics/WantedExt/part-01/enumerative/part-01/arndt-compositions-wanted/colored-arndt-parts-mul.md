# Colored Arndt parts mul

`C_k(x) = ∑_n c(n, k) xⁿ = x^{k+⌊k/2⌋}(x² + x + 2)^{⌊k/2⌋} / ((1 - x)^{2k}(1 + x)^{3⌊k/2⌋})`,
stated multiplicatively.
