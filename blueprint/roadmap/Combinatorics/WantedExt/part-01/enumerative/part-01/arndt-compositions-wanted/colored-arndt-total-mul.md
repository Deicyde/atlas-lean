# Colored Arndt total mul

`∑ c(n) xⁿ = (1 - x)²(1 + x)³(1 - x + x²) / (1 - x - 3x² + x³ + 2x⁴ - 4x⁵ - x⁶ + x⁷)`, with
`c(n) = ∑_k c(n, k)`, stated multiplicatively.
