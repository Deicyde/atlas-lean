# Arndt Pal Beta

The series
`β(x, y, w) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} (-1)^{n+1} x^{n²+n} y^{2n} wⁿ / ((1 - x)(xw; x)_{2n-1})` in
`ℚ[[x, y, z]]`, for a series `w` (the paper's third argument; `w = z` and `w = x` occur).
