# Colored Arndt Count four

The colored Arndt compositions of `4` are `4₁, 4₂, 4₃, 4₄`, `(3_a, 1₁)` for `a ≤ 3` and
`(2_a, 1₁, 1₁)` for `a ≤ 2`: `c(4, 1) = 4`, `c(4, 2) = 3`, `c(4, 3) = 2`.
