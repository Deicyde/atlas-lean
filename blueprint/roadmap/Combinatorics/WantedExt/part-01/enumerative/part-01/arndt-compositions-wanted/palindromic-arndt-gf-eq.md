# Palindromic Arndt GF eq

`PA(x, y, z) = xyz/(1 - xz) F(x², y², xz) + x³y³z/((1 - x²)(1 - xz)) F(x², y², x²)
- x³y³z/((1 - xz)(1 - x³z)) F(x², y², x³z)`.
