# Values of the map $U$

**Values of the map $U$.** The map $U$ sends $(2, 3)$ to $(1, 1, 1, 2)$, $(1, 4)$ to
$(1, 4)$, $(3, 2)$ to $(5)$, $(1, 1, 1, 2)$ to $(2, 1, 2)$ and $(1, 3, 1)$ to $(4, 1)$.
