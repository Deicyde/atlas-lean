# Sum four pow central Binom one sub odd Harmonic eq stirling

Proposition 5: for natural `n`, `r`,
`∑_{k=0}^{n-1} 2^{2k}/(2(n-k)-1) C(2(n-k),n-k) k^r (1 - O_{n-k-1})
= C(2n,n) ∑_{k=0}^r 2^{2k} C(2k,k)^{-1} k! {r \brace k} C(n,k) (O_n - O_k)`.
