# Quadruple VSet

`V_{n,j}`: the sequences `w ∈ W_n` whose last descent top is at position `n - 2`, with
`w_n ≤ n - 3`, `w_{n-1} ≠ 0` and `w_{n-2} - w_{n-1} = j` (one-indexed entries).
