# Is Weak Ascent Seq

Weak ascent sequences: `a₁ = 0` and `aᵢ ≤ 1 + wasc(a₁ ⋯ a_{i-1})` for `i ≥ 2`
(the analogue of `IsAscentSeq` with weak ascents).
