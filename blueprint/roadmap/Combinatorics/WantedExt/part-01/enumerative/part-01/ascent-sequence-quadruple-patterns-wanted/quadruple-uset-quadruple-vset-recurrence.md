# Quadruple USet quadruple VSet recurrence

Lemma 2: `u_n = |U_{n,1}|` and `v_n = |V_{n,1}|` both satisfy
`f_n = 5 f_{n-1} - 7 f_{n-2} + 2 f_{n-3}` (for `n ≥ 7`, stated in `ℤ`) with `f_6 = 1` and
`f_j = 0` for all `j ≤ 5`.
