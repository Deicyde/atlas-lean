# Weak Ascent wilf class206

Theorem 10 (Class 206): `{100, 102, 120, 210}`, `{101, 102, 120, 201}`,
`{101, 102, 120, 210}` and `{102, 110, 120, 210}` are WA-Wilf equivalent, stated as
equality of each count with the first.
