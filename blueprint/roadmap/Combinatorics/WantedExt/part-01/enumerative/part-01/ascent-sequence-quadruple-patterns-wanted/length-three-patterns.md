# The thirteen patterns of length three

**The thirteen patterns of length three.** The set of patterns of length three consists of
the thirteen words $000$, $001$, $010$, $011$, $012$, $021$, $100$, $101$, $102$, $110$, $120$,
$201$ and $210$.
