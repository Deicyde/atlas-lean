# Anti Recurrence Complement

The non-anti-recurrence number `B_m` (for `m ≥ 1`), the complement of `antiRecurrence a`;
for `k = 2` the subsequences `B¹_n = B_{2n-1}` and `B²_n = B_{2n}` are the paper's
`B¹`, `B²`.
