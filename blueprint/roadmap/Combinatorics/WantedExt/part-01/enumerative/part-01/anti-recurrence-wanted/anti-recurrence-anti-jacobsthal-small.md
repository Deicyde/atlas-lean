# First anti-Jacobsthal numbers

**First anti-Jacobsthal numbers.** The anti-recurrence numbers $A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4$ of the
linear form $(2, 1)$, the anti-Jacobsthal numbers (OEIS A304499), are $4, 11, 19, 25$.
