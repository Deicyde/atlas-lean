# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4.** If `a` is `A_1`-bounded (`A_1 ≤ (k - 1)τ + 2`), then `A_n - κn`
(`κ = kτ + 1`) is `τ`-automatic, stated with `IsKAutomatic` (finite `τ`-kernel, Eilenberg's
characterization of the paper's DFAO definition; `2 ≤ τ` follows from `ha` and `hk`); indexed
from `0` as `n ↦ A_{n+1} - κ(n + 1)`.
