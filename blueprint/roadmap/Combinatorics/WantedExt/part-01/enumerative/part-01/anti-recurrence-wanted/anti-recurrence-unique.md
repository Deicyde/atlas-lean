# Lemma 2

**Lemma 2.** A positive linear form `a` of dimension `k > 1` determines the anti-recurrence
sequence and its complement: a pair of sequences (indexed from `0`, so `A i = A_{i+1}`) is
strictly increasing, complementary in the positive integers, and satisfies
`A_{i} = ∑_j a_j B_{ik + j}`, if and only if it is the pair constructed by the mex rule.
