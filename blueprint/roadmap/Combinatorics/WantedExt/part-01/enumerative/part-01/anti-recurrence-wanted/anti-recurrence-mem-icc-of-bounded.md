# Lemma 5

**Lemma 5.** Let `a` be `A_1`-bounded, `A_1 ≤ (k - 1)τ + 2` (Definition 1), and
`κ = kτ + 1`. Then `A_n ∈ I_n = [κ(n - 1) + 1, κn]`, and the initial `B`-block of `I_n` is an
interval: the first `k` numbers of `I_n` are `B_{(n-1)kτ + 1}, …, B_{(n-1)kτ + k}`.
