# Theorem 2

**Theorem 2.** The anti-Jacobsthal numbers (`a = (2, 1)`) satisfy Kimberling's bounds (2):
for `n ≥ 1`, `0 ≤ A_n - 7n + 4 ≤ 3`, `0 ≤ 3B¹_n - 7n + 6 ≤ 4` and `0 ≤ 3B²_n - 7n + 2 ≤ 3`.
