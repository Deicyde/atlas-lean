# Anti Recurrence anti Pell small

The anti-Pell numbers (`a = (1, 2)`, OEIS A304502) start `5, 11, 20, 26`; their
complement starts `1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9` (so `B¹ = 1, 3, 6, 8, …`, `B² = 2, 4, 7, 9, …`).
