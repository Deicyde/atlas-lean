# Restricted Smooth Capacity Dist eval zero

Mansour and Shattuck's Theorem 10, formula (43): for `3 ≤ i ≤ n`, the number of
zero-capacity restricted smooth words of length `n` ending in `i` is
`∑_{j=i-3}^{n-3} C(n-3, j)`.
