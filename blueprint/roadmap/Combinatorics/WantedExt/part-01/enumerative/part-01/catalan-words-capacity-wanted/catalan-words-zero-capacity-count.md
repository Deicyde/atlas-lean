# Catalan Words zero capacity count

Mansour and Shattuck's Theorem 3, totals: for `n ≥ 1`, `a(n, 1; 0) = F_{2n-3}` (with
`F_{-1} = 1`) and `a(n; 0) = F_{2n-1}`.
