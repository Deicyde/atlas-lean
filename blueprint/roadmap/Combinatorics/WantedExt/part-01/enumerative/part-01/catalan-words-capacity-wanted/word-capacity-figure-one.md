# Capacity of the word $122341123212323$

**Capacity of the word $122341123212323$.** The word
$w = 1\,2\,2\,3\,4\,1\,1\,2\,3\,2\,1\,2\,3\,2\,3$ of length $15$ has capacity $10$ (Mansour and
Shattuck's Figure 1).
