# Catalan Words capacity leading Coeff

Mansour and Shattuck's Corollary 4, leading coefficient: for `n ≥ 4`, the number `cₙ` of
Catalan words of length `n` with maximum capacity is `2` if `3 ∣ n` and `1` otherwise.
