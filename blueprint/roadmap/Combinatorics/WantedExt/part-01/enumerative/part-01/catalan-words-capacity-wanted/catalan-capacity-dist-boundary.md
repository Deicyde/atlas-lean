# Catalan Capacity Dist boundary

Mansour and Shattuck's Lemma 2, boundary values: `a(n, 1) = a(n - 1)` for `n ≥ 2`, and
`a(n, n) = b(n, 1) = b(n, n) = 1` for `n ≥ 1`.
