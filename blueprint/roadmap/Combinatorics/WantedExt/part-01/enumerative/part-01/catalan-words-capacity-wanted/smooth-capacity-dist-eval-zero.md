# Smooth Capacity Dist eval zero

Mansour and Shattuck's Theorem 6, formula (34): for `2 ≤ i ≤ n`, the number of
zero-capacity smooth words of length `n` ending in `i` is `∑_{j=i-2}^{n-2} C(n-2, j)`.
