# Catalan Words

The set `𝒜ₙ` of Catalan words of length `n`: positive letters, first letter `1`, and
`w_{i+1} ≤ w_i + 1`. Letters of such words are at most `n`, so the ambient box `[1, n]ⁿ` loses
nothing (see `mem_catalanWords_iff`).
