# Catalan Words capacity subleading coeff

Mansour and Shattuck's Corollary 4, subleading coefficient: with
`α = deg a(n; q)`, the coefficient `dₙ` of `q^{α-1}` is `2m`, `m + 1`, `m + 2` for
`n = 3m, 3m + 1, 3m + 2 ≥ 7`, and `d₄ = 13`, `d₅ = 7`, `d₆ = 8`.
