# Catalan Words Ending In gen Fun

Mansour and Shattuck's Corollary 3: with `C(x) = ∑ Cₙ xⁿ` the Catalan generating function,
`A_i(x; 1) = ∑_{n ≥ i} |𝒜_{n,i}| xⁿ = xⁱ C(x)ⁱ` for `i ≥ 1`, using `a(n, i; 1) = |𝒜_{n,i}|`.
