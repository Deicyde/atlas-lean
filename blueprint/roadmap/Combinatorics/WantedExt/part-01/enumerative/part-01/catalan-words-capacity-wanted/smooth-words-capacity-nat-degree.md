# Smooth Words capacity nat Degree

Mansour and Shattuck's Theorem 5: the maximum capacity over smooth words of length
`4m + p` is `deg r(4m + p; q) = m(2m + p - 1)` for `m ≥ 1`, `0 ≤ p ≤ 3`.
