# Smooth Words

The set `ℛₙ` of smooth words of length `n`: positive letters, first letter `1`, and
`|w_{i+1} - w_i| ≤ 1`. Letters of such words are at most `n`, so the ambient box
`[1, n]ⁿ` loses nothing (see `mem_smoothWords_iff`).
