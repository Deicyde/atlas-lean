# Restricted smooth words

**Restricted smooth words.** For $n \ge 0$, the set $\mathcal{R}^{\ast}_n$ of restricted smooth
words of length $n$ consists of the smooth words of length $n$ in which no two adjacent letters
are both equal to $1$.
