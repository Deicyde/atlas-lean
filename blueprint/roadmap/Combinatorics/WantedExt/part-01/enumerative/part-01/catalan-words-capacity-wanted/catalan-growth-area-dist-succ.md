# Catalan Growth Area Dist succ

Mansour and Shattuck's Lemma 1: for `m ≥ 2`, `j ≥ 1` and `ℓ ∈ [j]`,
`c(m, j, ℓ) = q^ℓ ∑_{i=1}^{ℓ+1} c(m-1, j, i)`, with `c(1, j, ℓ) = q^ℓ δ_{j,ℓ}`.
