# Smooth Capacity Dist boundary

Mansour and Shattuck's Lemma 7, boundary values: `r(n, n) = s(n, n) = s(n, 1) = 1` for
`n ≥ 1`, and `r(n, 1) = r(n-1, 1) + r(n-1, 2)` for `n ≥ 2`.
