# Perm EGF q eq pow

Corollary 44 (Brenti): the `q`-analogue (`q` marking cycles) of the excedance
generating function over all permutations is `A(q,x,u) = (A(x,u))^q`, the power read as
`exp(q ln A(x,u))`: for every `L` with `L(0) = 0` and `exp L = A(x,u)`,
`A(q,x,u) = exp(q L)`.
