# R Associated Perm EGF q eq pow

Theorem 35: for every `r ≥ 0`, `A^r(q,x,u) = (A^r(x,u))^q` (`q` marking cycles),
with the power read as `exp(q ln A^r(x,u))`: for every `L` with `L(0) = 0` and
`exp L = A^r(x,u)`, `A^r(q,x,u) = exp(q L)`.
