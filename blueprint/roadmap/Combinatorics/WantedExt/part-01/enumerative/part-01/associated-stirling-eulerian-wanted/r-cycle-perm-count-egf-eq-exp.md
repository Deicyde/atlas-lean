# R Cycle Perm Count EGF eq exp

Corollary 26: for `r ≥ 2`, `∑_{n ≥ 0} |S_n^r| u^n/n! = exp(u^r/r)`, with `S_n^r`
the permutations of `n` objects with all cycle lengths equal to `r`.
