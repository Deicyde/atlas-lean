# Card r Cycle Perm

Corollary 25: for `r ≥ 2`, the number of permutations of `n` objects with all cycle
lengths equal to `r` is `n!/(r^{n/r} (n/r)!)` if `r ∣ n` and `0` otherwise (exact
natural-number division).
