# Fixed Points Perm excedance cycle sum neg

Corollary 60: for `n ≥ s`, summing over the `s`-fixed-points-permutations `𝓕_n^s` of
`n` objects, `∑ x^{e(π)} (-s)^{c(π)} = (-1)^s s^n x^{n-s}`. Restricted to `n ≥ s`, where
`x^{n-s}` is a monomial.
