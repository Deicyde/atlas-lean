# Circular RCycle Perm EGF eq

Theorem 8: for `r ≥ 2`, the excedance generating function over circular
permutations of exactly `r` objects (circular `r`-cycle permutations) is
`C^{(r)}(x,u) = x A_{r-1}(x) u^r/r!`.
