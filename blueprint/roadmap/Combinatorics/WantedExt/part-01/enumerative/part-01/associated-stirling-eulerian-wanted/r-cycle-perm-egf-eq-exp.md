# R Cycle Perm EGF eq exp

Theorem 21: for `r ≥ 2`, the exponential generating function of the numbers
`A^{(r)}_{n,m}` (all cycle lengths equal to `r`, by excedances) is
`A^{(r)}(x,u) = exp(x A_{r-1}(x) u^r/r!)`.
