# Circular RMinus Perm EGF eq

Theorem 7: for `r ≥ 1`, the excedance generating function over circular
permutations of at most `r` objects is
`C^{r-}(x,u) = ∑_{n=1}^{r} C_n(x) u^n/n! = u + x ∑_{n=1}^{r-1} A_n(x) u^{n+1}/(n+1)!`.
