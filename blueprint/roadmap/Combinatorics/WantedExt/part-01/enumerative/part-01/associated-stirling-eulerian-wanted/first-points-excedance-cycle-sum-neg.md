# First Points excedance cycle sum neg

Corollary 48: summing over permutations of `n + s` objects whose first `s` points lie
in distinct cycles, `∑ x^{e(σ)} (-s)^{c(σ)} = (-1)^s s^{n+s} (x-1)^n`.
