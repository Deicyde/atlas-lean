# Colored Polygon Dissection Count hexagon two

A hexagon cut by one diagonal into two quadrilaterals or into a triangle and a
pentagon: with `γ 1 = 2`, `γ 2 = 3`, `γ 3 = 5` and `γ j = 0` otherwise,
`p_{4,2}(γ) = 3 · 3² + 6 · 2 · 5 = 87`.
