# The zeroth Carlitz $q$-Bernoulli polynomial

**The zeroth Carlitz $q$-Bernoulli polynomial.** For real $q \ne 1$ and every real $x$, the
Carlitz $q$-Bernoulli polynomial $\beta_0(x)$ equals $1$.
