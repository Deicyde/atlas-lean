# The $q$-analogue of $0$ is $0$

**The $q$-analogue of $0$ is $0$.** For every real number $q$, $[0]_q = 0$, where
$[x]_q = (q^x - 1)/(q - 1)$ is the $q$-analogue of a real number $x$, taken to be $0$ when
$q = 1$.
