# Extended Carlitz Beta Poly

The extended Carlitz `q`-Bernoulli polynomials `β_n^{(r)}(x)`: `β_n^{(0)}(x) = η_n(x)` and
`β_n^{(r+1)}(x) = q^{-x} (β_n^{(r)}(x) + (q - 1) β_{n+1}^{(r)}(x))`; the numbers are
`β_n^{(r)} = β_n^{(r)}(0)`.
