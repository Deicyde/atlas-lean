# Sum qpow mul q Bracket pow

`q`-power sums: for `r ≥ 1`, `∑_{k<N} q^{rk} [k]^n = q^{rN} S_n(N) - S_n(0)`, where `S` is the
Carlitz-type sequence with associated sequence `1/((q - 1)[k + r])`.
