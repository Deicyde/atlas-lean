# Iterated Deriv inv one sub exp div self zero

With `f(x) = (1 - e^x)/x` for `x ≠ 0` and `f(0) = -1` (passed with its defining equations):
for `k ≥ 0`, `(1/f)^{(k)}(0) = -B_k`.
