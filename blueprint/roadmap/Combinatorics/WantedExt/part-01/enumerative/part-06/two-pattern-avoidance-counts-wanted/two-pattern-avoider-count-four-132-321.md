# Permutations of length four avoiding $132$ and $321$

**Permutations of length four avoiding $132$ and $321$.** Exactly $7$ permutations of
$\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ avoid both patterns $132$ and $321$, that is,
$A_4(132, 321) = 7 = 1 + \binom{4}{2}$.
