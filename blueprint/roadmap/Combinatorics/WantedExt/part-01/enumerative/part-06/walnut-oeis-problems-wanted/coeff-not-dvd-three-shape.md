# Coeff not dvd three shape

Hajdu, Tijdeman, and Varga, as recalled by the source: if no coefficient of
`(x-1)^c (x+1)^d` is divisible by `3`, then `c + d + 1` is `3^j`, `2·3^j`, `3^i + 3^j`,
or `2·3^i + 3^j` for some `i > j ≥ 0`.
