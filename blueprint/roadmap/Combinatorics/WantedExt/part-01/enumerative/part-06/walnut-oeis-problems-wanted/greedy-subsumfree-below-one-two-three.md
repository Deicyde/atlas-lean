# First members of $S_{1,2,3}$

**First members of $S_{1,2,3}$.** The members of the greedy $3$-subsumfree sequence
$S_{1,2,3}$ below $26$ are exactly $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 13, 14, 15, 25$.
