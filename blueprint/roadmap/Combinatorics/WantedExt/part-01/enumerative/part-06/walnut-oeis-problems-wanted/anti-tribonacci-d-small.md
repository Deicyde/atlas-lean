# First anti-Tribonacci numbers

**First anti-Tribonacci numbers.** Writing $D_n$ for the $n$-th anti-Tribonacci number,
the first four anti-Tribonacci numbers are $D_1 = 6$, $D_2 = 16$, $D_3 = 27$ and $D_4 = 36$.
