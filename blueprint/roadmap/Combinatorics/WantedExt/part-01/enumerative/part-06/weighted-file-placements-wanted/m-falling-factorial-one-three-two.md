# The value $1\downarrow_{3,2} = 3$

**The value $1\downarrow_{3,2} = 3$.** The $2$-falling factorial of $1$ with three factors is
$1\downarrow_{3,2} = 1 \cdot (1 - 2) \cdot (1 - 4) = 3$.
