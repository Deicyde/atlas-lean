# Iterated Partial Sum fib add two

For all `k, ℓ ≥ 0`,
`P^{(k)}((F_{n+2}))(ℓ) = P^{(k)}((F_n))(ℓ) + P^{(k)}((F_n))(ℓ+1)`.
