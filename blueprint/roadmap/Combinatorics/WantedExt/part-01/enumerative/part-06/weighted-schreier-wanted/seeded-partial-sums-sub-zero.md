# Seeded Partial Sums sub zero

Comparison of seeded and unseeded iterated partial sums: for a seed sequence
`s = (b_n)`, `t^s_{k,n} - t^0_{k,n} = ∑_{i=0}^{k-1} C(n,i) b_{k-1-i}` for all `n ≥ 0`.
