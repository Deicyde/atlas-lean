# Word Pairs same Length overlap sum

Corollary 3: for `n ≥ 1` and every `q`, the sum of `q^{overlap}` over pairs of words of equal
length `n` over `[k]` (the coefficient of `x^{2n}` in `P(x, q; k)`) is
`∑_{j=1}^{k} C(k, j) j! S_{n,j} (qj + k - j)^n`.
