# Non Intersecting Word Pairs same Length card

Corollary 2: for `n ≥ 1`, the number of non-intersecting pairs of words of length `n` over
`[k]` is `h_k(n) = ∑_{j=1}^{k-1} C(k, j) j! S_{n,j} (k-j)^n`.
