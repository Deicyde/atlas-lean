# Lemma 16

**Lemma 16**: every `w ∈ \mathcal{A}_{\{001,210\}}(n, k)`
has the form `0 1 ⋯ (k-1) k^{n-k}`, or
`0 1 ⋯ (k-1) k^y x^{n-k-y}` with `1 ≤ y < n - k` and `x < k`.
