# Finite removal preserves $r$-largeness

**Finite removal preserves $r$-largeness.** Let $r$ be a natural number. If a set
$D \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ is $r$-large and $F \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ is finite, then $D \setminus F$
is $r$-large.
