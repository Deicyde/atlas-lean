# Finite unions of lacunary sets are not 2-large

**Finite unions of lacunary sets are not 2-large.** For every $n \ge 1$ and all lacunary sets
$D_1, \dots, D_n$, the union $D_1 \cup \dots \cup D_n$ is not 2-large.
