# Is RLarge

`D` is `r`-large when it contains only positive gaps, `r ≥ 1`, and every
`r`-coloring of the positive integers has a monochromatic `D`-AP of every
requested positive length.  The universal length quantifier formalizes
"arbitrarily long."
