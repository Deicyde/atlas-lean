# Theorem 3, part 3

**Theorem 3, part 3.** For a nonempty closed `K ⊆ βS`, the family
`F_K = {E | Ē ∩ K ≠ ∅}` (sets belonging to some member of `K`) has the Ramsey
property and `β(F_K) = K`.

The paper omits `K ≠ ∅`; for `K = ∅` the collection `F_K` is empty, hence not a
family.
