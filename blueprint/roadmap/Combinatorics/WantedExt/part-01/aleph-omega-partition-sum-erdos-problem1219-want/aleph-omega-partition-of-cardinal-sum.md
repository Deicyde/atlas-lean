# Aleph Omega Partition Of Cardinal Sum

Erdős Problem 1219: if `n₀ < n₁ < ⋯`, the cardinals `2^{ℵ_{nₖ}}` are strictly increasing and
`2^{ℵ_{n₀}} > ℵ_ω`, then `∑ₖ 2^{ℵ_{nₖ}} → (ℵ_ω)²₂`.
