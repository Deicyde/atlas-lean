# Arrows Pairs

The partition relation `κ → (μ)²₂`: for every type `α` of cardinality `κ` and every symmetric
`2`-colouring `c` of pairs of elements of `α`, some `H ⊆ α` of cardinality `μ` has all pairs of
distinct elements of the same colour.
