# Complex binomial polynomial

**Complex binomial polynomial.** For an integer $n$ and a natural number $d$, the complex
binomial polynomial $\binom{X+n}{d} \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ is

$$\binom{X+n}{d} = \frac{1}{d!} \prod_{i=0}^{d-1} (X + n - i).$$
