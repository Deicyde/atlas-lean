# P Basis zero interlaces perturbation

For positive `d` and `w`, `pᵈ₀` CL-interlaces
`pᵈ⁺¹₀ + w(2X+1)pᵈ₀`.  This follows the numbered INTEGERS statement's requested direction,
not the differently worded arXiv excerpt.
