# Canonical line

**Canonical line.** The canonical line $\mathrm{CL}$ is the vertical line of complex numbers
with real part $-1/2$, that is, $\mathrm{CL} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Re} z = -1/2\}$.
