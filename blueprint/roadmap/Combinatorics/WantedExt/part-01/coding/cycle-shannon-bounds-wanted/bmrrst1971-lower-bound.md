# Baumert et al. lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle

**Baumert et al. lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle.** The Shannon capacity of the
$7$-cycle is at least $343^{1/5}$, as an inequality in $[0, \infty]$.
