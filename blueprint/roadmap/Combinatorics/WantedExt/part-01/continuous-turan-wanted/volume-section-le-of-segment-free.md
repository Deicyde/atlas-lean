# Volume section le of segment free

Lemma 2.8: if `P = [a, a + c] × {b}` is a closed horizontal segment of length `c ≥ 0` and
`S ⊆ [0, n]²` is open and `P`-free, then every horizontal section `S_y` has `μ₁(S_y) ≤ c`.
