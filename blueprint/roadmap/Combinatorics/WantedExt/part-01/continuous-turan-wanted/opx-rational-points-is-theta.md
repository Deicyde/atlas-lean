# Opx rational points is Theta

Theorem 4.6: for nonempty open `P ⊆ ℝ²`, the set `Q_P` of points of `P` with rational
coordinates has `opx(n, Q_P) = Θ(n²)` as `n → ∞`. Nonemptiness is added (`Q_∅ = ∅`).
