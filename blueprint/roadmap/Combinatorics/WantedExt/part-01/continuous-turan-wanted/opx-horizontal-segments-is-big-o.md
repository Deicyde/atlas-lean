# Opx horizontal segments is Big O

Theorem 4.10: if `P` is a finite union of horizontal segments `[a_i, b_i] × {y_i}` with
pairwise disjoint `x`-ranges and distinct heights `y_i`, then `opx(n, P) = O(n)`.
