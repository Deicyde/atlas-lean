# Opx D is Theta pattern Ex D

Theorem 5.11: for finite `P ⊆ ℝ^d`, `opx(n, P, d) = Θ(ex(n, M_P, d))`, the constants
depending on `P`. Stated along natural `n` (the domain of `ex`).
