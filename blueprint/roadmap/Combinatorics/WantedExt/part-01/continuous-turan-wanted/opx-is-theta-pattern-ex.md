# Opx is Theta pattern Ex

Theorem 3.1: for finite `P ⊆ ℝ²`, `opx(n, P) = Θ(ex(n, M_P))` as `n → ∞`, the constants
depending on `P`. Stated along natural `n` (the domain of `ex`).
