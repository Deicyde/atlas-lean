# Opx rotate reflect

Lemma 2.1: `opx(n, P)` is invariant under `90°` rotation `(x, y) ↦ (-y, x)` and the horizontal
and
vertical reflections `(x, y) ↦ (-x, y)`, `(x, y) ↦ (x, -y)`. Stated for all real `n`.
