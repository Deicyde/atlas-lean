# Closed Form U integral and near V

For `n ≥ 7`, `U(n) = n²/6 + 2n - 181/36 + (-1)ⁿ/4 - (2/9) cos(2nπ/3)` is an
integer and `0 ≤ U(n) - V(n) < 1`, where `V(n) = n²/6 + 2n - 11/2`.
