# Parity interpolation

A sequence given by `f` on even indices and `g` on odd indices satisfies
`aₙ = ((1 + (-1)ⁿ)/2) f(⌊n/2⌋) + ((1 - (-1)ⁿ)/2) g(⌊n/2⌋)`.
