# Van der Waerden ideal

**Van der Waerden ideal.** The van der Waerden ideal $W$ consists of the sets
$A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ for which there is an $n > 0$ such that $A$ contains no arithmetic
progression $a, a + r, \dots, a + (n - 1) r$ of $n$ terms with $a \in \mathbb{N}$ and $r > 0$.
