# Is Sidon of two mul nth le

Lemma 1: a set `A ⊆ [1, ∞)` with `2 A(n) ≤ A(n + 1)` for all `n` is a
Sidon set. The hypothesis forces `A` to be infinite or empty, so the paper's
standing assumption that `A` is infinite is not added.
