# Block sets

**Block sets.** For natural numbers $n$, $C$, $M$ and $K$, the block
$\mathrm{Block}(n, C, M, K)$ is the set of natural numbers $M l + C \cdot 2^k$ with
$0 \le k < n$ and $1 \le l \le K$.
