# Exists is Sidon card gt rpow sqrt two sub one

Theorem 3, item 3 (Ruzsa, as quoted): some Sidon set `A` satisfies
`|[1, n] ∩ A| > n^{√2 - 1 - o(1)}`: for every `ε > 0`, eventually
`|[1, n] ∩ A| > n^{√2 - 1 - ε}`.
