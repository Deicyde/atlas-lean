# Generated ideals are closed under unions

**Generated ideals are closed under unions.** Let $P$ be a property of subsets of
$\mathbb{N}$, and consider the ideal generated by $P$, the family of all subsets of finite unions
of sets with property $P$. If $A$ and $B$ lie in this ideal, then so does $A \cup B$.
