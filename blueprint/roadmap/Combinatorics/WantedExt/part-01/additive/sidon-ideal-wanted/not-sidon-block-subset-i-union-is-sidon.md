# Not sidon Block subset i Union is Sidon

Lemma 3: for `n ≥ 1` and `C ≥ 1`, `Block(n, C, C 2^n + 1, (n - 1) (n choose 2) + 1)`
is not covered by `n - 1` Sidon sets.
