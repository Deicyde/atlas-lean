# Color one dominant of rainbow Free pow Diff

In an exact rainbow-free coloring `c` of the positive integers with `r` colors for
`x - y = z ^ k`, the color `c 1` is dominant: every bichromatic string `i, i + 1, …, i + ℓ - 1`
(`i ≥ 1`) contains it.
