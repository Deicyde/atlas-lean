# Rainbow Free pow Diff iff

An exact 3-coloring of `ℤ/pℤ` is rainbow-free for `x - y = z ^ k` iff `0` is alone in its
color class, every component of `G^k_p` is monochromatic, and `c a = c (-a)` for all `a`.
