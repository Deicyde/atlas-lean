# Card pow Map components eq two iff of even

For even `k > 0`, the digraph `G^k_p` has exactly two components iff every prime factor of
`p - 1` divides `k` (the source's `p - 1 = 2^{β₀} q₁^{β₁} ⋯ q_ℓ^{β_ℓ}`, `β_i ≥ 0`, where
`k = 2^{α₀} q₁^{α₁} ⋯ q_ℓ^{α_ℓ}`).
