# Theorem 6 (sharp lower bound)

**Theorem 6 (sharp lower bound).** Under the hypotheses of Theorem 5,
`(2^{2r-1} + 2^{r+r₁-1} - 2^{r+1} + 2^{r₁}) 2^{n/4} + (2^r - 2^{r₁} - η) 2^{n/4 - 2^{r-2}}
  ≤ f*_max(G)`.
