# Hassler–Treglown

**Hassler–Treglown.** With `n = 2^k`, `f_max(ℤ₂^k) = (C(n-1, 2) + o(1)) 2^{n/4}`; with
`n = 3^k`, `f_max(ℤ₃^k) = ((n-3)(n-1)/3 + o(1)) 3^{n/9}`.
