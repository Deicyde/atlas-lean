# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4.** There is an absolute `C` such that for every `ε > 0` and all sufficiently
large even-order `G = ℤ_{2^{α₁}} ⊕ … ⊕ ℤ_{2^{α_r}} ⊕ K` (`r, αᵢ ≥ 1`, `|K|` odd) with
`n/2^r ≥ C`, `2^{(1/4-ε)n} ≤ f*_max(G) ≤ 2^{(1/4+ε)n}`, i.e. `f*_max(G) = 2^{n/4+o(n)}`.
