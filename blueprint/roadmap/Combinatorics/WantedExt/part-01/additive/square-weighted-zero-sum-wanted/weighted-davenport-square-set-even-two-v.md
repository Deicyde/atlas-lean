# Weighted Davenport square Set even two Valuation lower Bound

Theorem 20: for `n ≥ 2`, if the exponent of two in `n` is even and at
least two, then `D_{S(n)} ≥ 3` (in `ℕ∞`).
`n.factorization 2` formalizes `v₂(n)`.
