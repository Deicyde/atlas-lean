# Odd Combinations inter of is Perfect Cover

Let `S` be a perfect cover of an abelian group of order `2^n`. (a) For disjoint
`A, B ⊆ S`, `𝒯(A) ∩ 𝒯(B) = {0}`. (b) If `A ⊆ S` and `s ∈ S \ A`, then `s ∉ 𝒯(A)`.
