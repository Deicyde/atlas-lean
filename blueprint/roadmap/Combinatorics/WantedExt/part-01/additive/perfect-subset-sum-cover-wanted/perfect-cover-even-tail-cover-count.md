# Perfect Cover even tail cover Count

In the setting of Lemma 23, the elements `s_{k+2}, …, s_{m+n}` form a perfect cover of
the subgroup `V ⊕ 2ℤ_{2^n}`: every `(x, y)` with `x ∈ V` and `y` even is the sum of exactly
one subfamily.
