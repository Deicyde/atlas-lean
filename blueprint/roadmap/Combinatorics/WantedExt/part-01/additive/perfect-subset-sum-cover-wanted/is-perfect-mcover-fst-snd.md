# Is Perfect MCover fst snd

If `s` is a perfect `l`-cover of `H ⊕ K` with `|H| = 2^m` and `|K| = 2^n`, its first
coordinates form a perfect `2^n l`-cover of `H` and its second coordinates a perfect
`2^m l`-cover of `K`.
