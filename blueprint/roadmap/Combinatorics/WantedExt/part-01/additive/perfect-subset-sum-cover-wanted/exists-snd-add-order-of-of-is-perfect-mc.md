# Exists snd add Order Of of is Perfect MCover

If `s` is a perfect `m`-cover of `H ⊕ ℤ_{2^n}` with `|H|` a power of two, then the
second coordinates of `s` contain elements of each order `2^j`, `1 ≤ j ≤ n`.
