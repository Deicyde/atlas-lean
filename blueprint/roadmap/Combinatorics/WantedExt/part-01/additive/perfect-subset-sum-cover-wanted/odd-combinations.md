# Odd combinations

**Odd combinations.** For a finite subset $A$ of an abelian group $G$, $\mathcal{T}(A)$ is the
set of all sums $\sum_{x \in A} c_x x$ with integer coefficients $c_x$ such that every $c_x$ is
either $0$ or odd.
