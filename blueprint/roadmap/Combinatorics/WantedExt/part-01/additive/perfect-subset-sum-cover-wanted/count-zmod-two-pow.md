# Number of perfect covers of a cyclic 2-group

**Number of perfect covers of a cyclic 2-group.** For every $n \ge 0$, the cyclic group
$\mathbb{Z}_{2^n}$ has exactly $2^{n(n-1)/2}$ perfect covers.
