# Number of points in the max-norm ball

**Number of points in the max-norm ball.** For every natural number $h$, the max-norm ball
$B_h = \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : \max(|x|, |y|) \le h\}$ has exactly $(2h+1)^2$ points.
