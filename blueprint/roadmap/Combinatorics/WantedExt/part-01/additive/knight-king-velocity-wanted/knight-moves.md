# Knight moves

**Knight moves.** For integers $a$ and $b$, the $(a,b)$-knight's move set is

$$N_{a,b} = \{(\pm a, \pm b), (\pm b, \pm a)\} \subset \mathbb{Z}^2,$$

the finite set of the points $(a, b)$, $(-a, b)$, $(a, -b)$, $(-a, -b)$, $(b, a)$, $(-b, a)$,
$(b, -a)$ and $(-b, -a)$.
