# The usual knight reaches (1, 1) in two moves

**The usual knight reaches (1, 1) in two moves.** For the usual knight's move set
$N_{1,2} = \{(\pm 1, \pm 2), (\pm 2, \pm 1)\}$, the piece distance of the point $(1, 1)$ is $2$.
