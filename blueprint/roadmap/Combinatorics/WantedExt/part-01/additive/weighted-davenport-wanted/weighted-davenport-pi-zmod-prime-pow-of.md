# Weighted Davenport pi zmod prime pow of dvd

Same `G`, `c`, `β`; `A ⊆ {1, …, β}` with `p^{e_1 - c} ∈ A`. If `β ∣ p^{e_1}`, then
`D_A(G) = 1 + ∑ (⌈p^{e_i}/β⌉ - 1)`.
