# Weighted Davenport zmod prod self of dvd

For `n ≥ 2` and a divisor `m ≥ 2` of `n`, every `A ⊆ {1, …, ⌈n/(m-1)⌉ - 1}` with
`n/m ∈ A` has `D_A(Z_n ⊕ Z_n) = 2m - 1`.
