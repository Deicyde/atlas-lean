# Weighted Davenport one pi zmod prime pow

Olson's theorem: for a prime `p` and exponents `e_i ≥ 1`,
`D(Z_{p^{e_1}} ⊕ ⋯ ⊕ Z_{p^{e_r}}) = 1 + ∑ (p^{e_i} - 1)` (the rank `r = 0` case is the
trivial group and is included).
