# Weighted Davenport pi zmod prime pow const

For a prime `p`, `1 ≤ a ≤ e` and `r ≥ 1`, every `A ⊆ {1, …, ⌈p^e/(p^a - 1)⌉ - 1}` with
`p^{e-a} ∈ A` has `D_A((Z_{p^e})^r) = 1 + r(p^a - 1)`.
