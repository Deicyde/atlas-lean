# Modular Schur Number prime

For a prime `p` and `l ≥ p - 1` positive with `l ≢ 1 (mod p)`: `S_p(k, l) = k` for
`1 ≤ k ≤ p - 1` and `S_p(k, l) = p - 1` for `k ≥ p - 1`.
