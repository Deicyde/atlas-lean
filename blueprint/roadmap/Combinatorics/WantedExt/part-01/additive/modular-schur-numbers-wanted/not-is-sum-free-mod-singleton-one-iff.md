# When the singleton $\{1\}$ is sum-free

**When the singleton $\{1\}$ is sum-free.** Let $m \ge 1$ and $l$ be natural
numbers. The singleton $\{1\}$ is not $l$-sum-free modulo $m$ if and only if
$l \equiv 1 \pmod{m}$.
