# Mod Eq of is Sum Free Mod pair

Part 1: for a prime `p`, positive `i`, and `l ≥ p^i - 1` with `l ≢ 1 (mod p)`, if `a`
and `b` lie in a common `l`-sum-free set modulo `p^i` (equivalently `{a, b}` is
`l`-sum-free modulo `p^i`), then `a ≡ b (mod p)`.
