# The empty range has a sum-free partition

**The empty range has a sum-free partition.** For all natural numbers $m$, $l$ and
$k \ge 1$, the empty range $\{1, \dots, 0\}$ admits a partition into $k$ classes that are
$l$-sum-free modulo $m$.
