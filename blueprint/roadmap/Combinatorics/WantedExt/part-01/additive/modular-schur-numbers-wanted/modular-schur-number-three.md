# Modular Schur Number three

For positive `k, l`: `S_3(k, l) = 0` when `l ≡ 1 (mod 3)`; otherwise `S_3(1, l) = 1` and
`S_3(k, l) = 2` for `k ≥ 2` (quoted from [4]).
