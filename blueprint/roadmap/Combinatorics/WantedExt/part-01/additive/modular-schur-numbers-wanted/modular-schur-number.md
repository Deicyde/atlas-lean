# Modular Schur Number

The generalized Schur number modulo `m`, `S_m(k, l)`: the largest `n` for which
`{1, …, n}` admits a partition into `k` sets that are `l`-sum-free modulo `m`
(for `m ≥ 1` the admissible `n` are at most `m - 1`).
