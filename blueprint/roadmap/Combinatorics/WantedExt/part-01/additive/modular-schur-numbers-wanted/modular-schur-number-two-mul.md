# Modular Schur Number two mul

For `k, n ≥ 2` and positive `l` with `l ≢ 1 (mod 2n)`: `S_{2n}(k, l) = 1` when
`l ≡ n + 1 (mod 2n)`, and `S_{2n}(k, l) ≥ 2` otherwise.
