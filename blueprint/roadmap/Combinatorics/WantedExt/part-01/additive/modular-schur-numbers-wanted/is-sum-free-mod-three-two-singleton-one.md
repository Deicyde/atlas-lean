# $\{1\}$ is $2$-sum-free modulo $3$

**$\{1\}$ is $2$-sum-free modulo $3$.** The singleton $\{1\}$ is $2$-sum-free modulo $3$,
since $1 + 1 \not\equiv 1 \pmod{3}$.
