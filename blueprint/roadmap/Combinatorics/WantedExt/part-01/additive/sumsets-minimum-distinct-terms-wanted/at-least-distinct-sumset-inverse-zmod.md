# At Least Distinct Sumset inverse zmod

If `A ⊆ ℤ_p`, `|A| = k ≥ 2`, `1 ≤ r ≤ k`, `h ≥ r + 2` and
`|h^{(≥ r)}A| = h(k - 1) - r(r - 1) + 1 ≤ p - 2`, then `A` is an arithmetic progression.
