# Cauchy davenport nsmul

Cauchy–Davenport for `h`-fold sumsets: for a nonempty `A ⊆ ℤ_p` and `h ≥ 1`,
`|hA| ≥ min(p, hk - h + 1)` with `k = |A|`.
