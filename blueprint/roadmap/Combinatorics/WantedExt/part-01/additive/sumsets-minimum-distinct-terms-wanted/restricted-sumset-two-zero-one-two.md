# Restricted twofold sumset of {0, 1, 2}

**Restricted twofold sumset of {0, 1, 2}.** In the integers, the restricted sumset
$2^{\wedge}\{0, 1, 2\}$, the set of sums of two distinct elements of $\{0, 1, 2\}$, equals
$\{1, 2, 3\}$.
