# Restricted Sumset two zero one two

`2^{0, 1, 2} = {1, 2, 3}` in `ℤ`: the restricted sumset `h^A` of the paper (all sums of
`h` distinct elements of `A`) is `MetaMathlibExt.restrictedSumset`.
