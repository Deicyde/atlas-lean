# Plagne min nsmul card

Plagne: for `1 ≤ k ≤ m` and `h ≥ 1`,
`μ(ℤ_m, k, h) = min{|hA| : A ⊆ ℤ_m, |A| = k} = min{(h⌈k/d⌉ - h + 1) d : d ∣ m}`.
