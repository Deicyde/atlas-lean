# Lev nsmul card ge

Lev: let `A ⊆ ℤ` with `|A| = k ≥ 3`, `min A = 0` and `gcd(A) = 1`. Then for `h ≥ 2`,
`|hA| ≥ |(h - 1)A| + min{max A, h(k - 2) + 1}`.
