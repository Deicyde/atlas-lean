# At Least Distinct Sumset eq nsmul add restricted Sumset

For a nonempty finite `A` in an abelian group with `1 ≤ r ≤ |A|` and `h ≥ r`,
`h^{(≥ r)}A = (h - r)A + r^A` (with `0A = {0}`).
