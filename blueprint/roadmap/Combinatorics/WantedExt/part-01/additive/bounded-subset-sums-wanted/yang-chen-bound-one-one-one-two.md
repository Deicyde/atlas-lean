# Yang–Chen bound at $(1, 1, 1)$ and $h = 2$

**Yang–Chen bound at $(1, 1, 1)$ and $h = 2$.** The Yang–Chen bound $L(\bar r, h)$ for
$\bar r = (1, 1, 1)$ and $h = 2$ equals $3$.

Here $\mu = 2$, $\eta = 0$ and $\delta = \theta = 0$.
