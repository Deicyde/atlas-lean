# Card bounded Subsequence Sums ge of pos

For `k ≥ 2` positive integers `0 < a₁ < ⋯ < a_k` with positive multiplicities `t̄`,
`h = t₁ + ⋯ + t_k`, `1 ≤ α` and `α + β ≤ h - 1`: `|Σ^β_α(𝒜)| ≥ L(r̄, h - β)` with
`r̄ = (h - α - β, t₁, …, t_k)`, and the bound is attained.
