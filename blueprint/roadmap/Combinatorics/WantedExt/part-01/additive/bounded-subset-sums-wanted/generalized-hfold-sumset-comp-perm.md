# Generalized HFold Sumset comp perm

Reordering: `h^{(r̄)}A = h^{(r̄_π)}A_π` for every permutation `π` of the indices (stated
for every `h`, tuple `r̄` and indexing `a`; the paper assumes `h ≥ 2`, `r̄` positive and `A` a set).
