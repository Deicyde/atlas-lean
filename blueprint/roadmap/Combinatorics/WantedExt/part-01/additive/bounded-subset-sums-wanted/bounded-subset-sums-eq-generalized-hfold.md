# Bounded Subset Sums eq generalized HFold Sumset of zero not mem

For `A = {a₁, …, a_k} ⊆ G` with `0 ∉ A` and `α + β ≤ k - 1`,
`Σ^β_α(A) = (k - β)^{(r̄)}A₀` with `A₀ = {0, a₁, …, a_k}` and `r̄ = (k - α - β, 1, …, 1)`.
