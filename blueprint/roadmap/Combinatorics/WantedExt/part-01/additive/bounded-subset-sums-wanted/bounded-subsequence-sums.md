# Bounded Subsequence Sums

The set `Σ^β_α(𝒜) = {σ(ℬ) : ℬ ⊆ 𝒜, α ≤ |ℬ| ≤ |𝒜| - β}` of subsequence sums of the
sequence `𝒜` (a multiset) with at least `α` and at most `|𝒜| - β` terms.
