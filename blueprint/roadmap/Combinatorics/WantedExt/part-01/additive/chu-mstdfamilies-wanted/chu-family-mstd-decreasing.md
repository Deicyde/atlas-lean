# Chu family mstd decreasing

Theorem 4: for every positive integer `k` and
`M₁ ∈ {(1, 1), (1, 1, 2), (1, 1, 2, 1)}`, `A = (0 | 1, 1, 2, M^{k+1}, M^k, M₁)` is MSTD:
`|A - A| < |A + A|`.
