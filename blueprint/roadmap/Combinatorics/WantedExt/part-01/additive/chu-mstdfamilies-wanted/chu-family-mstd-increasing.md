# Chu family mstd increasing

Theorem 3: for every positive integer `k` and `M₁ ∈ {(1, 1, 2), (1, 1, 2, 1)}`,
`A = (0 | 1, 1, 2, M^k, M^{k+1}, M₁)` is MSTD: `|A - A| < |A + A|`.
