# Chu Family KLT mem sub

Lemma 1: with `T = t(kl + l + 1)`, the positive differences of `A` include `4i` and
`1 + 4i` for `1 ≤ i ≤ 1 + T`, and `3 + 4i` for `0 ≤ i ≤ T`.
