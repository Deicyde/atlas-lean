# Lunar prime count is Equivalent

Corollary 12 (conjecture of Applegate, LeBrun and Sloane): for fixed `b ≥ 2`, the number
`π_b(n)` of prime lunar numbers of `ℒ_b` with `n` digits (degree `n - 1`) satisfies
`π_b(n) ∼ (b - 1)² bⁿ⁻²`.
