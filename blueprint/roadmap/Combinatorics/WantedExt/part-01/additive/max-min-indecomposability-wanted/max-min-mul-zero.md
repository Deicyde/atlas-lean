# Constant coefficient of a max-min product

**Constant coefficient of a max-min product.** For all sequences
$g, h \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, the max-min product satisfies
$(g \otimes h)_0 = \min(g_0, h_0)$.
