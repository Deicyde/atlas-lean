# Is Lunar Prime

A prime lunar number of base `b` (Applegate, LeBrun and Sloane): every factorization
`f = g ⊗ h` with `g, h ∈ ℬ_b[x]` (coefficients below `b`) has `g` or `h` equal to the unit
`b - 1`.
