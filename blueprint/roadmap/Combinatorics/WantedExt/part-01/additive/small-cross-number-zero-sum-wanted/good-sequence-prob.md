# Good Sequence Prob

The probability that a uniformly random length-`t` sequence in `Γ` is good, namely
`#{good g : Fin t → Γ} / |Γ|^t` (the probability space `S(Γ, t)` of the source), for a finite
group `Γ` as in the source.
