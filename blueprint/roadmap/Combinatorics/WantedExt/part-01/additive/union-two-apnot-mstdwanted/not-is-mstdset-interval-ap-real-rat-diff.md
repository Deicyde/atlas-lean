# Lemma 10

**Lemma 10.** For `n ≥ 3`, real `x ∉ ℕ` with `x > n - 1`, `d = s + p/q` with `0 < p < q`
coprime, `x + id ∉ ℕ` for `i ∈ [0, m]`, and either `m ∈ [2, q - 1]` or (`m ≥ q` and `qd > n - 1`),
`I_n ∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
