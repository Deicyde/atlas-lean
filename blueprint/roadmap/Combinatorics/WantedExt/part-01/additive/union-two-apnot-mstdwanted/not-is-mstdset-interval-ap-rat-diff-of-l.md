# Lemma 6

**Lemma 6.** For `n ≥ 3`, `d = s + p/q` with `0 < p < q` coprime and `qd ≤ n - 1`, natural `x ≥
n + 1` and `m ≥ q`, `I_n ∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
