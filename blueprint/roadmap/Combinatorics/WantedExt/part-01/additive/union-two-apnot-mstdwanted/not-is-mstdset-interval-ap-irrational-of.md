# Lemma 7

**Lemma 7.** For `n ≥ 3`, real `x ∉ ℕ` with `x > n - 1`, a positive irrational `d`, and `x + id
∉ ℕ` for `i ∈ [0, m]`, `I_n ∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
