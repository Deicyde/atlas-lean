# Lemma 17

**Lemma 17.** For integers `n ≥ 3`, `m ≥ 2`, `d ≥ n` and real `x ∉ ℕ` with `0 < x < n - 1`, `I_n
∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
