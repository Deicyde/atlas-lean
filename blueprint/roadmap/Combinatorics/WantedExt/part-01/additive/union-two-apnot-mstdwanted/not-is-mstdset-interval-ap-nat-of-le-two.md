# Lemma 1

**Lemma 1.** For integers `n ≥ 3`, `m ≥ 2`, `d ≥ 2` and natural `x ∈ [n + 1, 2n - 2]`, `I_n ∪
J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
