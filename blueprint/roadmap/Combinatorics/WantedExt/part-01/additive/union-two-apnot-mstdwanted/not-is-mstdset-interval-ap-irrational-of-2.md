# Lemma 9

**Lemma 9.** For `n ≥ 3`, real `x ∉ ℕ` with `x > n - 1`, a positive irrational `d`, and `x + jd
∈ ℕ` for some `j ∈ [1, m]`, `I_n ∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
