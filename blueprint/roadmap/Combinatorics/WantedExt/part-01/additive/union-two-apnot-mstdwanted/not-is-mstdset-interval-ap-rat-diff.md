# Lemma 5

**Lemma 5.** For `n ≥ 3`, `d = s + p/q` with `0 < p < q` coprime, natural `x ≥ n + 1`, and
either `m ∈ [2, q - 1]` or (`m ≥ q` and `qd > n - 1`), `I_n ∪ J(x, m, d)` is not an MSTD set.
