# Lemma 20

**Lemma 20.** For reduced `x = s₁ + p₁/q₁` and `d = s₂ + p₂/q₂` with `gcd(q₁, q₂) > 1`, at most
one `i ∈ [1, q₂ - 1]` has `x + id ∈ ℕ`, at most one `j ∈ [1, q₂ - 1]` has `2x + jd ∈ ℕ`, and such
`i` and `j` differ.
