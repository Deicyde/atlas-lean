# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1.** The union of two finite arithmetic progressions `X₁ = J(a, m₁, d₁)` and `X₂ =
J(b, m₂, d₂)` (positive common differences) with `max X₁ < min X₂` is not an MSTD set.
