# Vanishing family

**Vanishing family.** Let $n$ be a natural number. A family $F$ of functions on blocks over
$\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ with values in a type with a zero is vanishing if it is $m$-vanishing for
every positive integer $m$.
