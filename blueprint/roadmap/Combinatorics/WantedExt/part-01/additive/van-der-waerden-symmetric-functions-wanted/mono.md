# Monotonicity of $m$-vanishing

**Monotonicity of $m$-vanishing.** Let $F$ be a family of functions on blocks over
$\mathbb{Z}/n$ with values in a type $M$ with a zero. If $F$ is $m$-vanishing and $m' \le m$,
then $F$ is $m'$-vanishing.
