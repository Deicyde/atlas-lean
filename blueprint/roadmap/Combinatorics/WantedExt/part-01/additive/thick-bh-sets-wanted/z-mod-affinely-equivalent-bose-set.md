# Theorem 4, Bose part

**Theorem 4, Bose part.** With `q = |F|` and `p` the characteristic of `F`, and `b, e`
both admissible for `Bose_h`, each of (i) `b ≡ e (mod (q^h - 1)/(q - 1))`,
(ii) `θ^b - θ^e ∈ F`, (iii) `b ≡ p^i e (mod q^h - 1)` for some `i`, implies
`Bose_h(q, b) ∼ Bose_h(q, e)`.
