# Theorem 5(a)

**Theorem 5(a).** For `h ≥ 2` and all `n, k ∈ ℕ`,
`R_h(n) ≥ n^(1/h) - 2^44 n^(154/(155h))` and `R_h⁻¹(k) ≤ k^h + 3^(155h) k^(h - 1/155)`.
