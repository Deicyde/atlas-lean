# Theorem 5(c)

**Theorem 5(c).** For `h ≥ 2`, for all sufficiently large `n`,
`R_h(n) ≥ n^(1/h) - n^(21/(40h))`, and for all sufficiently large `k`,
`R_h⁻¹(k) ≤ k^h + 2^h k^(h - 19/40)`.
