# Theorem 5(d)

**Theorem 5(d).** Assuming the Riemann Hypothesis, for `h ≥ 2`, all `k ≥ 1` and all `n`,
`R_h⁻¹(k) < k^h + log(20k) k^(h - 1/2) + 2k^(h-1) log^(2h)(20k)` and
`R_h(n) ≥ n^(1/h) - (7 + (log n)/h) n^(1/(2h))`.
