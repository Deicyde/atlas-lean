# Theorem 11

**Theorem 11.** For `h ≥ 2`, for all sufficiently large `k`,
`R_h⁻¹(k) < k^h + 2^h k^(h - 19/40)`, and for all sufficiently large `n`,
`R_h(n) ≥ n^(1/h) - n^(21/(40h))`.
