# Dudek–Grenié–Molteni

**Dudek–Grenié–Molteni** (as quoted in the paper): assuming the Riemann Hypothesis, for
every integer `n ≥ 2` there is a prime strictly between `n^2` and
`(n + (1 + 1/log n)^2 log n)^2`.
