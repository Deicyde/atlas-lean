# Stanchescu Difference Bound

Stanchescu's bound: for every `d ≥ 2`, every sufficiently large full-dimensional finite
`A ⊆ ℤ^d` has `|A - A| ≥ (2d - 2 + 1/(d - 1))|A| - (2d² - 4d + 3)`.
