# Sphere Triangulation Colour Groups Iso holds

Resolved true: Blackburn and McCourt (Theorem 1) prove that for every finite triangulation of the
sphere whose faces are properly coloured black and white, the abelian group generated by the
vertices with relations `r + c + s = 0` for the white triangles `{r, c, s}` is isomorphic to the one
defined by the black triangles; the Kourovka Archive records 17.35 as "Conjecture is proved".
