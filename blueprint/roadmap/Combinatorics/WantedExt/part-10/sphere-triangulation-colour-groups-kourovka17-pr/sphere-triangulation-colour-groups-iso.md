# Sphere Triangulation Colour Groups Iso

Kourovka 17.35 (I. M. Wanless, N. J. Cavenagh): for every finite triangulation of the sphere
whose triangles are properly coloured black and white, the groups `G_W` and `G_B` defined by the
white and by the black triangles are isomorphic.
