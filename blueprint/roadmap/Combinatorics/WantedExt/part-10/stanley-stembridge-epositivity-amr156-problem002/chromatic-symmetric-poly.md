# Chromatic Symmetric Poly

The chromatic symmetric function `X_G` of a graph on `n = |V|` vertices, in the variables
`x₀, …, x_{n-1}`: the sum of `∏_v x_{κ v}` over all proper colourings `κ : V → Fin n`.
