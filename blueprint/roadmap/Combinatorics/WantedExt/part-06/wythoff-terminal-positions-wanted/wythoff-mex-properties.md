# Wythoff Mex properties

Lemma 18: for `ℓ ≥ 1`, (1) `(a_n)` and `(b_n)` are strictly increasing; (2) they partition
`ℕ_{>ℓ}`; (3) every `j > ℓ` is `b_n - a_n` for a unique `n`; (4) `a_{n+1} - a_n ∈ {1, 2}`,
`b_{n+1} - b_n ∈ {2, 3}` and `b_{n+1} - b_n = a_{n+1} - a_n + 1`.
