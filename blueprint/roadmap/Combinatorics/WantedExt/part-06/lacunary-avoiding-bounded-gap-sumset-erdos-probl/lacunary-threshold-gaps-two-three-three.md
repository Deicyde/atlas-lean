# Lacunary Threshold Gaps Two Three Three Fold

The case `d₁ = 2`, `d₂ = 3`, `k = 3` of Erdős Problem 1112: the threshold `r₃(2, 3)` exists,
that is, for some `r` every lacunary `B` with ratio `r` is avoided by `A + A + A` for some `A`
with gaps in `[2, 3]`.
