# Consecutive sums

**Consecutive sums.** Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $a_0, \dots, a_{k-1}$ be natural
numbers. The consecutive sums of $a$ form the finite set of natural numbers
$a_u + a_{u+1} + \dots + a_v$ for indices $0 \le u \le v \le k - 1$.
