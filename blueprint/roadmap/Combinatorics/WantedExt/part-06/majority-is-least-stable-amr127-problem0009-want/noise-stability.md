# Noise Stability

The noise stability `Stab_ρ[f] = ∑_{S ⊆ [n]} ρ^{|S|} f̂(S)²`, the Fourier expansion of
`E[f(x) f(y)]` for uniform `x` and a `ρ`-correlated copy `y` of `x`.
