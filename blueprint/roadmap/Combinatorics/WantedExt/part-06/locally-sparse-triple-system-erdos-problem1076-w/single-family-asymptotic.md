# Single Family Asymptotic

Erdős Problem 1076 as worded: for every `k ≥ 5`, `ex₃(n, 𝓕_k) ∼ n² / 6`, with `𝓕_k` the
single family of `3`-graphs with `k` vertices and `k - 2` edges.
