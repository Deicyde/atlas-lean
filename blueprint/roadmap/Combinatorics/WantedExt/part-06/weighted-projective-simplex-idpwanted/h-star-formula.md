# H Star Formula

The explicit polynomial `∑_{b=0}^{∑qᵢ} z^{w(q,b)}` from Theorem 5.
It is named a formula, not defined to be `h*`; Theorem 5 relates it to the
independently defined Ehrhart-series condition.
