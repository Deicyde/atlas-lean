# Affine Free Sum

The affine free sum `P *_v Q`, embedded as
`conv((P×0) ∪ (0×(Q-v)))`.  Sum-indexed coordinates are canonically
`ℝ^(n+m)` and avoid choosing an explicit equivalence with `Fin (n+m) → ℝ`.
