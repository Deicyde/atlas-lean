# Idp reflexive implies necessary

Corollary 1, coordinate form: an IDP reflexive weighted simplex satisfies
`1+∑ᵢ(qᵢ mod qⱼ)=qⱼ` for every coordinate, exactly `SatisfiesIDPNecessary`.
