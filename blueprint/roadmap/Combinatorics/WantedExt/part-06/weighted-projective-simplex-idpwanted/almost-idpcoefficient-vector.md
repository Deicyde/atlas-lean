# Almost IDPCoefficient Vector

The exact coefficient vector represented by the paper's ellipsis:
`(1,7,15,14)`, followed by `n-2` copies of `(16,14)`, then `(15,7,1)`.
For `n=2` the repeated middle block is empty.
