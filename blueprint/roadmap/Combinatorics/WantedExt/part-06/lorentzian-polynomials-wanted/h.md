# Homogeneous polynomials of degree $d$

**Homogeneous polynomials of degree $d$.** For natural numbers $n$ and $d$, $H^d_n$ denotes
the space of homogeneous polynomials of degree $d$ in $\mathbb{R}[w_1, \dots, w_n]$, including the
zero polynomial.
