# Optimal Golomb rulers with 3 to 11 marks

**Optimal Golomb rulers with 3 to 11 marks.** The optimal Golomb ruler lengths with
$m = 3, 4, \dots, 11$ marks are, in order, $3, 6, 11, 17, 25, 34, 44, 55, 72$.
