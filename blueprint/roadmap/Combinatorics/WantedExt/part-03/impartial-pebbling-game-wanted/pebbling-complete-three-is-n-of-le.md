# Pebbling complete Three is N of le

Lemma 9: for `1 ≤ ℓ ≤ 2k - 1`, a `K_3` game with `6k - 2ℓ + 1` pebbles, largest pile at
most `4k - ℓ + 1` and smallest pile at most `2k - ℓ` is an N-game (`k` positive).
