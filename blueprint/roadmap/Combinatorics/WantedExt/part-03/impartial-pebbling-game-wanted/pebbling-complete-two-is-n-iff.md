# Pebbling complete Two is N iff

Theorem 1(a) (quoting Fiorini, Lind, Woldar, and Wong): on `K_2` with `(2:1)`-moves, a game
`Γ(a₁, a₂)` with an even total is an N-game iff `a₁ ≢ a₂ (mod 3)`, and with an odd total iff
`a₁ ≡ a₂ (mod 3)`.
