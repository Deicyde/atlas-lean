# Pebbling complete Three is P two KSub One

Lemma 5: for `0 ≤ ℓ ≤ k - 1` and `ℓ₁ ≤ ℓ₂` with `ℓ₁ + ℓ₂ = ℓ`, every game in
`Γ{2k-1-ℓ, k-ℓ₁, k-ℓ₂}` on `K_3` is a P-game (`k` positive).
