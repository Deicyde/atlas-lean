# C4 Pattern closed under moves

Lemma 15: `\hat X_{aabb} ∪ X_{abac}` and `X_{aaab} ∪ X_{abab} ∪ X_{aabc}` are each closed
under `(2:1)`-pebbling moves on `C_4`.
