# Pebbling complete Three is N of ge four KAdd One

Lemma 12: a `K_3` game with `6k - 1` pebbles and a pile of at least `4k + 1` pebbles that
is not in `Γ{4k+1, k-1, k-1}` is an N-game (`k` positive).
