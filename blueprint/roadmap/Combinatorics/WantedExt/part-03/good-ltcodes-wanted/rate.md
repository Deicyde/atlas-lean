# Rate of a binary code

**Rate of a binary code.** The rate of a binary code $C \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^n$ is its
dimension over $\mathbb{F}_2$ divided by its length $n$, as a real number; it is $0$ when
$n = 0$.
