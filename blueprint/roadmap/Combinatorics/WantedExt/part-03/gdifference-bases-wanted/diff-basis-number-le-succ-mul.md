# Diff Basis Number le succ mul

If `q` is prime and `√(n/v) ≤ q`, then `η_g(n) ≤ (q + 1) η_g(v)`. Corrected reading: the
paper prints the factor `q`, obtained from the misprinted Lemma 4; its proof gives `q + 1`.
