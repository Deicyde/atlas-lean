# Subdivision

The `(k - 1)`-subdivision `H^{k-1}` of a graph `H` on `Fin m`: every edge `ij` (`i < j`)
is replaced by a path `i — (ij, 0) — ⋯ — (ij, k - 2) — j` of length `k` through `k - 1` new
vertices.
