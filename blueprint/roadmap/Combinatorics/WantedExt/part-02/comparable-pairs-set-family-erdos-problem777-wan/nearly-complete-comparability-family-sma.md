# Nearly Complete Comparability Family Small

Erdős Problem 777, third question: for every `ε > 0` some `δ > 0` makes every family of `m`
subsets of `[n]` with more than `m^{2 - δ}` comparable pairs have `m < (2 + ε)^{n/2}`.
