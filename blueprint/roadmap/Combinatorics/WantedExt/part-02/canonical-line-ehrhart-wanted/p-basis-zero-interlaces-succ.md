# $p^d_0$ CL-interlaces $p^{d+1}_0$

**$p^d_0$ CL-interlaces $p^{d+1}_0$.** For every natural number $d \ge 1$, the symmetric
basis polynomial $p^d_0$ CL-interlaces $p^{d+1}_0$.
