# Maker Wins Within of hales Jewett

For `|S| = n`, if `N` is a Hales–Jewett dimension for two colors and
alphabet `S` (every 2-coloring of `S^N` has a monochromatic line), Maker wins
within `⌈n^N / 2⌉` moves.
