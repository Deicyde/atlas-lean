# Parallelogram spanned by two vectors

**Parallelogram spanned by two vectors.** For $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the parallelogram
spanned by $u$ and $v$ is the set $\{s u + t v : 0 \le s \le 1,\ 0 \le t \le 1\}$.
