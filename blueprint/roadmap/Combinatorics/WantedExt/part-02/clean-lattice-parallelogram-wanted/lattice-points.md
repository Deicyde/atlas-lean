# The integer lattice in the plane

**The integer lattice in the plane.** The lattice points of $\mathbb{R}^2$ are the points
$(a, b)$ with $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$; they form the image of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
