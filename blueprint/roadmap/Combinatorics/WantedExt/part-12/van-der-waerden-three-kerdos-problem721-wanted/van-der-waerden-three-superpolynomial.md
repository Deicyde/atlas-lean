# Van Der Waerden Three Superpolynomial

Erdős Problem 721, lower bound (Green's superpolynomial bound): there is `c > 0` with
`W(3, k) ≥ exp(c (log k)^{4/3} / (log log k)^{1/3})` for all large `k`, i.e. no `N` below this
value forces `W(3, k)`.
