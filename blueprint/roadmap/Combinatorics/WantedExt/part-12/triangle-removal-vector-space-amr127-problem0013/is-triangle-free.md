# Triangle-free subsets of $\mathbb{F}_2^n$

**Triangle-free subsets of $\mathbb{F}_2^n$.** A set $A \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^n$ is
triangle-free if there are no $x, y \in A$ with $x + y \in A$ (the case $x = y$ is allowed).
