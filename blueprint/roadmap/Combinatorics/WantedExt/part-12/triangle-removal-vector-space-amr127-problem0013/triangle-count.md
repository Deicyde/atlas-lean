# Triangle Count

The number of pairs `(x, y) ∈ 𝔽₂ⁿ × 𝔽₂ⁿ` with `x, y, x + y ∈ A`; divided by `4ⁿ` it is the
probability `Pr_{x,y}[x, y, x + y ∈ A]` for independent uniform `x`, `y`.
