# Contains Generalized Triangle

An `r`-graph `G` on `Fin n` contains a generalized triangle `𝒯_r` (the book `B_{r,2}`): edges
`D₁, D₂, D₃` with `|D₁ ∩ D₂| = r - 1`, `D₁ △ D₂ ⊆ D₃` and `D₃` disjoint from `D₁ ∩ D₂`.
