# Slow ANim one two five is P iff

Theorem 3 (3): for `A = {1, 2}` and `n = 5`, a position in canonical form is a
P-position iff its parity pattern is `(e,e,e,e,e)`, `(e,e,o,o,o)`, `(o,o,e,e,o)` or
`(o,o,o,o,e)`.
