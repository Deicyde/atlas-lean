# Sylver four pair bounds

4-pairs with `7 < q < x_q` satisfy Conway's bounds `2q < x_q < 3q`. The 4-pair is given
by the characterization of Proposition 7: `x_q` odd, `{4, x_q}` an N-position and
`{4, x_q, q}` a P-position.
