# Hibi Symmetric Unimodal refuted

Resolved false: Mustaţă and Payne (Example 1.1) give a reflexive `6`-dimensional lattice polytope
with `h*`-vector `(1, 6, 8, 6, 8, 6, 1)`, which satisfies `h*_i = h*_{6-i}` and is not unimodal.
