# Hamidoune Some RAt Least Three

Hamidoune's conjecture holds for some `r ≥ 3`: every finite nonempty loopless digraph with
minimum out-degree and minimum in-degree at least `r` has an arc `(x, y)` with `κ(y, x) ≥ r`.
