# $(0,2)$-graphs

**$(0,2)$-graphs.** For a natural number $n$, a simple graph $G$ on the vertex set
$\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is a $(0,2)$-graph when every two distinct vertices of $G$ have either
exactly zero or exactly two common neighbors.
