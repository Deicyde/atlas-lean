# Vertices of the Grassmann graph

**Vertices of the Grassmann graph.** For natural numbers $k$ and $\ell$, the vertices of the
Grassmann graph $\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)$ are the $\ell$-dimensional linear subspaces of
$\mathbb{F}_2^k$.
