# Maximum Nim Grundy div two five

For the Section 3 rule `f(x) = ⌊x/2⌋`, a pile of five has Grundy number one.
This is the paper's `k = 2`, `G(5) = 1` check, with natural division representing
the floor; there is no deviation.
