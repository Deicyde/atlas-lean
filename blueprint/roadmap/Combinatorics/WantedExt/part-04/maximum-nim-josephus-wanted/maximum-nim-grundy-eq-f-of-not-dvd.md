# Maximum Nim Grundy eq F of not dvd

Lemma 3(ii): for `k ≥ 2`, every positive nonmultiple `x` has the same Grundy
number as `F(x)=⌊(k-1)x/k⌋`. The expression `(k-1)*x/k` is `F`; there is no deviation.
