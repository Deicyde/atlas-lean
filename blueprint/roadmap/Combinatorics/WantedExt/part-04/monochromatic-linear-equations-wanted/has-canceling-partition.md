# Has Canceling Partition

The equation `a₁x₁ + ⋯ + aₖxₖ = 0` has a canceling partition: its coefficients split
into pairs `{aᵢ, aⱼ}` with `aᵢ + aⱼ = 0`, encoded as a fixed-point-free involution `σ` of the
indices with `aᵢ + a_{σ i} = 0`.
