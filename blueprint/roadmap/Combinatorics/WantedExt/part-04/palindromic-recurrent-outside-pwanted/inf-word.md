# Infinite words

**Infinite words.** An infinite word over the alphabet $\{0, 1, 2\}$ is a sequence
$u = u_0 u_1 u_2 \cdots$ of letters indexed by the natural numbers, that is, a map
$\mathbb{N} \to \{0, 1, 2\}$.
