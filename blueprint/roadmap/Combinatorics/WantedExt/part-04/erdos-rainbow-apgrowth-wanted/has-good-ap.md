# Has Good AP

A colouring `f` of `[0, N)` has a good `k`-term arithmetic progression:
there are `a`, `d` with `0 < d` and every term `a + d * i` (for `i < k`)
in `[0, N)`, such that the progression is monochromatic or rainbow.
