# Density

The density `d(H, W)` of a graph `H` on `Fin k` in the graphon `W`: the probability that the
`W`-random graph on `Fin k` is isomorphic to `H`, i.e. the sum of `τ(G, W)` over the graphs `G` on
`Fin k` isomorphic to `H`.
