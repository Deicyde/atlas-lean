# Proposition 52

**Proposition 52.** For `n ≥ 1`: `ab ϱ_F^{2n+1}(b) = (ϱ_F')^{2n+1}(b) ba` and
`ba ϱ_F^{2n}(b) = (ϱ_F')^{2n}(b) ab`. Here `ϱ_F^{k+1}(b) = ϱ_F^k(a)` is written
`(fibWord k).map (!·)`.
