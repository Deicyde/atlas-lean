# Proposition 11

**Proposition 11.** (a) The reflection `w ↦ w̄` maps `a𝒲` onto `b𝒲` and `b𝒲` onto `a𝒲`.
(b) For `w, w' ∈ 𝒲`, `w̄ = w'` iff `r_w = -r_{w'}`.
