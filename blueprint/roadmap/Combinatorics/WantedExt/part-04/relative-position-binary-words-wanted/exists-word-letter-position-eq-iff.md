# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4.** A function `p : ℕ → ℤ_{≥0}` is `p_a` of some `w ∈ a𝒲` iff it is strictly
increasing, `p(1) = 0` and `Δp > 1` infinitely often; it is `p_b` of some `w ∈ a𝒲` iff it is
strictly increasing, `p(1) > 0` and `Δp > 1` infinitely often.
