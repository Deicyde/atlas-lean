# Corollary 48

**Corollary 48.** For `w ∈ b𝒲`, both `Δp_a` and `Δp_b` are codings of `w` iff `w = f̄` or
`w = (ba)^ω`; they are both isomorphic to `w` iff `w = f̄` ("aperiodic" dropped as in
Theorem 46).
