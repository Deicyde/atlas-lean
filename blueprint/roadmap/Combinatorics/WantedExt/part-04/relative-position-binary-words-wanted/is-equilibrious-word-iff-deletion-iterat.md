# Lemma 21

**Lemma 21.** A finite word `u` of length `2k` is equilibrious (as many `a`'s as `b`'s) iff
`D^k ∘ Pre_u` is the identity on `𝒲`. (`IsEquilibriousWord` is stated on `List (Fin 2)`; the
letter `a = true` is sent to `0` and `b = false` to `1`.)
