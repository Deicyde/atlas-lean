# Proposition 51

**Proposition 51.** For the iccanobiF substitution `ϱ_F'` (`a ↦ ba`, `b ↦ a`) and `n ≥ 1`:
`ab ϱ_F^{2n}(a) = (ϱ_F')^{2n}(a) ba` and `ba ϱ_F^{2n-1}(a) = (ϱ_F')^{2n-1}(a) ab`.
