# Theorem 30

**Theorem 30.** For `w ∈ 𝒲` and `d ∈ (0, 1)` the following are equivalent:
`Freq(b) = d`; `Freq(a) = 1 - d`; `p_b(n)/n → 1/d`; `p_a(n)/n → 1/(1-d)`.
