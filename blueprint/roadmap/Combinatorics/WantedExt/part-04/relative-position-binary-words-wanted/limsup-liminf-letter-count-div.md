# Corollary 29

**Corollary 29.** For `w ∈ 𝒲` and a letter `α`,
`limsup #_α(m)/m = limsup n/p_α(n)` and `liminf #_α(m)/m = liminf n/p_α(n)` (both sequences are
bounded, so the real `limsup`/`liminf` are the classical ones).
