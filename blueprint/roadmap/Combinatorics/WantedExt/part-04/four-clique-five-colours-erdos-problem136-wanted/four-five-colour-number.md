# The $(4, 5)$-colouring number $f(n)$

**The $(4, 5)$-colouring number $f(n)$.** For a natural number $n$, $f(n)$ is the least
natural number $k$ such that $K_n$ has a $(4, 5)$-colouring with $k$ colours; such a $k$
always exists.
