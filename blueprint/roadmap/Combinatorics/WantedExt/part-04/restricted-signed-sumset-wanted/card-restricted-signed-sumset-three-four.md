# The restricted signed 3-fold sumset of $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$

**The restricted signed 3-fold sumset of $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.** The restricted signed 3-fold
sumset $3^{\wedge}_{\pm} \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ has exactly $19$ elements.
