# Card restricted Signed Sumset four ge of equal outer gaps eq

All successive gaps differ from `2*a₁`; additionally `a₄-a₃=a₂-a₁` and
`a₅-a₃=2*a₁`, using zero-based indices in `Fin 5`. The conclusion is the stated
`|4^∧_±A| ≥ 26`.
