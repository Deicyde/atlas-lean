# Tomaszewski Conjecture holds

Resolved true: Keller and Klein proved Tomaszewski's conjecture: for every `a ∈ ℝⁿ` with `∑ aᵢ² =
1`, a uniformly random sign vector `x` satisfies `|∑ aᵢ xᵢ| ≤ 1` with probability at least `1/2`.
