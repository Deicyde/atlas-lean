# Tournaments

**Tournaments.** Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. A relation $T$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is a
tournament when $T(x, x)$ holds for no $x$ and, for all distinct $x$ and $y$, exactly one of
$T(x, y)$ and $T(y, x)$ holds.
