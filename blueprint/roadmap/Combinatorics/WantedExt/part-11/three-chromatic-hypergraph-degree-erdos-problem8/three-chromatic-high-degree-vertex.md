# Three Chromatic High Degree Vertex

Erdős Problem 833: there is an absolute `c > 0` such that for every `r ≥ 2`, every finite
`r`-uniform hypergraph with chromatic number `3` has a vertex in at least `(1 + c)^r` edges.
