# Sumset Ratio Limsup At Least Three

Erdős Problem 245: for every infinite `A ⊆ ℕ` with `|A ∩ [1, N]| = o(N)`,
`limsup_N |(A + A) ∩ [1, N]| / |A ∩ [1, N]| ≥ 3`, stated as: for every `ε > 0`, infinitely many `N`
have `|(A + A) ∩ [1, N]| > (3 - ε) |A ∩ [1, N]|`.
