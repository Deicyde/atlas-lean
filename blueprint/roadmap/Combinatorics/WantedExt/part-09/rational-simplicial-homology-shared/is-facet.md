# Facet

**Facet.** For a finite family $K$ of finite subsets of $\mathbb{N}$, a finite set
$F \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ is a facet of $K$ when $F \in K$ and $F$ is maximal under inclusion in
$K$: every $G \in K$ with $F \subseteq G$ equals $F$.
