# Two Colouring Short Progressions

Erdős Problem 984: the positive integers can be coloured with two colours so that, for every
`ε > 0`, every monochromatic arithmetic progression `a, a + d, …, a + (k - 1) d` (`a, d ≥ 1`) has
`k ≤ C_ε a^ε`.
