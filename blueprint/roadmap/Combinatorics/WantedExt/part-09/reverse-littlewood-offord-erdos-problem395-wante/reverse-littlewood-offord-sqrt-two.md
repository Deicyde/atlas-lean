# Reverse Littlewood Offord Sqrt Two

Erdős Problem 395: for unit complex numbers `z₁, …, zₙ`, uniformly random signs give
`|ε₁ z₁ + ⋯ + εₙ zₙ| ≤ √2` with probability `≫ 1 / n`.
