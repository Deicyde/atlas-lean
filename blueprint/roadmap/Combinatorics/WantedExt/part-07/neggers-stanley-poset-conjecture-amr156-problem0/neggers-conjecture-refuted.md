# Neggers Conjecture refuted

Resolved false: Stembridge found naturally labeled posets on `17` elements whose `W`-polynomials
have non-real zeros, disproving Neggers' conjecture and hence the Neggers–Stanley conjecture
(Brändén had earlier given counterexamples with non-natural labelings). Source: R. P. Stanley,
Positivity Problems and Conjectures in Algebraic Combinatorics (1999), Problem 20 (Neggers–Stanley
conjecture); resolution: J. R. Stembridge, Counterexamples to the poset conjectures of Neggers,
Stanley, and Stembridge, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 359 (2007), 1115-1128; P. Brändén, Counterexamples
to the Neggers-Stanley conjecture, Electron. Res. Announc. Amer. Math. Soc. 10 (2004), 155-158
(non-natural labelings), Stembridge, Introduction: there exist naturally labeled narrow posets on 17
vertices whose W-polynomials have non-real zeros (and none smaller), disproving Neggers' original
conjecture; Figure 1 gives one, with W-polynomial t + 32t² + 336t³ + 1420t⁴ + 2534t⁵ + 1946t⁶ +
658t⁷ + 86t⁸ + 3t⁹, https://websites.umich.edu/~jrs/papers/pocon.pdf.
