# W Polynomial

The `W`-polynomial `W(P, x) = ∑_{w ∈ 𝓛(P)} x^{d(w)}` of the poset `P` on the labels `Fin n`
generated by the relation `R` (`R i j` meaning `i <_P j`). A linear extension is a word `w` listing
every label once with `w a` before `w b` whenever `R (w a) (w b)`; linear extensions of `R` and of
its transitive closure coincide, so `R` need not be transitive.
