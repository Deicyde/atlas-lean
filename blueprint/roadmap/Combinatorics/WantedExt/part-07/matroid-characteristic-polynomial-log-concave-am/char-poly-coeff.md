# Char Poly Coeff

The coefficient of `λ ^ j` in Rota's characteristic polynomial
`χ_M(λ) = ∑_{I ⊆ E} (-1)^{|I|} λ^{r(E) - r(I)}` of a finite matroid `M` on the ground set `E`.
