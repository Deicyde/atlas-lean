# Pairwise sum theorem

**Pairwise sum theorem.** For every finite coloring of $\mathbb{N} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$ there
is an infinite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ such that $A \cup \{a + a' : a, a' \in A, a \ne a'\}$
is monochromatic.
