# Uncommonness of $x + 2y - z - 2w = 0$ over the integers

**Uncommonness of $x + 2y - z - 2w = 0$ over the integers.** For every $r \ge 2$,
the equation $x + 2y - z - 2w = 0$ is $r$-uncommon over the integers.
