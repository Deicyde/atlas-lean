# $D$-diffsequences

**$D$-diffsequences.** Let $D$ be a set of natural numbers and $k$ a natural number. A
finite sequence $x_0, \dots, x_{k-1}$ of integers is a $D$-diffsequence when for every $i$ with
$i + 1 < k$ there is $d \in D$ with $x_{i+1} - x_i = d$.
