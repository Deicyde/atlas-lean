# Stirling numbers of the first kind and the rising factorial

**Stirling numbers of the first kind and the rising factorial**:
`∑_{k=0}^{n} stirlingFirst n k * x ^ k = x (x + 1) ⋯ (x + n - 1)`
(Graham–Knuth–Patashnik, §6.1).
