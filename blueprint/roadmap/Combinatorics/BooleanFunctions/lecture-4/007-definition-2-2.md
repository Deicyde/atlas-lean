# Definition 2.2 (Lecture 4, Section 2)

Given a function $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and $i \in [n]$, the $L^2$ influence of $i$ on $f$ is defined as $I_i[f] = \|\partial_i f\|_2^2$. The total $L^2$ influence of $f$ is $I[f] = \sum_{i=1}^n I_i[f]$.
