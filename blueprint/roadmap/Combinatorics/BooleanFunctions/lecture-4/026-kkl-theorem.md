# Theorem 5.5 (KKL theorem)

There is an absolute constant $c > 0$, such that for any $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$, there is $i \in [n]$ such that $I_i[f] \geqslant c\frac{\log n}{n} \operatorname{var}(f)$.
