# Claim 5.1

For a function $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and $i \in [n]$, we have that $\partial_i f(y) = \sum_{S \ni i} \widehat{f}(S) \chi_{S \setminus \{i\}}(y)$.
