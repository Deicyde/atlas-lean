# Claim 1.2

For all $\varepsilon, \delta > 0$, there is $q = O\left(\frac{\log(1/\delta)}{\varepsilon^2}\right)$ and an algorithm performing the following task. Given a sequence of random input-output pairs of $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to [-1,1]$ and a character $S \subseteq [n]$, the algorithm produces an estimate $a_S$ of $\widehat{f}(S)$ such that $\Pr\left[|\widehat{f}(S) - a_S| \geqslant \varepsilon\right] \leqslant \delta$.
