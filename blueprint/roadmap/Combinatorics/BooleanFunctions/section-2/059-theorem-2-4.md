# Theorem 2.4

Suppose $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ is a voting rule such that the probability of reaching a Condorcet paradox when sampling the votes $x, y, z$ as $(x_i, y_i, z_i) \sim \operatorname{NAE}_3^{-1}(1)$ for each $i$ independently is at most $\varepsilon$. Then, $f$ is $\varepsilon$-close to a dictatorship or an anti-dictatorship.
