# Theorem 4.1

There exists an absolute constant $C > 0$, such that for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $W^{=k}[f] \leqslant \left(\frac{C}{k}\right)^k M(f) \log\left(\frac{k}{M(f)}\right)^{k-1}$.
