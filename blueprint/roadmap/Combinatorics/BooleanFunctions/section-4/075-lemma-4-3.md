# Lemma 4.3

For all $\alpha, \zeta > 0$ there exists $r \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varepsilon > 0$ such that the following holds. Suppose $\zeta < p < 1/2$ and $\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H} \subseteq \mathcal{P}([n])$ are monotone families such that $\mu_p(\mathcal{G}), \mu_p(\mathcal{H}) \geqslant \alpha$, and each one of them is $(r, \varepsilon)$-quasi-random. Then there are disjoint $G \in \mathcal{G}$, $H \in \mathcal{H}$.
