# Theorem 3.5

There exists $0 < \alpha < 1/2$ such that for all $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$, $\mathbb{E}_{x}\left[\sqrt{s_f(x)}\right] \geqslant \Omega\left(\operatorname{var}(f)\log^{1/2-\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{\operatorname{var}(f)}\right)\log^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{M(f)}\right)\right)$.
