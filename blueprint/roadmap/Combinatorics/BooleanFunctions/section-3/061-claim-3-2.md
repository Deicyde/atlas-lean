# Claim 3.2 (Section 3, Noise sensitivity)

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$. Then $\operatorname{NS}_{\varepsilon}(f) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{S} (1 - (1-2\varepsilon)^{|S|}) \widehat{f}(S)^2$.
