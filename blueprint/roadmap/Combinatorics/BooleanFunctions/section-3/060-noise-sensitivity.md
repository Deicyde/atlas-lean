# Definition 3.1 (Noise Sensitivity)

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, and $\varepsilon > 0$. The noise sensitivity of $f$ with parameter $\rho$ is defined as $\operatorname{NS}_{\varepsilon}(f) = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Stab}_{1-2\varepsilon}(f)$.
