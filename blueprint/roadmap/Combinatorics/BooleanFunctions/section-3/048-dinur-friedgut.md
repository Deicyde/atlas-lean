# Theorem 3.1 (Dinur-Friedgut)

For all $\zeta > 0$, $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists $J \in \mathbb{N}$ such that the following holds. If $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \{0,1\}^n$ is an intersecting family, and $\zeta < p < 1 - \zeta$, then there exists an intersecting $J$-junta $\mathcal{J} \subseteq \{0,1\}^n$ such that $\mu_p(\mathcal{F} \setminus \mathcal{J}) \leqslant \varepsilon$.
