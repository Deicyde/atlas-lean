# Corollary 2.8

Suppose that $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ satisfies $W^{\geqslant d}[f] \leqslant \varepsilon$, and let $(J,z)$ be a $p$-random restriction. Then $\mathbb{E}_{J,z}\left[W^{\geqslant 2pd}[f_{\bar{J}\to z}]\right] \leqslant \varepsilon + \exp(-\Theta(pd))$.
