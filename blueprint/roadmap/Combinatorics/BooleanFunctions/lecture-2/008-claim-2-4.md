# Claim 2.4 [1]

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $J \subseteq [n]$ and $S \subseteq J$. We have $\mathbb{E}_{z}\left[\widehat{f_{\bar{J}\to z}}(S)^{2}\right] = \sum_{T \subseteq \bar{J}} \widehat{f}(S \cup T)^{2}$.
