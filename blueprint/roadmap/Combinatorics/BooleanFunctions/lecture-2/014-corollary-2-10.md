# Corollary 2.10

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0,1]$ be such that $pd \geqslant 10$. Suppose that $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ satisfies $W^{\geqslant d}[f] \leqslant \varepsilon$, and let $(J,z)$ be a $p$-random restriction. Then $\mathbb{E}_{J,z}\left[W^{\approx pd}[f_{\bar{J}\to z}]\right] \geqslant \Omega(W^{\approx d}[f])$.
