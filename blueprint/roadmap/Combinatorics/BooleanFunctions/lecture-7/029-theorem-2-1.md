# Theorem 2.1 (Lecture 7, Section 2)

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists $J \subseteq [n]$ of size at most $2^{O\left(\frac{I[f]}{\varepsilon \operatorname{var}(f)}\right)}$ and a $J$-junta $g \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ such that $\|f - g\|_2 \leqslant \varepsilon$.
