# The type of noncrossing perfect matchings

`NCMatching n` is the subtype of functions $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ that are
[noncrossing perfect matchings](is-ncmatching.md) of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$. It is finite, since a
matching is determined by its restriction to $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, and its cardinality is counted
by [Counting noncrossing perfect matchings](card-nc-matching.md).
