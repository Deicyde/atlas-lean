# Coppersmith's 1997 bound on the dual exponent

**Coppersmith's 1997 bound on the dual exponent.** For every field $F$, the dual matrix
multiplication exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\alpha(F) > 0.29462$.
