# Williams–Xu–Xu–Zhou bound on the dual exponent

**Williams–Xu–Xu–Zhou bound on the dual exponent.** For every field $F$, the dual matrix
multiplication exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\alpha(F) > 0.321334$.
