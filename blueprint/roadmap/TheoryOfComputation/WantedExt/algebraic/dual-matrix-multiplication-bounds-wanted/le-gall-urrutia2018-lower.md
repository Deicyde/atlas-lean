# Le Gall–Urrutia bound on the dual exponent

**Le Gall–Urrutia bound on the dual exponent.** For every field $F$, the dual matrix
multiplication exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\alpha(F) \ge 0.31389$.
