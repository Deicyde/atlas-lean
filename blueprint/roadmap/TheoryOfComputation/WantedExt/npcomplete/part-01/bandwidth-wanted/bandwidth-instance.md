# BANDWIDTH instances

**BANDWIDTH instances.** An instance of BANDWIDTH is a triple $(n, E, K)$ of a natural number
$n$ (the number of vertices), a list $E$ of ordered pairs of natural numbers (the edges) and a
natural number $K$ (the bound).
