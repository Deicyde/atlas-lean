# Well-formed BETWEENNESS instances

**Well-formed BETWEENNESS instances.** A BETWEENNESS instance $(n, T)$ is well formed when
every triple $(a, b, c)$ in $T$ satisfies $a < n$, $b < n$ and $c < n$, and $a$, $b$, $c$ are
pairwise distinct.
