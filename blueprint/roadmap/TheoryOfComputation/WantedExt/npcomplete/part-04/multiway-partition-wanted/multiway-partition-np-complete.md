# MULTIWAY NUMBER PARTITION is NP-complete

**MULTIWAY NUMBER PARTITION is NP-complete.** Source: M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson,
*Computers and Intractability* (1979), MULTIWAY NUMBER PARTITION (the `K = 2` slice is
PARTITION).
