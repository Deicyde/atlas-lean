# MONOTONE 3-SAT is NP-complete

**MONOTONE 3-SAT is NP-complete.** The decision problem MONOTONE 3-SAT belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to MONOTONE 3-SAT.
