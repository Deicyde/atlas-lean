# Optimum of a MAX-3SAT instance

**Optimum of a MAX-3SAT instance.** The MAX-3SAT optimum of a bitstring $x$ is the maximum,
over all bitstrings $a$ of length $|x|$, of the MAX-3SAT value of $a$ on $x$.
