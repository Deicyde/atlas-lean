# Degree of a variable in a 2CSP instance

**Degree of a variable in a 2CSP instance.** For a 2CSP instance $(n, \Sigma, C)$ and a
natural number $v$, the degree of $v$ is the number of entries $(u, w, P)$ of the list $C$ with
$u = v$ or $w = v$.
