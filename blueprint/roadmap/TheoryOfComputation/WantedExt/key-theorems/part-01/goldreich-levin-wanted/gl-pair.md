# Concatenation of two bitstrings

**Concatenation of two bitstrings.** For $n$-bit strings $y$ and $r$, their concatenation is the
$2n$-bit string whose bit $i$ is $y_i$ for $i < n$ and $r_{i - n}$ for $n \le i < 2n$.
