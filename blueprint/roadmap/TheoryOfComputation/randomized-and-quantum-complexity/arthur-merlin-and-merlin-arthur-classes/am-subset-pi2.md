# AM is contained in $\Pi_2^p$ (Babai)

**AM is contained in $\Pi_2^p$ (Babai).** Every decision problem in the Arthur–Merlin class
$\mathsf{AM}$ lies in $\Pi_2^p$, the second universal level of the polynomial hierarchy.
