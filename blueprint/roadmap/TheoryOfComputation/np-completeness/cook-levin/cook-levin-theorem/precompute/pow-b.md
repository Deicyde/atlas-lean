# Step budget of the power loop

**Step budget of the power loop.** For natural numbers $j$ (the number of rounds), $p$ (the
start value) and $b$ (the base), the step budget of the power loop is
$j \, (p \, (b + 1)^j \, (3 b + 5) + 5) + 1$.
