# Uniqueness of quotient and remainder

**Uniqueness of quotient and remainder.** Let $W$, $t$, $t'$, $r$ and $r'$ be natural
numbers with $r < W$ and $r' < W$. If $tW + r = t'W + r'$, then $t = t'$ and $r = r'$.
