# Boundary before a literal

**Boundary before a literal.** For $V, n, l \in \mathbb{N}$ and literal positions
$pos : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, the boundary before literal $l$ is $pos(l)$ if $l < n$, and $V$
otherwise.
