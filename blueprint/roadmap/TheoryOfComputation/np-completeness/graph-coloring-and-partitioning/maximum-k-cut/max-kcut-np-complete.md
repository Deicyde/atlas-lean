# MAXIMUM k-CUT is NP-complete

**MAXIMUM k-CUT is NP-complete.** The decision problem MAXIMUM k-CUT lies in NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to MAXIMUM k-CUT.
