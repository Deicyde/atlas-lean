# Monotonicity of trilinear rank bounds

**Monotonicity of trilinear rank bounds.** Let $F$ be a semiring and $T : A \to B \to C \to F$
an array. If $T$ has trilinear rank at most $r$ and $r \le s$, then $T$ has trilinear rank at
most $s$.
