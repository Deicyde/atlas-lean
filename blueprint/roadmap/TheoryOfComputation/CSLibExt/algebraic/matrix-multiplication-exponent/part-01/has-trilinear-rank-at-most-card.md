# Trivial upper bound on trilinear rank

**Trivial upper bound on trilinear rank.** Let $F$ be a semiring and $A$, $B$, $C$ finite
types. Every array $T : A \to B \to C \to F$ has trilinear rank at most
$|A| \cdot |B| \cdot |C|$.
