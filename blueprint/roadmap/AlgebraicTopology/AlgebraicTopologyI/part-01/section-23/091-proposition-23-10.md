# Proposition 23.10

Let $\mathcal{I}$ be a direct set, and let $M \colon \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathbf{Mod}_{R}$ be a $\mathcal{I}$-directed system of $R$-modules. There is a natural isomorphism $(\varinjlim_{I} M_{i}) \otimes_{R} N \cong \varinjlim_{I} (M_{i} \otimes_{R} N)$.
