# Definition 1.7

The $n$th singular homology group of $X$ is: $$H_{n}(X) = \frac{Z_{n}(X)}{B_{n}(X)} = \frac{\operatorname{ker}\left(d \colon S_{n}(X) \rightarrow S_{n-1}(X)\right)}{\operatorname{im}\left(d \colon S_{n+1}(X) \rightarrow S_{n}(X)\right)}$$
