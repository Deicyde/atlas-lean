# Definition 5.4

A map $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$ is a homotopy equivalence if $[f] \in [X, Y]$ is an isomorphism in $\operatorname{Ho}(\mathbf{Top})$. In other words, there is a map $g \colon Y \rightarrow X$ such that $fg \simeq 1_{Y}$ and $gf \simeq 1_{X}$.
