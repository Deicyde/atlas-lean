# Theorem 6.1

A homotopy $h \colon f_{0} \simeq f_{1} \colon X \rightarrow Y$ determines a natural chain homotopy $f_{0*} \simeq f_{1*} \colon S_{*}(X) \rightarrow S_{*}(Y)$.
