# Theorem 9.1 (The homology long exact sequence)

Let $0 \rightarrow A_{*} \rightarrow B_{*} \rightarrow C_{*} \rightarrow 0$ be a short exact sequence of chain complexes. Then there is a natural homomorphism $\partial \colon H_{n}(C) \rightarrow H_{n-1}(A)$ such that the sequence $\cdots \rightarrow H_{n}(A) \rightarrow H_{n}(B) \rightarrow H_{n}(C) \xrightarrow{\partial} H_{n-1}(A) \rightarrow \cdots$ is exact.
