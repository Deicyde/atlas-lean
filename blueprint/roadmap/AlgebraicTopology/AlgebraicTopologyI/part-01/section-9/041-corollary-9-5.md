# Corollary 9.5

Let $0 \rightarrow A_{*} \xrightarrow{f} A'_{*} \rightarrow 0$, $0 \rightarrow B_{*} \xrightarrow{g} B'_{*} \rightarrow 0$, $0 \rightarrow C_{*} \xrightarrow{h} C'_{*} \rightarrow 0$ be a map of short exact sequences of chain complexes. If two of the three maps induced in homology by $f, g$, and $h$ are isomorphisms, then so is the third.
