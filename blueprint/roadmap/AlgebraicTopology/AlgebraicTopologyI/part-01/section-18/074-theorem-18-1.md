# Theorem 18.1

Let $X$ be a finite CW-complex with $a_{n}$ $n$-cells. Then $\chi(X) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^{k} a_{k}$ depends only on the homotopy type of $X$; it is independent of the choice of CW structure.
