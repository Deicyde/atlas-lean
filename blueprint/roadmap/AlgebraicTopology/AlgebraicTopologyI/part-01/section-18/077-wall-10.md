# Theorem 18.4 (Wall, 10)

Let $X$ be a simply connected CW-complex of finite type. Then there exists a CW complex $Y$ with $r(k) + t(k) + t(k-1)$ $k$-cells, for all $k$, and a homotopy equivalence $Y \rightarrow X$.
