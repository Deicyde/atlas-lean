# Corollary 10.3

Assume that there is a subspace $B$ of $X$ such that (1) $\bar{A} \subseteq \operatorname{Int} B$ and (2) $A \rightarrow B$ is a deformation retract. Then $H_{*}(X, A) \rightarrow H_{*}(X/A, *)$ is an isomorphism.
