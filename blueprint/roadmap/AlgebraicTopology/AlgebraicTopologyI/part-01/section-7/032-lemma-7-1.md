# Lemma 7.1

Given three chain complexes $A_{*}, B_{*}$, and $C_{*}$, and maps $\times \colon A_{p} \times B_{q} \rightarrow C_{p+q}$ that satisfy bilinearity and the Leibniz formula, these data determine a bilinear map $\times \colon H_{p}(A) \times H_{q}(B) \rightarrow H_{p+q}(C)$.
