# Theorem 13.5 (The locality principle)

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a cover of a space $X$. The inclusion $S_{*}^{\mathcal{A}}(X) \subseteq S_{*}(X)$ is a quasi-isomorphism; that is, $H_{*}^{\mathcal{A}}(X) \rightarrow H_{*}(X)$ is an isomorphism.
