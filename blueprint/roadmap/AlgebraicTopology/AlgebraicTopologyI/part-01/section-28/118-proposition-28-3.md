# Proposition 28.3

Let $f \in S^{p}(X)$, $g \in S^{q}(Y)$, and $h \in S^{r}(Z)$, and let $\sigma \colon \Delta^{p+q+r} \rightarrow X \times Y \times Z$ be any simplex. Then $((f \times g) \times h)(\sigma) = (f \times (g \times h))(\sigma)$.
