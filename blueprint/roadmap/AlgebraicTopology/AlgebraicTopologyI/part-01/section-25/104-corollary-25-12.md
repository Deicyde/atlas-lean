# Corollary 25.12

Suppose furthermore that $\theta$ is a natural isomorphism. If each $G_{n}$ is $\mathcal{M}$-free and $F_{*} \rightarrow F \rightarrow 0$ is an $\mathcal{M}$-exact sequence, then any natural chain map $F_{*} \rightarrow G_{*}$ covering $\theta$ is a natural chain homotopy equivalence.
