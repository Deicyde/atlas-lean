# Lemma 25.10

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with a set of models $\mathcal{M}$ and let $F, G, G' \colon \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathbf{Ab}$ be functors. Suppose that $F$ is $\mathcal{M}$-free, let $G' \rightarrow G$ be a $\mathcal{M}$-epimorphism, and let $f \colon F \rightarrow G$ be any natural transformation. Then there is a lifting of $f$ through $G' \rightarrow G$.
