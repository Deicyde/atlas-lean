# Theorem 25.13 (Eilenberg-Zilber theorem)

There are unique chain homotopy classes of natural chain maps: $S_{*}(X) \otimes S_{*}(Y) \leftrightarrows S_{*}(X \times Y)$ covering the usual isomorphism $H_{0}(X) \otimes H_{0}(Y) \cong H_{0}(X \times Y)$ and they are natural chain homotopy inverses.
