# Definition 25.4

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, and fix a set $\mathcal{M}$ of objects in $\mathcal{C}$, to be called the "models." A functor $F \colon \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathbf{Ab}$ is $\mathcal{M}$-free if it is the free abelian group generated by a coproduct of corepresentable functors. That is, $F$ is a direct sum of functors of the form $\mathbb{Z} \mathcal{C}(M,-)$ where $M \in \mathcal{M}$.
