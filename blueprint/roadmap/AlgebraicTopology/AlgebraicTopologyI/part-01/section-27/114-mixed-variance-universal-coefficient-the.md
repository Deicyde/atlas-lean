# Theorem 27.1 (Mixed variance Universal Coefficient Theorem)

Let $R$ be a PID and $N$ an $R$-module, and let $C_{*}$ be a chain-complex of free $R$-modules. Then there is a short exact sequence of $R$-modules, $0 \rightarrow \operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{1}(H_{n-1}(C_{*}), N) \rightarrow H^{n}(\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(C_{*}, N)) \rightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_{R}(H_{n}(C_{*}), N) \rightarrow 0$, natural in $C_{*}$ and $N$, that splits (but not naturally). Taking $C_{*} = S_{*}(X; R)$, we have the short exact sequence $0 \rightarrow \operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{1}(H_{n-1}(X; R), N) \rightarrow H^{n}(X; N) \xrightarrow{\beta} \operatorname{Hom}_{R}(H_{n}(X; R), N) \rightarrow 0$ that splits, but not naturally. This also holds for relative cohomology.
