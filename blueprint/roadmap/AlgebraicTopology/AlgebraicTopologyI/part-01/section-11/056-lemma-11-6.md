# Lemma 11.6

The sequence $\cdots \longrightarrow C_{n+1}^{\prime} \xrightarrow{\left[\begin{array}{c} h \\ -k \end{array}\right]} C_{n+1} \oplus A_{n}^{\prime} \xrightarrow{\left[\begin{array}{ll} k & f \end{array}\right]} A_{n} \xrightarrow{\partial} C_{n}^{\prime} \longrightarrow \cdots$ is exact.
