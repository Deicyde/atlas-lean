# Theorem 11.4 (The locality principle)

The inclusion $S_{*}^{\mathcal{A}}(X) \subseteq S_{*}(X)$ induces an isomorphism in homology, $H_{*}^{\mathcal{A}}(X) \xrightarrow{\cong} H_{*}(X)$.
