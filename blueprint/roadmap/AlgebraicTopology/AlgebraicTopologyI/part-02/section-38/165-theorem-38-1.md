# Theorem 38.1

Let $M$ be an $n$-manifold and $K$ a compact subset. An $R$-orientation along $K$ determines a fundamental class $[M]_{K} \in H_{n}(M, M - K)$, and capping gives an isomorphism: $\cap[M]_{K} \colon \check{H}^{n-q}(K; R) \xrightarrow{\cong} H_{q}(M, M - K; R)$.
