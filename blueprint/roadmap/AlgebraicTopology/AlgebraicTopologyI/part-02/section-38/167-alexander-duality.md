# Theorem 38.4 (Alexander duality)

For any compact subset $K$ of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, the composite $\check{H}^{n-q}(K; R) \xrightarrow{\cap[\mathbb{R}^{n}]_{K}} H_{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n}, \mathbb{R}^{n} - K; R) \xrightarrow{\partial} \widetilde{H}_{q-1}(\mathbb{R}^{n} - K; R)$ is an isomorphism.
