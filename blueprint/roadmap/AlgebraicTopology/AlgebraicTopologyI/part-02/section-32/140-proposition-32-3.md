# Proposition 32.3

Let $A_{1} \supseteq A_{2} \supseteq \cdots$ be a decreasing sequence of compact subsets of $M$, and assume that the theorem holds for each $A_{n}$. Then it holds for the intersection $A = \bigcap A_{i}$.
