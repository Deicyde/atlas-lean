# Theorem 32.1

Let $M$ be an $n$-manifold and let $A$ be a compact subset of $M$. Then $H_{q}(M \mid A; R) = 0$ for $q > n$, and the map $j_{A} \colon H_{n}(M \mid A; R) \rightarrow \Gamma(A; o_{M} \otimes R)$ is an isomorphism.
