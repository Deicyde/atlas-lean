# Theorem 35.2 (Long exact sequence)

Let $(K, L)$ be a closed pair in $X$. There is a long exact sequence $\cdots \rightarrow \check{H}^{p}(K, L) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p}(K) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p}(L) \xrightarrow{\delta} \check{H}^{p+1}(K, L) \rightarrow \cdots$ that is natural in the pair.
