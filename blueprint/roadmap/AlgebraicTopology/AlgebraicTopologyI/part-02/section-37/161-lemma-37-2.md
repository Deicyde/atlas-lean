# Lemma 37.2

Let $A_{1} \supseteq A_{2} \supseteq \cdots$ be a decreasing sequence of compact subspaces of $M$, and let $A = \bigcap A_k$. Then $\check{H}^{p}(A_{k}) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p}(A)$ is an isomorphism.
