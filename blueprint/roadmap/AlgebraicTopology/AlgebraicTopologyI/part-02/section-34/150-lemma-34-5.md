# Lemma 34.5

Under the regular neighborhood condition (for every open $U \supseteq K$, there exists an open $V$ with $U \supseteq V \supseteq K$ such that $K \hookrightarrow V$ is a homotopy equivalence), $\check{H}^{*}(K) \rightarrow H^{*}(K)$ is an isomorphism.
