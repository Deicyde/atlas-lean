# Lemma 34.3

Let $K \subseteq V \subseteq U \subseteq X$, with $K$ closed and $U, V$ open. Then the diagram involving the cap product actions by $H^{*}(U)$ and $H^{*}(V)$ on $H_{*}(X, X - K)$ commutes.
