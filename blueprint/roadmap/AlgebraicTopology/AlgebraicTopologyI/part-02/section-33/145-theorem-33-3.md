# Theorem 33.3

Let $R$ be a PID. Assume that $H_{p}(X)$ is a finitely generated free $R$-module for all $p$. Then $\times \colon H^{*}(X; R) \otimes_{R} H^{*}(Y; R) \rightarrow H^{*}(X \times Y; R)$ is an isomorphism.
