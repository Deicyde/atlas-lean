# Points of the complex affine plane

**Points of the complex affine plane.** A point of the complex affine plane is a pair
$a = (a_0, a_1)$ of complex numbers, that is, an element of $\mathbb{C}^2$, with coordinates
indexed by $0$ and $1$.
