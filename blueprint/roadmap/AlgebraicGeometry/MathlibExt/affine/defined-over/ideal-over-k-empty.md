# The ideal over $k$ of the empty set

**The ideal over $k$ of the empty set.** Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure $\bar k$.
For every $n$, the ideal $I_k(\emptyset)$ of the empty subset of $\bar k^n$ is all of
$k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.
