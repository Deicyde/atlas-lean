# Submaximal upper bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

**Submaximal upper bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant.**
The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at most
$1/\sqrt{10}$.
