# Eckl's 2008 lower bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

**Eckl's 2008 lower bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant.**
The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at least
$4/13$.
