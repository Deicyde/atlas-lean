# Cremona group

**Cremona group.** For a field $k$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Cremona group
$\mathrm{Cr}_n(k)$ is the group of birational automorphisms of affine $n$-space
$\mathbb{A}^n_k$ over $\operatorname{Spec} k$.
