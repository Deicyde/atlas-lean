# Theorem 11.1

If $X = \operatorname{Spec}(A)$, then $\mathbf{QCoh}(X) \cong \mathbf{Mod}(A)$, given by $\mathcal{F} \mapsto \Gamma(\mathcal{F}) = \mathcal{F}(X)$.
