# Proposition 15

Some properties: 1.\ $\mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{F}^\#$ is exact; in particular it does not change the stalks. 2.\ $\mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{F}^\#$ is left adjoint to the embedding $\mathbf{Presh} \to \mathbf{Sh}$, and is an isomorphism if $\mathcal{F}$ itself is a sheaf. 3.\ $\mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}_x$ is an exact functor; in other words, a sequence of sheaves $0 \to \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{F}' \to \mathcal{F}'' \to 0$ is exact iff $0 \to \mathcal{F}_x \to \mathcal{F}'_x \to \mathcal{F}''_x \to 0$ is exact for all $x$.
