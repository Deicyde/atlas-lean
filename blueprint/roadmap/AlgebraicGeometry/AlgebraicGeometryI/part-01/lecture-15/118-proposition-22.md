# Proposition 22

$\operatorname{Pic}(X) = \operatorname{Div}_C(X)/\operatorname{Im}(\mathcal{K}^*) = \Gamma(\mathcal{K}^*/\mathcal{O}^*)/\operatorname{im}\Gamma(\mathcal{K}^*)$.
