# Corollary 26

If $X$, $Z$ smooth, $Z$ closed in $X$, then we get a short exact sequence of locally free sheaves $0 \to \mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2 = T_Z^*X \to \Omega_X|_Z \to \Omega_Z \to 0$, and thus $K|_Z = K_Z \omega(\mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2)$. If $Z$ is a divisor, then $\omega(\mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2) = \mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2 = \mathscr{O}(-D)|_Z$, thus $K_X(D)|_D = K_D$, which is the adjunction formula.
