# Proposition 36

We have a short exact sequence $0 \to \Omega_{\mathbb{P}^n} \to \mathcal{O}(-1)^{\oplus (n+1)} = \mathcal{O}(-1) \otimes V^* \to \mathcal{O} \to 0$ where $\mathbb{P}V = \mathbb{P}^n$. As a corollary, $K_{\mathbb{P}^n} = \mathcal{O}(-(n+1))$.
