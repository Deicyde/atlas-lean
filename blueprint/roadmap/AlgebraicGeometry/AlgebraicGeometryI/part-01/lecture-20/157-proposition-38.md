# Proposition 38

Let $E$ be the exceptional locus over $x$ when blowing up $X \ni x$. Then the cone of $E$ is $\operatorname{Spec}((\bigoplus_{n\geq 0} \mathfrak{m}_x^n/\mathfrak{m}_x^{n+1})_{\mathrm{red}})$, which we call the tangent cone. If $x$ is a smooth point, then $\bigoplus_{n\geq 0} \mathfrak{m}_x^n/\mathfrak{m}_x^{n+1}$ is given by $\operatorname{Sym}(T_x^*X)$.
