# Proposition 35

The following hold: 1.\ If $Z \subseteq X$ is a closed subvariety, then we have $\mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2 \to \Omega_X|_Z \to \Omega_Z \to 0$. 2.\ If $\mathscr{I}_Z$ is locally generated by functions with linearly independent differential (that is, for all $x$ in $Z$, there exists $U \ni x$, $f_1, \ldots, f_m$ on $U$ such that $\mathscr{I}_{Z \cap U} = (f_1, \ldots, f_m)$, $df_i|_y$ is linearly independent for any $y \in U$), then the sequence is exact at left. 3.\ If $X$ is smooth, the last condition can be checked at $x$.
