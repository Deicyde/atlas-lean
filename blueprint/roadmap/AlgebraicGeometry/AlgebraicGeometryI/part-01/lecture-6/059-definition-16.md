# Definition 16

Let $X$, $Y$ be irreducible varieties, and let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a dominant map of degree $n$. $f$ is unramified over $y \in Y$ if $\#f^{-1}(y) = n$. Otherwise, we say that $f$ is ramified at $y$ or that $y$ is a ramification point of $f$.
