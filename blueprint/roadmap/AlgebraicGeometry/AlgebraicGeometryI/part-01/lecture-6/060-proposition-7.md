# Proposition 7

Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a finite dominant map of irreducible varieties and let $R \subseteq Y$ be the set of ramification points. $R$ is a closed subset of $Y$ and if the field extension $k(X)/f^*k(Y)$ is separable, then $R \neq Y$.
