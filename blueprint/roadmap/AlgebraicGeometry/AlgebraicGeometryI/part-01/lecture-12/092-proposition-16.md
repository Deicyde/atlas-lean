# Proposition 16

$j_* j^* \mathcal{F} = \varinjlim(f^{-n}\mathcal{F})$, where $j \colon U \hookrightarrow X$, $j_*\mathcal{F}$ means the sheaf whose section on $V$ is $\mathcal{F}(U \cap V)$, and the right side is the formal notation denoting copies of $\mathcal{F}$, where $\{f^{-n}\mathcal{F},\, n = 0, 1, \ldots\}$ are combined in a direct system.
