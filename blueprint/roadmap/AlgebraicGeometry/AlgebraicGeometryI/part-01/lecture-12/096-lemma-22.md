# Lemma 22

Let $i \in I$ be a directed system indexing sheaves $\mathcal{F}_i$. If $X$ is a Noetherian topological space, then $\varinjlim_{\mathbf{PreSh}} \mathcal{F}_i$ is a sheaf. Hence $\varinjlim_{\mathbf{PreSh}} \mathcal{F}_i = \varinjlim_{\mathbf{Sh}} \mathcal{F}_i$.
