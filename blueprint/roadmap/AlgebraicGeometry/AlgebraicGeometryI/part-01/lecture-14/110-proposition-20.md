# Proposition 20

The following are true: 1.\ $M \mapsto \widetilde{M}_{\mathbb{P}^n}$ is an exact functor. 2.\ Every $\mathcal{F}$ that is a quasicoherent sheaf on $\mathbb{P}^n$ is of the form $\widetilde{M}$ for some $M$, every coherent such $\mathcal{F}$ comes from some finitely generated $M$. Moreover, given a quasicoherent sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $\mathbb{P}^n$, $\mathcal{F} \cong \widetilde{M}$ where $M = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} \Gamma(\mathcal{F}(n))$.
