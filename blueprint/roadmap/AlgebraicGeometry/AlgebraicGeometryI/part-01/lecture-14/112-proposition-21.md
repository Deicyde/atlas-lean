# Proposition 21

$\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbb{P}^n)$ is equivalent to $\mathbf{Mod}_{\mathrm{gr}}(A)$ mod out the locally nilpotent elements, and $\mathbf{Coh}(\mathbb{P}^n)$ is equivalent to $\mathbf{Mod}_{\mathrm{gr,f.g.}}(A)$ mod out the finite dimensional elements.
