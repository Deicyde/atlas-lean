# Corollary 18

If $\mathcal{F}$ is coherent, then there exists $d, k$, such that $\mathcal{O}(-d)^{\oplus k} \to \mathcal{F}$ is a surjection (equivalently, a surjection $\mathcal{O}^{\oplus k} \to \mathcal{F}(d)$). In other words, every coherent sheaf is a quotient of a vector bundle.
