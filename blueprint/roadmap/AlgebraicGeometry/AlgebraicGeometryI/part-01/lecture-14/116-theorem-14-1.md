# Theorem 14.1

When $X$ is factorial (for instance, when $X$ is smooth), $\operatorname{DW}(X) = \operatorname{DC}(X)$. Generally, $\operatorname{Pic}(X) = \operatorname{DC}(X)/K^*$, i.e.\ the quotient of Cartier divisors by the principal divisors.
