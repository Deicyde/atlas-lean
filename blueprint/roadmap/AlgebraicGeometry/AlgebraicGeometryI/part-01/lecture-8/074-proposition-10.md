# Proposition 10

(Catenary property). Let $X$ be an algebraic variety, with $X = Z_n \supsetneq Z_{n-1} \supsetneq \cdots \supsetneq Z_0$ where each $Z_i$ is closed irreducible. If this chain cannot be refined, then $\dim Z_i = i$.
