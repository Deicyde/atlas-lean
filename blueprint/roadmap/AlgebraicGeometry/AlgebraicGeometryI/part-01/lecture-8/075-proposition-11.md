# Proposition 11

If $A = k[X]$ where $X$ is affine of dimension $d$, then $D_V(n) = \Theta(n^d)$; that is, there exist constants $c'$, $c$ such that for all $n$, $c' n^d \leq D_V(n) \leq c n^d$.
