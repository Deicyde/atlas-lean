# Definition 9

A morphism of algebraic varieties $f \colon X \longrightarrow Y$ is called affine if $Y$ has an open cover $Y = \bigcup U_i$ where the $U_i$ are affine open pieces such that the $f^{-1}(U_i) \subset X$ are affine.
