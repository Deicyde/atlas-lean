# Definition 11

The dimension of a Noetherian topological space is the maximal number such that there exists a chain $X \supset Z_n \supsetneq Z_{n-1} \supsetneq Z_{n-2} \supsetneq \cdots \supsetneq Z_0$ of irreducible subsets in $X$.
