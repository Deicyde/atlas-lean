# Definition 10

The morphism $f$ is finite if there is an affine open cover $Y = \bigcup U_i$ such that $f^{-1}(U_i) = \operatorname{Spec} A$ and $U_i = \operatorname{Spec} B$ with $A$ a finitely generated $B$-module.
