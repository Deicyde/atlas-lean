# Proposition 18

Suppose $X \to Y$ is affine. Let $\mathcal{A} = f_*\mathcal{O}_X$, then $\mathbf{QCoh}(X) = \{\mathbf{QCoh}(Y) \text{ with an } \mathcal{A} \text{ action}\}$, where the map is $\mathcal{F} \mapsto f_*\mathcal{F}$.
