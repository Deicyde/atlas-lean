# Proposition 19

$i_* \colon \mathbf{QCoh}(Z) \to \mathbf{QCoh}(X)$ (or coherent to coherent) is a full embedding and the image are the $\mathcal{F}$s such that $\mathcal{I}_Z \mathcal{F} = 0$.
