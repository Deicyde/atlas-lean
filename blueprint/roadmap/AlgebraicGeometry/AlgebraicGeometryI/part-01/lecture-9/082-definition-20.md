# Definition 20

Let $X$ be an affine variety, $x \in X$. We write $\operatorname{Bl}_x(X) = \widetilde{X}_x$ to denote $\widetilde{X}$ for an embedding $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ where $x \mapsto 0$.
