# Definition 37

If $X$ is a smooth irreducible variety of dimension $d$, then $\Omega(X)$ is a locally free sheaf of rank $d$. Then the top exterior power $\omega(X) = \bigwedge^d \Omega(X)$ is a locally free sheaf of rank $1$. We call it the canonical line bundle or the canonical sheaf.
