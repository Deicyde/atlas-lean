# Proposition 33

Suppose $Z \subseteq X$ is a closed subvariety. 1.\ We have an exact sequence $\mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2 = \mathscr{I}_Z|_Z \to \Omega_X|_Z \to \Omega_Z \to 0$. 2.\ If for all $x \in Z$, $\mathscr{I}_Z$ is locally (around $x$) generated by $f_1, \ldots, f_m$ such that $df_i|_x$ are linearly independent at $x$, then the sequence is short exact, and $\mathscr{I}_Z/\mathscr{I}_Z^2$ is a locally free sheaf of rank $m$ where $m$ is the codimension.
