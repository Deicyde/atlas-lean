# Proposition 31

Suppose $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$, $x \in X$ is a smooth point if and only if there exist $f_1, \ldots, f_m \in I_X \subseteq k[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ which locally generate $I_X$ and $df_i|_x$ are linearly independent.
