# Proposition 26

$\operatorname{Pic}^{\circ}(X)$ is itself a complex variety as well as a compact abelian Lie group. In fact, one can define an algebraic group $\operatorname{Jac}(X)$ on it, such that for a curve $X$, the Abel--Jacobi map is algebraic.
