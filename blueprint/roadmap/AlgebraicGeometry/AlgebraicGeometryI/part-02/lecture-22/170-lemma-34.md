# Lemma 34

If we have a short exact sequence $0 \to \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}' \to \mathcal{T} \to 0$, where $\mathcal{T}$ is torsion and the other two sheaves are torsion free, then $\deg(\mathcal{E}') = \deg(\mathcal{E}) + \ell(\mathcal{T})$.
