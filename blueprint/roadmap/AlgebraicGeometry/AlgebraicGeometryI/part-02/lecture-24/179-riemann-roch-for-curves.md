# Theorem 24.2 (Riemann-Roch for Curves)

Let $X$ be an irreducible complete (or smooth) curve. Then $\chi(\mathcal{F}) = \deg(\mathcal{F}) - \operatorname{rank}(\mathcal{F})(g_a - 1)$ where $g_a = \dim H^1(\mathcal{O})$.
