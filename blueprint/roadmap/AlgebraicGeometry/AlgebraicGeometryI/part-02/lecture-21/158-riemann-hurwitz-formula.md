# Theorem 21.1 (Riemann-Hurwitz formula)

Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a morphism of smooth irreducible curves. Then $k(X)/k(Y)$ is a separable extension. Let $d_x$ be the ramification index at $x$. Assume that $d_x$ is prime to $\operatorname{char}(k)$. Then $f^*K_Y \to K_X$ extends to an isomorphism $f^*K_Y(R) \simeq K_X$ where $R = \sum_{x \in X} (d_x - 1)x$.
