# Theorem 21.2 (Chevalley's theorem)

Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a morphism of varieties. Then: $\operatorname{im}(f)$ is constructible. Furthermore, if $X$, $Y$ are irreducible and $\operatorname{im}(f)$ is dense in $Y$, then the function $f(x) \mapsto \dim f^{-1}(f(x))$ is upper semi-continuous. In other words, for any $d$, $\{f(x) \mid \dim f^{-1}(f(x)) \geq d\}$ is closed in $\operatorname{im}(f)$. Finally, there exists a non-empty open subset $U$ in $Y$ such that $\dim f^{-1}(y) = \dim X - \dim Y$ for all $y \in U$.
