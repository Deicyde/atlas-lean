# Proposition 39

Let $X$ be a normal irreducible affine variety and $Z \subset X$ be a closed subvariety. If $\dim Z \leq \dim X - 2$ then $k[X] = k[X \setminus Z]$. Therefore, for normal varieties, the regular functions extend from the complement of a codimension $\geq 2$ closed subvariety to the whole space.
