# Newton polygon of a support

The convex hull in `ℝ²` of a finite set `A ⊆ ℤ²` of
exponent vectors (Sturmfels, CBMS 97, Section 3.1, `New(f)`). This is the Laurent analogue of
the [Newton polygon](../../../Algebra/polynomials/newton-polygon/newton-polygon.md) `NewtonPolygon.newtonPolygon`, which takes a bivariate polynomial with exponents in `ℕ²`.
