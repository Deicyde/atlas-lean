# Tangent space at the origin of an abelian variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ and of its complexification

Let $A'$ be an [abelian variety](../../../abelian-varieties/abelian-scheme.md) over $\mathbb{Q}$, let $T_0(A')$ be its tangent space
at the origin, a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space, and let $Y_{A'}$ be the tangent space at the origin of the complex abelian
variety $A'_{\mathbb{C}}$, as in
[holomorphic $1$-forms and tangent space](../holomorphic-one-forms-steps/holomorphic-one-forms-and-tangent-space.md). The
formation of the tangent space at the rational point $0$ commutes with the extension of scalars from $\mathbb{Q}$ to
$\mathbb{C}$:

$$Y_{A'} = \mathbb{C} \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} T_0(A').$$

## Depends on

- [Abelian scheme](../../../abelian-varieties/abelian-scheme.md), for abelian varieties over $\mathbb{Q}$ and their origin
- [Holomorphic $1$-forms and tangent space of a complex abelian variety](../holomorphic-one-forms-steps/holomorphic-one-forms-and-tangent-space.md),
  for the tangent space $Y_{A'}$ of $A'_{\mathbb{C}}$ at the origin

## Proof depends on

- [Abelian scheme](../../../abelian-varieties/abelian-scheme.md), for the origin, a rational point of $A'$

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the tangent space at a rational point $x$ of a scheme of finite type over a field $k$ as the fibre over $x$ of
  $X(k[\varepsilon]) \to X(k)$, that is, $\mathrm{Hom}_k(\mathfrak{m}_x / \mathfrak{m}_x^2, k)$, and its compatibility with the
  extension of scalars from $\mathbb{Q}$ to $\mathbb{C}$ at the rational point $0$ of $A'$;
- the identification of the tangent space of the variety $A'_{\mathbb{C}}$ at $0$ with the holomorphic tangent space of
  $A'(\mathbb{C})$ at $0$.
