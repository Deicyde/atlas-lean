# Holomorphic $1$-forms of an abelian variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ with an action of a number field: statement and steps

[Holomorphic $1$-forms of an abelian variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ with an action of a number field](number-field-action-on-holomorphic-one-forms.md)
(Shimura, J. Math. Soc. Japan 25 (1973), §1, p. 527) and the step of its proof beyond linear algebra over $K$, the
splitting of $\mathbb{C} \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} K$ and the duality between holomorphic $1$-forms and tangent vectors: the
tangent space at the origin of an abelian variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ spans that of its complexification, which gives the
holomorphic $1$-forms a $\mathbb{Q}$-structure stable under the endomorphisms defined over $\mathbb{Q}$.
