# Definition (Newton polygon)

The Newton polygon of a polynomial $f(x, y)=\sum a_{ij} x^{i} y^{j}$ is the convex hull of the set $\{(i, j): a_{ij} \neq 0\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$.
