# Definition 4.5

Let $a=(a_{n})$ be a $p$-adic integer with each $a_{n}$ uniquely represented by an integer in $\in [0, p^{n}-1]$. The sequence $(b_{0}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots)$ with $b_{0}=a_{1}$ and $b_{n}=(a_{n+1}-a_{n})/p^{n}$ is called the $p$-adic expansion of $a$.
