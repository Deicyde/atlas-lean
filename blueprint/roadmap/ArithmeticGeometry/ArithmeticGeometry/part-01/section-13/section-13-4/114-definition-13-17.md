# Definition 13.17

Let $Z$ be an algebraic set in $\mathbb{P}^{n}$, the (homogeneous) ideal of $Z$ is the ideal $I(Z)$ generated by all the homogeneous polynomials in $\bar{k}\left[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{n}\right]$ that vanish at every point in $Z$. We say that $Z$ is defined over $k$ if its ideal can be generated by homogeneous polynomials in $k\left[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{n}\right]$, and write $Z / k$ to indicate this. If $Z$ is defined over $k$ the set of $k$-rational points on $Z$ is $Z(k)=Z \cap \mathbb{P}^{n}(k)=Z^{G_{k}}$ and similarly for any extension of $k$ in $\bar{k}$.
