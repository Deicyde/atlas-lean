# Definition 13.2

Let $Z$ be an algebraic set defined over $k$. The affine coordinate ring of $Z/k$ is the ring $k[Z] = k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}] / I(Z/k)$. We similarly define $\bar{k}[Z] = \bar{k}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}] / I(Z)$.
