# Definition 13.10

Let $V \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n}$ be a variety, and let $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m} \in \bar{k}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ be a set of generators for $I(V)$. A point $P \in V$ is nonsingular (or smooth) if the $m \times n$ Jacobian matrix $M(P)$ with entries $M_{ij}(P) = \frac{\partial f_{i}}{\partial x_{j}}(P)$ has rank $n - \dim V$; otherwise $P$ is a singular point of $V$. If $V$ has no singular points then we say that $V$ is smooth.
