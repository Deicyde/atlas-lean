# Definition 13.21

If $Z \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n}$ is any affine algebraic set, we can embed it in $\mathbb{P}^{n}$ by identifying $\mathbb{A}^{n}$ with the affine patch $U_{0}$ of $\mathbb{P}^{n}$; we write $Z \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n} \subset \mathbb{P}^{n}$ to indicate this embedding. The projective closure of $Z$ in $\mathbb{P}^{n}$, denoted $\bar{Z}$, is the projective algebraic set defined by the ideal generated by all the homogenizations (with respect to $x_{0}$) of all the polynomials in $I(Z)$.
