# Definition 13.14

The complement of $H_{i}$ in $\mathbb{P}^{n}$ is the affine patch (or affine chart) $U_{i} = \{(a_{0}: \ldots: a_{i-1}: 1: a_{i+1}: \ldots: a_{n}) \in \mathbb{P}^{n}\}$ which corresponds to a copy of $\mathbb{A}^{n}$ embedded in $\mathbb{P}^{n}$ (note that fixing $a_{i} = 1$ fixes a choice of representative for the projective point $(a_{0}: \ldots: a_{i-1}: 1: a_{i+1}: \ldots: a_{n})$).
