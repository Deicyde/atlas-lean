# Corollary 17.4

Let $P$ be a point on an affine variety $V \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ with $I(V) = (f_1, \ldots, f_m)$, and let $J_P = J_P(f_1, \ldots, f_m)$. Then $\dim T_P(V)^{\perp} = \operatorname{rank} J_P$ and $\dim T_P(V) = n - \operatorname{rank} J_P$. In particular, the rank of $J_P$ does not depend on the choice of generators for $I(V)$ and $P$ is a smooth point of $V$ if and only if $\dim T_P = \dim V$.
