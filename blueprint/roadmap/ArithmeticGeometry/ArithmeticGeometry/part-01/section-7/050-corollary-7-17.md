# Corollary 7.17

The completion $\hat{k}$ of $k$ is complete. Moreover it is the smallest complete field containing $k$ in the following sense: any embedding of $k$ in a complete field $k'$ can be extended to an embedding of $\hat{k}$ into $k'$.
