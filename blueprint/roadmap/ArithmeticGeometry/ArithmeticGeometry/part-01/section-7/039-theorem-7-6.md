# Theorem 7.6

Let $L$ be a number field. For every $\alpha \in L^\times$ we have $\prod_{\mathfrak{p}} |\alpha|_{\mathfrak{p}} = 1$, where $\mathfrak{p}$ ranges over all the primes of $L$ (both finite and infinite).
