# Corollary 12.18

There is a one-to-one inclusion-reversing correspondence between radical ideals $I \subseteq \bar{k}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ and algebraic sets $Z \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n}(\bar{k})$ in which $I=I(Z)$ and $Z=Z_{I}$.
