# Lemma 11.11

Let $S$ be a finite set of primes $p \leq \infty$, and for each $p \in S$ let $x_{p} \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times}$ be given. Then there exists an $x \in \mathbb{Q}$ such that (i) $x \in x_{p} \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times 2}$ for each $p \in S$. (ii) $|x|_{p}=1$ for all but at most one finite prime $p_{0} \notin S$.
