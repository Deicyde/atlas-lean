# Theorem 11.6

Let $p \leq \infty$ be any prime of $\mathbb{Q}$. Under the metric $d(x, y)=|x-y|_{p}$, the set $\mathbb{Q}$ is dense in $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ and the set $\mathbb{Z}$ is dense in $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$.
