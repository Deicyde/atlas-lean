# Theorem 11.7 (Weak approximation)

Let $S$ be a finite set of primes $p \leq \infty$, and for each $p \in S$ let $x_{p} \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}$ be given. Then for every $\epsilon>0$ there exists $x \in \mathbb{Q}$ such that $|x-x_{p}|_{p}<\epsilon$ for all $p \in S$. Equivalently, the image of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\prod_{p \in S} \mathbb{Q}_{p}$ is dense under the product topology.
