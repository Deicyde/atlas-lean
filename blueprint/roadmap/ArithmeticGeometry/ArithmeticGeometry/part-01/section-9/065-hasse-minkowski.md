# Theorem 9.10 (Hasse-Minkowski)

A quadratic form over $\mathbb{Q}$ represents 0 if and only if it represents 0 over every completion of $\mathbb{Q}$, that is, over $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ for all primes $p \leq \infty$.
