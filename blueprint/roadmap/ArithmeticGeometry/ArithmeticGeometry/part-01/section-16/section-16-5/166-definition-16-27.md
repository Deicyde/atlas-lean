# Definition 16.27

Let $X$ be a variety with coordinate ring $k[X]$ and let $P \in X$. The local ring of $P$ on $X$ is the ring $\mathcal{O}_{P} \coloneqq \mathcal{O}_{P,X} \coloneqq k[X]_{m_P} = \{g/h \in k(X) : h \notin m_P\}$.
