# Lemma 16.32

Let $R$ be a valuation ring of $F$. For any $x_0, \ldots, x_n \in F^{\times}$ there exists $\lambda \in F^{\times}$ such that $\lambda x_0, \ldots, \lambda x_n \in R$ and at least one $\lambda x_i$ is a unit in $R$.
