# Theorem 16.28

Let $X$ be a variety such that for every subvariety $Z \subseteq X$ and valuation ring $R$ of $k(Z)/k$ there exists a point $P \in Z$ such that $\mathcal{O}_{P,Z} \subseteq R$. Then $X$ is complete.
