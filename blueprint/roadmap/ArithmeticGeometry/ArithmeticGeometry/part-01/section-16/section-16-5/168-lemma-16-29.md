# Lemma 16.29

Let $A$ be an integral domain contained in a field $K$ and let $\phi: A \rightarrow k$ be a homomorphism to an algebraically closed field $k$. Then there exists a valuation ring $B$ of $K$ containing $A$ and a homomorphism $\Phi: B \rightarrow k$ that extends $\phi$. The kernel of $\Phi$ is then the maximal ideal of $B$ and $k$ is its residue field.
