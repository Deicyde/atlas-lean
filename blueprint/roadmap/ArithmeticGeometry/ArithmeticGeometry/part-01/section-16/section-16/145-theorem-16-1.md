# Theorem 16.1

Let $S$ be a subset of $\mathbb{A}^{n}$, and let $\{U_{a}\}_{a \in A}$ be any collection of open sets of $\mathbb{A}^{n}$ whose union contains $S$. Then there exists a finite set $B \subseteq A$ for which $S \subseteq \{U_{b}\}_{b \in B}$.
