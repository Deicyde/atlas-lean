# Theorem 8.4

For any $f \in \mathbb{Z}_p[x]$ the following are equivalent: (a) $f$ has a root in $\mathbb{Z}_p$. (b) $f \bmod p^n$ has a root in $\mathbb{Z}/p^n\mathbb{Z}$ for all $n \geq 1$.
