# Definition 15.2

A function $r \in \bar{k}(X)$ is said to be regular at a point $P \in X$ if $gr \in \bar{k}[X]$ for some $g \in \bar{k}[X] \subseteq \bar{k}(X)$ for which $g(P) \neq 0$ (we then have $r = f/g$ for some $f \in \bar{k}[X]$).
