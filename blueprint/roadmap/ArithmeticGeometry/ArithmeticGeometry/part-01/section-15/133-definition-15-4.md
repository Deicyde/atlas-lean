# Definition 15.4

Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^m$ and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ be affine varieties. We say that a tuple $(\varphi_1, \ldots, \varphi_n)$ with $\varphi_i \in \bar{k}(X)$ is regular at $P \in X$ if the $\varphi_i$ are all regular at $P$. A rational map $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ is a tuple $(\varphi_1, \ldots, \varphi_n)$ with $\varphi_i \in \bar{k}(X)$ such that $\varphi(P) \coloneqq (\varphi_1(P), \ldots, \varphi_n(P)) \in Y$ for all points $P \in X$ where $\varphi$ is regular. If $\varphi$ is regular at every point $P \in X$ then we say that $\varphi$ is regular.
