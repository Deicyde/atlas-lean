# Definition 14.3

In the Zariski topology on $\mathbb{A}^{n}$ (resp. $\mathbb{P}^{n}$), the closed sets are precisely the algebraic sets. Any algebraic set in $\mathbb{A}^{n}$ (resp. $\mathbb{P}^{n}$) then inherits the subspace topology.
