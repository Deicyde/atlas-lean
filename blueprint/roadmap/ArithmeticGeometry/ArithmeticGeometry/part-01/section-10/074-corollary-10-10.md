# Corollary 10.10

The Hilbert symbol $(a, b)_{p}$ is a nondegenerate bilinear map. This means that for all $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times}$ we have $(a, c)_{p}(b, c)_{p}=(a b, c)_{p}$ and $(a, b)_{p}(a, c)_{p}=(a, b c)_{p}$ and that for every non-square $c$ we have $(b, c)_{p}=-1$ for some $b$.
