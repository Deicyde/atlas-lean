# Definition 10.1

For $a, b \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times}$ the Hilbert symbol $(a, b)_{p}$ is defined by $(a, b)_{p} = \begin{cases} 1 & a x^{2}+b y^{2}=1 \text{ has a solution in } \mathbb{Q}_{p} \\ -1 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
