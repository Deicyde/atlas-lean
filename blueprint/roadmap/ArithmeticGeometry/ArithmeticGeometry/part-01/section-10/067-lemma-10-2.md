# Lemma 10.2

For any $a, b \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times}$, the following are equivalent: (i) $(a, b)_{p}=1$. (ii) The quadratic form $z^{2}-a x^{2}-b y^{2}$ represents 0. (iii) The equation $a x^{2}+b y^{2}=z^{2}$ has a primitive solution. (iv) $a \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}$ is the norm of an element in $\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\sqrt{b})$.
