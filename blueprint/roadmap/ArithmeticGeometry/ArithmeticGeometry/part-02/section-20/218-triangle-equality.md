# Lemma 20.2 (Triangle equality)

Let $v: F^{\times} \rightarrow \Gamma$ be a valuation on a field $F$. For any $x, y \in F^{\times}$ such that $v(x) \neq v(y)$ we have $v(x+y)=\min(v(x), v(y))$.
