# Corollary 20.4

Let $\mathcal{O}_{1}, \ldots, \mathcal{O}_{n}$ be distinct discrete valuation rings of a function field $F/k$. The ring $\mathcal{O}=\cap_{i} \mathcal{O}_{i}$ has exactly $n$ nonzero prime ideals $(t_{1}), \ldots, (t_{n})$, each principal and generated by a uniformizer for $\mathcal{O}_{i}$. Every nonzero $f \in \mathcal{O}$ can be uniquely factored as $f=u t_{1}^{e_{1}} \cdots t_{n}^{e_{n}}$ with $u \in \mathcal{O}^{\times}$ and $e_{i}=\operatorname{ord}_{P_{i}}(f) \geq 0$.
