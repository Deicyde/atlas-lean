# Corollary 20.7

Let $C/k$ be a curve with at least two distinct rational points. Then $C/k$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{P}^{1}/k$ (with the isomorphism defined over $k$) if and only if $\operatorname{Pic}_{k}^{0} C=\{0\}$.
