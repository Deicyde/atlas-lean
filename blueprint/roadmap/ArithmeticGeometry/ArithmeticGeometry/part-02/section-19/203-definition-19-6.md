# Definition 19.6

Let $C/k$ be a curve with $k=\bar{k}$. A divisor of $C$ is a formal sum $D \coloneqq \sum_{P \in C} n_P P$ with $n_P \in \mathbb{Z}$ and all but finitely many $n_P = 0$. The set of points $P$ for which $n_P \neq 0$ is called the support of $D$. The divisors of $C$ form a free abelian group under addition, the divisor group of $C$, denoted $\operatorname{Div} C$.
