# Definition 19.11

Let $f$ be a nonzero element of a function field $F/k$. The divisor of $f$ is $\operatorname{div} f \coloneqq \sum_{P \in X_F} \operatorname{ord}_P(f) P$. Such divisors are said to be principal.
