# Definition 19.18

Let $\phi: C_1 \rightarrow C_2$ be a morphism defined over $k$, and $\phi^*: k(C_2) \rightarrow k(C_1)$ the corresponding morphism of function fields. The ramification index (also called the ramification degree) of $\phi$ at a closed point $P$ of $C_1$ (equivalently, a place $P$ of $k(C_1)$) is $e_{\phi}(P) \coloneqq \operatorname{ord}_P(\phi^* t_Q)$ where $t_Q \in k(C_2)$ is a uniformizer at $Q = \phi(P)$, that is, a generator for the place $Q$ of $k(C_2)$. If $e_{\phi}(P) = 1$, then $\phi$ is unramified at $P$, and if $e_{\phi}(P) = 1$ for all closed points $P$ of $C_1/k$ we say that $\phi$ is unramified.
