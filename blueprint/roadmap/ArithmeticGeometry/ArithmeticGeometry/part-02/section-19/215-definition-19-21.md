# Definition 19.21

Let $C/k$ be a curve and let $F/k$ be the corresponding function field. If $P$ is a closed point of $C/k$, or a place of $F/k$, we define the degree of $P$ to be the dimension of the residue field $k(P) = \mathcal{O}_P/m_P$ over $k$ (where $m_P = P$ if $P$ is a place of $F/k$), that is, $\deg P \coloneqq [k(P):k]$. Equivalently, $\deg P$ is the cardinality of the closed point $P$ as a $G_k$-orbit of points in $C(\bar{k})$. The degree of a divisor $D = \sum n_P P$ in the group of $k$-rational divisors is $\deg D \coloneqq \sum n_P \deg P$.
