# Lemma 25.12

Let $k$ be a number field and let $\phi: \mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{P}^m$ be a morphism $(\phi_0 : \cdots : \phi_n)$ defined by homogeneous polynomials $\phi_i \in k[x_0, \ldots, x_n]$ of degree $d$. There is a constant $c$ such that $h(\phi(P)) \leq d\, h(P) + c$ for all $P \in \mathbb{P}^n(\bar{k})$.
