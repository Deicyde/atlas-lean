# Theorem 25.14

Let $k$ be a number field and let $\phi: \mathbb{P}^{n} \rightarrow \mathbb{P}^{m}$ be a morphism $(\phi_{0}: \cdots: \phi_{n})$ defined by homogeneous polynomials $\phi_{i} \in k[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ of degree $d$. Then $h(\phi(P))=d h(P)+O(1)$.
