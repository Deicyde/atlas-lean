# Theorem 24.20 (Mazur)

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve. The torsion subgroup of $E(\mathbb{Q})$ is isomorphic to one of the fifteen subgroups listed below: $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ ($n=1,2,3,\ldots,9,10,12$), $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z}$ ($n=1,2,3,4$).
