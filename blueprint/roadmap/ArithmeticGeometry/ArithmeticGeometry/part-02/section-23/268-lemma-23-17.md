# Lemma 23.17

Let $E / k$ be an elliptic curve in $\mathbb{P}^{2}$, and let $L_{1}, L_{2}$ be lines in $\mathbb{P}^{2}$ defined by linear homogeneous polynomials $\ell_{1}, \ell_{2} \in k[x, y, z]$. Let $f \in k(E)$ be the image of $\ell_{1} / \ell_{2}$ under the map $k(\mathbb{P}^{2}) \rightarrow k(E)$ induced by the inclusion $E \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{2}$. Then $\operatorname{div} f=(L_{1} \cap E)-(L_{2} \cap E)$.
