# Definition 18.9

An abstract curve is the topological space $X = X_{F}$ with rings of regular functions $\mathcal{O}_{X, U}$ determined by the function field $F/k$ as above. A morphism $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ between abstract curves or projective varieties is a continuous map such that for every open $U \subseteq Y$ and $f \in \mathcal{O}_{Y}(U)$ we have $f \circ \varphi \in \mathcal{O}_{X}(\varphi^{-1}(U))$.
