# Definition 22.5

The adele ring of the function field $F/k$ is the subring $\mathcal{A}=\mathcal{A}_{F}$ of the direct product $\prod_{P} F$ consisting of those elements $\alpha=(\alpha_{P})$ for which $\alpha_{P} \in \mathcal{O}_{P}$ for all but finitely many $P$. The elements of $\mathcal{A}$ are called adeles.
