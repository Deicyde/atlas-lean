# Theorem 22.20 (Duality)

For any divisor $D$ and canonical divisor $W = \operatorname{div} \omega$, the linear map $\phi: \mathcal{L}(W-D) \rightarrow \Omega(D)$ defined by $\phi(f) = f\omega$ is an isomorphism of $k$-vector spaces. In particular, we have $i(D) = \ell(W-D)$ for all divisors $D$.
