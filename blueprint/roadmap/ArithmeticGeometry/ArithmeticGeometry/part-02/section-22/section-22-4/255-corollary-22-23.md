# Corollary 22.23

For all divisors $D$ of a genus $g$ curve $C/k$ with $\operatorname{deg} D > 2g - 2$ we have $\ell(D) = \operatorname{deg} D + 1 - g$, equivalently, $i(D) = 0$.
