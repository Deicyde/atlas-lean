# Corollary 26.5

Let $C/k$ be a genus one curve and let $O$ and $O'$ be any two points in $C(\bar{k})$. Then the elliptic curves $(C, O)$ and $(C, O')$ over $\bar{k}$ have the same $j$-invariant.
