# Theorem 26.12

Let $C/k$ be a genus one curve. There is an elliptic curve $E/k$ related to $C/k$ by an isomorphism $\phi: C \rightarrow E$ such that for every automorphism $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\bar{k}/k)$ the isomorphism $\varphi_{\sigma}: E \rightarrow E$ defined by $\varphi_{\sigma}\coloneqq \phi^{\sigma} \circ \phi^{-1}$ is a translation-by-$P_{\sigma}$ map for some $P_{\sigma} \in E(\bar{k})$. The curve $E$ is unique up to $k$-isomorphism.
