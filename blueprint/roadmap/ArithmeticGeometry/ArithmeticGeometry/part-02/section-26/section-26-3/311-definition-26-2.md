# Definition 26.2

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve with Weierstrass equation $y^{2}=x^{3}+a_{4} x+a_{6}$. The $j$-invariant of $E$ is $$j(E)\coloneqq 1728 \frac{4 a_{4}^{3}}{4 a_{4}^{3}+27 a_{6}^{2}}$$
