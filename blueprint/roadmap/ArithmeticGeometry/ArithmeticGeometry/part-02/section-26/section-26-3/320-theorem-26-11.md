# Theorem 26.11

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic not equal to 2 or 3. The automorphism group of an elliptic curve $E/k$ is a cyclic group of order 6, 4, or 2, depending on whether $j(E)$ is equal to 0, 1728, or neither, respectively.
