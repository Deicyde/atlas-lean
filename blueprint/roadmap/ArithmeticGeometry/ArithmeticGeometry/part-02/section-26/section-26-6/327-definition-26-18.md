# Definition 26.18

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. Two $E$-torsors $C/k$ and $C'/k$ are equivalent if there is an isomorphism $\theta: C \rightarrow C'$ defined over $k$ that is compatible with the action of $E$. This means that $\theta(Q+P)=\theta(Q)+P$ holds for all $Q \in C(\bar{k})$ and $P \in E(\bar{k})$. The Weil-Châtelet group $\mathrm{WC}(E/k)$ is the set of equivalence classes of $E$-torsors under this equivalence relation.
