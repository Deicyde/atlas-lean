# Lemma 21.8

For any two divisors $A \leq B$ we have $\mathcal{L}(A) \subseteq \mathcal{L}(B)$ and $\operatorname{dim}(\mathcal{L}(B)/\mathcal{L}(A)) \leq \operatorname{deg} B - \operatorname{deg} A$.
