# Tukey equivalence

**Tukey equivalence.** Preordered sets $D$ and $E$ are Tukey equivalent (cofinally
equivalent), written $D \equiv_T E$, if $D \le_T E$ and $E \le_T D$, where $\le_T$ is Tukey
reducibility.
