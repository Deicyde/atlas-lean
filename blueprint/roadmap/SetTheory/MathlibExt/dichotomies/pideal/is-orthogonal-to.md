# Orthogonality to a family of sets

**Orthogonality to a family of sets.** Let $I$ be a family of subsets of a set $\alpha$. A
set $X \subseteq \alpha$ is orthogonal to $I$ if $X \cap a$ is finite for every $a \in I$.
