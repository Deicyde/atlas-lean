# Equality modulo finite sets

**Equality modulo finite sets.** Subsets $A$ and $B$ of $\mathbb{N}$ are *equal modulo finite*,
written $A =^{\ast} B$, if their symmetric difference $A \mathbin{\triangle} B$ is finite.
