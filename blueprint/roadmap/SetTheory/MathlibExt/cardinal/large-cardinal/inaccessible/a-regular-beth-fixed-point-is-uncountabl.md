# A regular beth fixed point is uncountable: regularity gives...

A regular beth fixed point is uncountable: regularity gives `ℵ₀ ≤ κ`, and
`κ = ℵ₀` fails the fixed-point equation since `ℶ_ω > ℶ_0 = ℵ₀`.
