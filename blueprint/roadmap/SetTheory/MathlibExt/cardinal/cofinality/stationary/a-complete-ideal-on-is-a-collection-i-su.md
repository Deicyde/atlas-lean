# A *κ-complete ideal on κ* is a collection I ⊆ 𝒫(κ) such...

A *κ-complete ideal on κ* is a collection I ⊆ 𝒫(κ) such that:

1. ∅ ∈ I.
1. If A ∈ I and B ⊆ A, then B ∈ I.
1. κ ∉ I.
1. If λ < κ and ⟨ A_α : α < λ ⟩ is a sequence of members of I, then [ ⋃_{α < λ} A_α ∈ I. ]
