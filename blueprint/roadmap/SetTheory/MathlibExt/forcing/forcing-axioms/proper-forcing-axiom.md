# Proper Forcing Axiom

The Proper Forcing Axiom: For any proper poset ℙ, any condition p∈ℙ, for any sequence (D_α :
α<ω₁) of dense subsets of ℙ, there is a filter G⊆ ℙ meeting every D_α and p∈ G.
