# Weak power below a strong limit cardinal equals the cardinal

For `2 ≤ x < κ` with `κ` a strong limit, `x ^< κ = κ`, where `x ^< κ` is the supremum of the
powers `x ^ y` over cardinals `y < κ`: every smaller power is below `κ` (see
[Strong limit cardinals are closed under exponentiation](strong-limit-cardinals-are-closed-under.md)),
while every `y < κ` sits below `2 ^ y ≤ x ^< κ`. No infiniteness hypothesis is needed, since a
strong limit cardinal is automatically infinite.
