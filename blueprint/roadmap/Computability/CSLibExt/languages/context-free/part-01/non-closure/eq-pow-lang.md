# The language $a^n b^n$

**The language $a^n b^n$.** For letters $a$ and $b$ of an alphabet, this is the language of the
words $a^n b^n$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$: $n$ copies of $a$ followed by $n$ copies of $b$.
