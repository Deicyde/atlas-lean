# Hopcroft's relation

**Hopcroft's relation.** For a DFA $M$ with finitely many states, Hopcroft's relation is the
set of pairs $(p, q)$ of states of $M$ such that $p$ and $q$ lie in a common block of Hopcroft's
partition.
