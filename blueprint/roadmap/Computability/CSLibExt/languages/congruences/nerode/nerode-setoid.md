# The Nerode setoid

**The Nerode setoid.** For a language $L$ over an alphabet $\Sigma$, the Nerode setoid of $L$
is the set $\Sigma^*$ of words equipped with Nerode equivalence for $L$, which is reflexive,
symmetric and transitive.
