# Cubic bound on the verifier's phase costs

**Cubic bound on the verifier's phase costs.** For natural numbers $X$, $W$, $P$, $R$ and $S$
with $P + R \le X$ and $S \le X$,
$P + 1 + (R(2X + 6) + 1) + (S + 1) + (S + 1) \le 16 (X + W + 1)^3$.
