# Triangular numbers

**Triangular numbers.** For a natural number $n$, the triangular number $T(n)$ is the sum
$0 + 1 + \cdots + (n - 1)$ of the natural numbers below $n$. It is defined by recursion:
$T(0) = 0$ and $T(i + 1) = T(i) + i$.
