# Col Check

The verifier: `x` decodes to `(n, es)`, every edge endpoint is below `n`, `w` has at least
`2 n` bits, every vertex `i < n` has a valid color, and the two ends of every edge `(u, v)` with
`u ≠ v` have different colors.
