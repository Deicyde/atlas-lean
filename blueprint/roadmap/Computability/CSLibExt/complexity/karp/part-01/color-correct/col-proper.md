# Proper 3-colorings of an edge list

**Proper 3-colorings of an edge list.** A map $c : \mathbb{N} \to \{0, 1, 2\}$ properly colors
a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers when $c(a) \ne c(b)$ for every pair $(a, b)$ in $E$.
