# Alt Quant

Alternating quantifiers over witnesses of length at most `bound |x|`:
`k` blocks remain, outermost `∃` when `isSigma` is `true`, `∀` when `false`.
`R (x, ws)` is the polynomial-time matrix on the list of witnesses so far.
