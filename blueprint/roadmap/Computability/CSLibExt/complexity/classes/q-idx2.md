# Index of a pair of bits

**Index of a pair of bits.** A pair $(a, b)$ of bits $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ has index $2a + b$,
its position in the enumeration $(0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 1)$ of the four pairs.
