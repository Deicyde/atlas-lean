# PCP

Non-adaptive `PCP` with `rbound |x|` random bits and at most
`qbound |x|` queries: `queries` picks the queried positions from `(x, rand)`,
`decide` decides from `(x, rand, answers)`. Completeness `≥ 2/3`,
soundness `≤ 1/3`.
