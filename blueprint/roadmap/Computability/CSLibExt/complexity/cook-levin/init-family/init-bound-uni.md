# Init Bound uni

With all constants `k ≥ 1` and `T = n = e = f = y ≥ 1`, every monomial is at most `k⁵ y⁵`; the
coefficients sum to `initBound (uni 1) 1 1 1 1 1 1 1 = 18928`.
