# Initial counters of the accept generator

**Initial counters of the accept generator.** For $T, H \in \mathbb{N}$, the initial counter
values are $T$ on the counter $\mathrm{Tn}$, $H$ on the counter $\mathrm{Hs}$, and $0$ on every
other counter.
