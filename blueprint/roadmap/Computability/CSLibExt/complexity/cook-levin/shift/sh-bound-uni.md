# Sh Bound uni

With all constants `k ≥ 1` and `T = H = y ≥ 1`, every monomial is at most `k⁷ y⁶` and the
coefficients sum to 828 (the value at `k = y = 1`). Plain `linarith` (monomials as atoms) suffices;
no products of hypotheses are needed.
