# Step budget for one one-hot clause

**Step budget for one one-hot clause.** For natural numbers $g$ and $V$, the step budget for
one clause is $V(3V + 6) + g(6V + 9) + 3V + 4$, where $6V + 9$ is the per-literal cost apart
from the gap loop.
