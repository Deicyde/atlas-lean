# Correctness of the assembled formula

**Correctness of the assembled formula** (`five_iff` through the concatenation): the host's
output formula is satisfiable iff some certificate `y ∈ Ys*` with `|x ++ y| ≤ m` makes `M` accept
at step `T`.
