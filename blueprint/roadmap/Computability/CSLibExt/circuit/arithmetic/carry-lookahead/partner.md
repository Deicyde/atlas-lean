# Kogge–Stone partner bit

**Kogge–Stone partner bit.** The bit combined with bit $i$ at level $k + 1$ of the Kogge–Stone
adder is bit $i - 2^k$ if $2^k \le i$, and bit $i$ itself otherwise.
