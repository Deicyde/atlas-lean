# Bottom word of a domino sequence

**Bottom word of a domino sequence.** For a sequence $s = d_1, \dots, d_k$ of dominoes, the
bottom word of $s$ is the concatenation $b_1 b_2 \cdots b_k$, where $b_i$ is the bottom word of
$d_i$.
